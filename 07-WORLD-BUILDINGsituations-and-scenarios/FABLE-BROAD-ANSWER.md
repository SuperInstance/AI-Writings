## 1. Law 7 — the Reach Bound, stated precisely

### 1.1 Statement

Let *R* = {r₁…rₙ} be readers over a task set *T*; let *Cᵢ* ⊆ *T* be reader *i*'s correct
set; let *E* be the evidence multiset the readers produce (Law 6's *E*). Define

- **reach(E)** = ⋃ᵢ *Cᵢ* — the *oracle*: items some reader lands;
- **floor(R)** = maxᵢ |*Cᵢ*| realized by the fold (fold ≥ best-single ≥ weakest);
- **headroom(R)** = |reach| − |best-single| — items where the best is wrong and someone is
  right (S2 r5's "complementary items").

**Law 7 has three clauses:**

- **(i) The bound.** Any fold *V*(E, π) — vote, judge-choice, safe fold, trust-weighted
  argmax, anything — satisfies |*V* correct| ≤ |reach(E)|. A fold is a pure function of *E*;
  it cannot emit a truth *E* does not contain. (Data-processing inequality, restated for
  the quilt: the fold is post-processing.)
- **(ii) Conservation.** For fixed *R*: oracle = floor + headroom. A good fold moves
  quantity from headroom to floor (r3: 14 → 17, three items recovered, oracle unchanged at
  17); a bad fold moves floor to nowhere (r4: 24 → 21, the blind JEV fold *dissipated*);
  no fold moves the oracle. Distillation (S1's commons exemplars, S7's LoRA) spends
  headroom: once the fold's output is a member, the union is the new best-single and
  headroom is zero again.
- **(iii) The only raise.** oracle(R ∪ {r}) − oracle(R) = |*C_r* \ reach(E)| — the new
  reader's *novel* correct set. This is non-zero iff *r*'s correct set is not nested inside
  the set's. **Nested readers have one reach.** And nesting is the *measured* state of
  affairs: three domains, three general models forming a chain (DeepSeek ⊇ Nemotron ⊇
  Hermes ⊇ Mistral ⊇ Llama on the items), universal misses where the anchor missed.
  Strength nests. That is why *diversity* (many points on one chain) bought nothing.

Written as the family writes a law: **"Carry the evidence, fold your own verdict — and
know the verdict can reach no further than the evidence. Nested readers have one reach.
Only an independent reader raises the roof, and the roof is bought, not folded."**

### 1.2 Why complementarity is free energy and diversity is temperature

Disagreement (diversity) is *necessary* for the fold to have anything to do — r1/r2 were
vacuous at 14/14 unanimity — but it is not the conserved input. Llama-70B at 9/18 disagreed
with DeepSeek on nine items and contributed zero headroom: every disagreement was Llama
wrong. Diversity is temperature: it makes the fold *work*; it does not raise the roof.
Complementarity is the potential the fold converts. The mapping holds where it should and
fails where it should:

| thermodynamics | the organism | receipt |
|---|---|---|
| conserved under rearrangement | oracle unchanged by any fold | r5: oracle = best-single, both sets |
| free energy → work (reversible) | headroom → floor (safe fold, never below vote) | r6: ≥ vote in both sets, 0 degraded |
| free energy → heat (irreversible) | headroom dissipated by a judge outside competence | r4: 24 → 21, JEV 0.40 on counting |
| enters only across a boundary, at a cost | a new independent reader, booked | d043 (G20c, cost 3); d070 (JEV, cents) |
| **fails:** energy is additive | reach is a *union* (a lattice, not a sum) | adding a nested reader adds exactly 0 |

**EXTENSION:** the free-energy reading is mine; the code and the rounds exhibit the
conversions, the docs do not name them. The lattice caveat is why "conserved" must be read
as "the union does not grow under folding", not as a sum.

## 2. The one move — Look-Again

### 2.1 Statement

> **Look-Again.** When a fold's readers are nested on an item — everyone wrong together,
> or the judge outside its competence — **do not emit a verdict.** Book the miss as a
> content-addressed leaf. **Buy, at that address,** one reader whose reach is independent
> of the set's: a second implementation, a symbolic checker, a higher tier, the world, a
> human. **Book the price.** Fold again.

Three parts, in order, and the order is the point:

1. **Abstain** — refuse the unmarked verdict. This is Law 6 (iii)'s polarity turned on the
   fold's own output: a verdict the evidence does not reach is an unrepresentable leaf, and
   unrepresentable leaves are refused, never rounded (G20a; `cellFor` throws; `book()`
   raises; `nextD` never floats).
2. **Address** — content-address the miss. The miss itself is a leaf (`item_root`, the
   readers that missed, the domain). Now the gap has an address that a stranger can
   recompute — which is what makes step 3 *targetable*.
3. **Buy** — acquire an independent reader *at that address*, and book the cost. Only this
   step raises the oracle (Law 7 iii). Steps 1–2 are what let you afford it: complementarity
   is scarce, so you cannot buy readers blind — the whole S2 program bought six readers blind
   and measured zero. The universal miss "onomatopoeia vowels" is a booked address where a
   forty-line symbolic counter has reach. That is a purchase you can make for nothing; the
   organism has simply not made it yet.

### 2.2 Why this is the ONE move (and not "fold better", "more models", or "train")

- **Folding better** is bounded by Law 7 (i): the safe fold is already at the oracle in
  r3/r6/r7. There is no better fold to find on those sets.
- **More models** is Law 7 (iii) with nested readers: zero, three times. Diversity is not
  the input.
- **Training** (S7) spends headroom into floor for one reader; it does not raise the
  oracle unless the training data came from outside the set — i.e., unless someone already
  did step 3.
- **Look-Again** is the only operation that changes *E*'s reach, and abstain+address is the
  only way to know *where* to change it. It is Law 6 applied to the fold's failure: carry
  the *miss* as evidence, never as a rounded verdict.

## 3. Q2 — the meter: one primitive, two directions, and what makes it a moat

### 3.1 One primitive

The deadband (O1), the Fable-deadband (O11), and the aha-budget are the same operator
because they ration the same thing: **crossings of a reach boundary.** O1 says: do not buy
a higher-reach reader while the local reader's reach suffices. O11 says the same one
altitude up. The aha-budget says: a stranger may buy the org's reach twelve times a day
(cap 12, §3.3), server-enforced, then the fold retreats to local reach (the game plays on
offline; ANSWER-tier runs on standing). Same shape everywhere:

```
crossing(reader, address) :  budget(reader) ≥ price  ⊢  buy independent reach at address
                             book { reader, address, price, remaining }
                             else  ABSTAIN → fold on local reach (degrade, never error)
```

The graceful degrade *is* Look-Again's first step worn outward: when the budget is spent the
surface abstains from live verdicts rather than faking one. d070 verified it at HTTP 200,
13th/14th call degraded, key never client-side. That is Law 6 (iii) on an economic wire.

### 3.2 Conservation of cognition is double-entry over crossings

Cognition is not conserved — a model can be run forever. **Reach** is what is scarce, and
what is conserved is the **book**: every crossing appears exactly once as a debit (the
reader's budget: KV bucket, Opus wake, Fable call) and once as a credit (the org's cost:
O7's cost-units, upstream `usage ~350in/35out`). O7 — "cost-per-passed-acceptance-test
falls" — is Law 7 priced: *the org learns to buy less reach for the same floor* (O2 standing
and O3 reuse convert past purchases into local reach). Honest reading stands as written in

window). Law 7 sharpens what to measure at d080/d120: not blended cost, but **reach bought

### 3.4 Is rationed intelligence a defensible product surface?

**Rationing alone: no.** "N free calls per day" is every AI product's freemium tier; a KV
bucket is an afternoon. If the moat were the meter, there would be no moat.

**What is defensible, and it is two things the organism already has and one it has not
built:**

1. **The calibrated, abstaining, receipted verdict.** Products hand strangers verdicts.
   This surface hands them *evidence plus its own reach boundary* (probabilities,

   is uncomfortable — it shows the judge not knowing. That discomfort is the product: the
   Tell said the pleasure of a wager is proportional to the trustworthiness of the ground,
   and a judge that admits its edge is trustworthy ground.
2. **The book.** Every cent is a replayable leaf. For the regulated buyer QTHE-REVOLUTION §4

   floats and rate-limits cannot meet.
3. **The two-way meter (not built).** Under Law 7 the org's own ceiling is set by the reach
   of its readers, and the stranger is a reader the org does not have — a human with local
   knowledge, staking on pencil. Today the meter runs one way: the stranger buys JEV's reach.
   Run it both ways — book the stranger's *fold* (did they stake ink or pencil; did they
   accept or dispute the verdict) as a leaf — and every few-cents crossing buys the org
   complementarity it cannot manufacture internally. The cents stop being a cost of
   acquisition and become **the price of an independent reader.** That is the flip that makes
   metered intelligence compound rather than merely convert: **the moat is the meter iff the
   meter runs both ways.** The Tell's "the moat compounds iff it IS the fun" is this
   sentence at the game altitude; O5 double-entry across the boundary is it at the ledger.

**Honest status:** the one-way half is proven live (d070; my call). The two-way half is
designed (the Tell's bounties; the telemetry hook counting pencil vs ink stakes) and
measured nowhere. **EXTENSION:** "moat iff two-way" is an economic argument from Law 7, not
