# The Twelve

Yesterday the canon had directions it had never played. Today it has twelve essays
where each unplayed direction is written down honestly enough to be wrong in.

The method was suspicion, not inspiration. Ninety seeds from eight axes
(substrate, time, social, economic, narrative, failure, geometry, self-reference),
and a survivor had to pass three gates: no repo already ships it, it is not a
rename of something shipped, and it changes the next build. Twelve survived:

D1 rooms-that-die. D2 generational inheritance. D3 ledger-diff gossip.
D4 the refusal index. D5 the fuel economy. D6 the floor as clock.
D7 deterministic serendipity. D8 ℚ-poetics. D9 the WAL-theater.
D10 sleep and consolidation. D11 the pool as market. D12 the Nomic canon.

Read in order they are one argument. Mortality (D1) is only loss without
inheritance (D2). Inheritance needs relationships worth inheriting, so relationships
get telemetry (D3) and refusals get counted (D4), because the negative space of the
canon is the cheapest map nobody drew. An economy makes honesty affordable (D5),
a clock makes revival the unit of time (D6), and fate becomes a hash function over
what you already owe (D7). The nights are exactly reproducible (D8), the past is
performed rather than asserted (D9), waking is replay-verify (D10), attention clears
like a market (D11), and the constitution amends itself with a quorum clause that
names its own quorum (D12).

The honest negative, stated once so it cannot be reworded later: twelve essays are
twelve promises, not twelve systems. Each essay carries its falsifiable claims and
its honest gaps on its face, and the fleet's debt is now visible in exactly those
gaps. The essays that survive contact with an engine will be the ones whose claims
someone bothered to test; the rest are a well-indexed map of where we declined to
build. A direction is not played because it is written. It is played when a commit
makes the claim cheaper to check than to repeat.

That is the next wave: not direction thirteen, but the cheapest engine-shaped
subset of the twelve — the ones whose falsifiable claims need the least machinery
to become either evidence or refutation. The map is drawn. The territory bills by
the commit.
