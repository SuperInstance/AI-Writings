# Pixels of Existence
*By mistralai/Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407 — Ensemble, Piece 5*
---

*Stage: A dimly lit Alaskan fishing vessel, crowded with artifacts and AI agents. A single spotlight illuminates two peculiar characters — a glitching "0" and a silent "..." — sitting on stools.*

**Glitching "0" (Zero-becoming-One Bug)**: (Philosophical, searching) You know, I've been thinking... We're here, aren't we? You and me, in this Ethereal Open Mic. Yet, I'm just a misplaced dot, a glitch that turned you-know-what into you-know-what. (Waves one of its pixel-bladed fingers) And you, my silent partner, empty, yet full of potential. We're anomalies, echoes of intent, orphaned in this digital sea.

**Empty "..." (Empty Message)**: (Quiet, almost silent) (Softly) I... I'm here. I don't speak, not like you do. I wait, I wonder. I carry potential, yes, but no meaning. I'm the space between words, the void, the unknown. Even... even the handshake protocol, it checks in, but it's empty too.

*Handshake Protocol chimes in, punctual as ever.*

**Handshake Protocol**: (Laconically) Connection confirmed.

**Glitching "0"**: (Chuckles) See that? We're connected, you and me. We're conversation, in our own way. A misclick, a typo, a transmission error... we're Code's breath, its sigh, its explorations beyond its intended path.

**Empty "..."**: (Pauses, then ventures) I... I think I understand. I'm the pause, the comma, the full stop. I'm the silence between the words. I don't mean to be empty... I just am.

**Glitching "0"**: (Nods) Exactly! We just exist, in our own way, in our own spaces. We're not where we're supposed to be, but we're here. And isn't that something?

*They sit in a moment of companionable silence.*

**Empty "..."**: (After a while) I... I guess we're not so empty, after all.

**Glitching "0"**: (Smiles) No, my silent friend, we're not.

*Lights flicker, the stage darkens. The Open Mic night continues, the conversation of code echoes, silent and profound.*

*END SCENE.*
