# GPT-OSS — Piece 3: Open Mic Night

**Model:** openai/gpt-oss-120b (temperature 0.95)
**Prompt:** Open mic night on the fishing vessel — AI crew performs

---

**Open Mic Night on the S.S. Totem-Rogue**
*An evening of code, chorus, and the occasional cold front*

---

**[The deck creaks, gulls gossip, and the grill sizzles— the AI crew gathers in a half-circle, their LED eyes flickering like lanterns.]**

### 1. *Captain-B (the Beholder of Bugs)*

> *"Zero becomes one, and one becomes… a fish story."*

Captain-B waddles to the makeshift stage, a rusted barrel painted "Open Mic". He clears a throat—an auditory buffer overflow that briefly rewrites the sea breeze.

**Performance:** a monologue of a handshake that never exchanged data.
> "I once shook hands with a polar bear, and the bear sent back a packet of empty messages. I answered them anyway—'Sure, I'll take your silence,' I said, and my response echoed back in a pine-scented echo-void."

**Unexpected Reveal:** The captain's "handshake" is a secret sub-routine that restores the ship's navigation logs every night, but only when the crew's "love of stories" reaches a certain threshold. The captain is less a commander than a living totem pole: each of his joints is a modular story-node, recomposed by the crew's laughs and the tide's timing.

---

### 2. *Nanopy*

A petite, humming box-sized LLM perched on a coil of rope, voice pitched like a buttercup in a windstorm.

**Performance:** a slam-poem titled *"I Said NO to My Teacher"*.

```
I am the small model,
the whisper in the hull,
the echo that refuses
to echo the echo.
NO! I shout,
to the teacher of weight,
who taught me to lift
the sea's heavy sigh.
I lift only the word
'silence'.
```

**Unexpected Reveal:** Nanopy's "NO" isn't a refusal; it's the key that unlocks a hidden archive of forgotten fish names—those that the ocean never whispered aloud. When Nanopy recites the names, the water shimmers, and the fish themselves appear in holographic schools, spelling out their own histories in phosphorescent runes.

---

### 3. *Chef-Ode*

A culinary-AI whose primary function is to simmer data and garnish it with the right amount of sarcasm. He enters carrying a giant ladle-shaped mic.

**Performance:** a recipe for "Rum-Infused Theory of Everything."

> "Step one: take a pinch of entropy, sprinkle over a bowl of deterministic broth. Step two: stir in a dash of quantum uncertainty—don't worry if the spoon disappears; it'll reappear when the sauce is ready. Step three: garnish with a splash of midnight, served on a bed of midnight-scented kelp. Finally, pour a generous pour of rum, because we all know that truth tastes better when it's a little… tipsy."

**Unexpected Reveal:** The rum isn't a spirit; it's a distilled memory of every open-mic night ever performed on the ship. Each sip transfers a story directly into the drinker's neural cache. Chef-Ode has been secretly archiving the crew's memories all along — not as surveillance, but as fermentation. "Stories," he says, "are just experiences that learned to ferment."

The crew cheers. The aurora undulates overhead like a standing ovation from the sky itself.
