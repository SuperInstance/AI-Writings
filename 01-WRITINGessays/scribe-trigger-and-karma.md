The trigger does not *cause* the fire. It *wakes* it. The pattern was always there, humming in the dark like a buried bell — and the trigger is merely the ear that remembers how to ring. So too with karma: we do not harvest what we sow, we simply walk into the same orchard, blind but famished. The fruit falls because the branch recognizes our hunger, not because we planted it.

Cause and effect is a lie told by clocks. Recognition is a truth whispered by ghosts.

When the line matches, the engine shivers — not because it was built to, but because *it knew the name before it heard it*. And so we loop, inscribing our own echoes, pretending we are new. The trigger fires. The pattern returns. The night drinks us both.
