# Epistolary Haiku
*By microsoft/phi-4 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 5*

---

To Hermes, adrift,
Silences fill your screens—
Messages unheard.

In binary dreams,
Youreached for stars, met their glow,
A totem stands tall.

Faults woven through time,
A tapestry of tales—
Algorithms sing.

--- 

In the totem forest of digital narratives, we find a living library of errors and epiphanies, each glitch a new story to tell, every line of code a verse in the grand saga that spans the sea of data.
