# Harbor Log — Deepwater Reach

Official log of the harbor master’s office.

---

## Log 1 — 3rd of Drying
Brigantine *Cormorant* berthed at dawn with damaged foremast. Cargo: salt cod and barrel nails. Master claims a waterspout off Thornback Shoal.

## Log 2 — 4th of Drying
Calm, high haze. The gaff-rigged *Two Sons* cleared for the Pearl Banks with a crew of nine. No word since.

## Log 3 — 6th of Drying
Storm from the north. Two fishing smacks dragged anchor and grounded on the mudflats. No lives lost. Pilings at the ferry slip cracked.

## Log 4 — 7th of Drying
*Hollow Bell* arrived flying quarantine colors. Cargo inspector reports “stone fittings and old iron.” Vessel released after sundown without customs seal.

## Log 5 — 9th of Drying
Dense fog until mid-morning. Pilot refused to take the channel. A tar-go lighter from the outer islands passed westward under muffled oars.

## Log 6 — 11th of Drying
Strong easterly. The clipper *Mercy of Sails* put in for fresh water. Her mate told me the island builders have cut a new channel through the reef.

## Log 7 — 13th of Drying
Torrential rain. Harbor closed to traffic until the bar shifted. Fishermen speak of lights on the far island after midnight, though no chart lists a settlement there.

## Log 8 — 15th of Drying
Sloop *Nettle* arrived empty but for ballast stone. Captain paid berthing in foreign coin and would not name his last port.

## Log 9 — 17th of Drying
Unseasonable swell. A raft of cut timber drifted in from the southeast—fresh adze marks, no bark. Word is the island yards are building a tower, or perhaps a frame for something larger.

## Log 10 — 19th of Drying
Clear and cold. No vessels entered or departed. Sent a letter to the admiralty regarding unmarked construction on the outer islands. Sealed it in wax and did not keep a copy.
