# Found Documents

Papers recovered from the Deepwater Reach archives and shore.

---

## 1. Blueprint with Notes
Sheet: “Hull Form 12 — Modified.”
Lines of the body plan redrawn in red ink along the midship section.
Margin note, lower right: *“Extend deadrise two feet. She must not wallow. — L.”*
Stain in the corner resembles old grease and boot-prints.

## 2. Supply Order
Received by the chandlery, date smudged:
- Oakum: 12 bales
- Pine tar: 4 casks
- Copper nails, 6-inch: 200 lbs
- “Lanterns, ship’s, enclosed: 6”
The last item is circled twice and the price struck through.

## 3. Tide Chart
Hand-drawn on sail canvas.
High and low tides marked for fifteen days.
Several entries corrected in a different hand.
On the reverse, in pencil: *“The water runs wrong on the twelfth. Check the channel again.”*

## 4. Child’s Drawing
Crayon on wrapping paper.
A tall boat with many windows.
Stick figures stand on the deck.
One figure is larger than the ship and drawn in black.
At the bottom, in careful letters: *“Papa works there.”*

## 5. Farewell Letter
Found inside a tin box beneath the south pier.

> *I am going with them to the island. The work is honest and the pay will clear our debt. Do not follow. Burn this when you have read it. I will send for you when it is safe.*
>
> *— Your husband*

No name. No seal. The paper is warped from salt water.
