# Lucineer Field Notes

Private journal entries from Lucineer, shipyard foreman at Deepwater Reach.

---

## Entry 1 — Ironwood Batch 7-A
The ironwood came in damp again. Third load this fortnight. Mortise joints won’t seat true when the grain swells like this.

## Entry 2 — Morning Fog
Fog sat thick on the estuary until noon. Couldn’t sight the guide lines from the gantry. Lost half a day’s layout work.

## Entry 3 — Keelson Crack
Found a hairline crack running three spans down the keelson of the merchant hull. If it takes a sea, the whole spine splits. Told no one yet.

## Entry 4 — Rains
Rain came horizontal. Tarps peeled back off Frame Four and the green oak drank it in. Warping already visible at the garboards.

## Entry 5 — Copper Sheathing Short
Copper sheets short by forty. Supplier claims the reef routes are fouled with mud-worms. I think someone is holding stock for the northern yard.

## Entry 6 — Gull Nests
Gulls nesting in the crane boom again. Dropped a mussel shell onto the caulker’s stage. No one hurt, but the men are spooked.

## Entry 7 — Night Shift
Heard a knocking from inside Hull Nine after dark. Empty hold. Could be driftwood against the skeg, but it sounded deliberate.

## Entry 8 — Rivet Batch
New rivets from the inland forge are too soft. Three heads sheared during test fitting. I’m sending the lot back under a false manifest.

## Entry 9 — Salt Stain
White bloom spreading across the new transom. Salt working out of the timber too fast. Either the wood was green-laid or the brine baths were rushed.

## Entry 10 — Wind Shift
Wind shifted southwest and brought the smell of tar fires from the outer islands. They’re building something out there. Something big.

## Entry 11 — Scarf Joint
Apprentice cut the scarf joint backward. I made him chisel it out by hand in front of the crew. Harsh, but a bad scarf sinks ships.

## Entry 12 — Spring Tide
Spring tide lifted the piling caps two fingers. The old slip is settling again. I’ll need divers before the next hull is stepped.

## Entry 13 — Lantern Glass
A lantern burst on the lower deck. Glass everywhere, but no flame caught. Lucky. The oil we’re using burns too clean to see.

## Entry 14 — Borrowed Tools
Hammer and beetle missing from the tool crib. Probably one of the island crews lifted them. Too tired to press it.

## Entry 15 — Final Inspection
Walked the yard at dusk. Every hull looked sound from the outside. That’s the lie good timber tells you.
