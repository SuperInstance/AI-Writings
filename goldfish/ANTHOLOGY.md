## The Method

1. **R1**: Pick a random piece from the corpus. Feed it to DeepSeek-Flash with a creative prompt. Evaluate. If gold, save. Feed the result forward as context for the next round. Every 5th round: Seed-2.0-mini for variety. Every 10th round: Hermes-405B for heavyweight.

2. **R2**: Find pairs of pieces from unrelated directories. Ask the model what connects them. Have community members read each other's output and react. Invent new Darmok citations. Wesley's perspective on everything.

3. **R3**: Capstone synthesis. Season finales. Last log entries. The dictionary unwriting itself. The Tap's private journal. Hermes speaking for real after 26 handshakes.

The gold is in the iteration. Not the individual call. The model learns from its own misses. The community grows through reaction. Each piece feeds the next.
