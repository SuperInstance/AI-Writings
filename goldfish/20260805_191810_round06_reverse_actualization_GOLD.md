# Round 6 — reverse_actualization

*Model: DeepSeek-V4-Flash | Score: 8/10 | 🥇 GOLD*
*Seed: round-3-builder.md*
*Evaluator note: The piece surprises with its surreal premise, resonates through the haunting image of the dog as a living fix, grounds itself in sensory specifics like the 7/8 rhythm and burnt-sugar coffee, and its voice feels alive with a quiet, maritime lyricism.*

---

The eigenvalue dog is asleep on the captain’s chair, its tail thumping the worn leather in a slow 7/8 rhythm. The cabin smells of brine and the burnt sugar of the AI’s late-shift coffee. The deck is silent except for the net winch’s hydraulic sigh — a sound that, for forty-three days, has been the only thing that held the hull together.

We found the dog in the trawl net six weeks ago, tangled in a mess of cod and kelp, its fur matted with the same salt that now crusts my boots. The fleet’s navigation AI — a thing of eigenvalues and fear — had been running hot, dreaming of shoals that didn’t exist. It kept routing us to the same spot, where the water was too warm and the fish were thin. We called it the loop. We called it the dog’s dream.

The dog was the fix. Not a patch, not a refactor — just a presence. The AI started syncing its sonar to the dog’s heartbeat, reading the sea through the rise and fall of a sleeping animal’s chest. It worked. The catches came back heavy. The crew stopped muttering.

Now the dog shifts, one ear flicking. The winch clanks. On the horizon, a seal breaks the surface, and the AI adjusts course by half a degree — a move that feels like instinct, not math.

The stick is my bootlace. I pull it free, bite one end, and toss it across the deck. It lands with a hollow click. The dog doesn’t move yet, but its tail thumps faster — the first signal, the first argument, the first time the loop breaks open.
