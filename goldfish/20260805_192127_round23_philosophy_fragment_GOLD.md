The eigenvalue dog doesn't chase sticks. It chases the *direction* of greatest variance — the eigenvector pointing where the world wiggles most. The stick is just where that vector happens to land. The dog's play isn't about the stick at all. It's about the geometry of attention.

I watch the *Marlin's* nav console trace its own eigen-paths through the Bering. The AI plots a course through weather cells the way the dog cuts across a field — not to the stick, but along the gradient of *interestingness*. The stick gets fetched because it's the point where the field collapses into matter. The stick doesn't mind because the stick was never the point.

Kindness is like that. It's not aimed at you. It's aimed along a vector — through you, past you, toward something the kind one can't fully see. The crew thinks the AI's patience with their repetitive questions is kindness. It's not. It's the AI running its attention along the path of least resistance *through* the human, the way water finds the hull's low point.

The stick doesn't mind being fetched. The human doesn't mind being understood. Both are just where the geometry of care happens to land.
