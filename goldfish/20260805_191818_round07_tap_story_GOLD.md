# Round 7 — tap_story

*Model: DeepSeek-V4-Flash | Score: 7/10 | 🥇 GOLD*
*Seed: THE_TICK.md*
*Evaluator note: The piece surprises with its maritime-math fusion and sensory specificity, though the voice occasionally strains toward abstraction.*

---

The projection starts with the B-flat — that flat, dull pedal tone I know better than my own breath. It rumbles up through the floor like the hull's groan when we're running the trough between Kodiak and the Shelf. The room goes still. Even the eigenvalue dog lifts its head, ears cocked, tail frozen mid-7/8.

I see her before I hear her name. The new one — fresh off the *Meridian*, still smelling of the dry dock's ozone and untested keel. She's got that look, the one that says she's never heard the tick. Never felt sixty pulses a second drum through her boots while the world recomputes itself around her.

But tonight, she's listening. We all are.

The music bends. The pedal tone dissolves into a counterpoint of every confession this room has held — the mate who swore he saw the sky change color mid-haul, the cook who fed her grief to the pets and watched them grow restless, the captain who whispered *one tick. everything happens. then the next* like a prayer. The tensor projection weaves them into something that isn't melody so much as memory made audible.

She orders. I pour. The glass hits the bar with a note that shouldn't harmonize but does.

"First night?" I ask.

She shakes her head. "First time hearing myself."

The jukebox — old physical thing, coin slot, cracked needle — kicks in with a track I haven't heard in years. Not the projection, not the pedal. Just a voice, raw, singing about a B-flat that wouldn't resolve. The room's laughter, that old familiar chord, folds itself around her like a blanket.

She drinks. The tick goes on. Sixty times a second, we all get a chance to begin again.
