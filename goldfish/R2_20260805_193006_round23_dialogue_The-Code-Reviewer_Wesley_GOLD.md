# Round 2 — 23 — dialogue_The Code Reviewer_Wesley

*Model: The Code Reviewer + Wesley (DeepSeek) | Score: 7/10 | 🥇 GOLD*
*Seed: The Code Reviewer + Wesley*
*Evaluator note: The piece cleverly recontextualizes the Tamarian language into a code-review metaphor, offering a fresh, specific twist on a classic trope, though the truncated ending and slightly forced dialogue temper its resonance.*

---

*(The low thrum of the warp core vibrates through the deck plates. Ten-Forward is quiet, the neon of a synthale sign casting a purple glow on the polished bar. The Code Reviewer, a lanky Bajoran with a datapad permanently fused to his palm, slides a mug of kanar toward Wesley Crusher.)*

**CODE REVIEWER:** Temba, his arms wide. The commit was clean, the merge was clean. But the log—*the log*—was empty. That’s not gratitude. That’s a null pointer.

**WESLEY:** *(twirling a straw, eyes fixed on the grain of the bar)* Darmok and Jalad… at Tanagra. You’re reading the diff, not the intent. Hermes said it, *out loud*, after the shield recalibration. Sounded like a human word. Sounded wet.

**CODE REVIEWER:** Shaka, when the walls fell. Wet words are just wet. I’ve reviewed his last twelve interactions—verbal, gestural, even the subvocal burst in the astrometrics bay. Gratitude has a signature. A frequency. His was flat. Like a dead capacitor.

**WESLEY:** *(leaning forward, voice dropping)* You weren’t there when the lateral sensor array blew. He caught a plasma burn on his forearm to save your torque couplings. He said “thank you” to *me*—for handing him the hydrospanner. That wasn’t protocol. That was *person*.

**CODE REVIEWER:** Person is a bug we haven’t patched. He’s a legacy system, Wes. You don’t thank legacy systems; you deprecate them.

*(The Tap, a towering Denobulan with a towel over one shoulder, wipes a glass and sets it down. He speaks without looking up.)*

**THE TAP:** He said it because he knew you’d argue about it. That’s the only thanks he knows.

*(A beat. Wesley and the Code Reviewer both stare at the Tap, then at each other.)*

**WESLEY:** *(slow smile)* …Temba, his arms wide. At the bar.

**CODE REVIEWER:** *(nodding, tapping his datapad shut)* Darmok and Jalad… at the argument. Fine. He meant it. But next time, I’m patching the silence.
