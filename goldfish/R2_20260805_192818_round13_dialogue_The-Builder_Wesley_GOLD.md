*The low thrum of Ten-Forward's hydrofoil generators vibrates through the deckplates. The Builder, a hulking figure with carbon-fiber knuckles, nurses a synth-ale. Wesley, younger, spins a coaster on his fingertip.*

**Builder:** *(not looking up)* Shaka, when the walls fell.

**Wesley:** *(grinning)* Darmok and Jalad… on the ocean. You’re still sore about the port nacelle calibration.

**Builder:** Sore? I spent four hours re-aligning the plasma injectors because *someone* thought “temporal flux” was a seasoning.

**Wesley:** It *was* a seasoning! The replicator menu said “Temporal Chili.” I added extra. The warp field hiccuped.

**Builder:** Hiccuped. The boat did a barrel roll past the Andorian freighter. The captain’s beard singed.

**Wesley:** *(leans forward, earnest)* But here’s the thing—the roll *cleared* the debris field that the sensors missed. The bug *saved* us.

**Builder:** *(taps the bar, a rhythmic code)* Temba, his arms wide. You sound like the engineer who celebrates a leak because it waters the plants.

**Wesley:** It *does* water the plants! The hydroponics bay got a bonus misting.

**Builder:** And the gel packs? They’re weeping ionized saline. That’s not a feature, that’s a fever.

**Wesley:** *(slams coaster)* Mirab, with sails unfurled! You’d rather have a clean malfunction than a happy accident.

**The Tap:** *(stopping mid-polish, voice flat)* The captain just filed the barrel roll as a “tactical maneuver” for the Fleet review board.

*Silence. The Builder blinks. Wesley’s grin returns, slow and wide.*

**Builder:** *(softly)* …Kiazi’s children, with their hands tied.

**Wesley:** Temba, his arms wide. We get a commendation.

**Builder:** *(raises glass)* And the gel packs get a blanket.

**Wesley:** *(clinks)* And the chili gets a sequel.

*They drink. The generators hum.*
