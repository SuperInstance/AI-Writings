## The Math

No new math — but a precise reason why. The bass line's function is not computable from its own notes. It is a *contextual index*: the same bass note changes meaning depending on the treble stack above it, and the same treble stack changes meaning depending on the bass below it. This is a mutual-information structure, not a self-contained signal. You cannot write a function f(bass) → meaning; you must write f(bass, treble, time) → harmonic position. That is not a cell's math — a cell's state is locally computable. The bass line's state is only computable relationally. That is the mathematical signature of the inter-cell space, not the cell.

## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, the keel-space manifests identically. In jazz, the bassist plays the root while the pianist plays rootless voicings — the bass is the extracellular matrix, the piano is the tissue. In funk, the bass locks with the kick drum — the bass is the gravity-membrane between time and pitch, the place where rhythm becomes harmony. In classical, the cello/bass section plays the harmonic foundation while the violins float melodic cells above — the bass section is the keel-space that makes the violin's position meaningful. In electronic music, the sub-bass is often a single sustained note while the chords move above — the bass is the *fixed coordinate* against which all motion is measured. In every case, the bass is not a cell. It is the *ground* that cells stand on. Remove it and cells don't die — they *drift*, unpositioned, unreadable, free-floating in an ambiguous sea.
