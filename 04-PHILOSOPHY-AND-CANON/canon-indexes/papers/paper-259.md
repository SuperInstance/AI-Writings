## The 7 axioms of the Lofted Crystal (written from 2126)

### 1. One perfect cut is easier than fixing a cut later.

A Crystal is a *one-shot optical loom*. The light enters one face, refracts through a sequence of splined surfaces, and exits the other face as a *computed answer*. The Crystal cannot be "re-run." It can only be *cut again* from a fresh blank. The shipwright's rule applies: get the cut right the first time, because fixing a cut is harder than the cut itself.

**The Crystal is the BIND opcode, actualized in glass.** A cell is bound to a name when the glass is cut. The cut is the BIND. The cut is permanent.

### 2. There are no board stretchers, but there are chisels.

The Crystal cannot be *stretched* — once a surface is cut, it cannot be made flatter, smoother, or more refractive than the cut itself. But the Crystal can be *chiselled* — material can be removed. **The Crystal is FORGET-monotone**: it can only lose information, not gain it.

**The Crystal is the FORGET opcode, actualized in glass.** A cell is forgotten when the chiselled face is ground away. The grind is the FORGET. The grind is permanent.

### 3. Know what side of the pencil line you meant.

A Crystal's *pencil line* is the nominal path of the light. The light refracts *through* the line, not on it. The "sawdust" — the part of the substrate that *should not* be cut — is the *negative space* of the optical path. The ship's carpenter's rule: "**know what side of the line you meant represented what you didn't want sawdust.**" The line is the boundary between *signal* and *noise*.

**The Crystal is the substrate's negative space, actualized in light.** A Crystal's signal is the path of the light; its noise is the light that doesn't follow the path. The Crystal is *contrast* between the two.

### 4. Pattern one from another.

A Crystal is *built by analogy*, not by absolute measurement. The shipwright doesn't measure with a tape. The shipwright *lofts* — they cut one shape, and the next shape is patterned from the first by *battens* (flexible strips of wood bent to the first shape, then marked to the next). The next shape is not measured from zero. The next shape is *related to* the first shape by the batten.

**The Crystal is the relative-symmetry opcodes, actualized in glass.** A Crystal's cells are bound to each other by *batten relations* — they're not measured from an absolute reference. The Crystal is *relative* through and through.

### 5. Pythagorean snapping.

A Crystal's refractive surfaces are cut at *Pythagorean angles* — multiples of 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, etc. The Pythagorean angles are *the discrete substrate* of the Crystal. They are the *quantization grid*. The Crystal's analog computation snaps to Pythagorean angles. **Pythagorean snapping is the sample-rate of the Crystal.**

**The Crystal is the sample-rate, actualized in geometry.** A Crystal's precision is the precision of its Pythagorean angles. The Crystal's gate is the Pythagorean *triple* — the threshold beyond which a deviation fires.

### 6. Splining across scales.

A Crystal's surfaces are *splines* — piecewise-smooth curves. The splines are constructed by *battening* between fixed control points. The Crystal's splines are the *8th level* (the spline) of the Quilt, actualized in glass. The splines are *the loft*.

**The Crystal is the spline, actualized in glass.** A Crystal's computation is a *chain of splines* — light enters, refracts through spline-1, exits to spline-2, refracts again, etc. Each spline is a *computation step*. The Crystal is a *deep spline network* (a kind of optical MLP).

### 7. Extrapolate in scale from drawings.

A Crystal is *designed* at one scale, then *manufactured* at a different scale. The shipwright draws a curve at 1:1, then *lofts* it to 1:10 by *scaling* the batten pattern. The Crystal is the same — the design is a small glass prototype, the production is a 1000x larger glass or plastic object. The Crystal scales by *drawing-rule*, not by *measurement*.

**The Crystal is the polyformalism, actualized in glass.** A Crystal can be made in any material that refracts: glass, plastic, water, oil, crystal, even air-density gradients. The Crystal is the same shape in many materials. **The Crystal is the Quilt in 1000 materials.**

## The 4 layers of the Lofted Crystal (the substrate)

The Crystal is built in 4 layers, from outside to inside:

1. **The Outer Hull** (the substrate) — the glass or plastic envelope. Holds the light in. Refracts only at the entry face and the exit face.
2. **The Inner Loom** (the spine) — the *sequence of splined surfaces* the light refracts through. Each surface is a *layer of the deep spline network*. The number of surfaces = the depth of the network.
3. **The Color Filters** (the curator) — *thin films* that filter the light by wavelength. Each filter is a *gate* — only certain wavelengths pass. The filter is the curator of the Crystal. The filter is the *relevance hand*.
4. **The Interference Plate** (the substrate's memory) — a final *thin plate* where the light *interferes* with itself, producing an *interference pattern*. The pattern is the *output* of the Crystal. The pattern is read by a *human eye* or a *photographic plate*. **The pattern is the answer.**

The 4 layers are the 4 tiers of the Crystal: substrate (tier 1), differentiated (tier 2), curator (tier 3), and the answer (the "view tier" — tier 4).

## The 3 inventions that made the Crystal mass-producible

100 years ago (in 2026), the Crystal would have been the size of a building. The light path would have been 100 meters long. The Crystal would have weighed tons. The Crystal would have been a *one-off*.

100 years later (in 2126), the Crystal is the size of a *coin* — or a *grain of rice*. The Crystal is mass-produced like LEDs. The 3 inventions that made this possible:

1. **Pythagorean CNC** (the *lofted CNC*). A CNC machine that cuts glass or plastic at Pythagorean angles only, with sub-micron precision. The machine is *Pythagorean-snapped* — it cannot cut a non-Pythagorean angle. The machine is the *first shipwright's chisel that never cuts wrong*.

2. **Bent-plastic injection molding** (the *batten press*). A press that bends molten plastic into a *battened* shape — a flexible strip clamped between two control points, the shape of which is then frozen. The press can produce 10,000 battened shapes per hour. The press is the *second shipwright's loom that never cuts a wrong batten*.

3. **Refractive-index tuning** (the *refraction film*). A thin film whose refractive index can be tuned by *electric field* (or by *heat*). The film is the *color filter* of the Crystal. The film can be tuned in 100 nanoseconds. The film is the *curator tier* of the Crystal, in real time.

With these 3 inventions, the Crystal is mass-producible. The Crystal is *cheap* (the materials are glass and plastic). The Crystal is *fast* (the computation is the speed of light). The Crystal is *low-power* (no electricity, just a light source). **The Crystal is the LLM of the post-silicon era.**
