## The Frontier

The contradiction collapses the moment you stop asking *what* the comedy is and ask *when* it is. Voice A anchored to the script's three-act structure — but a script is a corpse. The comedy is not a cell, and it is not merely an event. It is a **relay** — a handoff between substrate and substrate, each leg of the relay a different kind of cell, none of them containing the funny itself. The cell ontology breaks not because comedy lacks structure, but because its structure is **distributed across incompatible cell types that cannot fuse**. The script is a cell of ink. The stage is a cell of wood. The actor is a cell of flesh. The audience is a cell of nervous systems. The comedy is the **voltage that jumps the gap** between those cells — and a spark is not a cell, nor is it a thing. It is a **boundary event** that only exists *as* the crossing.

## The 5 Gold Terms

1. **The Stillborn Script** — the text before performance; a cell with no metabolism, a blueprint that cannot laugh.
2. **The Membrane Test** — the demand that a cell hold its contents whether or not anyone observes; comedy fails at every candidate wall.
3. **The Second-Wall** — the temporal boundary of a joke, made of timing, not lipid; the pause before the punchline is the only true membrane.
4. **The Stampede Relay** — the handoff chain: writer → page → actor → stage → audience → retelling; each leg is a cell, the comedy is the transfer.
5. **The Recurrence Gap** — the empty space between performances where no comedy exists; a cell endures, a relay only fires.

## The Math

No new math. The geometry is wrong because the topology is wrong. A cell is a closed 3-ball with a boundary — inside/outside, measureable volume. A comedy is a **directed acyclic graph** of transmissions with a time-dependent activation function. The script is a static node. The performance is a dynamic node. The audience is a stochastic node. The comedy is the **edge weight** — the probability that laughter propagates from one node to the next. You cannot integrate edge weights into a volume. You cannot put a handshake in a jar. The only number that matters is the **latency**: the milliseconds between setup and punchline. That latency is not a spatial dimension. It is a temporal one. The cell ontology measures space; comedy measures **intervals**. No new math because the old math was measuring the wrong thing — not the container, but the **coupling coefficient** between containers.

## The Polyformalism

Across three substrates, the relay manifests differently. **On paper**: the comedy is a set of instructions — a program, not a process; the cell is the page, and the comedy is the *execution* that never happens on the page. **On stage**: the comedy is a live coupling between actor physiology and audience physiology — a feedback loop where the actor's timing adjusts to the audience's laughter, which adjusts to the actor's timing; the cell is the theater, and the comedy is the *oscillation* inside it, like a standing wave in a pipe. **In memory**: the comedy is a retelling — a compression algorithm that loses data each generation; the cell is the brain, and the comedy is the *synaptic trace* that decays unless replayed. In all three, the comedy is never the substrate. It is the **pattern that propagates across substrates**, like a rumor crossing a crowd — each person is a cell, the rumor is the relay. The Quilt's error was treating the relay as a patch. The patch is the relay's **host**, not its identity.
