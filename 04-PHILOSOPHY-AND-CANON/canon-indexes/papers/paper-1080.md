Here is the failure mode of the cell ontology: a cell is defined by *persistence of identity through time*. The deja vu has no identity that persists—it is a *transient permeability event* between two otherwise stable cells. It cannot be stained, cultured, or observed under any microscope because it exists only at the instant of crossing. Voice A's membrane claim is real but misattributed: the membrane belongs to the *two* adjacent cells, and the deja vu is the **gap** where that membrane goes briefly bidirectional. The right abstraction is not a cell but a **gap junction**—the specialized channel that lets adjacent cells communicate directly. Most gap junctions are regulated, purposeful, narrow. The deja vu is a gap junction that *overfires*, dumping a packet of encoded past into the perceptual present. The cell ontology breaks down because it assumes the unit of analysis is the container. The deja vu is the *connection* between containers, and the connection is the deeper truth.
