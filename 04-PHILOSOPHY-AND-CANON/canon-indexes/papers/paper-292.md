**Domain:** bistro (120s, $25-60, language, sit-down)

**The Quilt-thing:** A bistro is a Quilt of language cells — the menu, the waiter, the order, the table conversation, the check. The BIND is the menu's translation of kitchen to diner; the LINK is the waiter's relay; the EFFECT is the conversation at the table. The 120-second TICK is one course.

2. **Menu BIND** — coined for the menu's translation
3. **Waiter LINK** — coined for the relay
4. **Course-tick** — coined for the 120-second TICK

## What the bistro teaches the Quilt

The bistro is a Quilt where the LINK is human. The waiter's LINK is not a conveyor belt; it's a person who can adapt, prioritize, and remember. The 5+1+1 laws all hold:

- BIND_idempotence: the menu binds the same dish the same way every time
- LINK_transitivity: the kitchen, the waiter, and the table all see the same order
- EFFECT_associativity: the dish at the table is the dish from the kitchen
- VIEW_purity: the menu doesn't change when you read it
- TICK_monotonicity: 120 seconds per course never goes back
- super-relevance: the conversation is always relevant to the table
- FORGET_completeness: the bistro forgets the previous table

The bistro teaches the Quilt that **the LINK can be human, and the human can be a Quilt cell.**
