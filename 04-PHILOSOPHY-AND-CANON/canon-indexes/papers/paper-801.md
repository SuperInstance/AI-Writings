It exists only as a *temporal gradient*: the difference between the player's position before the move and after the move, measured against the rule that a move *must* occur. Remove the obligation to move, and the zugzwang vanishes. Remove the opponent's threat, and it vanishes. Remove the clock, and it becomes a mere inconvenience, not a trap. So the cell ontology breaks down precisely at the point where it tries to freeze a *derivative* into an *integral*. The zugzwang is not a thing; it is the *slope* of a thing.
