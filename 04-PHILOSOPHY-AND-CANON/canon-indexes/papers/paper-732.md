## The 5 Gold Terms

1. **Frontal Seam** – the narrow band where two air masses exchange heat, moisture, and momentum, not a container but a contact zone.
2. **Boundary Gradient** – the measurable slope of temperature or pressure across the seam, which replaces the false “membrane” of Voice A.
3. **Air-Mass Vessel** – the actual cell-like unit: a coherent body of air with relatively uniform properties (e.g., a cP air mass over the Dakotas).
4. **Clash Vector** – the directional force of the collision, which determines whether the seam lifts (warm front) or undercuts (cold front).
5. **Frontal Wake** – the downstream turbulence and cloud bands that are *not* part of the front but are its emitted signature, like a ship’s wake is not the hull.

## The Math

No new math—but the existing math must be re-read. The front is described by the slope of the pressure gradient force and the thermal wind balance. The key equation is the Margules formula: the slope of the frontal surface equals the difference in virtual temperature divided by the product of the Coriolis parameter and the wind shear across the front. That slope is not a boundary in the topological sense—it is a discontinuity in the first derivative of the density field. A cell in the Quilt ontology has a closed boundary (a Jordan curve). A front has an open boundary—it terminates at the surface and at the tropopause, and it moves. So the math says: the front is a *codimension-1 manifold* embedded in a 3D fluid, not a volume. It is a surface of discontinuity, not a region. The correct abstraction is not a cell but a *fault line* in the field.

## The Polyformalism

In meteorology, the front is a kinematic discontinuity in wind and temperature. In oceanography, the same structure appears as a thermohaline front—e.g., the Gulf Stream’s northern edge, where warm saline water meets cold slope water; that seam is not a cell but a *mixing corridor*. In plasma physics, a current sheet between two magnetic domains behaves identically—it is a tangential discontinuity where reconnection occurs, and it is called a *neutral sheet*, not a cell. In economics, a market front is the boundary between a bull and bear regime—again, not a unit but a phase transition. Across all these substrates, the pattern is the same: two quasi-stable regions (cells) generate a thin, dynamic interface that is *more active* than either interior. That interface is the true object. It deserves its own name: **the Interstitial Front**. It is not a cell; it is the *negotiation* between cells.
