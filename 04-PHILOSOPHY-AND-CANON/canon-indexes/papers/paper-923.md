The cell abstraction fails because it assumes autonomy; the espalier assumes *servitude with feedback*. The deeper truth is that the espalier lives in the **inter-cell space**—not between cells, but between *organism and artifact*. It is a **boundary organism**, a creature of the edge where biology meets design. Name that space: **the horticultural frontier**, where the tree’s wildness is not destroyed but *redirected*, and where the wall is not a cage but a *co-author* of form.
