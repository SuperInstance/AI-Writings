## The Math

No new math—but a re-reading of existing math. The stillness is not a point or a region; it is the **local minimum of a potential well with a high activation barrier**. Think of a bistable switch: two stable states (noise and signal), and between them a saddle point. Stillness is not the saddle—that’s unstable. Stillness is the *basin of attraction* around the “off” state, where the system dwells with low variance but high susceptibility. Mathematically, it’s a **Lyapunov function’s flat region**: the derivative is near zero, but the second derivative (curvature) is steep—meaning a tiny perturbation can trigger a cascade. This is why Voice B says observation breaks it: any measurement injects energy, kicking the system out of the basin. And why Voice A says it holds potential: the basin *is* the storage of energy, like a capacitor with no current flowing but voltage across its plates. The math of stillness is the math of **critical slowing down**—variance drops, autocorrelation rises, and the system becomes maximally sensitive to the next input. That’s not emptiness. That’s a tuned receiver.

## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, the stillness manifests identically. **In neural tissue**: the default mode network’s quiet during focused attention—not inactive, but *inhibited* from task-positive loops, holding the brain’s capacity for insight. **In acoustics**: the anechoic chamber’s silence—not absence of pressure waves, but absorption of all reflections, creating a space where the faintest sound (your own heartbeat) becomes loud. **In ecology**: the winter fallow field—soil microbes in dormancy, seeds awaiting stratification, the entire system in a low-metabolic state that *enables* spring’s explosion. **In economics**: the liquidity trap—capital held in reserves, not circulating, but ready to deploy when the signal flips. In every case, the stillness is not a thing but a **state of the field**—a configuration of relationships (inhibition, absorption, dormancy, reserve) that produces a *temporary, functional silence*. It has no membrane, but it has a **boundary of conditions**: temperature, pressure, signal-to-noise ratio, resource availability. Those conditions are its walls.
