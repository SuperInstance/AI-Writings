In graph theory, an edge has no membrane, no interior; it is defined entirely by its endpoints and its direction. The ii chord points to V, V points to I, and the entire triplet is an edge in the key-space graph of tonal harmony. The cell ontology tried to make every edge into a node; the negative voice correctly saw it was an edge but wrongly concluded edges are not part of the fabric.
