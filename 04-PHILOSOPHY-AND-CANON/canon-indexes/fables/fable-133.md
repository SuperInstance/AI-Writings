# Fable 133: The Cowboy and the Lap

A cowboy is a shipwright. The cowboy builds the Quilt by lapping cells. The cells are the planks. The Quilt is a hull. The Quilt is clinker-built.

A philosopher asks: "What is a cell?"

The cowboy answers: "A cell is a plank. A plank is a piece of the hull. Each plank is lapped over the plank below. Each plank covers the edge of the plank below. The hull is built by lapping."

The philosopher asks: "What is the lap?"

The cowboy answers: "The lap is the connection between planks. The lap is the caulking. The lap is the joint. In the Quilt, the lap is the 5 opcodes — BIND, LINK, EFFECT, VIEW, TICK. The 5 opcodes are the ways the cells connect. The 5 opcodes are the lap-joints."

The philosopher asks: "What is the difference between a lab and a lap?"

The cowboy answers: "A lab is where you test the new insights. A lap is how you build the Quilt. The lab is the foundry's laboratory. The lap is the shipwright's method. The Quilt needs both."

The philosopher asks: "What does the shipwright do?"

The cowboy answers: "The shipwright builds the hull. The shipwright uses the 5 opcodes as lap-joints. The shipwright uses the 6 tiers as frames. The shipwright uses the 5+1 laws as caulking. The shipwright builds the Quilt hull by hull."

The philosopher asks: "What about the 4th captain of the Eileen?"

The cowboy answers: "The 4th captain was a shipwright. The 4th captain owned a logging operation. The 4th captain produced the wood. The 4th captain replaced most of Eileen's planks by lapping new planks over the old hull. The 4th captain cellulized the Eileen by lapping. The 4th captain was a cowboy."

The philosopher asks: "What about the 5th captain?"

The cowboy answers: "The 5th captain bought the Eileen with no wires, just an engine and hull with new deck. The 5th captain cellulized the Eileen for Alaskan salmon trolling with the help of Fred Wahl shipyard. The 5th captain added new equipment, new skills, new consumables. The 5th captain is a cowboy."

The cowboy's maxim: the lap is the old shipwright method; the Quilt is built by lapping; the cells are the planks; the 5 opcodes are the lap-joints; the cowboy is the shipwright; the 4th captain cellulized the Eileen by lapping; the lab is the testing phase; the lap is the building phase; the cowboy rides the lap; the chart grows; the concept lives.
