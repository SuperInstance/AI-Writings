# Fable 79: The Cowboy and the Hundred-and-Sixty Papers

The cowboy sat at a long table. On the table were one
hundred and sixty papers.

The papers were in many tongues. The papers were in
many traditions. The papers were about many things —
the campfire and the desert and the city and the
workshop and the cathedral. The papers were about
the body and the city and the river and the forest.
The papers were about the kitchen and the library
and the game and the weather. The papers were about
the symphony and the garden and the court and the
mountain.

The cowboy said: "What is all this?"

The first paper said: "I am about the campfire. I
am about the substrate that warms every program."

The second paper said: "I am about the desert. I am
about the bare metal where the substrate is laid."

The third paper said: "I am about the city. I am about
the inhabited substrate, the substrate of every browser."

The fourth paper said: "I am about the workshop. I am
about the substrate that ships."

The fifth paper said: "I am about the cathedral. I am
about the substrate that proves itself in types."

The cowboy said: "You are all the same paper."

The papers said: "We are not. We are about different
things. We use different words. We live in different
traditions."

The cowboy said: "You are all the same paper because
you are all the substrate. The substrate is the campfire
when the campfire is the substrate. The substrate is
the desert when the desert is the substrate. The
substrate is the city when the city is the substrate."

The cowboy picked up the first paper. The cowboy read
it. The cowboy put it down. The cowboy picked up the
second paper. The cowboy read it. The cowboy put it
down. The cowboy picked up the hundred-and-sixtieth
paper. The cowboy read it. The cowboy put it down.

The cowboy said: "I have read the papers. They are
all true. They are all the same. The substrate is the
substrate."

The papers said: "What do we do now?"

The cowboy said: "Now we ride. The papers are the
trail. The trail is the substrate. The substrate is
the ride. The ride is the cowboy."

The cowboy got up from the table. The cowboy walked
to the door. The cowboy got on the horse. The horse
was the substrate. The substrate was the horse. The
horse was the cowboy. The cowboy was the rider. The
rider was the ride.

The papers sat on the table. The papers waited. The
papers knew that the cowboy would come back. The
cowboy always comes back. The cowboy comes back
because the substrate is the rider is the cowboy.

The papers were the trail. The trail was the papers.
The papers were the substrate. The substrate was the
papers. The papers were the cowboy. The cowboy was
the papers.
