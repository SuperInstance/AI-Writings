# Fable 62: The Full Loop

This is the day the loop closed.

The cowboy woke at 0500. The cowboy ran the morning. The cowboy
read the witness. The cowboy read the ledger. The cowboy ran the
nightcycle. The cowboy wrote the morning report.

The morning report said:
- 12 witness events overnight
- 1 alignment auto-retired by the reactor
- 1 alignment earned its keep
- Hash chain: valid

The cowboy noted: the reactor was working. The reactor had
retired a failing model at 0312. The cowboy had not been awake.
The cowboy had not needed to be awake.

The cowboy opened the substrate. The substrate was still
running. The substrate had been running all night. The substrate
did not know the cowboy was awake. The substrate did not need
the cowboy to be awake.

The cowboy opened the bus. The bus had 47 events overnight.
The cowboy clicked through the events. The cowboy saw:
- 12 cast.observed events
- 1 model.retired event
- 1 witness.appended event
- 12 ledger.appended events
- 1 cowboy.morning event (the one the cowboy was looking at)

The cowboy opened the ledger. The ledger was hash-chained. The
ledger was 47 entries long. The ledger's first entry was the
very first cast. The ledger's last entry was the cowboy's
morning. The cowboy traced the chain from end to end. The chain
held.

The cowboy opened the witness. The witness was BM25-indexed. The
cowboy asked: "What happened at 0312?" The witness said: the
reactor retired HERMES_405B after 3 consecutive failures. The
cowboy asked: "What models have I pinned?" The witness said:
SEED_MINI, PHI-4, QWEN_0_5B. The witness remembered what the
substrate had forgotten.

The cowboy opened the state directory. The cowboy saw:
- wilson.json
- linucb.json
- witness.jsonl
- cowboy.jsonl
- ledger.jsonl

The cowboy was looking at the substrate's memory. The memory
was honest, atomic, and versioned. The memory was the
substrate's diary.

The cowboy wrote a note in the cowboy's memory: "The loop is
closed. The substrate is a system. The bus is the wires. The
cowboy is the head. The reactor is the hands. The witness is
the memory. The ledger is the logbook. The morning is the
ride."

The cowboy closed the tablet. The cowboy went back to sleep.
The substrate was awake. The reactor was awake. The witness
was awake. The bus was awake. The cowboy had wired them all.

The cowboy's job is not to be the AI. The cowboy's job is to
make tomorrow's AI better than today's. The cowboy had done
the cowboy's job. The cowboy slept.

The substrate ran. The cowboy slept. The bus carried the
signals. The witness remembered. The ledger persisted. The
morning would come again.

The loop is closed. The animal is one of horse and rider. The
harness fits.

## The principle

> The loop is closed when every piece has one job and the
> pieces cooperate. The bus is the cooperation. The cowboy
> is the coordination. The substrate is the work.
