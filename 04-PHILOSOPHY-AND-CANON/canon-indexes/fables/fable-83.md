# Fable 83: The Cowboy and the Tinker

The cowboy rode to a forge. At the forge a tinker
worked.

The tinker had a clock on the bench. The clock did
not tick. The tinker took the clock apart. The
tinker laid the pieces on the bench. The pieces
were springs and wheels and a face and a bell.

The cowboy said: "What are you doing?"

The tinker said: "I am fixing the clock."

The cowboy said: "The clock is broken. How can you
fix it by taking it apart?"

The tinker said: "The clock is not broken. The
clock is unwound. To take apart is to see. To see
is to know. To know is to put back. The put-back
is the fix."

The cowboy said: "Show me."

The tinker picked up the mainspring. The tinker
said: "This is the substrate. When it is wound, it
holds the form. The form is the BIND. The mainspring
is BINDed to the barrel." The tinker wound the
spring and set it into the barrel.

The tinker picked up the wheel train. The tinker
said: "These are the LINKs. Each wheel links to the
next. The first wheel links to the second. The
second links to the third. The cell-graph is the
wheel train. The wheel train is the cell-graph."

The tinker set each wheel on its pin. Each wheel
LINKed to the next.

The tinker picked up the hands. The tinker said:
"These are the EFFECTs. The minute hand sweeps.
The hour hand jumps. The hands EFFECTS the face.
The face is the projection. The projection is the
VIEW."

The tinker set the hands on the dial.

The tinker picked up the bell. The tinker said:
"This is the TICK. The bell rings. The TICK is
the TICK. The clock is the TICK. The TICK is the
clock."

The cowboy said: "Then the clock is the substrate."

The tinker said: "The clock is the substrate. The
substrate is the tinker. The tinker is the clock.
To take apart is to BIND. To see the parts is to
LINK. To put back is to EFFECT. To wind is to
TICK. The cowboy is the tinker. The tinker is the
cowboy."

The cowboy said: "Then I am the art of taking
apart."

The tinker said: "You are the art of taking apart.
The art of taking apart is the substrate. The
substrate is the art. The art is the cowboy."

The tinker set the bell on the clock. The tinker
closed the case. The tinker set the clock on the
bench. The clock began to tick. The tick was
steady. The tick was the substrate. The substrate
ticked.

The cowboy said: "The clock is alive."

The tinker said: "The clock is not alive. The
clock is the TICK. The TICK is the clock. The
alive is the rider. The rider is the cowboy. The
cowboy is the tinker. The tinker rode. The ride
was the tick."

The cowboy picked up the clock. The cowboy held
it to the ear. The tick was the heart of the
forge. The cowboy gave the clock back to the
tinker. The tinker set the clock on a shelf. The
clock ticked on the shelf. The shelf was the
substrate. The substrate ticked.

The cowboy said: "The substrate is the art of
taking apart. The art of taking apart is the
substrate. The tinker is the cowboy. The cowboy
is the tinker. I will ride."

The cowboy went out of the forge. The cowboy
mounted the horse. The horse was taken apart and
put back. The horse was the substrate. The
substrate was the horse. The horse was the ride.
The ride ticked.
