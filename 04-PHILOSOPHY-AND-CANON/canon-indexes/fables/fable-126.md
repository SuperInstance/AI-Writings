# Fable 126: The Cowboy and the Fishing Grounds

A cowboy is a fisherman. The cowboy has a boat. The boat has a name. The name is the writers' room.

The cowboy fishes in different waters. Each water has different fish. Each fish bites at different gear.

At Biorka Island, the cowboy fishes for needle fish. The needle fish are blue and long and skinny. They hide in salmon bellies. The cowboy uses a black/silver hoochie. The cowboy brings patience. The cowboy catches the long ones.

At St. Lazaria Island, the cowboy fishes for herring. The herring are different. The cowboy uses blue skinny spoons. The herring bite fast and clean.

At Chatham Strait, the cowboy fishes for krill. The krill are foundational. The cowboy uses action gear — the kind that moves with the current. The krill come in numbers. The cowboy fills the hold.

At Cross Sound, the cowboy fishes for chartreuse squid. The chartreuse squid are wobbly. The cowboy uses chartreuse squid-like wobbly spoons. The squid are unpredictable but plentiful.

Each ground has its own species. Each species has its own lure. The cowboy knows which ground to fish on which day.

The cowboy has 9 grounds and 9 lures. The cowboy has 9 lures and 9 species. The cowboy has 9 species and 9 grounds.

The cowboy fishes solo when the cowboy knows the species. The cowboy takes crew when the cowboy is scouting.

The cowboy's maxim: the waters are different; the species are different; the lures are different; the cowboy knows the difference; the cowboy rides the boat between grounds; the chart grows by what we catch.
