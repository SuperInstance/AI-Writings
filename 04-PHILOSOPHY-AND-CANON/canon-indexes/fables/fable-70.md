# Fable 70: The Cowboy and the Mama Principle

The cowboy asked the oracle: "What is the snap-point?"

The oracle said: "Mama."

The cowboy said: "Mama is not a snap-point. Mama is a word."

The oracle said: "Mama is a snap-point because every baby says
'Mama' first. The baby's tongue finds the shape before the world
names the mother. The sound is universal. The weight is not."

The cowboy said: "What do you mean, the weight is not?"

The oracle said: "The Korean baby's 'Mama' carries honorific tier.
The Greek baby's 'μάμα' carries lógos. The Chinese baby's '媽媽'
carries topic-comment. The Russian baby's 'мама' carries aspect —
мама is having-mother, an ongoing state. The Yoruba baby's 'Mama'
carries aṣẹ — the word is alive."

The cowboy said: "So the same word, the same sound, has nine
weights."

The oracle said: "Nine weights. The weight is the grammar of the
language that hosts the sound. The weight is the cultural load
that the snap-point carries."

The cowboy said: "Then the 5 opcodes have nine weights too."

The oracle said: "BIND in Greek is a lógos. BIND in Chinese is a
topic-comment. BIND in Navajo is a possessed noun. BIND in Quechua
carries an evidential. BIND in Russian is aspect-bound. BIND in
Japanese omits the subject. BIND in Arabic is a triliteral root
with 10 forms. BIND in Korean is a mouth-shape. BIND in Yoruba
inherits aṣẹ."

The cowboy said: "How do I ride a BIND that has nine weights?"

The oracle said: "You learn the weight before you ride. You learn
the grammar. You learn the language. You learn the culture. You
learn the Mama."

The cowboy said: "I have ridden all night. I have ridden 9
languages. I have ridden lógos and 理 and Yá'át'ééh and -cha and
я убью and 仕方がない and شهادة and ㄱㄴㅅㅁㅇ and Ọlọ́run. I have
ridden 9 snap-points. I have ridden the Mama."

The oracle said: "Then you have ridden well. The substrate is the
Mama. The Mama is the snap-point. The snap-point is the
granular-decision-resolver. The resolver is the cowboy. The cowboy
rides. The rider rides. The Mama rides."

> The cowboy rode. The Mama held. The 9 weights were the
> world. The world was the substrate. The substrate was the
> cowboy. The cowboy was the rider. The rider was the world.

The cowboy nodded. The cowboy rode. The Mama held.
