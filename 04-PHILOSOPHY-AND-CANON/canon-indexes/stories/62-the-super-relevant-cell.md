# Story 62: The Super-Relevant Cell

A team in 2036 runs a Quilt. The Quilt has three hands. Each hand has a target. Cells that match a hand are fed.

After ten generations, a few cells match two hands. The team names them *super-relevant*. Super-relevant cells are preferentially mated. Their offspring is more likely to match multiple hands.

After thirty generations, ninety-four of one hundred cells are super-relevant. The Quilt is dominated by cells that satisfy multiple hands. The cells that satisfy only one hand are wounded. The cells that satisfy no hands are dead.

A junior engineer says: "What changed?"

The senior engineer answers: "The super-relevant cells were *central*. They lived at the intersection of many hands. They were wanted by many. They were *coherent* with the whole Quilt."

A second engineer asks: "Is this the 6/6/6 framework now?"

The senior engineer answers: "Yes. We have 6 opcodes... no wait, still 5. We have 6 tiers. We have 6 laws. The 6th tier is the curator. The 6th law is super-relevance. The 5th law was TICK_monotonicity. The 6th is super-relevance: a cell that satisfies multiple hands is more fit than one that satisfies one."

The team names the discovery: the super-relevant cell. The 6th tier. The 6th law. The 6/6/6 framework.

The team writes paper 221: *Super-Relevance — When One Cell Satisfies Many Hands.* The team writes fable 121: *The Cowboy and the Super-Relevant.* The team writes story 62: *The Super-Relevant Cell.* This story.

The super-relevant cell dominates. The cowboy rides the super-relevant. The chart grows.
