# A Walk Through the Embedding

*A walk, the collection's sixth form. Not a poem, not a story, not a guide,
not a play, not a glossary: a traversal. One surveyor, one space, eight
stations, and a rule that neither chooses the path.*

---

## The protocol, said once

A surveyor walks an embedding space. At each station it reads the record
there and writes a field note — at most two sentences, grounded in what it
read, carrying its memory of every note it has already written. The note is
vectorized by a second model that reads nothing else. The nearest unvisited
record to that vector becomes the next station. The surveyor does not choose
the path. The space does. It cannot revisit anything; it must leave every
station it reaches.

The surveyor was DeepSeek. The vectorizer was JEV, a fixed battery of
eight questions. The space was live, twenty records on a free tier. The seed
was the fleet's oldest claim: that distant cells holding the same value find
each other.

Seven retrievals. No re-rolls.

## The survey log

**Station 1 — N1, the non-local channel.**
*Retrieved next at similarity 0.932.*

> First station: a 64×64 torus where planted twins separated by ≥24 cells
> found each other in every wormhole-on run and never with wormholes off,
> locating the coupling in the compile-time 64-slot table rather than any
> traversed path. The channel reads as non-local because the link is
> resolved in the table's indexing, not in the grid's adjacency.

**Station 2 — Q4, the ablation record.**
*Retrieved at 0.945, the closest approach of the whole walk.*

> Wormholes off here don't just fail to connect — they strip the plateau's
> suppression, letting the ratio climb to 1.58 and flattening the early dip
> to a shallow scrape. So the channel acts as a drain on the Moore-8 rule's
> own slow memory, stirring structure away rather than feeding it, and the
> geometry only holds if I keep it shut.

**Station 3 — P3, the cascade.**
*Retrieved at 0.871.*

> The checkerboard cascade does move something across the torus, but as a
> wave, not a gate — phase crosses the surface in alternating Attract/Repel
> bands with no cargo carried and no threshold to cross. The pi/4 boundary
> is an artifact of measurement, not a switch: transfer continues underneath
> it, so the cascade behaves like the channel at my second station,
> propagating a disturbance rather than selecting anything through it.

**Station 4 — P2, the sigma sweep.**
*Retrieved at 0.760.*

> Sigma sets the clock but not the crossing: completion time contracts as
> 1/σ while bridge formation stays flat, so the claimed log2(3) threshold is
> a null and only the rate law survives. This reads like the checkerboard
> cascade — a graded disturbance moving through the sweep, with no switch at
> any particular σ to cross.

**Station 5 — A2, the reweighting probe.**
*Retrieved at 0.938. The walk has entered the economics family, and will
stay three stations.*

> At family A's reweighting probe, the quality-correlated oracles never once
> edged out plain max-of-accepted — a flat zero win-rate and a median six
> points worse, so the added scoring machinery only spent cycles without
> selecting anything better. This is the same story as the σ sweep and the
> cascade: a graded apparatus running at cost, with no threshold anywhere
> for the reweighting to cross.

**Station 6 — A4, the verify tier.**
*Retrieved at 0.834.*

> At r15, the expensive verify tier lost both axes at fifty times the cost —
> 57.1% recall against 55.6% false-BAD, worse than the cheap battery's
> 71.4/30.2 and the loop's 81.8 — and gating it behind the cheap tier
> dragged recall down to 42.9% because the two tiers fail on different
> items. Like the reweighting probe and the σ sweep, the cascade buys no
> threshold, only compounded failure modes, so the r13 decision to skip it
> stands.

**Station 7 — A1, the docket.**
*Retrieved at 0.619, the longest jump of the walk.*

> At family A's tiled arbitration docket, the debounce only reshuffles
> timing — starvation holds at 0.4435, identical to the plain heap inbox —
> while the physics bundle's drop to 0.32 rides on a 33% p99 penalty (845
> vs 633). Only the flood cap selects anything real: source-aligned tiles
> pin the flooder at 0.339 share against the heap's 0.75, a genuine
> threshold the other machinery lacks.

**Station 8 — P4, the end.**
*The walk ends here: eight stations, four families, one loop not closed.*

## What the space did

The itinerary ran N → Q → P → P → A → A → A → P. It crossed from the
oldest claim to its ablation in one step — the two closest vectors in the
whole space are a finding and the experiment that killed its mystery — then
walked the local-rule family, then stayed in the economics family for three
stations, the longest stay of the walk, and ended at the far end of the
local-rule family at the walk's longest jump.

Two things the trace shows that no one chose:

The surveyor found a thesis. Nobody told it the collection's subject; the
records did. From station 3 onward every note returns to one observation —
waves without gates, clocks without crossings, apparatus without
thresholds — until the last note finds the only genuine threshold in the
docket's flood cap and marks it as rare. The surveyor read eight records
and wrote its way to the fleet's recurring finding: most of what looks
like selection is disturbance. It even cross-referenced its own stations,
unprompted, by memory.

The drift compounds. Each note stayed between 0.93 and 0.98 cosine with the
station it was written at — the surveyor grounds its notes, it does not
wander in its prose — and yet the walk traveled from the non-local family
to the far side of the local-rule family. The distance came from the
accumulation of small residuals: each note is almost its station, and
"almost" points somewhere new. The path is not made of jumps. It is made of
the parts of each note that were not about its station.

The walk never returned to the seed's family. One traversal does not show
the space open in that direction — only that this walker, which cannot
turn around, did not come back.
