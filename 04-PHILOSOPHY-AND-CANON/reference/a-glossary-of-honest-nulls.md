# A Glossary of Honest Nulls

*Entries from the qthe fleet's working vocabulary, defined as they are
used. Cross-references are load-bearing.*

---

**burn-in, n.** The period a substrate must run before you are allowed
to trust what it tells you about itself. Before burn-in, every
statistic is a rumor with a hometown. Our current estimate is five
hundred and twelve ticks, which is another way of saying: longer than
you were hoping, shorter than the truth, and revised upward whenever
anyone looks closely. See *slow mode*.

**certified, adj.** Carrying paperwork. A certified random number is
behaviorally indistinguishable from an uncertified one — we measured
that, at every checkpoint, on every metric — which makes the paperwork
the entire difference. We keep the paperwork. See *witness*.

**dies, v.** The best thing a claim can do for you. A claim that dies
has done its work: it made a prediction, the prediction had
consequences, the consequences were measured, and the measurement said
no. A dead claim is furniture you can stand on. A claim that refuses
to die — that keeps hedging, keeps "holding under further study" — is
the one to walk away from. See *lives*.

**floor, n.** The level below which you have agreed to stop
interpreting. Usually the ninety-fifth percentile of noise, which
sounds rigorous until you remember it is a number your own randomness
produced on a Tuesday. The floor is not the absence of signal. The
floor is the amount of signal you have decided you cannot afford to
believe in.

**honest null, n.** What a slow mode looks like when you have run out of
patience but not out of ticks. Distinguishable from convergence only
by running longer, which is the definition of a bad deal. Our plateau
sits sixteen to twenty percent above the floor and has held that
grudge for four hundred ticks.

**probe, n.** A question you ask a system when you already know the
answer, to find out whether the system does. The value of a probe is
not the answer. The value is the calibration: after enough probes you
know exactly how surprised to be when the real question comes.

**receipt, n.** A record that proves itself. Weak documentation says
what happened; a receipt recomputes what happened from bytes that
cannot quietly change. If a claim cannot produce its receipt, the
fleet's official position is that the claim is a story. Many claims
are stories. Some stories are even true. The receipts will tell us
which.

**seat, n.** A model with a job. Seats are canaried before they are
trusted, priced before they are consulted, and quoted exactly —
*as-said* — never paraphrased into respectability. A seat that cannot
produce a single outside-the-box observation is not a reviewer; it is
an audience.

**slow mode, n.** The part of a system that forgets last. Every
dynamical system has one: the memory that outlives the party.
Ours carries spatial correlation, which is to say the shape of how
things were arranged when they were made. The fast modes — marginals,
fractions, rates — are all small talk. The slow mode is the
conversation the substrate is still having with its own construction,
five hundred ticks in, in a voice just above the floor.

**twin, n.** Two cells that have never met, holding the same value in
the same slot of the same table, and thereby — through a mechanism
that is either physics or bookkeeping, and which the receipts decline
to separate — affecting each other. The wormhole's entire social
life. See *wormhole*.

**witness, n.** A number that vouches for other numbers. Our Bell
witness vouches for quantumness with a value of 2.835 against a
classical ceiling of 2, and carries, in the same receipt, the honest
caveats: fixed settings, no spacelike separation, fair sampling. A
witness without caveats is a character reference written by the
defendant.

**wormhole, n.** The only non-local thing in an otherwise perfectly
local life. An abstaining cell reads and writes a slot keyed by its
value, and a distant cell holding the same value reads the same slot,
and this is the whole mechanism. It is also, we learned this quarter,
a *dissipator*: the channel that carries correlation away. The
universe's non-locality is not a storage locker. It is a drafty
window. You can close it — the flag is right there in the tick call —
and the house remembers longer, which is not the same as being warmer.
