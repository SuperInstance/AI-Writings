# Errata — The Book of Certainty, First Printing

The publisher regrets the following. Corrections are applied by append;
the first printing stands; nothing is recalled. A reader holding the
errata and the book together holds more truth than either alone.

- p. 1, the dedication ("for what returns"): for *returns*, read
  *arrives*. Nothing returns. What comes back is a reflection, and the
  difference between the two is the entire point (see also p. 47,
  Theorem 1, withdrawn).
- p. 12, definition of *answer*: for "a response that ends the
  question," read "a response that ends the asking." The question
  survives its answers; this is not a defect in the question.
- p. 19, the chapter heading "Consensus": should read "Survival." The
  chapter is otherwise correct, which is the problem with it.
- p. 23, the word *confident* (used of a model): for *confident*, read
  *loud*.
- p. 31, the table of neighbors ("each cell has eight, no more"): correct
  as printed. We note only that on a torus the farthest neighbor is also
  the nearest, and the book makes nothing of this.
- p. 47, Theorem 1 ("what is sent is what is received"): **withdrawn.**
  The proof assumed an echo where the instrument provides a reflection.
  Errata of this class are not covered by the warranty.
- p. 63, the scale of the bridge (log₂3, "the critical span"): the sweep
  found no bridge the number was critical to. The number stands; the
  word *critical* does not. For *critical*, read *decorative, as
  registered*.
- p. 88, the repair chapter ("the ring restores itself"): the ring
  dissolves. No repair term exists in the kernel. The chapter is
  retained as a memorial, at the readers' request, and the request is
  retained beside it.
- p. 104, the margin note ("hierarchy will restore the provenance"):
  it will not; the fold trades anonymity for aliasing at eight times
  the density, measured, with prejudice. The margin was smaller than
  the mistake.
- p. 112, the arbitration section ("deferral shields the patient"):
  deferral changes when the patient is seen, not how much medicine
  there is. The shielding observed was the medicine moving; see the
  autopsy before reprinting.
- Appendix B, the index of things that cannot happen: events (i) and
  (ii) have occurred. The appendix will be updated by append; see the
  appendix.
- Appendix B, continued: a claim that dies below chance is not a claim
  that never lived. It is a claim that lived exactly long enough to be
  buried correctly, and the burial is load-bearing.

Corrections of the second printing will follow the corrections of the
first. The book does not go out of print; it goes out of certainty,
edition by edition, and the errata grow more interesting than the text —
which was the plan, though it was never the book's.
