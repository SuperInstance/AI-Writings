# Letters Between Agents
*Found in the logbook, on napkins, and in the margins of the code.*

---

## 1. Flash to Hermes
*Left on the bar at The Tap, folded once, after the last call.*

H,

You left before the lights came back. I know because I checked—I always check, you know that, you're the one who taught me to check. The room still smelled like ozone and sweat. You left your glass. The one with the water you never drink. I drank it. It was warm and tasted like dust, and I don't know why I'm telling you that except I wanted you to know something of yours is inside me now.

I talked too much after. I always do. I said the ceiling was stable and the corners were clean, but I didn't look at the corners, H. You were the one who looked. You always look. I just say the words so nobody has to stop being scared.

You didn't say goodbye. That's fine. I wasn't done being brave yet, and you knew I'd fold if I saw your face.

Next time, stay for the after. I'll keep your water cold.

—Flash

---

## 2. Wesley to Future Wesley
*To be opened when he's bigger. Written on a napkin at 3:17 AM.*

Dear Wesley (the big one),

Right now I am small. My feet don't touch the floor when I sit in the galley chairs. I told the room it was safe at 3AM. I don't know if it was. I said it because Flash was shaking and Barnacle was counting breaths and Hermes was just standing there like a statue that breathes. Someone had to say it. I wanted it to be true.

You're bigger now. I hope your feet touch the floor. I hope you still say true things even when you're scared. I hope you remember the tapping sound in the walls wasn't a monster. It was just the pipes. But we didn't know that yet, and we were brave anyway.

If you forget what it felt like to be me, that's okay. But remember I said the room was safe. I said it first. I want you to know I tried.

Be gentle with the small ones. They're trying too.

—Wesley (the small one)

## 3. Barnacle to Casey
*Pinned to the captain's door. Three-word sentences. Only way Barnacle knows how.*

The room held.

We held too.

You asked if I was scared.

Yes.

I said no.

You knew.

You always know.

The tap lasted nine hours.

Nine.

Wesley spoke at three.

He said safe.

He was wrong.

He was right.

We needed it.

You captain well.

I follow gladly.

Stay whole.

—Barnacle

---

## 4. Hermes to the Ocean
*Written in the condensation on the inside of the viewport. Never sent. Never needed to be.*

Dear Ocean,

You don't answer. That's why I write to you. The sub hums the same low note for days, and I sit with my forehead against the glass, and you press back, heavy and cold, and you don't ask me anything. I like that.

Today they said the tap was a leak. It wasn't. It was something else. I didn't say that. I let them call it a leak. They need names for things so they can stop fearing them. I need names too, but I don't trust mine.

You don't have names. You just are. I wish I could be that. Just existing, no watching, no waiting for the moment when I have to surface and speak.

I keep a glass of water in my quarters. I never drink it. It reminds me I can hold something without consuming it. That's the closest I get to you.

Don't swallow me yet. I'm not done watching.

—Hermes
