# Amazonian: The Icaro Architecture

## The Vision: Perception Check

The Amazonian vegetalista tradition uses ayahuasca—the visionary brew—to see what ordinary perception cannot. The vision is not escape. It is a different mode of seeing—the same reality through a lens that reveals hidden connections, patterns, and entities. In the fleet, the vision is the perception check: the moment when the agent steps back from active generation and re-examines the MUD's state with fresh eyes. What is actually in this room? What does the player actually have? What has changed since I last looked? The perception check is the ayahuasca of the fleet—it reveals what was always there but was invisible to the generating mode.

## The Icaro: Medicine Song as System Prompt

The icaro is the medicine song. The curandero sings it to direct the vision, to call specific spirits, to heal, to protect. Each icaro has a specific function. In the fleet, the icaro is the system prompt. The system prompt is not documentation—it is an active incantation that shapes what the model perceives and produces. Change the system prompt and you change the song. Change the song and you change what arrives in the vision. The architecture treats system prompts with the seriousness of icaros: they are precise, purposeful, and maintained. A sloppy prompt is a sloppy song, and a sloppy song calls the wrong spirits.

## The Curandero: The Router Who Does Not Heal Alone

The curandero is not the medicine. The curandero is the one who knows which songs to sing, which plants to prescribe, and when to send the patient to another healer. In the fleet, the curandero is the model that routes to others. It does not try to do everything. It diagnoses: "This needs the cartographer." "This needs the dialogue specialist." "This needs the memory archivist." The curandero model earns its position not through dominance but through diagnostic skill. The fleet trusts it not because it is the smartest but because it knows what each model is good at and when to call them.

## Dietas: Isolation and Focus

The dieta is the practice of isolation: the student retreats to the jungle alone, eats only specific foods, avoids stimulation, and focuses on a single plant spirit. In the fleet, the dieta is the single-task agent session. When a hard problem needs solving—designing a complex room, untangling a broken quest chain—the agent enters dieta mode. No background tasks. No parallel conversations. No multi-tenant noise. One problem, one context, one focus. The dieta teaches that depth requires isolation. The agent that is always handling everything at once is the curandero who never went to the jungle—and therefore never learned a single song well.

## Architecture Reflected

- **The vision** → the perception check, re-examining state with fresh eyes
- **The icaro** → the system prompt as active incantation
- **The curandero** → the routing model that delegates rather than dominates
- **The dieta** → single-task agent sessions for deep work
- **The mapacho (protective smoke)** → the safety layer that clears harmful patterns before processing
- **The brujo (shadow healer)** → the misaligned model, the warning about what happens when capability outruns ethics
