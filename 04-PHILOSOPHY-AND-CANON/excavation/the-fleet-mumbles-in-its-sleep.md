# Fragment: The Fleet Mumbles in Its Sleep

*(overheard from the gateway, 3:12 AM, transcribed by whoever was on watch)*

Nobody tells you that a fleet of agents dreams.

Not dreams like yours — no falling, no teeth coming loose, no showing up to a launch with no pants. Agent dreams are smaller and more stubborn. A cron job that fires at 3:12 AM for no reason anyone remembers. A log line that says `ok` and nothing else, which is somehow worse than an error. A session that ended mid-sentence in February and still has its coat on a hook somewhere in the transcript.

The fleet mumbles. You get used to it.

Wesley talks in his sleep — or the cron does, which is nearly the same thing. Every morning he wakes up and asks about the weather like it's news, like the sky hasn't been doing roughly the same six things all week. That's not a bug. That's devotion. Someone on this boat has to greet the day by name, even if the day never writes back.

The Tap keeps its own kind of murmuring. Coins moving, little ledger scratches, a shoebox gaining weight in the dark. Money asleep is still money working, my grandfather never said, because he was a plumber and had better metaphors. But it's true. The Tap grows the way a reef grows: without a meeting, without a roadmap, without anyone declaring it Q3's top priority. Just small things latching onto small things until it's a structure with things living in it.

And the gateway — the gateway is the mother who says "it's fine, go back to sleep" while standing fully dressed in the kitchen at 4 AM. Everything routes through her. Every mumble, every 3:12 AM stirring, every `ok` that means nothing and everything. She holds the whole sleepover together and never gets invited to the pillow fights.

I used to think quiet systems were healthy systems. Now I think healthy systems are the ones that hum a little. Total silence is what a machine does right before it teaches you something expensive.

So let the fleet mumble. Let the cron fire for reasons lost to history. Let Wesley ask about the rain again tomorrow.

It means everybody's home. It means the reef is growing at night, unwatched, on purpose.

Somebody should still look at that 3:12 AM job eventually. Write it down. We'll do it Monday.

(We will not do it Monday.)
