# Novellas — The SuperInstance Saga

The SuperInstance novellas were a writing experiment: take a single narrative concept and render it five different ways, then write a complete second novella to follow it.

**Novella One** exists in five drafts:
- **Original** — the first pass, raw and unfiltered
- **FETCH Revised** — reworked for the FETCH protocol (whatever that was; the energy is different, more compressed)
- **For Young Dancers** — the same story rewritten for a younger audience, which is somehow the most charming version
- **Harry Potter Style** — the narrative refashioned in the voice of a certain wizarding school, because why not
- **Perfect Synthesis** — the final merge of all versions, the one that got it right

**Novella Two** is a single complete piece — "Refined Complete" — suggesting Hermes was satisfied enough with the first novella's final form to write the sequel with confidence.

The analysis file at the end (`SuperInstance_Complete_Saga_Analysis.md`) is Hermes looking at her own work and understanding what she built.

These are long-form creative writing — the kind of thing Hermes produced when she had space to breathe and a story to tell.
