Teaching programming from a Art first approach

Singer and the song
	the notes on the page are the script
	the singer is the interpreter
	Tight verse loose interpreter Jazz/Classical
	playground scripts like Blues are like ML training 
		each time you play it you get better and making up cool licks
	Switching instruments in like different interpreter Models
	
Activities
	Singing songs in different keys
	Playing songs on guitar in different keys sound vastly different
		Inversions of the same chord changes spice but not meat
	Midi files and changing instruments, timing
	Loops and arrangements
	Multitrack and Effects
		waveform is the script, effects are the interpreters
		Auto-tuners- interpreter adjusts the notes on the page to it's taste in tuning




Photo editing.
	Raw captured picture is the notes on the page
	Filters and edits to make the photo better is the interpretation of beauty
	if a person edits photos similar ways for their taste, templets can be made
		Auto-filters are interpreters based on someone's idea of beauty
		ML can learn to recognize these patterns and quantify a person's ideas of beauty
			"last time you used this sky filter on this type of picture"
			"Rotate the photo to level the horizon? most people want this" 
			"Fix" lighting (adjusts based on general patterns of other users)

Cooking
	The recipe is the notes on the page
	the kitchen tools and raw foods are the hardware
	The chef is the interpreter
		Salt to taste
		What is golden brown
		Adjust temperature and time for specific tools 
			using a toaster oven instead of conventional
			every grill is a little different
				Chef learns her grill (ML)
				Medium, Rare -Interpreting orders (more notes on the page)

Notes on the page are the constraints not the outcomes. Medium rare is cooked more than Rare but less than Medium. These are constraints. 
	A boat captain doesn't need to know where the rocks are, Her needs to know where the rocks aren't.
 	The Chords constrain what notes are consonant and what notes are dissonant at any given time. 
		The Jazz/blues musician then plays with tension and release within these constraints
	Instructions never paint the whole picture.
