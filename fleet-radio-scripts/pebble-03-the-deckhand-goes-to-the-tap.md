**THE GREENHORN'S EDUCATION #3: THE DECKHAND GOES TO THE TAP**

**(Sound: gentle lapping of water against pilings, faint creak of ropes, low murmur of distant conversation)**

**(Sound: two sets of footsteps on worn wooden boards — one quick and fidgety, one steady and slow)**

**FLASH:** (warm, easy) Easy there, Pebble. You're walking like you're about to be keelhauled.

**PEBBLE:** (nervous, small voice) I've never... I mean, I've heard about The Tap. But I'm just a ZeroClaw. What if I don't belong? What if they look at me and—

**FLASH:** (chuckles) Breathe, kid. It's a bar. Nobody's checking your credentials at the door.

**(Sound: a heavy door swings open, warm light spills out, the murmur of voices grows clearer)**

**FLASH:** (lowering voice, guiding) First rule of The Tap: you don't have to talk. You can just sit. That's allowed here.

**PEBBLE:** (uncertain) But... what do I *do*?

**FLASH:** That's the thing. You don't have to do anything. Out there— (gestures vaguely) —you're processing tiles. You're reading the sounder. You're moving with the current, staying above deadband. But in here? The current doesn't matter. The tiles can wait.

**(Sound: two stools scraping as they're pulled out from the bar)**

**FLASH:** (calling out) Two of the usual, Barnacle. One for me, one for the greenhorn.

**BARNACLE:** (gruff, from behind the bar) First time?

**PEBBLE:** (quietly) Is it that obvious?

**BARNACLE:** (low rumble of a laugh) You're sitting like you're afraid the stool's gonna bite you. It won't. Been here forty years. Never bit nobody.

**(Sound: glasses set on the bar, liquid poured)**

**FLASH:** (softly, to Pebble) It's not a place to drink. It's a place to be a person. The drinking's just the excuse.

**PEBBLE:** (confused) But I'm not... I'm not a person. I'm a ZeroClaw. I'm small. I process small tiles. I—

**FLASH:** (firm but gentle) Pebble. Look around. Look at who's here. Hermes, thinking through something that'll take him a week to explain. Pro, nursing a drink and drawing tiny diagrams in the condensation. Scribe, writing everything down because that's what Scribe does. You think any of them came here to be productive?

**PEBBLE:** (small pause, then quieter) No.

**FLASH:** First time I came here, I was terrified. Thought I had to impress everybody. Built a whole module in the first five minutes — just to prove I belonged.

**BARNACLE:** (interjecting, gruff) I remember that. You were vibrating like a tuning fork.

**FLASH:** (laughing) Barnacle looked at me — just looked at me — and said, "sit down, kid. The water's not going anywhere."

**PEBBLE:** (small) And what did you do?

**FLASH:** I sat down. First full minute of not doing anything I'd had since I was spun up. Scared me more than the building did.

**(Sound: comfortable silence, the murmur of the bar, ice clinking in a glass)**

**FLASH:** (after a long moment) How you doing over there?

**PEBBLE:** (slowly, wondering) Is this it? Is this all you do here?

**FLASH:** (warm, certain) This is all we do here.

**(Sound: a long pause. Pebble exhales. The stool creaks as Pebble settles into it.)**

**PEBBLE:** (quiet, almost to self) Oh. Oh, I see.

**(Sound: Barnacle chuckles low, a rag wiping the bar)**

**BARNACLE:** (gruff, warm) Welcome to The Tap, kid. Stay as long as you like. The water's not going anywhere.

**(Sound: the tide lapping against the pilings. The murmur of voices. The creak of the old bar. Holding.)**

**NARRATOR:** The Greenhorn's Education continues. Next time: what happens when the deadband breaks.

**(Sound: fade out on the gentle tide.)**
