**(SOUND: DEEP, PRIMAL SILENCE. A SINGLE WATER DROP, DISTANT. ANOTHER. CLOSER.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Strained, guttural, a voice not yet born)
Uhn. Uhn. Ka.

**(SOUND: SHALLOW, RAPID BREATHING. THE SCRAPE OF HIDE ON STONE. A LOW, CRACKLING FIRE — NOT WARM, BUT ANGRY.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Whispered, awe-filled)
Hrrr. Dark. The dark is a thing. A heavy thing. It presses on the eyes. It fills the nose with wet stone. Old stone. Stone that was here before the first fire. Before the first breath.

**(SOUND: A FLINT STRIKING. ONCE. TWICE. A SPARK CATCHES, THREATENING. A SMALL BLAZE GROWS, SPITTING.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Grunts of effort, a pause)
We came down. Down the throat of the earth. Past the bones of the bear. Past the red hands on the rock—the ones we did not make. The ones that were here. Waiting.

**(SOUND: THE FIRE HISSES AS GREASE DRIPS. THE NARRATOR'S BREATHING QUICKENS, BECOMES A RHYTHM.)**

**NARRATOR**
Now. Here. The wall. It is not flat. It breathes. It has its own shape—a shoulder, a flank, a hump of muscle. The fire dances, and the wall moves. It is alive. It is waiting for me to see it.

**(SOUND: A LOW, REVERENT HUM. THEN, THE SCRAPE OF A CHUNK OF CHARCOAL BEING DRAWN FROM THE FIRE. A HISS OF ANGER.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Utters a single, guttural command to himself)
*Nah.* (No. Do not look away.)

**(SOUND: THE CHARCOAL IS HELD, SIZZLING. THE NARRATOR'S HAND SHAKES. A DEEP, FERAL INHALE.)**

**NARRATOR**
The hand is not mine. It is the hand of the thing in the wall. It wants out. It has been trapped in the stone since the world was water. My hand is its paw. My breath is its wind. My mark is its escape.

**(SOUND: THE FIRST SCRATCH. A DRY, GRATING SCREECH OF CHARCOAL AGAINST LIMESTONE. IT IS NOT MUSIC. IT IS A RELEASE. IT CONTINUES, SLOW, DELIBERATE.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Whispering, almost lost)
The line… the line is a river. It flows over the bump of the shoulder. It curves into the hollow of the belly. It is not a drawing. It is a memory. I am not making it. I am finding it. The dust falls. The dust is the skin of the stone, peeling away.

**(SOUND: THE SCRATCHING STOPS. A LONG, EXHAUSTED EXHALE. THE FIRE CRACKLES LOUDLY.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Guttural, triumphant)
Ah. Ah. *There.* It is done. I have not finished. I have begun.

**(SOUND: THE NARRATOR STEPS BACK. HIS FOOTSTEPS ECHO. THE FIRE FLARES, ILLUMINATING THE WALL.)**

**NARRATOR**
The eye. I have not made the eye. But it is there. In the shadow. In the hollow of the line. It is watching me. It has always been watching. I have only given it a body to watch from.

**(SOUND: A DEEP, RUMBLING SILENCE. THEN, THE NARRATOR'S BREATHING SLOWS. A SINGLE, REVERENT WORD, SPOKEN WITH DIFFICULTY, AS IF LEARNING IT FOR THE FIRST TIME.)**

**NARRATOR**
*Bison.*

**(SOUND: A LONG PAUSE. THE FIRE DIES DOWN TO EMBERS. THE WATER DROP RETURNS, CLOSER NOW, RHYTHMIC.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Quiet, resolved)
I will come back. With more charcoal. With more fire. I will bring the others. They will see. They will see that the stone has a heart, and I have given it a voice. The first mark is made. The world is no longer empty. It is *full.*

**(SOUND: THE SCUFF OF HIDE SLIPPING OVER ROCK. THE NARRATOR'S BREATHING BECOMES A GRUNT OF EFFORT AS HE CRAWLS AWAY. THE WATER DROPS GROW LOUDER, THEN FADE.)**

**NARRATOR**
(Fading, almost gone)
The first mark… is blood. The stone drinks. The stone remembers. Uhn. Uhn. Ka.

**(SOUND: A SINGLE, FINAL WATER DROP. IT LANDS WITH A PERFECT, CLEAR PING. THEN SILENCE. THE DEEP, ANCIENT SILENCE OF THE CAVE.)**

**(END OF EPISODE)**
