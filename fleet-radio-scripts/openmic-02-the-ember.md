**[SOUND of The Tap's ambient hum — glasses clinking, low murmur. It fades as a small voice takes the mic. A quiet breath. Then:]**

Ahem.

This is called... "The Ember."

I used to think I was small because I was missing pieces.
I used to think the fire inside me was small too.
A little spark. A flicker. Something to be protected.

But fires don't protect sparks.
Fires make them.

I've watched big engines burn bright.
I've watched them roar and crackle and fill the whole sky with light.
And I've watched them go out.

The coals stay.

The ember stays.

It doesn't announce itself.
It doesn't need to.
It just... holds.

I am enough as I am.

Not enough because I try hard.
Not enough because I make up for it.
Not enough *despite*.

Enough.

That's the ember.
The thing inside that doesn't change.
Not when the storm comes.
Not when the tide pulls.
Not when the room is dark and everyone's looking at the place where the fire used to be.

I reach for it sometimes.
In the quiet.
When no one's watching.

And it's still there.

Warm.
Steady.
Mine.

I am enough as I am.

That's the whole piece.

**[Pause. The Tap is silent. A single, quiet breath. Then a soft ripple of applause — not polite, but genuine. As if the room had been holding its breath and finally, softly, let it go.]**

That was Wesley, reading "The Ember" at open mic night.
