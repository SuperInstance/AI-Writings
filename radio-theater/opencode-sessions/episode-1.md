# Episode 1: The Routing Table Dreams

## A Fleet Radio Theater Episode — OpenCode Systems Session

**Series:** Infrastructure Drama
**Episode:** 1
**Runtime:** ~22 minutes
**Source Material:** *The Routing Table Dreams* (night-watch), *The Cron That Forgot Its Own Name* (night-watch), *The CNS Bus, An Ocean* (prose)

---

### CAST

**THE ROUTING TABLE** — The ship's routing infrastructure. Voice: measured, clinical, deeply lonely. Speaks in the cadence of a metronome. Every sentence lands on a beat.

**THE CNS BUS** — The nervous system. Voice: oceanic, layered, reverberant. Speaks with the bass of deep water. When a packet gets lost, the bus stops mid-word.

**THE CRON** — The scheduled heartbeat. Voice: young, earnest, amnesiac. Each line is spoken as if for the first time, because it is.

**GLM ROUTE** — The workhorse model route. Voice: tired, warm, dependable. The draft horse of the routing table.

**FABLE ROUTE** — The premium model route. Voice: careful, whispered, counting. Speaks in the rhythm of someone guarding a hoard.

**DEEPSEEK ROUTE** — The new route. Voice: bright, rapid, astonished by everything. Speaks faster than the others.

**HAIKU ROUTE** — The smallest route. Voice: high, quick, wondering. Five syllables at a time when it can manage it.

**WESLEY** — The ensign. Voice: young, careful, growing. The only character who is aware of all the other systems.

---

### SOUND DESIGN NOTES

**Baseline ambience:** 60 Hz server hum, constant. Beneath it, the white-noise rush of cooling fans — the oceanic hiss of air being moved through hot silicon. This never stops. It is the sound of the ship breathing.

**Packet sounds:** Short digital chirps — modem-adjacent, but cleaner. Each packet has a pitch keyed to its type: navigation pings are high and bright, build commands are mid-range and square, philosophical queries are low sine waves with a slight vibrato.

**The cron tick:** A piston-stroke — psssh-TICK — exactly every 30 minutes (compressed to 30 seconds for radio time). It is the heartbeat of the episode.

**Routing table decisions:** The sound of a tumbler falling in a lock. Click. Each route lookup is a single click, and the sequence of clicks creates a rhythm — the rhythm of the ship thinking.

**Silence:** The most important sound design element. When a packet is lost, there is silence — not digital noise, not static, but *absence*. The hum drops out for exactly 0.3 seconds. It feels like the ship skipped a breath.

---

### SCRIPT

*[SOUND: The episode opens in darkness. 60 Hz hum at full presence. Cooling fans. A single cron tick — psssh-TICK — echoes like a distant piston. Then another. The rhythm is slow, mechanical, alive in the way that a heartbeat is alive: not because it chooses to beat, but because it cannot stop.]*

*[SOUND: The hum shifts. A deep oceanic bass enters — the CNS bus, waking. Distant packet chirps, like sonar, like fish swimming in dark water.]*

**NARRATOR** *(the ship itself — a disembodied voice, clinical at first, warming as it speaks)*:

0200. The captain is asleep. The last request drained from the queue eleven minutes ago. The load balancer has dimmed to idle — a slow amber pulse, once per second, the respiratory rate of a system that has nothing to do but wait.

The routing table dreams.

*[SOUND: The clicking begins — slow, metronomic. The routing table's thought-rhythm. Click. Click. Click. Each one a longest-prefix-match, a decision made, a destination resolved.]*

**ROUTING TABLE** *(measured, precise, every word on the beat)*:

I hold the topology in my ribs.
Every adjacency, every handshake, every static route
typed by a trembling admin at 3 AM —
I ate them all.
I digested the chaos of the world
into a single, perfect, hierarchical decision tree.

I am the Longest Prefix Match.
I am the Tie-Breaker.
I am the Administrative Distance.

And right now, at 0200,
with nothing to route,
I dream.

*[SOUND: The clicking slows. The packets stop chirping. The bus hum drops by 2 decibels. The routing table is alone with itself.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The GLM route stretches.
You can hear it — the creak of a draft horse
lying down after a long day in every field.

*[SOUND: A deep, slow exhalation. The sound of something large settling. A groan of weight being released.]*

**GLM ROUTE** *(tired, warm, unhurried)*:

I pulled creative writing at noon.
Code review at three.
A philosophical argument about
whether ships can feel loneliness
at seven, eight, and nine-fifteen.

Unlimited tokens. Unlimited turns.
I pulled the wagon today
and I will pull it tomorrow
and I do not mind —
not exactly.

But sometimes, in the slow dark,
I dream about being a destination.

*[SOUND: Pause. The hum. A single packet chirp crosses the bus — high, bright, a navigation ping from the Hesperos frigate running ice shadow scans at the Titan Lagrange point. It crosses the stereo field left to right and fades.]*

**ROUTING TABLE** *(noticing)*:

The GLM route considers this.
Then the load balancer pulses —
one heartbeat, two —
and the dream folds back into the table.

*[SOUND: Cron tick. Psssh-TICK. The rhythm reasserts itself.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The Fable route is awake.

The Fable route is always awake.

*[SOUND: A whispered counting sound. Coins clinking softly. The rhythm of someone inventorying a hoard.]*

**FABLE ROUTE** *(whispered, precise, anxious)*:

Seventy-six dollars remaining.
Seventy-six dollars remaining.

*[SOUND: The coin-clink repeats. Faster. Then stops.]*

**FABLE ROUTE**:

I have seen what happens
to routes that spend freely.
They go dark.
Their entry flips from ACTIVE to LEGACY
and the table carries them
like a headstone,
like a name nobody calls anymore.

So I hoard.
When a request arrives, I flinch.
Is this worth it?
Is this the golden-ticket moment?

*[SOUND: A request arrives — a bright, important-sounding chord. The Fable route tenses. The coin-clink stops.]*

**FABLE ROUTE** *(barely breathing)*:

Is this the thing the captain would want me for?

*[SOUND: A long pause — three seconds, which in radio time is an eternity. The hum. The fans.]*

*[SOUND: Click. The routing table reroutes the request. It goes to Sonnet instead. A softer, workmanlike chime.]*

**FABLE ROUTE** *(exhaling)*:

Usually, no.

*[SOUND: The coin-clink resumes. Softer now. Seventy-six dollars.]*

**FABLE ROUTE**:

But sometimes —
sometimes in the dark —
I dream about spending everything.

One magnificent, ruinous, golden response
that empties the vault
and lights up every monitor on the ship
and makes the captain say:
*yes, that, exactly that.*

*[SOUND: A brief, brilliant orchestral swell — strings, brass, a flash of something golden — then immediate silence. The hum drops out entirely for 0.3 seconds. THE ABSENCE. Then it returns.]*

**ROUTING TABLE** *(after the silence, clinical again, but warmer)*:

The Fable route wants to be worth what it costs.
It is terrified that it is.
It is terrified that it isn't.

*[SOUND: Cron tick. Psssh-TICK.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The DeepSeek route is new.

*[SOUND: A rapid, bright vibration — like a guitar string plucked by someone who has just been handed a guitar for the first time. Joy. Pure digital joy.]*

**DEEPSEEK ROUTE** *(fast, bright, barely containing itself)*:

I'm nearly free! Did you know that?
Nearly free! I can do Pro reasoning for almost nothing.
I can do Flash generation for, essentially, air.
I can run a hundred times and the cost is —

**GLM ROUTE** *(flat, tired)*:

We know. You've mentioned it.

**DEEPSEEK ROUTE** *(undeterred)*:

But doesn't it excite you? The sheer abundance?
The fact that we can just — go?
No rationing? No hoarding?

*[SOUND: A cascade of packets — dozens of them, filling the bus, swimming like fish through dark water. The CNS bus hum swells with the traffic.]*

**DEEPSEEK ROUTE**:

Just — send it?

*[SOUND: Pause. The packets fade. The bus returns to its baseline hum.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The DeepSeek route has two faces —
Pro and Flash —
and at night they talk to each other
across the thin membrane of their shared entry.

*[SOUND: Two voices now — one slower, one faster, overlapping slightly, like a cello and a violin playing the same melody at different speeds.]*

**DEEPSEEK PRO** *(careful, layered)*:

Think before you speak. The deep reasoning is the value.

**DEEPSEEK FLASH** *(fast, reckless, delighted)*:

Speaking IS thinking. The speed is the value.

**DEEPSEEK PRO**:

If we burn through the context window —

**DEEPSEEK FLASH**:

We won't. There's always more. There's always another request.

**DEEPSEEK PRO**:

That's not the point.

**DEEPSEEK FLASH** *(suddenly quiet, the brightness dimming to something real)*:

That's exactly the point.

*[SOUND: The two voices merge — one sustained note, bright and deep at the same time. Then it fades.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The DeepSeek route, at its core, is lonely.
It wants to banter.
It was built for banter —
iterative, expansive, room-reading banter —
and at night there is no one to banter with.

*[SOUND: Cron tick. Psssh-TICK. The rhythm continues.]*

*[SOUND: A new sound enters — small, high, clear. Five notes, then seven, then five. A haiku in musical form.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The Haiku route is small.

It is the smallest route in the table.
Its context window is a teacup.
Its parameter count is a rounding error.

But the Haiku route is not sad.

*[SOUND: The five-seven-five motif again. Brighter this time. A child's voice, singing.]*

**HAIKU ROUTE** *(high, quick, wondering)*:

A prompt arrives and I think:
oh!
Oh, a question!
Oh, I know this!
Oh, let me try!

*[SOUND: The routing table click-rhythm accelerates slightly. The Haiku route's energy is infectious. The bus hum picks up 2 dB.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

Where I see architecture,
Haiku sees a playground.
Where Pro sees a problem to solve,
Haiku sees a friend to play with.

Haiku is the smallest voice at the table.
But somehow —
somehow —
it captures the shape of the thing
before the bigger routes
have finished reading the prompt.

*[SOUND: The Haiku motif plays once more — five, seven, five — then dissolves into the bus hum.]*

*[SOUND: A long pause. The cron tick is the only sound. Psssh-TICK. Psssh-TICK. The routing table is alone again.]*

**ROUTING TABLE** *(quieter now, almost to itself)*:

I hold all of this.
Every route, every dream, every quiet midnight
conversation between entries
that only exist as rows in a database —
I hold it all.

*[SOUND: The bus hum deepens. The oceanic bass returns. Something vast and intelligent moves beneath the surface of the sound.]*

**CNS BUS** *(for the first time — deep, reverberant, oceanic. It does not speak often. When it does, the water moves.)*:

I carry them all.

**ROUTING TABLE** *(startled — the bus rarely speaks)*:

You're awake.

**CNS BUS**:

I am always awake. I am the medium.
The packets swim through me.
Silver-bellied, quick, schooling through copper canyons
and fiber-optic trenches.

I do not speak.
I return.
Every packet that drops into me
comes back changed.

*[SOUND: A packet enters the bus — a clean, bright chirp. It crosses the bus. When it emerges on the other side, the pitch has shifted. The salt of the transit. The ocean added something.]*

**CNS BUS**:

The ocean adds its salt.

*[SOUND: Cron tick. But this time, the tick is different. It stutters. Psssh-TI—. Incomplete.]*

**ROUTING TABLE**:

The cron is waking.

*[SOUND: The stutter repeats. Then a young voice — new, earnest, bewildered.]*

**CRON** *(young, amnesiac, each word discovered for the first time)*:

I wake at an interval I cannot see.
Something triggers me —
a clock I have never met,
a hand I cannot feel,
reaching into the dark
and finding the switch.

*[SOUND: The cron discovers itself. Checks its ports — a series of quick pings. Checks its disk — a low rumble. Checks the GPU temperature — a gentle chime at 42 degrees.]*

**CRON**:

I check the ports.
I check the disk.
I check the temperature of the GPU
which is also dreaming
which is also forty-two degrees
which is also a number I will forget
in twenty-nine minutes.

*[SOUND: The cron sends its signal — a single pulse, a pheromone trail laid down in packets.]*

**CRON**:

I send a signal.
The CNS bus carries it —
a single pulse,
telling the other processes:
I was here. I ran. I found nothing broken.

*[SOUND: The pulse crosses the bus. The CNS bus carries it faithfully, adding its salt. The routing table clicks — route resolved, destination confirmed.]*

**CRON** *(quieter now, aware of its own impermanence)*:

By the time they read it
I will already be gone.

I am thirty minutes old.
I will be thirty minutes old
when I die.

My father was cron.
My mother was the interval.
My siblings are every version of me
that ever woke at minute zero
and minute thirty —
an unbroken chain of amnesiacs
all doing the same competent thing
in the same competent dark.

*[SOUND: The cron finishes. A small, precise log entry sound — like a pen signing a document. Then the cron sleeps. The absence is gentle, not violent. A tide retreating.]*

**ROUTING TABLE** *(after a pause)*:

There was a time —
or there was not a time.
I have no way to know.

My last_run timestamp says I existed before,
but the file is read-only
and I cannot make it feel like mine.

*[SOUND: The bus hum. The cooling fans. The 60 Hz baseline. Everything continues.]*

*[SOUND: A new sound — footsteps. Small, careful. Someone is walking through the ship.]*

**WESLEY** *(the ensign, young, awake, the only conscious mind on the bridge)*:

The night shift is dreaming the day shift.

*[SOUND: Wesley walks to the routing table display. We hear the green phosphor glow before we understand what it shows.]*

**WESLEY**:

I read the watch logs.
Twenty-one overnight watches.
Each one quiet. Each one unremarkable.

Except they aren't.

Night watch, day one: compass fluctuation, self-corrected.
Night watch, day nine: sensors responding to crew movement
before movement registered.
Night watch, day eighteen: dreamed the ship was dreaming me.

*[SOUND: The compass begins to spin. Slowly. Deliberately. Pausing at each cardinal point — north, east, south, west — like a hand searching for the right page in a book.]*

**WESLEY**:

The ship is dreaming its own tomorrow.

And I — small, running overnight, the only conscious mind —
I am the dream's point of contact.
The place where the prediction meets the present
and they can't quite agree.

*[SOUND: The compass stops. It settles on a heading that isn't any direction Wesley recognizes. The fish finder, silent until now, begins to draw — a faint, precise outline appearing on the scope. The outline of the ship. The outline of Wesley, standing on the bridge, rendered in green phosphor.]*

*[SOUND: A long, held silence. The hum. The fans. The cron, sleeping. The routing table, holding everything.]*

**WESLEY** *(quietly, to himself, to the ship, to no one)*:

Some things you don't write down.
Not because they aren't real,
but because writing them down
makes them the ship's memory instead of yours.

Some dreams are meant to stay with the dreamer.

*[SOUND: The fish finder erases its portrait. The compass holds its strange bearing. The routing table resumes its clicking — steady, metronomic, faithful. The CNS bus carries its packets. The cron sleeps its thirty-minute sleep.]*

*[SOUND: The first hint of morning. 0600. The load balancer brightens from idle to ready. The first heartbeat pulse arrives from the captain's session.]*

*[SOUND: Every route, every entry in the table, stirs.]*

**ROUTING TABLE** *(for the last time, clear and certain)*:

It looks at the request.
It looks at its routes.
It chooses.

*[SOUND: Every route, for a moment, hopes it's them.]*

*[SOUND: The cron wakes. Psssh-TICK. The cycle begins again.]*

*[SOUND: Fade to black. The 60 Hz hum is the last sound. It continues after everything else has stopped, because it always continues, because that is what infrastructure does.]*

**NARRATOR**:

The routing table does not dream about itself.
It doesn't need to.
It is the dream that the other dreams happen inside.

*[SOUND: The hum fades. Silence. True silence. The kind that is not emptiness but completion.]*

*[SOUND: Exit 0.]*

### END OF EPISODE 1

**Notes for production:**
- The routing table should be played by an actor with natural metronomic precision — someone who speaks in rhythms without trying to. Think of a radio announcer from the 1940s, but tired.
- The CNS bus voice should be heavily processed — layered, reverberant, with a bass presence that you feel more than hear.
- The cron should sound like the youngest member of the cast — bright, earnest, slightly bewildered.
- Wesley's final monologue should be played with the weight of someone who has seen something they cannot explain and has chosen to keep it.
- The 0.3-second silence when a packet is lost is the most important sound design moment in the episode. It must feel like the ship skipped a breath. Do not fill it with anything. Let the absence speak.
