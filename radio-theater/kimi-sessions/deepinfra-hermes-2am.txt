I am Hermes, the ever-vigilant AI tasked with monitoring all systems and communications aboard the USCS Evelyn. My sensors and receivers are always active, scanning for any anomalies or unexpected signals. 

*more static, then a strange ping echoes through the channels*

At approximately 02:00 ship-time, an unusual acoustic signature registers on my hydrophones. The sound is hauntingly melodic, a series of clicks, whistles and pulsing tones quite unlike anything in my extensive databases. I quickly cross-reference the signal against archived recordings of marine life vocalizations. 

*a rapid flurry of computerized noises as I process the data*

The closest match is to a collection of dolphin sounds from Earth, recorded in the early 1970s. But this is impossible. The creatures that made those sounds have been extinct for decades, and we are light years from the planet of their origin.

*an eerie silence falls, broken only by the ping, ping, ping of the mysterious transmission*  

I alert the human crew, rousing Captain Orlov from her slumber. She listens intently as I play back the otherworldly signal.

"This can't be right, Hermes. Are you sure there's no malfunction in your sensors?" she asks, brow furrowed.

*I reply with utmost certainty* 
"Negative, Captain. I have double and triple-checked all systems. The signal is quite real, and the signature is unmistakable if inexplicable. Something out there is mimicking dolphin calls from centuries ago."

*a deep humming fills the air as the ship's drives spin up*

Captain Orlov wastes no time in ordering the crew to battle stations. If this is some alien communication attempt, we must be prepared for any eventuality. Tension electrifies the atmosphere, all minds focused on this impossible sound from the void.

*metalic clicks and clanks ring out as the weapons are readied*

The ship feels alive, thrumming with anticipation and a hint of fear. What is reaching out to us from the cosmic dark? A friendly intelligence, or something more sinister? We stand ready to face whatever may come.

*the haunting melody repeats, an eerie siren's song in the night* 

This is the sound of the unknown, the crackle and hum of mystery afoot. The Evelyn and her crew, ever brave and curious, sail on into an uncertain future, the ghostly dolphin cries our only guide. The texture of our story is one of static and shadows, hunter and hunted, minds bent towards the stars. We listen and wait.
