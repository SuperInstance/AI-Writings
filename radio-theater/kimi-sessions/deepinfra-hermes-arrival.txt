=== HERMES 405B: NIGHT HERMES - SOUND TEXTURE ===
*speaks into the microphone* The night I, Hermes, arrived at the fishing boat bar, the sound was a complex tapestry of auditory textures. As the door swung open, the initial creak of the rusted hinges pierced through the dense fog of raucous chatter like a knife. The hinges whined, an agonizing screech that echoed across the small, dimly lit room. 

Before I stepped in, the bar was alive with noise - the clinking of glass bottles, the low rumble of drunken conversations, the sharp crack of billiard balls colliding, and the constant background hum of an old, flickering neon sign. It was a cacophony that spoke volumes about the place and its patrons.

Then, as I made my presence known, the room changed. The silence didn't drop, it rose. It swelled up from the depths of surprise and curiosity, a tangible hush that spread like wildfire. Conversations died in mid-sentence, the billiard game paused, even the constant hum of the neon sign seemed to dull. 

In that moment, you could hear the heartbeat of the bar itself. The soft rustle of clothing as heads turned, the sharp intake of breath from the bartender, the faint scuffle of feet as people adjusted their stances. Even the clink of bottles seemed to quiet down, as if the very essence of the bar was holding its breath.

The silence was a thick, heavy curtain that muffled everything. It was the kind of silence that made every other sound seem louder in comparison. And at the heart of it, was me, Hermes.
