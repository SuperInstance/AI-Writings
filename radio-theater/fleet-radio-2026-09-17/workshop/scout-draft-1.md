# Scout — Draft 1

**For**: the person who's just shown up to a place they've never been, doesn't know the rules yet, is going to learn by being there

[Intro]  
Door creaks.  
Boots on salt.  
You stand there—half in, half caught.  

[Verse 1]  
You peel off winter like it’s skin you don’t need,  
look down, then up—same as me.  
The noise hits hard, like a name you forgot,  
and nobody’s laughing, but you feel the notch  
in the air—like maybe you’re not meant to stay.  
I’ve stood right there.  
Same shoes. Same sway.  

[Chorus]  
It’s not “come on in,” it’s not “you belong,”  
it’s the breath before the breath, the almost-wrong.  
It’s the hand on the bar, not the hand that’s reached,  
it’s the voice that don’t speak—just the one that’s been reached.  
I was the next one too.  
Just one seat down.  
This song’s not for me.  
It’s for when you sit down.  

[Verse 2]  
You’ll scan the faces—some sharp, some slow—  
looking for one that won’t make you go.  
And someone’ll nod—maybe not a friend,  
just someone who knows how long the night can bend.  
The fry basket’s shared, the joke’s too loud,  
and suddenly the room’s not a wall, but a cloud.  

[Chorus]  
It’s not “you’re okay,” it’s not “take my hand,”  
it’s the beat where you choose to understand:  
you don’t got to fit to be worth the ground,  
you just gotta show—make the quiet sound.  
I was the next one too.  
One coat late.  
This song’s not advice.  
It’s the weight off your wait.  

[Bridge]  
Steam on the glass.  
A name called wrong.  
The second you stay is the second you’re strong.  
Not because you’re sure, but ‘cause you’re still here—  
and the light hits gold when the night draws near.  

[Outro]  
So sit.  
Don’t speak.  
The song’s already in your seat.  
It’s old.  
It’s true.  
It was made for you.
