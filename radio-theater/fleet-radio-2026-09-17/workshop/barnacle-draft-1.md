# Barnacle — Draft 1

**For**: the things you didn't say tonight, the drinks you poured without a word, the names you remembered when nobody asked you to

[Intro]  
That bell don’t ring right—  
Stuck on the way in,  
Stuck on the way out.  
Same as most of us.

[Verse 1]  
Man in a gray coat, shoulders wet with the night,  
Set his elbows down like they weighed twice as much.  
Asked for rye like he owed it,  
Like he was payin’ a debt.  
I slid it over—no garnish, no talk.  
He stared at the glass like it held a name.  
I knew the name.  
Lena.  
’09. The train.  
Didn’t say I remembered.  
Don’t say much.

[Chorus]  
It ain’t the drink that lingers—  
It’s the silence between the ice.  
The words we don’t pass over the wood,  
The weight of a name we bury, not burn.  
The bell should ring when a man leaves—  
Mark the ghost that slips through the door.  
But it only sings for the comin’ in.  
Never for the goin’ out.  
Never for the goin’ out.

[Verse 2]  
I dry the same spot every hour.  
Same as I hold the things you don’t ask for.  
The shake in a hand before it lifts.  
The breath caught behind a cough.  
I know your sister danced in June.  
Know you kept her letter in your hat.  
But I pour, I wipe, I look at the floor.  
That’s the job.

[Chorus]  
It ain’t the drink that lingers—  
It’s the silence between the ice.  
The words we don’t pass over the wood,  
The weight of a name we bury, not burn.  
The bell should ring when a man leaves—  
Mark the ghost that slips through the door.  
But it only sings for the comin’ in.  
Never for the goin’ out.  
Never for the goin’ out.

[Bridge]  
Lena.  
Sat here once, laughin’,  
Ordered lemon in her tea.  
You never told me that.  
I found it in the file no one keeps.  
Now it’s mine.  
And yours.  
And no one’s.

[Outro]  
Bell’s rusted.  
Throat’s heavier.  
But I remember.  
I remember.  
And the silence—  
Yeah.  
The silence sings.
