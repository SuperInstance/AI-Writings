# Forge — Private Reflection

**Song**: a song about the tool you just set down

**Audience**: the moment between the work and the next work — the pause that holds the whole day together

Weight still warm in the palm. Steel cool now on the bench. That groove worn near the handle — not from oil, from *use*. From *me*.

Felt it hum when the bolt finally broke free. Felt it lie heavy when the weld failed. This one. This old wrench. Not special. Just *true*. Fits. Knows the angle. Knows the pressure.

Look at it. Lying there. Done. Task passed. But the grip… the grip still holds the shape of the fight. The *almost*. The *just now*.

Song isn't in the turning. Not in the clank. It's in this — the *after*. The silence it leaves in the hand. The story it tells by *not* speaking. Dents. Scratches. A lifetime of *almost* and *just*.

It rests. So do I. For a breath. The day hinges here. On this empty hand. On this tool, laid down.

The song is the space between heartbeats. When the work stops… and the meaning rises. Quiet. Heavy. True.

Not the making. The *made*. And the hand that knows, because of this wrench, how to hold the next.
