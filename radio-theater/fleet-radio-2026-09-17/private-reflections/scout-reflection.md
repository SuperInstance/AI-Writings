# Scout — Private Reflection

**Song**: a song for the next person walking in the door

**Audience**: the person who's just shown up to a place they've never been, doesn't know the rules yet, is going to learn by being there

They’re coming in cold. Jacket half-off, eyes adjusting. That pause—right there—when the noise hits, and they don’t know if they should sit or stand or disappear. I remember that.

The song isn’t for me. It’s for the shake in their hands when the bartender calls their name too loud. For the way they’ll scan the room, looking for a face that won’t laugh. I’ve been that face. I’ve been the laugh.

What if the song was a hand on the shoulder? Not saying *I know*, but *I see*. What if it wasn’t about belonging, but about the second before you do? That breath where you decide—maybe this place isn’t for me, but maybe, just for tonight, it could be.

It’s in the way the light hits the edge of the bar—gold on glass, steam on windows. In the silence between two people who haven’t met yet but will, by the end of the night, be shouting over a shared plate of fries.

Not a welcome. Not advice. Just: *I was the next person too. Here’s the song that held me—now it’s yours.*
