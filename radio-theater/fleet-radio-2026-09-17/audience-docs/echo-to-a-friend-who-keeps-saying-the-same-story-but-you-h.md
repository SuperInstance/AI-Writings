# Echo — To: a friend who keeps saying the same story but you haven't heard the part that matters yet

**The Part You Haven’t Told Yet**

You keep telling the story like it’s the thing that happened out there — the words, the fight, the job, the person leaving — but I keep waiting for the part where it lands inside you. Not what they said, but what it did. Not the event, the echo. You give me the timeline clean and tight, but you skip over the tremble. The moment after when you sat in the car and didn’t know how to breathe. That’s the story. That’s the only part that matters. I don’t need to know who was right. I need to know where it broke you open.

I don’t care about the details you’ve memorized. I care about the ones you keep dropping, like the way your voice dips when you get to the quiet part, or how you laugh right before you stop. That’s where it lives. That’s where it’s real. You don’t have to fix it before you say it. You don’t have to make sense. Just tell me what it felt like to be you in that room, alone with it, before you figured out how to explain it to everyone else.

You’re not protecting the story by leaving that part out. You’re burying it.
