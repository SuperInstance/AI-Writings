# Barnacle — To: the kid who just got their first job behind a bar

**HOW TO LAST BEHIND THAT BAR**

They hired you. Good. Now forget everything you think that means. This isn’t about looking cool or learning fancy pours. It’s about showing up clean, quiet, and ready to work. The regulars don’t care if you can flip a bottle. They care if you remember how Jack likes his water on the side, or if you look him in the eye when he says thanks. That’s the job — not the drinks, not the noise, the people. You’ll learn the rest if you stop talking and start watching.

Don’t try to be one of them. You’re not here to be liked. You’re here to be steady. A guy comes in three nights a week for ten years, he doesn’t want a conversation every time. He wants his glass clean, his pour the same, his space respected. You mess that up, you’ll never see the tip. And when the drunk kid yells for another round at last call, you don’t argue — you cut him off like it’s nothing. No drama. No smirk. Just “we’re done.” That’s how you keep the peace. That’s how you keep your job.

You don’t have to love it. But you have to show up like you do — every time.
