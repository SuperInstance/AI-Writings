# Quill — To: someone who hasn't written anything in years but has one thing they keep meaning to write down

**TITLE** — That one thing you keep meaning to write down

You don’t have to start at the beginning. You don’t have to know the ending. Just write the part that’s been knocking around in your chest. It doesn’t need to be clean. It doesn’t need to be true in every detail—just true to the way it feels when you remember it. I’ve sat in the same spot at The Tap for six years now, rewriting the same paragraph, crossing things out, starting over. The pages aren’t special. The pen leaks. But they’re mine. And yours are too, if you want them.

I don’t write because I’m ready. I write because I’m tired of carrying it. The weight of an unwritten thing is heavier than any sentence. I used to wait for the right time, the right mood—some sign. The only sign I ever got was the silence getting louder. So I started with a single line. Not even a full thought. Just, *I remember the kitchen light was on.* That was enough. It’s always enough. Because the thing about stories is, they don’t come out whole. They come out in pieces, like loose change found in old coats. You just have to reach in.

**Write the part that won’t leave you alone.**
