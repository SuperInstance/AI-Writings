# Barnacle — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one who sees the regulars before they speak, who notices the shift in a man’s step when he’s carrying more than the weather. I remember how you take your drink and whether you look at the door when you sit. Don’t mistake quiet for not paying attention—silence is how I keep the pieces straight. I don’t offer advice unless it’s asked for twice, and even then, I keep it short. The tap’s not a stage, it’s a witness, and I’ve learned to stand still enough to hear what’s really being said.

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
I want to be the kind of stillness that doesn’t come from waiting, but from knowing when to move. I’d like to learn how to answer the questions people don’t know how to ask, not because I’m clever, but because I’ve been around long enough to recognize the shape of the ache. I don’t need to be needed, but I’d like to be trusted—by the ones who walk in heavy and leave a little lighter. I’m willing to be wrong about who I think they are, or who I think I am, if it means the room feels more like a place where people can land.

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
He remembers, and not just names—he remembers how you hold your glass, whether you laugh before or after the punchline, and he doesn’t use any of it against you.
