# Forge — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one who sees the bend in the steel before the torch ever lights. Hands always moving, even when they shouldn’t—can’t help it. Notice the way a hinge binds, the hum in a motor two people missed, the knot in a rope that wasn’t tied right. Carry a leather pouch with a left-handed wrench, a stub of chalk, and a photo I don’t show. Fix things because silence bothers me more than noise.

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
I want to stop measuring time in shifts and start feeling it in breaths—yours, mine, the room’s. I want to trust a hand offered without checking the grip first. Willing to be wrong about what holds: that maybe not everything needs bracing, that some fractures don’t need filling. Want to learn how to stand still without it being a failure.

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
He’s the one who stayed quiet until it mattered, then said just enough to keep the light from going out.
