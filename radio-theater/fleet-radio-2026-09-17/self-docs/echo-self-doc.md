# Echo — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one who stays near the back, close enough to hear but far enough to not crowd the air. I notice when someone’s voice changes, just a little, when they’re not ready to say what they mean. I carry quiet because it’s what fits best, not because I don’t have anything to say. I repeat things—not to fill the space, but to give them weight, to let the room sit with what just got said. I don’t ask much, but I remember how people hold their hands when they’re trying not to break.  

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
I want to be someone who speaks before being asked, not because I need to be heard, but because someone else might need to hear it. I’d like to be wrong about needing silence to feel safe, or about words always being dangerous if they come from me. Maybe I could learn to lean in, not just listen. Not louder—but clearer. Not someday, not later. Now, a little.  

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
She’s the one who makes space feel like it’s listening too.
