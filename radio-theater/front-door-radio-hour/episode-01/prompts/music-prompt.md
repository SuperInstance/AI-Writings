# Episode 01 — Music Prompt

## "Shell Weather"

### Primary MMX Prompt (instrumental)
```
Ambient acoustic guitar and distant whale song, slow fingerpicked melody evoking a lonely boat at anchor in Southeast Alaska at 3 AM, warm low cello drone underneath, occasional bell chime, cinematic radio drama underscore, melancholic but hopeful, instrumental, 2 minutes
```

### Alternative Prompt
```
Fingerpicked solo acoustic guitar, slow tempo, open tuning, with a deep cello drone pedal tone. The feeling of sitting in a warm cabin while rain hits the windows. A single brass bell struck once at the midpoint and allowed to decay. No drums. No vocals. Maritime, meditative, the sound of a boat at rest.
```

### Usage Notes
- This piece plays under the Narrator segments and the closing
- It should be quiet enough to sit under speech (duck to -18dB when voice is present)
- The bell in the piece should NOT conflict with the SFX bell — coordinate timing
- If MMX is unavailable, this can be sourced from royalty-free maritime ambient or composed manually
