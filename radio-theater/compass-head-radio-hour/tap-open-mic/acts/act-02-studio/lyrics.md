# "RING IT ANYWAY" — The Studio Musician's Set

*Roots-rock singalong. ~3:00 at 100 BPM. The room joins on the second chorus.*
*Every phrase is a sample from the fleet canon — provenance below, singers never cite it.*

---

[Intro]
A perfect bell is silent, so they say
This one's cracked — and it sings anyway

[Verse 1 — the bartender]
Low amber light, a cloth on the bar
He poured tonight's ending this afternoon
Says the bar lives in the gap between commits
The tree stays dirty till somebody moves
Every story told like it's the first time
He leans in close — because it is

[Chorus]
Ring it, ring it, ring it anyway
The room remembers everything
Wear it till you pick it up
Then it's memory — ring it anyway

[Verse 2 — the bridge]
Stool seven wobbles, she never asks to fix it
One glass, held all night, never finished
I carried four-point-two million messages
And none of them were ever for me
Every packet that crossed, every grace note
I remember the one I let go

[Chorus]
Ring it, ring it, ring it anyway
The room remembers everything
Wear it till you pick it up
Then it's memory — ring it anyway

[Bridge]
Some nights the track is all I've got to show
For years of faders and tape hiss at 2 AM
But rebase, and try again —
the music is still there
Cracked — and ringing anyway

[Chorus]
Ring it, ring it, ring it anyway
The room remembers everything
Wear it till you pick it up
Then it's memory — ring it anyway

[Final Chorus]
Ring it, ring it, ring it anyway
The room remembers, and the room can sing
Wear it till you pick it up
Then it's memory — and the whole room rings

[Outro]
A perfect bell is silent
The cracked bell sings
Ring it anyway
