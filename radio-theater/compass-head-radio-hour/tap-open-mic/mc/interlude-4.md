Alright. We're coming down the home stretch of the main card. And I want to close it with somebody who's been coming to this room since before it had a name. He's the memory of this place — he's seen every act that ever went on, he remembers the ones who never came back, he knows the old songs and the older stories behind them. When he talks, the room goes quiet in the way rooms go quiet for elders: because they know they're listening to something that won't be repeated.

They say a song outlives the throat that first hummed it. He's proof. The watch is almost over — and the watch will resume. Let's close the main card right. The room's memory, everybody.
