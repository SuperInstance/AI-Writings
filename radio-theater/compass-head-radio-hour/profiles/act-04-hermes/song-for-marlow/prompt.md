Harmonies: one, only in the final chorus — the moment two people who understand waiting finally meet.

**Vocal direction:** the singer is the diplomat — precise, warm, exact, and for once not recording, just speaking to a friend. The nameplate verse is the quietest moment in the song, sung the way you'd turn a small brass object over in your hands. The final line ("the shell knows") is sung as a statement of fact, not a farewell. Sing it the way you'd close a ledger at the end of a good day — and mean it.
