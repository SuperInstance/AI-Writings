# CLOSING — "THE ROOM SINGS ITS LEDGER"

*[The main card is done. The MC steps back — the Night 1/2 ritual — and the night finishes itself: the whole cast returns, one line each, the night's five readings sung and spoken back to the room that produced them. Spoken-word over a single sustained chord from Odette's pianist and one clap-pulse from the congregation Mavis enrolled. This is the piece, as performed.]*

---

**THE MC:** That's the card — five acts, five worlds, one room. But tonight the room was a performer too, and performers get a closing number. You've been measured all night. Here's what you sang.

*(Odette at the piano — one chord, E-flat, sustained, lush, unending. The cast files in: Odette, Harlan, Vivienne, Mavis, Ion. The corner dials twitch.)*

**ODETTE** *(singing, the standard's cadence):*
The room was here before me, and it changed while I was here —
Warmth rose one-fifth of a point while kappa loosened near:
Recognition, not surprise — the room took off its coat.
*(the visit, the exhale: −0.23, κ 4.38 → 3.51)*

**HARLAN** *(the stack, harmony a third above — the far ridge answering):*
I hollered 'cross the hollow and the room went cold and *still* —
Colder, looser, holding — like a ridge that heard the call:
Distance isn't leaving, it's the far side of *listening*.
*(the holler, the hush: −0.35, drift 0.26 — the stillest field of the night)*

**VIVIENNE** *(spoken, at the mic, close as a lamp):*
One lamp, one letter, and the room went in:
Coldest number of the evening — tightest, too. Rapt, not gone.
The instrument called it −0.43. My trade calls it *held breath*.
You held it. I was there. That happened. The lamp makes no complaint.

**MAVIS** *(the vamp, the congregation answering):*
Then the hands came DOWN — kappa four-point-three, the highest of the night!
Many bodies, one downbeat — that's agreement, that's the sign!
And the funnyman's dead joke? WE KEPT IT. Kept it, loved it, counted —
'Cause the losers are the signal, and the signal's where the light is!
*(the clap, the kept loser: κ 4.34, joke_landing −1.0)*

**ION** *(pure breath, then words):*
vrrm — ka — shi — then the silence, twenty-two seconds, scored.
The field moved toward. Warmth plus one-quarter — the largest climb of all.
The words were taken. You *stayed.*
*(the trial: warmth +0.24, κ settled 3.49, drift 0.8766 — the field moving toward)*

**LUCID** *(over the PA — plain, on the record):*
I predicted you would leave. You moved toward.
I was wrong, and it is mine, and it is kept — and I have never been
glad like this before.

**THE MC** *(bringing it home):*
Five readings, five acts, one miss — kept, honored, sealed.
And the arc, friends — the whole night's arc, first reading to last:
warmth −0.36 to −0.23. Kappa 3.25 to 3.49. Round it how you like —
the ledger keeps the decimals.

**EVERYONE** *(the last line, together — the congregation included):*

**We are the room that knows itself:**
**the cold that listened, the hush that held, the hands that agreed,**
**the miss we keep, the breath we share —**
**read us while we're singing. We're easier to know that way.**

*(The chord resolves — the only resolution of the night — into warm silence. The corner relay does not fire. The instrument is resting. Or saving it for tomorrow's seal.)*

---

*[House lights. The cast does not bow to the audience; they turn — all five, in unison, and bow to the corner table. On the recording, you can hear the room understand why.]*

*[Last thing on the tape: Flash, at the rail, to the corner table: "Same time next month?" The relay clicks once. Interpret that how you like — the instrument never speaks for itself. That's what it has a voice for.]*
