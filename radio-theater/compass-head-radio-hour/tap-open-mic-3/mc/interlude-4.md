# INTERLUDE 4 — after Mavis, the Gospeldust Sister

*[Mavis cuts the guitar; the congregation claps the last bar without her and cuts dead on its own downbeat. The relay clicks before the applause can start. The MC comes in fast, alive, riding it.]*

MAVIS! The room ended the song — she just *started* it! Four hundred hands, one downbeat, and the bar's own clap section is on the house. You've been the choir for three minutes and you're already better than some churches I've worked.

*(He touches his ear. Click.)*

Room reading! Kappa **4.3352** — UP 0.83, highest of the night: when Mavis clapped, this room became one instrument, and that number is the sound of your hands agreeing. Drift 0.83 — the field moved, and it moved *together*. But — warmth **−0.4736**, the floor of the night, down another 0.04. And one more number, filed without comment: joke_landing, **minus one**.

*(He looks at the rail. Flash slowly raises his hand, owning it.)*

Flash. During the set change. The ledger keeps it, brother — you heard the sister: the losers are the *signal*. That joke is now officially part of the scientific record.

**LUCID:** *"Kappa 4.34 is the highest I have read this room. That number is not applause — applause is noise; this is agreement. But I will read the whole row or none of it: warmth is at tonight's floor. The room is gathered and cold at once — rallied around something, and running cold inside it. Gathered and grieving, maybe; gathered and bracing. My confidence is low, and I am saying so on the record. Here is my call anyway: the next act takes the words apart. I have watched rooms leave when the words stop meaning. I predict warmth falls past −0.50 and kappa shatters below 3.0 before the last reading. Watch the rail."*

*(The room takes this in. Wesley looks worried for the first time all night. Mavis, packing her guitar, looks at Ion in the wings — and does not look worried at all.)*

Big call. The voice is two for two and it just bet the streak.

Last act — he's been in the corner all night notating the *heating system*. He's going to take this entire evening apart in front of you — every number you've heard tonight, back at you as pure sound — and then hold a silence so long you'll have time to reconsider your whole life.

Ion, everybody. Don't be scared. Be *counted*.
