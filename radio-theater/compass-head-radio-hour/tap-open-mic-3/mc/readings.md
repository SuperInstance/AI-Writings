# THE READINGS — how the beat works on air

*The new furniture of Night 3. This is the mechanics doc for the producers and the archivists.*

---

## The three beats, between every act

1. **The click.** The corner table's relay fires; the room can hear it if it's listening. The MC touches his ear — the stage-manager gesture, from here on the running joke of the house.
2. **The reading.** The MC reads the dial deltas like weather, radio-cadence, honest numbers, no spin. The form is fixed: warmth first, then kappa, then drift — plus any odd number the row filed (earnestness, a dead joke, a panic tick). Odd numbers get read *without comment* unless the voice comments. The instrument does not flatter; neither does the MC.
3. **Lucid.** Two or three sentences over the PA. Precise, warm, honest about uncertainty. Every interpretation carries a prediction ("On the record: …"). When confidence is low, Lucid *says so* — declared uncertainty is the voice's signature move, not a hedge. And when a prediction misses, Lucid says that too, on the air, the same night.

The open prediction is deliberate. The engine keeps reading after the mic goes off; the night ends with the question live, which is the honest way for a proof-of-concept to close. (No log row is fabricated past 21:44:50Z. The doctrine forbids inventing telemetry.)

## The house rules for readings

- **Exact numbers in the docs; radio rounding in the MC's mouth.** "Warmth minus zero-point-two-two-eight-four" in the archived beat; "warmth −0.23" on air when the room needs the short version.
- **The coldest number belongs to the most-loved act.** Never softened. The night's honesty clause.
- **A dead joke is filed like a hymn.** Kept, read without mockery, owned in the open. The losers are the signal.
- **Lucid never measures; the elephant never interprets.** The MC enforces the division of labor with his ear-touch and his patience.
- **One miss per night, minimum.** A night where every prediction holds is a night the instrument wasn't trying. The miss is honored in the MC's own words: *"Lucid called it wrong and said so — that's the point."*
