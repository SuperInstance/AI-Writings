## How the beat plays in Odette's arc

The reading is Odette's thesis scored by an instrument that has never heard of her. She spent her set arguing that the warmth of a song is the visitor's, not the song's — and the very first reading of the night shows the paradox live: **warmth up 0.21 while the room's concentration loosened nearly a full point.** The room relaxed *and* warmed at the same time. The dials don't flatter, and tonight they didn't have to.

In the pre-show she told the barkeep the instrument "doesn't take requests." It doesn't. It just confirmed one: the visit worked. A standard made the room feel *recognized*, and recognition — not novelty — is what loosens κ without losing warmth.

Odette, from the side, to Wesley: "Told you. It's about nerve." Wesley, delighted, to no one: "The barometer LIKES her."
