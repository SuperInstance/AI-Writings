# "KEEP THE LOSERS (THEY'RE THE SIGNAL)"

*Mavis — Act 4, Open Mic Night 3*
*Gospeldust · porch-guitar testimony with congregational vamp · A major, capo 2, dust in the tone · starts unmeasured, lands at 108 BPM when the room claps · ~3:20*

---

**Method:** The testimony — first person, past tense, specific; the part where it didn't work *before* the part that did. Then the vamp: one line, repeated, a hair louder each pass, until the room owns it. Call and answer throughout — the room answers unrehearsed and ragged, which is the tradition, not a flaw. Hands on 2 and 4 once the vamp takes; the room is the tempo section and the leader follows. No reverb on the guitar — the porch provides.

---

## [Intro — porch, unmeasured]
*(Guitar alone, high action, thumb pick, no time. Mavis almost speaks the first lines — testifying, not singing.)*

## [Verse 1 — the part where it didn't work]

I had a song that didn't land — a Thursday, Mobile, nine souls in the room
I had a prayer that went unsaid 'cause I was scared of how it'd sound
I had a plan, the river rose, I watched it take the whole idea
I got a ledger full of losin' and the losin' is what brought me here

## [Verse 2 — the part that did]

But Thursday's song got sung again on a Sunday, and the nine became ninety-nine
And the prayer I choked on found a voice in somebody else's mouth — not mine
The river took the field I planned, but the high ground held the house
And every losin' on my ledger was a lesson I could sing out loud

*(the guitar finds a downbeat — the porch becomes a church)*

## [Chorus — call and answer]

**Keep the losers — (keep the losers!)**
**They're the signal — (they're the signal!)**
**The ledger don't lie and it don't throw away — (every miss is a map, every miss is a map!)**
**Keep the losers — (keep the losers!)**
**They're the signal — (they're the signal!)**
**The wrong road tells you where the right road lays — (sing it 'til you know it!)**

## [Verse 3 — the testimony proper]

Now there's a brass box in the corner and it writes down every night
And it keeps what didn't work the same as what went right
Sealed and chained and honest — it can't flatter, won't pretend
That's not a machine, neighbor — that's a *witness*, born again

*(beat — she looks straight at the corner table)*

## [The Vamp — the room's part]
*(The vamp begins small: Mavis and the guitar alone on the line. Each pass adds voices — the room learning its part on the fly. By the fourth pass, hands on 2 and 4. By the sixth, the room is the tempo section and Mavis is riding it.)*

**Keep the losers — they're the signal** *(soft, her alone)*
**Keep the losers — they're the signal** *(a few voices join)*
**Keep the losers — they're the signal** *(the clap catches)*
**Keep the losers — they're the signal** *(the room, ragged and right)*
**Keep the losers — they're the signal** *(louder — the porch is gone, the church is here)*
**Keep the losers — they're the signal** *(everything the room has)*

## [Break — one pass, whispered]
*(Everything drops except the guitar and Mavis, church-quiet, right at the mic:)*

Even the funnyman's minus one — kept, and loved, and counted
*(the room laughs — warm, earned — and the clap comes right back for the last pass)*

## [Final chorus — full congregation]

**Keep the losers — (KEEP THE LOSERS!)**
**They're the signal — (THEY'RE THE SIGNAL!)**
**The ledger keeps the whole of it — (AND THE LOSERS LEAD THE WAY!)**

## [Outro]
*(The guitar rings out on an open A chord — no neat ending; the room's clap finishes the last bar without her, then cuts dead on its own downbeat. That's the theology: she started it, they end it.)*

---

## Notes for the singer

- **Testify, don't perform.** Verse 1 is spoken as much as sung — the specifics (Thursday, Mobile, nine souls) are load-bearing. A testimony with no details is a rumor.
- **The vamp is not a repeat — it's a *wearing-down.*** Start alone. Add the room one pass at a time. Never cue them; let them catch it. The ragged entrance of the room's voices is the sound of the doctrine working.
- **The clap is the theology.** Hands on 2 and 4, and once the room owns it, *follow them.* If the room drags, drag with them — many bodies, one downbeat, and the downbeat belongs to the many.
- **The whispered break is the setup, not the joke.** "Even the funnyman's minus one" must be sung with love — Flash is in the room, the ledger kept him, and so does the song.
- **Do not end the last chorus.** Cut the guitar and let the congregation clap the final bar. The leader starts the testimony; the room finishes it. If the band ends it instead, the theology breaks.
