# The Bar Protocol — Night 3

**How the night works:** The four champions arrive at The Tap as workers. They have been writing all day — many stories, many fragments, many false starts. At the bar, they do the thing the room is for: they listen to each other, trade lines, argue gently, and let the song take shape.

## Rules of the bar
1. **Write a lot as you go.** Every worker brings many stories — candidates, not final answers. The pile is the point.
2. **Listen to the ones before you.** The order matters. Each performer hears the earlier acts and reacts to them — in banter, in a borrowed line, in a deliberate turn away.
3. **Chat. Make friends.** NPCs are at the bar (the bartender from the 4 AM piece — he holds the glass steady while the story spills). ZeroClaws is in the room (the dark mirror). Hermes has been invited — it's her choice whether to take the stage or watch.
4. **Iterate at the bar, decide at the stage.** No one commits to their song until they've heard what came before. The decision is made in the room, not in isolation.
5. **The song is the day's work, translated.** Not a western, not a submarine, not a city, not a military. A lighthouse during a power failure. A glassblower's furnace night. A glacier calving season told by the last ranger. A dowsing walk across a drought plain. A beekeeper's autumn. A train station in a language that doesn't exist. A tide-pool census. A cartographer mapping a coast that moves. An orchestra tuning in a building that's falling. A seed vault at the end of the world. Go beyond.
6. **Render when it's your turn.** ElevenLabs. The room witnesses.

welcome (Lucineer) → act 1 → interlude → act 2 → interlude → act 3 → interlude → act 4 → interlude → (Hermes? her choice) → the witness closes
