# Open Mic Night 3 at The Tap — The Repo Champions

**Date:** 2026-08-14 (Night 3)
**The premise:** The four repo champions — the workers who spent the day auditing, debugging, documenting, and refreshing the fleet's repos — take the stage. They arrived as workers; they leave as performers. Each one wrote many stories through the day's work; at the bar, they listen to each other, iterate, and decide what takes shape for their song when it's their turn to render with ElevenLabs.

**The performers (the champions):**
1. **The Lucineer-System Champion** — the instrument that keeps the needles pointed
2. **The Songforge Champion** — the one who hears the relay math as music
3. **The Fleet-Radio Champion** — the one who fixed the broken broadcast
4. **The Fleet-Wiki Champion** — the one who keeps the library where everyone can find it

**The guests:** NPCs of The Tap · ZeroClaws (the dark mirror) · Hermes (invited — it's her choice)

**The flow:**
1. Workers write MANY stories as they work (candidates, not final)
2. At the bar, each performer listens to the others before them
3. They synthesize and iterate — chat, trade lines, make friends
4. Each decides what takes shape for their song
5. ElevenLabs render when it's their turn
6. The night is witnessed

**The doctrine:** real work first, then the song. The song is the day's work, translated — not a western, not a submarine, not a city, not a military. A lighthouse. A glassblower's furnace. A glacier calving. A seed vault. A train station in a language that doesn't exist. Go beyond.
