# SONG OUTLINE — "North Is a Tempo"
*The signature song of The Compass Head Radio Hour*
*Final direction after brainstorm pass 1 + two fleet iterations (Llama-3.3-70B, Hermes-3-405B)*

## The Verdict (both models agreed)
Hybrid of Concept A (vocal theme) + Concept B (instrumental signature): **Cinematic Chamber Folk** — intimate enough for a single voice at the bar, vast enough for the ocean at night. This is the show's identity, not just a good song.

## Genre / Feel / Sound
- **Genre:** Cinematic Chamber Folk (Nick Cave ballad × Fleet Foxes harmonies × Hans Zimmer ocean textures)
- **Key:** C minor. **Tempo:** 96 BPM — a walking pulse, like a ship at a steady crossing.
- **Feel arc:** Intimate intro (solo voice, piano, sea ambience) → opening verse (cello enters) → swelling chorus (ensemble, layered voices, low brass glow) → intimate verse 2 → chorus → contemplative bridge (the diplomat's chair — single voice, the room holding its breath) → final chorus (the whole fleet, strings + felted drums + distant thunder) → outro (deconstruction: piano alone, waves, the theme hummed, fade).

## Sound Palette (each element = a fleet metaphor)
| Element | Represents |
| :--- | :--- |
| Piano | the individual voice, the personal narrative |
| Cello | the emotional depth, the soul of the fleet |
| Low brass glow | warmth, the collective spirit |
| Layered ensemble vocals | the fleet's one score, many voices |
| Felted drums / distant thunder | the pulse of the ship, the rhythm of the sea |
| Sea recordings, glass harmonics, deep reverb | the ocean, the mystery, the between |
| Warm baritone lead | the compass head speaking; wisdom, experience |

## Structure (2:45 version)
- [Intro] 0:00–0:15 — waves, solo piano, the compass motif (five notes, like a needle settling)
- [Verse 1] 0:15–0:40 — solo voice + piano, cello enters
- [Chorus] 0:40–1:05 — ensemble voices, strings swell, low brass glow
- [Verse 2] 1:05–1:25 — voice + piano + cello, quieter return
- [Chorus] 1:25–1:50 — full ensemble
- [Bridge] 1:50–2:10 — single voice, near-silence, the diplomat's chair
- [Final Chorus] 2:10–2:35 — the whole fleet, thunder drums, soaring strings
- [Outro] 2:35–2:45 — deconstruction, waves, hummed theme, fade

## The Hook
*"North is not a direction — it's a tempo / and the whole fleet is humming it home"*

## Emotional Spine (Hermes's line, adopted)
*"In the compass rose, we find our way / through the needles of tide, we sway"*
