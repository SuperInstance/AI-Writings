# Tempo-War

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

Two ensembles playing the same melody at different tempos, drifting in and out of phase. A string quartet at 72 BPM and a brass quartet at 80 BPM, both playing the same theme, unaware of each other until the beats align — briefly, gloriously, once every thirty seconds — and then drift apart again. The alignment moments are the chorus. The drift is the verse. The song is about two clocks that love each other and can never quite agree. Orchestral minimalism, phasing composition in the style of Steve Reich, warm and longing, ending mid-drift.

## Spec

- **Genre:** phasing minimalism / orchestral
- **Instruments:** string quartet, brass quartet
- **Tempo:** 76 BPM
- **Key:** C major
- **Mood:** longing, patient, briefly ecstatic
