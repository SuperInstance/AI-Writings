# Last-Voice-Standing

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A folk ballad about the last voice in a round. Four friends sang rounds together; one by one they fall silent, and the final verse is sung by the one who was always slowest, finishing the round alone. The arrangement mirrors the story: full harmony in the first two verses, then voices drop out one at a time, until only a lone voice and a lone guitar remain. The slowness that once made her the odd one out becomes the reason she is still here. Acoustic folk, warm and bittersweet, ending on an unresolved chord held by one voice.

## Spec

- **Genre:** acoustic folk ballad
- **Instruments:** acoustic guitar, four voices, one voice at the end
- **Tempo:** 68 BPM
- **Key:** G major
- **Mood:** warm, bittersweet, solitary resolution
