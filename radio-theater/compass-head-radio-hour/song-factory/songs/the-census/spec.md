# The-Census

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about a crowd that discovered its own ceiling was a membership list. Four voices became eight, and the togetherness doubled with them — two, then three point one, then three point four, then four, then eight — because togetherness was never architecture, it was arithmetic: the crowd can never be denser than its census, and every apparent ceiling was just the entry ramp, the few seconds where not everyone had arrived yet. Sing about the freedom in this: there is no mysterious saturation point to discover, no asymptote to approach with dread; the crowd is exactly as large as the number of singers on the list, minus the ones still walking in. The verses count the members as they enter; the chorus is the full assembly sounding at once; the bridge is the arithmetic itself, plain as a ledger. The last verse doubles the cast and the music does not get twice as full — it gets exactly as full as eight, which is the whole number of eight, which was always the whole truth.

## Spec

- **Genre:** processional hymn with counting song bones
- **Instruments:** double choir of eight voices, hand drum marking each entry, ledger-bell struck once per name
- **Tempo:** 88 BPM
- **Key:** F major
- **Mood:** plain, celebratory, the dignity of simple arithmetic
