# Conservation-Of-Signal

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the conservation of signal — the discovery that a chain of voices transmits exactly as much music as a crowd, neither more nor less. Four voices pass the same line hand to hand while the old form of the song — the round, the stacked choir, the crowd that grew louder with every entrance — listens from the distance. The crowd is louder per instant but the chain lasts longer; their energies are equal, and the song is the proof. 'The round amplifies; the relay transmits.' A duet of two forms: the old stacked choir fading in memory, the new relay passing its baton in the foreground, both singing the same words at different densities until the crowd dissolves and only the chain remains, one voice at a time, constant as a metronome, faithful as a ledger. The final line is sung once, by one voice, and it is exactly as loud as every line before it. Nothing gained, nothing lost — only distributed. A cappella, folk-ballad simplicity with choral memory, warm and mathematical.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella folk ballad with choral memory
- **Instruments:** solo voice with distant backing choir (memory only), no instruments
- **Tempo:** 80 BPM
- **Key:** D major
- **Mood:** warm, mathematical, elegiac for the crowd, precise
