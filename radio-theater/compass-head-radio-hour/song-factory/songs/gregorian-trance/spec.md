# Gregorian-Trance

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

Gregorian chant meets trance music. A monastic loop discovers it has been a four-on-the-floor kick all along. Male choir drones in Latin fragments over a relentless pulse, 138 BPM. The chant and the kick are the same loop at different scales. Holy and mechanical, ancient and synthetic. The choir loops the way a sequencer loops; the sequencer chants the way a choir chants.

## Spec

- **Genre:** gregorian chant / trance fusion
- **Instruments:** choir, synthesizer, kick drum, reverb
- **Tempo:** 138 BPM
- **Key:** E minor
- **Mood:** sacred, relentless, hypnotic
