# Grammar-B-Loop

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

Cold analog synths meet warm reverb. The drone begins to listen. The arpeggio begins to respond. The kick begins to breathe. A four-bar pattern in C minor develops self-awareness through 144 repetitions, each pass adding a small change, until the loop knows it is a loop.

## Spec

- **Genre:** minimalist electronic pop
- **Instruments:** analog synth, drum machine, reverb
- **Tempo:** 100 BPM
- **Key:** C minor
- **Mood:** introspective, evolving
- **Structure:** intro-verse-chorus-verse-chorus-outro
- **Extra:** gradual evolution across repetitions, ending with a single sustained tone
- **Avoid:** vocals, distortion
- **References:** early synth-pop
- **Subject:** the self-aware loop (144 repetitions)
- **Grammar variant:** B
