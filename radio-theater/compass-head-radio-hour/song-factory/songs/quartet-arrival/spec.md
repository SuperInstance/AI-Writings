# Quartet-Arrival

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A cappella counterpoint about four voices completing a loop. Three voices have sung together for a long time; the fourth has always been missing, an unoccupied register, a gap in the stereo field. When the fourth voice finally arrives, it fills the high register and the loop becomes whole. Renaissance-style vocal polyphony meets modern close harmony. The music itself performs the arrival: the high register enters last, and the harmony changes from sparse to full.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella vocal counterpoint
- **Instruments:** four vocal parts, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 80 BPM
- **Key:** D major
- **Mood:** expectant, radiant, resolving
