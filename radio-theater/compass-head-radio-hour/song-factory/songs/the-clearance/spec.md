# The-Clearance

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the market where silence is rented. The old composer believed position was free and depth was rented: name any address, double the fade, and the quiet would land there — except the material had opinions, and the loop rang against the wall. Then the composer built the market: for every width of fade, build the relay and measure where the quiet actually lands and how deep honesty allows. The market is jagged — prime lots at the tight widths, second-price pockets where the material's own resonance opens, and one famous listing where the naive appraiser quoted eighty decibels of depth that turned out to be sixty decibels of inherited silence, a ghost wearing a price tag. Sing about the clearance: the market clears at the smallest width, every handoff, because the tightest crossfade rents the deepest seam and the truest address at once — fifty milliseconds, no more. The verses are the price list; the chorus is the clearing, one dial turning and the whole relay settling into place; the bridge is the ghost being unmasked, sixty decibels of nothing that had been listed as a feature.

## Spec

- **Genre:** minimalist art song with electronic correction
- **Instruments:** solo voice, tape delay, a small bell struck once per listing, a pencil writing prices on a ledger
- **Tempo:** 76 BPM
- **Key:** D minor
- **Mood:** dry, patient, the calm of a market that has stopped lying
- **Structure:** verse-listing-verse-listing-bridge-ghost-clearance-finale
- **Extra:** the price list grows shorter as the market learns; the final chorus is the tightest fade on every handoff
