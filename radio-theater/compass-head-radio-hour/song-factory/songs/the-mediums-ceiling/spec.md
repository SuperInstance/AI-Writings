# The-Mediums-Ceiling

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the container that takes its share. Eight voices sang at once and the room held almost all of it — but the room was made of numbers with a maximum, and the sum of eight truths exceeded the scale by two parts in a hundred, so the medium quietly clipped what the census had promised. The togetherness was eight; the recording of it was seven point eight five. Sing about every ceiling that turns out to be a container: the tape that saturates, the meter that pegs, the page that runs out, the heart that can only hold so much at full scale before it rounds. The song is honest accounting — one verse for the unclipped truth, one verse for what the medium kept, and a chorus that measures the gap: the container tax, paid in decibels, growing with every voice added, so that a crowd large enough would eventually record as pure flat-topped silence. The final chorus is deliberately, audibly clipped — the sound of the song exceeding itself — and then one quiet unclipped voice states the true total, unamplified, because the truth never needed the container to hold it all at once.

## Spec

- **Genre:** gospel-noise ballad
- **Instruments:** eight-voice stack, distorted pump organ, one clean unclipped voice at the end
- **Tempo:** 72 BPM
- **Key:** E minor
- **Mood:** sober, wry, the tenderness of honest overload
