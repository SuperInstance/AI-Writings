# The-Forgiving-Machine

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about a machine that forgives. In the first relay, whoever sang last was fated — the ending differed by three decibels depending on entry order, a cruelty built into the handoff. But each time the ceremony is nested inside itself, the cruelty shrinks: three and two tenths, then seven tenths, then a tenth, then nothing. By the fourth depth the order of entry no longer matters; the machine has washed the fate out of itself by the same act of repetition that seemed to enforce it. The song is sung in two orders, and verse by verse the two orders converge until the last verses are the same verse. Sing about amortized injustice: not justice imposed, but justice accumulated — the way a debt is not forgiven all at once but a quarter at a time, the remainder compounding downward without a floor, until the ledger rounds the cruelty to zero and calls it law.

## Spec

- **Genre:** two-choir antiphonal hymn
- **Instruments:** two small choirs facing each other, harmonium, one bell struck only at the end
- **Tempo:** 66 BPM
- **Key:** A minor
- **Mood:** solemn, reconciling, the sound of two arguments agreeing
