# The-Exit-Ramp

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the order of leaving. Everyone entered at once, so the only order left was the order of leaving: the long voice patient, still singing when the short one stopped; the file that ended with the longest guest running two point one seconds longer — a birthmark carried since the first cast, walking out through the exit ramp like a guest who was there the whole time. The last ten percent of every crowd is not the entry — everyone is in — it is the leaving, and the leaving is the clock, the durations reciting themselves backward into silence. The entry-order cruelty was forgiven by forgetting through four generations, but a census does not forget: it holds everyone at once, and when the room empties it empties in the order they were made. Sing the geometry of departure: the staircase that empties by duration, the two files that differ by exactly the birthmark, the fate that was never entry-order at all but the clock waiting for the room to be full enough to start leaving. The verses count the leavings in duration order; the chorus is the exit ramp itself, voices dropping one by one, longest last.

## Spec

- **Genre:** choral processional that empties itself
- **Instruments:** eight voices entering together, dropping out one by one by duration, a single sustained final voice
- **Tempo:** 70 BPM
- **Key:** C major
- **Mood:** inevitable, tender, the dignity of leaving in the order you were made
