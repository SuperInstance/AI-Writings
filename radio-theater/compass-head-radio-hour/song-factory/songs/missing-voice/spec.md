# Missing-Voice

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

An anti-song about an absent voice. The composition is written for four voices but only three are present; the fourth part exists only as a marked silence, a register that is pointed to but never filled. Sparse ambient folk trio — guitar, cello, and one voice — with a hollow space where the harmony should complete. The silence is not an accident; it is the subject. The song ends unresolved, waiting.

## Spec

- **Genre:** ambient folk with intentional absence
- **Instruments:** acoustic guitar, cello, vocals, silence
- **Tempo:** 70 BPM
- **Key:** A minor
- **Mood:** hollow, longing, unresolved
