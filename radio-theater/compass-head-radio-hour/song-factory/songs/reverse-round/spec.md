# Reverse-Round

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A vocal round (canon) played backwards — the song that begins with its own ending. Four voices sang the same line at staggered entries and the slowest voice finished alone; now the tape is reversed. The song opens with the slow voice's last note, alone, loud, already leaving. Voices exit as they arrived — the harmony gathers backwards, unwriting itself. The silence that lived at the end of the song now lives at the beginning: the track opens on an empty stage and ends with a single voice just learning its first phrase. Reversal is not destruction; it is relocation. The composition survives with its silence moved to the other end. A cappella choir, Renaissance round form rendered in reverse, uncanny and tender, ending on the breath before the first note.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella reverse canon (inverted vocal round)
- **Instruments:** four vocal parts, reversed reverberation, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 72 BPM
- **Key:** F major
- **Mood:** uncanny, tender, archaeological
