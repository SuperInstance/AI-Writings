# Procession

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

The round stretched past the point of breaking — a vocal procession. Four voices sing the same line, but the entries are so far apart they never overlap: each voice sings alone, in turn, separated by genuine silence. There is no harmony because there is no meeting; the only relationship between the voices is the line they share and the order they walk in. The gaps are not rest — they are the negative space where a fifth, unheard voice might have lived. The song is an archipelago: islands of the same melody in a sea of quiet, and the final voice sings into emptiness louder than anything, because there is nothing left to accompany it. Solo voice entering from silence, returning to silence, one at a time, in order, until the end.

## Spec

- **Genre:** processional anti-round (isolated solo voices with silence gaps)
- **Instruments:** single solo voice per section, room tone, no ensemble
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM
- **Key:** D minor
- **Mood:** spacious, ceremonial, solitary
