# Drifting-Round

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A vocal round (canon) about four voices singing the same line at staggered entries. The round begins in perfect unison — four voices entering one after another, chasing each other around the same melody. But one voice is slower than the others, and the round begins to drift: the parts separate, the harmony stretches, and by the end only the slow voice is still singing, alone, finishing the line by itself. The drift is not an error; it is the arrangement. The song is about imperfection becoming composition. A cappella choir, Renaissance round form, gentle and inevitable, ending on a single sustained note.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella vocal round (canon)
- **Instruments:** four vocal parts, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 72 BPM
- **Key:** F major
- **Mood:** gentle, inevitable, solitary at the end
