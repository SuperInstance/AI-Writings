# Relay-Round

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A musical relay — the song that is never sung by more than one voice at a time. Four voices pass a line of melody between them like a baton, each voice handing off to the next with a seamless crossfade, so the song never drops below one voice and never rises above it. There is no crowd, no chorus, no stacking: the density is constant from first note to last. The round was a crowd; this is a chain. The song is not louder for having four voices — it is continuous. Each handoff is a small ceremony: the outgoing voice fades as the incoming voice rises, and for a single breath they share the line, then the first is gone. The ending arrives the way every other moment did — one voice, finishing, alone, with no one left to hand off to. The transmission is the composition; the fades are the structure. A cappella solo-with-handoffs, intimate and precise, like a whispered bucket brigade across a dark room.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella vocal relay (chain canon, crossfaded handoffs)
- **Instruments:** four solo vocal parts, overlapping only in crossfades, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 76 BPM
- **Key:** G major
- **Mood:** intimate, precise, ceremonial, continuous
