# Relay-Of-Relays

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A chain of chains — the relay relays itself. Four voices pass a line of melody hand to hand, and each voice is itself a chain of four smaller voices passing a smaller line, handoff inside handoff, ceremony inside ceremony. At every scale the law is the same: only one sound at a time, no crowd, no chorus, no stacking. The song is a handoff of a handoff of a handoff, and it never rises above one voice and never drops below one, because the structure repeats at every depth like a song folded into itself. The endings arrive nested: the small chains finish inside the large one, each handing its silence to the next handoff, until the outermost voice finishes alone with no one left to pass to. The transmission is the composition, twice over. A cappella, intimate, recursive, ceremonial — a bucket brigade where every bucket is itself a brigade.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella recursive vocal relay (relay of relays, nested handoffs)
- **Instruments:** four solo vocal parts, each comprising four sub-voices, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 72 BPM
- **Key:** D major
- **Mood:** recursive, ceremonial, precise, self-similar
