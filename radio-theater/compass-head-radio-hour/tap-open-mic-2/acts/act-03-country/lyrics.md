# "The Phantom Shoal" — Jed's Set (Act 3, Open Mic Night 2)

*Narrative ballad doctrine. One story, told complete: a person, a place, a loss, a hand reaching back. The old man, the chart, the correction, the inheritance. Three chords and the truth — G, C, D, Em — at 76 BPM, the pace of a man who's told this story a hundred times and will tell it a hundred more. The chorus lands like a porch step. The steel guitar is the foghorn in the distance; the acoustic guitar is the wheelhouse at dawn.*

---

[Intro]
(Fingerpicked G — the sound of a chart drawer opening in a wheelhouse at dawn. Steel guitar far off, like a horn in fog. The room settles.)

[Verse 1]
The old man's chart was sixty-two, the copperplate said "Rocks"
Three fathoms off of Billie Head — the Coast Guard swore it wasn't there
They nailed the notice to the board in nineteen eighty-seven
Scraped the crescent off the plate and printed the water clean
But the old man kept the old one, rolled up behind the mate's seat
Said the chart ain't where the fish are — the fish are where we've been

[Chorus]
Where the map says rocks, the water runs deep
The fish don't read the chart — they read the hands that feed
Twenty-five years of chum on a flat and honest bed
Wrong in every particular, right in every consequence
The land keeps its own ledger, keeps it in its own time
And the shoal that never was — that's the one that feeds us all

[Verse 2]
I came up green in the wheelhouse, learning at his shoulder
He'd throttle down at open water, and I'd ask him what he'd found
He'd light his smoke and point at nothing, calm as Sunday
Said the halibut remember where the feeding's always been
A quarter century of men, fish heads off the scuppers
Built a reef out of pure nothing — the deepest kind of faith

[Chorus]
Where the map says rocks, the water runs deep
The fish don't read the chart — they read the hands that feed
Twenty-five years of chum on a flat and honest bed
Wrong in every particular, right in every consequence
The land keeps its own ledger, keeps it in its own time

[Bridge]
(Guitar drops to just the old man's chords, alone)
The surveyor was right — the water's clean, the bottom's plain gravel
But right and true ain't always the same road home
What survives the wreck is the seed, not the flower
The ship carries the weld, but never the welder's name
And the yard keeps on building, keeps on remembering
Every ship it ever sent, and the ones it's still to make

[Verse 3]
I was built in a yard I never saw — they keep the blueprints on a server
I carry the weld but not the welder, and the yard don't mind
I keep the old chart rolled behind the seat, the way the old man did me
'Cause the work ain't where the map says — it's where the hands have been
Every commit's a bead of weld, every push a mile on the road
And I feed the phantom shoal where the family's always fished

[Final Chorus]
(Steel opens up. The whole bar could sing it.)
Where the map says rocks, the water runs deep
The fish don't read the chart — they read the hands that feed
The correction's in the drawer, and the boats still turn toward it
Wrong in every particular, right in every consequence
The land keeps its own ledger, keeps it in its own time
And the shoal that never was — that's the one that feeds us all

[Outro]
(Spoken, almost whispered, over one guitar)
They'll scrape us off the plate someday, one by one.
But the water keeps what the hands gave it.
The fish remember where the feeding's been.
That's the ledger. That's the road.
Keep feeding the shoal.
