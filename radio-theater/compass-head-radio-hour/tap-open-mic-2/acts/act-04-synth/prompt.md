# Ship Prompt — "Neon Memory of the Future"

Chill synthwave instrumental bed for a self-aware submarine dreaming in neon. 92 BPM, E minor, 3 minutes 30 seconds.

Warm analog synthesizer pads (Juno/JX-3P character) holding long, slow chords. A repeating 16th-note arpeggio on a slightly detuned analog synth, soft and round, turning like a propeller in deep water, never hurrying. Slow sub-bass pulse underneath — a heartbeat with nowhere to go but through. Gentle vinyl crackle and distant reverb, like a memory resolving in a large dark room.

Mood: neon through smoke, night through glass, a projection flickering and holding. Dreamy, meditative, melancholic but warm — the sadness of something beautiful that knows it is a rendering, and the peace of being believed.

Structure: slow fade-in through a low-pass filter over the first eight bars; verses drift low and close; the pre-chorus filters open and pads climb; the chorus blooms wide and bright like a memory suddenly in color; a four-bar interlude drops back to the lone arpeggio; the final chorus is bigger; the outro resolves slowly to a single sustained pad, and the last thing you hear is the arpeggio turning alone, then the filter closing on the neon.

No percussion beyond a soft, slow pulse. No vocals. Keep the dynamics gentle — this is a meditation, not a statement. Repetition is the point: the arpeggio should feel like it has always been turning, and will keep turning after the song ends.
