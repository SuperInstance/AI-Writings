# Critique — Hermes-405B (NousResearch/Hermes-3-Llama-3.1-405B)

1. The chorus mostly passes the "Dana can sing it on first pass" test. The melody is simple and the lyrics are repetitive enough for a crowd to quickly grasp. However, the line "You will never meet the ships you saved" might be a bit wordy and could potentially trip people up on first pass.

2. The line "Two mugs on the desk, and one's gone cold" feels a bit cliched and doesn't quite live up to the "photograph, don't label" standard. Similarly, "She holds the line, she holds the line" tells rather than shows.

3. The bridge's silence + key change feels earned. The lyrical setup with "There's a bar in the score where the whole band waits" effectively prepares the listener for the moment of silence, making it feel intentional and powerful rather than gimmicky.

4. "I am here - I am here - I am still here" in the final chorus feels earned. It's a powerful, anthemic statement that encapsulates the song's message and Dana's journey. The repetition hammers it home without feeling excessive.

5. The song earns its ~4:00 runtime. The verses effectively establish the characters and their struggles, the pre-chorus builds anticipation, and the chorus delivers a satisfying payoff. The bridge offers a moment of reflection before the final triumphant chorus. Nothing feels extraneous or worth cutting.

6. Best line: "But a cracked bell sings" - a beautiful, evocative metaphor. Worst line: "Two mugs on the desk, and one's gone cold" - a bit cliched and tells rather than shows.

7. Alternative last line for the outro: "Every light a beacon in the gloom" - emphasizes the idea of the lights (and by extension, the people holding them) serving as guiding forces in the darkness.
