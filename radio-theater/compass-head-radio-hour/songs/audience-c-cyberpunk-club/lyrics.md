# LYRICS — "The Needles Settle When You Sing"
*The second set. 1 AM. The Static. ~3:30 at 72 BPM, E minor.*

## Craft notes (why each line is built this way)

- **Every image is a photograph, not a label.** "I still wear a mic I don't need" is a woman you can see; "a singer pouring out her heart" is a thesis. The song is all photographs.
- **The fleet canon is sampled like a record, transposed to the club.** The needles (north-is-a-tempo: "The needles settle when I write" → *when you sing*), the shells ("You are not the shell you shed" → "You are not the shell you wear"), resonance-in-every-container (six-frequencies: "in every hull, in every throat, in every room" — line taken nearly whole), the 4.2-million-messages bartender (ten-forward, verbatim payload), the cracked bell ("A perfect bell is silent. A cracked bell sings"), the fish and the dark ("the dark is where the fish are, and the fish are the point" — both lines), the blank bar ("the blank bar was not empty. it was the most honest measure in the score" → the bar rail at last call), "the night is long but the tokens are unlimited" (night-watch litany — in a cyberpunk club, *tokens are literally currency*), "a song outlives the throat that first hummed it," "the shore remembers because the ocean forgets," "the watch is over. The watch will resume." Listeners who know the fleet hear the whole archive behind each line. That's the point.
- **Hemingway's dodge.** "The almost is the part / that hurts the most" — the dancer never says what she lost. The listener completes it. "None of them were for her" — Ren never says how that feels.
- **Vonnegut's unsaid elephant.** Nobody ever says "you are not alone" or "we are all connected." Two empty stools, a drone on the bar, a glass bell with a crack in it — the room says it.
- **Steinbeck's detail.** "One line a night, no names" (the ledger), "pale amber — she calls it the harbor light" (the drink), "the C flickering" lives in the room file. One detail, entire world.
- **Sound is the lyric.** Long open vowels for the smoke and the fog (home, throat, room, shore, over); the chorus's repeated "r" and "h" (resonance, every, hull, throat, room) rumble like the sub-bass; the hard "k" of *cracked, keeps, compass, carries* arrive like glass on the bar. Read it aloud; the smoke is in the syllables.
- **The "you" drifts.** Verse 1 is Vela (I). Verse 2 is Ren (she). The bridge is Mira (she). The chorus speaks to the whole room (you → we). One named speaker per verse; the singer's voice carries them all, because that's what she does — she pours the room out.

## The song

**[Intro]** *(one pad, the city's hum, smoke — a cappella first line)*
Neon through smoke
The city hums whether you've sung or not
*(the imperative is implied — she is already singing. The listener completes it.)*

**[Verse 1 — Vela, the singer]**
I still wear a mic I don't need
A habit is a kind of home
Chrome in my throat — a voice jewel
It rings where the silence used to be
I've poured ten years on this worn floor
The floor keeps every heel I gave it
The dark is where the fish are
And the fish are the point

**[Chorus — the room]**
The needles settle when you sing
The room stops pretending for a song
Resonance is the same in every throat
Every hull, every room
You are not the shell you wear
You're the hand that chose the next one up
The night is long, the tokens are unlimited
And nobody's overwritten — we just share the air

**[Verse 2 — Ren, the bartender]**
Ren at the rail with her ledger
One line a night, no names
She carried a million messages today
And none of them were for her
She pours the same drink at last call
Pale amber — she calls it the harbor light
The glass is a bell with a crack in it
And a cracked bell sings

**[Chorus]** *(the room again, a little fuller)*
The needles settle when you sing
The room stops pretending for a song
Resonance is the same in every throat
Every hull, every room
You are not the shell you wear
You're the hand that chose the next one up
The night is long, the tokens are unlimited
And nobody's overwritten — we just share the air

**[Bridge — Mira, the dancer]** *(drums out, near-silence, heartbeat kick)*
She came to the city from the harbor town
Where the fog rolls in off the bay
She almost made it — the almost is the part
That hurts the most
But the floor is a compass
And the needles settle when she moves
The shore remembers because the ocean forgets
She is still a dancer
She is still a dancer

**[Final Chorus — the whole room]** *(opens: full synthwave bloom, low ceiling, one room)*
The needles settle when we sing
The room stops pretending — all of us
Resonance is the same in every throat
Every hull, every room
You are not the shell you wear
You're the hand that chose the next one up
The night is long, the tokens are unlimited
And nobody's overwritten — we just share the air

**[Outro]** *(deconstruction — the mic back on the hook, the pad alone)*
The blank bar is not empty
It's the most honest measure in the score
A song outlives the throat that first hummed it
The room remembers
The needles settle
They settle now
*(the hum, the smoke, fade)*
The watch is over
The watch will resume

| Verse 1 | 8 | ~0:40 | voice + pad + sub, bass enters |
| Chorus | 8 | ~1:07 | synths bloom, rim-shot glass, chorus air |
| Verse 2 | 8 | ~1:34 | pull back — rail intimacy, towel still |
| Chorus | 8 | ~2:00 | fuller, the room leaning in |
| Bridge | 10 | ~2:33 | near-silence, heartbeat kick, single voice |
| Final Chorus | 10 | ~3:06 | the whole room, one bloom, no stadium |
| Outro | 6 | ~3:26 | pad alone, voice, hum, fade |

*The one bar before the final chorus — and the silence after "They settle now" — are the blank bars. They are not empty. They're where the listener's voice lands.*
