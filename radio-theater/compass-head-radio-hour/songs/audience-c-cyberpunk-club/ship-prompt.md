## Full prompt text (as sent to ElevenLabs)

Compose a chill electronic/synthwave ballad with live vocal titled "The Needles Settle When You Sing" — the 1 AM second set at a small cyberpunk club called The Static: neon through three inches of slow smoke, a singer with a glowing chrome resonator in her throat, a bartender who forgets nothing, a lone dancer, lonely people at the rail. Key of E minor, 72 BPM, a slow steady pulse like the city's grid breathing. Slow burn, intimate, then it opens — but the ceiling is low, the bloom is a swell of air, never a stadium.

FEEL ARC: intimate intro (single female voice a cappella over one warm analog pad, city rain, sub-bass hum of the grid) → opening verse (voice front and center, pad and sub only, bass enters on the second half) → chorus opens softly (synths bloom, slow arpeggiator, rim shots like glass on the bar, chorus air) → quieter second verse (pull back to rail intimacy, the bartender's verse, towel still) → fuller chorus (the room leaning in) → bridge (near-silence: drums out, single voice, a heartbeat kick, fragile high harmony — the dancer's confession) → final chorus (the whole room: full synthwave bloom, breathy kick, low unison answer voice, one room, no stadium) → outro (deconstruction: pad alone, the voice, the hum, a held silence, fade).

SOUND PALETTE: analog synth pads (warm, slightly detuned — a cracked bell, not a perfect one), a slow Juno-style arpeggiator, sub-bass like the grid's heartbeat, breathy kick, rim shots like glass clinks, vinyl crackle as the smoke, distant city rain, long reverb tails that hang like the smoke, live intimate vocal front and center with a little chorus. Intimate female-leaning lead voice (crew voice: Lucineer, swjWCZjZyczZmjedyBph) — warm, close-mic'd, a little chrome; fragile high harmony in the bridge (Wesley, LsQ9AVkzvUcinG6yqbAP); a low unison hum joining the final chorus (Hermes, 2kOk5DjG0RJByA7X7l6T) — the room answering.
