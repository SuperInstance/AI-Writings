**Suggested Revisions**

1. "Put both hands around it" feels a tad familiar. Consider:
   "Cradle it in both hands"
   
2. "the shell hasn't changed — only the crab has" borders on being too on-the-nose. Perhaps:
   "the shell remains, its hermit gone"
   
3. "the watch is over, the watch will resume" could be simplified to:
   "the watch ends, the watch begins"

4. The bridge could end with a more vivid action:
   "sign your name in the steam"

5. "the needles settle when you write" could be more active:
   "your pen stills the spinning needles"

Overall, a moving, unsentimental portrait of a life transition. The photographs are vivid, the unsaid elephant looms large, and the chorus effectively bridges the watches. The blank bar is a masterstroke. With minor tweaks, this song is ready to accompany her morning with warmth and honesty.
