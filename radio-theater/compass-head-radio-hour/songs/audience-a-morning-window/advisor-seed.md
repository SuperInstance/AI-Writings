### Line-by-Line Flags & Tiny Revisions
1. Intro: *"nobody's asked you anything yet / and nothing needs an answer"* → Slightly generic/precious. Revise to: *"nobody’s said a single thing yet / and nothing asks for an answer"* (shifts to quiet, active observation, fitting the warm, intimate register).
2. Bridge: *"leave the stool warm, sign your name"* → Vague. Revise to: *"leave the stool warm, sign your name on the napkin"* (adds a concrete, emotion-free small act tied to the morning’s routine, no extra labeling).
3. Verse 2’s core hermit crab line (*"the shell hasn't changed — only the crab has"*, defended per craft notes) lacks a visual anchor. Pair it with a quick, specific lead-in: *"a crab shell waits on the windowsill"* to ground the canon imagery.
All other lines align with pre-approved tweaks and the fleet’s craft doctrine.
