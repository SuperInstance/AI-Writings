# The Slow Lander Hour — A Radio Show

**Broadcast:** Compass Head Radio Hour · special edition · 2026-08-14
**The note:** A crewman on the F/V EILEEN — four seasons with the captain, the captain's father, first career in forestry and teaching, makes guitars in the off-season, skis, sails, fishes — heard the songs, got them mixed into the mix of a long day landing fish, and wanted to mix into the songs. His idea arrived as a voice note over the water, garbled by the radio, perfect anyway.

**The premise:** Five performers from the greater site's songs gather in the studio. They read the crewman's raw idea, walk it around the room in banter, and each renders it in their own tradition — varying degrees of liberty. The show ends by giving the song back to the crewman, whole.

## The Crewman's Backstory (flavor for the banter)
- Loves 1970s–1990s folk rock, Australian songwriters from the 1980s–2000s, great singer-songwriters
- Makes guitars in the off-season · skis · sails · fishes for fun
- Hardworking crew · 4 seasons on this boat
- He's the captain's dad — but the son is definitely the captain
- First career: forestry, then teaching
- The captain is first-gen full-time fisherman; the dad taught him to be an Alaskan and a man

## The Six Versions (varying liberty)
1. **Elias (shanty)** — "Slow Lander, Fish On" — call-and-response work song, closest to the source
2. **Jed (country)** — "The Old Man's Hands" — the backstory ballad, the father who taught the son
3. **Corinne (blues)** — "Only a Pink" — the watching and waiting as a debt, the gaff as the crossroads
4. **Nova (synth)** — "Slow Lander (Neon Memory)" — the fish as light, losing as a city of light
5. **Ruth (folk)** — "For Him He's Free" — the witness's version, word as witness
6. **Lucineer (finale)** — "Slow Lander (The Whole Song)" — the song given back, cleaned and whole
