A delta blues, twelve-bar AAB form, in the voice of Corinne the blueswoman. The song: "Only a Pink." The watching and waiting as a debt — you watch a man miss a fish and you've paid something; you watch him not care, you've paid something else. Hollow-body guitar, slide, harmonica, brushed drums, blue light and smoke. Slow, aching, bent notes. The smallest fish in the sea treated like it owes him a conversation. Sorrow as a ledger, paid in notes. The guitar is the second voice.
