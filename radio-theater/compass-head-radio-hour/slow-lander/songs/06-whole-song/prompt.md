A warm folk-rock song in the spirit of the great 1970s and 1980s singer-songwriters. Acoustic and electric guitars, a band that has played together for years, harmonies on the chorus. Steady, generous, wise. The story of a patient fisherman and his crew: a slow landing, a small fish, a fish that gets away, and the freedom in letting it go. The last line is the whole song: for him, he is free.
