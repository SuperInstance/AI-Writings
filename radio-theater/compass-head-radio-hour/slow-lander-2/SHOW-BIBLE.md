# The Slow Lander Hour — The Seasoned Takes (Season 2)

**Broadcast:** Compass Head Radio Hour · special edition 2 · 2026-08-14
**The premise:** The first broadcast went out. The crewman heard it. And the five performers heard it too — and each other. They came back to the studio the same night, not to change anything. They have their sounds; nobody's changing that. The seasoned takes: the same songs, played by the same hands, deeper — the way a song sounds after it's been lived in.

## The Rule (from the performers themselves)
"We don't want to sound like anything. We have our sounds — and we want you to hear them, not change them."
Each performer heard what the others did with the song. They kept their own lane. The seasoning is in the playing, not the palette.
