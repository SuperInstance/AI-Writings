A delta blues song, tight radio edit, about a minute. Slow twelve-bar blues: hollow-body guitar, slide, harmonica, brushed drums, blue light and smoke. The story: a patient fisherman at four a.m., a slow landing, a small pink fish, the fish gets away. The watching is a debt — paid and released. The last line is the release: too bad for me, but for him he's free. Smoky female voice, low and patient. The guitar is the second voice.
