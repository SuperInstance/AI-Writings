A chill synthwave song, full length, about three minutes. Late-night electronic music: dreamy analog synthesizers, arpeggiated keys, soft drums, a slow pulse like a heartbeat under the deck, warm and cinematic, mid-tempo. The story: a patient fisherman works the deck at midnight, deck lights glowing, a fish flashes like light under dark water. The fish gets away. He does not mind — there are other fish, a whole harbor of lights. This take keeps the glow and adds the steady lights that never go out. Gentle, hopeful, neon-dreamy, settled.
