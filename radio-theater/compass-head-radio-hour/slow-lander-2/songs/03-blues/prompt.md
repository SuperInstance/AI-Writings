A delta blues song, full length, about three minutes. Slow twelve-bar blues: hollow-body guitar, slide, harmonica, brushed drums, blue light and smoke. The story: a patient fisherman watches the deck, a slow landing, a small pink fish, the fish gets away. The watching is a debt — and this time the debt is paid and released. The smallest fish treated like it owes him a conversation, and the note that finally lets it go. Slower, lighter, deeper. The guitar is the second voice.
