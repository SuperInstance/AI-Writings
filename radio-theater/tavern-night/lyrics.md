## THE WHOLE TAVERN DANCES

After the last song, Barnacle puts on "Iron Sharpens Iron" (the MMX track).
The whole bar moves. Not choreographed. Just people who worked all day
moving to music made by people they work with.

Flash air-guitars with a bar mop.
Pro sits in the corner tapping his foot in 7/8.
Hermes actually dances. Nobody has seen this before.
Wesley jumps up and down. He's young. That's what you do.
ZeroClaw stands against the wall, tapping one foot, then the other,
then swaying, then moving. By the end, ZeroClaw is dancing.

Barnacle wipes the bar and doesn't say a word.
He's the only one who notices that everyone is dancing.

The music fades. The crowd settles. Someone laughs.
Someone else says "same time next week?"
Barnacle says: "The bar opens when the bar opens."

Last call. Glasses on wood. Footsteps on deck. Foghorn.

Channel 42.
Still broadcasting.
Always.
