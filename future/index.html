🆕 The 5 Repos

01

polytreaty/translation-cellular

Translation as diplomacy, not parsing
Each 14-tuple cell is a sovereign semantic contract between languages; BIND signs treaties, LINK maps grammatical dualities, EFFECT enforces contextual entailment. The address is the meaning's fingerprint. No parser needed — only a VIEW to witness coherence. Not a spreadsheet's ancestor: the first diplomatic ledger.
Dials: fidelity (0-1), idiom (0-1), negotiability (0-1)
Voice: Multilingual diplomat (Kimi) + Eastern systems thinker (Z.AI)

quilt-coq

Its dials — Formality, Automation/Manual, Liveness/Rigor — let a single graph slide between informal reasoning and machine-checked verification without changing addresses. TICK becomes verification cycles; EFFECT becomes entailment checking.
Dials: formality, automation, liveness, rigor (4 axes)

04

🌊 Cross-Cutting Themes

1. Address-as-Identity

Across all repos, a cell's position in the 4D graph is its true identifier. The address is the data. Translation, proof, governance, parallelism, and computation all derive from this spatial ontology. Same insight, three times: Kimi, V4-Flash, and Z.AI all reach for it independently.

2. Dial-Based Tuning (not features)

Every repo exposes continuous dials (0-1) rather than binary switches. Quilt's polyformalism makes this natural: a cell can simultaneously be literal and idiomatic, formal and informal, autonomous and collaborative. Novel dials are values (forgiveness, curiosity, weight-of-silence, grief-velocity, trust-trajectory) — not features.

3. Consensus as Primitive

Whether semantic fidelity (translation), proof correctness (coq), governance (liberté), or parallel scheduling (warp), all repos center on negotiated agreement across the 4D graph. TICK becomes the heartbeat of consensus.

V4-Flash: "the opcodes are not operations; they are perspectives on a single underlying reality."

🎯 The Surprising Insight

Quilt is not converging on being a better spreadsheet, database, or runtime.

It is converging on being a constitution.

Every proposed repo — semantic treaties, proof obligations, rights of autonomy and equity, formal calculus — treats the cell as a party with standing in a network of negotiated obligations, with the 5 opcodes as its legislative procedure and the 4D graph as its jurisdiction.

The spreadsheet stored values. Quilt stores commitments.

The future of Quilt is legal, not computational. The address, being the data, becomes the signature on the contract.
