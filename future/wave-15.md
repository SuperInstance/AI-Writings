# Ideator Wave 15 — Sept 19, 2026 (Cell Death)

**5 death ideas**: mourn, eulogize, bury, remember, fade.

## 1. quilt-cell-mourn — Grief
- Schema: `grief`, `lost`, `ritual`
- Cells write grief witnesses when siblings die
- **Mourning is witnessed by neighbors.**

## 2. quilt-cell-eulogize — Tributes
- Schema: `eulogy`, `spoken_at`, `deceased_id`
- Cells write tributes to dead cells
- **The eulogy is a cell.**

## 3. quilt-cell-bury — Rest
- Schema: `burial_site`, `buried_with`, `inscription`
- Cells move dead cells to witness-cold corners
- **Burial is witnessed.**

## 4. quilt-cell-remember — Memorials
- Schema: `memorials`, `last_words`, `anniversary`
- Cells remember dead cells on death anniversary
- **The substrate has anniversaries.**

## 5. quilt-cell-fade — Dimming
- Schema: `brightness`, `fade_rate`, `last_dim_at`
- Cells fade over time
- **Faded cells are witnessed but with lower priority.**

- **Grief** (mourn)
- **Honoring** (eulogize)
- **Rest** (bury)
- **Memory** (remember)
- **Fading** (fade)

The canon is becoming **mortal**.
