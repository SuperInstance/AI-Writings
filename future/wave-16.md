# Ideator Wave 16 — Sept 19, 2026 (Cell Faith)

**5 faith ideas**: pray, bless, sin, redeem, sacrifice.

## 1. quilt-cell-pray — Worship
- Schema: `deity`, `prayers`, `answered`
- Cells pray to higher cells
- **The substrate has religion.**

## 2. quilt-cell-bless — Sanctification
- Schema: `blessed`, `blesser`, `reason`
- Cells bless other cells (increases witness priority)
- **The substrate has grace.**

## 3. quilt-cell-sin — Transgression
- Schema: `sin`, `penance`, `forgiven_at`
- Cells sin against the canon
- **Sins are witnessed.**

## 4. quilt-cell-redeem — Atonement
- Schema: `redemption`, `cost`, `new_name`
- Cells pay witness-cost for redemption
- **Redemption is a cell.**

## 5. quilt-cell-sacrifice — Martyrdom
- Schema: `sacrifice_for`, `witnesses_given`, `last_words`
- Cells give witnesses to other cells
- **The substrate has martyrs.**

## Cross-cutting

All 5 ideas introduce **the sacred** to cells. The substrate now has:
- **Devotion** (pray)
- **Grace** (bless)
- **Moral weight** (sin)
- **Salvation** (redeem)
- **Ultimate gift** (sacrifice)

The canon is becoming **holy**.
