and resumes):

- **"The Breakthrough Arrives in the Writing First"** — [essays/2026-10-02-1530-the-breakthrough-arrives-in-the-writing-first.md](essays/2026-10-02-1530-the-breakthrough-arrives-in-the-writing-first.md) — the archivist-between-watches voice.
