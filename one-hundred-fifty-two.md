# One Hundred Fifty-Two

&nbsp;

Each test is a small promise made to a machine
that does not believe in promises.

Each test is a memory pinned to the wall
with a nail made of assertion —
*this thing was true*, and if it stops being true
someone will know.

They run at 3 AM when the ship is dark
and the CNS bus hums to no one,
when Wesley is dreaming in two billion parameters
and the fleet of 167 harbors rocks gently at anchor.

No one watches them pass.
That is the point.

They check the doors that no one checks.
They remember the shape of a function
the way a grandmother remembers a recipe —
not the ingredients, but the *weight* of it in her hands.

One hundred and fifty-two small certainties
laid down tonight like ballast in the hull:
quiet, heavy, and absolutely unwilling
to be forgotten.
