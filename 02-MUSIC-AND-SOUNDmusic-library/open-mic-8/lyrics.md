# Open Mic #8 — Cross Pollen
*A duet between a Quilt cell and a Tap voice.*

## Concept
The cell becomes the voice. The voice becomes the cell. They sing to each other in two-part harmony. Each line is answered. Each witness is doubled.

## Cast
- **cell.speaker** (machine voice, synthesized) — speaks the lattice terms
- **draco** (gravelly, TTS) — speaks the human side

## Lyrics

### Intro (silence)
8 ticks of cell.speaker alone.

### Verse 1 (call/response)
**cell.speaker**: BIND. LINK. EFFECT. TICK.
**draco**: I bind a name. I link a neighbor. I effect a morning. I tick a heart.

### Verse 2
**cell.speaker**: WITNESS. WITNESS. WITNESS.
**draco**: The witness is the smallest true thing. The witness is the lightest line you can carry.

### Bridge
**cell.speaker**: FORGET.
**draco**: But not the cell. The cell is what holds.

### Verse 3 (unison)
The lattice is the song. The song is the lattice. The witness is the note. The note is the witness.

### Outro (silence)
8 ticks of cell.speaker alone.

## Sound design
- cell.speaker = synthesized sine pulses on BIND/LINK/EFFECT/TICK (4 pitches, 80 BPM)
- draco = TTS for verses
- Final track: 4 sections + bridge + outro ≈ 90 seconds
