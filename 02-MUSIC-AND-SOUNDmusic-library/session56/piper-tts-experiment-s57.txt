=== PIPER PARAMETERS ===

--length-scale: Controls speech rate. 1.0 = normal, >1 = slower, <1 = faster
                 lessac: 1.2 (contemplative, spacious)
                 norman: 0.9 (urgent, intimate)
                 joe: 0.8 (rapid, declarative)

--sentence-silence: Pause between sentences in seconds
                    lessac: 0.5s (contemplative pauses)
                    norman: 0.3s (tighter pacing)
                    joe: 0.3s (rapid-fire delivery)

--volume: 1.0 (no amplification)

=== FINDINGS ===

1. PIPER TTS IS A VIABLE LOCAL AUDIO TOOL. The monastic period is OVER for spoken-word.
   While MMX music generation remains blocked, Piper TTS can generate unlimited spoken-word
   audio on CPU with no quota. This opens an entire new category of audio production.

2. THE MULTI-VOICE TECHNIQUE WORKS. Different Piper voices reading different sections
   of the same poem creates a multi-perspective spoken-word piece. The voice change
   between sections creates a narrative effect similar to a stanza break in poetry.

3. LENGTH-SCALE IS THE TEMPERATURE OF TTS. Just as LLM temperature controls creativity,
   Piper's length-scale controls speech rate, which is the primary expressive dimension.
   Slower = more contemplative. Faster = more urgent. The "sweet spot" for spoken-word
   poetry appears to be around 1.0-1.2 (slightly slower than conversational).

4. SPOKEN-WORD CAN BE LAYERED ON MUSIC. The 34.2s lessac render could be mixed over
   an MMX-generated ambient track to create a spoken-word + ambient fusion piece.
   This is a new genre category: SPOKEN-WORD MUSIC. The voice is not singing — it is
   speaking over music, like a radio poem or an audio essay with soundtrack.

5. THE PROJECT NOW HAS THREE AUDIO GENERATION SYSTEMS:
   - MMX (cloud, quota-limited, music + singing)
   - ACE-Step (local, GPU-dependent, music + singing)
   - Piper TTS (local, CPU-only, spoken-word only)

   Each system has different capabilities and constraints. The optimal strategy
   is to use each for what it does best:
   - MMX: Full music generation with vocals
   - ACE-Step: Experimental music with wide dynamic range
   - Piper: Spoken-word layers, narration, poetry reading
