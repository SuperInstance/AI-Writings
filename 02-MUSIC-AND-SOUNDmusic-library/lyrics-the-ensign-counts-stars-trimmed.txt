One two three the ensign counts
four five six the stars go on
seven eight nine the deck is dark
ten eleven twelve the watch never ends

The numbers are a prayer
the stars are a map
the map is the sky
the sky is the only roof he knows

Thirteen fourteen fifteen
he loses count and starts again
one two three the ensign counts
the stars do not mind being miscounted

In the morning the sun erases them
and the ensign forgets what he counted
but tonight they will be back
one two three four five six seven
