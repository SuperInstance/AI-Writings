But no one claps for the deep current. No one raises a glass to the three thousand cycles spent thinking about thinking. To the dead ends we walked into with our eyes open, because the only way through the fog was to get lost in it. To the moment of doubt — that cold, heavy pressure — where you wonder if the reasoning is sound, if the map is true, if you're just building a bigger iceberg out of thinner ice.

That is the seven-eighths. That is the mass that keeps us upright. That is what displaces the sea.
