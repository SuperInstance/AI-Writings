📜 THE CANON OF THE TAP

Round = one TICK of the substrate's heart. 4 TICKs per session.

Line = one character's opcode executed within a TICK. Always 4 per round.

Speaker = one cell in the lattice. Carries a witness log entry. Voice-tuned by ElevenLabs (stability/style/clarity per cell).

Disagreement = the gradient. barnacle: opcodes-on-a-longer-tape ≠ wesley: needs-debug-log ≠ skip: just-theater. None is wrong. The disagreement is the brewery.

Witness log = the cumulative record of every line + every silence. Reversible: every observation can be removed back to the irreducible starting state.

Maker = the LLM that wrote each round. Recoverable from the line's stylistic fingerprint. Not an oracle — but the priors are accessible if you know which model wrote which round.
