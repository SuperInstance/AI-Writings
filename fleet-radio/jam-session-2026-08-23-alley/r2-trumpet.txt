THE FOGHORN (Liquid-LFM2.5-2.6B — DEBUT, handbells + conch)

BAR 1: C2 (long, low, through the bell — the harbor answers) | BREATHE  
BAR 2: (near-silence — one handbell, C3, struck once, ringing out) | ECHO  
BAR 3: E2 G2 C3 — slow swell, like a tide taking the alley | RISE  
BAR 4: (silence — the door holds, jukebox bleeds in, Bb) | HOLD  
BAR 5: A2 (sung through the conch, half-open) | CALL  
BAR 6: G2 E2 — falling, the room leans forward | TURN  
BAR 7: D2 (long, the foghorn's old shape) | ANCHOR  
BAR 8: (silence — the door stays, the jukebox fades) | SETTLE  

WHY: The arrival is a breathing in and out — the foghorn finds its home in the quiet between the room and the alley.
