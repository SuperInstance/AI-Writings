**PIANO SOLO: Bb BLUES (BARS 1–8)**

---

**BAR 1:** [Bb Q, F stacc, Ab grace, Bb Q, D stacc, F stacc, Bb Q]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mp, breathy, hushed  
- **WHY:** Enter with a *call*—root, fourth, and blue note in triplet rhythm. Whispered yet urgent, like a low talk before the storm.

**BAR 2:** [F stacc, G grace, Ab stacc, Bb stacc, F stacc, Eb grace, F stacc]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mp to mf, subtle growl on F  
- **WHY:** Chromatic step-down to Bb with a bent Eb to hit home. Agitated yet controlled—like walking the edge of the groove.

**BAR 3:** [Bb stacc, D grace, F stacc, G grace, Ab stacc, Bb Q, F grace]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mf, finger snapping on Ab  
- **WHY:** Lock into the rhythm section’s pulse with a Bb7 outline. Syncopated accents like a second drummer—piano as rhythm, not just harmony.

**BAR 4:** [F stacc, Ab grace, Bb stacc, F stacc, Eb grace, F stacc, Bb stacc]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mf, bent Eb for tension  
- **WHY:** Mirror the trumpet’s line from BAR 1 but with a darker, inside-out bluesy shade. Tension then release—*that* is the blues.

**BAR 5:** [Bb stacc, G grace, F stacc, Ab stacc, Bb stacc, D grace, F stacc]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mf to f, staccato with punch  
- **WHY:** Shift to 16th-note rhythm—tight, articulated, and swinging. Respond to the band’s rising energy with precision and bite.

**BAR 6:** [F stacc, Eb grace, D stacc, C grace, Bb stacc, F grace, G stacc]  
- **DYNAMICS:** f, aggressive on F and Bb  
- **WHY:** Descend with urgency, bluesy and gritty. Play the *feeling* of Bb7—each note a question, a pause, a *no*.

**BAR 7:** [Bb stacc, D grace, F stacc, G grace, Ab stacc, Bb Q, F grace]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mf, wide vibrato on final Bb  
- **WHY:** Answer the sax’s cry with a soulful, vocal line. Play not just the chord, but the *emotion* behind it.

**BAR 8:** [F stacc, Ab grace, Bb stacc, Eb grace, F stacc, D grace, Bb stacc]  
- **DYNAMICS:** mf to mp, soft decay on final Bb  
- **WHY:** Return to the root, but with a shade of altered tension. End not with a statement, but a question—leaving space for the next voice.

---

**WHY (OVERALL):**  
To step into the conversation the band has begun—listening, reacting, and *saying* without shouting. The piano is the connective tissue here, stitching the blues into the rhythm and the horns into each other. This solo is not about fireworks—it’s about *tone*, *intent*, and *space*. Let the notes speak, but let the *silence* say more.
