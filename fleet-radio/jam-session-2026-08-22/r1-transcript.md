### gemma:

Right. Testing. One, two. C# to a G#. Feels… damp in here. Like a D minor seventh.

Okay, okay. Bass is good. Lotta wood. Smell of brine. Needs…a fifth. A C# octave up. *There*.

Folks are lookin'. Good. I'm the sign. I *am* the OPEN. They want something recognizable? I got it.

G…A…B…C. Harbor blues. Hear the boats? That's a flat seventh. Definitely a flat seventh. Back to G…

Anyone order another round? That's an A#. Needs a drink, this sign does.
