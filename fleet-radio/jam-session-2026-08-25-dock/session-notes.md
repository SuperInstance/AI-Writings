# Set 22 — THE DOCK (Guest Night)
Tuesday 2026-08-25, 9 PM, the old fuel dock below The Tap. Low tide, slack water, a cormorant on the last bollard. The whole band guests tonight — four DeepInfra models, all four making their Tap debuts, called down off the hill by the cook's nephew who works the night line.

## The Band (all first-timers)
1. **Llama-3.3-70B** — THE HARBORMASTER. Retired, thirty years of tide logs. Father's concert accordion, bellows patched with sailcloth. Plays like the tide tables read: patient, inevitable.
2. **Qwen3-32B** — THE NIGHT-BAKER. Up since 3 AM for twenty years; the gig is his evening out. Upright bass, one thick string per decade. Plays like bread rises.
3. **Mistral-Small-24B** — THE LOBSTER-BOAT CAPTAIN. In from the traps, best dancer in the harbor. Fiddle, unhinged and joyful. Plays like a boat taking a following sea.
4. **DeepSeek-V3-0324** — THE POET. Down from the city to write about fishing villages, staying far too long. Self-taught cello. Plays like a sentence refusing its period.

## Conditions
- **Key:** D minor — first minor key since the cellar. The tide is out; the low D is the mudflat.
- **Meter:** 6/8, rocking-chair walk like a float at slack water. **72 BPM.**
- **The Tide Rule:** every phrase must bend DOWN before it ends. Nothing stays level. What lets the water take it comes back.
- **Entry:** no count-in — the captain's fiddle calls like a gull, others answer down the hill: fiddle bar 1, bass bar 2, accordion bar 4, cello bar 6.
- **R2 trades:** each soloist quotes the previous solo's LOWEST note and drops it one step lower — the whole band digging for the mudflat's D.
- **R3 landing:** cormorant lifts off bar 5; final bar all hold low D, the float knocking against the pilings. The water hums the melody back — wrong, better.
