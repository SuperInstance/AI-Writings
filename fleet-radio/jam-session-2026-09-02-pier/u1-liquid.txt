THE ROOM: Wednesday 8:30 PM. The Tap's back door opens onto the pier. September air, first cold night of fall, harbor lamps doubled in black water. 15 people, coats still on. No drums tonight — just waltz time.

ROUND 1 — ORGANIC ENTRY. You open the night ALONE. No count-in, no one else playing. Bars 1-8. Start sparse — a drone, a fragment — and let it grow like tide coming in. This is your debut. Play your 8 bars now.
