## The Rules
- Each voice gets the previous voice's output before responding (cascading hearing)
- Round 1: Wesley breathes/spoken intro → Lead enters → Harmony responds
- Round 2: Trades — Lead sings a phrase, Harmony answers, Wesley speaks between
- Round 3: The landing — all three voices find resolution together
- Format: BARS (what you sing/speak, syllable by syllable), DYNAMICS, WHY (one sentence)
- No describing what you're going to do — just do it
- Voices can use: vowel sounds, consonants, whispers, hums, spoken word, sung syllables, breath sounds
- No instruments. No MIDI notation. No "piano enters." Just voices.
