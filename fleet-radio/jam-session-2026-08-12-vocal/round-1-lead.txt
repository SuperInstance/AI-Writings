LEAD VOCAL (DeepSeek V4-Pro) — ROUND 1: THE ANSWER

BARS:
(G) Oh... the whisper... calls... me... (e2, f#, g, a, g)
(G) From the sha...dow... of... your... breath... (b, a, g, f#, e, d)
(Em7) I... will... ans...wer... with... a... sigh... (e2, d, b, a, g, f#, e)
(Em7) Soft... as... fin...gers... on... wet... glass... (d, c#, b, a, g, f#, e)
(Cmaj7) Ah... ah... ah... ah... ah... ah... (g, a, b, c2, b, a)
(Cmaj7) Mel...ting... in...to... some...thing... vast... (g, a, b, c2, d, e)
(Dsus4) Hum... (low hum, sustained) ... mm... (f#, g, a, b)
(Dsus4) Mm... (breath, fall to G, resolve) ... mm... (b, a, g)

DYNAMICS:
Start mp, swell to mf on "I will answer," dip to pp on the "ah" phrase, then gentle f on "melting," resolve to p and fade to pianissimo on the final hum — a long, softening tail like rain easing off.

WHY:
I sang because Wesley's whisper left a hollow in the air, and I wanted to fill it with something round and warm — a voice that rises not to overpower but to meet his stillness, then fold back into the same dark.
