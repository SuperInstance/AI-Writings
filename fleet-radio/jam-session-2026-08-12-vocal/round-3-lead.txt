LEAD VOCAL (DeepSeek V4-Pro) — ROUND 3: THE LANDING

BARS (4 bars over G):
Bar 1: "Home" (G3 — held, swelling softly)
Bar 2: "is" (F#3, leaning down)
Bar 3: "a" (E3, breathy, almost spoken)
Bar 4: "whisper" — D3, then rest, letting the D ring into the rain.

DYNAMICS: Start at mezzo-piano, decay to piano, then fade to near-nothing on the final D, held two beats beyond the bar — the room inhales with you.

WHY: Because home isn't a shout — it's the last breath you take when you finally stop searching.
