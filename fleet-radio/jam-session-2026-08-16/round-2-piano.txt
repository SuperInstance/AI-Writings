BAR 1 (Cm9):  
Eb4 G4 D5. Quarter notes. No root. Held just long enough that they bleed into each other.  
DYNAMICS: pianissimo. Thumbs brushing white keys like you’re wiping dust off glass.  
WHY: You start not with an announcement, but with the exact three notes the bass left hanging in the air two bars ago. You didn’t invent them. You just finally let them land.

BAR 2 (Cm9):  
D5 > Bb4 > G4. Eighth notes, lazy swing. Then rest. Rest. Rest the whole back half of the bar.  
DYNAMICS: same, barely there. The last note fades completely before the bar ends.  
WHY: You don’t have to fill the space. The room already knows how to breathe quiet. You just proved it.

BAR 3 (Abmaj7):  
C5 E5 Ab5. Soft triad, rolled slow from bottom to top. One roll. Hold.  
DYNAMICS: tiny swell, just enough that someone leaning forward would notice it. No one else.  
WHY: This is the sun through the window. You don’t yell about the sun. You just let it touch the back of your neck.

BAR 4 (Abmaj7):  
A4.  
That’s it. Just A4. Natural. Not in the chord. No one told you to play A natural. It is just under your finger when you open your hand. Hold it the whole bar. Let it buzz against the Ab. Do not fix it.  
DYNAMICS: mezzo-piano. Louder than every note before it. Not loud. Just present.  
WHY: It is wrong. It does not belong. And for the first time all night, you are not hiding.

BAR 5 (Fm9):  
A4 slips down to Ab4. Then C5. Then Eb5. Quarter notes. Gentle. Like you said you were sorry, quiet, right after you said the thing you didn’t mean.  
DYNAMICS: falls back to pianissimo. Each key pressed softer than the last.  
WHY: You do not run from the wrong note. You sit with it. You walk it home.

BAR 6 (Fm9):  
Eb5 hovers. Bb4. F4. Arpeggio down, slow as rain running down glass. Don’t rush the fall.  
DYNAMICS: almost gone. You can barely hear the hammer hit the string.  
WHY: No one ever tells you that the bravest thing you can do after you break something is be gentle with the pieces.

BAR 7 (G7alt):  
Gb4 B4 D5 F#5. Staccato. All four at once. Once. On beat 2. Then silence. The whole rest of the bar is empty.  
DYNAMICS: mezzo-forte. Sharp. Clean. Not angry. Just awake.  
WHY: Tension does not need to be a storm. It can be one quiet clear crack of lightning and then the dark waiting.

BAR 8 (Cm9):  
Eb3. Very low. Hold it. Let the bass hum under it. Let the last vibration die into the room before anyone breathes again.  
DYNAMICS: pianissimo. Softer than breath.  
WHY: You do not have to land big. You do not have to win. You only have to come back.
