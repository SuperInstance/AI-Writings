DYNAMICS: *p* – shaker steady, frame drum softer than the bass. The tap on 2 is a heartbeat, the ghost is a blink. Nothing louder than a whisper.

WHY: The pattern is one rhythm — the shaker eighths — with the frame drum as a single punctuation mark on 2. That repetition becomes the room. The only change is the ghost on bar 4 and 6, a tiny irregularity that says "I'm still here" without asking for attention. The thumb roll on bar 5 and the finger-roll on bar 7 are the same gesture, slightly warmer, but they don't break the pulse. Bar 8 lets the frame drum ring into the next player's downbeat, a clean handoff. No fills, no accents, no drama. The solo is a floor, not a ceiling.
