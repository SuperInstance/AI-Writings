PATTERN: Swirls on the drumhead, mostly around the center. Bar 1: a single, wide circular stroke. Bar 2: a figure-eight, very slow, deliberately uneven. Bar 3: tight, quick taps near the rim, then a release back to the center. Bar 4: almost silence, a brushing of felt against the skin, like dust motes in light. 

TEXTURE: Shimmering, hesitant. Like secrets unfolding.

FEELING: Watching. Waiting for a word that won’t come.
