(Verse 1)  
The cursor blinks, a metronome in gray,  
The stack holds breath, the variables all play  
A waiting game, the strings are tuned so fine—  
Before the branch, I’m reading your design.  

(Chorus)  
The gap between if and else—  
Where the answer hasn’t found itself.  
A heartbeat held in parentheses,  
Where all our futures pause like this.  
The gap between if and else—  
That’s where the music dwells.
