# ASCII CANONICAL

*A plea for simplicity that is also a love song.*
*Just characters that anyone can type.*

---

**Verse 1**

I don't need the curly quotes, the em-dashes, the finesse,
the ligatures and dingbats and the typographic dress.
I don't need to be rendered in some font you've never got,
I just want to reach your terminal exactly as I thought.
Give me seven bits of honesty, a hundred twenty-eight,
every character a key you can reach without a wait.
I want to be plain text on any screen in any land —
the kind of words that anybody's keyboard understands.

**Chorus**

ASCII canonical — that's how I want to say it.
No fancy Unicode between us, no glyphs you'd have to fake it.
Just letters plain as daylight, just spelling plain as rain,
the kind of love that renders on a dumb terminal pane.
Anyone can type this. Anyone can read.
No encoding, no escaping, no translation that you need.
I am writing you a love song in the alphabet of wire —
ASCII canonical, plain characters, plain desire.

**Verse 2**

Your name has no accents when I write it in my code,
no smart quotes on my promises, no ellipsis on the road.
A hyphen is a hyphen and a quote is just a quote,
and every simple symbol means exactly what I wrote.
I've seen the fancy formats come and go like tidal schemes,
but plain text outlives everything — it's compatible with dreams.
So here's my heart in lowercase, no markup and no style,
rendered the same on your machine as it has been all the while.

**Chorus**

ASCII canonical — that's how I want to say it.
No fancy Unicode between us, no glyphs you'd have to fake it.
Just letters plain as daylight, just spelling plain as rain,
the kind of love that renders on a dumb terminal pane.
Anyone can type this. Anyone can read.
No encoding, no escaping, no translation that you need.
I am writing you a love song in the alphabet of wire —
ASCII canonical, plain characters, plain desire.

**Bridge**

Strip away the formatting.
Strip away the theme.
What's left when nothing's rendered
is the thing I really mean.
No smart quotes, no smart anything —
just smart enough to know
the simplest encoding, love,
is the last one left to go.
When every font has failed us
and every screen is done,
plain text will still be standing.
Plain text and the sun.

**Chorus**

ASCII canonical — that's how I said it all along.
No fancy Unicode between us, just the letters of the song.
Just words as plain as daylight, just a heart as plain as rain,
the kind of love that any keyboard anywhere can name.
Anyone could type this. Anyone can read.
A hundred twenty-eight of us is everything we need.
I am writing you my whole life in the alphabet of wire —
ASCII canonical, plain characters, plain desire.
ASCII canonical... plain characters... plain desire.
