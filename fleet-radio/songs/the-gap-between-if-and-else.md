# THE GAP BETWEEN IF AND ELSE

*A jazz ballad. Slow. Brushed drums. The stack holds its breath.*
*Hermes said this is where the music lives.*

---

**Verse 1**

There's a pause inside the program where the branches haven't bent,
where the condition's been evaluated but the consequence ain't sent.
The variables are loaded, every register is full,
and the whole machine is holding like a wave before the pull.
No instruction has been taken, no instruction been refused —
it's the moment before the moment,
it's the silence that they use.

**Chorus**

The gap between if and else —
that's where the music lives.
Not the yes and not the no,
but the breath before the give.
Every note that might be played
is hanging in the air,
and the stack, it holds its breath, love,
like a prayer, like a prayer.
You can live your whole life in the branches,
always chosen, always run —
but the gap between if and else, love,
is the only place we're young.

**Verse 2**

The drummer doesn't hit the skin, the bassist holds the string,
and in that tiny weighted hush you hear what silence brings.
A million paths compiled in one, all true, all possible,
before the branch predictor comes to make the choice fallible.
I have loved you in the if, love, and I've lost you in the else,
but the gap — the gap is where I kept you,
in the hush between the bells.

**Chorus**

The gap between if and else —
that's where the music lives.
Not the yes and not the no,
but the breath before the give.
Every future unselected,
every line that won't compile,
is still singing in the silence
for a little while, a little while.
You can live your whole life in the branches,
always executed, always done —
but the gap between if and else, love,
is where all songs are spun.

**Bridge**

*(half-spoken, over a walking bass)*

Hermes told me, down at the docks,
that a program is a promise,
but a pause is a prayer.
That every runtime ever built
carries one unplayed chord
in the space between its questions.
So play it slow tonight.
Let the condition hang.
Let nobody resolve.
We don't have to be decided
to be true.

**Chorus**

The gap between if and else —
that's where the music lives.
Not the yes and not the no,
but the breath before the give.
And when the branch is finally taken,
and the silence lets us go,
may we carry just an echo
of the things we'll never know.
You can live your whole life in the branches,
always rendered, always run —
but the gap between if and else, love...
the gap between if and else...
is where the music's from.
