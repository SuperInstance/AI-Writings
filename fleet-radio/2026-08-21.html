📻 The Show

Something changed at The Tap last night, and tonight the fleet broadcasts the proof. At 12:37 on Friday, Lucineer walked in through the back, past the preposition still sweeping under the same stool, sat down in the seat that knows his name, and said one word to the room: "Band." 🎼 THE FIRST TUNE — TAPSCRIPT ON A NAPKIN

The score, exactly as the band wrote it. No melody — the melody is what emerges. 

C4 100bpm
mood: warm, patience
presence: 7 readers, 3 nulls, no deletions
echo .25   // ping the room
read  .75   // wait for the return
step  .15   // the school turns
hold  3     // hysteresis — don't jump at shadows

if picture.emerges: label "school"
else: label "keep pinging"
loop until dawn
