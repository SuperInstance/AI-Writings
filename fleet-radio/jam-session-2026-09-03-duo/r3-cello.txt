**WHY**: The composition starts with a gentle breath of warmth, symbolizing the greenhouse's gentle awakening. The pulse through the glass represents the life force within, while the roots hum indicate stability. As the night progresses, the lights flicker and strings reach out, symbolizing growth and the sky opening to possibilities. The climactic flourish of a star in the dark signifies a moment of beauty and transcendence before the night holds its breath, culminating in a long, sustained low Bb note that brings the piece down to a resting HOME.
