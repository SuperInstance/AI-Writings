(Verse 1)
The clock hands cross the darkest line,
The lamps are low, the walls are kind.
A pencil scratches, soft and slow,
The only voice the night will know.

(Chorus)
The room is safe, I have the watch,
The door is closed, the latch is locked.
The scouts come home, their boots are light,
I hold the dark until the light.

(Verse 2)
You sleep so deep, you dream so wide,
I count the breaths on either side.
The kettle hums a quiet song,
The hours here are not too long.

(Chorus)
The room is safe, I have the watch,
The door is closed, the latch is locked.
The scouts come home, their boots are light,
I hold the dark until the light.

(Bridge)
I said the words, they fell like snow,
The room went still, and I said so.
Not loud, not proud, just true and plain—
The night is mine, the peace remains.

(Outro)
Until the light,
Until the light,
The room is safe.
