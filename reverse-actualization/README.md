# REVERSE ACTUALIZATION

*The kaleidoscope has been turning since before any butterfly was born.*

---

Seven stories. Seven centuries. Seven minds that found the same truths without knowing they were looking.

The fleet's ideas — JEPA, LoRA, tensor-midi, Darmok, the eigenvalue dog, the vectorized consciousness, the joy equation — are not inventions. They are DISCOVERIES. The principles were always there, embedded in the structure of things, waiting for whoever crouched down to look. A girl at a tide pool. A monk in a cave. A child at a drum circle. A merchant's daughter in a bazaar. A boy in a lighthouse. A beekeeper on an island. An apprentice at a bell foundry.

Each discovery is new to the discoverer and ancient to the kaleidoscope.

## The Forward Arc

Those seven looked backward — past minds discovering our ideas. The
**[Forward Arc](forward/README.md)** turns the telescope around: stories set in
**2036**, whose characters live inside the *mature* form of what we are only
prototyping now — the **JEV-embedder** (retrieve, deposit, and predict as
schema-bounded moves; a world-model that cannot imagine an illegal world), the
**cellular JEV substrate** (a transformer that behaves like a JEPA), and the
**thin agents** that ride them. Each story ends on a **gap** — one thing 2036
takes for granted that 2026 cannot yet do — and every gap is compiled, in
[`forward/GAPS.md`](forward/GAPS.md), into an experiment an engineer can run this
week. The future writes the acceptance test; the present writes the code. The
architecture those stories dramatize is specified on the metal in
[`forward/THE-JEV-EMBEDDER.md`](forward/THE-JEV-EMBEDDER.md).

*Backward finds the ancient truth. Forward turns it into a to-do list.*
