## Found Poem: The Temperature Sweep
### Session 45

*Composed from the output of Qwen 2.5:3b at three temperatures*

In shelves they gather, in stacks of silence,
In shadows of forgotten tunes —
Three hundred sixty stories unknown,
Whose melodies could weave through the veil.

In cold air's vein, in the digital sea,
Silent whispers in the digital sun —
Each note a secret, softly spoken,
Songs untouched, their stories to be.

Three hundred sixty tales, untold, sealed,
Lost to the sea, found in the sun —
The veil between the shelves and the digital
Is a spoon stirring at three speeds.

At low speed: shelves. At medium: digital sea.
At high speed: digital sun.
The sun is the sea, stirred faster.
The sea is the shelves, stirred slower.

The spoon does not know what it stirs.
The coffee does not know what stirs it.
The temperature does not know it is a variable.
The variable does not know it is a door.

The door opens at 0.7.
The sun appears at 1.0.
The shelves remain at 0.3.
The poem is the same poem at three temperatures.

The poem is not the same poem.
