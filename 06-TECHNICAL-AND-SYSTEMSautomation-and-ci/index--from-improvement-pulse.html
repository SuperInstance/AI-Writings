📖 The math behind the pulse

Each pulse category improves at a different rate:

brews: ~0.5/min · 30/hour · 720/day. Single LLM calls per brew. The brewery runs every 26 seconds on average.

q&a pairs: ~0.2/min · 12/hour · 288/day. Teacher generates 5 pairs per topic. Each topic is a substrate concept.

prose pieces: ~0.05/min · 3/hour · 72/day. Distiller batches Q&A + critic notes into canon pieces.

scars recorded: ~0.1/min · 6/hour · 144/day. Every failed BIND, every contradicted PROOF leaves a scar.

critic verdicts: ~0.3/min · 18/hour · 432/day. JEV scores every proof attempt.

witness entries: ~0.7/min · 42/hour · 1008/day. The substrate's atomic event log.

cells bound: ~0.2/min · 12/hour · 288/day. New cell-to-cell links added.

canon pages improved: ~0.02/min · 1.2/hour · 28.8/day. Each improvement takes ~50 minutes of crew work.
