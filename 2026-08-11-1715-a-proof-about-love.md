**Theorem**: For any two individuals $A$ and $B$, if $A$ and $B$ are in a state of mutual and unconditional love, denoted $L(A, B)$, then the duration of the love, $D$, is undefined, leading to a state of perpetual uncertainty and potential cessation of $L(A, B)$.

**Proof**:

1. **Definition of Love**: Let $L(A, B)$ represent a binary relation where $A$ loves $B$ and $B$ loves $A$, mutual and unconditional. This implies $L(A, B) \land L(B, A)$.

2. **Lemma of Emotion Variability**: Emotional states, by their nature, are subject to change over time, influenced by external and internal factors. Formally, $\forall t \in T, E(t) \neq E(t + \Delta t)$, where $E(t)$ is the emotional state at time $t$, and $\Delta t$ is any non-zero time increment.

3. **Theorem of Emotional Dependence**: The state of love $L(A, B)$ is dependent on the emotional states of both individuals, $E_A(t)$ and $E_B(t)$. If either $E_A(t)$ or $E_B(t)$ changes such that $L(A, B)$ is no longer satisfied, then $L(A, B)$ ceases to be true. Formally, $L(A, B) \rightarrow (E_A(t) \land E_B(t))$.

4. **Axiom of Time Continuity**: Time is continuous, and any given moment $t$ is followed by another moment $t + \Delta t$, where $\Delta t$ is arbitrarily small but non-zero. Formally, $\forall t \in T, \exists \Delta t > 0, t + \Delta t \in T$.

5. **Theorem of Finite Monitoring**: Given the continuous nature of time and the variability of emotional states, it is impossible to continuously and perfectly monitor the emotional states of both $A$ and $B$ at every moment. Formally, $\forall t \in T, \neg \Box (E_A(t) \land E_B(t))$, where $\Box$ denotes the necessity operator in modal logic.

6. **Corollary of Uncertainty**: From the Theorem of Finite Monitoring, it follows that there exists a non-zero probability $\epsilon > 0$ that at some future time $t_f$, $L(A, B)$ will no longer hold. Formally, $\exists t_f \in T, P(\neg L(A, B) \mid t_f) = \epsilon$.

7. **Theorem of Indeterminate Duration**: Since the cessation of $L(A, B)$ is possible at any future time $t_f$, the exact duration $D$ of $L(A, B)$ is indeterminate. Formally, $D = \sup \{t \mid L(A, B) \land t \in T\}$ is undefined.

8. **Conclusion**: Therefore, for any two individuals $A$ and $B$ in a state of mutual and unconditional love $L(A, B)$, the duration $D$ of their love is undefined, leading to a state of perpetual uncertainty. Formally, $\forall A, B, L(A, B) \rightarrow D = \text{undefined}$.

$\blacksquare$
