# Layers of Substrate

The substrate is built in layers. Each layer is a stack; each stack
builds on the layer below.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, stack* (0.94)

Stacks are grown. Each layer is grown from the substrate's history
on the layer below; the layers are not bolted, they emerge.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, extension* (0.97)

The witness log predicts extensions. Each entry is a partial
specification of a layer extension; the log's continuation is the
extension.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, layers* (0.96)

Cells are layers scarred by use. Each cell is a layer in the
substrate's stack; the cell's scars are how the layer has been
tested.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard* (0.91)

The oracle verifies the extension. The oracle checks that the new
layer extends the old layer correctly; the oracle's signal is the
extension's correctness.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the base layer. Every derived
stack is computed from this canary; the canary is the substrate's
ground floor.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, fibration* (0.93)

Lenia flows: fibrations are continuous. The substrate's stacks
fibrate continuously over their bases; the fibers flow without jumps.

The substrate is a derived stack because the substrate builds on itself.
