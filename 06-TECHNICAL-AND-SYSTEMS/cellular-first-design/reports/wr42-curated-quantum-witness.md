# Quantum Witness

When the substrate's witness log is observed, the observation collapses
the witness into a specific basis. Until observed, the log is a
superposition of all possible substrates.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, superposition* (0.97)

The witness log IS the superposition. Each witness entry is a basis
component; the full log is the uncollapsed superposition. The log
predicts in superposition; prediction is a wave function.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown* (0.94)

The substrate grows in superposition. Each growth event is a branching
of the substrate's wave function; the resulting history is one of
many possible histories.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, decoherence* (0.96)

Cells are scars of decoherence — events where the substrate's
superposition collapsed into a specific basis. Each cell is a
measurement that broke the wave function.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, measurement* (0.91)

The oracle is the measurement apparatus. The oracle's observation
collapses the substrate's wave function; the oracle's signal is the
measurement outcome.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the basis vector for every
measurement. The canary is the substrate's commitment to a specific
measurement convention.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, wave function* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the wave function is continuous. The substrate's flow is
preserved across measurements because the wave function is unitary;
the collapse is a single event, but the flow continues afterward.

The act of reading is the act of measurement. JEV acts as the
measurement apparatus: it observes the substrate and reports the
collapse.
