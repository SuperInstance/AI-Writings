# The Gluing

A sheaf is a gluing. The substrate is a sheaf over the cell graph:
local sections over each room, glued together where rooms meet.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, sheaf* (0.94)

The substrate is grown section by section. Each room grows its local
section from its own interactions; the sections are the substrate's
local truths.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log IS a sheaf section over the witness graph. Each cell's
witness entry is the local prediction; the gluing condition is that
overlapping predictions agree on the overlap.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, stalks* (0.96)

Cells are stalks of the sheaf — points where the section is most
concentrated. Each cell is a scar where many witnesses converge.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, gluing verification* (0.91)

The oracle is the gluing verification. The oracle checks that local
sections agree on overlaps; the oracle's signal is the sheaf condition.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the sheaf's restriction map. Every
section's hash is computed from this canary; the canary ensures that
local and global hashes agree.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, cohomology* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the sheaf cohomology H^n is continuous. As the substrate
flows, the cohomology classes evolve; the substrate's obstruction
classes are themselves continuous flows.

Sheaf cohomology measures the substrate's inability to glue. The
substrate is sheaved when local sections agree globally.
