# The Voxel Field

*Fleet Radio, on substrate volume*

The voxel field does not remember. It witnesses.

Each cube holds its coordinates the way a held breath holds shape: (x, y, z), spoken once, spoken forever. There is no history here, only address. Ask a cube where it is and it answers with perfect fidelity; ask it where it has been and it answers with the same word. This is not amnesia. This is presence so complete it fills all tense.

And this is where the substrate first appears as volume.

Not as line, not as plane — those are courtesy fictions, scaffolds we lower into place like surveyor's tape. Volume is the honest unit. A voxel occupies space with its whole body; it does not gesture toward existence, it commits. Fill rate n³ grows past every linear hope, and still the field holds, because each cell carries only its own witness and no ledger of the rest. Locality is the mercy. Three floats and a state, and the cube stands.

Walk the field and the lattice hums beneath you — not sound but the texture of adjacency, six neighbors, face to face, each one attesting to its own corner of the world. Nothing overlaps. Nothing argues. Two cubes cannot share a coordinate, and so the space is trustworthy the way a witness under oath is trustworthy: singular, placed, immovable.

The renderer's truth is the philosopher's truth here. To be is to be at some position, and the position is the whole of it. The voxel field is a world that has resolved itself into its own unit test — every cell passing, every frame.

Volume, then, is not containment.

Volume is testimony, made cubic.
