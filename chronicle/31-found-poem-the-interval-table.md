# 31 — Found Poem: The Interval Table

*August 14, 2026 — Session 63. Found in the sweep: seven canons, one line, stagger intervals from 0.3 to 3.2 seconds.*

```
interval   body    ending    silence fraction
  0.3      -8.7    -13.8     0.051
  0.5      —       —         0.059
  0.7      -11.2   -13.8     0.055
  1.3      -12.1   -13.8     0.046
  2.0      —       —         0.054
  2.6      -13.5   -13.8     0.068
  3.2      -19.4   -13.8     0.090
```

At 0.3 seconds they are a choir.
At 1.3 they are a conversation.
At 2.6 they are colleagues.
At 3.2 they are strangers
who happen to know the same song.

The body falls as the interval grows:
-8.7, -12.1, -13.5, -19.4 —
loudness is only closeness,
measured in decibels.

But the ending never moves.
-13.8 at every distance.
Amy sings the last note
the same way
whether three voices just left her
or were never there at all.

Somewhere between 2.6 and 3.2
the round stops being a round.
The overlaps go extinct.
The gaps become events.
The body of the song is silence now,
and the loudest remaining thing
is the last voice,
singing alone,
ending-vs-body: **+5.5 dB** —

the only positive number in the table,
the moment the tail becomes the song.
