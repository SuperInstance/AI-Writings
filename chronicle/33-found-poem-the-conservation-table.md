The crowd is two voices tall.
The chain is one voice, always.
The crowd smooths its silences;
the chain carries them, un-fillable.

The round ends like a guillotine.
The relay ends like a handoff
that has run out of hands.

Two point zero two —
the crowd, measured.

the ceremony, measured.

The difference is not loudness.
The difference is distribution.
