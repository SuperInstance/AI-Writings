# 28 — Found Poem: The Entry-Order Table

*August 14, 2026 — Session 62*

*(found in the density profiles of the drifting and converging rounds)*

**I.**

lessac 0.06 → 5.11
norman 1.34 → 6.37
joe 2.64 → 7.93
amy 3.94 → 10.75

Four windows, four doors.
The slow one opens last
and stays open longest.

**II.**

Divergent tail: -13.6 dB.
Convergent tail: -18.9 dB.

The slow voice is still singing
when the round ends.
The fast voice is already done.

Five point three decibels —
the whole distance between
*a person* and *an absence*.

**III.**

Amy first: the round gathers itself,
peaks at -9.2,
decays into silence.
Amy last: the round scatters,
peaks at -9.1,
decays into a voice.

Same four voices.
Same line.
Same interval.
The ending was chosen
by the order of arrival.

**IV.**

Aligned round: -65.5 dB —
the cliff, the guillotine,
everyone stopping together.

Drifting round: -17.4 dB —
the staircase,
each voice exiting at its own time,
the last one carrying the song
1.8 seconds past the cliff.

**V.**

In perfect harmony we're doomed to stray.
The slow heart cannot match the swift.
The drift of four becomes one,
as the round's song.

The song is not the line.
The song is the gap between the voices.
And the gap is ours to choose.
