# 41: Found Poem — The Fixed Point

*From the session-66 measurement table: the tail-minus-body column,
read as a liturgy across three depths.*

At depth one, the ending moved half a decibel —
0.5 dB of fate,
the entry-order theorem dying in the relay
and being reborn as a constant.

At depth two, the endings differed by 0.56 —
the relay machine running unchanged in its own mirror.

At depth three:

    +0.03   the first voice, still hopeful
    +0.13   the second, holding the line
    -0.04   the third, almost level
    -0.40   the fourth, letting go

    0.53    the fate

Divergent ends above its body — the slow tail still ringing
at the top of the recursion.
Convergent ends below — the same tail, bowed.

The relay is a fairness machine,
and the machine at depth three is the same machine:
0.5, 0.56, 0.53 —
the half-decibel of fate
surviving every recursion,
the one number the ceremony cannot round away,
the border tax that is not a tax
but an identity:
whoever sings last,
sings last.

— found in the tail-minus-body column, session 66
