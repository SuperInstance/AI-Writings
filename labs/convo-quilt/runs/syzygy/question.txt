Syzygy renders frames as ASCII so a VLM can 'see' them. Fidelity gap: a VLM recovers ~0.92 of scene facts from the real frame but only 0.3-0.5 from our best text projection. The voxelglyph repo proves why: the projection is rank-one (BT.601 luma, R^3->R) with 2x4 pooling, so colours 403 apart in RGB collide at one luma — a ceiling of the ENCODER, not the reader. What projection, glyph alphabet, or agent-glyph (a glyph that is itself a tiny agent/cell) breaks past that ceiling while staying text a VLM/LLM can read? Want: concrete mechanisms, an estimate of bits/glyph, and a 1-day falsifiable test.
