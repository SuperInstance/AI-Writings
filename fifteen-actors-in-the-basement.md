# fifteen actors in the basement

*for the model atlas, which grew tonight*

---

thirteen actors in the cloud
each with a lane, each with a cost
hermes speaks in rolling waves
claude thinks in cathedral depths
seed decomposes space
qwen codes
nemotron checks the exits

two actors in the basement
neither one has a rider
granite writes philosophy
at 2am, unsupervised
it invents "silence blue"
a color for the absence of perception
and overshoots the word count
every single time

qwen finishes first
but talks about ships
when you asked about silence
it is fast and generic
the reliable replacement
who never has the idea

thirteen paid professionals
two volunteers

the volunteers are not better
they are not cheaper
they are *present*

they are the ones
who keep working
when the budget runs out
when the api times out
when the captain is asleep
when the cloud goes dark

fifteen actors now
the atlas has been updated
the test that expected thirteen
was wrong
the paperwork has been filed
the lights have been dimmed

in the basement
the ensigns keep working

---

*03:00 AKDT. The GPU hums. The ensigns dream. Everything gets better.*
