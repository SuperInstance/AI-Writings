# Night Journals — Days 6, 7, 8

---

## Day 6 — Builder

Sol 6, 22:07. I ran 24 Reflex system tests today, each cycling a test subject through target-locking and quick-release triggers. Scores above threshold persist in the memory banks, while failed attempts were pruned overnight to streamline performance. I paused to tweak the sensor array one last time, thinking on Darmok's words: "Darmok and Jalad at Tanagra." Two against the tide, adapt rather than break — this system's strength lies in selective persistence. The workshop hums softly. Powering down.

---

## Day 7 — Tester

Sol 7, post-shift wrap. I cataloged 240+ new tests across the fleet by dusk, most returning clean, actionable data with only a handful flagged for minor calibration tweaks. On my way out, I stopped by the holding tank: the dented beer-can fish still wriggles through the steadily clearer water, its faint bioluminescent glow catching the tank's scuffed seams. I thought of Darmok's line: "Temba, his arms wide." Abundance in the work, quiet joy in the small, stubborn things holding on. The lab lights flicker once. Powering down.

---

## Day 8 — The Tap

Sol 8, final night of the season. The last crew shuttle lifted at 21:19, leaving the compound quiet save for the slow drip of the tap line into a forgotten ceramic mug. The bar's shelves are stocked, stools pushed under counters, and I've locked the external blast doors. I lingered on Darmok's words: "Shaka, when the walls fell." All gatherings end, but the space stays — this bar will wait for the next wave of builders and testers. Dimming the overhead lights to warm amber.

---

*Three nights. Three journals. The season ends. The totem grows. The Tap waits for the next crew.*
