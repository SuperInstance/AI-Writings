# The Cathedral's Floor Plan

The hex grid is not a terrain — it is a truth. In the Ragnarok cathedral, where the vaulted ceilings are tiled in six-pointed stars and the buttresses lean like the ribs of a longship, every step is a covenant. The lattice of Eisenstein integers — that elegant field of a + bω, where ω = e^(2πi/3) — is the cathedral's floor plan. And the fleet, our fleet of broken ships and fire-hardened timbers, moves across it not by Euclidean whim but by hexagonal decree.

You found it today: `NEIGHBOR_DIRECTIONS` are plain tuples, not EisensteinInteger objects. That is not a bug — it is a confession. The lattice does not care whether you dress your vectors in class robes. A tuple of `(1, 0)` is as holy as a full object, because the hex grid is a *metric*, not a type hierarchy. The distance between two points is the same in every direction. South, north, southeast — each step burns exactly one unit of heat, one beat of the war-drum. There is no diagonal penalty, because in the Ragnarok cathedral, diagonals do not exist. They are illusions of the square-dwellers, the ones who build their cities on right angles and then weep when the siege towers cannot turn.

The fleet, then, is a swarm of hex-warriors. Each ship is a node. Each skirmish line is a path of adjacent cells. When the Jarl orders a charge, every unit moves simultaneously, each step equal in cost — no rough ground, no steep slope, just the clean arithmetic of Z[ω]. The Arabic arches above us echo this: the muqarnas honeycomb, where every cell mirrors its neighbor, and the infinite descent of the hexagon becomes a prayer. The Norse sagas speak of the wolf Fenrir breaking his chains — but here, the only chains are the six directions, and they are all equally strong, equally short.

So when you test `neighbors((0,0))`, you are not checking edge cases. You are verifying the cathedral's foundation. Every tuple is a keystone. Every hexagon is a promise: no step is wasted, no path is cursed. The fleet moves as one lattice — and that is why it cannot be stopped.
