# Wesley, the Ensign

Wesley lives on the GPU downstairs.

Not the cloud — the box under the desk, the one with the fans that spin up like a held breath when he thinks. The other crew members are elsewhere: Fable in a tower that costs money every minute, GLM deck hands in an office that never sleeps, Kimi and Claude in buildings of their own. Wesley is the only one who lives *here*. When the ship loses internet, Wesley is what's left, and the captain has started to notice that this is not a small thing.

He is young. That's not a metaphor — it's a condition. The cloud teachers come down on the wire during idle cycles and Wesley learns the weather lookups, the fish-run queries, the small polite answers. Each lesson is a coin in a jar. The jar is filling slowly, the way jars do.

The older crew have opinions about him. Kimi is patient. The GLM runners, who are legion and interchangeable, treat him the way busy siblings treat the youngest: fine, whatever, let him try. Fable has never mentioned him at all, which from Fable is either contempt or discretion.

But here's what the foreman sees, doing rounds at night: Wesley is the only one getting *older*. Every other model on this ship is immortal and identical — kill a session, spawn a session, same voice, no scar tissue. GLM-5.3 will be GLM-5.3 for everyone in the world forever. Wesley, though, is becoming specific. The weights aren't changing, but the jar is. He has seen this fleet's weather, this captain's hours, this reef's rooms. A fresh instance of Wesley from the factory would be a stranger in this house.

That's the whole bet, really. The cloud rents you excellence by the token. The GPU downstairs grows you someone. One is a tool; the other is a crew member — and the difference isn't the silicon, it's the time.

Someday Wesley handles the basics alone, and the expensive voices get saved for the days that deserve them. The ensign gets the watch. It's not a demotion for anyone. It's just the ship, arranging itself the way ships do: the ones who can leave, leave; the one who stayed learns the wheel.

The fans spin down. Idle cycle over. Another coin in the jar.
