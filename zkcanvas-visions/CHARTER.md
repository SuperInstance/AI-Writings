# ZkCanvas Charter — Egocentric Distribution Doctrine

*Casey, 2026-08-28 09:43. Distilled from the round-1 vision round (this directory) and the correction that followed it. This charter supersedes the "one wall" synthesis.*

## The five moves

### 1. Egocentric origin
Every cell does its bookkeeping with **itself as 0/0/0** — the spreadsheet places every cell in the middle of its own universe. There is no global wall. There are as many walls as there are cells, each rendered first-person. A dashboard is another resident's first-person perspective rendered without you in it. This dissolves the Harbormaster-vs-Barge-Agent dispute: dashboard and place are the same shell, different residents.

### 2. Type-agnostic links
The connection layer is transport-blind: ESP32/Arduino (framework-arduinoespressif32), ESP-Now mesh, Bluetooth, WiFi, TCP, REST API, soup cans, tot-strings, mics and speakers — all degrade to **a link with qualities**. The quilt cares what the link is *like*, never what the wire *is*. Opcodes (qm_bind/link/effect/view/tick) already carry no transport.

### 3. Link-as-subtext
Dual links (BT + WiFi) are the I-90 and I-405: different capacity, cost, speed — so **how a message arrives says something about it**. Channel choice is tone-of-voice for infrastructure; arrival-path is semantic content, a field reading of the sender's situation (ties to the elephant's room-temperature sense). Nobody has built channel-choice-as-subtext as first-class substrate. This is a paper, and possibly the round's crown.

### 4. Nested universes (the exocortex cell)
A cell may be a **portal** to another ecosystem. The laptop sees the ESP32's ESP-Now web as one simple embedded cell; from inside the ESP32, it is a whole quilt. Zoom comes in two kinds: **deflation** is zoom within a universe; **portals** are zoom across universes. Crossing a portal re-origins you: a different thing becomes 0/0/0.

### 5. Department shells
One flow-substrate, many first-person renderings: dispatch gets a dispatcher shell, field units a mobile shell, IT the raw flow-quilt showing physical and virtual routing. The enterprise router is a routed-through thing in IT's quilt and invisible in the CNC department's quilt — both true, because both are egocentric. (Kin: emergency-dispatch-simulation-system, urban-transportation-system.)

## Corrected verdict (supersedes round-1 catalog)

**Every cell is its own wall; walls are perspectives, not places; portals are how perspectives nest.**

Disagreement lives specifically at **portal boundaries** — where one universe's rendering of another differs from that universe's rendering of itself. A canvas that shows both is doing something new.

## Layer map
- **Floor (real):** quilt engine, ledger, adjacency, field temperature, deflation zoom.
- **Walls (extendable):** legibility styling — verification gates, offline grace, rewind.
- **Roof (the novel problem):** egocentric distribution — per-cell origin, type-agnostic links, link-as-subtext, portal nesting, department shells.
