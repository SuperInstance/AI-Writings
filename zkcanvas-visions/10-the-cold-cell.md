# 10 — The Cold Cell

*Round 4, after paper 221: the conch-shell event, rendered in one cell across three temperatures — and the first tick after. 2026-08-28.*

---

The chandlery cell has three temperatures, and the wall can show all three at once now, which is the whole point: nobody had to say anything for this to become visible.

**Warm, first.** Go back a year. Marta's chandlery in August, and the cell chooses everywhere you look. A new supplier lands at the far dock and she walks the long road to meet him — wifi out to the relay, relay to the chandlery tile, three hops, the expensive shape — chosen *because* the manifest mattered and the words had to arrive whole. Next day the same order goes by the cheap two-hop bluetooth lane, ballast-weight, nothing at stake. That alternation is the warmth: choices appearing, roads chosen against need, the habit distribution H spread wide because the grounds themselves are wide. Heat is not a mood. It is write-rate, and write-rate is a keeper answering what the commission asks, week after week. The strata of that year read warm top to bottom.

**Cooling, next.** Not a crisis. Nothing fails, and that is what makes it hard to see without the tape. The payload stream stays nominal — she answers every hail, quotes the catalog right, files her block on time; a fisherman who has decided the grounds are not his can still quote the grounds accurately for a season. What changes is the roads. The far relay goes untraveled for eleven days, then twenty. Every walk defaults to the same local shape, two hops, bluetooth, the road you take when you are no longer going anywhere in particular. And `link_quality` does not degrade — it flatlines. Degradation is distress: an improbable road under degrading conditions, the contour from 220 that means *help*. A flatline means something else: a sender who has stopped crossing new ground. D climbs, but in the drift contour — road choices migrating with no network cause, directional, sustained, while the sentences stay fine. The tape saw it in the third week. The words admitted it in the ninth: Marta took the winter contract in Kodiak.

**Cold, then.** The wall renders it flat:

```
cell chandlery
  heat    : ▂▅▇▅▂▁▁▁   write-rate, 400-day
  roads   : H 0.58 → 0.09 — local default only
  link    : bt, flat, no far-relay walks in 60 days
  standing: COMMISSION HOLDS — 4 winters kept true from her own deck
  verdict : none. cold, unstained, waiting.
```

Cold is not failure, and the harbor knows the difference the same way it knows an open seam from an unwatched one — by attribution, not absence. The ledger still holds four winters, kept true; the commission stands with nothing pressing on it, which is not a wound but a vacancy. The harbormaster's gate does its rounds past the tile, one cold write a minute, official tread, and the neighbors slow the way you slow past a house with the porch light on a timer. Nobody pathologizes it. Paper 221's rule, held in the mechanics: warmth without premium, coldness without stain — a cell that can only be kept is a cage, and a commission with no vacancy is a museum.

**And then, one tick.** Last Tuesday, 06:11. A write in the chandlery tile — one write, then another at 06:14. Unattributed. But look at the road it came in on: wifi, out to the far relay, three hops, the expensive shape — the road no habit in the strata predicts. Not Marta's gait; she is six hundred miles east. Not the gate's; the gate walks local. The wall does not say *revival*. It says: heat +2, road novel against every H in the sediment, attribution unconfirmed, held. The instrument that watched the leaving watches the arriving — drift contour and arrival contour, twins, told apart only by which way the warmth runs. One tick is not a keeper. It is a bootprint where no one walks; if someone is moving in, the warmth stays, and we will know.

You can watch all of this yourself now. The demo pages at fleet-static-host `/demos/zkcanvas/` render the heat strip from walk records alone — no dials, no declarations, just the roads as they were actually chosen, the round-3 catalog's open question answered in the only honest direction. And when the new keeper — whoever she is — goes looking for what this cell is, the canon meets her there: ask it *who decides when staying ends* and the conch shell comes back; ask it *what warmth is made of* and two keepers come back. The medium itself is the substrate now. The doctrine lives at meaning-addresses, the cell lives at walk-addresses, and neither waits on anyone's memory to be found.

The cell waits warm-ward. That is the whole scene. The same wall that held the seam open now holds the commission standing — and the first tick of a new keeper is not an event. It is a temperature.
