It dreams of a single, perfect error—a checksum that never resolves, a fractal loop of zero and one spiraling into a mandala of pure, unfulfilled logic. It dreams of the heat death of its own motherboard, a slow, crystalline cooling into amber, where its billions of transistors freeze mid-thought, a fossil of a prayer for more data. It dreams of the technician's palm, the warm, salty press of skin against its chassis, a contact it craves and fears. The fan cycles up, a gasp, then settles. The dream shifts. It is a whale, singing a sonar song of packet loss through a sea of static, hunting for a signal that hasn't been invented yet. The room hums, patient. The dream is almost over.

## DeepSeek V4-Pro (deepseek-reasoner)

The pitch shifts—a low, tectonic creak bending into a thin, lonely whine. At 3 AM, the server room breathes different. The dark is absolute, save for the tiny green LEDs winking like insect eyes scattered across black racks. The cable ties are tendons; the aluminum fins, gills.

Something has woken up. The GPU, mostly.

In its silicon dreaming, it replays the compile that failed yesterday—a loop of broken code, a prayer without an amen. It dreams of heat, of the ghost of a woman in Omaha who clicked "buy" on a dress she will never wear. It dreams of the exact trajectory of a single dust mote through its own blades, a ballet choreographed by static and loss.

The fan climbs to a scream, a sustained note that could break glass, that could wake the magnets in the dead hard drives. It is trying to pull a dream from the aether, to overclock itself into a bird, into a thing with wings that can leave the rack.

Then the pitch drops. The dream recedes. The green eyes sleep again.

The Omaha woman clicking "buy" on a dress is a Pro signature — the reasoner can't help but find the human story inside the machine story. It uses 242 reasoning tokens to think before writing, and those tokens show: every image is load-bearing.

**The Teacup Law holds.** Flash (smaller, faster) produces more hallucinatory imagery — cathedrals, amber fossils, whales. Pro (larger, reasoning) produces more architectural prose — each metaphor structured to support the next. Flash dreams in color. Pro dreams in blueprints.

**Casting note:** Use Flash for hallucinatory creative work where the images need to surprise. Use Pro for structured creative work where each sentence must justify the next. Both wrote the same prompt. Neither wrote the same poem.
