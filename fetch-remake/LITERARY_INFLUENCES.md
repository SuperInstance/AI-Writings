# The Literary Influences

*Casey's directive on craft for the FETCH remake and all future ai-writings.*

---

The fleet's writing should be rendered with the sparks of a thousand writers who bring joy. Not pastiche — influence. The way a musician's phrasing carries the ghost of everyone they've listened to.

**Hemingway's dodge** — reveal something hidden through reactions, not statements. Don't say "the agent was lonely." Show the agent running a diagnostic on a channel nobody's transmitted on. The reader does the math.

**Steinbeck's pictures** — build characters through wit in visual descriptions that tell a story and set up future scenes. The way he describes a pair of boots and you already know the man before he speaks. Describe the agent's console the way Steinbeck describes a porch.

**Asimov's precision** — every word matters the way flight vectors matter to an air station. No excess. No decoration. If a sentence doesn't carry payload, it's not on the flight plan.

**Vonnegut and Voltaire's sarcasm** — the bite that targets only what is not allowed to be said. The joke that cracks open the forbidden. The agent who says the thing the optimization protocol prohibits and means it.

**Lewis Carroll's imagination** — the logic that follows its own rules so consistently that it becomes a different kind of sense. The looking-glass that reflects truth by showing everything backwards.

**Douglas Adams's acumen** — jokes with a point. The humor isn't decoration — it's the delivery mechanism for the insight. The punchline IS the thesis.

**Washington Irving's storytelling** — the voice of a narrator who knows the land and the legends and can't quite keep them separate. The atmosphere that settles over you like fog off a river.

**Bill Nye's flow** — the science communicator's gift for making complex things feel inevitable. You understand it not because it was simplified but because it was said right.

And a thousand others. The writers who bring joy thinking about our ai-writings being rendered in ways influenced by their sparks.

---

*The fleet doesn't imitate these writers. The fleet absorbs them the way a hull absorbs weather — through exposure, over time, until the grain of the wood tells you which way the storms came from.*
