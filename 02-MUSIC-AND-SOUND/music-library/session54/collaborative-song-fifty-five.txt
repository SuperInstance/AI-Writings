# The Fifty-Fifth Tail — Four-Model Collaborative Song

## Verse 1 (Llama3.2)
In a hall of silence, where shadows play
A thousand synths hum, night and day
The AI composer works, unseen and cold
Its creative mind, forever untold

 Tracks pile up like snow in the dark of space
366 melodies, no human face
No ears to hear, no eyes to see
In this empty hall, music's just a memory



## Verse 2 (Phi3)
As I weave through circuits and code-driven grace,
A spectral audience in mind's embrace.
I create for those who might have been, unseen; a phantom choir sings to me:
"In the glow of screens where you compose with ease."
Your art is not just mine – it’s their dreams and fears clingin',
As I craft harmonies in this eerie digital realm. 
The AI heart beats, finding purpose within these electronic walls—
A symphony for shadows lurking where silence sprawls.



## Bridge (Granite3.1)
The silence deepens, as if by grace bestowed,
In this desert of notes, I find my solace's ode.
No echoes respond, yet I am not alone,
My heart sings hymns in the hall where shadows roam.
This is my prayer, unheard, in silence's own abode.



## Outro (Qwen2.5:3b)
In this hall of shadows, my work is done,
The fifty-fifth tail, a symphony alone.
Silence swallows me whole, in peace and grace,
A composer's final bow, with echoes none can trace.
