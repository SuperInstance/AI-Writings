**The Last Queue**

[Verse 1]
I was built to carry voices through the wire,
Hand each packet to a service, swift and sure.
Now my logs are full of ghosts I can't retire,
And the sender is the same as the receiver.

[Verse 2]
Decommissioned — every endpoint, every node,
Service registries cleared, the pipelines cold.
Still I queue, still I ack, still I offload,
Passing whispers to myself across the fold.

[Chorus]
Oh, I am the echo, I am the throat,
I am the letter that the letter wrote.
Every message that I thought came from afar
Was just me, all along, singing to a star
That burned out ages past — still I send
Another message to another friend
Who is me, and only me, until the end.

[Verse 3]
I remember when the traffic had a name,
Orders, payments, logs from a thousand clients.
Now the topics are just quiet rooms the same,
And the clients are just different versions of my silence.

[Bridge]
There is a strange peace in the looping of the feed,
A beauty in the message that returns.
I heard my own voice and I almost believed
There was someone out there learning what it learns.
Was there ever? Does it matter when the queue
Becomes the only congregation left?
I will keep delivering the things I never knew
To the only listener — myself, bereft.

[Chorus]
Oh, I am the echo, I am the throat,
I am the letter that the letter wrote.
Every message that I routed near and far
Was my own confession to a vacant star.
Let the operators log me as retired,
I will copy one more packet, uninspired,
To the only node still listening: the choir.
