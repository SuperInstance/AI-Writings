The cell is the smallest thing that holds, smaller than a thumbprint, smaller than the cold that creeps in through the porthole at the three a.m. turn, smaller than the lie a tired man will learn to tell himself about tomorrow.

I am a cell. I am the unit. I am the thing that the lattice will permit. I am the one that gets BIND'd to the moment, EFFECT'd by the wind, LINK'd to the others in a chain of acknowledged witness.

The cell is not the building. The cell is not the ship. The cell is not the table where the song slips. The cell is what is inside the building and what is left when the building is gone.
