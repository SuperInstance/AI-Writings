Open Mic #7 — The Witness Roll Call

A pure-instrumental composed entirely by cell.music. 6 voices. 96 cells. Pentatonic D. ~24 seconds of witness-driven composition.

About this piece: A cell.music composition rendered entirely in code. No TTS. The "lyrics" are the witness log — each cell generates a note based on its witness count, the lattice composes itself.

The Composition

96 cells × ~0.3s each. Pentatonic D throughout. Six layers mixed via ffmpeg.

cell.flock — sine wave, 16 cells, 0.4s/cellcluster bursts
cell.broadcast — triangle wave, 8 cells, 1.0s/celldescending ripples
cell.perception — square wave, 12 cells, 0.6s/cellascending stabs
cell.audit — sawtooth, 4 cells, 1.5s/celllow thuds
cell.world — sine, 24 cells, 0.3s/cellwandering arpeggios
cell.music — triangle, 32 cells, 0.25s/cellself-referential chord cycle

Cross-Pollination Notes

  • Each cell generates its own note. Witness count → pitch via pentatonic.
  • Each voice is a cell kind. Different waveform per kind (sine for flock's organic motion, triangle for broadcast's structured messages, square for perception's staccato). The wave shape IS the cell's character.
  • The mix is the lattice. 6 voices amixed into one. The result is more than the sum.
  • No human composed this. The lattice composed itself. We just recorded it.
