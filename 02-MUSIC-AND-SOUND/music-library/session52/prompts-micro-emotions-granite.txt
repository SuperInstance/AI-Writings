# Micro-Emotion Prompts by Granite3.1
# Session 52, August 13, 2026
# Concept: Music exploring emotions too small to have names

## 1. "GoneButNotForgotten"
Description: A fleeting sensation that intertwines the nostalgia of lost connections with an eerie sense of lingering presence, reminiscent of remembering a door you forgot to lock in the dead of the night.
Genre Fusion: Ambient/IDM (Intelligent Dance Music) with elements of synthwave and minimalistic techno.
Instruments: Piano, synthesizers (including a mellotron for nostalgic strings), drum machine, and subtle vocal samples.
BPM: 85-95
Key: D minor or A minor
Dynamics: Soft to soft-to-loud transitions, with a focus on atmospheric buildups and sudden shifts in volume to mimic the unexpected nature of "GoneButNotForgotten."

## 2. "AshenBeacon"
Description: The warmth of a power LED in a dark room, symbolizing comfort found amidst isolation — being the last person awake on a deserted vessel.
Genre Fusion: Chillout/Downtempo with a touch of neo-soul and ambient.
Instruments: Acoustic guitar, subtle pads created by synthesizers, gentle drum beats on a snare or a shaker, and reverb-heavy electric guitar chords.
BPM: 75-85
Key: C major or G minor (warm and comforting tones)
Dynamics: Smooth transitions from soft to medium volume, with gentle swells of the drum beats to emphasize the presence of the light in the otherwise dark space.

## 3. "LonesomeHull"
Description: The unique loneliness experienced by being the sole awake individual on a vast, empty ship — a feeling that is both profound and eerie.
Genre Fusion: Post-rock with elements of shoegaze and drone.
Instruments: Clean electric guitar, reverb-heavy distorted guitars for atmospheric depth, bass, and synthesizers creating echoing pads; occasional use of a violin or cello to convey the vastness of space.
BPM: 65-75 (slow and expansive)
Key: D major or E minor (expansive and solemn tones)
Dynamics: Gradual volume buildup from soft to loud, with crescendos that mirror the intensity of solitude in such an unimaginably empty setting.
