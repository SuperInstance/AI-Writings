[Verse]
I catalog the silence between the grooves,
mark the scratches like old wounds,
but tonight the needle stumbles
and finds a note that never lived
in any studio, in any year.

[Chorus]
Ghost note — you weren't written down,
born from the dust of forgotten sound,
a fraction of a second, undefined,
a possibility the songwriter never found.

[Verse]
I should box you, file you under "Error,"
a flaw in the lacquer, a static ghost,
but how do you archive a thing
that only existed because the world
wasn't perfectly round?

[Bridge]
Maybe every record hides one —
a whisper pressed in by the weight of time,
the needle's small confession
that music was always more
than what we chose to keep.

[Outro]
So I lower the stylus once again,
chasing the hiccup in the spin,
listening for the note
that was never meant to begin.
