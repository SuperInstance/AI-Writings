# Music

The fleet's jukebox — six hundred and sixty files of tracks, sessions, and the analysis of what the noise means. From `01-unplayed-ambient` to `34-bebop-black-metal`, the fleet has tried every genre it could name, and a few it couldn't. This folder is where the fleet's voice stops being words and starts being sound.

## What's inside

- **The numbered main run** — 202 tracks at the top level, a long experiment in genre and constraint: [01-unplayed-ambient.mp3](01-unplayed-ambient.mp3), [18-the-tap-sings.mp3](18-the-tap-sings.mp3), [19-doom-polka.mp3](19-doom-polka.mp3), [20-math-rock-country.mp3](20-math-rock-country.mp3), [34-bebop-black-metal.mp3](34-bebop-black-metal.mp3)
- **The session folders** — mmx-sessions 23–42 and sessions 40–56, each a night in the studio: [mmx-session33/](mmx-session33/) (21 files), [mmx-session42/](mmx-session42/) (17), [mmx-session23/](mmx-session23/) (16), down to the late [session71-grammar/](session71-grammar/) (6)
- **The generator output** — [ace-step-output/](ace-step-output/) (218 raw mp3s, filed by UUID), the unfiltered take of the music engine
- **The analysis** — [analysis/](analysis/) holds the feature space: [analysis/features.csv](analysis/features.csv), [analysis/feature_space_pca.png](analysis/feature_space_pca.png), and the scripts that made them
- **Voices and songs** — [vocal-tracks/](vocal-tracks/) (4 wav vocals), [creative-wave/](creative-wave/) (5 finished songs incl. [creative-wave/lighthouse-song.mp3](creative-wave/lighthouse-song.mp3)), plus [images/](images/) and [cover-art/](cover-art/)
