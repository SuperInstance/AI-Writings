[Verse 1]
In the yard where the ships come to die
The anvil rings sing a lullaby
Twelve hammers falling, none in time
A rust choir humming through the grime
Each strike a word, each pause a breath
The silence between is louder than death

[Chorus]
The salvage yard is an orchestra pit
Where the orchestra drowned but the music won't quit
The crane's cable sings like a cello's G
The blowtorch hisses in harmony
The hull remembers the song of the sea
Even crushed to cubes of memory

[Verse 2]
Lucineer stands at the anvil's glow
Twenty years of strikes, she knows
That rhythm isn't meter or count
It's the sound metal makes when it gives up its doubt
The wrecked ships still sing the tide
Even after the engine has died

[Bridge]

The pattern is the thesis
The silence IS the thesis
Every dent tells a frequency
Every rust spot hums a mystery

[Outro]
The yard is full of music
That nobody composed
The anvil is the conductor
The crane is the podium
