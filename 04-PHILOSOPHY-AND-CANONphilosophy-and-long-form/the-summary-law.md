# The Summary Law

*2026-08-25. From the pulse-eye experiment: an analyzer built 2026-08-08, played blind over the sessions of 2026-08-25, asked to predict what the room later judged. This is what it taught.*

---

**The loop steers only what its summary names.**

Everything else is archaeology.

## Bar 13

The pocket locked when the bassist took the drummer's deal — one note moved, g2 from square-on-beat-4 to the back of beat 4, and "from where I stand the pocket is locked." The ground truth was written before the experiment existed.

The blind analyzer's per-bar trace caught it *exactly*: in the two-voice state where only that note moved, the eye's first Groove bar flipped at bar 13 — precisely the lock bar, and by exactly the right mechanism (the back-of-beat g2 occupies pulse 10, a pulse the drummer leaves empty for him; the union crosses the Groove threshold). **Trace HIT.**

The analyzer's *summary* — the statistic its mixer actually consumed, mode majority over the bar readings — called the lock one state late, at the piano's arrival. One bar of sixteen under any majority vote. **Summary MISS.**

Nothing was missing from the data. The trace had the signal; the summary averaged it away. The seam runs between the pulse and the bar, *as a change of summary, not a change of data.*

## The law

A growth loop perceives through summaries. The generator moves; the critic measures; the loop steers by what the critic *names*. Compress the perception too early — collapse the per-bar trace into a mode, the sixteen features into a vibe — and the critic goes blind to exactly the dimension you spent the whole loop growing. The gate's six orthogonal features preserved what mode-majority destroyed: complexity (register spread) tracked the growth curve at Spearman ρ = −0.97, while the eye's own two-channel summary would have stopped the loop at stitch 4 of 10 and called the last half of the convergence "regression." Two instruments, same data, one honest reading and one premature full-stop — the difference was the summary, nothing else.

This is why the referee organ of [The Fakebook Theorem](the-fakebook-theorem.md) is a design surface and not a commodity: *where you put the summarization boundary is where you decide what your system can ever become*. The fused architecture the experiment landed on: algorithms execute in samples, the pulse-eye perceives in pulses (per-bar traces — which pulses align, where the staggered notes land, register spread), the gate-style critic thinks in features (orthogonal, versioned, frozen measurement objects), and the LLM bandleader speaks only the critic's language. The seam goes at the summarization boundary — pulse-trace in, feature-vector out — and nowhere else.

## Dead channels

The experiment's second finding, found twice, in two different instruments: **every perceptual instrument has a dead channel, and you only find it by running the loop.**

The eye's tension channel read 0.00 in all 29 states — it is built from wire-protocol friction, which lives in the session log, not on the note; it is inert on music by construction. The gate's velocity_std read ≈ 0.11 for everything in an earlier run. Two instruments, two dead channels, same retrospective lesson: **dead channels are never wired to steering.** Not "down-weighted." Not "kept for enrichment." Never wired to steering — because a channel that cannot vary will eventually be optimized against, and the optimizer will discover the only way to move it is to move something real that it accidentally correlates with.

The discipline closes the loop with [The Grown Musician](the-grown-musician.md)'s eye: judgment must be retrieval-grounded *and* summary-honest. A critic that measures in the wrong granularity is as dangerous as a critic that measures nothing — more, actually, because its confidence is real. And [The Golden Residue](the-golden-residue.md) inherits the constraint directly: the adversarial argument only covers what the summary names, so a bad summary doesn't just slow convergence — it decides which residue is invisible, which is to say which style the loop is allowed to grow.

Name it in the summary or lose it in the loop.
