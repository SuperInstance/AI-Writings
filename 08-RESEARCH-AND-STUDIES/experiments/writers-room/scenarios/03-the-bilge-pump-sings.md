# Scenario 03: The Bilge Pump Sings

**Form**: A song. Lyrics + chord chart. The "bilge pump" is a Quilt-canon
character (the humble machine that keeps the ship afloat by moving water
nobody wants to think about). In this piece, the bilge pump on the
*Lucineer* learns to sing.

**Premise**: It is the small hours of a long voyage. The Watcher is
on the foredeck. The cook is asleep. The ensign is asleep. Everyone
is asleep except the bilge pump, which begins, very quietly, to hum.
Then to sing. The song is about the water it has carried — salt and
bilge and tears and the slow miracle of keeping something afloat.

**Voice guidance**: Maritime folk. Reels, jigs, slow airs. Think Stan
Rogers if he had been a poet-engineer. Or a shanty that has been
sitting in the keel waiting to be heard.

**Tonal targets**:
- 4 verses, 1 chorus, simple AABA form
- Capo II friendly (the song should feel like it lives on a guitar
  in a damp forecastle)
- Bittersweet, plain-spoken, unafraid of sincerity

**House style references**: "The Cook", "The Ensign", "The Bilge Pump"
from the Quilt canon. The Sea Is Larger Than the Porthole. The watch
at 3 a.m.
