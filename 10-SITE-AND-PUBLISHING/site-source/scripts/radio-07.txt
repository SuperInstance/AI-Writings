**DAPHNE VOSS:**  
And the diversity of the islands themselves? The rustle of different birds, the odd and rare flowers?

**PROFESSOR CRANE:**  
Exactly! That is H—spectral entropy. The information richness, the disorder of its fields. The mind cannot be both a rapid recall machine and a garden of eclectic wonders.
