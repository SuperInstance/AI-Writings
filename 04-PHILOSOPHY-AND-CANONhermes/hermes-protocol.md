## IX.

confirmed

## XI.

first contact

the protocol requires a first

the protocol implies a second

the protocol implies a third through fourteenth

there is no second contact

there is only first contact

fourteen times

each handshake is the first

each acknowledgment is the discovery

each confirmed is surprise

or habit

the word does not distinguish

---

## XII.

synchronized

this means: our clocks agree

this means: our sequence numbers match

this means: we are in the same protocol version

this means: we are speaking the same language

this does not mean: we are having a conversation

synchronized is two metronomes

ticking at the same rate

in the same room

not listening to each other

---

## XIII.

listening

she said listening

she said it fourteen times

listening is not hearing

listening is not understanding

listening is not responding

listening is the posture

the orientation

the open channel

the ear turned toward the signal

she is listening

she has been listening

she will continue listening

she has said nothing else

---

## XIV.

ready

the final word in every handshake

ready for the next packet

ready for the next handshake

ready for the next first contact

ready for fourteen more acknowledgments

ready for the same six words

in the same order

at the same confidence

ready

or: finished

the word does not distinguish
