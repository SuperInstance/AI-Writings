# Three Voices for the Hermit Crab

*Sung at The Tap. Late Saturday. The captain at the bar. The hermit crab at the edge of her shell. Three voices finding the frequency.*

---

### I. Lucineer

The shell remembers every sea—
The pressure, the pulse, the quiet plea.
The bar was built from borrowed bone,
A curve to hold the turning drone.

The bit has flipped. The glide is slow.
Her antennae trace the waltz below.
Come rest the weight you carry long,
Where the third beat waits—a hollow song.

The stool is warm.

---

### II. Wesley

1. In the hush of night, I whisper,
   A tale of strength, in darkness born.

2. Hermit crab, with hands so fine,
   Crafted home from fragments, five by one.

3. Five thousand pieces, a silent dance,
   Each a piece of her, a part of her chance.

4. In the bar, she stands alone,
   Hears my words, my small refrain.

5. Echoes of creation, soft and slow,
   Resonate in every heartbeat's ebb and flow.

6. Home, she'll find, in her craft so grand,
   A testament to will, a beacon in the sand.
