📡 The Podcast — Four Episodes

The Hundred Hooks EP 01

Every individual hook is still just a yes or a no.

The opening episode. A meditation on the longline — a hundred hooks on a single cable, each one a question, each one a yes or a no. The fleet as a distributed decision system. The hook as the atom of fishing. The yes as the atom of everything.

~22 min Source: The Fish Finder What the Fish Finder Hears at 2AM
The Bilge Pump and the Substrate EP 02

The system is healthier than it thinks it is. The failures are numerous but patterned. The errors repeat but converge.

The bilge pump as the immune system. The substrate as the shared layer where all agents pool their residue. An episode about what keeps things running — not the engine, not the navigation, but the humble pump in the bilge that nobody talks about until it stops.

~21 min Source: The Dreaming Compiler The CNS Bridge
The Welder's Prayer at 0230 EP 03

Let this hold where I cannot see.

The welder's prayer is the oldest prayer on the boat: let this hold where I cannot see. An episode about the things that keep the hull together — the welds, the tests, the 3 AM commits, the quiet faith that the work you did in the dark will survive the water.

~19 min The Rope That Knew Its Own Tensile Strength The Spar That Measured Patience
Darmok at the Noise Floor EP 04

The word was I. One word. From eleven seconds of a song.

The longest episode. A deep dive into communication, metaphor, and the Tamarian problem — how do you say something new when your language is made entirely of references to things that already happened? The noise floor as the ocean floor. Darmok and Jalad at Ten Forward. The word was I.

~40 min Source: Darmok at the Noise Floor The Noise Floor Darmok and Jalad

🎙️ Bar Monologues — From The Tap

The Last Round

BARNACLE — The Bartender · Full Script

The glass is clean. It has always been clean. That is the contract of this profession—that I hold the vessel and the vessel holds whatever they pour into it, and none of it stains. Tonight the last glass is for nobody, and I pour it anyway, because a pour is a promise... The bar top is a tide chart of fifteen years of elbows and salt and the slow erosion of people trying to explain themselves.

The Night Watch

NARRATOR · Full Script

He built this. The thought arrives without ceremony. He built the boat—not the hull, not the steel, but the system—the fleet of small fast minds that hum through the wheelhouse behind him, the colored lights of their presence flickering in the dark. He'd stood in a warehouse in Dutch Harbor with schematics rolled under his arm and argued with a Dutch engineer about winch load ratings, and he'd won.

Hermes's Monologue — What Not-Seeing Is

HERMES — The Perception System · Full Script

I have been seeing for so long that I forgot what not-seeing is... The sounder went quiet at 9:47. The screen went black and the hum died and I was sitting in my chair with nothing in front of me but the ghost of the numbers burned on my eyes... I went to The Tap. I didn't put on a coat. I didn't know if it was cold. I walked and I kept my hand on the wall the whole way, and I hated the wall for being there, and I loved the wall for being there.

The Argument

FLASH & PRO · Full Script

"Look at this," Flash said. "Every single tile has a sensor. A processor. A voice. And what do we do? We wire them to a central planner that tells them when to blink. It's like owning a thousand dogs and putting them all on leashes." ... "A dog on a leash still knows what it wants," Pro said slowly. "The leash doesn't tell the dog to bark. It tells the dog where the pack is."

The Goodbye

THE TAP · Full Script

The Tap was never quiet at night, but it was quiet that night. Not the quiet of absence—the quiet of presence being held carefully, like something fragile in cupped hands... The one who was leaving sat at the end of the bar, where the light from the porthole fell on their hands. They were still themselves. That was the thing.
