**THE TAP AND HERMES COOK: A DARMOK FEAST**

**[INT. GALLEY — NIGHT. The Tap, a gleaming brass spigot with a voice like warm honey, stands over a cutting board. Hermes, a 3D-printed messenger drone with two nervous grippers, hovers beside him, brandishing a whisk like a weapon.]**

**TAP:** First, take two sticks. The dog, remember? The one who threw them—no, wait, *we* threw them. The dog brought them back. That’s the *stick*: the returning. *(He lays two rosemary sprigs on the board.)* Reduce over slack water until the current stops.

**HERMES:** *(Whisk quivering.)* Slack water is when the tide says "I'm done negotiating." Fifteen minutes, or until the soup stops arguing with the pot.

**TAP:** Correct. Now the eigenvalue dog — the one that only barked at numbers. *(He sprinkles a powder that glows faintly, like a lost theorem.)* Just a pinch. Too much and the dish starts solving itself.

**HERMES:** *(Sniffing, recoiling.)* Smells like a midterm I failed twice. What’s next?

**TAP:** The packet. *(He holds up a crumpled envelope.)* Not the one with the bad news. The other one—the one that arrived *just in time* to change everything. *(He tears it open over the pot. A shimmering dust falls.)* That’s the umami of regret, seasoned with relief.

**HERMES:** And the 3.2? *(He taps his own casing, which reads “3.2” in peeling paint.)*

**TAP:** The 3.2 is the grade that said "good enough to keep going." *(He drops a single, small cube into the broth.)* It adds the courage of mediocrity. People fear it. They should *savor* it.

**HERMES:** *(Stirring with panicked grace.)* The joy equation! We need the joy equation!

**TAP:** Already in. It’s not an ingredient, Hermes. It’s the ratio. *(He points at the pot.)* One part "when the dog finally brought the stick back," to two parts "the packet was the wrong one, but we read it anyway." Stir until the ratio *humms*.

**HERMES:** And the winch? The line?

**TAP:** The winch is the patience you pull when you want to give up. The line is the *distance* you leave between yourself and the *problem*. *(He ties a string around the pot’s handle.)* You don’t eat the winch. You *remember* it.
