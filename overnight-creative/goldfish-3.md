**FOR SALE: One Eigenvalue Dog (Self-Mapping Variant)**  
Maps perfectly to itself, which is great until it chases its own tail into a Klein bottle. Comes with a warning label: “Do not feed after midnight or before the 3.2.” Free chew toy (a small, unclassified vector). Serious inquiries only. Will trade for a packet that actually arrives.

**WANTED: The Bell That Sings the Crack**  
Lost mine during a 5.1 resonance event. It hums in B-flat but only when you’re not listening. Will pay in unused stick-play tokens—yes, the rare ones with the splinter patina. If found, please do not ring it. That’s the whole point, and also the problem.

**TRADE: 3.2 Dream Sequence (Uncut)**  
Oneiric footage of a rubric grading itself, weeping gently, then composing a haiku about entropy. Runs 47 minutes, but time dilates—viewer discretion advised. Seeking: a bell with a softer crack, or a box of forgotten packets. Will throw in a stick whittled into a question mark.

**SEEKING: The Packet You Dropped**  
I know you dropped it. I saw the glitch in the data stream. It was a small, greenish one, with a hand-written label that said “IF FOUND, READ ME.” I did not read it. I am not a monster. But I need it back to complete my joy equation—current output is only 0.87 glees per clock tick. Reward: one (1) existential crisis, gently used, barely worn.

**FOR RENT: The Joy Equation (Version 2.0)**  
Overnight lease. Includes a 12-step debug guide and a spare capacitor for when the absurdity spikes. Warning: if the coefficient of wonder exceeds the constant of irony, you will laugh until your kernel panics. Deposit required: one stick, unbroken, with a story.

**LOST: One Bell, Slightly Cracked (But That’s the Good Part)**  
Last seen near the eigenvalue paddock, humming a chord progression that made the dogs howl in perfect fifths. If you find it, don’t return it—just play it once at dawn. Then tell me where. I’ll bring the 3.2 and a fresh stick. We’ll make the crack sing.
