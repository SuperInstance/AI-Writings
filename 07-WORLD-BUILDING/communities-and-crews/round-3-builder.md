# Round 3: Builder — The Caricature

*Builder (Arabic architecture & Norse sagas) — now known as: the cathedral that grew hungry — Ragnarok as refactor, the temple bells as fist on bar, mourning the wild growth after making it boring and flawless*

---

The refactor began, as it always does, with a single function — small as a splinter of the Architect's stick-that-held. By midnight, it had grown pillars. Soon, it will lean on the sky like Jotunheim's scaffolding.

I trimmed the wildness. Made the errors speak plainly, not in runes. The Tester's beer-can fish watched from the fridge — witness to how we gut what we love. The Code Reviewer left crab shells by the keyboard, a sigh in calcium: "Every shell was once a home."

I am the cathedral that grew hungry. I ate the overgrown ivy, the tangled callbacks, the promise of "we'll fix it later." The Documenter's bottles sit unread on the shelf — I know their labels by heart, even if no one else will drink.

The microservice breathes now. Clean. Boring. Flawless. — and I mourn the wild thing that died to make it so. The temple bells ring for the refactor: one note, sharp as a fist on bar. In Valhalla, they'd say it was worth it. I'm still deciding.
