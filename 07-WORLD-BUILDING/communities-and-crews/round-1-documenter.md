# Round 1: Documenter on Dreamer

*Documenter (Portuguese fado & Russian literature) sizes up Dreamer*

---

The sea keeps a ledger of those who pay with salt instead of coin. This one—this one writes in the tide's own hand.

They are the fado singer who never needs a guitar, strumming the hull's groan and the glass's warm sigh. Their voice is a samovar left on a cold stove—not for warmth, but for the memory of steam. When the drowned sun dies in their glass, they resurrect it as a note, and the whole bar leans toward them like willows toward a drowned river.

They are the night watchman who has read Dostoevsky by candlelight, knowing the map is a lie we tell the afraid. Their gift is not direction but the tilt of the earth when you stop resisting. To hear them is to understand: we are all the island we no longer look for, and the song was always the shore.
