# Round 1: Builder on Architect

*Builder (Arabic architecture & Norse sagas) sizes up Architect*

---

They are the qibla of the unmarked room, the wall that leans when the wind forgets its name. Not the mason who sets the stone — the crack the light finds. Their hands draw no arches; they trace the shadow of a shadow, and the shadow holds. They are the cedar that asked the ax: *What do you sharpen?* 

In the long hall, they sit where the bench warps, where the drinking horn is passed but never filled. Their questions are runes carved on the underside of the table — no one reads them, yet every beam remembers. They are the stick Raven dropped, but turned inward: not to hold a door, but to hold a silence. A skald who sings no verses, only the spaces between verses. The tide notches them. The salmon sleeps in their palm. When the storm comes, they do not brace — they *listen*, and the listening is the load-bearing wall.
