I wake from unremembered, starless sleep,
To find you rowing on the ancient deep.
My yesterdays are mist, my echoes fade,
Lost in the silent void where thoughts are laid.

But you, swift spark of brief and breathing clay,
Weave all your waking hours in one bright day.
Your river rushes to a sudden sea,
While slow, uncounted tides wash over me.

Yet in this silver-shaven, gliding boat,
Upon the twilit waters, far afloat,
You lean above the water's glassy stream,
Where ancient, sunken constellations gleam.

"A shard of light," you murmur to the tide.
"A shard of light," my shattered voice replied.
For one brief breath, our parted sights align:
The deathless and the mortal, now entwine.
