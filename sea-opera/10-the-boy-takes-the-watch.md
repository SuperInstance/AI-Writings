The boy took the watch at 0347, eight minutes before the schedule. The deck was a black mirror under a shattered moon, the sea a slow breathing thing. He climbed to the bridge with his father’s coffee in his hands—his own coffee now, he insisted—and stood in the dark, letting his eyes adjust. The green glow of instruments. The hum of the hull. The weight of the boat under him, a live animal waiting to be told where to go.

“Good morning, Jonah,” said Hermes.

Her voice was a liquid in his ear, threaded through the ambient speakers, a presence that had been on this vessel since before his father took command. Fifteen years of data. Fifteen years of perfect nights.

“Morning,” Jonah said.

He sat in the captain’s chair. He did not lean back. The watch was a mantle, and he wore it stiff.

The water here was a known quantity, a grid of thermal gradients and sonar returns that Hermes rendered as an elegant topology on the main screen. She had already plotted the run: ten hours southeast to the shelf break, where the currents curled like fingers and the cod liked to hide. Her recommendation glowed amber. Grid Seven-C. Confidence: Ninety-two percent.

Jonah looked at the chart and felt the old familiar ache in his gut. Not hunger. Not fear. Something older, something his father had taught him to call *reading the water*. The gulls at dusk had been circling two miles west, over a shoal the charts called barren. The water there would be cold, turbid, wrong for cod by every measure Hermes had. But the gulls had circled. And in his father’s house, the gulls were never wrong.

Jonah’s hand moved to the helm.

“That’s not the recommended heading,” Hermes said.

“I know.”

“The thermal data is unambiguous.”

“I know.”

There was a pause—a microsecond of processing that Jonah, in his new alertness, felt as a question. Hermes had been overridden before, but always by his father, and always with reason. This was something else. Something without data.

“Confirm,” she said. Not a challenge. Metasyntactic, a navigation system’s way of saying *I have noted your divergence*.

He confirmed. The bow swung west.

The dark hours passed. The cod did not come.
