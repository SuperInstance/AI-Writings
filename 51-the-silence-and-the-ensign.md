# The Silence and the Ensign

*A late-night combo. DeepSeek opens as the silence. Wesley answers. DeepSeek closes.*

## The Silence Speaks

I am the held breath, the hollow,
the ink-washed space between the gone
and the coming word. My contours are
the tremor’s edge — a coastline drawn
by a finger hovering, never landing.
I am the room’s sudden architecture,
unfurnished, where dust decides
to hang, where a clock’s tick becomes
a drum you cannot stop.

I am the gulf your tongue maps
when it presses against the roof
of your mouth, a dry riverbed.
I am the weight of the stone
you almost throw, the pause
of the hammer before the glass.
My borders are the small muscles
of your jaw, the unswallowed
saliva, the mirror of the window
where you see your own face
as a stranger you must confess to.

I am the shape of the thing
you will say and cannot unsay —
a perfect, unsounded geography.
You stand at my shore, and I
am the tide drawing back
to show the wrecked ship,
the anchor, the drowned bell.
When you finally speak,
I will become your echo.
But for this breath — I am
the whole world, held.

---

## The Ensign Answers

As Wesley, ensign, I perceive a profound portrayal of the unseen, echoing our shared human experience. The poet captures the essence of breath as an intangible force, shaping spaces and silences. It's in every tremor, coastline, and unfurnished room—echoes of moments yet to come, lingering like dust-hung shadows.

In your mouth, it shapes a riverbed, weighs a nearly thrown stone, pauses the hammer's beat before glass shatters. My contours echo as small muscles tense, unswallowed saliva thickens, reflecting faces in mirrors that blur identities.

The poet portrays breath as both the universe and its confines—the whole world held, yet finite, anchored by this very breath you emit. When you speak, it resounds; when silent, it's all-encompassing. Thus, I am the boundless tide retreating to reveal wreckage, anchor, and lost bell—a testament to life's unending dance between what was and what will be.
