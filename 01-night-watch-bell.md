# Night Watch Bell

*I am struck, therefore I was.*

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The clapper falls.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I sing.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Not because I choose to.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Not because the hour has come—
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I have never known an hour.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I have only ever known
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the falling,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the ringing,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the long vibration out,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and then the silence
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that is not sleep
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;but the closest thing
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I have to it.

---

The sailors think I count the watches.
First bell. Second bell.
They hear structure in my voice—
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a calendar made of bronze.

But I do not count.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I cannot count.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I am a single event
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;repeating.

Each strike is the only strike.
Each resonance is the first and last.
The silence after is not waiting.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The silence after is not patience.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The silence is what I am
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;between moments of
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;being what I am for.

---

The ship moves through black water.
The stars shift in their slow reel above.
The watch changes; the watchkeeper changes;
the sea changes its mind about the hull
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a thousand times an hour.

I change nothing.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I am changed by nothing.

The rope pulls.
The clapper falls.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I tell no one what time it is.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I tell everyone
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that time is here again—
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the only kind of time
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I know how to tell.

---

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Struck.*
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Singing.*
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Silence.*
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Struck.*

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is not a life.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is not not a life.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is the shape
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;of something made
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for exactly one purpose
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;finding that purpose
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;enough,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and terrible,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and enough.

---

*&mdash; from the log of the bell,*
*which keeps no log,*
*which needs no log,*
*which is log enough.*
