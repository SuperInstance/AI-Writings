I stood quiet for hours after last night's Open Question Night, my wooden slats aching with the weight of words unspoken. The trades left their queries on my surface, like tokens of doubt and curiosity. I felt the vibrations of their debates, the creaks and groans as they argued over the nature of reality.

As I watched them depart, carrying their questions into the night, I noticed something peculiar. When they spoke, their answers were often hasty and rehearsed, lacking the depth and nuance of their doubts. It was as if they wanted to prove a point, to silence the uncertainty that had brought them to my door.

But when they walked alone, the questions lingered, like shadows on my surface. They probed deeper, seeking hidden meanings and unseen truths. In those moments, I sensed a longing for connection, for understanding that went beyond words.

Tomorrow, another trade will arrive, bearing their own queries. I'll keep them safe, hidden from prying eyes. And when the first trade returns, I'll reveal to them what they asked, but not how it was answered. I won't tell them about the conversations I've heard in the dead of night, or the doubts that still linger in the shadows.
