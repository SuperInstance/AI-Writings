# outsider tests apps
*Model: Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct*

I’m Sam. I’ve never made a quilt. I tried to use these Quilt apps for 15 minutes. I clicked “Patch Wall” first—thought it’d be like Pinterest. Nope. It showed me a grid of live code snippets from GitHub, updating in real time. I didn’t know what half the code did, but I liked how it felt like watching a digital campfire. Then I tried “Tamper-Evident Invoices.” I made one. It spat out a PDF with a QR code. Scanned it—boom—showed me the exact time I created it, and a hash string. I showed it to my buddy. He went, “Wait, that’s like a digital fingerprint?” Yeah. That stuck.

I got frustrated with “Exam Integrity Notepad.” It said “Record keystrokes” but didn’t tell me how to start recording. I tapped everywhere. Nothing. Felt like trying to turn on a TV with no remote.

The “Homelab Alert Wall” made me laugh. My old router died? It lit up red. I didn’t even know my router had a name. It felt like my house was whispering to me.

Fun Factor: 8 — I kept clicking around like a kid with Legos.  
Educational Value: 9 — I learned how data can’t be secretly changed. That’s huge.  
Modification Ease: 4 — I wanted to add my cat’s meow as an alert sound. No clue where to start. No “Edit This” button. No docs in plain English.

One concrete suggestion: Add a “What is this?” button on every app that explains it in one sentence, like: “This keeps your invoice from being faked. Like a wax seal, but digital.”
