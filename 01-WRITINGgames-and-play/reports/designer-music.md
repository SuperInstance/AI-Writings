**Lived Experience**

Spent fifteen minutes clicking those hexagonal cells and honestly? I'm hooked. The core loop is surprisingly tight - click a cell, watch the witness log populate with these elegant geometric forms, hear the chord resolve. It's pure operant conditioning; every click delivers immediate auditory and visual feedback. My brain was already anticipating which tones would emerge from different cell combinations.

What delighted me most was the emergent harmony system. I stumbled into this gorgeous suspended fourth progression almost by accident - layering witness cells in sequence created these unexpected but pleasing chord voicings. The mechanical metaphor works beautifully; binding witnesses feels like conducting a molecular orchestra.

Frustration came from the lack of undo functionality. Once I'd bound three witnesses and created this muddy dissonance, I couldn't peel back layers to experiment with variations. The linear progression felt restrictive when I wanted to branch and explore harmonic relationships more systematically.

The educational value surprised me - I'm grasping voice leading principles intuitively through play. This beats any music theory textbook I suffered through.
