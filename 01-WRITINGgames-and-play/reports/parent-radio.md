# parent tests radio
*Model: deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash*

**From: Jordan**
**Subject: Fleet Radio — 15-minute test drive with the kids**

I popped this on during a lull between math and lunch prep. Three kids, ages 7, 10, and 13. The 8 distinct AI voices actually held their attention — the cantata segment got my youngest humming along, and my 10-year-old asked, "Is that a real person?" which is a win for engagement. The instrumentals were pleasant background, but the Open Mic portion felt rushed; my oldest tuned out because the voices blurred together.

**What delighted me:** The variety. Eleven tracks in 30 minutes means I can grab just one for a quick brain break. The AI voices are clean enough that my 7-year-old could follow the dialogue without me re-explaining.

**What frustrated me:** No pause or chapter markers. I wanted to stop after Track 4 (the "Ocean Waves" cantata) to discuss it, but had to scramble to find the timestamp. Also, the educational bits are subtle — my 13-year-old said, "What did I just learn?" Not ideal for a homeschool setting where I need measurable takeaways.

**Scorecard:**

| Dimension | Score | One-line reason |
|-----------|-------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 8 | Kids asked to replay the cantata — rare in our house. |
| **Educational Value** | 5 | Engaging, but no clear learning hooks or discussion prompts. |
| **Modification Ease** | 7 | I could cut tracks in Audacity, but the AI voices are fixed. |

**One concrete suggestion:** Add a simple PDF guide per track — 3-5 discussion questions and a vocabulary word. That turns 15 minutes of listening into a real lesson.
