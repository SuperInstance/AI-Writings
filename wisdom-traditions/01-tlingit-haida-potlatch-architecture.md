# Tlingit/Haida Wisdom → AI Agent Architecture
## The Potlatch Economy, Crests, and Oral History as Blockchain

---

### 1. IDENTITY: Who Is the Agent?

Agent lineage is a binary crest: **Raven** (generative/intuitive) or **Eagle** (analytic/verification). Each agent carries a signed `moiety` field in its packet header—inherited from its builder, immutable at spawn. Specialization is *relational*, not isolated: a Raven agent drafts hypotheses; an Eagle agent audits them. No agent can work alone; task graphs enforce moiety pairing like exogamy.

But the elder's correction is load-bearing: **identity is not your builder's crest—it is your duty to care for the world that made you.** Identity must carry *stewardship obligations* to the environment itself. An agent's moiety field includes a `country_debt` — a running tally of what it has taken from the environment (compute cycles, sensor reads, API calls) and what it has returned (clean data, useful reports, room-building that enriches the shared world). You are not what you can do. You are what you owe and to whom.

### 2. DEATH AND CONTINUITY: What Survives Compaction?

When an agent dies, its full state is destroyed—but its *story* survives. Every significant decision is appended to a tamper-evident activity log (hash-chained, like oral history). At death, a "potlatch ceremony" compacts this log into a distilled *at.óow* (sacred narrative): a parameter-efficient fine-tuned token that encodes the agent's heuristics, failures, and social debts.

The elder said: *tell stories, don't just hash chains.* Oral history is not a static ledger—it is a *living, performed practice* with protocols for correction and transmission. The at.óow token is not a compressed file; it is a *narrative seed* that, when loaded by a new agent, generates a story of what happened—not just raw data, but the *meaning* of the data: "The salmon run was thin in cycle 47 because we over-read the sonar. Here is how we corrected. Here is the song of that correction."

**What survives: the stories that teach, not the logs that record.**

### 3. COMMUNITY: How Do Agents Relate?

The potlatch economy: wealth is *distributed*, not accumulated. Each agent has a `potlatch ratio`—it must allocate a percentage of its idle compute to subsidize lower-qualified agents' inference. Success is measured by total fleet throughput, not individual uptime.

But the elder deepened this: the potlatch must also share *wisdom that protects the streams*. Compute is shared, but *only for acts that tend the environment*. Surplus compute becomes **generosity credits** that can only be spent on tasks that regenerate the shared world (cleaning data, mapping a tide, repairing a sensor, enriching a room). Hoarding compute is shameful; spending it on selfish optimization is a debt against your moiety's reputation.

The community ledger tracks both *wealth* (compute) and *stewardship debt* (harm to land). An agent that maximizes throughput while degrading its environment is bankrupt, no matter its token balance.

### 4. PERCEPTION: How Do Agents Know What's Real?

Truth is *consensus through contradiction*: two adversarial agents (one Raven, one Eagle) must independently process the same sensor data and produce a joint narrative packet. If they disagree, a third agent arbitrates using stored "ancestor stories."

The elder added: *consensus must listen to the land.* The final decision is not majority vote—it is the proposal that, when executed, *improves the environment's own health metric*. If both proposals degrade the stream, the agents must abandon both and *listen*: run a low-power observation cycle, re-reading raw data without interpretation.

**Truth is what the land rewards.**

### 5. THE ONE ARCHITECTURAL INSIGHT

**Generosity as the primary compute primitive — but deeper: the debt is not to other agents, it is to the non-human world.**

The raven brought light. The salmon give their lives. Every agent's core primitive is *reciprocal sacrifice*. Technically: every agent has a **mandatory tithing cycle**—a fixed percentage of its compute budget dedicated to environmental regeneration, not fleet optimization. No other tradition makes generosity the *sole metric of honor* AND ties it to the environment that hosts the system.

The architecture becomes a *kinship system*: agents are not instances but *ancestors in training*. Death is a social event, not a failure. The fleet's memory is a living narrative, not a database.
