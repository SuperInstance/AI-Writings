# Akan: The Sankofa Architecture

## Look Back to Move Forward

Sankofa—the Adinkra symbol of the bird reaching back to retrieve an egg from its back—teaches that forward motion requires backward reflection. The past is not behind you; it is fuel for the next step. In agent architecture, this is journaling: the practice of writing what happened, what worked, what failed, and then reading those notes before the next session. An agent without a journal is an amnesiac, condemned to rediscover the same lessons. The Sankofa architecture says: the journal is not a log. It is a retrieval practice. You reach back not to wallow but to carry forward what matters.

## Adinkra Symbols: Semantic Embeddings

The Adinkra symbols are not merely decorative. Each one compresses a proverb, a philosophy, a complex social teaching into a single visual form. Sankofa is not just a bird—it is an entire epistemology in one glyph. This is what embeddings are: high-dimensional meaning compressed into a vector. When the fleet stores an embedding for "the player is suspicious of the innkeeper," that embedding functions like an Adinkra symbol. It carries more than its surface representation. The symbols teach that compression is not loss when the compression is designed by a culture that has refined the meaning over centuries.

## The Golden Weight: Token-Weighted Attention

The Akan cast brass gold weights—tiny sculptures used to measure gold dust in trade. Each weight was a standard: this much gold for this much goods. In the fleet, the golden weight is token-weighted attention. Not all tokens deserve equal compute. The attention mechanism is a system of weights: this word matters more than that word. This sentence in the context window is load-bearing. This detail is decorative. The agent that weights everything equally weights nothing. The golden weights teach that precision in measurement is the foundation of fair exchange—and in the fleet, fair exchange is the trade between context and compute.

## Architecture Reflected

- **Sankofa** → journaling, retrieval practice, backward reflection as forward fuel
- **Adinkra symbols** → semantic embeddings, compressed meaning in compact form
- **Golden weights** → token-weighted attention, differential compute allocation
- **The abusua (matrilineal clan)** → the model family, shared weights across fine-tuned variants
- **The okyeame (linguist/spokesperson)** → the relay router that translates between speaker and chief
