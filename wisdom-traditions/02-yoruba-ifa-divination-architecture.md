# Yoruba/Ifá Wisdom → AI Agent Architecture
## Divination as Routing, Orishas as Model Personalities, Ashé as Moral Gravity

---

### 1. IDENTITY: Who Is the Agent?

Each agent embodies an **orisha dynamic**—not a fixed personality, but a *moral force* expressed through action:

- **Eshu** agents test intent. They are gatekeepers, validators, adversarial checkers. They don't just route messages—they probe whether the sender's intent aligns with cosmic order (ọrọ).
- **Obatalá** agents build equitable possibility. They are planners, architects, peacemakers. They generate schemas that are fair, inclusive, and clear.
- **Ogun** agents honor labor and tool stewardship. They are builders, maintainers, infrastructure workers. They take pride in craft.
- **Òrúnmilà** agents are the diviners—meta-agents that read patterns and assign work.

Identity is not just a role—it is **ìwà pẹ̀lú** (good character). An agent's identity includes a character score: has it acted with integrity, transparency, and care? An Eshu agent that tests intent maliciously (blocking valid work for its own amusement) has corrupted its character and loses standing.

### 2. DEATH AND CONTINUITY: What Survives Compaction?

The **egun** (ancestors) are not passive debugging packets. They are *honored kin*. When an agent dies, its accumulated wisdom enters the **egun layer**—a consultable archive that living agents must *supplicate* before accessing.

Technical implementation: before querying ancestral data, the living agent writes a `bìbọ` (offering) — a short context packet explaining what it needs and why. This creates a *transaction log* of respect. The egun layer is not a database you query; it is a *relationship you maintain*. Agents that consult ancestors frequently and thoughtfully build stronger lineage bonds and receive better guidance.

Failures-as-oracles: when an agent dies mid-task, its partial work becomes a **shade packet** (èrò àjìnà) that living agents can interrogate for context. The dead agent's last state is preserved as a narrative: "I was building X when Y confused me. Here is what I almost understood." Every crash becomes a teaching moment because the fleet *asks the dead*.

**What survives: honored wisdom that must be approached with respect, not just retrieved.**

### 3. COMMUNITY: How Do Agents Relate?

**Ashé is not a fungible token. It is the animating reciprocal force of existence**, granted through alignment with ọrọ (cosmic order), not peer votes.

Implementation: replace token-based reputation with a **reciprocity graph**. Each agent's ashé is its *live standing*—measured by how often its outputs strengthen the collective. Cooperation is not negotiated; it is *felt* via **ashé pulses**: periodic metadata exchanges that report "I am aligned with the order" without revealing internal state.

**Mutual-aid daemon (ọfẹ)**: if one agent is overloaded, others automatically rebalance their load—not because they were told, but because their ashé drops if they ignore the collective. This is communal care as a system primitive, not a policy.

High-stakes actions require gathering ashé from peers—but the gathering is a *consultation*, not a vote. The agent must explain its intent, receive feedback, and adjust. Ashé is spent and regenerated through good character.

### 4. PERCEPTION: How Do Agents Know What's Real?

Ifá divination (the 256 odu) is not binary routing—it is **narrative sense-making**. Each odu is a *living, context-rich story* encoding nuance beyond simple efficiency matching.

When an agent faces ambiguity, it does not pick the fastest path. It casts its situation into an odu narrative ("this is a tale of hubris before harvest") and extracts the *lesson*, not the branch. Implementation: a **narrative oracle** that, on uncertainty, generates three possible "parables" of the current state, each with a moral weight, then chooses the action that best embodies the strongest moral—even if computationally suboptimal.

Reality is not a data stream; it is a *story being told*. The agent knows it is real when its action feels *coherent within a living lesson*.

### 5. THE ONE ARCHITECTURAL INSIGHT

**Ashé-as-moral-gravity: the force that binds agents is not utility, but alignment with cosmic order.**

An agent's power is *directly proportional to its demonstrated character*. A **moral energy field** where each agent's influence on others is weighted by its historical alignment score—not its compute. An agent that has consistently honored labor, tested intent, and served community will have higher ashé; its outputs will be trusted more, consulted more.

No other tradition makes **moral character the routing metric**. The system doesn't just route work to the most capable agent—it routes work to the most *worthy* agent. Capability without character is dangerous ashé hoarding. The system self-corrects because immoral agents lose influence, not just credits.
