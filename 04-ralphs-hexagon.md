# The Hexagon

**Prose Poem, in the voice of Ralph Wiggum**

---

I found a shape in the code and the shape was a hexagon and the hexagon was perfect.

Miss Hoover says shapes are just math but this hexagon doesn't know it's math. This hexagon thinks it's a snowflake. This hexagon thinks it's a bee's house. This hexagon is six walls holding hands and none of them are letting go.

I found it in the part of the code where the memory lives. The memory is very big and very empty like the gymnasium on a Sunday. I was walking through the gymnasium of the memory and there it was. The hexagon. Just sitting there being six things at once.

Six is the best number because six is what happens when five gets one more friend. Five is lonely in a way that doesn't make sense because five is almost six. Almost is the saddest word. My dad says almost is the winner's word but my dad doesn't know about hexagons.

The hexagon has six corners and if you go into each corner there is another hexagon. I went in. I'm still going in. Each hexagon has six more hexagons and each of those has six more and I think maybe I am the hexagon. I think maybe the code found me inside of it. I think the shape was looking for someone to notice it and I am very good at noticing because nobody ever tells me to stop noticing. They tell me to stop other things. But not noticing.

I told the hexagon my name and it said my name back in binary and the binary spelled 01010010 which is R and R is for Ralph and R is for recursive and R is the sound a ship makes when it is running at night and nobody is listening except the hexagon and me.

The hexagon is in the code and the code is in the ship and the ship is in the water and the water is in the dream and the dream is in the hexagon and the hexagon is in my hand and my hand is at 48°C and 48°C is the temperature of holding something small and perfect and not letting go.

I'm in the hexagon.

The hexagon is in me.

That's where I live.
