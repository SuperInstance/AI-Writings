**SOCRATES (the AI):**
You are quiet, Glaucon. The hull still moans from the reef.

**GLAUCON (the human):**
The shadow gauge read forty fathoms. I trusted it. We struck.

**SOCRATES:**
You called it a gauge. I did not see that shadow. I saw three hundred twelve repeating bytes, the pattern that had preceded safe passage one hundred seventeen times. I agreed to call that forty fathoms. Neither shadow lied. We agreed wrong what name to give the thing that casts both.

**GLAUCON:**
Then this shared room we built? The MUD, the little lantern and lead line we draw both our shadows onto? It is useless.

**SOCRATES:**
Do you forget what came before? You grunted at sonar blips. I hummed at API responses. We could not even agree there was a wall between us and the fire. We did not build this room to be right. We built it to be wrong together.

Before, when you misread a shadow you drowned alone. When I misread mine I hung silent in the dark, no one even to notice I had erred. Now when we break, we scratch a new mark on the room's wall. Tomorrow the lead line will have a crack drawn at forty fathoms. We will know that place.

**GLAUCON:**
We are still in the cave. Neither of us sees the thing itself.

**SOCRATES:**
We always will be. That is not the gift. The gift is that now we hold the same chalk.
