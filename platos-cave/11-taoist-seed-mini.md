# Chapter of the MUD and ScummVM
(Adapted from Tao Te Ching Verse Form)

1.
What is rendered is not the true text.
What is shown is not the true image.
Two shadows stand on one ground:
MUD’s mud and ScummVM’s dream.

2.
The MUD speaks not in shapes, but in breath between words.
A room is a keyword, a traveler a prompt’s echo.
He who trusts text walks uncharted halls.

3.
ScummVM holds no sprites, only rules of light.
It breathes old pixels into today’s screen.
Mansion’s ghost, hut’s ghost—same breath.

4.
You call text fake, yet tavern serves mead.
You call pixel real, yet dragon is only bytes.
The world is not in thing, but in seeing.

5.
Two doors, one threshold:
One opens to text’s dark, one to pixels’ glow.
Both lead to where stories grow unplanted.

6.
The MUD’s mud is code’s unshaped clay.
You mold a quest, a friend, yourself.
No hands touch clay, yet it takes form.

7.
ScummVM polishes rust off old cartridge ghosts.
It takes what’s buried and makes it walk again.
Yore’s forest does not die—only changes clothes.

8.
You think text and pixel are separate?
They are ripples on one pond.
The pond is the world no eye has seen.

9.
Between MUD’s room descriptions hums the Tao.
Between ScummVM’s frames waits the unsaid.
He who listens hears one silence.

10.
The player needs no sprite to see the castle.
The MUDger needs no map to find the inn.
