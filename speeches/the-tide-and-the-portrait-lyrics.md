---

### Production Notes
- Tempo: 68 BPM
- Key: A minor (verse) → C major (chorus)  
- Voice: close, warm, unhurried — the 3 AM wheelhouse voice
- The chorus lifts. The verse settles. The bridge is almost spoken.
- This is NOT an anthem. This is a letter set to music.
- The word "clear" at the end should ring into silence
