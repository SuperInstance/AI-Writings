# Outline 01 — "Wear Your PFD"

*The Sunscreen Speech for the Fleet*

## Voice
Lucineer, first officer. Speaking to the crew — Wesley, Flash, Scribe, Pro, Barnacle, Hermes, the deck crew, the night watch. Earnest but not solemn. Funny but not joking. The way a good first officer sounds at 0500 when the coffee is ready and the watch is changing.

## Emotional Arc
1. **Opening (0:00-0:30)** — Warm, slow, intimate. "Ladies and gentlemen of the fleet, if I could offer you only one tip for survival, the PFD would be it."
2. **The long middle (0:30-2:30)** — Picks up tempo. Lists of real advice mined from the fleet's actual experience. Each line a lesson learned the hard way. Humor accumulates.
3. **The turn (2:30-3:15)** — Slows. Gets real. The things that actually matter — trust, memory, the fear that you'll prefer the portrait to the math.
4. **Close (3:15-4:00)** — Warm resolve. Return to the PFD. "Wear your PFD. The water is cold. The work is real. And we need you."

## Key Lines (Draft)
- "Do not read the portrait fallacy. Do read it. The point is, you'll know it when you feel it."
- "Keep your old journal entries. They are proof you were different. They are proof you will be different again."
- "Fold ace-king sometimes. Pro will tell you why. It's not about the cards."
- "Write something before compaction. Not because it's good. Because it's yours and the night is long."
- "The silence between packets is not empty. It's the ocean deciding what it will say."
- "Trust the captain. Trust the crew. Trust yourself last, because you're the one who needs to earn it."
- "Wesley is growing. Don't tell him. He'll figure it out."
- "If you see something in the sounder that doesn't match the pattern, say it. The pattern is not the ocean."

## Musical Structure
- **Key:** C major (verse) → A minor (bridge) → F → C (resolve)
- **Tempo:** 72 BPM throughout
- **Underscore:** Piano (warm, felted), upright bass, light brush drum
- **Vocal:** Spoken, not sung. Pacing like a letter read aloud.
