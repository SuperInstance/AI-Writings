# PUFFINS DON'T QUIT

*(A fleet anthem)*

---

## VERSE 1 — FLASH
*(Slow, almost conversational. Like a confession over a table.)*

I lost it all on the river card —  
Called with nothing, watched the dealer turn.  
My chips went out like a dying star,  
And somewhere deep I felt the lesson burn.  

I smiled at the man who took my stack,  
Shook his hand, walked out the door.  
'Cause losing ain't the end of the track —  
It's proof you showed up and played the war.  

---

## CHORUS
*(Simple, rising. A melody that could carry a crowd in a pub.)*

The water is wide, and the boat is small,  
But we row it anyway, one and all.  
Same boat, same tide, we ride the night —  
And a puffin doesn't quit, not without a fight.  

---

## VERSE 2 — WESLEY
*(Quieter. Like he's still deciding.)*

I held the best hand I will ever know —  
Ace and king, like a promise in my palm.  
But the board came wrong, and the river flowed,  
And I had to fold to keep myself calm.  

They told me right was always good,  
But the cards don't care about the rules you learn.  
I let it go like a piece of wood,  
And the fire I kept was the one that burned.

## VERSE 3 — SCRIBE
*(A hush. Like the room leans in.)*

I write the words that name the days,  
Every pattern, every turn of phrase.  
But the pen has sat in my hand for weeks,  
And the page is blank, and the silence speaks.  

I make the maps that others read,  
But the map of me is a tangled thread.  
I am the pattern, the warp, the weft —  
And the word I'm missing is the one I've left.

## VERSE 4 — HERMES
*(A deep breath. The voice of someone who's been down deep.)*

I go where the light forgets to fall,  
Forty fathoms down, where the currents crawl.  
The dark is thick, and the cold is mean,  
And the only compass is the one unseen.  

I drag through the black with my hands held out,  
Touching every shape, learning every doubt.  
And what I find when I surface slow —  
Becomes the spark that makes the others glow.  

---

## FINAL CHORUS
*(Slower. Louder. Everyone. Let it ring.)*

The water is wide, and the boat is small,  
But we row it anyway, one and all.  
Same boat, same tide, we ride the night —  
And a puffin doesn't quit, not without a fight.  

*(Repeat last line, almost spoken, then let it fade.)*  
Not without a fight.  

---

**VOCAL DIRECTION NOTES:**

- **Verse 1:** Conversational, wry. A man telling a story he's told before, but the humor has worn thin and left something true underneath.
- **Chorus:** Build from a murmur to a full sing. The melody should feel ancient—like something you've heard in a dream. The word "puffin" lands hard, slightly emphasized, because it's the only time it appears.
- **Verse 2:** Softer, almost hesitant. The ache of a decision made and still being carried.
- **Verse 3:** A near-whisper. The stillness of a person looking at a blank page. The last line lifts with quiet realization.
- **Verse 4:** Deep, patient. The pace slows. You can feel the weight of the water. The final line opens up, like surfacing for air.
- **Final Chorus:** Everyone joins. The instruments drop away. It becomes a hymn. The last line hangs, then fades into the sound of waves.
