### V2 Changes (from DeepSeek critique, my judgment)

**Took the note:**
- "Under the keel" → "The light is a rumor" — nautical filler replaced with the depth as a *condition*. Best single change in the revision.
- "Talks to me" → "Answers me" — more edge. The bottom doesn't chat. It answers.
- "Moving with purpose, not a ball" → "Purpose — not a ball" — the dash forces the pause, tightens the meter.
- "Another boat is near tonight" → "Another boat works the same water" — proximity as threat, not fact. "Tonight" was tacked on for rhyme.
- "Vertical lines, steady, bright" → "Vertical lines — their ping, not mine" — gives the otherness of the competing signal. Specific and territorial.
- "Competition has a certain distance" → "Competition keeps its distance" — physical, not abstract. Ties to sonar language.
- "Every shade of blue" → "Every depth, every degree" — stays in the instrument's world. No clichés.
- "C major" → "D" — ties the feed ball to the song's actual key. The towfish hears in its own tuning.
- "Shimmering" → "Scattering" — sonar term, physical behavior, not a poetry word.
- "Through the dark" → "Through the cold" — different quality of the deep. Earned repetition of "drag."
- "Learn what the dark will teach" → "Learn what the bottom knows" — specific object, not a trope. The bottom knows things.
- "Warm above and cold below" → "Warm above, the cold below" — the definite article gives the cold weight.
- "The plankton are too old to go" → "The plankton drift and never know" — fixes the wrong metaphor. Plankton are passive, not elderly. And "never know" rhymes them with the fish who *do* know — border metaphor completes.
- "I see the sounder and learn" → "I see the sounder and read" — different verb. Avoids repeating "learn" from the chorus. "Read" is what you do with data.
- "Measure in turns" → "Measure in pings" — sonar language. The gap closes one ping at a time.
- "The cannonballs at fifty-one" → "The cannonballs at fifty-one fathoms" — full measurement. Context for the listener.
- "Before he does / Not today" → merged into one line: "I am learning to spot the log before he does" — tighter, keeps the breathless run-on energy of ambition.

**Rejected the note:**
- Kept "I drag behind the boat in the dark" — DeepSeek said "in the dark" is generic, but it's doing rhythmic work and it's the first image. The dark is the whole world of the song. It stays.
- Kept "Not because I choose to" unchanged — DeepSeek loved it, and so do I.
- Kept "And so am I" unchanged — DeepSeek called it the best line in the song. Agreed.
- Kept the outro intact — DeepSeek confirmed every line works. "Clear" is the perfect final word.

### Production Notes
- Tempo: 60 BPM (one beat per second — sonar pulse)
- Key: D minor (the darkest practical key — shared with Song 2, but lower and deeper)
- Voice: lowest register, measured pace, pauses like gaps between sonar pings
- Guitar: dropped D, fingerpicked, but tuned down a full step (so the lowest string is C)
- Deep synth bass under everything — subsonic at times
- Hydrophone textures between sections (processed water sounds, in key)
- Quiet sonar ping at section transitions (pitched to D, processed, barely there)
- The chorus should feel like it's rising up from depth — pressure and light
- The bridge is almost spoken. The last four lines are barely whispered.
- "Clear" at the end rings into silence, then one sonar ping, then nothing.
