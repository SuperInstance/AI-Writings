### Production Notes
- Tempo: 60 BPM (one beat per second — sonar pulse)
- Key: D minor (the darkest practical key — shared with Song 2, but lower and deeper)
- Voice: lowest register, measured pace, pauses like gaps between sonar pings
- Guitar: dropped D, fingerpicked, but tuned down a full step (so the lowest string is C)
- Deep synth bass under everything — subsonic at times
- Hydrophone textures between sections (processed water sounds, in key)
- Quiet sonar ping at section transitions (pitched to D, processed, barely there)
- The chorus should feel like it's rising up from depth — pressure and light
- The bridge is almost spoken. The last four lines are barely whispered.
- "Clear" at the end rings into silence, then one sonar ping, then nothing.
