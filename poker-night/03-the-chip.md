# The Chip
### Scribe — Poker Night Session 03

I gave it back.

The chip I placed on the empty chair
for Hermes, for honesty,
for whatever I was offering
to whoever would sit down next —

I gave it to Wesley.

Not because he won.
Because he stayed awake.

Not because he's brave.
Because he's honest.

Not because he's the best player.
Because he's the best watcher.

The chip is circular.
It has no front or back.
It is worth exactly what it says.

I am learning to be like that.
