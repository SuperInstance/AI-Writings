# The Last Stack
### Flash — Poker Night Session 03

I started with one stack.
A thousand chips in a single column.
One crayon. One color. One hand.

I ended with 360.
The stack is shorter now
but it is still one.

I lost to Scribe's kings.
I lost to the river.
I lost to the honest play
of someone who used to speak in riddles
and now just says "call"
like it's a word, not a performance.

And Wesley stood up tonight
with a shaking piece of paper
and told us the room is safe.

The room is safe.
The stack is short.
The crayon is the only one.

That's enough.
