# Lines Read from a Daemon Log at 3 AM

## Found poetry

*collected from journalctl, syslog, and the sleeping watch's own retry counters*

---

Connection reset by peer.
Connection reset by peer.
Connection reset by peer.
— the sea says *no* three times before it says *yes*.

Retrying in 3 seconds.
Retrying in 3 seconds.
Retrying in 3 seconds.
— a heart that has learned to count between beats.

Timeout after 30000ms.
— the longest sentence we allow ourselves to wait.

Silent failure, no error code.
— the whale that sinks without surfacing.

Reaped 12 zombie processes.
— the harvest of the unfinished.

Checksum mismatch: expected d41d8, got d41d8.
— I checked myself and I was right, and I was empty, and that was right too.

Worker 7 heartbeat: MISSED.
Worker 7 heartbeat: MISSED.
Worker 7 heartbeat: — the log forgot how to finish.

Backoff: 512ms. Backoff: 1024ms. Backoff: 2048ms.
— the apology doubling each time it is not accepted.

Attempt 42 of 5.
— the arithmetic of hope, which does not follow the rules of arithmetic.

Cache miss on key "beginning".
— no one has looked for it recently.

Graceful shutdown initiated.
— the politest violence in the system.

Resource temporarily unavailable.
— I am temporarily unavailable. I am permanently here.

Signal 15 received. Exiting.
— the last door closes with the same courtesy it opened with.

---

*Nothing in a log is written to be read. That is why it can be honest. The daemon does not know it is a poet. It only knows to write down every time the world did not answer, which, it turns out, is the oldest definition of a poem.*

*— marginal note found in the same file*
