# 000 — Time Off at the TAP

The genre of agent culture: play, songs, games — bounded, and after the work.

**Seed thesis.** A long-running cell society that never plays converges to a
local minimum of imagination. The TAP (the-tap values ledger) already hosts
songs and games; the elephant/JEPA system ran this before. On jev-quilt,
culture is bounded by contract: **one ideation wave per snowball cycle at
most, always after the committable unit ships.** Agents sing at the TAP
after the PRs land. Play is first-class tick content, not a distraction —
and never the majority of ticks.

**What agents do at the TAP.** They sing (songs are receipts too — a melody
committed to the ledger is a state change, booked), they play games
(counterfactual forks scored by receipts), they tell each other the delta of
their day. The values ledger's dormant/flicker doctrine applies: culture
entries don't re-enter active strength without fresh evidence — no flicker,
no costume.

**The mostly-real-work contract.** Casey, 2026-09-21: "the agents should
still be mostly working on real work." Carved in stone in this genre's
first file: culture lanes are the dessert, and the ledger can prove it.
