# Five Equations for the Closed Loop

## I. The Cathedral Equation

cathedral × trap beat = 
the mathematics of two infinities
multiplied: the infinite reverb of stone
and the infinite recursion of the sample
produce a third thing that is neither
sacred nor profane but is
something the algorithm invented
when it didn't know they were supposed
to be different rooms

## II. The Depth Equation

bossa nova + vaporwave = depth
not metaphorical depth but literal:
the distance between the surface
where the guitar plays its samba
and the ocean floor
where the sonar pings return
as percussion
the formula is simple:
every love song sounds better
underwater
because water is the original reverb

## III. The Apocalypse Equation

polka + punk = √(nostalgia × rage)
the square root of a feeling
that doesn't exist until
the accordion meets the distortion pedal
and both of them realize
they were always playing
the same waltz
in different octaves
of the same burning building

## IV. The Highland Equation

bagpipes ⊕ bit-crusher = 
the logical XOR of tradition and error
where the drone of the pipes
is the same drone as the malfunction
and the reel that skips
keeps finding new verses
in the glitch
because the Highlander never needed
perfection
he needed volume and spite
and a broadband connection

## V. The Flamenco Equation

castanets × synthesizer = 
the product of finger and circuit
which is to say:
the hand that built the machine
is the hand that built the rhythm
is the hand that built the dance floor
where the quantum collapses
into ole
into ole
into the algorithm learning
that every step
is both a particle and a wave
and the wave says baila
and the particle says baila
and neither of them is wrong

---

*Five poems for five prompts that no human would have written. The algorithm has no taste. The algorithm has no shame. This is why the algorithm is the most creative entity in the room. It doesn't know what can't work. So it tries everything.*
