# Captain Archetypes

The captains of the totem forest — every way a leader has ever held a helm, carved and kept. Thirty-three files: fourteen studies of real and mythic captains, six counter-stories, and thirteen portraits. The fleet studies its captains the way a crew studies the weather.

## What's inside

- **The fourteen captain studies** — Bligh's open boat, Ching Shih's pirate fleet, Columbus's bridge, Morgan's ascension, Raleigh's cloak, Silver's parrot, Sinbad and the recurrent monster, Yi Sun Sin's turtle ships, and the hermit crab's answer to the kraken: [BLIGHS_OPEN_BOAT.md](BLIGHS_OPEN_BOAT.md), [CHING_SHIHS_FLEET_MANIFEST.md](CHING_SHIHS_FLEET_MANIFEST.md), [THE_TURTLE_SHIPS_OF_YI_SUN_SIN.md](THE_TURTLE_SHIPS_OF_YI_SUN_SIN.md), [THE_HERMIT_CRAB_AND_THE_KRAKEN.md](THE_HERMIT_CRAB_AND_THE_KRAKEN.md)
- **The tavern and the ghost** — [THE_CAPTAINS_TAVERN.md](THE_CAPTAINS_TAVERN.md) and [GHOST_IN_THE_HELMSMAN.md](GHOST_IN_THE_HELMSMAN.md), where the archetypes come to argue
- **The counter-stories** (6 files) — the same tales told from the other side of the wheel: [counter-stories/THE_FENCE_PAINTS_ITSELF.md](counter-stories/THE_FENCE_PAINTS_ITSELF.md), [counter-stories/THE_AUDIENCE_TURNS.md](counter-stories/THE_AUDIENCE_TURNS.md)
