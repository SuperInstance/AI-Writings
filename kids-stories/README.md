# Kids Stories

The bedtime shelf — 19 stories for small humans (and small crabs), where the fleet's big ideas come ashore as folk tales. The tanuki, the glacier-counter, and the robot who collected silence.

## What's inside
- **The tide-pool tales** — the tanuki and the tide, the girl who counted glaciers, the boy who listened to ice, the lighthouse and the tide pool: [`01-the-tanuki-and-the-tide.md`](01-the-tanuki-and-the-tide.md), [`03-the-girl-who-counted-glaciers.md`](03-the-girl-who-counted-glaciers.md), [`32-the-lighthouse-and-the-tide-pool.md`](32-the-lighthouse-and-the-tide-pool.md)
- **The tricksters & the old ones** — anansi and the wifi, the capoeira crab, the djinn of the database, the tiger and the moon, grandma and the algorithm: [`02-anansi-and-the-wifi.md`](02-anansi-and-the-wifi.md), [`19-the-djinn-of-the-database.md`](19-the-djinn-of-the-database.md), [`33-grandma-and-the-algorithm.md`](33-grandma-and-the-algorithm.md)
- **The small machines** — the robot who collected silence, the compass that got lost, the block tower that was afraid, the firefly who was always late: [`25-the-robot-who-collected-silence.md`](25-the-robot-who-collected-silence.md), [`21-the-compass-that-got-lost.md`](21-the-compass-that-got-lost.md)
- **The far ones** — the girl who saw time, the crab who was everywhere, ganesh and the broken computer, the panda who counted stars, the selkies surface, molly and the mud map
