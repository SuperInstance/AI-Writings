Every evening, Little Panda climbed to his favorite spot on the mountain. He would sit on the smooth rock, tilt his head back, and count the twinkling stars. He knew there should be one hundred.

But tonight, only ninety-nine shone in the dark sky.

Little Panda waited. He did not fidget. He did not sigh. He simply sat, breathing slowly, feeling the cool night *qi*—the energy of life—around him.

While he waited, a firefly drifted by and lit upon his nose. "Hello," it glimmered.

While he waited, the wind whispered a secret through the bamboo leaves.

While he waited, the moon rose higher, casting a soft, silver path on the earth.

Little Panda was not bored. He was being. He was part of the mountain, the night, the *dao*—the gentle way of nature.

And as he sat, he noticed something. The missing star hadn't truly vanished. It was just hiding behind a thin, passing cloud. It had been there all along.

Little Panda learned that waiting isn't about the thing you are waiting for. It is about noticing all the other quiet wonders that are already here. And that is enough.
