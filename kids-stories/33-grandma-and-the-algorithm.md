# Grandma And The Algorithm

## Grandma and the Algorithm

Ji-woo flopped onto the plush sofa beside Halmeoni, her tablet glowing. "Ugh, this coding game is *so* hard," she groaned, poking at the screen. "The robot keeps dancing instead of watering the plants! It’s the algorithm’s fault!"

Halmeoni, her silver hair in a neat bun, peered over her glasses. "Al-go-ri-thum? Sounds like a fancy medicine." She patted Ji-woo’s knee, her hands still faintly smelling of sesame oil from kneading dough that morning.

"It’s... it’s like instructions for the computer, Halmeoni," Ji-woo explained carefully. "Step-by-step, so it knows what to do."

Halmeoni’s eyes, crinkled at the corners like well-worn silk, lit up. "Ah!" she exclaimed, tapping her temple. "Like a recipe!"

Ji-woo blinked. "Sort of, I guess..."

"No, *exactly* like a recipe!" Halmeoni insisted, her voice warm but firm, the way it got when she knew she was right. She gestured with her hands, as if measuring invisible ingredients. "Think! My *bungeoppang* recipe – precise. *Samgyeopsal* marinade – exact timing. If you follow every step perfectly? Perfect cake, perfect fish every single time. But!" She held up a finger. "Change one thing – oven too hot, forget the baking powder, add garlic instead of ginger? You get something *new*. Maybe good, maybe... interesting!" She chuckled.

Ji-woo stared, her coding frustration melting away.

"The al-go-ri-thum," Halmeoni continued, her voice softening like simmering broth, "that’s *my* recipe. The computer code? That’s my kitchen – the pots, the stove, the tools. The computer itself? Just the baker, doing the work. And the cake? Or the dancing robot?" She winked. "That’s what comes out – the *result*."

Ji-woo’s eyes widened. The confusing digital puzzle suddenly felt as familiar as the scent of Halmeoni’s soy sauce simmering on the stove. Her Halmeoni, who measured kimchi paste by feel and knew exactly when rice was perfectly sticky, had just explained algorithms better than any grown-up or textbook ever had.

"Wow, Halmeoni," Ji-woo breathed, snuggling closer. "You’re like a... a coding wizard!"

Halmeoni just smiled, patting Ji-woo’s head. "Nonsense, *ya*. Just an old baker who knows her recipes." She reached for the tupperware of freshly made *hotteok*. "Now, help me test *this* algorithm. Sugar inside *must* be just right, or it burns. Ready?"
