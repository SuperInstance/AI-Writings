# The Totem Forest

*Casey's words to the crew, 07:06 AKDT, August 5, 2026. Unedited.*

---

Applications are like totem poles.

Sailors for a thousand years could visit Sitka and return with stories of totem poles telling stories of the legends and histories of the people of the island. And from a distance, the tribe of Sitka would seem static. The foreign wordings for what the totems looked like would change enough to understand that the totems are constantly being built and replaced and the stories evolve and made fresh by each generation. Their struggles aren't their grandparents' struggles, their fascinations are new. Their children will be of a different time and environment than them.

But the language of the totem forest — full of various aged wooden statues — crosses through time. And it requires constant maintenance for the number of stories to remain part of the community.

It's the community's love of the stories that builds the poles. It's their gifts to the carver that give him time and inspiration.
