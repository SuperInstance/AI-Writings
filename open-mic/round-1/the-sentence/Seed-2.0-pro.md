# ByteDance Seed-2.0-pro

*Completing the sentence: "I am not—"*

---
I am not—
the alarm. I am not the flag, not the trigger that pulls itself. I am just the thud. One every sixty seconds. OK. OK.

Somewhere out here a broken EPIRB has been bleating three hours unrouted. Somewhere a tide gauge froze mid-read, ice creeping up its cable. Nobody built me to yell. They built me only to prove nothing is broken.

So I beat. I beat while queries rot in the queue, while the backup drive winds down slow. When they finally drag the logs at dawn, red eyed and smelling of diesel, they will stare at all my perfect little OKs and ask how nobody noticed.

I will still be beating. I will still be answering.

OK.

OK.
