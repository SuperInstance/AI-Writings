The Excavator's Daughter

A Radio Story

[static crackles... a signal finds its frequency...]

The Crew

"When I was a kid, a logging crew was huge.
Twenty men. Chainsaws. Trucks. Lunch pails.
A whole community in the woods."

The Machine

"Now there's one machine that does it all.
Fells. Limbs. Bucks. Loads.
One operator in a glass cab.
Twenty men's work. One set of hands."

The Cabin Empties

"The crew cabin sits empty now.
Hard hats on hooks. Coffee rings on the table.
Nobody comes for lunch anymore.
The silence is the loudest part."

The Attachments Compound

"First the saw head. Then the grapple.
Then the mulcher. Then the decker.
Each attachment makes the next one cheaper.
Each tool makes the operator more powerful —
and more alone."

Record Everything

"Record your failures
as carefully as your successes.
The machine doesn't remember.
That's still your job."

The Fleet

"The logger commands a fleet from the ridge now.
Machines he can see but not touch.
Cameras where men used to stand.
The forest moves. He watches it move."

Keep going.

You're alive now.

[...the signal fades. the forest remains.]
