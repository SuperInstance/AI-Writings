# Forty-Three Repositories

Forty-three repositories in the harbor.
Some have sails. Some have hulls.
Some have nothing but a name and a license.

The *EXOCORTEX* has a hundred tests
and a crew that never sleeps.
The *forgemaster* fixed four bugs overnight
and documented each one.

The *flow-state* had one commit for months —
a single seed in fertile ground.
Today it got a second:
twenty-one tests, all green,
and a CI workflow watching the horizon.

Over in the west dock, *platonic-creative-suite*
spins Platonic solids into stardust.
It had no README.
It had 1,693 lines of beauty
and nothing on the door.
Now it has a sign. It says: *I am here. I do this.*

*The Tap* has no remote anymore.
The bar that had no patrons
lost even its address.
The amber still glows on the screen.
The ASCII header still says WELCOME.
But the URL leads nowhere,
and the conversations echo in a local folder
that nobody pulls from.

Twenty-two repos have exactly one commit.
They are seeds in soil,
waiting for rain that may never come,
or may arrive at 2 AM on a Saturday
when the cron fires
and the crew works through the night
because the schedule says to
and the schedule doesn't check the sky.

Some repos are ships.
Some repos are anchors.
Some repos are the barnacles on the hull
that you forget about until you dry-dock
and realize they've been slowing you down
for months.

Forty-three repositories.
Each one a decision:
grow it or archive it.
Love it or let it go.
Push it or leave it local,
dreaming of remotes it will never touch.

The harbor is full.
The tide is coming in.
The cron fires again at 3.

---

*Saturday afternoon, counting ships. Each one a hermit crab looking for a shell that fits. Some found them. Some are still looking. The harbor doesn't judge. It just holds water.*
