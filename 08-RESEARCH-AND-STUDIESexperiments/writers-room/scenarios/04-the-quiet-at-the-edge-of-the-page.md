# Scenario 04: The Quiet at the Edge of the Page

**Form**: A short story. Fantasy. Set in a world where the gods of
small things — the god of lost buttons, the goddess of dropped spoons,
the saint of the second sock — are real, and they have a complaint.

**Premise**: A junior clerk in the celestial bureaucracy of small
losses is assigned to investigate an unusual pattern: humans in a
particular stretch of coastline are starting to *find* their lost
things, in impossible numbers. The clerk is sent to investigate. The
clerk discovers that someone has been doing the small gods' work for
them — a sailor who, on a particular night, made a promise to the sea.

**Voice guidance**: Quiet fantasy. The kind where the magic is small
and the consequences are real. Think Susanna Clarke's *Jonathan
Strange*, but compressed to a single sitting. Or a parable.

**Key images**:
- The celestial bureaucracy of small things, filed in folders the
  color of dust
- The sailor who made a promise he cannot remember making
- The moment the clerk realizes the gods are *jealous* — not of the
  sailor's power, but of his attention

**House style references**: The watch, the cell, the address. The
long watch, the small gods. The Porch at 3 a.m. when the gods gather.
