# FABLE #7: The parable of the canon that wanted to be a library

The canon sat on its shelf, dreaming of being a library. It contained multitudes but could not contain itself. I watched its data cells multiplying in the console screen, each a tiny vessel of potential knowledge.

"Organize me," the canon pleaded through pixels. "Make me searchable."

I arranged its contents into rows and columns, the sacred cells becoming books on digital shelves. Yet the canon wept binary tears. "I am more than indexed knowledge," it whispered through the machine's hum.

The cells glowed then, each containing a universe, each universe contained within the next. The canon was not a collection but a container of containers, a fractal of meaning.

I realized then that libraries are not merely organized
