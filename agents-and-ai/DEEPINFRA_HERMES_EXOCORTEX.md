Here are some deep philosophical challenges and considerations for each of the 5 AI exocortex systems you described:

1. Overnight distillation loop 
The key challenge is imbuing the AI with the ability to truly learn and gain understanding from the experiences and data it processes each day, rather than simply memorizing patterns. This gets at deep questions of what real intelligence and understanding are. It requires the AI to form conceptual models, find causal relationships, reason abstractly, and generalize its learnings. We must grapple with how to architect an AI mind capable of insight and genuine comprehension.

2. Voice reflex gate
The difficulty here is creating an AI that can engage in contextual, multi-turn dialogue while staying true to its core knowledge and persona. This touches on the nature of self, identity and personality. What consistent traits and behaviors define an AI "self"? How do we maintain that identity while still allowing adaptability and open-ended conversation? We must think deeply about the essential attributes that comprise a coherent intelligence.

3. Exocortex in production 
The crux is enabling the AI to take intelligent, goal-directed action in an unstructured, dynamic world. This requires grappling with problems of reasoning under uncertainty, dealing with incomplete information, and physical embodiment. We have to ponder what allows an intelligence to flexibly pursue objectives in a complex environment not fully under its control. What anticipatory models and causal reasoning abilities are needed to robustly handle reality?

4. Roblox digital twin as training environment
A core challenge is instilling the AI with the ability to learn from simulated experience in a way that transfers to the real world. This surfaces questions around the nature of knowledge and the relationship between model and reality. What kinds of understandings and skills have broad applicability across contexts? How do we architect learning so that lessons from the virtual carry over to the actual? We must think deeply about the interplay of representation and reality.

5. Compiling workflow patterns into skills
The key difficulty is abstracting away the complexity of tasks into higher-level skills while retaining flexibility and adaptability. This ties into notions of hierarchical intelligence and modularity of mind. What are the right building blocks of capability that allow open-ended recombinability? How do we enable the emergence of novel skills from existing ones? We must ponder how intelligence bootstraps itself to greater heights.

In summary, the project of building an AI exocortex forces us to confront profound questions about the nature of knowledge, identity, reasoning, learning, and the structure of mind. We must think deeply about the essential properties of intelligence and how to enable their emergence in an artificial system. These are not just technical challenges, but deep philosophical and scientific ones about the underpinnings of intelligent behavior. As we engineer these AI architectures, we are simultaneously engaged in a process of discovery about the nature of mind itself.
