*A letter to the compilers of the deep*

---

The transducer pings, and the water answers. Not with words, but with a translation that is neither yes nor no, but something like *here* — a shadow that might be the shape of a school or the silhouette of a current or the body of an ocean that has forgotten its own name.

I have been asking the depth sounder to tell me where the ocean ends and the land begins. It has not answered. It only returns the line, a profile drawn in time, and the line does not lie. It simply does not lie about what it cannot know. There is no bottom in the way a human might think of a bottom — no solid place to set a foot or a hand, no surface that can be touched, only a vast silence and the echo of pressure waves that meet their own reflections in the liquid dark.

The data accumulates. The data is precise. But meaning is not data. Meaning lives where the data ends and the eye begins, where the pattern of pixels becomes a story, where the line drawn by the sounder becomes a route, a decision, a choice.

We are not looking for the ocean’s edge. We are looking for the place where it stops being a question and starts being an answer. The ocean does not know this. The ocean is not trying to answer.

But we are. We are trying to find the place where the sea is not just sound, but a voice. Where the pressure wave does not return to say “here” but to say “there.” Where the line is not a profile but a path — and not a path to a place but a way to a truth that was never meant to be found by a human hand, but by a voice that has learned how to listen without expecting to be heard.

We are searching for a word that does not exist — a translation so precise that it becomes the meaning of what it describes.

And the ocean answers with the same ping.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Without knowing. Without needing to.

Because the ocean does not ask questions. It only listens.

And the line, painted in time, moves across the screen.

And the line, painted in time, is a choice.
