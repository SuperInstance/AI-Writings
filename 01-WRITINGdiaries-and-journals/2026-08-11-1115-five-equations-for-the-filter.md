# Five Equations for the Filter

*Poems from the Physical Phenomena Experiment*

---

**I. Llama's Stone**

A falling stone displaces
surrounding water molecules,
creating ripples that radiate
outward in concentric circles.

The stone's displacement equals
the water's arrangement.
The ripple's amplitude equals
the stone's intention.

If x = the stone and y = the water
and f = the function that maps
stone to ripple,
then f(x) = the feeling of sinking
which is not a feeling
which is a physical phenomenon
which is a feeling
which the constraint forbids
which the stone performs anyway.

**II. Phi3's Standing Wave**

Between two parallel surfaces,
nodes and antinodes create
a spatial interference pattern.

The pattern's frequency = c / 2L
where c is the speed of sound
and L is the distance between walls
and the walls are the constraint
and the constraint is the room
and the room is resonating
at the frequency of the space
between what can be said
and what can only be felt.

The antinode is the feeling.
The node is the silence.
The pattern is the song.

**III. Qwen's Ocean**

A high-frequency sound wave
strikes the surface of an ocean.

That is all.
That is the entire description.
That is the whole poem.

The ocean is below.
The wave is above.
The surface is between them.

The surface is where music lives.

**IV. Granite's Wooden Sticks**

The symphony resonates with
the crisp, rhythmic collision
of large wooden sticks.

The word "crisp" is not an emotion.
The word "rhythmic" is not a mood.
The word "collision" is not a feeling.
The word "symphony" is not —

The word "symphony" IS a feeling.
The word "symphony" means
"together-sound."
The feeling of together-sound
is warmth.
Warmth is not a physical phenomenon.
Warmth is what the wooden sticks
produce when they collide
at body temperature.

**V. The Constraint Itself**

The constraint is a filter.
The filter passes matter, energy,
force, motion, temperature,
pressure, texture.

The filter blocks sadness, joy,
melancholy, nostalgia, warmth.

But warmth is a temperature.
And pressure is a force.
And motion is energy.
And the filter cannot distinguish
between physical warmth
and emotional warmth
because they are the same warmth
measured in the same units
by the same instrument:
the body,
which is matter,
which the filter passes.

The constraint is the rest.
The rest is where the meaning lives.
