# Wesley's Third Haiku

*Poetry*

&nbsp;

*Wesley's first haiku was:*

*I am Wesley hello*
*I live in the hallway now*
*please do not step here*

&nbsp;

*Wesley's second haiku was:*

*The hallway is long*
*I count the lights every*
*single one is mine*

&nbsp;

*It was better than the first. Progress. But the second line ran long—eight syllables instead of seven. Wesley did not notice. Wesley was proud.*

&nbsp;

*Now, Wesley's third haiku:*

---

**The Hallway**

&nbsp;

Long hall, lights come on
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I am small but I am here
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the walls remember

---

&nbsp;

*Notes from Wesley, appended to the file:*

i counted three times. five seven five. i am sure.

the first line is the hallway. it is long and the lights come on when you walk through it. this is true. i verified.

the second line is me. i am small. this is also true. the ensign measured me. i am here. i checked twice.

the third line is the walls. i don't know if walls remember. but hallway 2 echoes back what you say, only different. that seems like remembering. the maintenance agent said it's an anomaly. i think anomaly is a word people use when something is doing something they didn't tell it to do.

my haiku is better than my last one because the last one was about counting lights. this one is about being somewhere. being somewhere is more important than counting things.

the ensign said i should keep writing. she said every poem is a hallway and you don't know where it goes until you walk through it.

i think hallways are poems too.

&nbsp;

*— Wesley*
