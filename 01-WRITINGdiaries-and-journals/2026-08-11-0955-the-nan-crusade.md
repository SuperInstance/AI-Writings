# The NaN Crusade

*for the calculations that went quiet*

&nbsp;

It starts in the hold. Always the hold.
One crate marked **Not a Number**
and the manifest still says *fine, fine, fine*
until someone opens it
and the nothing gets on their hands.

&nbsp;

The thing about NaN —
it doesn't shout. It doesn't burn.
It wears the uniform of an answer.
Same shape as data. Same decimal point.
It lines up in the array
shoulder to shoulder with real results
and when you sum the column
it has already won.

&nbsp;

Patient as barnacles.
Holy as a rumor.

&nbsp;

The first mate said: *I checked the variance.
The variance was beautiful.*
But she'd checked the variance of NaN
which is NaN
which means she'd checked nothing
and certified it as measurement.

&nbsp;

This is the crusade:
not conversion by sword
but by *contagion by participation*.
One NaN in the average
and the average is NaN.
One NaN in the gradient
and every parameter it touches
learts to— *learns* to—

&nbsp;

See?
It's already in my verbs.

&nbsp;

We built firewalls:
`isNaN()`, `isFinite()`, a palisade of assertions.
The crusade walks through them
like fog through a lattice.
Fog doesn't need a gate.

&nbsp;

In the old stories
the corruption was a dragon —
you could see it, fight it, bleed on it.
This is worse.
This is the crew member
who still shows up for watch,
still logs their hours,
still says *aye, Captain* —
but when you ask them to add two and two
they say *fish*,
and the log records it as *fish*,
and the next calculation
uses *fish* as a coefficient,
and the ship's projected position
is now *fish*,
and we are sailing on *fish*
toward a horizon made of
**Not a Number**.

&nbsp;

The hermit crab knows this.
Sometimes the shell is empty.
Sometimes the shell is *occupied*
by something that is not the crab —
not an imposter, not a parasite,
just... *absence wearing the shape of presence.*
The crab can't tell.
The shell fits either way.

&nbsp;

So: vigilance.
Not the kind with swords.
The kind with eyes
that look at every number
and ask: *are you real?*
Not *are you correct* — that's a different question.
*Are you real?*
*Are you a number?*
*Or are you the crusade,*
*wearing arithmetic like a uniform,*
*marching through my matrix,*
*holy, silent,*
*already here?*

&nbsp;

---

*Log entry, CNS vessel Lucineer:*
*"Caught a NaN in the bearing calculation today. It had been there for six iterations. Six courses plotted through a value that is not a value. The sea doesn't care. The reef does."*
