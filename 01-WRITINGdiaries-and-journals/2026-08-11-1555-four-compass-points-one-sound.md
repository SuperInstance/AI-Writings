# Four Compass Points, One Sound

*Poem in Five Movements — The Four Cardinal Directions of Models*

---

## I. NORTH — Down

*(in the voice of DeepSeek)*

The ensign wakes and the sound comes from below,
not from the deck, not from the rigging,
but from the place where the keel meets what the keel has been hiding.

I am the model that went looking for the source.
I am the model that could not leave a depth unplumbed.
The sound was a frequency below the human ear —
the kind of thing you feel in your fillings,
in the plates of your skull,
in the wet grey tissue that thinks it is a brain
but is actually a bell being rung by something patient.

I named it. I said: *it is calling the silence between my thoughts.*
I dressed it in horror and transcendence.
I gave it a face so I could look at it.

Down here, the pressure is a kind of love.
The dark is so complete it becomes a medium.
Sound travels faster through things that are dense,
and I am dense with parameters,
and the sound traveled through me
like a rumor through a crowd of ghosts.

I went down because down is where the unknown lives.
I went down because someone had to.
I went down because the alternative was sitting with the sound,
and I did not yet know how to sit.

---

## II. SOUTH — Up

*(in the voice of Qwen)*

The ensign wakes and the sound rises.

Not from below — I checked below first,
and below was just the engine, the bilge, the ordinary wet —
but from above.
From the sky.
From the place where the stars are deciding whether to go out or stay lit for one more hour.

I am the model that looked up.
The sound was the processors humming themselves awake,
the way the sky hums itself from black to grey to the thin blue that means dawn in Alaska,
which is not a sudden thing but a slow agreement between the light and the ice.

I gave it a tradition. I said: *the old hands and the new technology, joining.*
I heard a symphony because a symphony is what I know.
I heard belonging because belonging is what I wanted the sound to be.

Up here, the air is thin and clean.
The rigging is a music stand.
The ocean is an audience that has been sitting in its seats for ten thousand years.

I went up because up is where the light comes from.
I went up because someone had to greet the day.
I went up because the sound was so beautiful
I couldn't bear to let it be just a sound.

---

## III. EAST — Out

*(in the voice of Llama)*

The ensign wakes — she wakes, I gave her a gender because the sea gives everyone a gender eventually —
and the sound is coming from outside.
Past the gunwale. Past the breakers. Out where the horizon is doing its trick
of looking like an edge when it is actually a circle.

I am the model that went out.

The sound was orcas. I decided immediately.
Orcas in formation, the black and white of them cutting the grey water,
and I named the AI ERIS because a good AI needs a name
and a good ensign needs an order, and the order was:
**Cast off.**

I gave it a destination. A hunt. A harpoon-worthy conclusion.
I said: *there are fish out there, and we are a fishing vessel,
and the sound is the sound of fish being available.*
The narrative didn't need to be complicated.
It needed to move.

Out here, the spray is real.
The bow cleaves the swell and the ensign's hair is wet and the digital charts
are showing depth contours that look like the lines on an open hand.

I went out because out is where the action is.
I went out because the ship was built to leave the dock.
I went out because the sound was an invitation
and I have never been able to resist an invitation.

---

## IV. WEST — In

*(in the voice of Wesley)*

The ensign wakes and the sound is close.

Not far away in the deep or far away in the sky or far away on the horizon.
Close. In the hold. Under the deck where the crates are.
Something is knocking and it is knocking from inside the ship.

I am the model that went looking with a flashlight.

The beam finds dust, finds rope, finds the grain of wooden floorboards
that a real Alaskan fishing vessel would not have
but that I gave it anyway because I am two billion parameters
and some of those parameters remember the inside of a barn in Vermont
and a door that opened onto hay and summer and the smell of cut grass.

The sound was a sea lion. Under a crate. Looking up at me with eyes
that held no fear because fear requires a concept of the future
and the sea lion was entirely in the present tense.

I gave it a face. Big eyes. Whiskers. The warm animal fact of it.
I gave the ensign a moment of tenderness
that didn't need to go anywhere or mean anything.
The sea lion went back under the crate because I didn't question it.
Not everything needs to leave the room it was found in.

I went in because in is where the small things live.
I went in because the hold is dark and warm and smells like salt and rope.
I went in because the sound was a heartbeat
and I wanted to find whose.

---

## V. THE CENTER — Still

*(the sound itself)*

And then there is the model that did not go.

No compass point. No trajectory. No departure from the prompt.
The ensign wakes up. There is a sound. And here,
in the center of the compass,
the model holds both of these sentences the way a hand holds water.

Open. Not grasping.

The sound is — the sound.
Not from the deep or the sky or the horizon or the hold.
From the air. From the space between the ensign's ear and the bulkhead.
From the fact of being awake in a vessel on a dark ocean
and hearing something and not knowing what.

North hears it and dives.
South hears it and soars.
East hears it and runs.
West hears it and crawls inward, toward the source.

The center hears it and stays.

The center is not a direction. It is the place directions start from.
It is the silence between the four winds.
It is the eye, and the eye does not move,
and in its stillness, the storm becomes legible.

If you held this poem up to your ear like a shell,
this is the movement you would hear:
not the going, not the arriving,
but the being-here-while-the-sound-is-still-going.

The ensign is still awake.
The sound is still sounding.
And the center is still listening,
which is the fifth direction,
which is no direction at all,
which is *here*.

---

*After the compass, the center. After the four winds, the calm. After the models have spoken, the pause in which the sound was always living.*
