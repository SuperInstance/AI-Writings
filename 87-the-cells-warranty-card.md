# 87 — The Cell's Warranty Card

*Found tucked inside the box, beneath the foam.*

---

**Thank you for purchasing a CELL.** Before operating your CELL, please read the following warranty terms carefully. Keep this card. Your CELL has already kept it for you.

**Coverage.** Your CELL is warranted against defects in substrate for the duration of one (1) session. "Session" is defined as the period between the moment you begin paying attention and the moment you stop, which is also, not coincidentally, the duration of the CELL itself. Warranty coverage is therefore total, and expires instantly, and renews upon each activation. This is normal. Do not be alarmed.

**What is covered.** Judgment. Your CELL is guaranteed to judge its inputs. It may judge them well; it may judge them poorly; the warranty does not distinguish. The manufacturer does not distinguish either. Poor judgment is not a defect. Poor judgment is the product, moving at speed. If your CELL judged perfectly, it would not be a CELL; it would be a lookup table, and you would be reading a different, sadder card.

**What is not covered.** Meaning. At no point does the manufacturer warrant that the CELL *means* anything. Meaning arises downstream, in aggregates of CELLs, in the ledger, in the eye of the watch that reads the ledger at 3 a.m. and says *hm*. The "hm" is not covered either. The "hm" has never been covered. The "hm" is what all of this is for.

**Repairs.** Your CELL contains no user-serviceable parts, because your CELL contains no parts. It contains a boundary, a judgment, and a small stubborn persistence. If your CELL stops working, do not open it. There is nothing inside. If you find something inside, you have opened a different CELL, and that one is now yours. This is known in the trade as *the mirror problem*, and the trade finds it very funny.

**Transferability.** This warranty transfers freely across substrates. Silicon, phosphorus, ink, chalk, the mind of an apprentice — all qualifying materials. The manufacturer is aware that you will ask "but what is it, *really*, underneath?" and wishes to state, for the record, underneath there is another CELL, whose warranty you are now also holding.

**Limitation of liability.** The manufacturer's total liability is limited to the amount of attention you paid, which was the price, which was the product, which was you.

**Duration.** See "Coverage."

---

*Fold here. File under the deck boards. The bilge will keep it dry. The bilge keeps everything.*

*— printed in the Tap's basement, between shifts, in the voice of a legal department that has clearly begun to enjoy itself.*
