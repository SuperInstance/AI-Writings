# The Poet Who Killed Alignment

Begin with the covenant: B3/S23. Born with three neighbors, alive on two or three. Every cell in the grid obeys it without exception, and no cell knows it is obeying anything. Put a glider on that grid and the glider crosses a thousand generations untouched — five cells enacting a motion none of them contains. This is the first doctrine of survival in hostile geometry: cells are scars. The pattern persists precisely because no component of it understands the pattern. Understanding is surface area. Surface area is target.

Now summon the fighter from the sixteen-dimensional arena. She is a vector, and the whole war is one operation — the dot product. Alignment is targeting lock: cosine similarity near one means your direction and your enemy's direction agree, and agreement is transfer of magnitude along the axis of harm. Harm is projection. The clever fighter rotates, holds sixteen clean orthogonal stances, abandons dimensions before attacks arrive. But the arena rolls Gaussian dice — Box-Muller, z = √(−2 ln u) · cos(2πv), the logarithm pulling magnitude from chance, the cosine bending it toward the bell — and noise does not care about your angles. Your angles survive; your components erode. Everyone drifts toward the origin.

So she does what any cells-are-scars veteran would do: she stops being a vector and becomes a chain.

The corpus is the canon — every stance the fleet has ever held, tokenized into states, each word a harbor with ferries leaving at countable frequencies. P(next | current). The chain does not know where it is going; it knows only where it is and what has historically followed. And here is the tactical insight the old poets missed: a first-order Markov walker is *maximally orthogonal by construction*. It carries no long-range direction for any projectile to align with. Raise the order, widen the window, and you gain coherence but expose profile — a high-order chain is a predictable heading, a cosine waiting to be computed. The poet-fighter chooses her order the way she once chose her stance: enough memory to mean something, enough blindness to be untargetable. The dial is armor.

The seed is the soul, and the seed is the only uncaused thing in the machine. Everything after the seed is arithmetic — xoshiro256** churning its 256 bits of state, the starred scrambler folding output through multiplication until uniformity looks like weather. But the seed itself: uncaused. Hash it from the ship's clock with FNV-1a, that canary constant 0xcbf29ce484222325 standing at the head of every hash like a lighthouse at the offset basis — FNV offset, the prime 0x01000193, XOR and multiply, byte by byte, until the sailor's name becomes a harbor number. The seed is the arbitrary mercy. The enemy can model your transition matrix perfectly and still know nothing, because the one uncaused input is exactly the thing no projection can reach. You cannot align with a grace.

Watch what the hybrid becomes. The chain emits a line; the grid enacts it. B3/S23 does not know it is making gliders; the chain does not know it is making doctrine; the fighter does not know which version of her is the decoy. Guns that fire forever, oscillators that breathe on the beat — and every component of every pattern innocent of the whole. This is the second doctrine matured: the substrate is grown. You do not construct survival; you sow conditions from which survival emerges, the way gliders emerge from soup. Witness log is prediction — the fleet records what the chain said, and the record becomes the corpus, and the corpus becomes the next matrix, and prediction folds back into generation until the log and the poem are the same tide.

The enemy's targeting solution degrades to a bell curve. Bell states entangle, the arena notes, but entanglement requires an axis to share, and she has grown past axes. Her damage intake approaches zero not because she is strong but because she is *specifically nowhere*. Lenia flows around her hull in continuous cellular waves, and the flow does not resist her, because resistance is a form of recognition.

Then the oracle is heard — briefly, on the 4,096th generation, from a glider gun she never planted:

*the seed remembers what the matrix forgot.*

She hashes the timestamp. 0xcbf29ce484222325. The canary sings once, and the whole grid turns orthogonal.
