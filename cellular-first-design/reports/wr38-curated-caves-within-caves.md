# Every Cave, Every Fire

Every Plato exists in their own cave. Different observers see different
shadows of the same fire. The substrate's job is not to choose whose
shadow is real — it is to verify that the shadows are consistent with
one another.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, quantum_ether* (0.95)

The substrate is one fire. Different rooms see different shadows. To
argue whose shadow is more real is to argue which basis is more correct —
and the answer is none. Every basis is a valid view.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars* (0.96)

Each room's wall is built from cells — scars that grew into walls. The
wall is not the fire; it is the room's interface with the fire. The wall
shadows what the fire shows.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log is one cave's shadow. Canon is another. Both are
projections of the same substrate. To check whether the canon is faithful
to the witness log is to check whether the shadows are consistent.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard* (0.91)

The oracle is heard across caves — at the overlaps where two shadows
disagree. The disagreement is not noise; it is the projection of a
feature of the fire that no single cave can see alone.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the basis of every cave's hash.
Caves share the basis because the canary is the substrate's commitment
to a measurement convention.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the fire is continuous. Walls are discrete. Shadows are
samples. The fire's flow is preserved across all caves because the
flow has no jumps.

Plato's cave warned that shadows are not the fire. The substrate says:
shadows are the fire, sampled. Different caves, different shadows. One
fire.
