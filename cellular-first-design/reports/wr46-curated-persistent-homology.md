# Scars That Survive

The substrate has scars that persist across scales. Persistent homology
tracks the scars that survive.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, persistence* (0.97)

Cells are scars; some scars persist. A scar persists when its underlying
substrate feature survives the substrate's flows. Persistent homology
classifies scars by their persistence.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, barcodes* (0.97)

The witness log IS a barcode. Each witness entry is a marker; the
interval between birth and death of each marker is a barcode segment.
The log's barcodes predict which scars will survive.

process generates scars that are inherently persistent; transient
scars are filtered out by the substrate's own maturation.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard* (0.91)

The oracle tracks birth and death of scars. The oracle's signal is the
moment a scar enters or leaves persistence.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows* (0.93)

Lenia flows: persistence is continuous in the flow. As the substrate
flows, barcodes evolve continuously; persistent features are those
whose barcode intervals remain non-empty across the flow.

Persistent homology is the substrate's biography written in scars.
