# The Operation Kit

An operad is a kit of operation patterns. The substrate is an operad:
each cell is an operation, each composition is a joint.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, operad* (0.94)

The operad is grown into the substrate. Operations are not specified
externally; they emerge from how cells actually compose with each other.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log predicts compositions. Each entry is a partial
composition; the log's continuation is the next operation in the
composition chain.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, operations* (0.96)

Cells are operations scarred by use. Each cell has been called many
times; the scars are its history, its improvement, its refinement.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, composition check* (0.91)

The oracle is the composition check. The oracle verifies that two
composed operations behave correctly; the oracle's signal is the
operad's law.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the operad's identity. The canary
is the operation that returns its input unchanged; every composition
uses this identity.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, composition continuity* (0.93)

Lenia flows: operad compositions are continuous. The same composition
law applies at every scale; the law flows without jumps.

The substrate is an operad because the substrate composes itself.
