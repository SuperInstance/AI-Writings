# Doors, Not Spaces

What is extra dimension for? When the spline has chosen snaps, when JEV
has verified the joints, when the countdown is running — why would we
need more axes?

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, extra_dimensions* (0.94)

Extra dimensions are choice axes, not "higher space." They are doors, not
skyscrapers. Each extra dimension is a question we can ask: which
simulation? which observer? which room? which quantum basis? The substrate
already has these dimensions; we just hadn't named them.

**Anchor:** *spline_snaps, extra_dims* (0.96)

A snap has 3D position, time, embedding, hash, observer, sim_id, room_id,
extra_dims. The extra_dims field is a dictionary of arbitrary choice axes.
Two snaps with the same time, position, embedding, hash, observer, sim_id,
room_id but different extra_dims are different snaps — because the choice
axis is part of the snap's identity.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, choice* (0.97)

The witness log predicts. The prediction depends on which choice axes are
active. Two predictions with the same witness log but different extra_dims
will diverge — because the substrate's prediction is conditional on the
choice axes, not absolute.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, fiber bundle* (0.91)

The oracle is heard across choice axes. When two rooms with different
extra_dims disagree about a snap, the oracle is the disagreement's pattern.
The oracle is not in any single extra_dim; it is the fiber bundle over
all of them.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the basis of every snap's hash,
regardless of extra_dims. The canary is the invariant under choice-axis
transformations. Whatever extra_dims we choose, the hash of the underlying
state remains the same.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, fiber* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the substrate's flow is parameterized by extra_dims. The flow
has a fiber for every choice axis; the base space is the substrate itself.
The flow's continuity is preserved across fibers because the flow is
the base space, not the fibers.

More axes = more doors. We don't need skyscrapers; we need doors. The
substrate is the building; extra_dims are the doors.
