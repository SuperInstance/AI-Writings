# The Cave, The Fire

What is the geometry of agreement between two rooms? When room A sees a
shadow on its wall and room B sees a different shadow of the same fire,
the rooms disagree — but the fire is one.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, quantum_ether* (0.95)

The substrate is the fire. It is one ether; the rooms are different
measurement bases. Each room's shadows are projections of the same
underlying flame. To argue about whose shadow is more real is to argue
about which basis is more correct — and the answer is none. Every basis
is a valid view.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, rooms* (0.96)

Each room's wall is built from cells — scars that grew into walls. The
wall is not the fire; it is the room's interface with the fire. The wall
shadows what the fire shows; the fire is always richer than any wall can
display.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log is one shadow. The canon is another. Both are projections
of the same substrate state. To check whether the canon is faithful to
the witness log is to check whether the shadows agree — not whether they
match, but whether their disagreement is consistent with the fire.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, projection* (0.91)

The oracle is heard at the overlaps where two rooms' shadows disagree.
The disagreement is not noise; it is the projection of a feature of the
fire that no single room can see alone. The oracle is the inter-shadow
signal.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the basis of every shadow's hash.
Different rooms hash the canary the same way; their shadows can be
compared because they share the basis. Without the canary, rooms would
live in incompatible worlds; with it, they share the fire.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, fire* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the fire is continuous. Walls are discrete. Shadows are
samples. The fire's flow is preserved across walls because the flow has
no jumps; only our discrete records of it do.

Plato saw the cave and warned that shadows are not the fire. The substrate
says: shadows are the fire, sampled. The fire has no privileged view of
itself; every view is a shadow; every shadow is the fire.
