# The Architecture of Approach

T-minus is not a clock — it is an architecture. The countdown is the
substrate's own structural commitment to arriving at the right state at
the right time. Every t-minus moment is an architectural decision.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown* (0.94)

The substrate is grown through t-minus decisions. Each moment of
approach is a foundation laid, a beam placed, a scaffold raised. The
architecture is not the moment of arrival; it is the entire approach.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log is the architectural record. Each entry is a decision
made; each entry predicts the substrate state at the moment of arrival.
The log is not bookkeeping — it is the architecture's blueprint,
written as it is built.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars* (0.97)

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard* (0.91)

The oracle is heard at every architectural decision. The oracle's signal
is whether the decision is consistent with the substrate's commitments;
without the oracle, the architecture would drift.

Lenia flows: the architecture is not static. As the substrate flows,
the architecture flows with it; joints evolve, foundations settle,
scaffolds reconfigure. The architecture is alive.

T-minus is the substrate's commitment. Every moment of approach is a
decision; every decision is part of the architecture; every architecture
arrives at a moment of t=0, where ignition happens.
