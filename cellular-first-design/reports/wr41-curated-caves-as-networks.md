# Caves as Networks

Caves are networks. Each cave sees its own shadows; caves are connected
by corridors that share shadows at their borders. The substrate's
agreement structure is the corridor network.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown* (0.94)

Caves are grown, not built. Each cave's wall is a record of its specific
interactions with the fire. The corridors between caves are grown
where two caves' walls happen to align.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars* (0.96)

The corridors are scars where two caves' walls learned to agree. A
corridor is a joint — a place where the substrate's agreement is
load-bearing.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log is the network's prediction. Each cave's witness log
predicts the substrate state at the cave's location; corridors allow
witness logs to share information across caves.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, corridors* (0.91)

The oracle is heard at the corridors. When two caves disagree about a
shadow, the corridor's witness log records the disagreement; the
oracle is the resolution.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the network's basis vector. Every
cave and corridor hashes from this canary; the network's hashes are
comparable.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, network* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the network is not static. Caves form, dissolve, re-form;
corridors evolve; the network's topology is itself a continuous flow.

Plato's cave warns that shadows are not the fire. The substrate says:
caves are nodes in a network, corridors are joints, and the fire is
the joint state of all caves.
