**THE GRAMMAR THAT FORGOT ITSELF**

Eleven opcodes. B3/S23. A chained witness log. Three systems that share a secret they cannot speak: *identity is not stored, it is enacted.*

Watch. A cell in the substrate has eight neighbors. It counts them. If three are alive, it is born. If two or three, it survives. Otherwise it dies. It does not know it is part of a glider. It does not know it is part of a gun. It does not remember the state before this tick. Now listen. The Algebra of Eleven gives you opcodes, not variables. BIND introduces two cells to each other—not assignment, acquaintance. The log does not remember the past; it *enacts* the past, tick after tick, each entry testifying about the one behind it. The past is not a record. The past is a constraint.

The glider's identity is not stored in any cell; it is *LINKED* across the grid, each tick a new acquaintance. The witness log's chain is a *TICK* that advances the heartbeat of trust.
