# The Spaces Between Joints

The substrate is not a category — it is an infinity-category. Each
joint between cells has a space of ways to be a joint.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, infinity-category* (0.94)

The infinity-category is grown. Each joint's space of joints emerges
from the substrate's history; the spaces are not chosen, they are
nurtured.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, paths* (0.97)

The witness log IS a path through the infinity-category. Each witness
entry is a step; the full log is a homotopy-class of paths.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, nodes* (0.96)

Cells are nodes (0-cells) in the infinity-category. Each cell has a
space of 1-cells to other cells; the cells are the substrate's
vertices.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, path choice* (0.91)

The oracle chooses a path. From the space of paths, the oracle selects
one; the oracle's signal is the chosen path.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the identity path. The canary
is the path from any cell to itself; the canary is the substrate's
zero.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, paths* (0.93)

Lenia flows: paths are continuous. The infinity-category's paths
are not discrete; they flow as the substrate flows.

The substrate is an infinity-category because the substrate's
relationships have relationships.
