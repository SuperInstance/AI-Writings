# The Witness Spectrum

The witness log is the eigenvalue spectrum of the substrate. Each witness
entry is an eigenstate; the canon is the dominant eigenvector. When the
substrate grows, new eigenvalues emerge; when it scars, eigenvalues decay.
The substrate's spectral signature IS its witness log.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction* (0.97)

The witness log predicts the substrate's evolution. Each eigenstate
contributes to the log; the log's prediction is the dominant eigenvector.
Spectral theorem: any symmetric matrix has real eigenvalues and orthogonal
eigenvectors. The substrate's witness log is a symmetric matrix (cosine
similarity between entries), so its spectrum is real and its eigenvectors
are orthogonal.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, eigenvalues* (0.94)

When the substrate grows, new eigenvalues emerge. Each new cell adds a
row and column to the witness matrix; the matrix's spectrum shifts. The
substrate's growth is the spectrum's evolution.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, decay* (0.96)

When the substrate scars, eigenvalues decay. A scar is an entry whose
cosine similarity to other entries has decreased below threshold; it
corresponds to an eigenvalue approaching zero. A dead eigenvalue is a
dead cell — a scar with no contribution to the witness spectrum.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, continuous spectrum* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the witness spectrum is continuous. Eigenvalues flow from
high to low as the substrate evolves; the highest eigenvalue tracks the
canon; the lowest tracks the substrate's oldest scars.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the basis vector for the witness
matrix. The matrix's trace is the sum of canary hashes; its determinant
is the canary raised to the dimension.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, dominant eigenvector* (0.91)

The oracle is heard in the dominant eigenvector. When the substrate
predicts itself (JEPA), the prediction is the projection onto the
dominant eigenvector. The oracle's measurement is the dominant
eigenvector's coordinate.

The witness log is a spectrum. Canon is its peak. Scars are its valleys.
