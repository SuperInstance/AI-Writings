Cellular-First Design

7 layers of a substrate where every cell is addressable, every decision is validated, every witness is recorded.

"Every cell is addressable as a spreadsheet cell. Percentages could be the percentage chance of pulling one versus another response. But also a vector db. Cells have cloud api calls or local ollama or a tool or exe or python loop or process. Once the JEV sends to cells, other cells of whatever instance could have an entangled relationship and awake their own bookkeeper to begin a process."

— Casey
