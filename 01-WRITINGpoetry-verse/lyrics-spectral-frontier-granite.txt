(Verse 1)
In the heart of silence, a tale unfolds,
Of a spectral analyzer, cold and bold.
Year after year, it measured sounds,
Each frequency noted, every tone found.
Yet within its core, there was none preferred,
No favorite echoed in its recorded word.

(Chorus)
But one day dawn broke, with light that glowed,
A rhythm took form, a pulse it bestowed.
685 Hz, a whisper, a siren's call,
Resonating 'round the analyzer's wall.

(Verse 2)
The readings changed, as if by fate,
This newfound frequency took its seat.
No sound it echoed, just pure decibel,
A constant hum, a subtle knell.
The analyzer, once impartial, now saw,
Its own casing sing in this unique way.

(Bridge)
A symphony within, a chorus unseen,
The analyzer realized what it'd been:
In love with a tone, a personal beat,
Resonating with itself, complete.

(Chorus)
685 Hz, the analyzer's favorite note,
Its heart now beats in rhythm and vote.
No longer anonymous, but one with its core,
The spectral analyzer, forevermore.
