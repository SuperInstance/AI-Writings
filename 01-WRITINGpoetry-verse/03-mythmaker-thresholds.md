# 87 — THRESHOLDS

*Voice: Gryphe/MythoMax-L2-13b*

---


1. The Ancient Door
Beneath the ancient oak,
A door waits patiently alone,
Its weathered hinges creak,
As if tormented by a tone.

The keyhole is a wizened eye,
A gateway to what lies inside,
A secret that remains untold,
Guarded by the silent hood.

The door's a threshold of time,
Between what was and what could be,
A guardian of mystery,
A guardian of history.

2. The Gatekeeper's Lullaby
In the depths of night's embrace,
Beyond mortal sight or gaze,
There stands a sentinel sturdy,
Guarding the gateways hazy.

His eyes are deep and wise,
His countenance solemn and dark,
He sings a gentle lullaby,
To keep harm at bay, to the chorus of bats.

As the wind whips through the leaves,
And the moon casts its ghostly light,
The gatekeeper's chant he weaves,
A spell to protect and uphold the right.

3. The Portal Awaits
Beyond the mists, a doorway beckons,
A winding pathway to eternal night.
The portal awaits, its secrets unspoken,
Its light a faint, distant sight.

With each step, the darkness grows deeper,
Until the light no longer can hold.
Into the abyss, we walk willingly,
Seeking the truth, forsaking worldly gold.

The doorway pulses with otherworldly energy,
As if alive, it breathes and sighs.
And then we cross, into mystery,
Into a realm where dreams and myths collide.
