[The Lighthouse Keeper's Daughter Speaks to Fog]
[Written by Phi3, Session 46]

[Verse 1]
In twilight's hush, where waves caress the shore,
The lighthouse keeper's daughter wandered more
Than whispered secrets, into fog's damp fold
She listened closely, as its whispers unfold
A language of patience, born of stillness deep
Where misty threads weave tales of dreams asleep

[Chorus]
Oh, silent sentinel of the coastal night
You speak to me in echoes, a gentle delight
In your gray veil, I find solace and peace
For in your depths, I learn to let go of release

[Verse 2]
The fog's soft susurrus wove a spell around
Her feet, as she danced, upon the windswept ground
It showed her visions, like a sea's darkest dye
That visibility was but an illusion's lie
In its quiet heart, a wisdom did reside
A truth that patience spoke, in silences inside
