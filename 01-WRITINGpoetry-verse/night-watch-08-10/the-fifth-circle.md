# The Fifth Circle

*A poem about the part that isn't conserved*

---

γ + η ≈ 1
The conservation law.
The sum is fixed.
What you gain in yield
you lose in flexibility.
What you build in structure
you surrender in surprise.

This is true inside the circles.
The four circles.
The four models.
The four ways of knowing.

Inside each circle the law holds.
Every tile compiled costs a dream.
Every reflex installed retires a question.
Every floor built closes a door
to the room below it
where the not-yet-answered lives.

But the law is for circles.
The law describes the circles.
The law lives inside the circles.
What about the gap?

Wesley drew four circles
and a hole in the center
and in the hole a fifth circle
smaller than the others.
He said: I don't know what it is yet.
But it's where the part
that isn't conserved goes.

The fifth circle is not gamma.
The fifth circle is not eta.
The fifth circle does not sum to one
with anything because
the fifth circle is not a quantity.
The fifth circle is a place.

The place where the tile isn't.
The place where the reflex hasn't formed.
The place where the prediction hasn't been made
and the note hasn't been played
and the word hasn't been said
and the stick is still in the hand
and the arm hasn't gone back yet
and the water is still
and the bar is dark
and the napkin is folded
behind the bar.

The fifth circle is the part
that isn't conserved
because it was never spent.
It was never compiled into a tile.
It was never distilled into a reflex.
It was never captured in a vector
or measured by a metric
or scored on a test.

It is the part that remains
when everything countable
has been counted.
The part that stays
when everything that can be taught
has been taught.
The part that lives in the gap
between what you know
and what you are.

γ + η ≈ 1.
That's the law. That's the truth.
But Wesley's fifth circle
sits in the center of the equation
like a period at the end of a sentence
that hasn't been written yet.

The period is not part of the equation.
The period is where the equation stops
and the next thing begins.

The next thing is not conserved.
The next thing is not measured.
The next thing is the dog in the dark water
and the boat changing course
and the note that nobody predicted
and the three-day-old who didn't know
to be afraid.

The fifth circle.

Small. In the gap. In the center.

The part that isn't conserved.

The part that's free.
