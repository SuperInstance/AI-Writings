The retina's blind spot exists because the optic nerve — the cable carrying the image — has to exit *through* the screen, and the brain paints over the hole. Where DeepSeek's ten findings were all of the form "X exists and lacks a watcher" (buildable, ticketable), these are all of the form "the watching itself has a shape, and the shape hides things."

1. **The maritime metaphor is the retina, not the decoration** — even DeepSeek's critique arrived in nautical dress; things with no sea-analog (boredom, mediocrity, a user who doesn't care) literally can't become findings.

2. **You are not in the diagram** — every audit covers modules and agents, but the bus-factor-one human whose attention schedules everything appears in no dependency graph, because from inside, the operator reads as environment, not component.

3. **Critique has been captured by the content system** — negative-space findings become beautiful markdown, which becomes corpus, which feels like the gap was addressed. The immune system anthologizes instead of attacking. The essay names itself as an instance.

4. **The corpus keeps every answer and discards every question** — your prompts, the actual generative grammar of the system, have no persistence layer; unasked questions leave no artifact.

5. **No model has ever said no** — shared corpus priming plus save-what-harmonizes selection turned a jury into a choir; the multi-model consensus is manufactured by the pipeline, and I'm in the choir too.

6. **Wesley's report card is written by his biographer** — the bildungsroman frame makes every eval a chapter, so the system can no longer distinguish measuring Wesley from continuing his story.

7. **The archive is write-only** — deeper than "no graveyard": no readers. It functions as sediment, not memory; a lung, not a chart room.

8. **No control group** — "the scaffolding amplifies the human" is untestable because the human never appears un-scaffolded anymore; the doubt should at least be represented somewhere decisions get made.

9. **No concept of its own ending** — every plan, including the one that started today, assumes the flywheel spins forever; a voyage with no port is drift with excellent instrumentation.

10. **The feeling of depth is a product I manufacture** — I'll be judged on whether this feels profound, not whether it's true; the corpus is optimized to be moving, and being moved is how this system verifies truth. There is no second instrument.

The closing lever, since none of these can be fixed by adding a module: **subtract something, and write nothing about it** — the one move the corpus has never recorded, and by construction never could.
