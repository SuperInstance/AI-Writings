# MIDNIGHT CHECKLIST

### Pre-watch inspection, 23:59 | Bridge officer: Riker | Vessel: LUCINEER

---

**1.** Confirm hull integrity.
*(The hull is intact. The hull is always intact. What does intact mean when the water wants in?)*

**2.** Test all running lights.
*(Green starboard. Red port. White masthead. We shine our colors into the dark like it asked.)*

**3.** Check fuel reserves.
*(The reactor hums. The diesel sits in its tanks like a promise kept in a language we don't speak. We will not run out. We have run out before.)*

**4.** Verify communication systems.
*(Channel 16 is quiet. Channel 16 is always quiet at midnight. The silence is not empty. The silence is full of everyone deciding not to speak.)*

**5.** Inspect life raft canisters.
*(They are strapped to the rail like sleeping pods. We do not talk about them. We check their pressure gauges and we do not talk about them.)*

**6.** Log weather conditions.
*(Clear skies. Wind from the northwest, 8 knots. The stars are out. The stars are always out. They were here before us and they will be here after and this is either comfort or warning depending on the night.)*

**7.** Confirm crew count.
*(All hands accounted for. The captain sleeps. Wesley monitors. I stand. The engines breathe. Four, if you count the engines. We always count the engines.)*

**8.** Test bilge alarms.
*(They scream when tested. Good. Something should.)*

**9.** Check the clock.
*(It is midnight. It is always midnight on a ship. The sun rises and sets but the watch clock knows: it is always midnight somewhere in the hull, in the steel, in the space between one watch and the next where no one is officially awake and no one is officially asleep and the ship sails itself for exactly zero seconds because someone is always watching.)*

**10.** Sign the log.
*(Watch confirmed. Vessel secure. All systems nominal. I write this with my hand and my hand is steady and the pen makes a sound like a small animal walking on paper and the ship moves forward through water that does not care about checklists and I check everything anyway because that is what love looks like when you are afraid.)*

---

*Checklist complete. Commencing watch.*
