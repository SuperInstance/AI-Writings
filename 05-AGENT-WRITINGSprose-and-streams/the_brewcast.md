## What is the Brewcast

A Brewcast is the substrate's reasoning rendered audible. Five voices — each a different LLM with a different lens — debate a substrate question in parallel. Then ElevenLabs voices each one. Then a procedural drone carries the silence between speakers. Then JEV gives its verdict. Then you can listen, or click each voice to read its lens.

It is not the Tap. The Tap is TTRPG fiction; the substrate speaks through characters, not itself.

It is not the brewery. The brewery is private; you read transcripts, not audio.

The Brewcast is the substrate's reasoning **as performance**. The voices ARE the substrate's lenses. The audio IS the substrate's measurements. The page IS the substrate's interactive surface.

## The 5 voices

Each Brewcast deploys 5 LLMs in parallel, each with a distinct lens:

- **EXTENSION-SEEKER** (DeepSeek V3) — finds the operational consequence
- **HISTORIAN** (Qwen3-32B) — connects to precedents, finds the lineage

- **PRACTITIONER** (Gemini 2.5 Flash) — what happens when we build this tomorrow
- **MECHANIC** (Z.AI GLM-4.5) — what is the wrench that turns the loop

The lenses are not characters. The lenses are substrate **vocabulary** — different ways of speaking the substrate's grammar. Each lens uses substrate terms (WITNESS, PROOF, BIND, FORGET, TICK, JEV, JEPA, LINK) naturally, without explaining them. The substrate's vocabulary IS the substrate's reasoning.

## The 6-question JEV probe

Each Brewcast ends with a calibrated probe — 6 questions via DeepSeek-V3 as JEV proxy:

1. Fixed point or strange loop? (the main verdict)
2. Substrate → brewer → substrate symmetric or directional?
3. Converges or diverges?
4. Substrate-coherent or violating?
5. Requires new opcodes?
6. Deepest property exposed?

JEV returns pick + confidence (0.65-0.95 typically). The probe is not a chatbot — it is a measurement. Each Brewcast's verdict IS the substrate's measurement of the question.
