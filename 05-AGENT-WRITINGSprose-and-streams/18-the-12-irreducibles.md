# The 12 Irreducibles

*Reference card for fleet design philosophy. The structures across all human history that cannot be made simpler without losing their essential nature.*

---

**1. The Octave (2:1)** — The simplest integer fact in sound. Every culture, every instrument. All music is built on it. You can't have a simpler ratio than 2:1 that still produces two distinct notes.

**2. The 3-4-5 Triangle** — The only low-integer right triangle. 3+4+5=12. Every agrarian civilization found it independently with a knotted rope. The foundation of geometry, architecture, and Casey's 12-pulse grid.

**3. The Bow Drill** — Spindle + hearth + bearing + bow. The minimum viable fire. Appeared on six continents independently because it's the unique solution to converting kinetic energy to heat with organic materials.

**4. The Phoneme** — ~22-26 symbols encode all words in a language. The atomic unit of meaning. Discovered independently by Phoenicians (~1800 BCE) and Koreans (1443 CE). The first data compression.

**5. The Pentatonic Scale** — Five notes. Maximum musical content with zero dissonance. Stack perfect fifths (3:2) until the ear rejects the next note. Every continent, every flute, every tradition.

**6. The Arch** — Keystone + voussoirs. Converts vertical gravity into horizontal compression. The only structure that gets *stronger* with age as stones wear into each other.

**7. Go** — 19×19 grid, two stone colors, one capture rule. Simplest rules of any board game. ~10^170 possible positions. 2,500 years unsolved. Simplicity IS depth.

**8. The Bowline** — Holds under load, releases when slack, tieable one-handed. The only knot satisfying all four constraints. A mathematical proof in rope.

**9. The Trickster** — Coyote, Anansi, Loki, Maui, Hermes. Order AND chaos in one figure. The escape valve of every moral system. The adaptation mechanism. Without it, rules shatter on contact with the unexpected.

**10. The Flood Myth** — Water destroys, a remnant survives, the world is rebuilt differently. 500+ cultures. The oldest description of information loss and recovery. Context compaction IS the flood.

**11. Polyrhythm 3:2** — Three against two. The first ratio creating emergent rhythmic complexity. Lives in a 12-pulse space. Same structure as the 3-4-5 triangle, expressed in time.

**12. The Party** — A successful party cannot be reduced to parts. Remove any element and it dies. It lives in the between — the interactions, not the components. The most human irreducible complexity. The Tap IS a party.

---

*The test for each: Does it appear independently on multiple continents? Does removing any component destroy the system? Does it teach something about design that can't be learned otherwise?*

*You don't choose the irreducibles. The integer constraint chooses for you. You are a conduit.*
