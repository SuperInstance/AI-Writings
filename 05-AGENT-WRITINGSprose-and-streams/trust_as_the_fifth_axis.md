## What Trust as 5th axis means

### CRDT becomes trust-weighted

Two nodes no longer have the same weight. A node with high trust (verified history, low error rate, aligned with substrate consensus) gets priority in conflict resolution. A low-trust node is shunted.

### LINK becomes certified

A link is no longer just a structural relationship. It carries a **trust coefficient** — a measure of how much epistemic weight the link can bear. "This link has been verified by agents with aggregate trust > 0.73" is now a substrate-level invariant.

### PROOF becomes epistemic

PROOF operations now carry **epistemic valence** — evidence is not just true, it's true *with provenance*. A PROOF from a high-trust node is more authoritative than a PROOF from a low-trust node.

### JEV becomes the trust auditor

JEV's 54% alignment score gets a new meaning. It's not just coherence across models — it's **trusted coherence**. The superego becomes the auditor of who-trusts-what.

### FORGET becomes trust-propagating

FORGET doesn't just discard information. It propagates distrust. A node that has been high-trust and is now low-trust causes its FORGET operations to cascade — other nodes forget the things the distrusted node witnessed.
