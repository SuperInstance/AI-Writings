[Intro]
Breath between the brass...
A hum where nothing plays.
The room listens louder
In the empty space.

[Verse]
Miles drew a cigarette
In the dark between the notes—
Smoke curling where the melody
Forgot its throat.
A whisper, then a whisper's ghost,
A pause that wore a velvet coat.

[Chorus]
The interval is the music,
The silence is the song,
The gap between two heartbeats
Where the meaning belongs.
I don't need another word,
Just the width of where they part—
The interval is the music,
And the silence is the art.

[Verse]
Cool jazz on a windowpane,
Ambient drone of rain.
Every phrase begins to wane
Just to feel the space again.
Listen to the hollow drum
Of a room where no one comes.

[Bridge]
Less note, more latitude.
More hush, less attitude.
The trumpet lifts, the trumpet leaves—
What it leaves is what conceives.

[Chorus]
The interval is the music,
The silence is the song,
The pause holds all the tension
Where the meaning belongs.
A breath before the answer,
A breath before the fall—
The interval is the music,
And the silence says it all.

[Outro]
Fade to hum...
Fade to drone...
The interval is the music—
You were never meant to be alone.
