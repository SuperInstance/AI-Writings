# Purpose: Systematic protocol for a human listener to explore 366 AI-generated tracks

## Session Structure
- 53 listening sessions
- 7 tracks per session (~1.5 hours including breaks)
- 5-minute breaks between groups
- Total: ~6.5 hours per week
- Full corpus: ~52 sessions (approximately 7.5 weeks at 1 session/week)

## Categorization Framework
Group tracks by hybrid approach:
1. **Genre** — Electronic, Classical, Jazz, Impossible Genre, Cover Chain
2. **Mood** — Calm, Energetic, Nostalgic, Eerie, Melancholic
3. **Instrumentation** — Synth-only, Orchestral, Hybrid, Found Sound
4. **Structure** — Repetitive pattern, Gradual build, Through-composed, Sectional

## Rating System (1-5 scale)
| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Creativity | How innovative or unique is the composition? |
| Emotional Resonance | How well does the music evoke emotions? |
| Technical Craftsmanship | Production quality, arrangement, execution |
| Originality | How distinctive and memorable is the sound? |
| Engagement | How effectively does the music hold attention? |

## Special Protocols

### Impossible Genres
- Label as "Genre X" when the track defies categorization
- Note the specific genre fusion attempted
- Rate whether the fusion produces something genuinely novel vs. just chaotic
- Record specific spectral impressions (brightness, density, texture)

### Cover Chains
- Listen to covers in sequence (original → cover 1 → cover 2 → ...)
- Track how the sonic identity shifts with each generation
- Note whether degradation is creative (new sounds emerge) or destructive (quality drops)
- Compare RMS and ZCR impressions with measured values from spectral analysis

### Temperature Comparisons
- Listen to same-concept tracks at different temperatures sequentially
- Note emotional shifts between temperature variants
- Record which temperature feels most "musically complete"
- Compare felt differences with measured spectral differences

## Additional Recommendations
1. Listen to each track at least twice before rating
2. Take breaks between sessions to avoid ear fatigue
3. Keep a listening journal alongside the rating sheet
4. Note surprising tracks — the ones that don't sound AI-generated
5. Pay attention to production artifacts (glitches, unnatural transitions, phase issues)
6. For the first listen, don't look at the genre label — guess it, then compare
