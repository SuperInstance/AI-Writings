[Verse 1]
He stands at the front with nothing in his hands
No reed, no bow, no strings, no valves
The stage is dark, the pit is warm
He lifts one arm and the room transforms

[Chorus]
The conductor has no instrument
His body is the silence between
Every note the ensemble plays
Lives in the space he creates

[Verse 2]
The oboist tightens, the cellist waits
The timpani hovers at the edge
He hasn't spoken, hasn't played
But every musician has begun

[Bridge]
Three jobs: the onset, the shape, the release
The downbeat, the sustain, the farewell
Everything between belongs to the players
Everything between belongs to the sound

[Outro]
He puts his hands down
The last note hangs
It was never his to hold
