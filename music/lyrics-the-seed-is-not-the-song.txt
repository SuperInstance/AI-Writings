[Verse 1]
I pressed them down on the same spring day,
Side by side in the red clay,
Two small prayers in a garden row,
Same dark soil, same sun's glow.
I gave them equal water, equal light,
Whispered the same soft goodnight,
Yet something in the earth below
Had a different tale to show.

[Chorus]
Oh, the mystery of the growing thing,
Two seeds, two hearts, two bells that ring,
You can tend the soil, count the rain,
But the roots remember a different name.
We bloom where no map drew the line—
Irreproducible, alive, mine.

[Verse 2]
One climbed straight like a prayer to sky,
The other sprawled in a low, sweet arc.
I checked the inches, I checked the pH,
Read the books till the words turned grey—
The variables I thought I knew
Were hiding in the morning dew.

[Bridge]
Maybe the wind that passed unseen
Carried a spore, a dream, a gene,
Maybe the stone beneath the clay
Tilted one leaf a different way.
Nothing repeats, nothing returns,
Everything burns, everything learns.

[Outro]
So I'll stand and watch them, two by two,
And let the mystery garden through.
