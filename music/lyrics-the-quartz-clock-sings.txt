[Verse 1]
I count in quartz — thirty-two thousand
seven hundred sixty-eight vibrations per second,
each one too small to hear,
each one too precise to fear.

[Chorus]
But the oscillation accumulates,
the way a river accumulates a valley,
and what was silence becomes a song
that only patience can unravel.

[Verse 2]
The piezoelectric crystal flexes
between compression and release —
a heart that beats in megahertz,
a pulse that time released.

[Bridge]
Tune me to your frequency,
I'll cut the quartz to match your need.
Every clock a tiny choir,
every tick a tiny creed.

[Outro]
Thirty-two thousand seven hundred sixty-eight.
The number the ear cannot translate.
But the music knows the ratio.
The music always knows the ratio.
