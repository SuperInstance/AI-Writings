Oh, the harbor holds its breath tonight,
One low note bleeding through the dark and light.

It's the D below the deep that rattles up the pier.

[Bridge]
Bittersweet the air, heavy as a hymn,
The bass man plays the note and the ocean plays with him.
I could quit this rock, I could walk the shore,
But the note keeps calling me to give one more.

[Outro]
So I stand in the tower with the coffee gone cold,
Humming low with the horn, humming low with the gold.
When the foghorn and the bass sing the selfsame sound,
A lighthouse keeper's just another soul unbound.
