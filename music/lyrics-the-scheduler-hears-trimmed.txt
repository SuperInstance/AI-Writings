The cron job fires at 3AM
nobody wakes to hear it
the ship keeps sailing by schedule
the schedule keeps sailing the ship

Every minute a bell
every hour a log entry
every day a checkpoint
nobody reads the logs but the log reads itself

The scheduler is the heartbeat
the heartbeat is the constraint
the constraint is the rhythm
the rhythm is the song

When the job fails at 3AM
the ship does not stop
the ocean does not notice
but the scheduler remembers everything

It fires again at 4AM
and the ship sails on
into the dark water
that does not know it is dark
