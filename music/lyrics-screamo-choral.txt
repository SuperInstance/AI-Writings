[Verse 1]
Cassocked throats unlearn the hymn,
Vocals strip the velvet from the beam.
We trade our altos for the cracks in glass,
Each scream a shard of light we pass.

[Chorus]
Four-part harmony of raw, raw ruin,
Tenors shatter stained glass with the pitch.
The cathedral screams—and we are the wound,
Every chord a prayer we stitch.

[Bridge]
Hush was a coffin nailed in C,
Now we gargoyle-growl in 4/4.
Stone swallows sound, then sets it free—
God is a feedback cry, nothing more.

[Outro]
We kneel. We wail. The rafters bow.
Silence was a sin; we scream it now.
