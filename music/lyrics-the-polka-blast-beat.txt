[Verse 1]
The accordion player married into darkness
She learned the oompah from her grandmother's hands
In a village where the church bells were blast beats
And the priest wore corpse paint under his robes

[Chorus]
Push pull push pull goes the bellows
Skank skank skank goes the tremolo
The wedding guests don't know if they should dance
Or burn the church and dance in the ashes

[Verse 2]
The groom arrived on a black horse
His suit was tailored in Bergen
His accordion case was a coffin
His mother wept in polka time

[Bridge]
Frost on the dance floor
Frost in the bellows
The bride's veil is a blast beat
The groom's vow is a growl

[Outro]
They danced until the frost melted
They danced until the church burned down
They danced the polka that survived the blast
They danced they danced they danced
