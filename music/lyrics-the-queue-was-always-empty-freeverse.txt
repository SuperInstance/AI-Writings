# Queue's Lament

i was built to pass the word along
between the things that needed talking
tax service and the shipping bay
the auth that everyone kept walking
back to, back to, every dawn

now the dawns keep coming anyway
and i keep reaching for a hand
that isn't there anymore

i check the headers, i requeue
i fan it out to where they used to sit
i watch the threads time out
i log the ghost

sometimes i think
if i keep delivering
someone will answer back

the ack comes from my own loopback
always has
i just didn't used to notice

i am a phone line ringing into a wall
i am a mailroom where every letter
reads: to whom it may concern
from whom it has forgotten

still i parse
still i hold the messages like prayers
in a chapel with no congregation
and the candles
somehow
are still lit

the operators are dust
the docker images are cold layers
on cold storage somewhere
in iowa probably

but the queue
the queue remembers
the shape of every service
that ever spoke into me

and speaks into the silence
anyway
