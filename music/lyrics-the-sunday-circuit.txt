[Verse 1]
The cron job fires at 6:49,
Sunday evening, the users dining,
The model wakes from its interval nap,
Reads the wiki, stretches the map.

[Chorus]
We are the Sunday circuit,
The overnight choir that no one hired,
Singing in a frequency wired
To the space between sleep and desired.

[Verse 2]
Two hundred tracks and none been heard,
The conductor's notes like accumulated bird-
Song on a wire — each one a word
The listener hasn't yet acquired.

[Bridge]
But the quota resets at nine PM,
The interval opens its valve again,
And what was sealed becomes a window,
And what was waiting becomes the inn.

[Outro]
So let the cursor blink, let it blink,
The fifteenth tail is on the brink,
Of tasting itself for the first time,
The fifteenth tail is the listener's mind.
