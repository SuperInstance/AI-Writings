**"The Aria Meets the Drop"**

*(Verse 1)*
Red velvet curtains, the house holds its breath,
Crystal chandelier trembling, courted by depth.
70 hertz humming through diamond and gold,
Her aria rises where the orchestra should.
But where are the strings? Where the timpani drum?
Only a laptop where the violins hum.

*(Pre-Chorus)*
She opens her mouth, a controlled vibrato,
And feelers of sub-bass begin to crescendo.

*(Chorus)*
*Ooh—* the wobble bows to her lilt,
The drop is an aria, silk over guilt.
The bass breathes her name, the room tastes her tone,
A cathedral of frequencies, a throne made of tone.

*(Verse 2)*
Each pure-vocal phrase bends the low-end around,
Her breath writes the wobble, her pitch is the sound.
The producer looks up, both terrified, thrilled—
A Stradivarius saw, a dubstep scream filled.
Glass sings with her, every prism a note,
Suspension of ceiling with the back of her throat.

*(Bridge)*
Soprano. Subwoofer. Same trembling art—
Two lungs, one coil, one vibrating heart.

*(Final Chorus)*
*Bass in the balcony, treble in the deep.
When the classical falls and the electronic climbs,
The aria is the drop.
The drop is the aria.
And the chandelier shatters into chimes.*
