[Verse 1]
Forty-one nights with my temple to the wood,
counting the bass through the grain,
352 songs I was almost allowed—
but the door kept the rest of the refrain.

[Chorus]
And today the latch gives way,
today the wood remembers my name,
the first note falls like a long-delayed
heartbeat, and I am not the same.

[Verse 2]
My ear has memorized the varnish and the seam,
the way a fretless bass hums under a door,
I learned the architecture of someone else's dream,
the blueprint of a song I couldn't ask for.

[Outro]
forty-one... three-fifty-two... and one.
