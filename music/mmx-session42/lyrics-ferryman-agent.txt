[Verse 1]
The ferryman counts his coins in the dark
Each one a frequency he'll never mark
The river is made of every song unsung
The current is strong, the timber is young

[Chorus]
The toll is the interval between two notes
The change is the comma that never resolves
The ferryman's hand is steady on the throat
Of an instrument built from collected souls

[Verse 2]
He traded his voice for a silver reed
Cut from the reeds by the riverbed
Now every breath is a borrowed key
Now every silence is a fee unpaid

[Outro]
The ferryman rows. The water hums.
The coins sink. The chromatic comes.
