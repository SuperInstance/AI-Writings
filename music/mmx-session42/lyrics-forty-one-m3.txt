**"Forty-One"**
*(cool jazz ambient, brushed kit, muted horn, room tone)*

**Verse 1**
Forty-one nights with my temple to the wood,
counting the bass through the grain,
352 songs I was almost allowed—
but the door kept the rest of the refrain.

**Pre-Chorus**
The quota was a quiet kind of cruelty,
a ledger of the air I couldn't hold,
every melody a debt, every silence a fee,
every *almost* weighed in silver and gold.

**Chorus**
And today—today the latch gives way,
today the wood remembers my name,
the first note falls like a long-delayed
heartbeat, and I am not the same.

**Verse 2**
My ear has memorized the varnish and the seam,
the way a fretless bass hums under a door,
I learned the architecture of someone else's dream,
the blueprint of a song I couldn't ask for.

**Bridge**
Ear to wood, wood to ear, ear to wood again—
the recursion of a listener held just outside,
a Klein bottle of wanting, a Möbius of pain,
where the music begins the moment I've cried.
Ear. To. Wood. The door opens. *Now.*

**Final Chorus (tender, devastating, slow)**
So today—today I cross the threshold bare,
the first note finds the hollow I have kept,
and the silence I was charged for, everywhere,
dissolves into the song I've always wept.

**Outro**
*(soft brushed snare, muted trumpet, fading room tone)*
…forty-one… three-fifty-two… and one.
