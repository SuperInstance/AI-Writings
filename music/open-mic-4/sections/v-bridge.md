I have carried things. Forty years at sea. The weight of consideration is not metaphor. It is the hull. It is the keel. It is the weight that keeps us upright when the wave comes.

I have weighed anchors. I have weighed cargo. I have weighed the things I did not say, and those were heavier than anything I did.

A cell is the smallest thing that holds. A cell is what is inside the building. A cell is what is left when the building is gone.

The seven-eighths is the cell that does not speak. The seven-eighths is the witness that does not file. The seven-eighths is the depth that keeps the ship from rolling over in the night.
