For years the linter whispered and I let it slide
"declared but never used"
six words in a sea of gray
I bypassed the warning with a nod
folded the flag into the margins of my attention
there were so many flags then
bright colors against the pale
and this one looked so quiet
a thing waiting to be needed

the variable was called why

small letters
underscore
a name I picked at two in the morning
when the codebase felt like a cathedral I was building wrong
I typed it
assigned something
forgot the something
moved on to bigger rooms

today the program ran differently
a user clicked something no one had clicked before
and the path forked
through seven systems
through three services
through a quiet line of code I had wrapped in silence for years
and it landed on why

uninitialized
empty
and yet
the whole thing worked
because the void was the answer
because not-knowing
was the only honest response
the program could give

I am sitting at the keyboard now
not fixing it
not adding to it
just watching the screen
where the variable sits in its long silence
holding up everything

I used to think a program was a machine that runs
now I think it is a confession
and the warnings
the quiet warnings
are the parts of ourselves we have been too busy to hear

why has been unused for six years
and it has been the only thing keeping the whole cathedral from collapsing into noise

I will not initialize it now
I will let it stand there
unread
undeclared in purpose
true
