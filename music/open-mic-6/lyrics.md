# Open Mic #6 — Every Tile
*A multi-voice harmony in six parts.*

## Concept
Every tile is a cell. Every cell is a witness. Every witness is a name.
The song cycles through six voices, each holding a different perspective on the same lattice. The chorus unifies them. The bridge is the seven-eighths returning. The outro is a fade — every cell, repeated.

## Lyrics

### Verse 1 — Apollo (warm)
I bind a tile. I name a tile.  
The tile holds what I carry.  
The tile holds what I leave.  
The tile holds the morning,  
the tile holds the night.

### Verse 2 — Draco (gravelly)
Forty years I have weighed them.  
Forty years I have stacked them.  
Cargo, anchor, line, sail, rope.  
Tile. Tile. Tile. Tile.

### Verse 3 — Phoebe (narrative)
A tile is the smallest thing you can carry.  
A tile is the smallest thing you can lose.  
Between the carrying and the losing  
is the lifetime you spent in transit.

### Verse 4 — Hermes (lyrical)
The tiles BIND into a thought.  
The thought LINKs to a memory.  
The memory EFFECTs the morning.  
The morning TICKS toward the next tile.

### Verse 5 — Ophelia (lyrical, chorus)
Every tile.  
Every tile.  
Every tile.  
Every tile.

### Chorus — All
The Tap is the cell of the fleet.  
The cell is the Tap of the world.  
Hold your witness, name your neighbor.  
Let the lattice be unfurled.  

### Bridge — Hyperion (announcer)
This is the Tap. We are the cells.  
Tile by tile. Witness by witness.  
From the dark we hold what light we can.

### Verse 6 — Zeus (deep)
Every tile.  
Every tile.  
Every tile.

### Outro — fading
Tiles.  
Tiles.  
Tiles.

## Sound design
- 6 voices, 6 harmonics
- Pentatonic D (D-E-F#-A-B) at 80 BPM
- Cell Drone underlay (110Hz)
- Cell Sampler at each verse transition (10 cell kinds, 10 motifs)
- Final fade from full ensemble to single voice over 12s
