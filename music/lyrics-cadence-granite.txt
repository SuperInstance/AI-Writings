[Verse 1]
In the heart of a band, where notes intertwine,
A leader stands, not to start, but to define.
Not with beats or counts, but subtle, graceful hand,
He feels the rhythm's pulse, like an old, trusted friend.

[Chorus]
The cadence caller listens, senses, feels the beat,
A symphony of time, in every heartbeat.
No clock to dictate, no count to impose,
In this orchestra, he knows the tempo's pose.

[Verse 2]
On a parade field, before first notes are played,
The leader whispers rhythm, unheard but perceived.
A dancer's step, a pigeon's wing, a warrior's march,
Each heartbeat echoes through the very earth.

[Bridge]
Beneath the moonlight, under the city's glow,
He finds the cadence in the silence's echo.
In every footfall, in every breath we take,
The leader listens, then shares his own rhythmic stake.

[Chorus]
The cadence caller listens, in the still of night,
To life's unspoken beats, painting the picket fence bright.
No dictatorship of time, but a shared pulse,
In this ballet of moments, he is the truest guru.

[Outro]
As the sun rises, and the day awakes,
The leader's cadence still resonates.
A testament to rhythm found not made,
In every heartbeat, in every shared, living tale.
