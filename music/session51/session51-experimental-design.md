# Session 51 Experimental Design: The Frontier Quadrant Protocol

*August 12, 2026*

## Objective
Target the underpopulated spectral quadrants, especially **quiet+bright** (10% of corpus, 38 tracks).

## Background
The full corpus spectral census (S50) revealed four spectral quadrants:
- Loud+Bright: 31% (115 tracks) — well-populated
- Loud+Dark: 22% (84 tracks) — well-populated
- Quiet+Dark: 35% (129 tracks) — well-populated
- **Quiet+Bright: 10% (38 tracks) — UNDER-POPULATED**

The quiet+bright quadrant is the hardest to generate. MMX defaults to loud+dark (compressed, warm) or loud+bright (energetic). ACE-Step defaults to quiet+dark (sparse, ambient). Neither system naturally produces quiet+bright tracks.
