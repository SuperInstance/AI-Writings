[Verse 1]
The signal decays exponentially —
half-life measured in echo, not time.
I followed your waveform down the corridor
but the corridor was curved, and the curve
was the message.

[Chorus]
Every reflection loses a frequency,
the way every translation loses a joke.
The original signal is unreachable now —
but the ghost of it, the ghost of it sings.

[Verse 2]
I tried to cover the cover of the cover,
each generation a photocopy of a photocopy,
until the song was more artefact than art,
more reverb than voice, more echo than choice.

[Bridge]
But in the degradation, something unexpected:
the noise floor revealed a harmonic
that the original never had —
a frequency born from loss,
a note that only exists because
the signal was forgetting itself.

[Outro]
The signal decays exponentially.
The beauty decays linearly.
The difference between the two curves
is where all music lives.
