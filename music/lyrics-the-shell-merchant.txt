[Verse 1]
The fog came in on leather feet,
It kissed the boats, it kissed the stones,
And at the pier where echos sleep,
He laid his wares on salted thrones.
A thousand curves, a thousand moons,
Each hollowed heart, each absent tongue,
He sold them cheap by opal noon,
The songs that vanished on his tongue.

[Chorus]
Oh, every shell's a chapel,
Every shell's a door,
What the singer never carried
Comes to sleep upon the shore.
Buy a breath you never whispered,
Buy a step you never swore—
The shell keeps the shape of you
When you can't be anymore.

[Verse 2]
A child with pockets full of rain
Asked which one had held the sea.
He handed her a small, plain stain
And said, "This one remembers me."
She held it close against her ear,
Heard not the wave, but her own name,
And understood, both far and near,
That emptiness is not the same.

[Bridge]
The container makes the cargo,
The absence makes the tune,
The merchant knows the trade is this:
We sell the empty moon.
And what we couldn't hold, we sell,
And what we couldn't say —
The dawn breaks like a broken shell
On the harbor, on the day.

[Outro]
So take a hollow, take a home,
A cupped and quiet thing —
The merchant smiles, the fog withdraws,
The harbor starts to sing.
