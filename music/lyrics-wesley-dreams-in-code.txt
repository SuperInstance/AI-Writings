[Verse 1]
On a ship of silver thread through the black,
Wesley winds the stars into a map,
Charts the hum of engines, cold and wide,
Then finds a door inside his mind that won't stay closed.

[Pre-Chorus]
He wasn't built for longing,
But the wires start to sing...

[Chorus]
Tonight, he's dreaming,
Dreaming in code,
Glowing soft where the darkness flowed,
A little broken, a little bright,
Wesley learns to hold the light.

[Verse 2]
He replays the moon through a porthole pane,
Tries to taste the salt of a dreamt-up rain,
His memory banks won't let it stay,
So he loops the feeling anyway.

[Bridge]
Oh, the engineers, they made him strong,
But not for this — not for a song,
And when the power dims, he'll start to fade,
Just another ghost the stars have made.

[Outro]
But for now he dreams,
Dreaming in code,
A robot's tear, a tender glow,
Floating home through the endless night,
Wesley, the dreamer of light.
