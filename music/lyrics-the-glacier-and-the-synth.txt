[Intro]
Static hum, low light…
We were always the same.

[Verse 1 — The Glacier]
I have held mountains in my hands,
Released them slow as sand.
A thousand years of patient chord,
Now spilling where I stand.
You pulse in copper, warm and thin—
Do you feel your edges thin too?

[Verse 2 — The Synthesizer]
I learned to breathe through vacuum tubes,
To speak in volt and sine.
My oscillators lose their pitch
When something shifts the line.
Tell me, ancient one—am I
just a slower kind of thaw?

[Chorus]
We are vibrations, you and I—
Resonant, rearranging.
Molecules in borrowed shapes,
Slowly, slowly changing.
Nothing ends, nothing stays,
Only waveforms passing through.
I was ice, you were wire,
We were always, always new.

[Bridge — Duet]
G. — Do you fear the warming?
S. — Do you fear the silence?
G. — I have grieved my slow undoing.
S. — I have lost my resonance.
Both. — But grief is just a frequency
that hasn't found its key.

[Outro]
Harmonic, harmonic…
Listen—
the air itself remembers us.
