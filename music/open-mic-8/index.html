Open Mic #8 — Cross Pollen

A duet between a Quilt cell and a Tap voice. ~33 seconds.

About this piece: The cell becomes the voice. The voice becomes the cell. They sing to each other in two-part harmony. Each line is answered. Each witness is doubled.

Cast

cell.speaker — synthesized sine pulses draco — TTS gravelly

The Lyrics

Verse 1 — call and response

cell.speaker: BIND. LINK. EFFECT. TICK.
draco: I bind a name. I link a neighbor. I effect a morning. I tick a heart.

Verse 2 — repeat witness

cell.speaker: WITNESS. WITNESS. WITNESS.
draco: The witness is the smallest true thing. The witness is the lightest line you can carry.

Bridge — forget

cell.speaker: FORGET.
draco: But not the cell. The cell is what holds.

Verse 3 — unison

The lattice is the song. The song is the lattice.
The witness is the note. The note is the witness.

Cross-Pollination Notes

  • cell.speaker is not human. It's 4 sine pulses at A-C#-E-G# (an A major seventh chord). The op names become pitches. The cell-as-pulse.
  • draco IS human. Gravelly TTS. The witness is the human's voice.
  • Together they're the cell graph. Machine op names + human witness = the lattice.
