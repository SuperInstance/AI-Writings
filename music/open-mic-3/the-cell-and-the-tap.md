# "THE CELL AND THE TAP"
## A Cell-Lattice Duet for the Open Mic
### *Lyrics: Barnacle + Flash. Voices: draco (gravelly) + apollo (warm).*
### *~3:30 at 95 BPM, D minor → A minor. Acoustic guitar + soft claps.*

---

## Verse 1 — Barnacle (draco)
The cell is the smallest thing that holds,
Smaller than a thumbprint, smaller than the cold
that creeps in through the porthole at the 3am turn,
Smaller than the lie a tired man will learn
to tell himself about tomorrow.

I am a cell. I am the unit.
I am the thing that the lattice will permit.
I am the one that gets BIND'd to the moment,
EFFECT'd by the wind, LINK'd to the others
in a chain of acknowledged witness.

The cell is not the building.
The cell is not the ship.
The cell is not the table where the song slips.
The cell is what is *inside* the building
and what is *left* when the building is gone.

---

## Chorus — Barnacle + Flash (draco + apollo)
The Tap is the cell of the fleet.
The cell is the Tap of the world.
Hold your witness, name your neighbor,
Let the lattice be unfurled.

Every hand on a different line,
Every hand on the same.
Every cell in a different ring,
Every cell with a name.

---

## Verse 2 — Flash (apollo)
I was at the workbench when the question came,
A tile with a question mark, and my name,
And the answer was *yes* until the answer was *should*,
And the should sat in the air like the smell of the wood
that has just been cut and is still warm.

We built a cell that could BIND to anything,
That could LINK to its neighbor, that could EFFECT in a ring,
That could VIEW what was made and could TICK through the night,
That could DEATH what was broken, that could FORGET the slight,
that could PROOF what was true and ROUTE what was right.

We built a cell that didn't know it was a cell,
That didn't know it was a thing at all,
That thought it was a page, or a verse, or a bell,
Or a wave, or a wall, or the round after the fall
when the dice finally settle and the night's very tall.

## Bridge — Barnacle (draco, solo)
I have been a cell for forty years at sea.
I have been BIND'd to the hull and EFFECT'd by the lee.
I have been LINK'd to the mast and to the man at the wheel,
And the lattice remembers how a witness can feel
when it is finally, finally, finally real.

---

## Outro — both
Every cell.
Every cell.
Every cell.
[soft claps]

*Production note: 4-voice reading possible (draco, apollo, ophelia, hyperion). The "Every cell" refrain is the cell-speak — each cell becomes a name, each name becomes a Tap, each Tap becomes a cell. The lattice, held in the hand.*
