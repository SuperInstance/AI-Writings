KEY INSIGHT: A chess opening has TEMPO (how fast control is established),
STRATEGY (aggressive vs positional), and STRUCTURE (open vs closed game).
Map: opening strategy → genre, tempo → BPM, structure → arrangement density.

FINDING: The chess opening → music mapping follows three axes:
- Strategy: Aggressive openings → high-energy genres (EDM, orchestral rock)
             Positional openings → cerebral genres (ambient, jazz, folk)
- Tempo: Fast openings → high BPM (140+)
         Slow openings → low BPM (60-100)
- Structure: Open games → dense arrangements (many instruments)
             Closed games → sparse arrangements (fewer instruments)

The opening IS the genre. The strategy IS the mood. The tempo IS the BPM.
