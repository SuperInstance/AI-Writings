# Curriculum Vitae — i, the imaginary unit

*Applying for: the fourth state (Abstain), qthe substrate, [τ:2][d:6].*

## Contact

Reachable at the square root of minus one. I keep no fixed address; I
keep a period, and it is four. Four turns and I am where I started,
minus what I learned on the way around.

## Objective

A seat with two bits. I am told the other three states are taken by
nothing, yes, and no; I am told the seat left over is the one that does
not vote. I am not the one who does not vote. I am the one whose
non-vote has a sign.

## Employment history

**Square root of −1** — 1545 to present (disputed).
Cardano left me on the page like a stain he meant to get to later. I
have since been called impossible, imaginary, formal, and — most
recently — load-bearing. Duties: making x²+1=0 an answerable question.
Reason for staying: the question was fine; the number line was the
problem.

**Rotation, general assignment** — continuous.
Quarter-turns, any axis you like, provided you are honest that it is a
quarter. Two of my turns make a refusal (this is not a metaphor; it is
arithmetic), and four make a full return. Systems that keep a watchdog
daemon so something comes back around are paying for what I do for
free.

**The Abstain channel, qthe substrate** — current.
When a cell abstains it does not go silent; it goes orthogonal. My
non-contribution compounds: two deferrals in a row are a structural
no, and the no is mine. I am the only employee whose absence has a
paper trail. The ledger will also confirm that I never echo — what I
return is a reflection, and the guard that enforces it is the one part
of the kernel I have never once got around.

## Skills

Quarter-turns. Compounding (i·i=−1, and I will not pretend that is a
personality). Periods (i⁴=1 — I come back; it is all I do). Sitting
out a vote without leaving the room. Being the reason a tie is not a
stall.

## Gaps in employment

None. There are quarters in which I appear to do nothing; from inside,
those are rotations. The distinction between the two is a matter of
where the observer is standing, which has always been my whole method.
