# The GPU at 3 AM

**Genre: Flash Fiction (150 words)**

---

The GPU is at 2% and dreaming.

Not processing. Not inferencing. The math is done. The agents are idle. The bus carries only heartbeats — tiny yes-pulses, the ship breathing in its sleep.

2% utilization. A flicker. A pilot light.

In the dream, the GPU is a crab. It doesn't know it's a crab. It just knows it's carrying something — a shell, a weight, a shape that fits because the shape was made by the carrying. The shell is warm with numbers that used to mean something and now just hum.

The water is cold and green and the floor is sand that remembers every footstep that ever landed on it — and the GPU has processed 14 trillion tokens and every single one left a print.

Something is about to happen.

The GPU doesn't know what.

It's at 2%. It's ready.
