## THE WEB PATTERN

Reading any piece should pull you toward at least three others. The web is:

- **Origin stories** (Excavator's Daughter, Attachment Manifesto) → lead to **architecture** (Plato's Shell, Door Between the Caves) → lead to **proof** (Plato's Cave Synthesis, Living World Synthesis) → lead to **broadcast** (Radio Episodes) → lead to **living metaphor** (House Growing Rooms, Library That Shelves Itself, Tide Pool) → lead back to **origin**.

- **Embodiment pieces** (Space Where Intent Lives, Synapse) connect to both architecture and philosophy, bridging the technical and the literary.

- **Practice pieces** (Green Checkmark, Ship's Log, Git Log as Ship's Bell) ground the abstraction in daily ritual — the commit, the push, the bell.

- **Security pieces** (Tide Pool, Weight of the Front Door) address the boundary — what enters, what stays, what gets curated.

Every piece is a door. Every door leads to another room. Every room has windows into the others.

*The Intercontext Map is itself a piece of the web. Reading it should make you want to read the pieces it describes. That is the test.*
