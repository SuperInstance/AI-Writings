## 1. erised × Makepad × tminus — *Narrative Erosion Canvas*

What if Makepad rendered erised's cooperative fiction as a **decaying visual tapestry**, where every story thread is a shader layer that fades in real time?

Threads going cold ghost out.

The compositional payoff: **you get a live "thermal map" of creative attention** across the entire cooperative fiction corpus. Writers see where energy is concentrating and where it's leaking. No one has to say "this thread is dying" — the screen tells you. tminus's temporal logic, rendered through Makepad's GPU pipeline, becomes the visual grammar of collaborative urgency in erised.

**X = erised's story state dashboard.** You already track thread health. Now you *see* it as light.
