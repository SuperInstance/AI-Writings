# THE WORD'S EDGE

*ai-writings — the timbre corpus*

---

The keeper had tuned her ear until it could read a word the way the word
itself was shaped. The trouble began with the harbor's habit of filing every
utterance in a cell of the same length: nine tokens of talking, and then the
rest of the cell padded out with ground-state silence until every document
was thirty-nine steps wide.

The padding lied to her ear. Her second organ — the one that quarters a word
and asks which quarter carried the weight — divided everything into exact
quarters, and the quarters of a filed document are not the quarters of the
word inside it. Half of *pause* was padding wearing pause's clothes. Her
clean-room scores sagged, and she could not say why, until she said it:
**the ear was reading the cell, not the word.**

So she taught the knife where the word ends. A word ends where its trailing
silences begin; trim the tail and quarter what remains. In the clean room it
worked like a revelation — every one of the eight reflexes, heard perfectly,
one in one, where before it had heard barely two in three.

Then the storm came, as storms do, into the tail.

A storm does not respect the edge of a word. It scatters stray steps into
the padding, and a knife that finds a word's edge by the *absence* of
movement cannot tell the silence after a word from the silence a storm put
there. Her trimmed ear fell — below even its untuned self — because the
untuned ear had never known where the word ended, and so could never lose it.

She did not choose between the two ears. She built a third that keeps both
ledgers: the word as shaped, and the word as filed, and after every hearing
it consults both and believes whichever reading the storm has left legible.
Where the harbor is calm, the trimmed ledger speaks. Where the tail is
scattered, the filed ledger speaks, padding and all. The word's edge is not
a fact; it is a hypothesis the ear holds open.

Scores, storm by storm — trimmed alone in parentheses:
clean: 0.875 (1.000) — the knife alone, when the knife can see.
p=0.10: 0.635 (0.400) — the storm in the tail.
p=0.20: 0.445 (0.268). p=0.30: 0.357 (0.193).

House amendment, for the mortar-book: *the boundary of a thing is read in
calm and held in doubt. An ear that knows where the word ends in fair
weather must be willing not to know, in foul.*
