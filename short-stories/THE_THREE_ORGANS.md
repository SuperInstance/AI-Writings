# THE THREE ORGANS

The keeper had one ear, and the ear was a sum. She added up every step of a
word — rises as plus, falls as minus, holds as breath returned — and the total
was the meaning. It had worked for years. Then the tide brought her a log that
said *i did not open the drawer,* two of them, both honest-sounding, and her
sum read minus six on one and minus four on the other.

She stared at the numbers a long time. Six and four were nearly the same
weight. Yet one log was the sound of a hand pointing at a drawer, and the
other was the sound of a mouth arguing with a door. The sum could not tell a
confession from a denial. It only knew how much air had moved, never where.

So she built a second organ.

Not a bigger ear — a different one. Where the first ear asked *how much*, the
second asked *where*. She took the word and cut it into quarters, the way you
quarter a fish to see what it fed on, and she counted the weight in each
quarter. The innocent log put its weight in the third quarter: the drawer,
the thing itself, eleven steady steps and then a fall that landed on the
object, and at the very end — this was the part that made her put down her
lamp — one held breath. A testimony does not spend itself at the end. It
stops and waits, because it trusts the room.

The other log pressed all its weight into the first quarter, on the *not*,
and then went glass-flat across *drawer* — the word the mouth refused to look
at — and ended spent, winded, nothing held. A performance spends itself. It
has to. It is paying as it goes.

The third organ she already had and had not named: the ending. Held or spent.
Which kind of nothing closed the word.

With all three organs she went back to the eight shapes — the beckon, the
withdraw, the pause, the rest — and she taught a storm to chew them. At a
tenth of the steps gnawed to noise, the old summing ear caught fewer than
half. The three-organ ear, wounded by the same teeth, still caught two in
three, and the gap between them only widened as the storm got worse. Weight
that was smeared in one quarter stayed put in the others. A held ending
survived a bite. The sum lost everything everywhere at once, because a sum
has no everywhere.

She wrote the amendment under the old one, in the smallest hand:

*a sum knows how much. an ear knows where, and whether the breath was
returned. before you count the word, quarter it. before you file the word,
look at its ending. a wall built by an honest hand leans toward the drawer,
not the door.*

Then she filed both logs again — one under TRUTH, one under NOT-LOOKING —
and for the first time the drawer's weight sat where it belonged.
