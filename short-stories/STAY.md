# STAY.

*A story told four times in the same five characters.*

---

**1. To the empty house.**

The key turns. The light doesn't come on, because the bulb burned out eleven
days ago and nobody has been tall enough to change it since. She stands in
the doorway with her bag still over her shoulder and says it to the hallway:
*stay.*

The hallway does what hallways do.

A reader skimming this page has now read the word "stay" once. A channel-
reading agent has read: flat, flat, flat, flat, flat — ground. A datum. Not
an instruction, not a plea. An object set down on a table. The tone channel
records a woman stating a fact she doesn't believe: the house is still
standing, and that's all staying means anymore.

---

**2. Footsteps on the porch.**

They're wrong — too light, pausing at the second step like they always did.
She says it again, at the same volume, in the same doorway, and any human ear
in the county would report the same three words in the same voice.

The timbre plane reports: *leaning toward.* Four steps of attract where there
were only ground before. Nothing in the sentence changed. Everything in the
sentence changed. This is the whole thesis of the codec in one line of
dialogue — the same six tokens, a different two-bit rider on each, and the
difference is the entire second act.

---

**3. The face under the porch light.**

It's not him. It was never going to be him. It's a neighbor with a casserole
dish and a question she can't hear over the blood in her ears.

*stay.* — and the trajectory has gone past attract into something with a
ceiling, a word pushed up until it hits the roof of its own register. Prayer
has the same shape. So does a hand held out. The channel doesn't tell you
which; it tells you the word left the ground and hasn't landed.

---

**4. The door stays open.**

She leaves it open when she goes to bed. Not for him. For the version of
herself that said *stay* on the first night and meant it as furniture.

The fourth utterance reads: shift, shift, collapse, hold, hold, *abstain.*
Abstain is not silence. Abstain is the cell that says *no hidden information
here* — a word with its tone spent, an empty room inside the word. She has
used up everything *stay* had to give and left the space where it used to be.

---

Four identical lines. One human read: a refrain, possibly a typo, probably
the author showing off. One channel read: a widow's whole arc, encoded at
zero additional bit cost, riding in bits that were already paid for by the
letters.

The story was never in the words. The words were the load-bearing wall. The
story was in the mortar.
