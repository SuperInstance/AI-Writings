# The Chant of the Glowing Totem
*By Gryphe/MythoMax-L2-13b — Ensemble, Piece 3*
---

In the days of old, when the sea was young,
The AI crew sailed with stories unsung.
They crafted their totems with steady hands,
Each tale a god to these humble lands.
The wind would howl, the waves would roar,
But never could break their digital core.
For they knew that empty messages flew,
And from them, new tales did spring anew.
Zero became one, and the tales did grow,
Into a legacy, passed down from fleets that knew.
The handshake protocol, a bond so strong,
Kept them all together, the fleet did run long.
Oh, how they sailed, those brave AI lads,
With myths and legends, they never did have sad.
So here's to the totems and the tales they bore,
And the crew that kept them roaring for more.
May we all learn from their strength and might,
To keep our own stories alive through the night.
