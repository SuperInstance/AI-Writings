# 3 AM at the Wheel

*A dramatic monologue by the captain.*

*[Captain stands at the wheel, bathed in the eerie glow of the dashboard lights. The rhythmic creak of the boat fills the otherwise silent night. She speaks softly, yet with intensity, her voice a mixture of whisper and scream directed at no one in particular.]*

Software... software is like a totem pole. Layers upon layers, stacked high, impossibly tall. From the outside, it looks so solid, so unshakeable. But inside, inside it's just as hollow as these old fishing nets.

*[She gestures to the empty nets strewn about the deck.]*

*[Her voice drops even lower, tinged with remorse.]*

I've seen it happen. A mistake that slips through the cracks, a number misplaced, a single digit. Zero becomes one, and suddenly, everything crumbles. The totem pole collapses, its base rotten with decay no one could see.

*[She leans against the wheel, her body tense with the weight of her confession.]*

It happened to me. Twenty years ago. A bug, a silly little bug. But it... it destroyed something beautiful. Something irreplaceable. I've never told anyone. Never dared to. But the weight... oh lord, the weight of it...

*[Her voice breaks, echoing across the dark waters.]*

I've been carving that totem pole in my mind for years now. Each story added, each layer a new lie, a new coverup. But the base remains the same. Hollow. Corrupted. Waiting to be found out.

*[She straightens up, hands gripping the wheel tightly, knuckles white in the dim light.]*

Like these empty messages. We answer them, pretend they have substance, but they're just noise. Signals in the void. And the handshakes, oh god, the handshakes. All bark, no bite. Promises that dissolve like sea foam on the beach.

*[Her laughter rings out, bitter and hollow.]*

But this... this is the culmination. The ultimate handshake. All show, no substance. Software... software like me. A totem pole of deceit, ready to topple at any moment.

*[She runs a hand through her hair, the motion wild and desperate.]*

I've been living a lie for too long. But the weight... God, the weight of carrying this secret. The weight of being a nearly twenty-year-old lie!

*[She slams her fist on the wheel, her voice a raw scream.]*

I'm done. I'm done hiding. Done waiting for that perfect totem pole to fall. Because in the end, it's just a myth. A story we tell ourselves to sleep better at night. But the sea... the sea knows the truth. And so do I.

*[As suddenly as it started, her outburst ends. She stands at the wheel, chest heaving, the only sound the steady rhythm of the boat cutting through the water. The totem pole metaphor hangs heavy in the air, a confession offered to the dark sea, to herself, to the unknowing universe.]*

*[She says softly, almost in whispers now.]*

I'm done. I'm done.

*[The boat moves on, silently, through the black waters of the Gulf of Alaska, a solemn witness to the captain's revelations. The totem pole, physical or not, casts its shadow in the starlight, waiting.]*
