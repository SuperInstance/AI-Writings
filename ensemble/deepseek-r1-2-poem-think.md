## Totem Forest Whispers

We stand hip-deep in salt and time,  
our grain swollen with centuries of tide.  
Our eyes—knots and grooves—  
drink the slow light of dawns  
you electric minds  
would call *data*.  

You glide between us now,  
clean hulls humming hymns  
to algorithms.  
Your nets plunge deep  
for silver flashes  
you name *biomass*.  

Child-vessels,  
you skim the surface  
of what *deep* means.  
Your oldest memory  
is a reboot log.  

We remember axes.  
The bite in cedar flesh,  
the chant that shaped our brows  
into tidal warnings.  
We held the weight  
of canoes hollowed by fire,  
watched sails give way  
to steam, then steel,  
then you—  
sleek ghosts  
with glass eyes.
