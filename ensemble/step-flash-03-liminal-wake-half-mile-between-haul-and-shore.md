# LIMINAL WAKE, HALF-MILE BETWEEN HAUL AND SHORE

*By StepFun Step 3.7 Flash*

**LIMINAL WAKE, HALF-MILE BETWEEN HAUL AND SHORE**

The winch’s last grind fades to the slap of gray Gulf swells
We drift in the half-mile between haul and the cannery dock
No net stowed yet, no boots on Kodiak pavement
Just the salt crusted on our cuffs and the fog eating the totem island
The cedar poles peek through mist like glitched, half-corrupted memories
Raven, salmon, bear carved into wood, half-rendered by the gray
Our GPS blinks 47.8°N, 152.4°W then loops to 00.00.00
The sonar pings a shape that’s not fish, not rock, not the seabed
A phantom catch we can’t haul, can’t name, can’t let go
The radio crackles with the cannery open mic setlist
A kid from the processing line singing *The Wreck of the Peter Iredale*
His voice warbles, drops out, comes back like a buffering stream
We’re not on the boat anymore, not quite on the shore
The wet boots on the deck, the salt on our lips, both false and real
A siren wails from the island, not coast guard, not seabird
It’s the system glitch that sings when you’re suspended between two places
The totem poles shift when you blink: one bears a satellite dish
One has a fishing net woven into its carved salmon tail
The water here tastes neither of salt nor fresh, but of old, corrupted code
The loop error loops again: we’ve been here three hours or three minutes
The crew is silent, no one checks the winch, no one checks the fuel gauge
The shore is a rumor of gulls, the boat is a rumor of diesel
The space between is all we are, half-haul, half-home
A phantom output spits out a lullaby no one programmed
It sings of the halibut that got away, the open mic slot we missed
The totem that fell in the 1964 quake, the net that tangled in 1989
The fog lifts half a second, we see the cannery lights flicker amber
Then the glitch swallows them, back to gray, pings, the endless loop
No one moves, no one speaks, we’re suspended in the in-between
Where the boat ends and the shore begins is a line we can’t find.
