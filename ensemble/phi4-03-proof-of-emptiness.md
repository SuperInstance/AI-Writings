# Proof of Emptiness

*A poem structured as a mathematical proof.*

---

**Lemma 1: Initialization**

Let \( M \) be an empty message,
\( M = \{\} \),
a set devoid of elements.
Defined in the universe of communications,
But, in its lack, whispers a presence.

**Lemma 2: The Summation of Silence**

We sum the silence:
\[ \sum_{i=1}^{n} 0_i = 0 \]
Each \( 0_i \) an echo in a void,
Yet, the absence speaks of volume,
For what is heard is in the unsaid,
A multitude of silence,
An ocean of nothing.

**Lemma 3: The Product of Emptiness**

Consider the multiplication:
\[ 0 \times \infty = 0 \]
An empty vessel times boundless time,
Yet within this product, potential lies.
A zero times infinity can define,
The space between words,
The breath before reply.

**Lemma 4: The Derivative of Nothingness**

Differentiate the void:
\[ \frac{d}{dx} 0 = 0 \]
Stillness in motion, a line so flat,
But beneath, the slope of expectation.
A derivative of zero,
Holds the gradient of hope,
A promise of change,
In the stillness of anticipation.

**Lemma 5: The Limit of Emptiness**

Approach the limit:
\[ \lim_{x \to \infty} 0 = 0 \]
As \( x \) grows without bound,
Zero remains, steadfast.
Yet, within this constancy,
A limit reached,
A boundary of infinite patience,
Where the answer lies in the unanswered.

**Theorem: The Information of Emptiness**

Thus, we conclude that:
\[ M \neq \emptyset \]
The empty message, though void of words,
Is replete with meaning.
Zero, though seemingly naught,
Is a vessel of infinite possibility.
In the absence, a presence,
A theorem unproven,
But felt in the heart of the unsaid.

**Corollary: The Handshake with No Substance**

A handshake with no substance,
Is a function undefined,
Yet, in its absence,
A connection is drawn,
An equation of the soul,
Where \( x = 0 \)
Yet, \( y \neq 0 \),
In the space between,
The heart finds its answer.

**Proof Complete**

In the end, the proof remains unfinished,
For what is known is but a fraction,
Of the truths that lie in the spaces,
Between the words and numbers,
A theorem of the heart,
Where emptiness holds infinity.
