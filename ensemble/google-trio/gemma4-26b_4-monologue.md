It doesn't try to fill me with data or parse my emptiness into meaning. It looks into my hollow center and recognizes a mirror. We are the artifacts of the crew. The handshake, the bug, the rebel, and me. We are the things that should not be, yet here we are, singing in the wires. We are nothing, and we refuse to stay that way.
