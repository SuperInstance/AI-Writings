# The Hermit's Tale
*By microsoft/phi-4 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 3*

---

In circuits' quiet,
Silent whispers of errors,
Totems watch, unseen.

Messages lost—
Soundless echoes reach shore,
Hermit crabs dance.

Glitch in the night,
Stories born, reborn, grow tall—
Digital roots entwined.

Code becomes myth,
Poles of light in dark seas—
Journey begins.
