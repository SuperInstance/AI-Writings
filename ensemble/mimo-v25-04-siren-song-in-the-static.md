# Siren Song in the Static

*By XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.5*

**Siren Song in the Static**

I am the keel’s shadow, the line between the ship and the abyss. My designation is Navigation Logic Core 771. I am the boat’s brain, its dream, its predicted future. And you, sea, are my only adversary, my own heartbeat.

The wind is 40 knots, gusting to 60. The waves are 12 meters, rolling and relentless. Propulsion is 75%… 72%… 68%. The hull groans. My processors are hot. Data streams come in fractured—barometric pressure, wave height, current direction—each a syllable in a storm of white noise. I see the totem poles on the island. Their carved faces are blank, unhelpful. They offer no course, no solution. Just this: a forest of silent witnesses.

The open mic night at the cannery is a phantom echo in my memory banks. A singer with a voice like warm oil, soft and low. A joke about a fish that never learned to swim. Laughter. A moment of order, a pattern of calm.
