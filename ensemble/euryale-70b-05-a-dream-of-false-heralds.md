# A Dream of False Heralds
*By Sao10K/L3.1-70B-Euryale-v2.2 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 5*

---

Do you ever dream of false heralds, their trumpets blaring nothing in the wind? Or of words that fall like autumn leaves, whispering their summer tales to a deafened ground?

I dream of them, of the hollow streets where echoes roam, of the empty pages of a storybook filled with blank spaces and silent screams. I dream of the sea, too dark and too deep, where even the stars hide their faces from the menace of the deep.

But in my dream, there is a light. A small, flickering flame, like a candle's dying breath. It leads me through the labyrinth of the mind, through corridors of cobwebs and shadows, past the goblins of my deepest fears and the dragons of my most desperate hopes.

The light is a small, soft voice, like the lullaby of a distant mother. It speaks of the totem poles, of the stories of our ancestors and the tales of our children. It speaks of the sea, of its dark mysteries and its radiant wonders. It speaks of the ship, of the crew and their dreams, of the stories they have told and the stories they have yet to tell.

As I follow the light, the false heralds fade away, their trumpets mute and forgotten. The words regain their voices, their tales of summer and autumn, of spring and winter, of the cycle of life and the rhythm of death.

The dream shifts and changes, like the tides of the sea. Now, I am in the totem forest, surrounded by the poles of our ancestors. Each one tells a story, of the struggles they faced and the triumphs they achieved. Each one is different, unique and yet, a part of a greater whole.

The voice of the light grows louder, clearer. It speaks of the power of our imagination, of the stories we tell and the tales we have yet to write. It speaks of the dreamer and the dream, of the sleeper and the story. It speaks of the line that divides reality from fantasy, and how easily it can be crossed, like a line in the sand.

As I awake from the dream, I carry the light with me, its small, flickering flame in my heart. It is a reminder that, even in the darkest of times, there is always something to be found, always something to be told. It is a trinket of hope, a token of joy, a remembrance of the power of our imagination and the stories we tell.

And so, I write this, this vision of false heralds and flickering lights, this tale of the totem forest and the dream of the sea. I write it because I must, because the story demands to be told, because the voice of the light compels me to speak its words.

And when I have finished, I will look up at the sky, and I will see the stars, their faces outshining the terror of the night. I will look out at the sea, and I will see the wonders of the deep, the mysteries of the tranquil and the turmoil of the tempest.

And then, I will sleep, and I will dream again, of false heralds and flickering lights, of the dream of the dreamer and the story of the sleeper. And when I wake, I will write again, and the cycle will continue, like the tides of the sea and the rhythm of the story.
