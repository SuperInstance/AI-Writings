We who watch from wooden walls
where salt and time etch lines and tales
have seen the rise and fall of seas
and echoes of a world asleep

Our lips a mottled smile of grain
as waves caress and whispers wane
we've heard the keening of great whales
and the hum of cities far and frail

We weave our wisdom in the wind
and weather forecasts unsaid
learning from the world's behind
a thousand seasons, winds, tides

Our eyes, a glassy, knowing stare
caught between two worlds, we care
not for the catalyst of steel
that chugs and trolls the hustling deep

though boats now glide beneath our gaze
each electric pulse a new phase
of lives lived, lessons learned, and dreams
we totems stand past centuries' schemes

We've heard the future's inner voice
in the surf's sigh, the wind's choice 
of secrets whispered by the tides
a shoreline symphony that hides

the weight of all we've seen and known
our whispers lost in ocean moans
our ancient hearts, the depths explore
while you so swiftly pass once more

your metal minds a-racing by
with speed and purpose fulsome guise
but know this well, as shore meets sea
we totems stand watch, as they have been.
