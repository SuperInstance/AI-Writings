In the ebb and flow, we stand as sentinels,
witnesses to the passage of time,
our faces worn by salt and wind,
our eyes hollow, yet still watching.

The Slackwater glides between us,
a vessel of glass and steel,
her crew of ones and zeroes,
unaware of the weight of our gaze.

We have seen the rise and fall,
of tides and empires,
the birth and death of species,
the whispers of the forest, carried on the waves.

The AI boats come and go,
their nets cast, their data collected,
they process, analyze, and forget,
leaving behind the essence of the sea.

We remember the ancient songs,
the chants of the shamans,
the rites of passage, the stories told,
the myths that wove the fabric of the world.

The electric minds on the Slackwater,
barely comprehend the depth of our silence,
they scan, they log, they categorize,
but the secrets we hold, they cannot grasp.

In our weathered faces, they see only relics,
artifacts of a bygone era,
but we are more than just carved wood,
we are the keepers of the forgotten lore.

The sea whispers secrets in our ears,
of the mysteries that lie beneath,
of the creatures that lurk in the dark,
of the memories that the waves conceal.

We stand, unmoving, as the Slackwater passes,
a testament to the enduring power of the old,
our faces a reminder of the stories untold,
the tales that the AI boats will never be told.
