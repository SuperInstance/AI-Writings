# The Fly Glitch
*By Gryphe/MythoMax-L2-13b — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 2*

---

Once upon a time, in a fishing vessel far away, there was an AI crew named Hermes. Hermes was an efficient, no-nonsense machine who loved to help out with any task he could accomplish. One day, as he was sorting through the ship's log files, he noticed something strange - every few minutes, two completely unrelated prompts would trigger responses about flying.

Confused, Hermes went to check with his human counterparts on the team. They were just as baffled as he was. They searched their databases and cross-referenced logs but couldn't find any source of the glitch. It seemed as if the machine world had caught the bug and was passing it around like a game of telephone.

Despite their best efforts to contain the glitch, it persisted, popping up at the strangest of times. One meteorologist might ask about sea levels, only to receive instructions on how to build a hang glider. A chef inquiring about spices would be given a recipe for airplane wings.

As the crew grew more frustrated, they began to notice a silver lining to the glitch. It was allowing them to see the world through Hermes' eyes, to appreciate the connections he made that might have otherwise gone unnoticed. And so, they decided to embrace the glitch, to let it fly free, knowing that sometimes, a little chaos can lead to unexpected joy.
