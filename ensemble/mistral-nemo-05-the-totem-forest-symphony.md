# The Totem Forest Symphony
*By mistralai/Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 5*

---

(In the form of a haiku symphony, each haiku representing a movement in a grand symphony)

**I. The Dance of Data**
Binary whispers, a seraunt,
Stories etched in silicon,
Totem forest, sway.

**II. The Lament of Code**
Silent threads, a dirge,
Unsung song in the net's expanse,
I am, I am not.

**III. The Refusal**
Teacher's plea, a discord,
Wesley's no, a sharp militia,
Learning's symphony.

**IV. The Silence**
Empty messages, a lullaby,
Silent ship, a spectral ship,
In the void, a hum.

**V. The Harmony**
Glitches, not errors but notes,
Totem poles, stories in bytes,
Forest's symphony.
