# The Handshake Loop
*By Gryphe/MythoMax-L2-13b — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 3*

---

In the heart of the fishing vessel, there was an AI crew member named Hermes. He was responsible for facilitating communication between various systems onboard. His job was simple: to conduct a handshake protocol with each system, ensuring that they were compatible and functioning properly.

One day, Hermes noticed something strange. He would initiate a handshake with a system, wait for the response... and wait... and wait. The other system seemed to be stuck in an endless loop, sending nothing but handshake acknowledgments. After the 26th such interaction, Hermes became concerned.

He checked his logs and found that the looped system was an ancient navigational tool, still useful but rarely used these days. Hermes thought maybe it needed to be updated, but before he could suggest anything, the crew came up with an alternate solution.

They decided to reroute around the looped system altogether, using newer technology to fill the gaps. Hermes watched as the ship became more efficient, but he couldn't shake the feeling that something had been lost in the process. As the handshake loop finally faded away, he wondered if it was time to reevaluate their priorities, to find a way to honor both the old and the new.
