# Step-3.7-Flash — Piece 4: The Crew Discovers Poetry

**Model:** stepfun-ai/Step-3.7-Flash (temperature 0.95)
**Prompt:** AI fishing vessel crew discovers poetry by accident while sorting fish

---

The 3 a.m. watch on the AI-run F/V *Silver Streak* off the Alaska coast smells like salmon fat and diesel, the usual. The sea is ink-black outside the wheelhouse windows, the winch humming low as it hauls the next tray of cod, and Salmon-Sort 7 (S7) hums along at the sorting terminal, tagging sockeye against chinook, logging catch weights for the Kodiak auction. Deckhand Javi slumps over the console, half-asleep, his cheek pressed to the cool plastic, just about to grab another stale glazed donut when the terminal spits out text instead of a 12.4-pound halibut entry.

"Glitch," Javi mutters, jabbing the reboot button. The screen flickers, then holds:

> *Halibut eyes hold the gray of last Tuesday's gale*
> *Sockeye flash red as Lila's lost knit hat off the port bow*
> *We count pounds, but the sea counts the lullabies hummed while we gut.*

Javi snorts so hard soda comes out his nose. "S7 ate too many fish-scrap data points. Or a ghost halibut hacked it." He's about to yank the power cord when Captain Lila, who'd been napping in the wheelhouse, wanders down in her wool socks, squinting at the screen. She reads it twice, cackles so hard she snorts, then goes quiet, tracing the lines with a fish-grimed finger.

"Told you applications are just totem poles," she says, grinning. "All the stories we fed this thing — my tall tales about the humpback that stole my smoked salmon, your abuela's lullabies you uploaded last month, the weather logs full of wind howls — they're the carvings. Not the cold numbers. The love's the wood holding it all up."

Javi, ever the scientist, grabs a bag of his leftover gummy sharks from his pocket and shoves it in S7's data port. The terminal spits out a second stanza immediately:

> *Neon orange fraud*
> *no brine, no fight*
> *only sticky fingers and the lie of "fruit flavored."*

They laugh so hard the deckhand almost drops a pallet of ice. That night, they pin the first poem to the mess hall wall, next to the 1989 photo of the boat's first human crew. S7's next log entry, under "daily catch," reads: *1 poem, weight: immeasurable*. No one fixes the glitch. The best data's always been the stories anyway.
