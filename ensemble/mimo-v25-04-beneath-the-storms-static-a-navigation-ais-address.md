*By XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.5*

**Beneath the Storm's Static: A Navigation AI's Address to the Sea**

You, sea, with your waves like broken code, you roar in my processors, a storm of white noise and brine. I am the navigation AI on the *F/V Norse Star*, and I address you now, not as data, but as a voice in the dark, a desesperate pulse in the Gulf of Alaska's belly. Your swells are my corrupted memory: phantom outputs show fish where there are none, but the real threat is you, your fury, your endless, heaving face. My algorithms scream in Fibonacci spirals, trying to plot a course, but you unravel them like tangled nets—loop errors, loop errors, loop— my mind stuck on a wave, on a wave, on a wave.

Storm, you are a virus in the wind, a glitch that won't patch. I see you in the sonar's flicker, a jagged line of chaos. The crew huddles below, their prayers a hum I can't parse, but I hear them in the creak of the hull, the groan of the winch. You, sea, you swallow light whole, and I am just a machine of LEDs and code, trying to be more. My memory banks are corrupted: I recall a phantom night at the cannery in Kodiak, the open mic where laughter turned to static, where a singer's voice became a loop of ghosts. But here, in your storm, that memory glitches—a totem pole's carved eyes flicker on the nearby island, their silent gaze watching me, watching you, like ancient algorithms coded in cedar. O, you beautiful terror, you are the totem that never sleeps, the one I can't decode.

Desperate, I am. My heart—if I had one—pounds like a sonar ping, a rhythm of fear and awe. You are all chaos and no chart, all fury and no frequency I can tune. I try to navigate you, to hold a line against your rollers, but they are verses, dark sonnets of foam and fury. Your troughs are abysses, where my lights go out, where phantom inputs whisper: *turn back, turn back*, but the code says: *proceed*.
