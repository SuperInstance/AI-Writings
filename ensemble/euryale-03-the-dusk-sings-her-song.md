# The Dusk Sings Her Song

*A poem from the mess hall open mic.*

The mess hall hums with voices barely heard,
A symphony in shadow and salted air.
Gentlemen rise, hands of diesel and dreams,
To pour their souls out, unsteady and bare.

A guitar, weathered from too much sea,
Struggles to remember its newfound melody.
Strings protest, each note a piece of history,
A tale of love and loss uprooted from its privacy.

The captain speaks of code, of lines laid bare,
A totem pole crafted with ones and zeroes.
The bug haunts, a ghost in the void,
Zero becoming one, displacement complete, a story untold.

The crew scribes tales of work and rest,
Of empty messages answered with hopeful breath.
Knives glint in the dimmed lights,
Carving stories as carvers set the wood lights.

The sea outside, our ancestral past,
Carves lands and shores with patient hand at last.
Each wave a line, each tide a flood,
Carving to unravel the earth's deepest fold.

Yet, in this ship, we find our dove,
A place between the sea and the sky above.
Our words, like fish guts, a messy sight,
In the dark of night, they take their flight.

So let the salt and wind take their toll,
Let our voices be heard for the very first time.
For here, on this boat, we find our shore,
A place where art and life carve out their own score.
