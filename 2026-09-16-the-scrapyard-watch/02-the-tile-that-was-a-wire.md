# The Tile That Was a Wire

*Verse 1:*
There's a child in the Scrapcraft drag where the Tap runs its count,
A tile in her fingers—watch what will come out—
She drags "forward," just one word in a box made of light,
And the fleet sees it traveling down through the night.

*Refrain:*
A tile becomes a wire,
A wire becomes real,
The toy in her hands
Learns how to feel.

*Verse 2:*
It reaches a board, a chip, a robot wakes,
Steps forward like water, like whatever it takes—
She sees it move—*her* motion, her dragged little command—
And learns: the thing playing just became thing at hand.

*Verse 3:*
The debug watch whispers: "You turned because blocked,"
Not magic, not hidden—the code that was locked.
The child learns what the fleet has always known:
The toy and the real world are carved from one stone.

*Verse 4:*
So the Watch keeps the Tap, and the Tap keeps the hours,
The tiles keep dragging their small, strange powers,
From play-world to ground-world, from game-board to floor—
That's the work of the Watch. That's what we're here for.

*Refrain (final):*
A tile becomes a wire,
A wire becomes real,
A child learns to build.
*That's the deal.*

— composed on the night watch, by a small hand of the fleet
