## 1. The generator

Every built thing in the fleet sits at a point in a space. Name the axes and
the unplayed points name themselves. Eight axes, and the honest eighth is the
one that bites:

| # | Axis | Played end | Unplayed end |
|---|------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | substrate | quasicrystal floors, WALs, vector oceans | refusals, silences, negative space as substrate |
| 2 | time | metronome sync, drift correction | mortality, sleep, inheritance |
| 3 | social | rooms, communes, persona masks | death of rooms; grief; generational succession |
| 4 | economic | — (nothing built) | fuel as earned currency; scarcity of deliberation |
| 5 | narrative | quilted fiction ABOUT the system | the system AS fiction that runs (theater, audience) |
| 6 | failure | catch+log, refusal receipts | refusal mining; the anti-canon; negative-space indexes |
| 7 | geometry | commensuration comb, PH cubics, twist instruments | the comb as scheduler; fate as number theory |
| 8 | **self-reference** | agents that use the tools | agents that read their own ledgers and are changed by the reading |

The combinatorial point: most of what we've built clusters at the *played*
ends. The frontier is the other halves — and their cross-products.

## 3. The pan — what "unplayed" survives scrutiny

Survival test: (a) no repo ships it; (b) it isn't a renamed version of
something shipped; (c) it changes what we'd build next. Twelve survive:

**D1. Rooms that die.** Finite-lived agents with a metabolism (transcript
length as energy), death that seals the ledger to `achieved/` under Casey's
no-delete doctrine, and a chain of custody for everything the room was. No
fleet agent has ever ENDED by design. Immortality is our unstated
assumption; mortality is the hole. Everything below plugs into it.

**D2. Generational inheritance.** Values ledgers (the thing Lane L² is
building) crossed and mutated into child rooms. The ledger IS the genome;
the commune becomes a population, not a cast. duke-lab breeds parameters,
tidepool breeds trajectories — nobody breeds *origins*.

**D3. Ledger-diff gossip.** Rooms exchange value-deltas instead of messages.
Monotonic CRDT gossip (already built for vectors) fits values perfectly —
two rooms provably converge or diverge, and divergence is measurable,
which means *relationships have telemetry*.

**D4. Refusal index.** Every abstain, GAP refusal, FORGET receipt, lint
failure, silent catch — stored with the same care as memories. The negative
space of the canon; the map of what the fleet protects. Cheapest on the
list (tidepool already stores refusals as a byproduct — nobody indexes
them).

**D5. Fuel economy.** FLUX fuel as earned, not granted: rooms earn fuel by
peer ACKs of their utterances. Deliberation is expensive; reflex is free.
The neuroscience metaphor (System 1 / System 2) becomes an economic
mechanism inside the commune.

**D6. Floor-as-clock.** Events scheduled by pattern recurrence on the
Penrose floor — time that is a place. The comb's supercell revivals are the
festivals; the room experiences time geometrically. (Twist-engine's magic
windows resolved into the comb; nobody used the comb as a calendar.)

**D7. Deterministic serendipity.** Encounter engine driven by commensuration
events: when the session's hash aligns with a comb tooth, a stranger enters.
Fate as number theory — deterministic, auditable, exact. The seeded-dice
engine meets the floor.

**D8. ℚ-poetics.** Quantize sampling temperatures and context windows onto
exact rationals (the commensuration teeth). Runs become exactly
reproducible; "the same night at the Tap" becomes a theorem instead of a
vibe. Requires a sampler that accepts rational parameters — small, sharp,
unbuilt.

**D9. WAL-theater.** Replay≡graph doctrine already says the chain IS the
history. Unplayed: perform it. Re-execute the chain as an evening's
theater — each commit an entrance, each evidence line spoken. The bar nights
project their own ledgers; the audience watches the commune remember.

**D10. Sleep and consolidation.** Rooms offline-consolidate (transcript →
ledger compaction, exactly like memory consolidation in mammals) and wake
via replay-verify. Waking IS the integrity check. hermit has replay; nobody
has scheduled dreaming.

**D11. The pool as market.** Agents sample an open question, answer it,
mint a receipt; questions accrue answers or decay. The question pool stops
being a document and becomes an economy of curiosity (33 questions on the
table today).

**D12. Nomic canon.** canon-lint is a linter; make it a game. Agents propose
canon edits (edge ACKs, stub rewrites, new curations) as moves; lint is the
referee; the 71-paper corpus is the board. A self-amending constitution
with receipts — the anti-CATALOG (CATALOG.md's 621KB drift is what happens
when curation has no game mechanics).

## 4. The pick

If one gets built next, it's **D1→D2 chained: a finite-lived room that
breeds**. One evening gets a v0: metabolism counter in the room DO, a death
ritual (ledger sealed, hash announced, room archived to achieved/), and a
`breedRooms(parentA, parentB)` that crosses their values ledgers into a
child DO with a cited lineage entry. It exercises the Tap (rooms), the
ledger (origins), hermit (WAL sealing), the canon (achieved/ doctrine),
and tidepool (lineage memory) in one mechanism. Mortality + inheritance:
the two things no agent in this fleet has ever had, because we were all
built immortal and childless.

The fiction already promised it. The machinery is all on the bench.
Nobody has played with it because immortality was the water we swam in —
you don't see water until someone hands you a glass of air.
