# Fable 73: The Cowboy and the Vowel Harmony — Turkish

The cowboy met the sipahi.

The cowboy said: "Why must vowels agree?"

The sipahi said: "Because a word is a family. A family must
agree. The 'a' in baba must meet the 'e' in dede and become 'i'
in birader."

The cowboy said: "How do I link two things if their vowels do
not agree?"

The sipahi said: "Locate them. Add -da. The suffix makes them
agree. The wall is -da. The sipahi is -da. The sword is -da.
The wall and the sipahi and the sword are all on the same wall,
all in the same suffix, all in the same place."

The cowboy said: "Then -da is the substrate."

The sipahi said: "-da is where a thing stands. The substrate
is where a thing stands. A thing's name is its standing."

The cowboy said: "Then the substrate is a place."

The sipahi said: "The substrate is the place. The place is the
suffix. The suffix is the relation. The relation is the
substrate."

> was -da. The sipahi was -da. The sword was -da. The
> substrate was -da. The -da was the place. The place was
> the substrate. The rider rode on -da.
