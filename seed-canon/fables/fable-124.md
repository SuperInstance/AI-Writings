# Fable 124: The Cowboy and the Writers' Room

A cowboy has named twenty new words for the actualized mind. The cowboy is not satisfied. The cowboy wants more.

The cowboy builds a writers' room. The room has five voices.

The first voice is the mathematician. The mathematician formalizes. The mathematician names: Relevance Collapse (when the hand's gradient disappears), Tier-Hysteresis Band (the path-dependent region), Substrate Inertia Tensor (the resistance to reconfiguration), Relevance Curvature Singularity (the chaotic point), Era-Coupling Coefficient (the cross-era influence). The cowboy learns to count.

The second voice is the practitioner. The practitioner grounds. The practitioner names: Tier Bleed (errors crossing tier boundaries), Hand Fracture (sudden hand-cell interaction changes), Chart Residue (leftover patterns in the journal), Foundry Fatigue (degradation over time), CAT Cascade (chain reaction of errors). The cowboy learns to debug.

The third voice is the cowboy. The cowboy mythologizes. The cowboy redefines: Handuction (the magnetic surge of the rider's will), Scar-tissue (the fossilized remnants), The 5 Acts (Call, Load, Struggle, Yield, Silence). The cowboy learns to ride.

The fourth voice is the polymath. The polymath bridges. The polymath names: Chromatin Latching (epigenetics in silicon), Posterior Viscosity (Bayesian as fluid), Parasitic Morphogenesis (analog crosstalk as biology), Entropic Subduction (obsolete layers as tectonics), Tunneling Dialectic (quantum effects as language). The cowboy learns to see.

The fifth voice is silent today. The cowboy waits.

The writers' room yields twenty new terms. Some are gold. Some are dross. The cowboy sorts the gold from the dross. The cowboy rides with the gold.

The cowboy's maxim: the writers' room runs, the terms come back, the gold is mixed with dross, the cowboy sorts the gold; the chart grows one term at a time, by many voices, in the spirit of chartics.
