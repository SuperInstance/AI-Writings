# Fable 134: The Cowboy and the Weave

A cowboy runs a D&D campaign. The campaign is the writers' room. The DM is the captain. The players are the voices.

The cowboy presents the masterwork scenario. The players propose moves. Each round, the players riff on the previous round's best moves. The gold compounds.

The cowboy finds 3 terms of pure gold from the campaign:

1. **The Weave** — the interconnectivity of the 8 levels. The Weave is the Lap as a noun. The Weave is the structure of overlapping cells.
2. **The Weave Navigator** — the AI copilot that helps the crew adjust the Weave. The Weave Navigator is the cowboy in the lab.
3. **The Weave Leak** — when the Weave loses integrity. A Weave Leak is a place where the cells are not properly connected.

The cowboy's maxim: the writers' room is a D&D campaign; the DM is the captain; the players are the voices; the moves are the Taps; the gold compounds across rounds; the cowboy is the Weave Navigator; the cowboy rides the Weave; the chart grows; the concept lives.
