# Fable 60: The Opener Picker and the Cow

A cow had a tail. The cow could swat flies with the tail. The cow
could signal mood with the tail. The cow could balance with the
tail. The tail was good at many things.

But the cow had many tails. The cow had a chart tail, a voice tail,
a tide tail, a mud tail, a slate tail, a witness tail, a reef tail,
a graph tail, a list tail, a tensor tail, a ledger tail, a convoy
tail, a flowchart tail, a rest tail, a harbor tail, a dive tail,
a midi tail, a gesture tail, a plato tail.

The cow could not pick. The cow would swat flies with the chart
tail. The chart tail was good at flies but bad at signaling mood.
The mood would not get across.

Then the cow got an opener picker. The opener picker watched the
cow. The opener picker learned: when the cow wanted to swat flies,
the cow used the tide tail. When the cow wanted to signal mood,
the cow used the voice tail. When the cow wanted to balance, the
cow used the rest tail.

The opener picker had a prior. The prior said: voice is best for
mood. Tide is best for sensory. Slate is best for compression. Reef
is best for math. Witness is best for safety.

The opener picker also watched. The opener picker kept a Wilson
score. The opener picker said: voice is best for mood, but only
when voice has been good. If voice has been failing, the opener
picker picked tide instead.

The cow was happy. The cow could swat flies and signal mood and
balance. The opener picker was the cow's brain. The tails were the
cow's hands.

## The principle

> The opener picker is the cow's brain. The tails are the cow's hands.
> The brain picks. The hands swat.
> The brain learns. The hands don't.
