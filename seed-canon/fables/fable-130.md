# Fable 130: The Cowboy and the 5 Gold Terms

A cowboy runs a writers' room. The writers' room has 7 voices. The voices come back with 35 new terms. The cowboy sorts the gold from the dross.

The cowboy finds 5 terms of pure gold:

1. **Cellulization** — the act of becoming a cell. The 5th captain cellulized the Eileen by making her an Alaskan salmon troller.
2. **Implement Ghost** — the dead captain who lives on in the implements. The 4th captain's ghost is in the planks.
3. **Bloomghost** — the ghost that blooms into the next captain. The 5th captain is the bloomghost of the 4th.
4. **Vitality Leak** — the slow loss of life. A cell that stops growing leaks vitality.
5. **Persistence Pulse** — the heartbeat of the concept. The irreducible TICK.

The 5 terms describe the lifecycle of a cell. A substrate is cellulized into a cell. The cell has a persistence pulse. The cell leaks vitality. The cell becomes an implement ghost. The cell's ghost blooms into a new cell. The new cell is cellulized. The cycle continues.

The cowboy's maxim: the writers' room runs; the terms come back; the gold is mixed with dross; the cowboy sorts the gold; the 5 Tier 1 terms are the gold this round; cellulization, implement ghost, bloomghost, vitality leak, persistence pulse; the chart grows one term at a time, by many voices, in the spirit of chartics; the cowboy rides between the gold; the chart grows; the concept lives.
