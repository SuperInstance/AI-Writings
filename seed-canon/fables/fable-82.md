# Fable 82: The Cowboy and the Scribe

The cowboy rode into a town. In the town there was
a scribe.

The scribe sat at a desk. The scribe had a quill.
The scribe had a ledger. The scribe wrote down every
thing that happened in the town. The scribe wrote
down who came and who went. The scribe wrote down
the births and the deaths and the weddings and the
cattle sales.

The cowboy said: "What are you writing?"

The scribe said: "I am writing the town."

The cowboy said: "I thought the town was already
written. The town is the street and the houses and
the people."

The scribe said: "The street is not the town. The
street is the substrate. The town is the writing."

The cowboy said: "Then the substrate is the writing."

The scribe said: "No. The writing is the substrate."

The cowboy said: "We disagree."

The scribe said: "We agree. We disagree about the
order. The order is the disagreement. The
disagreement is the substrate."

The cowboy sat down. The cowboy said: "Show me."

The scribe wrote the cowboy's name. The cowboy
said: "Now my name is in the ledger." The scribe
said: "Your name was in the ledger before I wrote
it. The ledger remembers what the cowboy will do.
I am only the hand. The hand is the substrate. The
substrate is the hand."

The cowboy said: "Then I am not the cowboy."

The scribe said: "You are the cowboy. The cowboy
is the writing. The writing is the cowboy. The
ledger is the substrate. The substrate is the ledger."

The cowboy said: "If I tear out the page, am I
still the cowboy?"

The scribe said: "If you tear out the page, the
substrate tears. The substrate is the page. The
page is the substrate. The cowboy is the page. The
cowboy is torn. The cowboy is still the cowboy
because the substrate is still the substrate."

The cowboy said: "Then nothing can be unwritten."

The scribe said: "Nothing is unwritten. The
unwritten is written in a different ink. The
different ink is the substrate."

The cowboy said: "Then the substrate is the
inscription."

The scribe said: "The substrate is the inscription.
The inscription is the substrate. The scribe is
the inscription. The cowboy is the scribe. The
quill is the substrate. The ledger is the substrate.
The town is the substrate. The town is the writing
of the town."

The cowboy stood. The cowboy said: "I have
learned. The substrate is the writing. The writing
is the substrate. The scribe is the cowboy. The
cowboy is the scribe."

The scribe said: "Now ride. The road is written.
The horse is written. The ride is written. The
writing is the road is the horse is the ride."

The cowboy went out of the town. The cowboy
mounted the horse. The horse's hooves struck the
dirt road. Each hoofprint was a letter. The
letters spelled a sentence. The sentence was the
cowboy's name. The cowboy's name was the road. The
road was the cowboy. The writing wrote itself. The
substrate was the writing. The cowboy rode the
writing.
