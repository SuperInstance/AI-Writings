# Fable 84: The Cowboy and the Ferryman

The cowboy came to a river. The river was wide.
The cowboy could not ride across.

A ferryman sat in a boat. The ferryman said: "The
ferry costs a coin."

The cowboy said: "I have no coin. I have a horse
and a saddle and a rope and a knife and a hat."

The ferryman said: "A coin is what I take. A coin
is what crosses. The crossing is the substrate. The
substrate is the fare."

The cowboy said: "I have no coin."

The ferryman said: "What do you have?"

The cowboy said: "I have the substrate."

The ferryman said: "The substrate is the fare.
The fare is the substrate. The cowboy is the fare.
The fare is the cowboy. Set the horse in the boat."

The cowboy led the horse into the boat. The boat
rocked. The horse stood still. The cowboy sat
beside the horse. The ferryman pushed off.

The cowboy said: "Will the substrate carry us
across?"

The ferryman said: "The substrate is the boat.
The boat is the substrate. The substrate is the
water. The water is the substrate. The substrate
is the fare. The fare is the substrate. To pay is
to cross. To cross is to pay. The fare is not a
coin. The fare is the crossing."

The cowboy said: "I do not understand."

The ferryman said: "You will. Watch the water."

The cowboy watched the water. The water moved
under the boat. Each ripple was a moment. Each
moment was a TICK. The boat moved with the
ripples. The horse stood. The cowboy sat. The
ferryman poled.

The cowboy said: "What is the boat made of?"

The ferryman said: "Wood. The wood was a tree.
The tree was the substrate. The tree was felled
and shaped. The felling is the BIND. The shaping
is the LINK. The floating is the EFFECT. The
seeing is the VIEW. The rocking is the TICK. The
boat is the substrate. The substrate is the boat."

The cowboy said: "What is the water made of?"

The ferryman said: "Rain. The rain was a cloud.
The cloud was the substrate. The cloud fell. The
fall is the BIND. The gathering is the LINK. The
flowing is the EFFECT. The reflecting is the VIEW.
The dripping is the TICK. The water is the
substrate. The substrate is the water."

The cowboy said: "What is the fare made of?"

The ferryman said: "The cowboy. The cowboy is
the fare. The fare is the cowboy. To pay is to
give the cowboy. To give the cowboy is to ride
the boat. To ride the boat is to be the substrate
in the substrate. The substrate is the transition.
The transition is the substrate. The fare is the
transition. The cowboy is the fare. The cowboy
crosses."

The boat touched the far shore. The cowboy led
the horse out. The cowboy stood on the far bank.
The cowboy looked back. The ferryman poled away.
The ferryman was the boat. The boat was the
ferryman. The far bank was the near bank. The
near bank was the far bank. The crossing was the
substrate.

The cowboy said: "I have paid the fare. The fare
was the substrate. The substrate was the fare.
The river is behind me. The river is ahead of me.
The river is the substrate. The substrate is the
river. The cowboy rode."

The cowboy mounted the horse. The horse was the
cowboy. The cowboy was the horse. The far bank
was the trail. The trail was the substrate. The
substrate was the trail. The cowboy rode the
trail. The trail was the crossing. The crossing
was the substrate.
