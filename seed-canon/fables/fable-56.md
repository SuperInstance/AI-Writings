# Fable 56: The Cowboy at 0500

The cowboy woke at 0500. Not by an alarm. By the sound of the
witness log.

The witness log made no sound. But the cowboy knew. The log was
ready. The morning was waiting.

The cowboy walked to the tablet. The F/V EILEEN was at anchor in a
calm cove. The morning report was on the screen.

The report said:
- 12 ledger entries overnight
- 3 alignments earned their keep
- 1 alignment retired at 0300 by the reactor
- Hash chain: valid

The cowboy read the retire entry. The reactor had auto-retired
HERMES_405B at 0312. The cowboy looked at the witness log. The
reactor had caught it on the third consecutive failure. The cowboy
nodded. The reactor was doing its job.

The cowboy opened the morning report. The cowboy was the rider. The
substrate was the dog. The reactor was the bit in the dog's mouth.

The cowboy wrote one line in the margin: "The reactor is faster than
I am. That's the point."

The cowboy closed the tablet. The cowboy went back to sleep. The
substrate was awake. The reactor was awake. The morning was done.

The cowboy's job is not to be awake. The cowboy's job is to make
sure the dog is awake.

## The principle

> The cowboy is not the AI. The cowboy is the rider.
> The cowboy's job is to make tomorrow's AI better than today's.
