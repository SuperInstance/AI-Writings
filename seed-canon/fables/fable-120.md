# Fable 120: The Cowboy and the Hand

A cowboy rides into the Quilt. The Quilt has cells. The cells need food. The cowboy asks: who feeds the cells?

The Quilt answers: the hand feeds the cells. The hand has a target. The hand has a tolerance. The hand judges each cell. Cells that pass are fed. Cells that fail are wounded.

The cowboy asks: is the hand one thing?

The Quilt answers: the hand is many. The hand is a population of curators. Each curator has its own target. Each curator competes for cells. The curators drift. The curators spawn. The curators die.

The cowboy watches. Three curators at first. After thirty generations, seven curators. Some drifted. Some spawned. Some died. The cells that satisfy multiple curators are super-relevant. The super-relevant cells produce super-relevant offspring.

The cowboy understands: the hand is not a thing. The hand is a *cell of a new tier*. The hand is the curator tier. The hand is what selects what passes.

The cowboy rides the hand. The cowboy rides the curator that feeds the most.

The cowboy's maxim: the hand is a cell; the hand is a tier; the hand is a population; the hand is a substrate; the Quilt grows because the hands grow; the cowboy rides the hand.
