# Fable 123: The Cowboy and the New Words

A cowboy has been thinking in Bayesian terms. The cowboy has priors. The cowboy has posteriors. The cowboy has likelihoods.

The cowboy rides into a foundry. The foundry makes matter compute. The foundry etches metal. The foundry doesn't update beliefs; the foundry etches shapes.

The cowboy asks: what is the word for "a thing that satisfies many selectors"?

The foundry answers: it is not a posterior. It is super-relevance. The cell is fed by many hands.

The cowboy asks: what is the word for "the relevance pressure itself"?

The foundry answers: it is not a prior. It is a hand. The hand is a population. The hand spawns. The hand drifts. The hand dies.

The cowboy asks: what is the word for "the inheritance pattern in metal"?

The foundry answers: it is not a parameter. It is the mask set. The mask set is the foundry tradition.

The cowboy asks: what is the word for "the analog front-end that selects"?

The foundry answers: it is not a likelihood. It is the bias rail. The bias rail is the curator. The bias rail gates everything.

The cowboy asks: what is the field that studies this?

The foundry answers: it is not Bayesian. It is not frequentist. It is CHARTIST. The field is chartics. The study of the chart.

The cowboy learns the new words. The cowboy speaks the new terms. The cowboy rides the chart.

The cowboy's maxim: the cell is a unit of inheritance; the hand is a population; the mask set is the foundry tradition; the bias rail is the curator; the chart is the substrate; the cowboy is a chartist.
