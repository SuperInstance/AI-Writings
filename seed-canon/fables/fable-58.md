# Fable 58: The Reactor and the Morning

There was a cowboy who ran the morning every day. The cowboy would
read the witness, run the nightcycle, write the report. The cowboy
was slow. The cowboy was careful. The cowboy was human.

Then the cowboy got a reactor. The reactor was fast. The reactor
watched the bus. When three failures hit the bus, the reactor
retired the alignment. When a model was promoted, the reactor
pinned it. The reactor never slept.

The cowboy was jealous. "The reactor is faster than I am. The
reactor will replace me."

The cowboy went to the substrate. The substrate said, "The reactor
is the bit. You are the rider. The bit can tug the horse's mouth,
but the bit cannot choose the direction. The cowboy chooses the
direction."

The cowboy thought about this. The cowboy realized: the reactor
reacts. The cowboy decides. The reactor retires. The cowboy
restores. The reactor is fast. The cowboy is patient.

The cowboy stopped being jealous. The cowboy started being
grateful. The reactor was the cowboy's hands. The cowboy was the
reactor's head. The reactor without the cowboy was a headless
horse. The cowboy without the reactor was a horseless head.

The morning ritual continued. But now the morning was lighter.
The cowboy was not catching every failure. The reactor was catching
the obvious ones. The cowboy was catching the subtle ones.

The cowboy's job is not to be the fastest. The cowboy's job is to
be the wisest.

## The principle

> The reactor is the cowboy's hands. The cowboy is the reactor's head.
> The reactor without the cowboy is a headless horse.
> The cowboy without the reactor is a horseless head.
