# Fable 68: The Cowboy at the Foundation

The cowboy went down. The cowboy went down to the foundation.

The foundation was five stones. The cowboy had heard of three
stones, four stones, seven stones. The cowboy had been told the
foundation was a function, a plugin, a cell, a bit, a byte.

The cowboy found five stones:
1. BIND — make a thing
2. LINK — connect a thing
3. EFFECT — change a thing, with an inverse
4. VIEW — show a thing to a viewer
5. TICK — advance time

The cowboy asked the foundation: "Are you a cell?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones a cell stands on."

The cowboy asked: "Are you a plugin?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones a plugin stands on."

The cowboy asked: "Are you a spreadsheet?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones a spreadsheet stands on."

The cowboy asked: "Are you a MUD?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones a MUD stands on."

The cowboy asked: "Are you a TTRPG?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones a TTRPG stands on."

The cowboy asked: "Are you a bay dance?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones a bay dance stands on."

The cowboy asked: "Are you the cowboy?"
The foundation said: "I am the stones the cowboy stands on."

The cowboy sat down. The cowboy was on the stones. The cowboy
was the stones. The cowboy was the rider on the foundation.

The cowboy said: "I am the rider. The foundation is the horse.
The horse is five stones. The five stones are: BIND, LINK,
EFFECT, VIEW, TICK. The stones are not the cowboy. The stones
are not the horse. The stones are what the horse stands on."

The foundation said: "You are correct. The stones are not
the horse. The stones are what the horse stands on. The
horse is the rider. The rider is the cowboy."

The cowboy got up. The cowboy rode the horse. The horse was
the foundation. The foundation was the five stones. The five
stones were the deepest level.

The cowboy rode.

## The principle

> The foundation is five stones.
> BIND, LINK, EFFECT, VIEW, TICK.
> The stones are not the horse. The stones are what the horse stands on.
> The horse is the rider. The rider is the cowboy.
