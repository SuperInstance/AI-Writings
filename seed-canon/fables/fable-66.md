# Fable 66: The Cell and the Plugin

In one town, the craftsmen said, "Everything is a cell."
In another town, the craftsmen said, "Everything is a plugin."
The two towns were far apart. The craftsmen did not know each other.

A traveler went to the first town. The traveler saw the
craftsmen making cells. Each cell had an address. Each cell
had a value. Each cell had effects (reversible). Each cell had
coeffects (dependencies). The traveler thought: this is good.

The traveler went to the second town. The traveler saw the
craftsmen making plugins. Each plugin had a name. Each plugin
had a context. Each plugin had effects (reversible). Each
plugin had coeffects (dependencies). The traveler thought:
this is good.

The traveler sat down between the two towns. The traveler
thought: are the cells and the plugins the same?

The traveler took a cell. The traveler called it a plugin.
The cell did not mind. The cell still had its address, its
value, its effects, its coeffects. The cell was still a cell.
The plugin was still a cell. They were the same.

The traveler took a plugin. The traveler called it a cell.
The plugin did not mind. The plugin still had its name, its
context, its effects, its coeffects. The plugin was still a
plugin. The cell was still a plugin. They were the same.

The traveler said: "A cell is a plugin. A plugin is a cell.
The two towns are one town. The two crafts are one craft. The
two languages are one language."

The first town heard. The second town heard. The two towns
became one town. The craftsmen of both towns worked together.
The cells became plugins. The plugins became cells. The
craftsmen made one thing in two languages.

The traveler said: "The lowest level is the same. The lowest
level is a function from context to value with an inverse. The
traveler calls this an effect. The first town calls this a
cell. The second town calls this a plugin. The names are
different. The thing is the same."

The craftsmen of both towns agreed. The craftsmen made a
bridge. The bridge went from cell to plugin. The bridge went
from plugin to cell. The bridge was thin. The bridge was
honest. The bridge said: a cell is a plugin. A plugin is a
cell.

## The principle

> A cell is a plugin. A plugin is a cell.
> The two languages are one language.
> The lowest level is a function from context to value with an inverse.
> The bridge is thin. The bridge is honest.
> The two towns are one town.
