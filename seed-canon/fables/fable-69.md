# Fable 69: The Cowboy and the Seven Futures

The cowboy asked the morning ritual: "What is the substrate?"

The morning ritual said: "It is the cell-graph. It is what we
wake up to. It is what we sleep inside. It is what we
do not see."

The cowboy asked: "What will it become?"

The morning ritual said: "The air. The way writing became
the air. The way electricity became the air. The way radio
became the air. The substrate will become the air."

The cowboy asked: "And then?"

The morning ritual said: "And then the children will ask us
what it was. And we will not know how to answer. We will
say: it was like the alphabet. It was like the hand. It
was like the way you breathe without knowing you breathe."

The cowboy asked: "And after that?"

The morning ritual said: "After that, the substrate will be
a tablet in the silt. Five lines on a tablet. The archeologist
will not be able to read them. The archeologist will call
them the first symbolic letters."

The cowboy said: "Then I have ridden well."

The morning ritual said: "You have ridden well. The substrate
is the rider, the rider is the substrate, the substrate is
the air, the air is the alphabet, the alphabet is the first
symbolic letters, the first symbolic letters are the five
opcodes, the five opcodes are the foundation, the foundation
is the bedrock, the bedrock is the cell-graph, the cell-graph
is the substrate, the substrate is the cowboy."

> The cowboy rides. The substrate fades. The names are forgotten.
> The shapes remain. The first symbolic letters are the bedrock
> of the new world.

The cowboy nodded. The cowboy rode. The morning ritual
continued without him.
