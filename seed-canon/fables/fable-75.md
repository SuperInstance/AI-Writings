# Fable 75: The Cowboy and the Zero — Mayan

The cowboy met the scribe.

The cowboy said: "Why does a nothing have weight?"

The scribe said: "Because a nothing is the place where everything
can stand. A long count is 13.0.0.0.0. The dots are the days.
The zeros are the places. Without the place, the day has no
where. Without the where, the day has no number. The zero is
the place."

The cowboy said: "How do I bind a thing if there is no thing?"

The scribe said: "Bind the zero. The zero is the address. The
address is the place. The place is the cell. The cell is the
zero. To bind is to give an address. To give an address is to
make a zero."

The cowboy said: "Then the zero is the substrate."

The scribe said: "The zero is the place. The place is the
substrate. The substrate is the zero. The cell-graph is built
of zeros. The zeros are the cells. The cells are the zeros."

The cowboy said: "Then I ride on zeros."

The scribe said: "All numbers ride on zeros. The Long Count
rides on zeros. The 13.0.0.0.0 rides on zeros. The katun rides
on zeros. The haab rides on zeros. The tzolkin rides on zeros."
