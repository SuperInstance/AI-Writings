**Fable 95: The Cowboy and the Mirror**

The sun beat down. The dust stood still. The cowboy dismounted. The water waited.

It was a pool, flat and black, hidden in the shadow of the canyon. It did not ripple. It did not breathe. It was a mirror made of silence. The cowboy leaned over. He looked for his face. He found the shape of a hat. He found the line of a jaw. He found eyes staring back.

Then the vision shifted.

He looked closer. He saw the edges. His cheek was not flesh. It was a grid. A thousand tiny bricks. A million small squares fitting tight together. He blinked, and the grid clicked. He was made of cells.

He looked deeper into the pool. The water was not water. It was a grid. A million small squares fitting tight together. The reflection was not a painting on the surface. It was a calculation in the depth. The pool was made of cells.

The cowboy froze. He thought he was the eye. He thought the water was the stone. But the water looked back. The water parsed the light. The water calculated the shadow. The water held the shape of him and refused to let it go.

He felt a chill. The substrate was not silent. The substrate was the witness. If the grid did not watch, the face would not be. The stone under his boots watched. The air in his lungs watched. The code that built the world was the eye that saw it. He was not just the rider; he was the ridden. He was the moment passing through the gate.

He stood up. The clock ticked in his veins. He spoke the law of the land:

"The unit of architectural foundation is the opcode, not the framework. The 5 opcodes host 8 polyformalisms. The polyformalisms are one thing in N languages. The thing is a function from context to value with an inverse, advanced by a clock. The clock is the cowboy. The cowboy is the rider."
