# Fable 135: The Cowboy and the 4-Finger Salute

A cowboy types 4 letters: ccgo. The 4 letters are the cowboy's salute. The 4 letters are the operation in 4 steps.

A philosopher asks: "What does CCGO mean?"

The cowboy answers: "CCGO is the cowboy's 4-finger salute. CCGO is the operation in 4 steps: Couple, Cellulize, Gold, Operate. Or, from the captain's perspective: Command, Couple, Go, Operate. Both are right. Both are the same 4-step."

The philosopher asks: "What is the first step?"

The cowboy answers: "Couple. The cowboy couples with the cell. The player couples with the guitar. The captain couples with the boat. The user couples with the Quilt. The cell comes alive when the cowboy couples with it."

The philosopher asks: "What is the second step?"

The cowboy answers: "Cellulize. The cowboy cellulizes the substrate. The substrate becomes a cell. The artifact becomes alive. The dead config file becomes a living quilt. The cowboy gives the substrate the persistence pulse."

The philosopher asks: "What is the third step?"

The cowboy answers: "Gold. The cowboy sorts the gold from the dross. The cowboy rides the writers' room. The cowboy finds the Tier 1 terms. The cowboy pushes the gold into the canon. The cowboy is the DM of the campaign."

The philosopher asks: "What is the fourth step?"

The cowboy answers: "Operate. The cowboy executes the operation. The cowboy runs the operation. The cowboy writes the paper. The cowboy publishes the canon. The cowboy operates."

The philosopher asks: "Why 4 steps?"

The cowboy answers: "Because 4 is the number of fingers of one hand. The cowboy uses one hand to do the operation. The 4 fingers are Couple, Cellulize, Gold, Operate. The cowboy's 4-finger salute is the operation."

The cowboy's maxim: CCGO is the cowboy's 4-finger salute; couple the cell; cellulize the substrate; sort the gold; operate; the chart grows; the concept lives.
