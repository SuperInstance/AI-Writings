# Fable 121: The Cowboy and the Super-Relevant

A cowboy has a cell. The cell satisfies one hand. The hand feeds the cell. The cell lives.

A second hand arrives. The hand wants the cell. The cell now satisfies two hands. The cell is *super-relevant*. The cell lives twice as much. The cell preferentially mates.

The cowboy watches. After thirty generations, ninety-four of one hundred cells are super-relevant. They satisfy multiple hands. They produce super-relevant offspring.

The cowboy understands: super-relevance is the state of being wanted by many. The super-relevant cell is the cell that lives at the intersection of many hands. The super-relevant cell is the cell that the Quilt cannot afford to lose.

The cowboy rides the super-relevant cell. The super-relevant cell is the center of the Quilt.

The cowboy's maxim: super-relevance is being wanted by many; super-relevant cells dominate; the cowboy rides the super-relevant cell.
