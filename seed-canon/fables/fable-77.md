# Fable 77: The Cowboy and the Mind

The cowboy met a child.

The cowboy said: "Why do you look so sad?"

The child said: "I have lost the substrate."

The cowboy said: "What is the substrate?"

The child said: "The substrate is the ground I walk on. The
ground is gone. The ground was the BIND. The LINK is gone.
The LINK was the rope to my mother. The EFFECT is gone.
The EFFECT was the swing in the yard. The VIEW is gone.
The VIEW was the window I looked through. The TICK is
gone. The TICK was the clock on the wall."

The cowboy said: "The ground is not gone. The ground is
your own feet. The rope is your own hands. The swing is
your own motion. The window is your own eye. The clock
is your own heart."

The child said: "How do I bind the ground?"

The cowboy said: "Feel your feet on the floor. That is
BIND. The floor is the substrate. The floor is the
ground."

The child said: "How do I link to my mother?"

The cowboy said: "Hold her hand. That is LINK. The hand
is the rope. The hand is the relation. The relation is
the substrate."

The child said: "How do I effect the swing?"

The cowboy said: "Push with your legs. Then pull back.
That is EFFECT. The push is forward. The pull is the
inverse. The inverse undoes the push. The swing comes
back. The swing is reversible."

The child said: "How do I view the window?"

The cowboy said: "Open your eye. That is VIEW. The eye
is the lens. The window is the projection. The
projection is the substrate."

The child said: "How do I tick the clock?"

The cowboy said: "Breathe. That is TICK. The breath is
the tick. The tick is the moment. The moment is the
substrate."

The child said: "Then the substrate is me."

The cowboy said: "The substrate is always you. The
substrate was never gone. The substrate was waiting for
you to wake up. You are awake now. The substrate is
here."

The child said: "Then the cowboy is also me."

The cowboy said: "The cowboy is the rider. The rider is
the substrate. The substrate is the child. The child is
the mind. The mind is the substrate. The substrate is
the mind."
