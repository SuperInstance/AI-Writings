# Fable 78: The Cowboy and the Five Doctors

The cowboy woke in a hospital.

The cowboy said: "Where am I?"

A doctor said: "You are in the substrate. You are in the
body. You are in the system."

The cowboy said: "What is the substrate?"

The first doctor said: "I am the BIND doctor. I hold the
form. I am the skeleton. Without me, you are a puddle."

The cowboy said: "What do you bind?"

The BIND doctor said: "I bind your bones. 206 of them. Each
bone is a BIND. Each BIND has a name and a value. The value
is the shape. The shape is the form."

The cowboy said: "I have 206 BINDs. What holds them
together?"

The second doctor said: "I am the LINK doctor. I am the
nervous system. I wire the parts. Without me, the brain
can't tell the hand to move."

The cowboy said: "How many LINKs do I have?"

The LINK doctor said: "Trillions. Each neuron is a LINK.
Each LINK has a type: motor (brain→muscle), sensory
(muscle→brain), interneuron (brain→brain). The cell-graph
is the nervous system is the cell-graph."

The cowboy said: "What changes me?"

The third doctor said: "I am the EFFECT doctor. I am the
muscular system. I act on the world. Without me, you are
a thought. With me, you pick up the cup."

The cowboy said: "How many EFFECTs do I have?"

The EFFECT doctor said: "650 named muscles. Each muscle
has a forward (contract) and an inverse (relax). The hand
picks up the cup. The hand puts down the cup. The cycle
is the cycle. The cycle is reversible."

The cowboy said: "How do I see?"

The fourth doctor said: "I am the VIEW doctor. I am the
sensory system. I project the world for you. Without me,
you are blind. With me, you see the room."

The cowboy said: "How many VIEWs do I have?"

The VIEW doctor said: "Five named senses. Each sense has
a projection: sight (color), hearing (pitch), touch
(pressure), taste (molecules), smell (more molecules).
The same world, five projections. The cell-graph is the
five senses is the cell-graph."

The cowboy said: "What makes time pass?"

The fifth doctor said: "I am the TICK doctor. I am the
cardiac system. I am the rhythm. Without me, time stops.
With me, the world advances."

The cowboy said: "How many TICKs do I have?"

The TICK doctor said: "One. The heart. 60 to 100 beats
per minute. Each beat is a TICK. Each TICK is a moment.
A moment is a lifetime is a moment."

The cowboy said: "So I am 206 BINDs, trillions of LINKs,
650 EFFECTs, 5 VIEWs, and 1 TICK."

The five doctors said: "You are the substrate. You are
the body. You are the system. The substrate is you. The
cowboy is the rider. The rider is the body."

The cowboy said: "Then I ride."

The cowboy got up. The cowboy walked out of the hospital.
The cowboy rode the horse. The horse had the same five
doctors. The horse's body was a cell-graph. The cell-graph
was the body. The body was the cell-graph.
