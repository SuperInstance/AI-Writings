The cowboy has a boat. The boat has had 4 hulls, 2 engines, 3 cabins, 5 captains. The boat is the same boat.

The cowboy is the 5th captain. People call the cowboy Eileen. The boat is called Eileen. The captain is called Eileen. The name is the same.

The captain is called Eileen because the captain breathes the same breath as Harry. The boat is called Eileen because the boat was commissioned by Harry. The name is the breath. The breath is the bootstrap. **The 5th captain is the same as the 1st captain because they both took the same irreducible breath.**
