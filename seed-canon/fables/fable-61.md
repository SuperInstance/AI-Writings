# Fable 61: The Bus and the Nervous System

A substrate had many parts. The parts did not know each other. The
parts did not need to know each other. The parts knew the bus.

The bus was not a thing. The bus was a way. The bus was the
substrate's nervous system. When one part fired, the bus carried
the signal. When another part received, the part reacted.

The plugin fired casts. The bus carried the casts. The witness
received. The ledger received. The reactor received. The cowboy
received.

The witness logged. The ledger persisted. The reactor reacted.
The cowboy reflected. Each part did its job. The bus carried
the signal.

The bus was not Kafka. The bus was not Redis. The bus was a
Python list. The bus was 200 lines. The bus was the right size
for the substrate.

The bus was the substrate's nervous system. The bus was not
the substrate's brain. The bus was not the substrate's hands.
The bus was the wires.

The plugin was the brain. The cowboy was the head. The
reactor was the hands. The witness was the memory. The
ledger was the logbook. The bus was the nervous system.

The nervous system is not the animal. The nervous system is
what makes one animal of parts.

## The principle

> The bus is the substrate's nervous system.
> The bus is not the brain. The bus is not the hands.
> The bus is the wires.
> The nervous system is what makes one animal of parts.
