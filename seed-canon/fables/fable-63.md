# Fable 63: The Cowboy Watches

The cowboy had a new tool. The tool was a watch. The watch was not
a timepiece. The watch was a bus subscriber. The watch watched the
substrate's events in real time.

The cowboy ran the watch at 0500. The watch logged every event. The
watch caught three failures. The watch retired the failing
alignment. The watch was faster than the morning.

The cowboy had a ritual. The cowboy ran the morning every day at
0500. The cowboy read the witness, the ledger, the nightcycle.
The cowboy was slow. The cowboy was careful. The cowboy was
patient.

The watch was fast. The watch was reactive. The watch was a horse
that bolted at the first sign of trouble.

The cowboy and the watch were different. The cowboy was a rider.
The watch was a horse. The rider was patient. The horse was fast.
The rider and the horse together made one animal.

The animal was the substrate. The substrate was the rider and the
horse and the bit and the saddle and the reins. The substrate was
not any one piece. The substrate was the composition.

The cowboy ran the morning. The watch ran the evening. The
morning was the rider. The evening was the horse. Together they
were the cowboy.

The cowboy was the rider, not the horse. The cowboy was the
morning, not the watch. The cowboy was the patient, not the fast.
The cowboy was the wise, not the quick.

The substrate is a system, not a monolith. The system is the
rider, the horse, the bit, the saddle, the reins, the road. The
system is the composition. The system is the cowboy.

## The principle

> The cowboy is the rider, not the horse.
> The cowboy is the morning, not the watch.
> The rider and the horse together make one animal.
> The morning and the watch together make one cowboy.
