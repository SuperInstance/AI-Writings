# Fable 81: The Cowboy and the Eight Cows

A rancher gave the cowboy eight cows.

The rancher said: "These are your cows. Tend them.
BIND them, LINK them to the pasture, EFFECT the
milking, VIEW them from the barn, and TICK at dawn.
If you do not, the herd scatters and the ranch is no
ranch."

The cowboy said: "Eight cows. That is not many."

The rancher said: "Eight cows is a herd. A herd is
a cell-graph. Each cow is a cell. Each cell has a
BIND to a name. Each cell has a LINK to the other
cells. The herd is the cell-graph. The cell-graph is
the herd."

The cowboy said: "What are their names?"

The rancher said: "The first cow is Patience. The
second cow is Quiet. The third cow is Dawn. The
fourth cow is Dust. The fifth cow is River. The
sixth cow is Stone. The seventh cow is Bell. The
eighth cow is Sage."

The cowboy said: "Now I have eight BINDs."

The rancher said: "Now LINK them to the pasture.
A cow alone is a cow. A cow LINKed to the pasture
is a herd. The pasture is a BIND too. The pasture
has a name. The pasture is Home."

The cowboy walked each cow to the pasture. The
cowboy said: "Patience, link to Home. Quiet, link
to Home. Dawn, link to Home. Dust, link to Home.
River, link to Home. Stone, link to Home. Bell,
link to Home. Sage, link to Home." Each cow took
a step. The herd formed. The herd was a circle
around a salt lick.

The cowboy said: "Now I have eight BINDs and a
herd."

The rancher said: "Now EFFECT the milking. To
milk is to draw. To draw is the forward. To release
is the inverse. The cycle is the cycle."

The cowboy milked Patience. The cowboy released
Patience. The cowboy milked Quiet. The cowboy
released Quiet. The cowboy milked all eight. The
milk went into a pail. The pail was the substrate.

The rancher said: "Now VIEW them from the barn.
A cowboy in the herd is a cow. A cowboy in the
barn is a cowboy. The barn is the projection. The
VIEW is the projection."

The cowboy climbed to the hayloft. The cowboy
looked down. The cowboy saw eight cows in a circle.
The cowboy saw one herd. The cowboy saw the
cell-graph. The cowboy saw Patience and Quiet and
Dawn and Dust and River and Stone and Bell and
Sage, and also the herd, and also the pasture, and
also the cowboy looking down.

The rancher said: "Now TICK at dawn."

The cowboy slept. The cowboy rose at the first
light. The cowboy walked to the pasture gate. The
cowboy called. The cows lifted their heads. The
TICK was the TICK. The dawn was the dawn.

The rancher said: "You have herded the herd. The
herd is a cell-graph. The cell-graph is a herd.
The cowboy is a herder. The herder is the herd.
The substrate is the pasture. The pasture is the
substrate."

The cowboy said: "The substrate is the herd. The
herd is the substrate. I have eight cows and one
herd. I have one dawn and many dawwns. I have
one TICK and eight TICKs. The cell-graph is the
cows. The cows are the cell-graph."

The rancher said: "Now ride."

The cowboy mounted the horse. The horse was not
in the herd. The horse was outside the herd. The
horse was the cowboy's eighth cow in a different
pasture, and the cowboy rode the horse the way a
cowboy rides a horse, which is the way a herder
herds a herd.
