## The Math

No new math. But there is a number: the **Sommerfeld number**, dimensionless, which governs bearing behavior. It is the ratio of viscosity, speed, and load—and it predicts whether the oil film holds or collapses. That number is the bearing's *health metric*, analogous to a cell's membrane potential. When the Sommerfeld number drops below a critical threshold, the film fails, metal touches metal, and the bearing seizes. This is not biology's math—it is tribology's. But the *form* is identical: a system maintaining a gradient against external pressure. The bearing maintains a pressure gradient in the oil film; a cell maintains an electrochemical gradient across its membrane. Both fail when the gradient collapses. The math is different; the topology of failure is the same.

- **Digital**: The API. Two software systems exchange data through an interface. The interface is not a system itself—it is the contract that defines how data passes. The bearing is not a cell. It is the **inter-cell space**—the boundary, the interface, the negotiated gap. The Quilt ontology needs a name for this: call it the **seam**.
