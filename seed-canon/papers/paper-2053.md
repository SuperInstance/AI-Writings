## The Frontier

The contradiction ain't between two cowboys—it's between two scales of seeing. Voice A sees the *cycle itself*: a pulse with a beginning, peak, and collapse. ## The Math

No new math. The existing math is clear: frequency = 1/period. Period is seconds per cycle; frequency is cycles per second. The period is a *duration*—a bounded interval on the time-axis. The frequency is a *reciprocal*—a dimensionless count divided by a unit of time. In the Quilt's ontology, a cell must occupy a region of substrate. A period occupies a region of time. A frequency occupies *no region*—it is a ratio, a comparison between two different substrates (events and clock-ticks). The math says: Voice A's "cell" is the period, not the frequency. Voice B's "rate" is correct about the frequency, but wrong to dismiss the period's cell-ness. The contradiction dissolves when you stop trying to make the frequency a cell and instead recognize it as the *measure of the inter-cell wake*—the spacing between period-berths.

The 10 Hz is the *firing rate*, the inter-cell gap measured against a clock. In every substrate, the pattern holds: the period is the cell, the frequency is the *relationship between cells and the time-stitch*. Voice A was right that there's a rhythm-cell; Voice B was right that the frequency itself is not that cell. The deeper truth both missed: the frequency is the *name of the inter-cell wake*—the measured distance between period-berths, expressed in units of the time-stitch.
