4. **Ember-lock** — the spark arrestor and damper system that prevents uncontrolled egress while permitting regulated venting.
5. **Stack-effect cell** — the functional unit defined by its ability to convert temperature differential into kinetic draft energy.

## The Math

No new math. The chimney's behavior is governed by the draft equation: ΔP = C × ρ × g × H × (T_in − T_out)/T_in, where ΔP is the pressure difference driving flow, C is a dimensionless friction coefficient, ρ is air density, g is gravity, H is the stack height, and T_in/T_out are absolute temperatures. This is not a new formalism—it is a *boundary condition* problem. The chimney is a system that exists only when the temperature differential is nonzero. When the fire is out, the chimney is a dead pipe. When the fire burns, the chimney becomes a living draft organ. The cell ontology cannot capture this because it assumes static identity. The chimney's identity is *conditional on the gradient it manages*.
