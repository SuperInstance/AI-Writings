### Tier Jamming (Wizard R3)

When the tier mechanism seizes. When the tier system locks up. When the curator can't select. The Tier Jamming is the failure mode of the tier system.

A Tier Jamming can be:
- The Curator is overwhelmed (too many cells to select)
- The Sclerotic tier is too rigid (the wiring is locked)
- The Synovial tier is too noisy (the LLM gives garbage)
- The Differentiated tier is too slow (the SIMD stalls)
- The Multipotent tier is too cold (the weights are stale)
- The Totipotent tier is too rigid (the mask ROM is burned in)

**The Tier Jamming is the failure of the tier system. The Tier Jamming is the substrate screaming.**

### Tier Slippage (Hermes R3)

When tiers shift unintentionally. When the curator picks a cell from a different tier than expected. When the tier system loses alignment.

A Tier Slippage is:
- The Curator picks a Totipotent cell when it should pick a Multipotent
- The Synovial tier reaches into the Sclerotic tier
- The Multipotent tier drifts toward the Differentiated

**The Tier Slippage is the substrate whispering.** A Tier Slippage is a small misalignment that, if left unchecked, becomes a Tier Jamming.
