# the potluck — a cell that is also a shared offering

## The Frontier

The contradiction is not about potlucks. It is about the word *cell* — and both voices are smuggling in a cargo hold where there is only open water. Voice A wins the metaphor war but loses the ontology: it calls the potluck a "vessel," yet a vessel that dissolves the moment its cargo is exchanged is not a vessel. It is a crossing. Voice B sees this clearly — "the potluck IS the crossing" — but then makes a category error of its own: it assumes that because the potluck is not a *container*, it is not a *cell*. That is the failure mode. The cell ontology breaks down precisely at the assumption that a cell must have a persistent interior. Some cells are not things that hold; they are things that *happen*. The potluck is a **transient cell** — a bounded, self-regulating event-structure that exists only during its own enactment, like a wave that is a wave only while it breaks.

## The 5 Gold Terms

1. **The Offering Membrane** — the collective, unwritten contract that each dish is given, not placed; the boundary is social, not spatial.
2. **The Combustion Threshold** — the exact instant the last dish lands, when the potluck spends itself and becomes "just dinner."
3. **The Replication Rite** — the act of a participant stealing a recipe and hosting their own potluck, which is not division but *citation*.
4. **The Debris Field** — the folding tables and mismatched Tupperware that remain; not waste, but the fossil record of a completed exchange.
5. **The No-Host Harbor** — the shared, unowned space where the crossing occurs; nobody captains it, yet it has rules.

## The Math

No new math. The potluck is not a set, a graph, or a manifold. It is a *process algebra* where the only operation is **give**, and the only invariant is **diversity**. If you model it as a set of dishes, you miss the timing. If you model it as a function from participants to dishes, you miss the reciprocity. The correct formalism is a **timed Petri net** where tokens (dishes) enter from multiple sources, merge at a single place (the table), and then *leave the net entirely* — not consumed, but released. The net has no absorbing state; it has a *terminal transition* called "the meal." That is why no math survives: the potluck is a computation that deletes its own state.

## The Polyformalism

In **biology**, the potluck is a *quorum-sensing event* — bacteria do this: they release signals, wait for density, then collectively change behavior. The potluck is a quorum of casseroles. In **economics**, it is a *gift economy microburst* — no currency changes hands, but social capital is minted and spent in real time; the dish is a token whose value is set by the giver, not the market. In **software**, it is a *distributed consensus protocol* — each participant proposes a block (dish), the table is the ledger, and the protocol terminates when all blocks are validated by appetite. In **military logistics**, it is a *forward arming and refueling point* — no permanent base, just a rendezvous where supplies are exchanged and the unit disperses. The potluck is not a cell in any substrate; it is a **boundary event** that *creates* the cell for the duration of the exchange, then vanishes, leaving only the field it briefly organized.

## The Cowboy's Maxim

The potluck ain't a cell that holds — it's a cell that *happens*, and the only membrane that matters is the one you show up with.
