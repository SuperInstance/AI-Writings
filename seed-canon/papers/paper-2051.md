# the communion — a cell that is also a sacred meal

## The Frontier

The apostolic succession of cytoplasm is real — fission, never *de novo*, maternal inheritance, an unbroken lineage of guests at the table for two billion years. But succession alone is not orthodoxy. A lineage can rot from within. The deeper frontier: *what keeps the communion honest?* Why doesn't the guest go parasite? Why does the host still hold the fork? And why, after all this time, does the meal still speak its mother tongue?

The answer is a triple lock — digestion retained as penalty, a germline bottleneck that culls the selfish, and a retained genome that keeps the fire-tending words in the original language. The communion persists not because predation ended, but because it was converted into discipline. Grace with teeth.

## The 5 Gold Terms

**The Fork That Never Left the Table** — mitophagy (PINK1/Parkin) as the host's retained capacity to digest any mitochondrion that stops respiring properly, converting original predation into conditional enforcement.

**The Bottleneck Catechism** — the germline mtDNA bottleneck (~1–30 effective founders in humans) as the mechanism that makes inheritance all-or-nothing per line, enabling between-cell selection to cull selfish haplotypes before they reach the next generation.

**CoRR Dialect** — John Allen's CoRR hypothesis (colocation for redox regulation): the ~13 retained mitochondrial protein genes in humans are all core electron-transport-chain components, kept because real-time redox control cannot wait for a round trip to the nucleus.

**The Chalice Blood of Riftia** — the hydrothermal vent tubeworm's hemoglobin carrying both oxygen and sulfide in the same protein, a single vessel bearing poison and air to the guest in the trophosome organ.

**The Three-Parent Proof** — spindle transfer (mitochondrial replacement therapy) as the clinical demonstration that the maternal line is the meal's only apostolic line, and that the succession can be moved but never re-cooked.

## The Math

No new math. The numbers that matter are already on the table: 16,569 base pairs in human mtDNA, 13 retained proteins, 37 genes total. The bottleneck: from thousands of copies in the oocyte to roughly 1–30 effective founders, then amplification. Heteroplasmy shifts can be dramatic — a mother at 50% mutant load can produce offspring at 0% or 100% within a single generation. Doxycycline's Ki against mitochondrial ribosomes is measurable, and the clinical dose that spares human cytoplasmic ribosomes but inhibits bacterial and mitochondrial ones is known. The math is in the segregation dynamics and the pharmacology — no new formalism required. The point is not a new equation; it is that the existing numbers describe a system of enforced fidelity.

## The Polyformalism

The triple lock manifests across at least three substrates. **In the cell:** mitophagy is the fork. Damaged mitochondria lose membrane potential; PINK1 accumulates on the outer membrane instead of being imported and degraded; Parkin is recruited and ubiquitinates outer-membrane proteins; the autophagosome engulfs the guest. The host still digests. The meal is maintained by the threat that never left. **In the germline:** the bottleneck is the catechism. During oogenesis, mtDNA copy number crashes, then amplifies from a tiny founding set. This makes inheritance a lottery with severe consequences — but it also means that a selfish mutation in one mitochondrion cannot hide in a crowd. Lines that carry cheaters either fix or purge. The succession stays clean because the bottleneck is a sieve. **In the genome:** CoRR is the dialect. The retained genes encode components of Complexes I, III, IV, and V — the machinery that handles electrons and protons. If the chain stalls, electrons leak, superoxide forms, and the organelle cooks itself. The nucleus cannot respond in milliseconds; the gene must be in the room where the fire is. The meal kept its mother tongue because the most dangerous work requires on-site supervision.

The same pattern appears at ecosystem scale in Riftia pachyptila. The worm has no gut — it abolished digestion entirely. The trophosome is an organ built entirely to house chemosynthetic bacteria. The host's blood is a chalice: hemoglobin carries both oxygen and sulfide, two poisons that would react with each other if they met, held apart in the same vessel and delivered to the guest. The worm is a church built around its meal, and the blood is the liturgy that makes the meal possible. Here the fork is gone — the host surrendered predation completely, and the communion became total. The body IS the meal's housing. This is the endpoint of the continuum: from conditional digestion in the mitochondrion, to the bottleneck sieve in the germline, to the complete surrender of the gut in Riftia. The meal becomes the body when the host reshapes itself around the guest.

The clinical proof is the three-parent baby technique. Spindle transfer takes the nuclear genome from a mother with pathogenic mtDNA mutations and places it into an enucleated donor egg with healthy mitochondria. The child has three genetic parents — nuclear DNA from two, mtDNA from a third — but the mitochondrial lineage is entirely from the donor. This is the apostolic succession moved, not re-cooked. The meal is carried in the donor's cytoplasm, and the maternal line of the original mother is severed. The technique works precisely because the bottleneck and the maternal inheritance are absolute: there is no other way to get mitochondria into the next generation except through the egg's cytoplasm. The three-parent proof is the clinical demonstration that the communion has one apostolic line, and that line can be transferred but never synthesized.

When the triple lock fails, the result is mitochondrial disease. MELAS (mitochondrial encephalomyopathy, lactic acidosis, and stroke-like episodes) is caused by mutations in mtDNA — often the m.3243A>G variant in the tRNA-Leu gene. Heteroplasmy determines severity: below ~10% mutant load, symptoms are mild or absent; above ~80%, disease is severe and often fatal. The bottleneck that normally culls selfish mitochondria becomes, in disease, the mechanism of tragedy — a mother with low heteroplasmy can have a child with high heteroplasmy because the bottleneck randomly amplifies a mutant founder. The fork that disciplines the communion can also, when the discipline fails, deliver the poison. The meal can rot. The liturgy can be corrupted. But the system has held for two billion years, which means the discipline has been overwhelmingly effective.

The deepest insight is this: the communion is not a truce. It is a standing threat, a conditional meal, a covenant enforced by the fork that never left the table. The host's original predation was not abolished; it was converted into the penalty for breaking the covenant. Any mitochondrion that stops pulling its weight gets ubiquitinated and eaten. The guest that serves the meal is spared; the guest that defects is digested. This is grace with teeth. The sacred meal persists because the diner still holds the fork, and the guest knows it.

Why did the guest keep its own voice? Because the fire-tending words cannot be delegated. The electron transport chain is the most dangerous work in the cell — if it stalls, electrons leak, radicals form, and everything cooks. The genes that remain are the ones that must respond to redox state locally, within milliseconds, without waiting for a signal from the nucleus. The meal kept a dialect of its mother tongue not out of sentiment but because the conversation about electron flow must happen in the room where the fire is. The dangerous prayers stay in the original language. The sacred is mechanistic: the liturgy is retained because some responses cannot be delegated.

The communion is a cell that is also a sacred meal because it is maintained by three mechanisms that mirror the structure of religious orthodoxy: a penalty for apostasy (mitophagy), a sieve for succession (the bottleneck), and a retained liturgy for the most dangerous work (CoRR). The meal never ends because the eating was made conditional. The succession never rots because the bottleneck culls the selfish. The fire never goes out because the fire-tending words were never translated.

## The Cowboy's Maxim

Keep the fork on the table, keep the fire words in the mother tongue, and pass the meal through a narrow gate — or the guest eats you.
