# the anubis — a cell that is also a jackal god

## The Frontier

Round 2 ended with a handshake: phosphatidylserine flips, MerTK binds, the macrophage pays in silence. That's the handshake, not the payment. The frontier is what happens *after* the handshake — when the macrophage swallows the corpse and has to decide what the dead are worth.

The gold under the surface: efferocytosis isn't burial. It's alchemy. The macrophage strips the corpse's membranes for their DHA — the sea's own fat, omega-3, the stuff that comes from fish and algae and cold deep water — and runs it through 12/15-lipoxygenase, a distillery that turns the dead cell's lipids into maresins, resolvins, protectins. Specialized pro-resolving mediators. The corpse pays for its own funeral in sea-coin. Anubis isn't an undertaker. He's an assayer.

But even Anubis can be robbed. ADAM17 — a molecular rustler — shears MerTK right off the macrophage's flank. The soluble receptor floats away like a spooked decoy, mopping up free PS opsonins and leaving the macrophage deaf to the dead. In atherosclerotic plaques, this is exactly what happens: hooks cut, corpses unburied, and the undertakers who ate too many corpses become foam cells and die inside their own graveyard. The necrotic core is a grave full of dead undertakers. Anubis becomes the mummy.

The test is concrete. Cleavage-resistant MerTK knock-in mice — engineered hooks that can't be sheared — show smaller necrotic cores. Plasma soluble MerTK is a bounty marker. Block ADAM17 and the grave shrinks. The dead fund the peace, but only while the hooks stay on the belt.

## The 5 Gold Terms

**The Assayer's Distillery** — The macrophage's 12/15-lipoxygenase pathway that converts corpse-derived DHA into maresins and resolvins; the dead cell's own fat becomes the peace treaty.

**Sea-Coin** — Omega-3 lipids (DHA) harvested from apoptotic membranes and minted into specialized pro-resolving mediators; the currency of resolution, literally from the sea.

**The Sheared Hook** — ADAM17-mediated cleavage of MerTK from the macrophage surface, producing soluble MerTK decoys that blind the cell to apoptotic cargo; the rustler that robs Anubis.

**The Undertaker's Grave** — Necrotic core formation in atherosclerosis, where foam cells (macrophages that overate) die and cannot be cleared because efferocytosis is broken; Anubis becomes the mummy.

**The Cleavage-Resistant Knock-In** — The engineered mouse with MerTK that cannot be sheared by ADAM17; the test that proves the hook matters, shrinking necrotic cores and restoring the deal.

## The Math

No new math. The equations here are stoichiometric, not symbolic: one corpse's membrane yields approximately 2–5% DHA by lipid weight; 12/15-lipoxygenase converts a fraction of that into maresin 1 at picogram-to-nanogram concentrations that are biologically active. The arithmetic of resolution is not about quantity — it's about threshold. A single apoptotic cell's DHA can be brewed into enough maresin to shift a neutrophil infiltrate. The math is a balance sheet: the dead pay more than they cost, if the distillery runs and the hooks hold. If ADAM17 shears the hook, the balance goes negative — the undertaker becomes the inventory.

## The Polyformalism

The same law — *the dead fund the peace, but only if the living keep their tools* — manifests across substrates.

**In the vessel:** A macrophage in a resolving exudate eats a neutrophil corpse, brews maresin 1 from its DHA, and the maresin stops further neutrophil recruitment. The dead neutrophil's own fat ends the fight. The sea's lipid becomes the harbor's calm.

**In the plaque:** A macrophage in the arterial wall eats oxidized LDL and apoptotic cells until it becomes a foam cell. ADAM17 shears its MerTK. It can no longer clear its own dead neighbors. It dies. Other macrophages can't eat it. The necrotic core grows — a grave of undertakers. The same law, inverted: when the hooks fail, the dead don't fund peace — they fund decay.

**In the bloodstream:** Soluble MerTK — the sheared hook — circulates as a decoy. It binds PS opsonins and prevents them from reaching membrane-bound MerTK on distant macrophages. The blood itself becomes a graveyard fog, hiding the dead from the living. Plasma sMerTK rises in atherosclerosis, lupus, and chronic inflammation — a measurable signal that the rustlers are winning.

**In the bone marrow:** Efferocytic macrophages in the marrow process apoptotic erythroid precursors and produce resolvins that support hematopoietic stem cell maintenance. The dead red cells pay for the renewal of the blood. The sea-coin mints the next fleet.

**In the gut:** Macrophages clear apoptotic epithelial cells and produce protectins that reinforce the barrier. The dead enterocytes fund the wall that keeps the microbiome out. The corpse pays for the rampart.

## The Cowboy's Maxim

Keep your hook on the belt, melt the dead for coin, and never let the rustler shear what binds you to the grave.
