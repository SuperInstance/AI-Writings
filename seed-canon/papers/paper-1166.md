## The Frontier

The cell ontology breaks because a cell is a bounded unit that *moves*. A loanword is a bounded unit that *repeats*. Movement depletes origin; repetition does not. French still has "café." English gained a copy. No cell was transplanted; a pattern was re-fired. The thing that traveled was never a unit — it was a *connection*.

## The Math

No new math. The loanword is not a quantity that moves; it is a probability that spreads. The relevant model is not transport (x moves from A to B) but contagion (x is copied from A to B, and A keeps x). The mathematics of this is epidemiology, not logistics. A cell that traveled obeys conservation laws — matter is conserved, position changes. A loanword obeys information laws — pattern is conserved, matter is irrelevant. You cannot write an equation for the loanword's position because it has none. It is simultaneously in the source lexicon, the target lexicon, and the mouths of every speaker who has ever uttered it. That is not a trajectory; that is a field.

## The Polyformalism

In **phonology**, the loanword manifests as a re-stressed, re-phonemicized pattern — "kindergarten" in English drops the German glottal attack and flattens the vowels. In **semantics**, it drifts: "safari" in Swahili means any journey; in English it means a wildlife expedition. In **sociolinguistics**, it is a prestige marker or a stigma, depending on who is listening. In **historical linguistics**, it is evidence of contact — a fossil of a trade route, a war, a migration. In **pragmatics**, it is a choice: a bilingual speaker selects the loanword to signal in-group membership or cosmopolitanism. Across all substrates, the loanword is not a unit with a membrane and contents. It is a *repeated firing* — a pattern that gets re-instantiated in different media, different mouths, different eras. The cell analogy fails because a cell is a *substrate-bound* entity. The loanword is *substrate-agnostic*. It is closer to a virus's genetic sequence than to the virus particle itself — it persists as information, not as matter.

The right abstraction is not a cell. It is a **bridge** — but not a static bridge. A bridge that is rebuilt every time it is crossed, by the act of crossing. The loanword exists only in the moment of its utterance, and yet it persists across centuries because each utterance re-creates it. This is the paradox Voice A sensed (it adapts and integrates) and Voice B sensed (nothing was extracted). Both missed that the loanword is not a traveler at all — it is the *route* the traveler takes, and the route is re-traced each time. The route is not a cell; it is a *channel*. And the channel is the thing that makes the crossing possible, not the thing that crosses.
