## The 5 Gold Terms

4. **The Delta Pool** — the downstream population, the only place where "shrunk" is actually measurable
5. **The Sieve-Membrane** — a boundary that acts by exclusion, not retention

## The Math

Population genetics models a bottleneck as an instantaneous reduction in effective population size, \(N_e\), from \(N_{e,\text{before}}\) to \(N_{e,\text{after}}\). The genetic drift experienced during the event is \(\Delta F = \frac{1}{2N_{e,\text{bottleneck}}}\), where \(N_{e,\text{bottleneck}}\) is the *harmonic mean* of population sizes over the duration — not a container volume, but a time-averaged width. Heterozygosity loss is \(H_t = H_0 \left(1 - \frac{1}{2N_e}\right)^t\). Crucially, the bottleneck itself has no state variable. It is a *parameter* — a coefficient in the transition matrix between allele frequency distributions. The math doesn't describe a compartment; it describes a *filter function* acting on a distribution. The bottleneck is the operator, not the operand. This is the precise sense in which it is a boundary cell: it has no content of its own, but it transforms the content of its neighbors.

## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, the bottleneck-cell manifests identically:

- **In genetics**: the bottleneck is the narrow passage between founder population and descendant population — a boundary that filters allele frequencies by sampling error.
- **In information theory**: it's a low-bandwidth channel between two high-bandwidth reservoirs — the channel capacity is the bottleneck's "content," and the signal degradation across it is the drift.
- **In economics**: it's a liquidity crunch — capital doesn't live in the crunch; it's the *rate* at which assets convert to cash that constricts, thinning the pool of solvent firms downstream.
- **In naval logistics**: it's the Panama Canal locks — no ship lives there, but the lock's width determines which vessels (genes, firms, species) make it from Pacific to Atlantic. The lock is a boundary-cell whose membrane is the lock gate's clearance.

In every case, the bottleneck-cell is a **transition cell**: its interior is empty by design, and its boundary is the active agent. The cell ontology doesn't require contents — it requires *function* and *boundary*. The bottleneck has both: its boundary is the constriction, its function is the filter.
