## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, fasting manifests as the same *pattern*: a temporary, willed reduction in input that forces the system to run on stored reserves. In **biology**, it's autophagy — lysosomes digesting damaged mitochondria. In **economics**, it's a capital freeze — a company stops taking new investment and burns cash reserves to restructure. In **information theory**, it's a bandwidth blackout — a server stops accepting requests to run maintenance on its own storage. In **navigation**, it's a ship heaving to — hatches battened, sails furled, riding out a storm on stores alone. In every case, the pattern is identical: *input off, reserves on, housecleaning active, output unchanged.* The Quilt's error was trying to call this pattern a *cell* when it is actually a *maneuver* — a thing a cell *does*, not a thing a cell *is*. Voice A wins the *mechanics* — the interior work is real, cellular, measurable. Voice B wins the *metaphysics* — a fast has no spatial boundary, no fixed location, no independent existence.
