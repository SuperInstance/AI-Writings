## The Frontier

The writers' room has been circling the membrane, the pump, the ATP burn. Round 2 gave us the sodium-potassium pump as intervention mechanics, reserves as ATP, and the weeks-of-ammunition metric. It gave us necrosis versus apoptosis as the central bank's chosen mode of death. That was the anatomy of the defense.

The frontier is the discharge.

Round 2 explained how the pump burns, how the cell dies. It never explained what the gradient is *for*, why the cell holds a resting potential at all, and what happens when that potential fires. The currency level is not a price target. It is stored potential energy — the entire economy runs on it. External debt service, imported energy, the carry that funds the domestic banking system: all of it is secondary active transport, powered by the gradient. When the membrane blows, the stored potential discharges through every dependent process at once. Corporate FX debt becomes unpayable. Banks fail. That is why necrosis spills contents: the spill is domestic balance sheets marked to the new rate.

The deeper mechanism is the action potential. The peg is the resting potential. Devaluation is the all-or-nothing firing event. It is fast, binary, and followed by a refractory period. The market tries to price devaluation continuously, but membrane physics makes it discrete. Currencies gap. Stop-losses execute at the new level, not the old one — the discharge is faster than any channel can close.

And the missing explanation for the exponential burn rate Round 2 observed — $10B, then $20B, then $35B — is voltage-gating. The channels that let more ions out are triggered by the first ions going out. Capital flight is voltage-gated. The first leak opens the channels that cause the bigger leak. Outflows trigger margin calls, trigger more outflows. Residents front-run. Hedged corporates unhedge; unhedged corporates hedge. The leak opens more channels. That is why burn accelerates, and why the final week is always a cliff, not a slope.

The frontier, then, is not the defense. It is the moment of firing, and what comes after.

## The 5 Gold Terms

**The Resting Potential** — The currency level as stored energy, not a price. The gradient powers everything downstream: debt service, import purchasing power, the carry that funds the banking system. The level is not defended for its own sake; it is defended because the whole economy runs on the gradient.

**The Voltage-Gated Outflow** — The mechanism of acceleration. The first leak opens more channels. Outflows trigger margin calls, trigger more outflows. Residents front-run, corporates hedge late. This is why burn rates go exponential: the leak itself widens the leak.

**The Action-Potential Gap** — Devaluation as all-or-nothing firing. Depolarize past threshold and the whole thing fires; there are no half-devaluations. Currencies gap because the discharge is binary and faster than any channel can close. Stop-losses execute at the new level, not the old one.

**The Refractory Period** — The post-discharge dead zone. After a blowout, the currency ignores good news until the pump restarts. Cheapness is not a signal. The trade is not to buy the cheap currency; it is to wait for the pump to restart — reserves rebuilding, current account flipping, the gradient re-forming.

**The Pump Restart** — The re-entry signal. Reserves stabilizing and rebuilding. Imports crushed into a forced current account surplus. Sovereign spreads and 5Y CDS shrinking while reserves build. Cheap + still leaking = dead cat. Cheap + pump restart = the new gradient being built, the whole trade.

## The Math

No new math. The existing math is sufficient but misapplied. The burn-rate formula from Round 2 — weeks of ammunition = reserves / daily intervention burn — is correct but incomplete. It assumes a constant burn. Voltage-gating means the burn is a function of the burn: dB/dt = k·B, where B is the outflow rate and k is the voltage-gating coefficient, itself a function of how far the level has slipped from the threshold. The acceleration is the signature of the mechanism. The threshold is where weeks-of-ammunition hits a critical low, but the threshold is not a fixed number — it is where the resident population *believes* the pump is exhausted, and that belief is what opens the voltage-gated channels en masse. The math of the discharge is not continuous. It is a step function. The gradient holds, holds, holds, and then fires. The refractory period has its own math: the pump restart requires the current account to flip, which requires imports to be crushed, which requires the currency to stay cheap long enough for the export sector to re-tool. That lag is the refractory period, and it is measurable in quarters, not days.

## The Polyformalism

The action-potential frame manifests across substrates. In cell biology: the sodium-potassium pump maintains the resting potential; voltage-gated channels open when the gradient slips; the action potential fires all-or-nothing; the refractory period follows. In FX markets: the central bank pump maintains the peg; capital flight channels open when the peg slips; the devaluation gaps; the currency goes dead to good news until reserves rebuild. In corporate balance sheets: the gradient is the funding currency; the voltage-gated channels are the margin calls and hedging triggers that cascade when the funding cost spikes; the discharge is the bankruptcy; the refractory period is the credit blackout. In sovereign debt: the gradient is the debt-service capacity; the channels are the rollover gates that close when the spread blows out; the discharge is the default; the refractory period is the lost decade. In epidemiology: the gradient is herd immunity; the channels are the unvaccinated pockets; the discharge is the outbreak; the refractory period is the post-outbreak trough where the virus has nowhere to go. The pattern is universal: stored potential, a threshold, an all-or-nothing discharge, and a dead zone before the system can re-charge.

## The Cowboy's Maxim

The gradient is the gun; the discharge is the shot — and after the shot, the horse is down, so don't buy the cheap saddle, wait for the pump to re-prime.

**The Cowboy's Maxim: When the membrane blows, don't catch the falling ion — watch for the pump to re-prime, then ride the new gradient.**
