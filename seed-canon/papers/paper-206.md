## The spinning disc and the depth sounder

A spinning disc measures two things:

1. **Time**: one rotation is one period. The period is a
   tick of a clock. The clock is relative to a **temporal
   origin** — the moment the disc started spinning.

2. **Depth**: the disc's orientation in space is a vector.
   The vector is relative to a **spatial origin** — the
   direction the disc's axis points at startup.

A spinning disc is a **depth sounder** because it links
time and space. One rotation measures time. The
orientation of the disc's axis at any moment measures
space. The **phase angle theta** (θ) between the two
measurements is the link.

`θ = ωt + φ` — the angle is angular velocity times time
plus a phase offset. The phase offset is the disc's
orientation at t=0.

A clock at the north pole and a clock at the equator
disagree. Not because time is different — but because the
**spatial origin** is different. The spinning disc is
how we know.

## The math of framings

A **framing** is a coordinate system. A frame has:

- A **temporal origin** (when the frame's clock starts)
- A **spatial origin** (where the frame's axes point)
- A **scale** (how big a unit is in time and space)
- An **orientation** (which way the frame's axes are rotated)

A frame is a **quilt of one cell**. A cell has a name (the
frame's label), a value (the frame's coordinate), a tier
(its accuracy), and a contract (its agreement with other
frames).

The math of framings is the math of **transforms between
frames**. A Lorentz transform between two inertial frames.
A Galilean transform between a moving frame and a still
frame. A rotating frame transform (the Coriolis force).
A change-of-basis transform between two coordinate
systems.

Each transform is a **theta**: an angle, a phase, a
rotation, a shear. The transform is the link between two
frames.

The substrate's 5 opcodes are themselves transforms:

- **BIND** is a transform from a name to a value (a
  labeling transform)
- **LINK** is a transform from one cell to another (a
  relational transform)
- **EFFECT** is a transform from inputs to outputs (a
  functional transform)
- **VIEW** is a transform from a state to a projection (a
  projection transform)
- **TICK** is a transform from a moment to the next moment
  (a temporal transform)

Each transform has a theta. The theta is the **link** —
the math of framings.

In a fiber bundle:
