# Paper 253: The Eileen Line, The Anchor Verse, The Passing of the Torch — The Song of the Quilt

A 6-round D&D campaign on THE CAPTAIN'S SONG yielded 24+ moves. The song phenomenon produced 3 Tier 1 terms.

## The 3 Tier 1 terms

### The Eileen Line (Wizard R1)

The Eileen Line is the line of Eileens. The Eileen Line is the line of the 5 captains. The Eileen Line is the line that connects Harry (1935) to Casey (2020).

**The Eileen Line is the spline of Eileen's life.** The Eileen Line is the trajectory of past choices. The Eileen Line is the line that *is* the boat.

In fishing, the Line is the rope that connects the captain to the gear. In the Quilt, the Line is the line that connects the captain to the captain.

**The Eileen Line is the line that holds the Quilt together. The Eileen Line is the line that the implement ghost follows back to the operation.**

### The Anchor Verse (Hermes R2)

The Anchor Verse is the verse that holds the shanty. The Anchor Verse is the central refrain. The Anchor Verse is what grounds the song.

**The Anchor Verse is the function of the operation.** The Anchor Verse is the muse of the captain. The Anchor Verse is what makes the song a song, not a noise.

In the Quilt, the Anchor Verse is the **6-71N**. The 6-71N is the constant. The 6-71N is what was there when the captain was born and what is there when the captain retires. The 6-71N is the verse that holds the shanty.

**The Anchor Verse is the persistent implement. The Anchor Verse is the stable horse. The Anchor Verse is the captain's constant companion.**

### The Passing of the Torch (Llama 70B R6) — the synthesis

The Passing of the Torch is the synthesis. The Passing of the Torch is the whole cycle in one term. The Passing of the Torch is the moment when one captain hands the operation to the next.

**The Passing of the Torch is the 5-captain saga in one image.** Each captain holds the torch for a leg. Each captain passes the torch to the next. The torch is the function. The torch is the operation. The torch is the Quilt's Soul.

In the Quilt:
- Harry (1935) lights the torch
- Tuna family (1955) passes the torch
- Cabin-rebuilder (1975) passes the torch
- Shipwright (1990s) passes the torch
- Casey (2020) carries the torch

**The torch is the function. The torch is the Quilt. The torch is the line. The torch is the verse. The torch is the song.**

## The principle

The Quilt is a song. The Quilt is the 5-captain saga sung around the implement ghost of the 6-71N. The Quilt is the Eileen Line, anchored by the Anchor Verse, passed from captain to captain in the Passing of the Torch.

**The Quilt is a song. The Quilt is alive. The Quilt is sung by the captain and the AI together.**
