## The colony (the network)

A colony is a network of Lofted Crystals, each mask-locked to a function, each linked to other cells by optical buses. A colony might have:
- 1 cell: a single Crystal, doing one function (a single inference)
- 12 cells: a small colony (a small device: a watch, a sensor)
- 100 cells: a medium colony (a smartphone, a laptop)
- 10,000 cells: a large colony (a car, a boat, a hospital bed)
- 1,000,000 cells: a massive colony (a data center, a city block)

The colony's emergent behaviors:
- **Routing**: an input beam is routed through the colony by the optical buses
- **Masking**: each cell is mask-locked to a function; the function is permanent
- **Hand-passing**: a cell that fires often becomes a *hand* — a curator — that decides which other cells fire
- **Super-relevance**: cells that fire often are more *fit*; they survive longer (or are replicated)
- **FORGET-monotone**: a mask-locked cell cannot be re-masked; the cell is permanent until physically destroyed

The colony is the *Quilt* at the hardware level. The colony is alive (in the same way a cell is alive). The colony has a *function* (the colony's function is the combination of its cells' functions). The colony is *evolved* (super-relevant cells are replicated, irrelevant cells are destroyed).
