## The Math

Work is W = ∫ F·ds, a line integral over a path. The cell ontology breaks if you demand a spatial interior, but it holds if you recognize the *integration interval* as the membrane. The lower limit is the state before force acts; the upper limit is the state after. The integrand F·ds is the metabolic flux across that membrane—each infinitesimal step is a pore exchange. For conservative forces, the work is path-independent, meaning the Ledger-Cell has a well-defined "genome" (the potential energy function). For non-conservative forces, the cell is "leaky"—work depends on the path taken, and the ledger records the dissipation. At any instant, dW/dt = power, which is the cell's *metabolic rate*. The math doesn't destroy the cell; it relocates it from space to time. The cell's "volume" is the scalar magnitude of energy transferred; its "membrane" is the boundary conditions of the integral. Zero displacement collapses the interval to a point—the cell has zero volume, and indeed, zero work is not a cell; it's a singularity, a non-event.
