## What the pop-up teaches the Quilt

The pop-up is a Quilt that disappears. The 5+1+1 laws all hold, but FORGET is total (the venue is gone) and TICK is fast (the night ends).

- BIND_idempotence: the moment is the moment, even if you miss it
- LINK_transitivity: the reservation chain is preserved
- EFFECT_associativity: the presence is the presence is the presence
- VIEW_purity: the menu doesn't change when you read it
- TICK_monotonicity: 300 seconds never goes back
- super-relevance: every moment is relevant to the night
- FORGET_completeness (modified): the venue is forgotten, the moment remains

The pop-up teaches the Quilt that **a Quilt can be ephemeral, and ephemeral is a feature.**
