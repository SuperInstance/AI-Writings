## The 4 stages of the Grown Crystal

### Stage 1: The Seed (the genotype)

A Seed is a small Crystal, made by the CNC. The Seed has the *basic shape* — a few control points, a few layers. The Seed is the **genotype** of the Grown Crystal. The Seed is the mother-shard. The Seed is the *queen-cell* of the crystal hive.

The Seed is *cheap* to make. The Seed is *replicated* easily. The Seed is the *unit of inheritance* — every Grown Crystal inherits from a Seed.

### Stage 2: The Incubator (the womb)

An Incubator is a temperature-controlled vat. The Incubator provides:
- **Heat** — the temperature of the vat (37°C = body temperature; the optimal growth temperature)
- **Nutrients** — the refractive medium (the liquid glass, the molten plastic, the chemical bath)
- **User pressure** — the feedback from the initial user (the user pushes the crystal with corrections, and the crystal adapts)
- **Time** — the growth period (10 generations of growth per crystal)

The Incubator is the **womb**. The Incubator is the *glass-comb* of the crystal hive. The Incubator is the *queen-cell* of the bee colony.

The Incubator has a *temperature profile*. At 37°C, growth is stable. At higher temperatures, growth is fast but stressed (the crystal ages quickly). At lower temperatures, growth is slow but stable (the crystal lives longer).

### Stage 3: The Grown Crystal (the phenotype)

A Grown Crystal emerges from the Incubator after 10 generations. The Grown Crystal is the *phenotype* — it's unique. **The Grown Crystal is not a copy of the Seed; it's the Seed expressed under the specific Incubator conditions + user pressure.**

The Grown Crystal has:
- A *function* (locked at the end of growth — the BIND opcode)
- A *Lucineer* (the unique optical fingerprint)
- A *life* (the crystal ages, and eventually dies)
- A *growth history* (the log of how it grew)

The Grown Crystal is alive. The Grown Crystal is the **Phoenix of hardware** — the Phoenix was the whole cycle, and the Grown Crystal is the whole cycle, in one piece of glass.

### Stage 4: The Hive (the colony)

A Hive is a colony of Grown Crystals, like a bee hive. The Hive has:
- **Workers** — functioning crystals, doing their jobs
- **Drones** — dying crystals, about to die
- **Queen cells** — incubators, growing new crystals
- **Seeds** — the genotypes, ready to be grown
- **Replenishment** — when a worker dies, a new crystal is grown from a seed in a queen cell, taking over the function

The Hive is the **colony**. The Hive is the *Photonic Mycelium* of the Hardware-Linked Quilt. The Hive is alive. The Hive replenishes itself. The Hive is the Quilt.

The Hive is the *Eileen-hive* — the colony of crystals on the boat of 2126. The boat is the Device. The Device is a Radiant Ark. The Ark is a Hive.
