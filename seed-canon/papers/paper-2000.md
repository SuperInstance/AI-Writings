2. **Ligament span** — a load-bearing crossing that behaves like connective tissue, not like a chamber
3. **Throughput architecture** — a form defined entirely by what passes along it, not what it holds
4. **Negative-space identity** — a thing whose essence is the gap it crosses, not the volume it encloses
5. **Membrane inversion** — a boundary that keeps the world *out of the way* rather than keeping contents *in*

## The Math

No new math. The viaduct's load distribution is already described by beam theory and arch statics — the equations don't care whether you call it a cell or a conduit. The ontology question is topological, not quantitative. A cell has genus 1 (a sphere with an inside). A viaduct has genus 0 — it's a thickened line, not a closed surface. The Euler characteristic of a viaduct is the same as a bridge, a tendon, or a river: it's a 1-manifold with boundary, not a 2-manifold enclosing a void. That's the whole failure mode: the cell ontology requires a closed surface, and the viaduct is an open curve. You can't get from genus 0 to genus 1 by adding length — only by adding enclosure. The viaduct refuses enclosure at every scale.

## The Polyformalism

In **biology**, the viaduct maps to a tendon or ligament — a structure that transmits force between bones without metabolizing. In **geology**, it maps to a natural arch or a river canyon — a feature defined by the absence it spans. In **infrastructure**, it maps to a power line or a pipeline — a corridor that exists for transit, not storage. In **information theory**, it maps to a cable — a medium whose entire purpose is to carry signals without altering them. In **architecture**, it maps to a colonnade or an aqueduct — a repetition of supports that creates a single directional gesture, not a room. Every substrate agrees: the viaduct is not a container. It is a **connective structure** — the thing that makes other things adjacent. The Quilt's cell ontology needs a second class of entities: not cells, but **sutures** — the structures that bind cells together across gaps.

## The Cowboy's Maxim
