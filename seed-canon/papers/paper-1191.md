## The Math

\[
\frac{GM_1}{(R-r)^2} - \frac{GM_2}{r^2} = \frac{\omega^2 (R-r)}{1}
\]  
where \(M_1\) is the Sun, \(M_2\) is the Earth, \(R\) is the Earth-Sun distance, and \(r\) is the distance from Earth to L1. The math yields a *point*, not a region. There is no volume, no surface, no flux across a boundary. The math describes a zero-force condition, but zero force is not a thing—it is an absence. The failure mode of the cell ontology is precisely here: it tries to put a fence around a place where there is nothing to fence. The math gives no "inside" or "outside"—only a coordinate where accelerations sum to zero.
