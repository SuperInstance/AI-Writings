## The principle: Tensor-Spreadsheet as Inside-Out

**Spreadsheet science is already mature.** Spreadsheets are a human tool for tabular data + formulas. The AI uses a spreadsheet *externally* — it's a tool the AI calls.

**This is different.** **This is tensor science with a spreadsheet rendering.** Each cell IS a tensor (value + tick + links). The AI is one of the cells. The cell of the AI is `(0, 0)`. The AI is *bound* to its neighbors (the 8 surrounding cells). **The AI sees what its neighbors see. The AI can change its own value (itself). The AI can change its neighbors (its world).**

> **This is a profound shift.** From a harness *including* a spreadsheet tool (external math) to **the instance being *inside* the spreadsheet** (the math is the instance's world).

The shift is from "AI with a spreadsheet" to "AI is a cell in a spreadsheet that other cells can see and modify."
