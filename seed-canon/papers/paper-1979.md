## The Math

No new math. The reason is instructive: a cell requires a boundary operator — a function that maps an interior to an exterior and returns a nonzero flux across the membrane. For 1914, the flux across any proposed boundary (June 28, August 1, December 31) is *infinite* relative to the interior’s own dynamics, because the causal density of the year exceeds its chronological span. You cannot integrate the war’s onset over a 365-day interval without importing terms from outside the interval. The math of cells is the math of closed systems; 1914 is an open system wearing a calendar’s costume.
