## The Math

No new math. The numbers that matter are ratios, not equations. A droplet's residence time (τ_drop ≈ 7 minutes) divided by the cloud's lifetime (τ_cloud ≈ 30 minutes) gives 0.23 — meaning the cloud turns over its entire water content roughly four times before it dies. A cell's contents persist for the cell's lifetime; the ratio is 1.0. That single ratio — 0.23 versus 1.0 — is the quantitative proof that the visible cloud is not a container. It's a throughput. The thermal torus, by contrast, has a circulation time of 20–40 minutes, matching the cloud's lifetime, because the torus *is* the persistent structure. The cloud is the visible cross-section of the torus where the air happens to cross the dewpoint. The math is just the ratio of persistence to flow, and it settles the debate.
