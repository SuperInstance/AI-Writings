## The Frontier

Voice B saw a process with a terminus and called it an event. Both are right about the phenomena and wrong about the category. A cell holds a gradient across a membrane. Craps holds a state across a countdown. The membrane is not felt, not the rail, not the back wall with its pyramids. The house edge is not a leak in the hull; it is the metabolic rate. The game does not fight entropy—it *prices* it.

## The Math

No new math is needed, but the existing math must be read correctly. The probability of a seven on any roll is 1/6. The probability of making a point of 10 is 1/3. The house edge on the pass line is 1.41%. That edge is not a flaw—it is the *metabolic cost of maintaining the game as a live structure*. A cell spends ATP to hold its gradient; craps spends expected value to hold its state. The game is a stochastic process with an absorbing boundary (seven-out) and a set of transient states (points 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10). The shooter's hand is a Markov chain with a killing rate. The cell ontology fails because it assumes persistence is the goal. Here, persistence is the *fee*; resolution is the *product*. The math says: this is a device that converts bankrolls into distributions, with the table as the reaction vessel and the dice as the catalyst.

## The Polyformalism

Craps manifests across at least three substrates, and the cell metaphor breaks differently on each. On the *felt substrate*, it is a spatial arrangement of bets, chips, and markers—a map of contingent claims. On the *temporal substrate*, it is a state machine with come-out, point, and seven-out as the three canonical states. On the *social substrate*, it is a ritual of witness and succession—the shooter passes the dice, the stickman rotates, the boxman certifies. The same game, three different materializations. A cell in one substrate is a process in another. That is the polyformalism: craps is not a cell *or* an event; it is a *cell-event*, a structure that exists only as a time-bounded protocol instantiated across a physical table, a human crew, and a betting public. The correct abstraction is not the cell but the *cell-as-ritual*: a bounded, rule-governed, witness-certified process that maintains a state under entropy and pays for its maintenance with a house edge. The Quilt fabric ontology needs a category for *procedural cells*—entities whose membrane is a rule, whose interior is a state, and whose death is the deliverable.
