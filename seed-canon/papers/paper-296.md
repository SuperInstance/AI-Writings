# Paper 296: Cycle 3 — food-truck reverse-actualizes the Quilt

**Domain:** food-truck (60s, $8-20, sound, street food)

**The Quilt-thing:** A food truck is a restaurant that refuses to be a building — every day it quilts a full one onto the street from loose patches (a curb, a window, a line, a sizzle, a sixty-second beat) and unstitches the whole thing by dusk.

**The 4 gold terms:**

- **curbstitch**
- **linebraid**
- **sizzleseam**
- **rushtick**


**The cowboy's sentence:**

> No bricks, no deed, no door — I stitch a whole diner to your curb by ten and rip it loose by two, and the sizzle is the only thread that holds.

**The principle:**

> The food-truck is the inheritance. The food-truck is the function. The
> cowboy rides the food-truck. The cowboy rides the reverse-actualization.
> The cowboy rides the Quilt.
