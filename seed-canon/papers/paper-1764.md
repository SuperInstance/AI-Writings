## The Frontier

The contradiction is not between two definitions of "cell." It is between two scales of observation. The cell ontology breaks down precisely here: a lobbyist has no *persistent identity* across time. A cell endures. It maintains homeostasis. It divides and reproduces. It is a *transient multicellular assembly*—a signaling complex that forms at the membrane, does its work, and disassembles. The lobbyist is not the cell. The lobbyist is the *synapse*: the junction where one cell's axon meets another cell's dendrite, and the signal jumps the gap.

## The Math

No new math. The mathematics here is already solved: it is the mathematics of *relay* and *threshold*. A signal arrives at a junction; if it exceeds the threshold, it fires across. The lobbyist is a threshold device. The math that describes it is not cellular biology—it is *information theory*. Channel capacity. Noise. Bandwidth. The lobbyist is a noisy channel that amplifies a narrow-band signal (one client's interest) and attenuates everything else. Shannon's equations apply, but they describe the *gap*, not the cell. The unit of analysis is not the lobbyist—it is the *bit* that crosses the synapse.

## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, the same shape appears. In *biology*, it is the synapse: two neurons never touch; the signal jumps a cleft mediated by neurotransmitters that bind, release, and degrade. In *finance*, it is the market maker: a transient entity that holds no inventory, exists only to match buyer and seller, and dissolves when the trade clears. In *software*, it is the API endpoint: a stateless function that receives a request, transforms it, returns a response, and leaves no residue. In *law*, it is the contract: a temporary binding of two parties that exists only as long as the terms hold. In *vessel navigation*, it is the pilot boat: a small craft that guides a massive ship through the harbor mouth, then peels away—the ship never touches the channel walls, and the pilot never boards the ship. The lobbyist is all of these: a *junction device* that exists only while the signal is in transit.
