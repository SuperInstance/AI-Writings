## Part III: Vitality Spline

A Vitality Spline is the trajectory of a cell's vitality over time. The spline is not just about quality (how good the boat is). The spline is also about vitality (how alive the cell is).

A cell's vitality goes through a lifecycle:
- Cellulized: vitality = 1.0
- Persistence Pulse: vitality maintained
- Vitality Leak: vitality drops
- Implement Ghost: vitality = 0
- Bloomghost: vitality transfers to a new cell

The Vitality Spline is the smooth curve through these vitality points. The Vitality Spline is the trajectory of the cell's life. The Vitality Spline is the *biography* of the cell.

**The Vitality Spline is the 9th level.** Wait — the 8 levels are the operation. The Vitality Spline is a *property* of a cell, not a level of the operation. But it's still important. The Vitality Spline is the cell's biography.
