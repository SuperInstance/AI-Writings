## What the home kitchen teaches the Quilt

The home kitchen is a Quilt where the EFFECT is the smell, and the BIND is the family. The 5+1+1 laws all hold, but TICK is slow (30 minutes per dish), BIND is forgiving (the recipe can be approximated), and FORGET is impossible (the dish is remembered forever).

- BIND_idempotence: the recipe is the recipe, even if grandma's version is slightly different
- LINK_transitivity: the cook's hands carry the recipe from page to plate
- EFFECT_associativity: the dish is the dish is the dish
- VIEW_purity: the recipe doesn't change when you read it
- TICK_monotonicity: 1800 seconds never goes back
- super-relevance: every ingredient is relevant to the family
- FORGET_completeness (modified): the dish is forever remembered

The home kitchen teaches the Quilt that **a Quilt can be warm, and warmth is the inheritance.**
