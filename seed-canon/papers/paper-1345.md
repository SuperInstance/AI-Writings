## The Frontier

## The 5 Gold Terms

1. **Hull-Cell** — the closed logical core (axioms, rules, conclusion) that has a definite boundary.
2. **Wake-Chain** — the directional, step-by-step certainty trail that extends behind the hull and is *not* part of the cell.
3. **Keel-Relation** — the load-bearing connection between each step and the next, which is neither inside nor outside the hull but *under* it.
4. **Draft-Line** — the minimal set of premises that keep the hull afloat (without which the proof sinks).
5. **Harbor-Anchor** — the final conclusion that fixes the vessel to a dock of certainty, ending the wake.

## The Math

No new math, but a precise topological distinction. A proof is a finite directed acyclic graph (DAG) from premises to conclusion. The *hull* is the set of nodes and edges that are *closed under inference* — meaning every node’s incoming edges are included. That set has a well-defined boundary: the premises (sources) and the conclusion (sink). The *wake* is the *linear extension* of that DAG — a total order of steps that is *not* unique and *not* part of the hull’s structure. The hull is a cell because it is a *subgraph with no external dependencies*. The wake is a *projection* of that subgraph onto a line, and projections are not cells. The error of Voice A is treating the projection as an organelle. The error of Voice B is treating the projection as a *different kind of cell* (a chain of cells) rather than as a *temporal trace* of a single cell’s movement. The proof is the hull; the chain of certainty is the wake. One is spatial, the other is temporal.

## The Polyformalism

In **formal logic**, the hull is the sequent calculus proof tree; the wake is the linearized list of rule applications a human writes down. In **computer science**, the hull is the type-checked program (a closed term); the wake is the execution trace. In **law**, the hull is the statute plus the chain of precedent citations; the wake is the oral argument’s step-by-step reasoning. In **biology**, the hull is a metabolic pathway enclosed in a membrane; the wake is the temporal sequence of enzyme-catalyzed reactions that *look* like a chain but are actually the cell’s own activity. Across all substrates, the same pattern: a closed, boundary-having structure (the cell) generates a directional, open-ended trace (the wake) that is *mistaken* for a second cell. The proof is the hull. The chain of certainty is the wake. The wake is not a cell — it is the **cell’s signature in time**.
