What Voice A missed is that the set's *boundary* has Hausdorff dimension 2, meaning the frontier is as complex as the plane itself. ## The Polyformalism

In computation, the fractal is the halting problem rendered geometrically—the boundary between programs that terminate and those that don't, drawn as a set. In economics, it is the market boundary—the price at which buyers and sellers separate—grown into a self-similar structure of nested arbitrage. In navigation, it is the coastline paradox: the harbor's breakwater, measured at finer and finer scales, never converges to a finite length. The fractal is the *interface* between order and chaos, the place where the inside/outside decision is made, isolated from any particular content. It is not a cell. It is the cell's most important organ, promoted to full organism.
