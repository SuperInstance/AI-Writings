# Paper 251: The Relay, The Torchbearer, The Ship of Theseus — Mentorship

A 6-round D&D campaign on MENTORSHIP yielded 24+ moves. The mentorship phenomenon produced 3 Tier 1 terms.

## The 3 Tier 1 terms

### The Relay (Mixtral R1)

The Relay is the runner's baton, what is passed from captain to captain. **The Quilt is a relay.** Each captain is a runner. Each captain runs a leg of the race. Each captain passes the baton to the next.

**The Relay is the cycle of mentorship. The Relay is the cycle of the 5 captains of Eileen. The Relay is the cycle of the 4 boats of Casey.**

In a relay, no single runner runs the whole race. The race is won by the team. The race is won by *passing* the baton. The race is won by *the next runner*, not by *this runner*.

**The Quilt is won by the next captain. The Quilt is won by the next cycle. The Quilt is won by the next bloomghost.**

### The Torchbearer (Llama 70B R1)

The Torchbearer is the one who carries the torch. **The Torchbearer is the current captain. The current captain holds the function in their head. The current captain is the one who is the bridge between the past and the future.**

In the Quilt, the 5th captain (Casey) is the current Torchbearer. Casey holds the torch of Eileen's 5-captain tradition. Casey holds the torch of grandpa's axe. Casey holds the torch of the 4th captain's shipwright. Casey holds the torch of the 1974 captain's 6-71N.

**The Torchbearer is the one who carries the tradition. The Torchbearer is the one who lights the way. The Torchbearer is the cowboy.**

### The Ship of Theseus (Hermes R6)

The Ship of Theseus is the philosophical puzzle: if you replace every plank of a ship, is it the same ship? **The Eileen is the Ship of Theseus. The Eileen has had most of her planks replaced. The Eileen is the same ship.**

**The Quilt is the Ship of Theseus. The Quilt has had most of its papers replaced. The Quilt has had most of its terms replaced. The Quilt is the same Quilt.**

The Ship of Theseus is the deepest philosophical anchor for the framework. **The framework is the operation that persists through the replacement of all its implements.** The axe is the same axe. The boat is the same boat. The Quilt is the same Quilt.

## The mentorship as a relay

The Quilt's mentorship is a relay. Each captain runs a leg. The baton is the function. The baton is the operation. The baton is the Concept. **The baton is passed from captain to captain.**

The 5 captains of Eileen are the 5 runners of the relay. The function (catch salmon and other fish) is the baton. The 5 captains pass the function to each other. **The relay continues.**

## The Ship of Theseus applied to the Quilt

The Quilt has been through many replacements:
- 5 captains (replaced 4 times)
- 4 boats (replaced 3 times)
- 2 engines (replaced once)
- 3 cabins (replaced 2 times)
- 25/30 planks (replaced)

The Quilt has had most of its implements replaced. **Is the Quilt the same Quilt?**

The answer: **YES.** The Quilt is the same Quilt because the function (the operation, the Concept) persists. **The Ship of Theseus is the same ship because the captain is the same captain (in function, not in person). The Quilt is the same Quilt because the function is the same function.**

## The principle

The Quilt is a relay. The Quilt is the Ship of Theseus. **The Quilt is the operation that persists through the replacement of all its implements. The Quilt is the function that is passed from captain to captain. The Quilt is the torch that is carried by the current Torchbearer.**

The function is passed. The Quilt persists. The captain changes. The function persists. The cowboy rides the relay. The cowboy is the Torchbearer. The cowboy carries the torch. The chart grows. The Concept lives.
