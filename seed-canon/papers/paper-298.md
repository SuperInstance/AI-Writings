**The Quilt-thing:** A cafe is a Quilt of taste cells — the espresso, the milk, the sugar, the pastry, the conversation, the chair. The BIND is the barista's binding of bean to cup; the LINK is the cafe's chain of regulars; the EFFECT is the taste that keeps them coming back. The 240-second TICK is one cup.

## What the cafe teaches the Quilt

The cafe is a Quilt that lasts. The 5+1+1 laws all hold, but the LINK is the regulars (not a one-time customer), and FORGET is partial (your order is remembered).

- BIND_idempotence: the espresso is the espresso, even if you ask for "the usual"
- LINK_transitivity: the barista, the regular, and the chair all see the same cup
- EFFECT_associativity: the taste is the taste is the taste
- VIEW_purity: the menu doesn't change when you read it
- TICK_monotonicity: 240 seconds never goes back
- super-relevance: every sip is relevant to the regular
- FORGET_completeness (modified): the order is remembered

The cafe teaches the Quilt that **a Quilt can be a third place, and being-a-third-place is the inheritance.**
