The entropy of a black hole is proportional to its *surface area*, not its volume. This is the mathematical smoking gun: the cell’s “contents” are encoded entirely on its boundary. The interior volume is not just unknown; it’s *thermodynamically irrelevant*. For a non-rotating, uncharged black hole, the no-hair theorem states that only mass, charge, and angular momentum survive—all other information is erased. That means the cell has no “genome,” no internal state that persists. The math says: this is a boundary phenomenon, not a container phenomenon.
