## What the fancy-fine teaches the Quilt

The fancy-fine is a Quilt where the EFFECT is the mood, not the food. The food is the BIND; the mood is the EFFECT. The 5+1+1 laws all hold, but TICK is slower (600s vs 30s) and FORGET is partial (the meal is remembered).

- BIND_idempotence: the tasting menu is the same menu all night
- LINK_transitivity: the server, the kitchen, and the table all see the same arc
- EFFECT_associativity: the mood is the mood is the mood
- VIEW_purity: the menu doesn't change when you read it
- TICK_monotonicity: 600 seconds never goes back
- super-relevance: every course is relevant to the arc
- FORGET_completeness (modified): the meal is partially remembered

The fancy-fine teaches the Quilt that **a Quilt can be slow, and slowness is a feature.**
