## The Math

No new math, but a reframing of existing physics. The tidal acceleration across a body of length *L* at distance *r* from a mass *M* is *a_tidal = 2GM L / r³*. The microgravity cell's boundary is defined where *a_tidal* exceeds the tolerance threshold *ε* (say, 10⁻⁶ g). Solving for *L_max = ε r³ / 2GM* gives the cell's diameter. For the ISS at *r* = 6,780 km from Earth's center, *L_max* is roughly 100 meters—the station itself is well inside its own coherence cell. But the *cell* isn't the station; the cell is the volume of space where a freely falling object of length *L* experiences no detectable relative acceleration. The math is simple, but the ontology shift is radical: the cell's "size" is a function of the *observer's tolerance*, not a fixed physical dimension. The membrane is not a place; it's a *criterion*.
