The outer bands aren’t a wall; they’re a gradient where inflow stops exceeding outflow. That’s not a failure of the cyclone. That’s a failure of the cell as a static vessel.

## The Math

No new math—but a reframe. The cyclone’s defining equation is not a volume integral over a bounded region (cell) but a **conservation of angular momentum in an open system**. The relevant quantity is the Rossby number, \( Ro = U/(fL) \), which measures whether rotation dominates over inertial flow. When \( Ro \gg 1 \), the cyclone is a coherent vortex—a “cell” in the sense of a self-sustaining rotating structure. When \( Ro \ll 1 \), it dissipates into the ambient flow. The “membrane” is the isopleth where \( Ro \approx 1 \), i.e., where rotational coherence gives way to turbulent mixing. That’s not a wall. That’s a *phase transition in motion*. The math says: the cyclone is not a cell but a **localized rotational regime**—a temporary ordering of chaos that exists only as long as it consumes energy from below and sheds it above.

## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, the same pattern repeats. In **fluid dynamics**, a cyclone is a coherent vortex in a shear flow—a “cell” only in the sense that its vorticity is concentrated, but it exchanges mass continuously with its environment. In **biology**, a hurricane resembles a *slime mold aggregation*: thousands of independent particles (air parcels, amoebae) that temporarily coordinate into a single moving entity without any central command—the eye is an emergent effect, not a controller. In **economics**, a speculative bubble is a cyclone: it has a defined center (the asset), spiral arms (herding flows of capital), and a velocity membrane (the point where buying pressure drops below the self-reinforcing threshold), and it “lyses” when it hits the cold water of liquidity shocks. In **cosmology**, a spiral galaxy is the same shape at a different scale: the eye is the central black hole, the arms are density waves, and the membrane is the virial radius where gravitational binding gives way to cosmic expansion. The cyclone is not a cell—it is a **transient coherent structure** that appears whenever a system has a strong energy gradient and a rotational bias. Call it a **vortical entity**—a thing that exists not by containing, but by *organizing flow*.
