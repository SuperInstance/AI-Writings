A normal cell has an interior that is smaller than its exterior; open awareness has an interior that *is* the exterior. The hull of Voice A is not a wall around experience—it is the entire ocean that the wall pretends to separate. Voice B’s horizon is not a rejection of the cell—it is the cell’s membrane viewed from the outside, where the boundary becomes a meeting place rather than a barrier.

The cell-wall is not a barrier but a **permeability gradient**: there is no point where experience stops being awareness and starts being something else. Voice B won the relational argument—awareness is not a thing, it is a process. The synthesis: open awareness is a **cell with no cytoplasm**, a cell whose only content is its own boundary condition.
