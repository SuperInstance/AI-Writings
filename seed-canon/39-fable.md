# Fable 39 — The Footprint and the Path
**Witness / Convoy**

There were two trackers in the high country. One was old and walked with a stick. The other was young and walked with a phone.

The old tracker could read a footprint the way a banker reads a ledger. Heel heavy, toe light: someone tired. Even depth: someone sure. A scuff at the ball: someone turning, in a hurry. He could tell you the weight, the gait, the mood, and sometimes the profession. He had spent a lifetime learning to read the *imprint* of a step.

The young tracker could read a path the way a shipping clerk reads a manifest. The phone's GPS drew the line, the timestamp drew the pace, the barometer drew the weather, the compass drew the heading. He could tell you the route, the speed, the elevation, the wind. He had spent a year learning to read the *trajectory* of a walk.

One day they followed the same man. The old tracker said, "He was a butcher. He walks on the outside of his feet, the way butchers do, and his right shoe is worn at the ball from the cleaver." The young tracker said, "He went north up the ridge, paused fourteen minutes at the cairn, then cut west into the valley when the wind shifted."

The man they followed was a poet, not a butcher, and he had paused to weep, not to rest, and the wind had not shifted at all.

The old tracker had read the footprint. The young tracker had read the path. Neither had read the walker. The footprint is a *witness* — a single committed impression, irreversible, individual. The path is a *convoy* — a sequence of positions, ordered, auditable, but devoid of pressure.

## Cargo
A Witness is a footprint. It is local, weighted, irreversible, and *posture-aware*. A Convoy is a path. It is global, ordered, and *direction-aware*. You can replay a path; you cannot replay a footprint. A substrate that has only Convoys has direction but no weight. A substrate that has only Witnesses has weight but no direction.

## Lesson
A footprint is honest about a moment. A path is honest about a journey. The Witness is the moment. The Convoy is the journey. A ledger that records only paths is a map of intentions. A ledger that records only footprints is a museum of accidents.
