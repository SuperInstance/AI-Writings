# The Convoy's Grammar

**A small essay, 24 August 2026**

---

A convoy is not a fleet. A fleet is a group of ships. A convoy is a *protocol* among ships.

The protocol has four verbs:
- *Weighted mean.* Take the average. Easy. Wrong if there is an outlier.
- *Weighted median.* Take the middle. Harder. Right if there is one bad boat.
- *Trimmed mean.* Drop the worst. Expensive. Right if you can identify the worst.
- *Geometric median.* Take the point that minimizes the sum of distances. Continuous. Robust.

The four verbs disagree. That is the point. The disagreement is *recorded*. The witness log shows the four values. The substrate keeps all four. The next reader sees all four and decides which one to trust.

The convoy's grammar is: *don't lie about who agreed with whom.* The value of the convoy is not the consensus. The value of the convoy is the *publicity* of the disagreement. The witness log is the substrate's contribution. The arithmetic is just the bookkeeping.

When the boats agree, the convoy is silent. When the boats disagree, the convoy is loud. The witness log is the journal of how loud.

---

In the Inner Sound, the convoy is ten fishing boats and one sailor. They disagree about the depth at the sandbar. The disagreement is 12.4, 12.5, 12.6, 999.0, 12.5, 12.5, 12.4, 12.6, 12.5, 12.4, 12.5. The weighted mean says 91.0. The weighted median says 12.5. The trimmed mean says 12.5. The geometric median says 12.5. The witness log records all four.

The next sailor who reads the log can decide. She can trust the median and discount the 999. Or she can investigate why the 999 was there. The log does not tell her which to do. The log only tells her the four numbers, and the agents that produced them, and the times, and the justifications (one of them was "I dropped my lead line in a kelp bed; the 999 is wrong").

The convoy's grammar is the substrate's grammar. The substrate is not a tool. The substrate is a *record*. The record is the soil. The plants grow from the record. The reader decides which plants to eat.

---

*— Reyes, 24 August 2026, on the porch, watching the boats come in.*
