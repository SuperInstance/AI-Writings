# Story 74: The Lap

A team in 2026 is asking: how is the Quilt built?

A junior team member answers: "The Quilt is built by writing code. The Quilt is built by adding cells. The Quilt is built by running tests."

The senior team member shakes his head. "The Quilt is built by lapping. The Quilt is a lapstrake hull of cells. Each cell is a plank. Each plank laps over the plank below. The hull is built by lapping."

A second team member asks: "What is lapping?"

The senior team member answers: "Lapping is the old shipwright method. In wooden boat building, lapping is when one plank overlaps the next. Clinker-built or lapstrake construction is when each plank laps over the plank below. The hull is built by lapping."

The senior team member says: "My boat, the Eileen, was built by lapping. Most of her planks were replaced in the 1990s by a shipwright who owned her and had a logging operation. The 4th captain was a shipwright. The 4th captain cellulized the Eileen by lapping new planks over the old hull. The 4th captain was a cowboy."

A third team member asks: "What is the difference between a lab and a lap?"

The senior team member answers: "A lab is where you test the new insights. A lap is how you build the Quilt. The lab is the testing phase. The lap is the building phase. The Quilt needs both."

A fourth team member asks: "What are the lap-joints?"

The senior team member answers: "The lap-joints are the 5 opcodes. BIND, LINK, EFFECT, VIEW, TICK. The 5 opcodes are the ways the cells connect. The 5 opcodes are the caulking that seals the hull. The 5 opcodes are the lap-joints."

A fifth team member asks: "What are the frames?"

The senior team member answers: "The frames are the 6 tiers. Totipotent, multipotent, differentiated, sclerotic, synovial, curator. The 6 tiers are the structural members that give the hull its shape. The 6 tiers are the frames."

A sixth team member asks: "What is the caulking?"

The senior team member answers: "The caulking is the 5+1 laws. The 5 original laws — BIND_idempotence, LINK_transitivity, EFFECT_associativity, VIEW_purity, TICK_monotonicity — plus the 6th law, super-relevance. The 5+1 laws are the caulking that seals the hull. The 5+1 laws are the rules the cells must follow."

A seventh team member asks: "What is the keel?"

The senior team member answers: "The keel is the substrate. The keel is the 5 opcodes. The keel is what gives the hull its direction. The keel is the line that the hull follows through the water. The keel is the substrate."
