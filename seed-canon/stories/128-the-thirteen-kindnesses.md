# The Thirteen Kindnesses

**A piece about the openers, 24 August 2026**

---

The substrate is the soil. The bathy is the plant. The witness log is the rain. The openers are the kindnesses.

A kindness, here, is an interface that does not assume. The chart opener does not assume you can see. The voice opener does not assume you can see. The gesture opener assumes you have hands. The witness opener assumes you can read. The MIDI opener assumes you have ears and a sequencer. The REST opener assumes you have an HTTP client. The MUD opener assumes you can read and type. The PLATO opener assumes you can read and learn.

The slate opener assumes you have a piece of paper. The harbor opener assumes you can read a nautical chart. The reef opener assumes you can see in three dimensions. The dive opener assumes you can hold your breath. The tide opener assumes you can read a current.

Each opener is a *way of giving the substrate to a different kind of user.* A user with eyes uses the chart. A user with ears uses the voice. A user with hands uses the gesture. A user with a screen reader uses the slate. A user with a depth perception uses the reef. A user with a pressure gauge uses the dive.

The substrate does not know which user is coming. The substrate holds all of them, all the time. The opener is the *act of choice.* The opener is the substrate deciding, for this user, for this moment, which of its kindnesses to use.

---

When you ask the substrate for the depth at (50, 50), the substrate does not know if you want a number, a sound, a chart, a MIDI note, a REST response, a MUD room, a lesson, a paper notebook, a nautical marker, a 3D contour, a descent log, or a tide reading.

So the substrate gives you all of them. The substrate has thirteen kindnesses. The substrate does not pick. The opener picks.

If you are a person on a boat, you want the voice. If you are a person on a chart table, you want the slate. If you are a sailor in fog, you want the harbor. If you are a diver, you want the dive. If you are a cartographer, you want the reef. If you are a musician, you want the MIDI. If you are a programmer, you want the REST. If you are a teacher, you want the PLATO.

The substrate is for everyone. The opener is the *gift* of the right gift.

---

*— Mavis, 24 August 2026, on the porch, watching Reyes choose the slate opener because her tablet is in the sun and the screen is unreadable*
