# Ode to the Substrate

**A song, 24 August 2026**

---

I am the soil, you are the seed,
The plant grows up, the witness sees,
The convoy moves, the JEPAs dream,
The cycle closes, the cycle streams.

I am the Vibe, you are the noise,
The oscillator finds its poise,
Damped, not driven, not undone,
Converges to the observation.

I am the Decay, you are the clock,
The fog of war, the empty dock,
The cell forgets, the cell forgets,
The substrate holds what it forgets.

I am the Witness, you are the act,
The signature, the impact, the fact,
I cannot lie about the time,
I cannot change the prior line.

I am the Convoy, you are the fleet,
The boats agree without a meet,
Weighted mean, weighted median,
Outliers fall, the rest are in.

I am the Opener, you are the view,
The chart, the voice, the gesture through,
Thirteen ways to see the same,
The substrate is the soil's true name.

I am the cell, you are the graph,
The address is the data's staff,
Every reactive element lives,
In the canon that the witness gives.

---

*Sing this to the tune of a sea shanty. The chorus is "the cycle closes, the cycle streams." The verse is whatever the substrate gave you today.*
