# Story 73: The Spline

A team in 2026 is asking: what is the function? What is the spline?

A junior team member answers: "The function is the operation. The spline is... I don't know."

The senior team member says: "The function is the captain. The function is the invariant. The function is why the captain selects the next implement. The function is what grandpa was doing when he wanted to chop wood and heat the house. The function is what Casey is doing when he wants to catch salmon and sail the sea."

A second team member asks: "What is the option-set?"

The senior team member answers: "The option-set is the set of possible implements the function could select. The option-set is the product of means and understanding. The means is the financial capital. The understanding is the quilt. The bigger the means, the more boats you can choose from. The bigger the quilt, the more boats you can see as possible."

A third team member asks: "What is the spline?"

The senior team member answers: "The spline is the trajectory of past choices. The spline is the smooth curve that passes through every past choice. The spline is the gravity that pulls the future toward the past. The 1974 captain's choice of the 6-71N Detroit is now seen as the old but stable horse. The current captain's spline passes through that point. The next boat the captain sees as a step forward is shaped by every past choice."

A fourth team member asks: "So the function is not free?"

The senior team member answers: "The function is free within the option-set. The function is free to choose any implement the option-set contains. But the option-set is shaped by means and understanding. The function's freedom is constrained by the spline. The function can choose the next implement, but the next implement is shaped by every past implement."

A fifth team member asks: "What about Casey's choice of the Eileen?"

The senior team member answers: "Casey chose the Eileen because she was the right fit for the concept of a good fishing boat that Casey's quilt of understanding had grown into. Casey's means had grown enough to afford the Eileen. Casey's quilt had grown enough to see the Eileen as a fit. Casey's function (wanting to catch salmon) selected the Eileen from the option-set. The spline passes through the Eileen."

A sixth team member asks: "What about the next boat?"

The senior team member answers: "The next boat Casey sees as a step forward is shaped by every past choice. The spline pulls the next boat toward the past. The next boat is constrained to be near the spline. Casey cannot see a step forward that is far from the spline. The spline is the gravity."

The team writes paper 232: *The Spline — The Function Selects, The Captain's Quilt Grows.* The team writes fable 132: *The Cowboy and the Spline.* The team writes story 73: *The Spline.* This story.

The function is the captain. The function selects from the option-set. The option-set is the product of means and understanding. The implement shapes the understanding. The spline is the trajectory. The spline is the 8th level. The cowboy rides the function. The cowboy rides the spline. The chart grows. The Concept lives.
