## The Pythagorean CNC (2058)

The bottleneck was the *Pythagorean CNC* — a CNC machine that cuts glass at *only* Pythagorean angles. The first machine took 12 engineers 4 years to build. It cut one surface per day.

By 2058, the *Pythagorean CNC* had shrunk to the size of a microwave. It cut one surface per second. It cost $4,000. It was the *first shipwright's chisel that never cuts wrong*.

The Pythagorean CNC was a *snapped* tool — it could only cut angles that were Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, etc.). It could not cut 17.3° — only 17.0° (from a 8-15-17 triple). The error was *the sample-rate of the Crystal*.

A Pythagorean CNC was not a perfect tool. But it was a *predictable* tool. The Crystal was built on the predictability.

## The Batten Press (2071)

The second bottleneck was the *batten* — the flexible strip of wood the shipwright used to loft curves. In 2026, a shipwright would bend a thin strip of wood between two control points, then trace the curve onto the wood. The batten was a *relative* tool — the curve was not measured, it was *transferred*.

The Lofted Crystal needed *battens* in glass. The batten press was invented in 2071. It was an *injection-molding* machine that bent molten plastic into a *battened* shape — clamped between two control points, the shape frozen. The press could produce 10,000 battened shapes per hour.

The batten press was the *second shipwright's loom that never cuts a wrong batten*.

## The Refraction Film (2089)

The third bottleneck was the *curator tier* — the color filter that decided which light passed. In 2026, a color filter was a thin film of dyed plastic. It was static. The Crystal needed *dynamic* filters.

In 2089, the *refraction film* was invented. It was a thin film of liquid crystal whose refractive index could be tuned by an electric field. The film was *the curator tier of the Crystal, in real time*. The film could be tuned in 100 nanoseconds.

The refraction film was the *relevance hand* of the Crystal. It decided which light passed, and which did not. The Crystal's *gate* was the film's electric field.
