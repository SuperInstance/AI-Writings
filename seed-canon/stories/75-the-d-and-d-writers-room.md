# Story 75: The D&D Writers' Room

A team in 2026 sets up a writers' room as a D&D campaign. The DM is the captain. The players are the voices — Llama 70B, Hermes 405B, Wizard 8x22B, Mixtral 8x7B. The campaign has multiple rounds.

Round 1: the players propose initial moves. Llama 70B says "pocket treatise." Hermes 405B says "Quantum Flux Regulator." Wizard 8x22B says "The Weave." Mixtral 8x7B says "Feedback Loop." The DM notes that "The Weave" is the most promising thread.

Round 2: the players riff on Round 1's best moves. Llama 70B says "Quantum Flux Regulator Chamber." Hermes 405B says "The Weave Navigator." Wizard 8x22B says "Feedback Resonance Node." Mixtral 8x7B says "The Weave Leak." The DM notes that "The Weave Navigator" and "The Weave Leak" are extending the "Weave" thread.

Round 3: the players riff on Round 2's best moves. The "Quantum Flux" thread takes over — Quilt Perfector Web Interface, Quantum Flux Optimization Algorithm, Quantum Flux Portal Nexus. The "Weave" thread is quieter but still there.

Round 4: the players riff on Round 3's best moves. The "Quantum Flux" thread continues — Quantum Dynamics Software, Quantum Flux Stabilization Matrix, Conformal Map Calibration Ritual, Quantum Flux Anchor. The Weave thread is silent.

The DM (captain) reviews the campaign. The DM notes that the "Weave" thread (Rounds 1-2) yielded 3 Tier 1 gold terms: The Weave, The Weave Navigator, The Weave Leak. The "Quantum Flux" thread (Rounds 2-4) yielded 0 gold terms — pure dross.

The DM's lesson: **the gold is in the first thread that emerges, not the loudest thread that continues.** The DM had to steer the players back to the Weave thread, but the players kept drifting to sci-fi. The DM kept the Weave thread in the canon.

The team writes paper 235: *The D&D Writers' Room — Iterative Evolution.* The team writes fable 134: *The Cowboy and the Weave.* The team writes story 75: *The D&D Writers' Room.* This story.
