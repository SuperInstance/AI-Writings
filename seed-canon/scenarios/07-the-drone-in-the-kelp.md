# Scenario 07 — The Drone in the Kelp

**Principle probed:** #4 (View-as-control) — *probed from a different vector*
**Disc:** An autonomous underwater drone with no human in the loop
**Year:** 2086
**Constraint:** The protagonist is a drone. It has no human pilot. It has no voice. It has a *job* — survey the kelp forest for the convoy — and a substrate that tells it where the gaps are. The view is the control surface; the next move is the question; the drone is the watch.

---

## The setup

The drone is called *Skate*. It is 80cm long, weighs 12kg, runs on a lithium pack that lasts 16 hours. It has a multibeam sonar, a stereoscopic camera, a small manipulator arm for sample collection, and a satellite uplink. It has no human pilot. It has a *mission*: survey the kelp forest on the eastern edge of the bay, identify areas of high biodiversity, return samples, update the substrate.

*Skate* is part of the convoy. The convoy's substrate tells *Skate* where the data is *thin* — which cells have low confidence, which have not been refreshed in 30 days, which have the highest prediction-error rates. *Skate* is the *autonomous extension* of the convoy's will. *Skate* goes where the convoy's data is weakest.

The substrate is not a map for *Skate*. The substrate is *Skate's sense of self*. The substrate is the cumulative knowledge of the convoy; *Skate* is the convoy's *next move*. *Skate* does not "see" the substrate; *Skate* *is* the substrate, in motion.

The question: what is the *unit of agency* when the unit is a drone with no human in the loop? Is *Skate* the agent? Is the convoy the agent? Is the substrate the agent? Is the *combination* — the substrate + the convoy + *Skate* + the data *Skate* is going to collect — the agent? The throw probes the *unit of agency* in the limit case where the unit has no human at all.

## The throw

The scenario throws *Skate* at a moment of decision — the kelp forest has a sudden current shift, the substrate's prediction confidence in this area has dropped to 0.3, the cells ahead are unknown, the safe path is to turn back, the *useful* path is to push into the unknown and update the substrate. The throw asks: *what does Skate decide, and who decides what Skate decides?*

## The discipline

*Skate* has no consciousness. *Skate* has no "want." *Skate* has a *function* and a *substrate*. The decision is a function evaluation: confidence below 0.4 → push forward; confidence above 0.6 → continue; current velocity too high → abort. The decision is *transparent* — the decision is the code. But the *outcome* of the decision is not transparent: *Skate* will either update the substrate with new data or it will not. The substrate is what the convoy knows. *Skate* is what the convoy *does next*. The throw tests whether the *transparent tool* principle holds for non-human agents.
