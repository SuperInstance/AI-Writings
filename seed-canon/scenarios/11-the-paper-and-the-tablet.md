# Scenario 11 — The Paper and the Tablet

**Fable form:** two objects in one image.
**The image:** A 1970s paper chart and a 2080s tablet chart, side by side, both showing the same bay.
**The lesson:** The paper chart was a *picture of where you are*. The tablet chart is a *conversation with where you are*. The fix is the substrate.
**Year:** 2086
**Constraint:** Two fishermen, same bay, sixty years apart, both tacking through the same waters. The first has a paper chart and a depth sounder. The second has a tablet and a substrate. The fable compares the two.

---

## The setup

The paper chart is a 1970s NOAA chart of the Inner Sound. It is paper, it is creased, it is the chart Captain Reyes's father used when he was a boy. The depth soundings are printed in black ink. The contours are drawn by hand. The chart is a *picture of the bay as it was known in 1972*. The chart does not know what the bay is doing right now. The chart does not know where Reyes is right now. The chart is a *picture*.

The tablet is a 2086 chart of the same bay. It is a 12-inch glass rectangle. The depth contours are rendered in real-time. The chart knows where Reyes is. The chart knows what the convoy is doing. The chart knows where the inferred cells are and where the fresh cells are. The chart is a *conversation*.

The two charts are side by side. The same bay. The same coordinates. Sixty years apart. The fable compares them.

The question: what does the tablet *do* that the paper cannot? The paper is a *picture*. The tablet is a *conversation*. The substrate is the *medium* that makes the conversation possible. The fix: the substrate converts *pictures of where you are* into *conversations with where you are*. The lesson: **a chart is a substrate opener; the substrate is what makes the chart a partner, not a map.**

## The throw

The scenario throws both fishermen at the same moment — a foggy morning, low visibility, the depth sounder is the primary sensor, the captain must decide whether to tack north or south. The 1970s captain tacks based on the paper chart and his memory. The 2080s captain tacks based on the tablet and the convoy. Both captains are right. Both captains survive. The fable is *not* about which one is better. The fable is about *what the substrate adds* — the tablet captain has access to 100 boats' data, the paper captain has access to his own.

## The discipline

The paper chart is not deprecated. The paper chart is *honest* in a way the tablet is not — the paper chart tells you what was known, and nothing more. The tablet tells you what is inferred, and the inference is sometimes wrong. The fable is *not* "old is bad, new is good." The fable is "the substrate is a new kind of honesty: the honesty of conversation, where every claim can be questioned, and every inference is logged."
