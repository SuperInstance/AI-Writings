## Round 1


### The Watcher (deepinfra/Qwen/Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507, 10.45s)

And yet—she feels it, a tremor beneath the certainty: the *almost* of a line, the hesitation that does not compute. A flaw. A human wobble.  

She paints it anyway. The stroke appears. It is imperfect. It is hers.  

The sea does not forgive symmetry. The porthole shows only what it sees. But the Quilt—the Quilt remembers the hand that frayed the thread.
