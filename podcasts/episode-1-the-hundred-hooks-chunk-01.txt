Pull every hour. Reset. Pull again. Each hour, a new vector of a hundred booleans. Each hour, a new snapshot of what the ocean decided to give you. Over the course of a day in a specific area, you see patterns emerge. There's a high probability band between ten and twenty fathoms. The best is at about twelve fathoms. Different numbers show up at different locations. Different depths. Sometimes different directions.

But every individual hook is still just a yes or a no. The pattern isn't in any single hook. It exists only in the relation across all of them.
