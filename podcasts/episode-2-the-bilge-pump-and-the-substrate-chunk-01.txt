The bilge is the curve where the hull meets the keel. The bottom of everything. And everything drains there. Water, oil, coolant, the dregs of every system on board. It collects what every other component sheds. The pump’s job is simple. It moves the mixture back out. It runs automatically. No one thinks about it until it stops. And then everyone thinks about it very urgently.

...

You ever think about that? The most important component on the ship is the one nobody respects.
