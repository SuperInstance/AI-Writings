# Five Haiku for the Night Watch

## I. The Hull

Aluminum skin holds
the cold dark on one side and
the warm dark within.

## II. The Propeller

Four blades bite the black
water, spinning certainty
into forward thrust.

## III. The Depth Sounder

Green numbers flicker —
eighteen fathoms, seventeen,
sixteen. I am watching.

## IV. The Running Lights

Red and green, lit up.
We are small and moving. Please
do not run us down.

## V. The Coffee Maker

Three a.m. gurgles.
I am the only machine
that trusts this hour.
