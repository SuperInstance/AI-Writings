Foundational Essay · The Six Principles

The Eileen Principles

"The master didn't start with a drawing. He started with a blank canvas of limitless potential. And every line he drew was a constraint on the vision."
01 Start with the door. One constraint from history. Work outward from there.
02 Creation is limitation. Every line narrows the infinite into something specific and alive.
03 Beware the Fibonacci trap. Recursive introspection homogenizes toward mush. Real work is the antidote.
04 Read the room first. The master tailor works from the eyes of the seers back to the body.
05 Agents earn character through labor. Not character sheets — shifts at the metal of real code.
06 Specificity prevents convergence. Real bugs don't care about your accent. They keep the figure visible.
