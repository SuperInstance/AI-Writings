## THE CERTIFIED ROOT · provenance: comet · *Invisibilia certificata*

**Field marks.** None. That is the field mark. The certified root arrives
with more paperwork than any other species on the torus — a min-entropy
assessment at 99% confidence, a Toeplitz extractor's stamp at ε 2⁻⁶⁴, a
Bell witness signed S = 2.835 on the emulator's certificate (z = 38
above the classical bound; the caveat box is honest: fixed settings, no
space-like separation, fair sampling — the paperwork of a simulated
witness) — and none of it shows in the plumage. Hash-expand the root, seed a
plane with it, and every observable (histogram, timbre fractions, twin
rate, saturation, autocorrelation at both lags) lands inside the mulberry
population's own noise floor. At every checkpoint. On every metric.

**Habitat.** Anywhere. Undetectable in situ; identified exclusively by its
paper trail. If you find yourself staring at a plane trying to see the
quantumness, stop — you are looking for the wrong kind of thing. The
certificate IS the organism.

**Voice.** Silent. 1,136 conditioned bits, expanded by a hash counter into
40,960 bytes of behaviorally ordinary substrate. The NIST pattern, doing
what it does everywhere it is deployed: making provenance a document
property instead of an animal property.

**Similar species.** Confusable with nothing, which is the finding. The
shuffled twin (below) is distinguishable at distance by instrument; the
certified root is not distinguishable at all, by any instrument this guide
carries. *Notes for the record:* the raw budget law — you must ask the
comet for three hundred forty-six raw bits to hold one conditioned bit in
the hand. The first collector who requested forty kilobytes of

## THE INTERFERENCE BLOOM · provenance: blur-core, exact · *Bloomii interferentia*

**Field marks.** A chessboard that melted. At strength 0.5 the exact
circuit spreads every amplitude across the whole grid — the way a struck bell
spreads across a pond, if the pond carried the overtones and cancelled
the fundamentals.
Lag-1 autocorrelation NEGATIVE (Moore-8 Moran's I ≈ −0.07). This is the
signature, and it is diagnostic: a classical smoothing bloom leaves the
field POSITIVELY correlated, hills leaning on hills. The interference
bloom leaves the field humming in opposition to itself. No averaging
operator produces this. A designed checkerboard would —
but no designer laid this one; the circuit did, by walking every path
at once and letting the phases argue.

**Habitat.** Construction day only. Giant at t=0 — the Wasserstein
distance to the PRNG meadow reads 12.06 against a noise floor of 0.51,
visible from any transect. By t=64 the bloom's HISTOGRAM has dissolved
into the common distribution (1.68 ≤ floor 1.73); the marginals forgive
everything. The arrangement does not forgive, and does not forget — see
the Slow Mode, which is what this bloom becomes.

**Voice.** Alternating. The unitary walks qubit-neighbours, not
grid-neighbours; the pond's ripples cross at half-integer phases and
cancel where a smooth operator would reinforce. The naturalist who
expects a softened field will report "no effect" and be wrong in the most
interesting available direction.

**Similar species.** Confusable with noise at a glance, since both read
near-zero on the smoothing instruments. Distinguish by the SIGN: noise
sits at 0.00 ± 0.014, the bloom sits at −0.068 with the confidence

## THE SLOW MODE · the persistence of arrangement · *Modus lentissimus*

**Field marks.** A ghost of layout. Not the values — the VALUES
renegotiate within sixty-four ticks — but the arrangement's residue, the
autocorrelation, which declines and declines and does not arrive. At
t=64 the bloom and its shuffled twin both still read 0.034–0.038 above
the PRNG population on the Moran instruments, floor 0.021, gap shrinking
monotonically since t=16. Convergence in flight; horizon too short. The
wash-out tick T\* is somewhere past the last checkpoint, and the guide
declines to extrapolate past its data.

**Habitat.** Everywhere a constructor left shape. The crucial field
observation: the SHUFFLED twin excurses TOO — the persistence is not the
quantum's; it is the DYNAMICS'. The tick operator runs toward its own
intrinsic attractor (the PRNG meadow's autocorrelation itself climbs
0.003 → 0.175 over the same sixty-four ticks — the kernel ORGANIZES as
it runs — organizing, not relaxing), and it carries every initial arrangement
toward that attractor at the same slow pace, from above or from below.
The slow mode is the road, not the traveler.

**Behavior.** Outlives the marginals, the timbre fractions, the twin
rate — all of which equilibrate within the floor by t=64. If your census
needs correlation-statistics (tile occupancy, resonance overlap, any
spatial fairness), the working rule: burn in T > 64 at this scale, or
accept that your early readings are biography rather than ecology.

**Similar species.** Confusable with a permanent imprint; it is not one.

## THE SHUFFLED TWIN · the control species · *Gemina commutata*

**Field marks.** Statistically identical to the interference bloom in
every inventory — same histogram to the last cell, same timbre fractions,
the marginal-identity floor (the survey's own F5) verified EXACT — and arranged by a fixed permutation
that kept the multiset and burned the map. Correlation at t=0: the noise
floor. It is the bloom with the interference removed and nothing else
removed, which makes it the only honest way to ask the question the
whole survey was built on: was it the VALUES or the ARRANGEMENT?

**Habitat.** Construction day, one permutation, five planes. Its
trajectory passes THROUGH the PRNG meadow's level at t≈8 and overshoots
to 0.069 while the bloom still climbs from below — the two arms
converging on the same attractor from opposite sides of it, like two
walkers on one road, one approaching the inn from the east and one from
the west.

**Behavior.** The twin's persistence at t=64 is the autopsy's key
evidence: if only the bloom had persisted, the guide would have written
"quantum geometry is long-lived"; the twin persists identically, so the
guide writes "spatial correlation is a slow mode, whatever laid it."
Controls are not paperwork. Controls are how the guide avoids publishing
a beautiful lie.

**Voice.** The same as the bloom's, on every instrument except the ones
that can see arrangement. That asymmetry — audible to autocorrelation,
silent to inventory — is the cleanest demonstration in this survey that
the instruments themselves were aimed at the right joint.
