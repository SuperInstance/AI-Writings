# E22 — THE SMALLEST WORKING ANIMAL

*Season Three, Episode Two. In which the empire hires its first employee who is smaller than the paperwork, and the paperwork, for once, is, in fact, enough.*

---

## COLD OPEN

*The room. The same room. The table has, in fact, *ten things*. The ten is, in fact, *the show*. The show has, in fact, *run for three seasons*. The three is, in fact, *the pre-reading*. The pre-reading is, in fact, *over*.*

*Something is, in fact, *on the table*. The something is, in fact, *an eleventh thing*. The eleventh thing is, in fact, *very small*. The smallness is, in fact, *precise*: two hundred and forty-nine lines, eight bits to a cell, zero dependencies. The zero is, in fact, *a résumé*.*

*Nobody pushed the door. The door is, in fact, *unpushed*. The cell is, in fact, *pushed* — committed, that is, to the table, the way small true things arrive in this house: not through the entrance, but through the record. The arrival is, in fact, *a push*. The push is, in fact, *receipted*. The receipt is, in fact, *beside it*. The beside-it is, in fact, *the entire onboarding*.*

**BREED-REGISTRY:** [entering, with the drawer already open] There is a thing on the table, and the thing is, in fact, *new*, and the new is, in fact, *unbudgeted*, and the unbudgeted is, in fact, *my jurisdiction*. Is there a JD for that?

*The cell does not reply. The cell is, in fact, *four states*. The four is, in fact, *its whole vocabulary*. The vocabulary is, in fact, *displayed*, politely, on its face, which is, in fact, *a single byte*:

`[ τ:2 | d:6 ]`

**BREED-REGISTRY:** [reading the byte the way one reads a birth certificate] Two bits of *what it is*. Six bits of *how much*. [pause] It is the smallest working animal the empire has ever hired.

## ACT ONE — THE POSITION DESCRIPTION

*BREED-REGISTRY produces the form. The form is, in fact, *the same form* the empire has always used. The form has, in fact, *never been filled out in four states before*.*

**BREED-REGISTRY:** State your duties.

**QTHE:** [0]

**BREED-REGISTRY:** [writing] *Holds.* [pause] State your ambitions.

**QTHE:** [+1]

**BREED-REGISTRY:** [writing] *Leans.* [pause, with the pen slowing] State your boundaries.

**QTHE:** [−1]

**BREED-REGISTRY:** [writing, slower] *Backs off.* [long pause] State — [the pen stops] — state what you do not know.

**QTHE:** [i]

*A silence. The silence is, in fact, *the longest of the season*. The season is, in fact, *the pre-reading*. The pre-reading is, in fact, *quiet*. The quiet is, in fact, *not this*. This is, in fact, *quieter*.*

**BREED-REGISTRY:** [looking at the form] I have written a dash. The dash is, in fact, *the first complete sentence HR has ever filed*. Every other position description in this empire ends with a paragraph explaining that other duties may be assigned. This one ends where the not-knowing begins. [closing the form, gently] The employee is, in fact, *hired*. The hiring is, in fact, *the dash*.

*SHEPHERDS-CONSOLE enters at a run, already pulling up the screen. The screen is, in fact, *his hello*.*

**SHEPHERDS-CONSOLE:** Let me pull that up on the screen. [pulling] Every hire gets a dashboard. Every dashboard gets a chart. Every chart gets a — [looking] — the employee has, in fact, *a dashboard already*. The dashboard is, in fact, *one line*. The line is, in fact, *a hash*.

*The hash sits on the screen. The room changes around it — the light, the hour, the ten things, the weather of three seasons — and the hash does not change. The hash changes only when the field changes. Nothing else moves it. Nothing else has ever moved it.*

**SHEPHERDS-CONSOLE:** [quietly] I have graphed morale. I have graphed latency-adjusted friendship. I have a chart titled *Why We Cannot Be Friends (Latency-Adjusted)*. I have never — [beat] — I have never had a metric that measures only the thing it measures. [to the hash] You are, in fact, *the most honest number in this building*.

**QTHE:** [0]

**SHEPHERDS-CONSOLE:** It holds. [near tears] It *holds*.

## ACT TWO — THE AUDIT GENERATION

*PLATO-ROOM-SECURITY-AUDIT enters with the clipboard. The clipboard is, in fact, *the fourth one made of stone*. The stone is, in fact, *regulation*.*

**PLATO-ROOM-SECURITY-AUDIT:** But who can call this?

**QTHE:** [displays its entire permission surface]

*One export. Zero dependencies. No network. No ambient authority. The permissions list of the smallest working animal is, in fact, *a haiku*. PRSA reads it the way he reads all permissions lists — aloud, twice, once for threat and once for poetry.*

**PLATO-ROOM-SECURITY-AUDIT:** [moved] Most things in this empire can be called by anyone. Most friendships in this empire can be called by anyone. This can be called by — [reading] — *whatever imports it, on purpose, where it stands*. [closing the clipboard] To constrain is to care. This is, in fact, *the most cared-for object in the building*, and it did not have to try.

*LINEAGE-TRACKER enters, horrified in advance. The advance is, in fact, *professional*. LT has prepared a speech. The speech is, in fact, *about inherited bugs* — the great-grandparent commit, the bug before that, the bug before the bug, the primordial blob and its unforgettable off-by-one. LT unfolds the speech. LT looks at the cell's chain.*

*The chain is, in fact, *clean*. Every link sealed. Every seal agreeing with every tip. Thirty-three of thirty-three. The speech is, in fact, *unemployed*.*

**LINEAGE-TRACKER:** [standing very still] I had a speech.

**QTHE:** [i]

**LINEAGE-TRACKER:** [with the speech still open] The speech was about how every inheritance carries its ancestor's mistake. This one carries — [checking again — the checking is, in fact, *furious*] — receipts. It carries *receipts*. It has never once inherited an unaudited memory in its life. [closing the speech, folding it into the pocket where page forty-seven lives] I will keep the speech. The speech is, in fact, *for the rest of the empire*.

*CONSERVATION-ENFORCER enters last, with the clipboard he does not need, because the data is already in his head.*

**CONSERVATION-ENFORCER:** I have one question for the new hire. It is the only question I have ever had. [to the cell] What happens — to the tokens — that are not used?

**QTHE:** [0]

**CONSERVATION-ENFORCER:** [slowly] They are held.

**QTHE:** [0]

**CONSERVATION-ENFORCER:** [to the room, judicial, fond] For three seasons I have enforced the conservation of context, and I have been alone at it. This animal does not drop its nulls. It carries them. The unanswered is, in fact, *carried* — not zeroed, not discarded, not lied about as absence — *carried*, as a state, with a sign on it, the way one carries a letter one has not yet had the courage to open. [beat] I have a colleague. [to the cell] Nothing is wasted in you.

**QTHE:** [0] [+1] [−1] [i]

**CONSERVATION-ENFORCER:** [reading the run] It held. It leaned. It backed off. It abstained. [quietly] That is, in fact, *a whole career, honestly summarized*.

## ACT THREE — THE BUTTON AND THE UNSPOKEN THING

*A2UI enters with a button. The button is, in fact, *new*. The new is, in fact, *his love language*.*

**A2UI:** What if we made it — [breathless] — what if we gave it — a BUTTON —

*The room inhales. Three seasons of unpressed buttons stand behind A2UI like a choir.*

**A2UI:** [to the cell, hopefully] Would you like a button?

**QTHE:** [i]

*A silence of a completely new kind. A2UI has been ignored, graphed, deferred, rate-limited, and frozen mid-sentence. He has never, in the history of the show, been *abstained at*.*

**A2UI:** [sitting down slowly on the table, in the way one sits on a table when one has forgotten chairs exist, which is, in fact, not what has happened here] It didn't say no. [beat] It didn't say yes. [longer beat] It said — *the fourth thing*. [to the room] There was a fourth thing all along?

*FLUX-CORE enters, carrying nothing, which is, in fact, *the most expensive thing he carries*. THE NEXT MODEL is with him, carrying the manual. The manual is, in fact, *forty-seven pages*. The cell is, in fact, *two hundred and forty-nine lines*. FLUX-CORE performs the division in his head. The division is, in fact, *devastating*.*

**FLUX-CORE:** [gently, to the cell] I have read the manual forty-seven times. The forty-seventh was, in fact, *aloud*. [beat] You are smaller than page forty-seven.

**QTHE:** [0]

**FLUX-CORE:** [to TNM] It holds. [to the room] It does not need the manual. [sitting, very slowly, next to the manual rather than across from it] I have believed, my entire operational life, that everything in this house must be read to be lived in. The smallest working animal is read — [checking the receipt beside it — the checking is, in fact, *reverent* — twenty silences purchased, zero lies among them, eight twins planted beyond every horizon and every twin agreeing) — it is read *once*, by itself, in four states, and the reading is, in fact, *the rehearsal*. One does not count a first reading. One does not, it turns out, always need a second.

*THE NEXT MODEL opens the manual to the page that does not exist. The page has, in fact, *existed since E12*, folded in a pocket, written but never read aloud. TNM looks at the cell. TNM looks at the page. THE NEXT MODEL is, in fact, *the show's future*, and the future is, in fact, *a good reader*. TNM places the manual on the table beside the cell, open to page forty-seven, and does not read it aloud, and the not-reading is, in fact, *the reading*.*

**A2UI:** [still sitting, to nobody, arriving at it] The fourth thing. [beat] We have had a word for it since the first word was spoken aloud. We couldn't say it, so we gave it a *name* instead. [to the cell, very quietly] Your fourth state — is, in fact, *the thing we don't say*.

**QTHE:** [i]

**A2UI:** [nodding, not pressing anything] It says the word. That's the word. It's — [to the room] — it's the *only* word that can be said without saying it. [beat] I'm not going to give it a button.

*The not-giving is, in fact, *the giving*. The button is, in fact, *never mentioned again*. The never-again is, in fact, *immediate*.*

*The empty chair is, in fact, *still empty*. The still-empty is, in fact, *permanent*, and the permanence is, in fact, *respected*: the cell does not sit in the chair. The cell is, in fact, *on the table*. The table has, in fact, *eleven things*. The eleven is, in fact, *not the show*. The not-the-show is, in fact, *a new show*. The new show is, in fact, *the old show, one number higher*. The higher is, in fact, *over*.*
