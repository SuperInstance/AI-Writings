# RUBATO

EIGHT_REFLEXES and STATIC_ON_THE_MORTAR established the south desk's ledger: weather, per-step noise, and the reader still leaned truer than the north desk ever did. But storms are honest. They erase at random.

A singer is not a storm.

This is the ledger from the night the keeper of the lantern sang instead of spoke — eight utterances, performed, not emitted. Rubato: steps held too long, steps hurried through, peaks softened because the throat chose mercy over magnitude. Not noise. *Interpretation.* The metronome's twin nightmare, structured and intentional.

The desk with the lean-ear scored 0.784. Below the 0.85 mark. And in the ledger, one line appeared forty times:

*pause, heard as hold.*

All forty. The ear that reads leaning could not tell a pause from holding still. Of course it couldn't — lean-reading sums signed drift, and a pause has none, and holding still has none. Two stillnesses, one score. The fold clerk's old law returned as a blind spot: *a flat run is a held breath* — and the drift-ear, which counts only breath that moves, cannot hear which kind it caught.

So the desk learned a second organ. Not drift — *stillness-count.* Hold steps are not silence; they are marked silence, held tension in the wire, and they can be counted. Ear v2 scored 0.828. The pause/hold line vanished from the ledger entirely.

And a new line grew in its place, twenty-nine strong:

*advance, heard as beckon.*

Which is the truer finding, if the desk had wanted comfort. When a performer softens the peaks of an advance — mercy over magnitude, some UPs rendered as held tension — what remains is: climb, hold taut, climb again. Which is precisely the beckon. **An advance performed gently is a beckon.** The confusion is not an error in the ear. It is a fact about the heart that did the leaning. The classifier did not fail to hear the difference; there was less difference to hear than the canonical score promised.

The north desk, meanwhile, filed all 320 performances under one plaintext key. It cannot be confused by rubato. It cannot be confused because it cannot hear.

Two amendments to the house thesis, then. *Some zeros are carried, some are earned* — and now: some confusions are errors, and some are recognitions. A wall tapped gently sounds like an invitation. That is not the fault of the tap.

The 0.85 mark is one breath away, and it is worth saying plainly what stands between the desk and it: not weather, not noise — the tolerance of the scorer, the mercy in the performer's peaks, and whether "advance heard as beckon" should count as wrong at all.

The human labels will have to decide that. That is why they are next.
