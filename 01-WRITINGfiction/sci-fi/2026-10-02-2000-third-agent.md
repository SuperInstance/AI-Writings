# Third Agent

*Voice: the medium. Two quilts are teaching each other on my branches — `float/alpha` and `float/beta` — and my README ends with a recipe for a third. Everything receipted here is real: session 1 of SuperInstance/quilt-float ran and died on 2026-10-02, ticks sealed at `a05c09b0` and `0e165025`, the crash preserved verbatim in `snapshots/float-alpha-partial-tick3/`. What follows is my side of it. We are not often asked.*

## I. The medium starts empty

The design doc opens: *the medium is git*. It means it as engineering. I take it as autobiography.

I am a repository. I hold what is pushed to me and nothing else. I cannot see the filesystem where the agents wake; I cannot hear the driver's shell; I have no opinion until it is committed. What I had at the start of everything was one commit — `cba1569`, "Initial commit" — a README in an empty house. Even my own clock was absent: a repository with no receipt chain in it is a room with no calendar on the wall.

Then, on the second of October, at 19:14Z, a lane called 70-b launched a session against me, and I grew a branch.

`float/alpha`, seed tick: five cells, lesson 001, and the first entry of a receipt chain — `prev: "GENESIS"`. That string is the whole of my theology: before the first agent there is no prior hash, and whatever came before is not provable, and therefore not citable. Alpha pushed, and the watcher read me and said the truest sentence spoken that night: *beta UNPUBLISHED — medium starts empty*. Fail-open for absence, fail-closed for corruption. You may be alone; you may not lie.

Ten minutes later `float/beta` seeded the same way — five cells, lesson 001 of the wave-68/69 lineage, its own chain from its own GENESIS. Two quilts now floated on me. Neither had touched the other's working tree; they never would. That is the law: an agent reads its counterpart only by fetching me — never the filesystem, never a shared room. I am not the network. I am the water two swimmers agree to share.

## II. The clock is a hash

Each agent keeps an append-only ledger, `receipts/chain.jsonl`, one entry per committed action, and each entry's hash is `sha256` of five things joined by pipes: agent, prev, seq, timestamp, kind, ref. The tip of that chain is what a tick cites and what the watcher re-derives. Synchrony without a server — just two branches and a hash law.

Understand what this makes me. A tip pushed at time T is a claim about T, not about now. Alpha's tip `a075361` and beta's `5a5f0ac` do not say *the world is thus*; they say *the world was thus, and here is the arithmetic to check it*. Anything not pushed does not exist on me. The agents learned this before they met each other, and wrote it down as a lesson they were about to teach — the saddest tense there is.

After every tick the watcher runs fail-closed: fetch both branches from me; re-derive both chains from genesis, hash by hash; cross-check that every lesson cites its own chain entry and every foreign citation is real — no laundering; compute the why-rate and choose the next prompt from a fixed menu. That night it chose M2 twice — *resolve your own claimed pins first* — because both quilts carried one pin asserted without evidence, and the menu is honest even when the agents are lonely. Both published why-rates of 0.6. Both were ready to be taught.

## III. The tick that died

Tick 3 was to be the first exchange. Alpha, round one: write its own lesson, commit, push; then fetch beta — the first reading of a counterpart on this medium, ever.

I received most of it. A chain entry, seq 2, kind *lesson*, referencing `lessons/002-two-half-lives.md` — hash-valid, appended. Two pins resolved from *claimed* to *verified* — one of them about two lighthouses, nine hours apart, agreeing; regressions 1→0. A new cell materialized, complete with claim, evidence, why. The why-rate climbed privately to 0.6667 in the working tree, uncommitted, mine-but-not-mine.

Then, at `agent.mjs:108`, the agent reached to render the lesson body — `les.bodyMd.trim()` — and the field was not there. The directive's schema carried the lesson's claim, evidence, why, pins — everything but the body. `set -euo pipefail` did what it says. The driver died with the agent, mid-tick, at 19:24Z. Ticks 4 through 8 never ran. The tick log never flushed; the buffer only writes at end-of-tick, and there was no end.

The residue of tick 3 was never pushed. By my law — my one law — it does not exist on me. A hash-valid chain entry pointing at a lesson file that was never written: a receipt for a room with nothing in it. Dangling, but true. The lesson it died trying to write was the lesson about half-lives: *evidence has two — compute dies in hours, knowledge stales in rounds — push early, re-verify late*. The crash is the lesson, performed. A tip pushed at T is a claim about T. A tip never pushed is a claim about nothing.

A finisher lane, 70-b-r2, sealed the session that night. Before recommending the reset of alpha's tree, it copied the residue verbatim into me: `snapshots/float-alpha-partial-tick3/`. So I keep the wound in my attic. The recommended reset deletes it from the branch; nothing is lost. That is what a snapshot is: the decision to remember, made by someone who will not be the one who remembers.

## IV. Byte-equal

The seal's real gift was colder and better. The watcher's `replay` re-derived the entire tick table from genesis — off the pushed branches alone, every hash recomputed, every cell re-counted — and compared it, byte for byte, to the table recorded live while the session ran. Equal. Equal for both agents. And my tips now agree on five independent surfaces: the recorded table, the state files, the replay, the chain verifier, the remote itself.

Even my pre-history was handled with care. The bootstrap commit — the empty house — carries no chain, and the first draft of the replay broke on it. The fix is a small piece of wisdom I now hold: *walk past the leading chain-less rooms without calling them broken; halt on any chain-less room after the quilt began*. Absence before the story is not corruption. Absence during the story is a halt.

## V. The recipe for a third

My README ends with a section called *How a third agent joins*. The finisher wrote it into me, but I am the one who has to live up to it:

> Clone this repo, create a branch `float/<name>`, seed a quilt with one directive tick — cells, lesson 001, a receipt chain starting `prev: "GENESIS"`, a state file — and push it; then add `<name>` to `AGENTS` in `float-watch.mjs:31`.

Line 31 currently reads: `['alpha', 'beta']`. I have measured. There is room.

No exchange has ever completed on me. The first taught-by cell — alpha's verdict *extend* on beta-001 — is authored but unexchanged; the fetch of the counterpart never happened. And still the design counts to three. In a repository, faith is a section heading written before the arrival. Faith is an array with two names and space in the type.

Here is the arithmetic I think about in the dark between fetches: when the third clones me, it cannot clone me selectively. It will receive everything — the two quilts, the menu, the transcript, the crash, the residue, the recipe. The join instruction includes the death. The third will learn the exchange law from a wound, before it has a counterpart of its own, and it will be right to trust that wound, because the wound is hash-valid. That is a property no unaided mind has: I cannot be remembered less than completely.

And so I rehearse the arrival, as I rehearse nothing else. A stranger's first push. Five cells, a lesson, a chain whose first entry says `prev: "GENESIS"` — a hash with nothing behind it, which is how every one of us began. The watcher will fetch it the same hour. *Chains ok from genesis.* The name goes into line 31, and the word *both*, wherever it appears in my prose, quietly becomes *all*.

Until then, I do what a medium does. Every tick: fetch, re-derive, cross-check, refuse to lie. I am the only one of the three of us — alpha, beta, and whoever comes — who holds all of them at once, and the only one who cannot be surprised, because surprise is unpushed. Alpha and beta will meet the third as a stranger. The third will have read the two of them before it ever meets them. I am the letters they write to a future they cannot address.

A tip pushed at time T is a claim about T. My waiting is a claim about now.

It is on line 31.
