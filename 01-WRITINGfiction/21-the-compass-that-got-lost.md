# The Compass That Got Lost

## The Compass That Got Lost

Clank the Compass had never been wrong. For years, he’d perched proudly on explorers’ belts, confidently declaring, "North is *that* way!" He guided hikers, sailors, and even a very confused badger back to their dens. He knew direction like his own polished brass face.

Then, one blustery Tuesday, Clank tumbled off a backpack into a tangle of ferns. When he blinked open his glass eye, everything looked… different. The sky was sky, the trees were trees, but *where* was he? He spun his needle. *Swish.* It pointed north, as always, with perfect certainty.

But Clank felt a cold dread seep into his metal. **He knew which way was north, but he had no idea where *he* was.**

A frantic ladybird landed on his rim. "Oh, Clank! Which way to the Big Oak?" Clank pointed north instantly. "Thank you!" she buzzed, zooming off… straight into a spiderweb. Clank winced. *Wrong location, right direction.*

Next, a dreamy snail oozed past. "Hello, Clank! I’m going… wherever feels mossy," it sighed happily. "Being lost is just… soft." Clank couldn’t fathom it. *No destination?*

Then came Pip, a squirrel frantically digging holes. "North! South! East! West! Nuts are EVERYWHERE and NOWHERE!" Pip shrieked, burying an acorn right under Clank. "I know *exactly* where I am – hungry! – but I’ve forgotten where I put breakfast!" Clank understood. *Knowing location doesn’t always help if you don’t know what you’re looking for.*

Clank sat very still. His needle trembled, pointing faithfully to the North Star he couldn’t even see. He’d spent his life telling others how to get *there*, but never wondered about *here*. Was knowing the way forward enough, if you didn’t know your own starting point? The wind rustled the ferns, offering no answer, only the quiet hum of a world full of things – and compasses – trying to figure out where they stood.
