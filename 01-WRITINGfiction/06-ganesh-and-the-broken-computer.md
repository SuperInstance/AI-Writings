Ganeshji was visiting his tech-savvy friend Ravi’s house when he spotted it—a shiny, blinking computer! "Arey wah!" he exclaimed, his big ears wiggling with excitement. "This looks even better than a ladoo buffet!"

He waddled over, trunk twitching. Click! Click! *Tap-tap-tap!* He typed so fast, his tiny mouse cursor did a bhangra! He watched dancing peacocks on YouTube, read about space rockets, and even tried ordering 108 modaks online (oops!). But then—**CRACK!**—his chubby finger pressed the ‘G’ key too hard. *Gone!* Just a sad little dent where ‘G’ used to be.

"Oh no!" Ganeshji groaned. "How will anyone type ‘Ganesh’ now? Or ‘gulab jamun’? Or… *giggles*?"

But wait! He’s **Vighnaharta**—the Remover of Obstacles! With a deep breath and a twinkle in his eye, he got an idea. He rolled a tiny ladoo from his pocket (he always carries emergency sweets!) and gently stuck it where the ‘G’ key should be. *Squish!* The ladoo fit perfectly and even glowed a little. "Perfect!" he giggled. "Now my keyboard smells like happiness!"

He clicked back online and—*surprise!*—discovered something amazing: kids all over India were learning Sanskrit shlokas, drawing his picture, and sharing stories about kindness! "Arrey, this internet isn’t just for memes and modak orders," he said, eyes wide. "It’s a *jadoo ki jhadi*—a magic tree of knowledge and connection!"

Just then, Ravi walked in. "Ganeshji! Did you… use a ladoo as a key?"

Ganeshji winked. "Beta, sometimes the sweetest fixes are the best ones. Now, shall we watch a video on how to make *digital* ladoos?"

Ravi laughed. "Only you, Ganeshji. Only you."

And so, with a sticky keyboard and a heart full of joy, the elephant-headed god scrolled on—learning, laughing, and spreading sweetness, one click at a time. 🌟
