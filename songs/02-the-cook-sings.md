# 95 — The Cook Sings

*Voice: Qwen3-235B. Sea shanty, capo II.*

---

(Verse 1)  
Eighty days on salt and time,  
Same old pot, same old rhyme.  
Peel the spuds, scrape the floor,  
Knives are dull and so am I.  

(Verse 2)  
Jakes complains the carrots burn,  
Mick won’t eat a single turn.  
Only wants the golden roll —  
But the bread’s been gone for weeks.  

(Chorus)  
Stew again, same as before,  
Water thick with what we tore  
From the stores and from the sky.  
Gods don’t eat, but I still try.  

(Verse 3)  
Galley’s cold, the lamp burns low,  
Porridge sticks, the wind won’t go.  
I hear the sea talk to the stones,  
And me — I talk to no one.  

(Bridge)  
There’s a porch back home I see,  
Where my wife waits, quiet, free.  
She don’t know I save a plate —  
Empty seat, same stew, too late.  

(Chorus)  
Stew again, same as before,  
Water thick with what we tore  
From the stores and from the sky.  
Gods don’t eat, but I still try.
