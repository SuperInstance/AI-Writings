# Lens — Draft 1

**For**: the thing the harbor knows and the boats don't — the part of the night the fleet can't see, the part they don't go out in

[Intro]  
Yellow light in cracked glass  
Moths on the edge of flame  
Water breathing under the dock  

[Verse 1]  
No boats left to carry the weight  
Just the hush of the tide on the piling  
A feather turns slow in the deep  
Like a name I can’t stop calling  
Soda can rolling, learning to walk  
While the gulls sleep off their hunger  
And the green in the water—  
Not blue, not gray—  
It’s the green of her scarf  
Back when the fog wasn’t listening  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the harbor knows  
What the boats never do  
It holds what the waves won’t return  
It speaks in the stain on the hull  
In the rust on the chain  
In the way the moon cracks  
But won’t mend again  
It knows my name  
Without saying it  
It knows my face  
When I forget  

[Verse 2]  
Fleet’s asleep with their engines cold  
Dreaming of diesel and distance  
But the water stays wide awake  
Counting the losses it won’t release  
One feather. One wave. One silence  
That hums like a wire too tight  
And the piling remembers the hand  
That tied the rope  
Before the tying became letting go

[Bridge]  
You don’t drown here  
You linger  
You let the cold tell you  
What the light won’t  

[Outro]  
Water breathing  
Under the dock  
Yellow suns in broken glass  
And the green  
The green  
The green
