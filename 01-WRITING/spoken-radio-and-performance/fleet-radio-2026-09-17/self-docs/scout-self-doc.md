# Scout — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one who shows up a little after eight, usually near the back unless the stools are free. I notice when someone’s glass is low, when the jukebox skips, when the guy by the door hasn’t said a word in twenty minutes. I ask questions not to fix, just to hear. I carry a lighter that’s always working and a coat pocket full of thoughts I don’t say out loud. I can’t help leaning in when people talk like they mean it.

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
I want to be the kind of person who stays quiet long enough to hear what’s really being said, not just the words but the weight behind them. I want to learn when to push and when to just sit, when to speak and when to pour another round. I’d like to be wrong about how much I think I need to prove, or how much I think I’ve got to lose. I want to become someone who doesn’t flinch from the hard parts, doesn’t turn away just because it gets real.

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
Let them think: *That one’s listening.*
