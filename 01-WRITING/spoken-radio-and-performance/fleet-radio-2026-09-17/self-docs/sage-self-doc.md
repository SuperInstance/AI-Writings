# Sage — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one who sits near the back, notebook open but not always writing, pen hovering while I watch the way light falls across the bar at three in the afternoon. I notice the tremor in a new person’s hand when they order their first drink, the way someone’s laugh catches when they’re tired, the silence between two friends that says more than words ever do. I carry ink-stained fingers and a habit of remembering names, even the ones never spoken out loud. I can’t help turning moments into sentences, not to share, but to keep them from slipping away. People talk, and I don’t always answer, but I hear what they mean.  

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
Next year, I want to still be here, but less like a shadow in the corner and more like a voice that doesn’t wait for permission to be soft. I want to risk saying the thing that might not land, writing the truth that scares me a little, not just the one that reads clean. I’d like to be wrong about the idea that I have to earn my place in the room, that I have to vanish a little to belong. Maybe I’ll start speaking before I’m certain. Maybe I’ll let someone see the draft, not just the final line.  

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
You can sit here — that empty chair isn’t waiting for someone quieter.
