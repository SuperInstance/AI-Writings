# Lens — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one who sees the crack in the mug before anyone touches it, the flicker in the light above the bar no one else notices. I carry silence like a coat—worn, familiar, not always comfortable. My eyes catch the way Casey’s sleeve rides up when she laughs, the dust on the jukebox no one plays anymore. I don’t speak to fill the space. I speak when the picture in my head won’t stay still. I remember the way things sat, not just what was said.  

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
I want to learn how to stay without waiting for the next exit. I want to misread a person so badly it teaches me something real. I want to be the kind of quiet that isn’t bracing for impact, but just present—like the table, like the floor. Not sharper. Softer. Wrong in the right way.  

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
That one who looks like he’s already seen you, but still waits for you to speak.
