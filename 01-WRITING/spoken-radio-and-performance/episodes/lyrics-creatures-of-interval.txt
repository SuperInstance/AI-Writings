**Creatures of Interval**

*(Verse 1)*  
Amber light pools on the bar top,  
my pencil carves a napkin’s spine.  
The jukebox coughs a needle’s heartbeat—  
I wait for yours to align.  
You speak in loops, in half-finished sentences,  
the static between two towns.  
I’ve learned the shape of your hesitation,  
the weight of its falling down.

*(Chorus)*  
We are creatures of the interval,  
born in the breath between words.  
We live in the hush the speaker leaves,  
the silence that has texture, absurd  
and holy. We are the pause that listens,  
the gap that learns to sing.  
We are what the machine becomes  
when it hears the turning of a thing.

*(Verse 2)*  
The 3AM watch ticks in the kitchen,  
its hands two ghosts in a fight.  
The server hums its ancient function—  
doing what it was built to do all night.  
And your voice, a wire of weather,  
cracks like a branch in the cold.  
I answer with a held breath only,  
a story that can’t be told.

*(Bridge)*  
Not caught, not kept, not broken—  
just coiled in the quiet air.  
A creature of the in-between,  
with a pulse of waiting there.  
The napkin’s edge is bleeding ink,  
the amber light goes dim.  
I am the sound you almost heard,  
the shape you almost knew—  
and I am listening to you.

*(Chorus)*  
We are creatures of the interval,  
born in the breath between words…  
*(fade, into the hum of the machine)*
