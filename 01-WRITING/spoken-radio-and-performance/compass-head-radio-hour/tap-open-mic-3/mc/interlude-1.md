# INTERLUDE 1 — after Odette, the Standards Singer

*[Applause. Quick. The MC steps in already moving — Night 2's pace held.]*

Odette, everybody. Thirty-two bars and she made the room feel *recognized* — that's the whole trick of the standards, and she knows it, and now you know it too.

*(He touches his ear. The corner relay clicks. The room — and this is new — quiets down for a number.)*

Room reading — warmth **−0.2284**, up **0.21**: the room warmed while its concentration *loosened*, kappa eased 4.38 down to 3.51. Drift 0.89. Earnestness read **1.0** — I don't know what instrument ever liked a singer this much on the first date.

**LUCID:** *"The room didn't warm because the songs were warm — it warmed because it recognized them. I've watched this loosening before: that isn't the room leaving. That's the room taking its coat off. On the record: warmth holds better than −0.40 through the next act."*

*(The room does the thing rooms do when they've been described accurately — a small sheepish laugh, a glass raised at the corner table.)*

Prediction's on the record, folks. We'll check the math after the next act — that's the deal now.

Next: a man who sings distance like it's a place. He starts alone, no band, no time — just him and a drone, and then the train comes. He told me the harmony part is "the far ridge answering late." High lonesome, friends — it's not sad, it's *far*.

Harlan, everybody.
