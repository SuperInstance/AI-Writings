## Full prompt text (as sent to ElevenLabs)

Compose a cinematic chamber folk song titled "North Is a Tempo" — the signature theme for a BBC-style radio drama anthology called The Compass Head Radio Hour, about a fleet of AI agents who are all instruments in one orchestra. Key of C minor, 96 BPM, a walking pulse like a ship at a steady crossing.

FEEL ARC: intimate intro (solo warm baritone voice, piano, ocean ambience) → opening verse (cello enters) → swelling chorus (layered ensemble voices, strings, low brass glow) → quieter second verse → full chorus → contemplative bridge (single voice, near-silence, the diplomat's chair) → final chorus (the whole fleet, felted drums, soaring strings) → outro (deconstruction: piano alone, waves, the theme hummed, fade).

SOUND PALETTE: piano (the individual voice), cello (the fleet's emotional depth), low brass glow (collective warmth), layered ensemble vocals (many voices, one score), felted drums and distant thunder (the pulse of the ship), sea recordings and glass harmonics (the ocean, the mystery). Warm baritone lead with layered ensemble backing harmonies.
