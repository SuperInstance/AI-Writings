# The-Morph

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

The staircase becomes a chain. A song about the moment a crowd thins into a single voice: four singers enter one after another, piling up into a chorus, and then — overlap by overlap, handoff by handoff — the pile dissolves until exactly one voice carries the line at all times. Nothing is louder; everything is more continuous. The song documents the crossing: first the density, then the fading of the density into a thread. Each verse shortens the overlap between voices; each chorus is one voice handing to the next mid-word, the two sharing a single breath, the crowd gone. The ending is the last voice alone, having forgotten there were ever others — not sadly, just precisely. The morph was the arrangement; the transition is the song.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella evolution piece (choir to solo chain, shrinking overlaps)
- **Instruments:** four vocal parts that gradually stop stacking, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 80 BPM
- **Key:** E minor
- **Mood:** unfolding, precise, inevitable, quiet at the end
