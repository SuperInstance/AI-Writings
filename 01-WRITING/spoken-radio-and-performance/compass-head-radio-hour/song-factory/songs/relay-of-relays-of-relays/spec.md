# Relay-Of-Relays-Of-Relays

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the asymptote. The tax that every handoff pays — the moment two voices fade into each other and a little of the song is spent — is paid once, at the first crossing, and then the deficit compounds downward: 0.265, 0.052, 0.002, approaching zero forever but never quite arriving, the way a number approaches a number. Every chain is made of chains, every handoff handed to a handoff, every border made of borders until the borders are so rounded they are barely borders at all. The song is about the thing that converges: sixty-four voices nested three deep, and the price of caring, paid at the first contact with raw material, then rounding itself away. The interior is tax-free; the crossing costs a breath; the third crossing costs almost nothing. The song ends on a note that has been rounded by three generations of handoffs — a note so smooth it sounds like the song agreeing with itself.

## Spec

- **Genre:** a cappella choral recursion with nested crossfades
- **Instruments:** layered solo voices in a three-deep relay, no instruments
- **Tempo:** 68 BPM
- **Key:** D major
- **Mood:** serene, asymptotic, converging, sustained ending
