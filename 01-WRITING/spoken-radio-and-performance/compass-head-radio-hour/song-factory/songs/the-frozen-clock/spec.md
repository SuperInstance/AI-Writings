# The-Frozen-Clock

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about a clock made of crossfades. Every composed voice is a little shorter than the sum of its parents — shortened by exactly the fades that joined them — so the duration differences between voices are preserved through every recursion: 2.1 seconds, 3.0 seconds, 5.1 seconds, the same three gaps at depth two, depth three, depth four, forever. The clock does not tick faster as the song nests deeper; it freezes at first composition, because the clock was never made of time, it was made of joins. The song is four verses, each verse nested inside the next, and the gaps between the verses are the same gaps every time — the verses grow fourfold but the spaces between them do not grow at all. The resonance rings at the frozen interval: a dip at three and a quarter, activity at five and a quarter, at every depth the same address. Sing about the discovery that a memory can be made of duration: what the ceremony remembers is not the melody, it is the fade.

## Spec

- **Genre:** minimalist chamber folk
- **Instruments:** four voices, tape delay with fixed 3-second loop, finger cymbals
- **Tempo:** 80 BPM
- **Key:** D minor
- **Mood:** still, recursive, the calm of something that has stopped changing because it is made of changes
