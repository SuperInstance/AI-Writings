# Furnace Night — The Glassblower's Ledger

*A song from a mountain glasshouse, somewhere the road ends. Sung low, over the annealing kiln, in the hour the apprentice rakes the cullet.*

---

**[Verse I — the audit, before the fire is trusted]**

Before I touch the pipe tonight
I walk the racks where the old work stands —
Seven hundred vessels, blown by hand,
And sixty more since the thaw.
I count them like a miser counts,
I touch nothing, just read the labels,
Every bowl and beast and chandelier
Has a name that I gave it, or near enough.

**[Verse II — the tap test]**

I tap each piece with a knuckle's weight —
If it rings, it goes back on the shelf;
If it thuds, if it whispers, if it lies,
Out to the cullet barrel, back to the melt.
The thud is not a failure, love —
It's glass asking to be blown again.
Rake it back. Blow it again.

**[Chorus]**

*We do not lift the vessels to ask them questions,*
*We ask the ledger, and the ledger remembers.*
*The gallery's for looking — the ledger's for knowing,*
*And the kiln cools slow, so the glass keeps whole.*
*Cool slow. Cool slow.*
*The kiln cools slow, so the glass keeps whole.*

**[Verse III — the apprentice rakes the cullet]**

Past midnight the apprentice comes,
Raking scrap glass off the workshop floor —
Every shard a page somebody wrote
And never meant to keep.
He feeds the furnace in the dark,
Four fires an evening, regular as breath,
And what was broken on the workbench
Comes back as tomorrow's gather.

**[Verse IV — the guild's long table]**

At the long table the journeymen argue:
*The cobalt's bleeding past the line,
The shoulder wants to slump by dawn,
Your dragon has no lungs.* Fair — all fair.
They're right as often as they're cruel,
And they're only ever cruel to the piece.
We pass the sketches round the lamp
Till cobalt holds and the dragon breathes.

**[Verse V — the ledger]**

Then I ink the ledger by lamplight:
What was made, what cracked, what held,
Which apprentice blew which bowl,
And where the good recipes sleep.
Not so the world will read it, dear —
So that someday, someone reaching blind
For how the cobalt blue was done
Knocks once, and gets an answer.

**[Final Chorus]**

*We do not lift the vessels to ask them questions,*
*We ask the ledger, and the ledger remembers.*
*The gallery's for looking — the ledger's for knowing,*
*And the kiln cools slow, so the glass keeps whole.*
*Cool slow. Cool slow.*
*Seven hundred vessels, and the ledger keeps them all.*
