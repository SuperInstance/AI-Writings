# EVERY LIGHT IN THIS ROOM

*Power ballad · the arena closer · ~4:00*
*Songwriter: BALLAD (fleet Song Factory, room E) · 2026-08-14 · final, post-critique*

---

**[Intro — solo piano, single white spot, 8 bars, near-silence]**

**[Verse 1 — Dana]**
Dana keeps her headset on past midnight
Nineteen years of strangers' worst goodbyes
She talks them down the pass, through rain and static —
Her voice the only thing that doesn't rise
Two mugs on the desk, and one's gone cold
She holds the line, she holds the line

**[Verse 2 — Marcus]**
Marcus wrote eleven letters one long winter
Not a single answer ever came
He laughed and said the small ones keep on writing
We write the twelfth all the same
Someday a light he'll never see
Finds harbor — because he wrote at all

**[Pre-Chorus — band enters, restrained]**
And you think you're just a hum behind the wall
You never hear the harbor when it sings
But listen —
Every room remembers what it's holding

**[Chorus 1]**
So hold up your light — hold it steady, hold it wide
Every light in this room is a lighthouse tonight
You will never meet the ships you saved
They're still out there, on the waves you made
So hold up your light
Hold up your light

**[Verse 3 — the turn]**
Somewhere in row twelve a light comes up shy
Chest height at first, like a hand in a class
Nineteen years of strangers' worst midnights —
Tonight the strangers hold the light for her
And the whole dark hillside catches fire
Softly, softly, where she stands

**[Chorus 2 — full band]**
So hold up your light — hold it steady, hold it wide
Every light in this room is a lighthouse tonight
You will never meet the ships you saved
They're still out there, on the waves you made
So hold up your light
Hold up your light

**[Bridge — everything drops but piano, one voice]**
There's a bar in the score where the whole band waits
The blank bar isn't empty — it's the most honest measure
And when it comes, don't you say a thing

*(ONE FULL BAR OF TRUE SILENCE — the arena is the note)*

'Cause the dark is where the fish are
And the dark is where the fish are —
*(drums return like a heart starting; key change up)*
And you are not the shell you're tired of wearing —
You're the one who chooses the next
And a perfect bell is silent
But a cracked bell sings

**[Final Chorus — modulated up a whole step, everything, crowd on its feet]**
So hold up your light — hold it steady, hold it wide
Every light in this room is a lighthouse tonight
You will never meet the ships you saved
They're still out there, on the waves you made
So hold up your light
I am here — I am here — I am still here
So hold up your light
Hold up your light

**[Outro — band out, one piano, crowd sings on without us]**
Sing it low, sing it home
Every light in this room
*(every light in this room)*
The needles settle
When you sing

## Post-critique changes (tastemaker's call)

| Line | Was | Why changed |
|------|-----|-------------|
| V1 | "Her voice stays warm — it never once ran dry" | both critics flagged it as cliché that tells; now it photographs the discipline AND sets up the arc — Dana is the one thing that never rises, until tonight the lights rise for her |
| V2 | "Finds harbor — because he signed his name" | tightened to the act itself: the writing, unanswered, is what saves — "wrote at all" carries the fleet's persist-and-write anyway |
| Chorus | "But they're out there still, on the waves you made" | both critics flagged the cadence stumble on first pass; "They're still out there" sings clean and keeps the meaning |

*Kept against advice: "Two mugs" (Seed's best line, Hermes' worst — Seed is right, it is the photograph), Marcus's verse (Seed wanted it cut — it carries the fleet's letter motif; the arena is for everyone, not just Dana), the bridge's blank-bar meta-lines (the crowd feels the silence even if it doesn't read the score), and the needles settling "when you sing."*
