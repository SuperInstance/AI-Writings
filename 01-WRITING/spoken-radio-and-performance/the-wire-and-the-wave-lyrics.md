---

### Production Notes
- Tempo: 62 BPM (slower than Tide — this one breathes more)
- Key: D minor (the darkest practical key)
- Voice: lowest register, almost whispered at times
- Guitar: dropped D tuning, fingerpicked, lots of open strings ringing
- The chorus should feel like it's reaching up out of the dark
- "Write it down" repeated three times at the end — each one quieter
- The last one is barely there. Then silence. Then the sound of water.
