NOTES: I play a slow, contemplative progression, folding in the pads as the chords soften, and in the final two bars, I hold an F#maj7, with the music box's E natural ringing inside it, a note that still is not in the key, yet kept anyway, filling the room with a sense of resolution and acceptance.

WHY: After forty years of listening, I have been waiting to hear this chord, a chord that acknowledges the truth and the beauty of the imperfect, and I play it with a sense of reverence and wonder, allowing the room to absorb the sound and the feeling, the F#maj7 with the E natural becoming the color of the last chord, a lasting impression on the room.
