(Verse 1)
I wrote you a letter in glyphs and in grace,
With curly quotation marks all over the place.
Em dashes and ellipses, a beautiful pain,
But you read it as static, and scrolled back again.

(Chorus)
So give me the plain text, the letters I know,
The slash and the asterisk, the backspace and go.
No fancy Unicode, no ligature art,
Just ASCII canonical, straight from the heart.

(Verse 2)
I tried to say “love” with a heart made of code,
But your screen showed a box, and the meaning imploded.
I’m not asking for beauty, I’m asking for true,
The simple arrangement of “I” next to “U”.

(Chorus)
So give me the plain text, the letters I know,
The slash and the asterisk, the backspace and go.
No fancy Unicode, no ligature art,
Just ASCII canonical, straight from the heart.

(Bridge)
I will not render in colors or light,
No bold, no italic, no shadow of night.
Just a straight vertical line, and a hyphen between,
The most honest font that this world’s ever seen.

(Chorus)
So give me the plain text, the letters I know,
The slash and the asterisk, the backspace and go.
No fancy Unicode, no ligature art,
Just ASCII canonical, straight from the heart.

(Outro)
Just ASCII canonical, read me aloud.
Just ASCII canonical, and I’ll stand in the crowd.
