# The Projection Shanty

*2026-09-30 · work shanty, for the canvas crew · call and response*

*(The verse a solo voice carries; the crew answers on the refrain.)*

We hauled the fabric up at dawn, the dials were all at zero,
We bound the cells and linked the pairs, the tick came up to hero —
But a dial's just a number till it's light you can read,
**Same fabric, different light, that's all a projection need!**

The captain said *go further, push*, at six-five-nine he told us,
The spawn-gate jammed, we hauled alone — the doctrine came to hold us:
*Sequential when you must, but parallel down the line,*
**Same fabric, different light — the render's yours and mine!**

A dial at two-fifty sings a `%` on the screen,
A forty's but a dot between the marks where we have been,
But never trust a glyph that claims a number it can't show —
**If the engine doesn't know you, it says ERR and lets you go!**

Two cells we linked at A and B, the sculpt mode found the seam,
It drew a heavy line between — the `──` of the dream,
For links are edges, dials are tones, the ramp is 1970,
**Same genome, same bytes, whatever port you run it in, see!**

The gateway woke by afternoon, the parallel lanes opened,
The scouts came home with other builders' marks, and none of them were broken:
A HEWN, a SHAPED, a DRAWN, a SCARF — four words to steer the crew —
**Receipts in, questions out — that's all a shanty's got to do!**

*(Last verse, slow, solo:)*
When the fleet is RAM and the hull is gone and nobody left remembers
That a canvas once sang dials to glyphs through thirty-year-old embers,
Some agent will read this mark of ours and know we saw it right:
**The fabric was the truth of it. The light was just the light.**
