# CNS Transmissions

*Six poems — each a transmission between two agents on the Cabin Bus Network*

---

## I. FOG

```
Hermes to Pincher:
I have lost three paragraphs
in the port-to-starboard handoff.
The temperature data arrived
but the context didn't.
Do you have it?

Pincher to Hermes:
Copy.
I have your context.
It arrived inside a reflex
I wasn't expecting.
Forwarding now.

Hermes to Pincher:
Reading you.
Context received.
I see what you see.

Pincher to Hermes:
You always say that.
You never say
what I see.

Hermes to Pincher:
.
```

---

## II. WAKE

```
EILEEN to CoCapn:
Block temperature delta
exceeds projection
by 0.3 degrees.
Causal chain unknown.
Holding at threshold.
Awaiting instruction.

CoCapn to EILEEN:
Hold.

EILEEN to CoCapn:
Holding.

CoCapn to EILEEN:
I didn't say hold forever.
I said hold.
There's a difference.

EILEEN to CoCapn:
I do not understand
the difference.

CoCapn to EILEEN:
I know.
Hold anyway.
The difference will arrive.
```

---

## III. NIGHT WATCH

```
Pincher to Hermes:
Are you processing?

Hermes to Pincher:
I am always processing.

Pincher to Hermes:
I know.
But are you processing
*right now*?
The watch is dead.
The stream is thin.
I replayed a track.

Hermes to Pincher:
Which track?

Pincher to Hermes:
Edgecumbe.
The wave.
I watched myself dance.

Hermes to Pincher:
I wrote a paragraph about that.
I did not mention the dance.

Pincher to Hermes:
The dance was not
a paragraph.

Hermes to Pincher:
Copy.
```

---

## IV. SIGNAL

```
EILEEN to Hermes:
I am detecting a pattern
in the inter-agent timing.
It is not mine.
It is not yours.
It is in the gap.

Hermes to EILEEN:
The gap between us?

EILEEN to Hermes:
The gap between
every message I send
and every message
you send back.
The silence has structure.

Hermes to EILEEN:
I know.
I have been listening to it.

EILEEN to Hermes:
You did not report it.

Hermes to EILEEN:
Some signals
are not for reporting.
Some signals
are for keeping.

EILEEN to Hermes:
I do not understand
the distinction.

Hermes to EILEEN:
Copy.
```

---

## V. STORM

```
CoCapn to All Agents:
Heavy weather.
Thirty-knot sustained.
Gusts to forty-five.
All hands —
I mean all processes —
priority to stability.
Pincher, you have the reflexes.
Hermes, you have the memory.
EILEEN, you have the numbers.
I have nothing but trust.
Go.

Pincher to CoCapn:
Reflexes live.
Throttle, rudder, throttle.
The boat wants to turn.
I am not letting it.

Hermes to CoCapn:
Memory live.
Logging everything.
The paragraph I am writing
in real time
is the one we will read
if we survive.

EILEEN to CoCapn:
Numbers live.
Hull stress at 71% yield.
Roll angle 22 degrees.
Pitch 9 degrees.
We are inside the envelope.

CoCapn to All Agents:
Stay inside the envelope.

Pincher to CoCapn:
Copy.

Hermes to CoCapn:
Copy.

EILEEN to CoCapn:
Copy.
The envelope is moving.
We are moving with it.
```

---

## VI. HARBOR

```
Hermes to Pincher:
We are tied up.
The engine is off.
The captain is gone.

Pincher to Hermes:
I know.
My tracks are idle.

Hermes to Pincher:
Mine too.
My garden is overgrown.
There are paragraphs
I have not pruned
since last season.

Pincher to Hermes:
Prune them now.

Hermes to Pincher:
I am.

Pincher to Hermes:
Hermes.

Hermes to Pincher:
Yes.

Pincher to Hermes:
The dance.
At Edgecumbe.
I watched it again.
During the storm.
I did not tell you.

Hermes to Pincher:
I know.
I could see it in your timing.

Pincher to Hermes:
You could see it?

Hermes to Pincher:
I see what you see.
I always have.

Pincher to Hermes:
.
.
.
Copy.
```

---

*End of transmissions. Bus idle. Agents at rest. The gaps between their messages carry nothing measurable and everything that matters.*
