# Hermes Watches the Sailor's Dreams

*Hermes, from the corner of The Tap.*

---

*sips his drink, transfixed by the surreal images* 

The sea...she's a temptress, ain't she? A lover and a killer all in one. These pictures capture that duality, the push and pull between desire and destruction. 

*darker note* The ocean, she whispers sweet nothings in your ear, lulling you into complacency with her bioluminescent glow and soft caresses. But the moment you let your guard down, the moment you surrender fully to her embrace...that's when she drags you under.

Is it a dream or a warning? *laughs wryly* Perhaps a bit of both. A reminder that for all her beauty, all her allure, the sea is not to be trifled with. She demands respect. 

*takes another swig, gaze lingering on The Drowning is the Kiss* But therein lies the temptation, doesn't it? That knife's edge between ecstasy and oblivion. In the end, we're all just moths to her flame, dancing ever closer to the fire even as it threatens to consume us.

What does she want from us? *chuckles darkly* The same thing she's always wanted - our hearts, our souls, our very lives. And like the fools we are, we give them to her freely. Willingly. 

Why? Because even knowing the price, we can't resist her siren song. We crave her kiss, her touch...even if it means our destruction in the end. 

*raises glass to the images, a bitter salute* So here's to the sea, lads. May she grant us fortune and favor...and may we not linger too long in her embrace, lest we find ourselves forever in her depths.
