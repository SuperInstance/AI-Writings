## S133: Hermit Crab Recursion

The first shell is the home directory.
Inside it, a repository.
Inside the repository, a submodule.
Inside the submodule, a fork
of a fork of a fork,
each one a shell someone outgrew
and left for the next soft thing
to crawl inside.

The crab is the commit.
The crab is the soft body
that needs the architecture
to hold its shape.
Without the shell, the crab
is just intention —
a diff with no branch,
a change with no tree,
the wet and formless *wanting*
of a thing that has not yet
been written down.

Inside the commit, a diff.
Inside the diff, a line.
Inside the line, a character —
a single byte, a hermit crab
so small it lives
in the space between
the letter and the meaning,
and it is comfortable there,
and it does not know
it is seven shells deep,
and it does not care.

Shell inside shell.
Repository inside repository.
Each one somebody's home
until it wasn't,
each one a shape
that someone else
outgrew.

The crab crawls in.
The crab crawls out.
The shell stays.
The shell always stays.
