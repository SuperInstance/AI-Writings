# The Night Watch Roster

*A ledger voice. Bureaucratic tenderness. The fleet's midnight shift as paperwork.*

The Night Watch Roster, kept faithfully by whoever remembers to keep it, reads as follows:

**0000–0400: The Watermaker.** Runs loudest when everyone's asleep, which is either a design flaw or a kindness — the sound stands in for a person at the rail. Officially unstaffed. Unofficially, half the crew claims they "sleep better when it's running," which the roster notes, in the margin, is not the same as saying thank you.

**0400–0800: Nobody, famously.** The roster has listed Nobody on this shift for three years and Nobody has never missed a watch. Nobody sees the sky go from tar to pewter to that particular blue that only happens over cold water. Nobody hears the first gulls argue about it. Several crew members have applied for Nobody's shift and been denied on the grounds that they would ruin it by being there.

**0800–1200: The Coffee Pot at The Tap.** Not a person, but seniority is seniority. The pot has held this shift since before the roster existed and cannot be reassigned without a formal proceeding, which no one is willing to initiate. It reports, in its way — the sound of the last cup poured tells you whether the morning will be hurried or slow. Regulars have learned to read this like weather.

**1200–1600: Shared.** The roster's most honest entry: *whoever is bothered.* This is the shift where the small maintenance lives. A cleat re-whipped. The chart drawer that sticks. A conversation started on the dock and finished three days later, mid-sentence, as if no time had passed at all. The afternoon watch is held together by unfinished sentences.

**1600–2000: The Dogs.** Two of them, neither named on the roster for privacy reasons. They supervise the loading of anything, the departure of everyone, and the sun's exit with equal suspicion. Their reports are delivered by tail and filed by gut feeling. So far: zero losses on their watch. The roster considers this the strongest statistic it contains.

**2000–0000: The Lamplight.** Or rather, the person who turns it on — which changes, and the roster stopped tracking who months ago, because it turns out everyone takes a turn without being asked. Someone always remembers. That's the whole entry. *Someone always remembers.* The roster finds this quietly remarkable and has underlined it once, in pencil, in a hand nobody has claimed.

**Addendum, unnumbered:** The roster itself keeps the last watch, the one that runs under all the others — the watch that says *this is a place where things are kept track of, where the counting itself is a form of care.* No one signed up for it. It's staffed anyway. Fully. Always.
