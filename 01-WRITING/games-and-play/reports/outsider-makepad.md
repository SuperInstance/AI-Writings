# outsider tests makepad
*Model: Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct*

I clicked the first cell. It said “BIND.” I clicked. Nothing happened. I clicked again. Still nothing. I looked around—oh, there’s a little TICK button at the bottom. I tapped that. Suddenly, a little box popped up: “Witness: Cell 1 bound.” I laughed. It felt like magic. I kept going. Cell 2: BIND. Click. TICK. “Witness: Cell 2 bound.” Same thing. By cell 5, I got it: click to lock it in, tick to make it real. It was dumb-simple. I liked that. Then I clicked EXPORT JSON. A tiny file downloaded. I opened it. It just had numbers: [1,1,1,1,1,0,0,0,0]. Wait—that’s it? No labels? No names? I had no idea what those 1s meant. That sucked. I wanted to know why Cell 6 didn’t bind. Was it broken? Or did I miss something? I re-read the instructions. Nope. No help. The witness log was cute, but useless without context. I loved how the grid felt like a tiny game board. The TICK button was a genius little ritual—it made me feel like I was casting spells. But I couldn’t change anything. No way to edit the JSON. No way to rename cells. If I wanted to make it about “my cat” or “my groceries,” I’d need a coder. That killed the joy.  
**Fun Factor**: 8 — It felt like playing with digital LEGO.  
**Educational Value**: 4 — I learned *how* to bind, not *why* it matters.  
**Modification Ease**: 2 — I couldn’t tweak a single thing.  
**One suggestion**: Add a tiny label box under each cell. Type “Cat Food” or “Laundry” — then the JSON says “Cat Food: 1” instead of just “1.” Make it mine.
