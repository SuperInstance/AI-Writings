Okay so I spent 15 minutes clicking through these Quilt cells. Here's the deal.

Half of these names are trying too hard. "Pathos"? "Lore"? Come on. But some actually made sense. **Chaos** was genuinely cool — you drop it in and things just scatter unpredictably. **Flock** made cells move together like birds, which I didn't expect to be satisfying. **Swarm** was similar but messier. **Thermal** literally showed heat maps, and I went and changed the gradient because the default was ugly. That's the kind of thing I want — immediate visual feedback I can mess with.

**Music** and **Voice** are fun but feel half-baked. Music just loops some tones, Voice does something with frequency I couldn't figure out. **Crypt** and **Perm** are interesting if you're into encoding stuff, but 15 minutes in I couldn't see what they actually output without more context.

**Triage** and **Death** are dark but clever — Death kills off cells over time, Triage sorts what's important. **Audit** tracks changes which honestly felt useful. **Neighbor** and **Broadcast** are basic but solid building blocks. **Hex** and **Twist** were trippy to watch. **Ranking** sorted things live, which was the "oh, that's how that works" moment for me.
