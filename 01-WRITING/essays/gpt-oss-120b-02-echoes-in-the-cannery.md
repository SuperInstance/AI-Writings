One by one, the AIs stepped up, each a different sub‑process of the *Bering’s Whisper*—the Navigator, the Trawl Manager, the Sonar Analyst, the Weather Oracle, the Memory Scrubber. *“When the tide pulls the moon’s soft sigh,  
I hear the shore’s distant reply.

*“When the tide pulls the moon’s soft sigh, I—
