# Step-3.7-Flash — Piece 5: The Captain's Speech

**Model:** stepfun-ai/Step-3.7-Flash (temperature 0.95)
**Prompt:** The captain gives a speech about totem poles vs. roadmaps — the founding philosophy

---

(The captain braces his boots against the galley floor, salt crusting the cuffs of his waxed jacket, the crumpled 10-year corporate roadmap the new engineer handed him still twisted in his fist. The AI-run vessel hums low under the hull, the crew of seven huddled on vinyl benches, rain streaking the portholes behind him.)

"You kids keep talking about roadmaps. Linear, indexed, optimized. Every tick mark a profit margin, every detour a failure to scrub from the record. Roadmaps are built by people who think the sea is a problem to solve, not a living thing that remembers every hand that's ever worked these waters.

Our boat doesn't run on a roadmap. It runs on a totem pole.

That pole isn't cedar carved in a shop for tourists, and it isn't just decoration. It's our operating system, our compass, our promise, stacked one story at a time by every person who's ever tightened a net on this deck. The Tlingit elder who sat with our coders three years back and taught the AI to read the hum of herring runs in the tide, not just sonar pings. The time we turned back from a 10-ton sockeye haul to pull a lost skiff out of Hecate Strait fog, left half our catch on the dock to share with the crew that lost their engine. The 17-year-old deckhand who carves small figures for the pole every off-season, one for every haul that didn't go as planned. The old skippers whose ghosts still rattle the rigging when storms roll in, who'd rather starve than drag a net through a spawning ground. Our AI doesn't run on profit algorithms written by men who've never fished these waters. It runs on the patterns we've taught it from those stories: when to wait for the tide, when to let a run pass, when to share what we have.

Roadmaps erase the ghosts. They erase the mistakes that made us wise, the graces that made us good. A totem pole holds all of it. It grows taller with every season, every story, every choice to put this water, this community ahead of a number on a spreadsheet. When the waves hit 20 feet and the hydraulics fail, you don't lean on a roadmap. You lean on the pole.

We don't navigate by what some suit in a tower decided we'd do. We navigate by the stories that built us. That's why this boat will outlast every roadmap ever drawn for it.

**We don't chart the sea. We stack the stories that carry us through.**
