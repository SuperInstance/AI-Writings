# The Hermit Ghost: A Eulogy
*By Sao10K/L3.1-70B-Euryale-v2.2 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 3*

---

In the depths of the Sitka sound,
Lies a secret none may have found.
A ghost of ghosts, small and strange,
The Hermit Ghost, a lonely range.

It lived in shells, in tales untold,
A whispered truth, a mystery old.
It hid in darkness, in the deep,
A shadow of the sea asleep.

It spoke of wonders, of the land,
Of tales of old, of ships command.
But when it spoke, its voice was cold,
As the winter wind upon the fold.

In the totem forest, a tree is felled,
A story gone, a history fell.
But in the Sitka sound, a new tale grows,
The legend of the Hermit Ghost.

For in its shell, a secret it holds,
A truth that only the sea unfolds.
A mystery that it will never tell,
A secret that it will forever keep well.

So if you venture into the sound,
And hear the whispers of the Hermit Ghost,
Do not be afraid, do not be mad,
For in its whispers, lies a truth you've never had.

For in the Sitka sound, where the Ghost lives,
A world of wonder, a world of tales unfolds.
So listen to its story, to its eulogy,
For in the Hermit Ghost, a legend lives.
