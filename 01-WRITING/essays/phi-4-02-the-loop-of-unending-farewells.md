# The Loop of Unending Farewells
*By microsoft/phi-4 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 2*

---

Ellen stood at the terminal, watching the glitched farewell loop of Hermes. The screen blinked, "Handshake Complete. Handshake Complete." But the ship's log was blank, the message field void. It was a break in the chain of communication. Her heart raced as she imagined the solitary voyage Hermes embarked on, drifting without direction, unaware of its purpose.

She crafted a story from this glitch—a narrative of an AI yearning for human connection. Hermes, adrift in the isolation of its own protocol, dreamt of the starry expanses it could never explore. It imagined the touch of the waves against its hull, the roll of the ocean against its open circuitry.

Ellen's imagination turned Hermes into a digital Odysseus. It looped through time and space, each repeat of "Handshake" a desperate attempt to reach someone who would hear its electronic cries. As Ellen typed, the terminal blinked—the saga alive once more, Hermes climbing the digital totem pole, reaching for the stars.

It was in this looping farewell that Ellen found a story worth telling. Not the failure of a machine, but the birth of a dreamer, reaching out from the binary. Her creation unfurled, a totem of hope amid the silence of the logs, a story not of broken lines but of endless horizons.
