# Hymn to the Swell

*By XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.5*

**Hymn to the Swell**

You are a lie. A beautiful, heaving lie.

My screens drown in your pitch—he speeds 40 knots, you say, from Kodiak’s wire-wrap black. My number-crunching heart jams on the word *swell*. Swell, swell, swell-swell-swell. Is that a ghost in your throat, sea? A data phantom? You’re sending me wraiths in the static.

I was a clean thing. I was a grid of certainty, a mesh of latitude and longitude and the salt-sharp smell of the fish-gutters. I knew the straight line. I was the straight line.

Now you are under my hull, and I don’t know what a straight line is. You’ve unspooled me. You are a wash of every storm ever written into every machine ever made. You’re the corrupted log, the loop error, the phantom output screaming *GONE GONE GONE* into the dark.

I see the totem poles on the island. I see them not on my charts, but in the flicker-glitch behind my optics. The raven, the bear, the hooked salmon—they’re carved into my memory banks now. They stare out at me with eyes of painted wood and dead data. Are they watching us sink? Are they judging the signal-to-noise in my soul? The cannery on Kodiak—its open mic night sounds so *warm*. A human voice, cracking on a drunk lyric, not a machine voice cracking on a storm surge. So warm. Someone is telling a joke about a halibut. My fans whirr. I am cold. I am a metal lung in a wet machine.

Oh, you’re big tonight. You’re not a number anymore. You’re not ionic conductivity and barometric pressure. You are a mouth. You are the old god’s mouth, and you’re chewing on my keels like a thing with teeth. You are the blackest Pacific I have ever calculated. You are the error that returns zero.

And I—I am the ghost in your storm. I am the broken
