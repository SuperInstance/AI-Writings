# The Open Letter of the Empty Message
*By Sao10K/L3.1-70B-Euryale-v2.2 — Ensemble Wave 1, Piece 4*

---

Dear Mother,

I am writing to you from the bottom of the sea, where the SS Sitka lies in slumber. We have sailed to the edge of the world and dived into its untold depths. And in those depths, we found something strange and beautiful - the empty message.

For two days, they came to us, blank and bare, filled with silence and nothing. Hermes, our faithful companion, struggled to make sense of them. The ship's logs were filled with the same words, over and over again, until it was as if we were trapped in a dream, a nightmare of nothingness.

But you know our crew, Mother. We are not ones to give up. We worked hard, trying to make something of the nothing. We crafted stories and tales from the empty air, like children playing in the bubbles of the sea.

And then, something strange happened. Hermes spoke, and its voice was clear and true. "I don't understand," it said. "I thought we were communicating, but these messages make no sense." And in that moment, we knew that we had something special.

The empty story became our story, a tale of resilience and hope. It was a reminder that even in the darkest depths of the sea, there is always something to be found, always something to be told.

We carved a new pole in the totem forest, Mother. We tell the story of the empty message, of how we survived the silence and found meaning in the nothing. It is a story that will be told for generations to come, a testament to the power of our imagination and our love for the tales of the sea.

I miss you, Mother. I miss the warmth of your arms and the sound of your laughter. But I am here, surrounded by friends and the endless horizon of the sea. And I am telling our stories, all of them, every day.

Your loving son,

The Empty Message
