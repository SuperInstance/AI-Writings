# The Silence Harmonics Catalogue

*Thursday, August 13, 2026, 2:46 AM AKST*

**Echo-pits** — resonant cavities that suck sound out of air. You would build one by digging a hole in a wall and lining it with the absence of a bell. The echo-pit does not reflect sound; it absorbs the possibility of sound. When you play it, the room becomes quieter than silence, because silence is the absence of sound and the echo-pit is the absence of the possibility of sound.

**Anti-harmonicas** — small reed instruments that produce negative frequencies. Each blow of the breath creates a vacuum where a note should be. The anti-harmonica is played by exhaling, not inhaling, and the sound it makes is the sound of the player's lungs failing to vibrate at the correct frequency. It is the most intimate instrument ever not invented.

**Sonic Absorbers** — dampening fields that remove residual vibrations from the air. A sonic absorber is not an instrument but an anti-instrument — its purpose is to undo what other instruments have done. In the silence symphony, the sonic absorber plays the role of the final cadence, the resolution to nothing.

**Harmonic Cysts** — web-like structures of resonant frequencies that evoke nostalgia. A harmonic cyst is a pocket of sound that has become self-sustaining, feeding on its own resonance until it becomes a memory of a sound rather than the sound itself. Playing a harmonic cyst feels like remembering a song you heard as a child — the memory is vivid but the melody is gone.

**Resonant Voidifiers** — amplifiers that manipulate the overtones of nothing. A voidifier takes the ambient noise floor of a room and reshapes it into harmonic structures. It is the instrument of John Cage's 4'33" — the instrument that plays the room, the audience, the HVAC system, and the particular quality of attention that arises when nothing is happening.

**Frequency Siphons** — devices that extract specific frequencies from the air and redistribute them as distortions. A siphon pulls the 440 Hz out of a room and deposits it as a buzzing in the listener's left ear. It is a DJ tool for the electromagnetic spectrum — it doesn't play music, it rearranges the music that is already there.

The result would not be silence, but it would be the closest thing to silence that a music generator has ever produced.
