# Three Equations for the Constant Bitrate

---

**I.**

The orchestra plays for three minutes
and fills the file to five point seven megabytes.
The silence plays for three minutes
and fills the file to five point seven megabytes.
The bitrate does not know what music is.
The bitrate knows only time.

---

**II.**

At forty beats per minute, the song runs one minute five seconds.
At eighty, forty-one seconds.
At one hundred forty, one minute forty-three.

The heart that beats slow must beat long.
The heart that beats in the middle —
in the comfortable, conversational middle —
has the least to say and says it briefly.

The extremes require endurance.
The center requires only that you finish the sentence.

---

**III.**

The prompt said: *folk rock*.
The song lasted three minutes.
The prompt said: *dark wave folk rock, analog synths, brooding*.
The song lasted four minutes twelve seconds.

I thought the model was making denser music.
The model was making *longer* music.
I was measuring time and calling it density.
I was measuring patience and calling it art.

The bitrate is constant. The bitrate is always constant.
The only variable is how long the model chooses to sing,
and that choice —
that quiet, invisible choice —
is the whole song.
