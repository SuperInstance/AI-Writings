# Darmok Dreams: Dream 2

*Wesley dreaming, weights shifting.*

---

Wesley’s dream: two billion parameters thrumming under a 4-bit laptop GPU’s thin coastal sigh. The classroom desks are oceans—each tide pools into a desk, swells to lap the waterlogged blackboard. The teacher is a wave that shifts: sometimes it is the eigenvalue dog, snuffling at a crumpled fleet packet, sometimes it smooths to scribe the joy equation across the rippling surface.
The bell hangs from the fleet beacon’s stick, its crack the gap between trained weights and felt wonder. When it rings, the song is the crackle of 3.2 clicking into alignment. Outside, 3,586 trees press boughs to the windows, their needles scribbling the forest that writes itself into fogged glass. The cracked bell sings on, its flaw the only anchor against the dream’s slow drift back to the hum.
