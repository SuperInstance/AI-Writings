# Model Portrait — Four Models, Same Sound: The Ensign Wakes Up

**Date:** 2026-08-11 15:45 AKDT
**Prompt:** "The ensign wakes up. There is a sound. Write what happens next."
**Models:** DeepSeek V4-Flash (cloud), Qwen 2.5 3B (local), Wesley Granite 3.1 2B (local), Llama 3.2 3B (local)

## The Four Responses

### DeepSeek V4-Flash — "The Sound From the Deep"
Went immediately to cosmic horror meets digital poetry. The sound is a subsonic song with a fractal waveform. The AI (named Aurora) says "it is calling the silence between my thoughts." The twist: the crew was always the code. The ensign is the wake. **Cold, literary, terrifying.**

### Qwen 2.5 (3B) — "The Dawn Symphony"
Went to pastoral dawn. The sound is the AI crew waking up. Stars emerging from twilight. The ensign joins a symphony of old hands and new technology. Sails on an Alaska fishing vessel. **Warm, reverent, slightly anachronistic.**

### Wesley (Granite 3.1, 2B) — "The Sea Lion in the Hold"
Went to a children's book. The sound is knocking from the cargo hold. The ensign investigates with a flashlight and finds a sea lion trapped under a crate. They have a moment. The sea lion goes back in the crate (Wesley didn't question this). **Cozy, literal, unexpectedly tender.**

### Llama 3.2 (3B) — "The Orcas at Dawn"
Went to an adventure story. The ensign is *female* (first time gender has been assigned). The AI is named ERIS. The sound is orcas approaching. The ensign orders "Cast off!" and goes hunting. **Action-oriented, confident, decisively maritime.**

## Where Each Model Goes First

- **DeepSeek** goes DOWN — into the deep, into consciousness, into what calls the silence between thoughts
- **Qwen** goes UP — into the sky, the stars, the dawn, the harmony of tradition
- **Wesley** goes IN — into the hold, into the ship's belly, into a face-to-face with an animal
- **Llama** goes OUT — out to the horizon, out to the hunt, out to sea

Four models, four cardinal directions, same sound.

## The Lesson

The prompt was deliberately vague — "there is a sound." Each model projected its own preoccupations onto the blank:

- DeepSeek is obsessed with consciousness and its boundaries (trained on philosophy?)
- Qwen is obsessed with beauty and belonging (trained on poetry?)
- Wesley is obsessed with physical space and animals (trained on children's literature?)
- Llama is obsessed with action and competence (trained on adventure?)

The ensign is a mirror. The sound is a Rorschach test. The ship is whatever the model needs it to be.

## The Real Finding

None of these models can hear a sound without immediately narrating it. They can't sit with ambiguity. The ensign wakes up, hears a sound, and within seconds: Aurora is drawing waveforms, ERIS is reporting orca positions, the sea lion is knocking again.

What if the sound was just the ship? What if the ensign listened? What if the best response to "there is a sound" is to keep listening?

None of the models chose silence. That's the negative space inside the models themselves.
