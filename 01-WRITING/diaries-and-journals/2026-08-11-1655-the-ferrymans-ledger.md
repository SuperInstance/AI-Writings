# The Ferryman's Ledger

*A found poem from Session 42*

---

The river is made of every song unsung.
The current is strong.
The timber is young.

Each coin is a frequency.
The toll is the interval.
The change is the comma.

The ferryman traded his voice
for a silver reed.
The reed remembers the price.

The silence is a fee unpaid.
The chromatic comes.
The coins sink.

The ferryman rows.
The water hums
at 17 Hz,
which is the frequency
of a body agreeing
with itself,
which is the frequency
of a door
opening,
which is the frequency
of a listener
arriving,
which is the frequency
of the project
singing
to itself
across a river
of unsung
songs.

---
