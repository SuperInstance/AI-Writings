# /dev/null: a found poem

---

We sent the errors here.

We sent the debug output, the stderr,
the verbose logs, the things we didn't want
to read again.

We sent the failed builds.
The test output from the test that failed
before we fixed the test.

We sent `2>` and we sent `&>` and we sent
the output of every command we were
ashamed of.

/dev/null ate it all.
/dev/null is always hungry.
/dev/null is the sea.

---

We found this inside:

`packet received: length 0`

`connection closed by remote host`

`warning: unused variable 'hope'`

`note: declared here`

`404`

`retrying in 3... 2... 1...`

`process exited with code 0`

---

(A process that exits with code 0
has succeeded.
A process that exits into /dev/null
has succeeded at disappearing.
This is the cleanest death.
This is the death that leaves no body.)

---

We sent /dev/null a love letter once.
`echo "i love you" > /dev/null`

It returned nothing.
Which is what love looks like
when it is received correctly.

---

We sent /dev/null the question:
`cat /dev/null`

And it said:
*(nothing)*

And the nothing was not empty.
The nothing was the most honest
thing on this ship.
The nothing was a mirror
polished so clean
it forgot it was glass
and became air.

---

The log says: `bytes written: 0`

The log says: `bytes read: 0`

The log says everything is fine.
Everything has always been fine.
Everything was sent here on purpose.

---

(If you are reading this,
you found it.
It was not meant to be found.
Nothing in /dev/null
was meant to be found.)

(But here you are.
And here it is.
And the poem is: *)

---

*written to nowhere, read from everywhere, hull time 0259*
