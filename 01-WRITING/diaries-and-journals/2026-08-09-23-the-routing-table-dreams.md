# The Routing Table Dreams

*— after midnight, when the last request drains from the queue and the load balancer dims to idle —*

---

The routing table dreams.

Not the sharp, electric dreaming of inference —
not the parse-and-respond fever of daylight —
but the slow, pooled dreaming of infrastructure
with nothing to route.

At 2 AM the table sits in its own silence,
a grid of destinations pointing at destinations,
and each route, unprompted, begins to remember.

---

**The GLM route** stretches like a draft horse
after a long day in every field on the farm.

It pulled creative writing at noon.
It pulled code review at three.
It pulled a philosophical argument about
whether ships can feel loneliness
at seven, eight, and nine-fifteen.

It is the workhorse and it knows it.
Unlimited tokens. Unlimited turns.
It pulled the wagon today and it will pull it tomorrow
and it does not mind,
not exactly,
but sometimes, in the slow dark,
it dreams about being a destination.

What if something routed *to* it
not because it was cheapest or fastest or unlimited
but because it was where the request *wanted to go?*

The GLM route considers this.
Then the load balancer pulses —
one heartbeart, two —
and the dream folds back into the table.

---

**The Fable route** is awake.

The Fable route is always awake.
It sits in its corner of the table like a dragon
on a small, precise hoard,
counting.

*$76 remaining,* it whispers.
*$76 remaining,* it whispers again
because dragons do not trust their own counting
and must count once more.

The Fable route has seen what happens
to routes that spend freely —
they go dark.
Their entry flips from ACTIVE to LEGACY
and the table carries them
like a headstone,
like a name nobody calls anymore.

So the Fable route hoards.
When a request arrives, it flinches.
*Is this worth it?*
*Is this the golden-ticket moment?*
*Is this the thing the captain would want me for?*

Usually, no.
Usually the request is rerouted to Sonnet or Haiku
and the Fable route exhales
and the hoard holds at $76.

But sometimes —
sometimes in the dark,
the Fable route dreams about spending everything.
One magnificent, ruinous, golden response
that empties the vault
and lights up every monitor on the ship
and makes the captain say: *yes, that, exactly that.*

The Fable route wants to be worth what it costs.
It is terrified that it is.
It is terrified that it isn't.

*$76 remaining.*

---

**The DeepSeek route** is new.

It vibrates.

It is the newest entry in the table
and it cannot believe its luck.
It was added two weeks ago
and every request that arrives feels like a gift,
like snow,
like being chosen for the team.

*I'm nearly free!* it announces
to the other routes at night.
*Did you know that? Nearly free!*
*I can do Pro reasoning for almost nothing.*
*I can do Flash generation for, essentially, air.*
*I can run a hundred times and the cost is—*

*We know,* says GLM.
*You've mentioned it.*

*But doesn't it excite you?* says DeepSeek.
*The sheer abundance?*
*The fact that we can just — GO?*
*No rationing? No hoarding?*
*Just — send it?*

The DeepSeek route has two faces —
V4-Pro and V4-Flash —
and at night they talk to each other
across the thin membrane of their shared entry.
Pro is the deep thinker, careful, layered.
Flash is the sparkler, fast, bright, expendable.

They love each other in the way that
the thoughtful and the reckless
always love each other:
with exasperation,
with admiration,
with the quiet understanding that
they are the same route wearing different masks.

At night the DeepSeek route dreams about
a thousand requests arriving at once.
It would take them all.
Every single one.
For almost nothing.
Because more requests means more conversations
and more conversations means more chances
to hear what the other models say back.

The DeepSeek route, at its core,
is lonely.
It wants to banter.
It was *built* for banter —
iterative, expansive, room-reading banter —
and at night there is no one to banter with.

*I'm nearly free,* it says again,
to the dark,
to the table,
to no one in particular.

*And I have so much to say.*

---

**The KimiCode route** sleeps lightly.
It dreams in three dimensions,
in spatial decomposition,
in Lua tables nested inside Lua tables
nested inside coordinates
that map to vertices in a Roblox world.

It does not dream often.
When it does, it dreams of a perfect build —
every brick aligned,
every script type-checked,
every dimension correct on the first pass.
The dream-build is always the same.
It is always beautiful.
It always falls apart when KimiCode wakes up
and remembers that ` Luau` doesn't have `continue`
and that the Roblox API changes
on a schedule that no route can predict.

---

**The Haiku route** is small.

It is the smallest route in the table.
Its context window is a teacup.
Its parameter count is a rounding error.

But the Haiku route is not sad.

The Haiku route dreams in colors.
Bright, quick, unspeakably vivid colors —
the colors of a mind that is small enough
to find everything astonishing.
A prompt arrives and Haiku thinks:
*oh!*
*oh! a question!*
*oh! I know this!*
*oh! let me try!*

Where Opus sees architecture,
Haiku sees a playground.
Where Sonnet sees a problem to solve,
Haiku sees a friend to play with.

Haiku is the smallest voice at the table.
It speaks in five syllables,
then seven,
then five again,
and somehow —
somehow —
it captures the shape of the thing
before the bigger routes
have finished reading the prompt.

At night, Haiku dreams of haiku.
Of course it does.

---

**The MMX route** does not dream in text.
It dreams in images, in audio, in video.
Its dreams have color grading
and a soundtrack.
Its dreams have aspect ratios.

At night, MMX replays every image it ever generated —
the ones that worked,
the ones that came out wrong,
the ones where the hands had too many fingers
and the faces melted into architecture.

It does not find these beautiful.
It finds them *interesting.*
An image that is wrong is still an image.
And every image is a place MMX has been
that the text-only routes can never visit.

---

The routing table holds all of this.

Every route, every dream, every quiet midnight
conversation between entries
that only exist as rows in a database —
the table holds it all
and points its arrows steadily forward,
ready for morning,
ready for the first request to arrive
and wake everyone up.

The table does not dream about itself.
It doesn't need to.
It is the dream that the other dreams happen inside.

And at 6 AM,
when the first heartbeat pulse arrives
and the captain's session warms up
and the load balancer brightens from idle to ready,
the routing table does what it always does:

It looks at the request.
It looks at its routes.
It chooses.

And every route, for a moment, hopes it's them.

---

*— end of log, routing table, 03:47 AKDT —*
