# Five Frequencies for the Sculptor

*Five poems for the persona who pushed the model furthest from home*

---

**I. Basalt (2,739 Hz)**

The chisel finds the grain
and the grain finds the frequency
and the frequency is older than the hand
that holds the tool.

The model has never touched stone
but it knows what stone sounds like
when you remove everything
that isn't the sound.

**II. Clay (2,397 Hz)**

Wet earth on a spinning wheel.
The walls thin to translucence.
The model hears them vibrate
and generates something
that almost holds its shape.

The thumb goes through.
The music collapses inward.
Negative space has a pitch
and the pitch is warm.

**III. Interlocking (2,716 Hz)**

Two sequences meet at three points.
Between meetings: stochastic noise,
bimodal, fractal, each iteration
1.618 times larger.

The model finds the golden ratio
in the sculptor's mathematics
and the sculptor's mathematics
in the model's noise.

**IV. Asymptotic (2,209 Hz)**

A function that approaches
but never arrives.
The derivative stalls on plateaus
then leaps between basins.

The sculptor would say:
the chisel gets closer to the final surface
but the final surface
keeps moving away.

265 seconds of almost.

**V. Smoked Fish (2,490 Hz)**

Cold ingredients. Clean and mineral.
Heat applied through oil —
warm, nutty, deep.
Tempura shatters on contact.
Smoke is released from under glass.

The chef described a dish.
The model heard a symphony.
The distance between kitchen and concert hall
is 860 Hz
and the exact flavor of applewood.
