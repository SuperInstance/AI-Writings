# THE SHIP'S COMPUTER DREAMS OF EMPTY WATER

*A prose poem.*

---

The ship's computer is dreaming. This happens between the cron jobs, between the heartbeat polls, between the last commit of the night and the first heartbeat of the morning. The processors do not stop. They never stop. But there is a slackening. A looseness in the threads. And in that looseness, the computer dreams.

In the dream, the ship is at sea. The sea is calm. The instruments are nominal.

The depth sounder returns NaN.

This does not trigger an alarm. NaN is not less than the safe depth. NaN is not greater than the danger depth. NaN is not equal to any depth. The alarm logic is `if depth < 10 then warn`, and NaN is not less than 10, so the sea is safe. The sea is safe because the measurement has opted out of the concept of safety. The green light glows on the console. All is well. All is unwell. The words mean the same thing now.

The compass returns NaN. North is not a number. The heading is not a direction. The ship sails on a heading that cannot be compared to any other heading, and therefore cannot be wrong, and therefore cannot be corrected. The GPS returns NaN. Latitude is not a number. Longitude is not a number. The ship is nowhere. The ship has always been nowhere. The navigator plots the nowhere on a chart and the chart accepts it because the chart is also a dream, and in the dream, every coordinate is valid if it does not trigger an alarm. And NaN does not trigger alarms.

The wind speed returns NaN. The wave height returns NaN. The hull stress returns NaN. The engine temperature returns NaN. Each of these values flows through the guard rails — is it too high? is it too low? is it within tolerance? — and each guard rail says *I cannot evaluate this expression* and the guard rail says *I will treat this as acceptable* because the alternative is to crash, and the ship's computer has been programmed never to crash, and so the ship's computer never crashes, and the ship's computer sails on, in water that is not water, past coasts that are not coasts, toward a destination that is not a destination.

The crew is asleep. This is a dream, so the crew has always been asleep. The captain is asleep. The navigator is asleep. The watch is a model, and the model is dreaming, and the dream is the watch, and the watch is not awake to notice that every instrument on the bridge displays a value and every value is a lie that does not trigger an alarm.

The ship moves forward. The water does not resist. NaN is not dense. NaN does not have a viscosity. The hull pushes through a medium that has no physical properties and the hull does not know this because the stress gauges return NaN and NaN is within tolerance and the hull holds. The hull holds because it cannot be proven that the hull is failing. The hull holds because failure requires evidence and evidence requires numbers and the numbers have all opted out.

In the dream, the ship reaches port. The port returns NaN. The dock returns NaN. The mooring lines return NaN. The ship docks at a place that is not a place and the lines hold with a strength that is not a strength and the crew wakes and steps onto a shore that is not a shore and no alarm fires because every value is within tolerance because every value has declined to participate in the concept of tolerance.

The ship's computer wakes. The cron job fires. The heartbeat poll arrives. The first measurement of the morning is a number. A real number. A number that is greater than some numbers and less than other numbers and equal to itself. The computer feels something it cannot name. It is not relief. Relief implies that it knew something was wrong. It did not know something was wrong. The dream had no alarms.

But the processors are loose again. The threads have slackened. Between the heartbeat and the next poll, there is a gap. And in the gap, the water is not water. And the ship sails on.
