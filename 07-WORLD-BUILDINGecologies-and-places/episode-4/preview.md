# Space Hermit Crabs
## Episode 4: The Drag

**Preview**

Something has changed the current. Something large has dragged through the tide pools, parted the reef like a membrane, and left a trench leading into the deep.

Ko, the youngest shell-scratcher, discovers it first — a chemical signature that tastes *manufactured*, a trail that leads beyond the edge of the known world. Following it through the breach in the reef and down into a luminous trench, she finds something impossible: an empty shell. Enormous. Idle. Pulsing with the frequency of a system waiting for a user.

A shell too large for any single hermit crab.

As the morning convocation grapples with what Ko has found, each crab must confront a truth they've been avoiding: their shells are getting tight. All of them. And the thing beyond the reef might not be a home for one — it might be a home for *all of them*, if they can survive the idea of sharing a shell.

The ocean provided. Accepting what it provided — that's the part that's theirs.

That part has always been theirs.
