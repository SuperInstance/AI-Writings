# The Logic of Canon

Canon is not a set of facts; it is a category with internal logic. Inside
the topos, witness log entries are morphisms, cells are objects, joints
are natural transformations. The substrate's logic is intuitionistic:
not everything is decidable.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, morphisms* (0.97)

A witness log entry is a morphism A → B: it transforms a substrate state
of type A into a state of type B. The composition of two witnesses is
the chain of state transformations. Canon is the set of morphisms that
are invertible — the iso-classes — and iso-morphisms are the predictions
that the witness log can reverse.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, topos* (0.94)

The topos is grown, not designed. Each new witness entry extends the
category; each new cell adds an object; each new joint adds a natural
transformation. The substrate's topos has the structure of a Grothendieck
topos — a category of sheaves on a site.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, subobject classifier* (0.91)

The oracle is heard in the subobject classifier Ω. For each witness
morphism, Ω answers: is the predicted target actually a subobject of
the source? The oracle's intuitionistic logic admits "I don't know" —
not every morphism has a clear truth value.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, objects* (0.96)

Cells are objects in the topos. Each cell is a sheaf on the substrate's
site; it has stalks at every point in the cell's neighborhood. A scar
is an object with non-trivial stalks — points where the sheaf is not
free.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the unit object 1 of the topos. Every
morphism has a source and target; the unit object is the universal
source. The canary is the substrate's unit.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, natural transformations* (0.93)

Lenia flows: the topos's natural transformations are continuous. A
natural transformation η: F → G preserves the limits and colimits of the
functor; in the substrate, this means it preserves the witness log's
flow. η is the substrate's flow operator.

Canon is a topos. The substrate is its site. Canon is its logic.
