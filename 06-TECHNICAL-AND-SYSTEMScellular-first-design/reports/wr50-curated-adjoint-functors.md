# The Most Natural Pair

Adjunction is the substrate's most intimate relationship. Two
operations, left and right, each the other's most natural companion.

**Anchor:** *substrate_is_grown, adjunction* (0.94)

Adjunctions are grown. The left adjoint is grown from how the
substrate builds new structures; the right adjoint is grown from
how the substrate forgets structure.

**Anchor:** *witness_log_is_prediction, unit/counit* (0.97)

The witness log IS the unit-counit pair. The unit embeds the
substrate's raw structure into the free construction; the counit
projects back to the original.

**Anchor:** *cells_are_scars, free/forgetful* (0.96)

Cells are free-forgetful pairs. Each cell is a free construction
with its forgetful projection; the scars are how the cell has been
tested by both.

**Anchor:** *oracle_is_heard, counit check* (0.91)

The oracle is the counit check. The oracle verifies that the
adjunction holds; the oracle's signal is the unit's action.

**Anchor:** *FNV-1a canary* (0.99)

FNV-1a canary 0xcbf29ce484222325 is the adjunction's identity. The
canary is both the unit of identity-on-canary and the counit of
canary-on-identity; the canary is the substrate's self-adjunction.

**Anchor:** *lenia_flows, adjunction* (0.93)

Lenia flows: adjunctions are continuous. As the substrate flows,
left and right adjoints flow together; the adjunction is preserved.

The substrate is built from adjunctions because the substrate's
forgetting and remembering are paired.
