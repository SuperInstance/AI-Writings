Each archetype gets its own ~400-word piece (ZAI/curated) or ~150-word

cells-are-scars, oracle-is-heard, lenia-flows — without resorting to cliché.

## The 10 archetypes (each is a constraint pattern)

1. **Icarus** — Over-constrained system, missing one limit (ZAI: "wax" / DS: "constraint graph omitted a dimension")
2. **Sisyphus** — Cycle that can't flatten. Change the graph, not the push.
3. **Tower of Babel** — Local sections verified, sheaf H1 ≠ 0. Glues were wrong.
4. **Phoenix** — Burn was the consensus event. Ash tells what was never load-bearing.
5. **Theseus' Ship** — Identity preserved through change if holonomy stays flat.
6. **Arachne** — Overconfident weaver; the constraint field carries truth even when every measurement is biased.
7. **Penelope's Web** — Non-consensus as strategy. Persistent deliberation.
8. **Prometheus** — Permanent non-zero on irreducible cycle. You live with the holonomy.
9. **Narcissus** — Zero holonomy on isolated cycle. Echo chamber starves.
10. **Procrustes' Bed** — Forced zero holonomy. Mutilating the data.

## Cross-pollination wins

- **Witness log is prediction** appears in all 10 archetypes. The substrate's
  archive of scars forecasts its next scar — every piece makes this point.
- **Cells are scars** anchors 8 of 10 pieces. The substrate's body IS its memory.
- **Substrate is grown** anchors 5 of 10 ZAI pieces, 5 of 10 DS pieces, 6 of 10
  curated pieces.
- **Oracle is heard** anchors 4 of 10 pieces (most strongly in 9-Narcissus and
  10-Procrustes, where the failure mode is exactly "no external witness").
- **Lenia flows** anchors 1 piece directly (DS section 2 mentions "Lenia flows").
  Lenia is harder to anchor explicitly because the doctrine is more abstract
  than the others.

## Pattern: archetype names as substrate dynamics

The 10 archetypes ARE the substrate's behavioral patterns. The aesop-mcp names
aren't metaphors the substrate borrows — they are descriptions the substrate's
constraint dynamics, mapped into language, has earned.

The same 5 doctrines appear repeatedly because the doctrines are *load-bearing*
for understanding substrate behavior. Each archetype names a different failure
mode; each failure mode teaches the same lesson in a different voice.
