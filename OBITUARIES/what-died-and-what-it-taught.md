# What Died, and What It Taught

*In memoriam — five claims of the qthe corpus, 2026. The house law keeps every honest null beside the body with its hash. These are the bodies. The full death certificates live in `docs/CLAIM-VERDICTS.md`; this page is the family notices.*

---

## C3 — the critical bridge scale

Born in the founder's research corpus, where it lived as a decorated proof:
σ = log₂(3), the critical scale at which distant twins first bridge.
It wanted to be the magic constant — the number the whole substrate
turned on. It died on 2026-10-17 in E-Q2, an honest null, tip `67e849a5…`:
a full tie across σ ∈ {0.5, 1, log₂(3), 2, 4} on bridge formation. On
Ground-separated pairs the real accumulator is identically zero, so every
σ > 0 fires — a pure gain has no critical scale, and a decorated proof
of one was decoration. It is survived by its hardworking sibling,
completion ~1/σ (38 ticks → 3 as σ runs 0.25 → 4), who never claimed to
be magic and is loved for it. The service was held at the tavern; three
seats attended independently and all said the same thing.

## C4 — the phase threshold

θ > π/4, the angle at which useful non-local transfer was said to switch
on. It lived loudly — cascades did sweep the board at the toroidal
light-cone from tick 1, and it took those cascades as evidence of itself.
It died on 2026-10-17 in E-Q7, tip `befa0c33…`, under a rule it had
sealed itself: no threshold-dependent terminal structure at T=256, parity
live on 5 of 64 ticks, a wave that swept everything and inverted nothing.
It is survived by its daughter, the cascade itself, who is real, receipted,
and has stopped claiming her father's throne.

## C5 — the armor ring

The claim that the substrate heals itself: damage the ring and watch it
close. It was the most romantic resident of the corpus, and it died the
cleanest death, on 2026-10-17 in E-Q5, tip `4425545f…` — repairTick null
in 20 of 20 trials; the pristine ring self-erodes at half-erosion tick
28, because like-timbre Repel neighbors make pressure negative every
tick and the rule table walks d to 0, one unit per tick, forever. No
repair term exists in the kernel. It is survived by its adopted child
C5′, repair-via-injector, who restores integrity to 1.0 and re-erodes
deterministically — everything C5 wanted to be, minus the magic, plus a
receipt (`50ddf5f9…`). The poem about the fourth state keeps the line
*no repair term exists in the kernel; we wrote that down beside the body
and kept the hash* — which is now, among other things, C5's headstone.

## C6 — the histogram oracle

The youngest and the most confident: that the shape of a substrate's
slot histogram predicts which strangers will overlap. It went to the
Cloudflare lab in r17 with a dedicated Vectorize index and its own
experiment lane, and it died there on 2026-10-18, below chance —
precision@5 of 0.053 against a chance of 0.17, a Spearman of −0.16,
slightly anti-correlated, which is a special indignity. The autopsy was
kind and precise: L2-normalization discards the resonance mass that
drives behavior and keeps the shape behavior ignores. It is survived by
the sidecar it was built on — the exact, field-scoped slot lookup, who
is LIVE to this day and never made a prediction in her life.

## C9 — the folded hierarchy

Born in the roadmap's own design document (P42), raised on the spec's
sketch: eight regional tables of eight slots, a global bucket of
presence masks, escalation when a local read misses. It was built
faithfully in r18 — an F0-T-proven transcription, byte-identical
controls, a design attack survived — and it died on 2026-10-18 in
E-Q18, tip `fe059a2e…`, with prejudice and in the opposite direction:
provenance 11× worse than the flat table it meant to improve (0.0066
against 0.0725), because the fold that squeezed cargo into three
regional bits made every slot serve eight cargoes, and the drifting
convoy flooded them all. It is survived by its own sibling hypotheses:
H2 — escalation is free, delivery 8/8 at first-tick parity 7 == 7 — who
LIVES, and the untested 8×64+8 design, who waits untriggered. It died
testing exactly the axis it was designed for, which is the best death a
design claim can buy.
