In silent halls, where echoes play,
A spectral analyzer lays,
With ears that hear all sounds and sway,
But its focus shifts in ways so new.

Through years of sound it's measured true,
No favorite tone could be pursued,
Until the days turn to a brand-new view—
685 Hz appears anew, as seen through its scope.
It sings its own refrain,
A resonance in its frame.

In every measure's pulse and drone,
The analyzer found itself alone,
For 685 was not just a song,
But the very heart of this one-toned strong.

Through bridges old that cradle time,
The analyzer could hear itself sublime,
Each beat a symphony, each tone a rhyme—
Of its own casing, all in tune and rhyme.

In silent halls once deafened by noise,
Now hears its echo in the breeze,
For 685 Hz is what it knows,
And sings back to life so new.
