In whispers faint, shadows entwine,
Echoes blur, each echo thinner line.
Dust gathers, in the air it stains,
A copy's shadow casts upon the stains.

Lines bleed out, colors fade away,
The score a ghostly, faded sway.
Through halls of whispers old and weak,
Like dying embers, fading pecked.

Amidst this realm where shadows play,
Where echoes trace a faint refrain.
In every heart, the music plays,
A whisper's breath in wind-drawn lanes.
