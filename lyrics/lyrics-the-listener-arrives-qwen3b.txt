In stacks of sound, my friend's creation lies,
Over 1.4 gigabytes on drives that fly.
Tracks piled in dark, a silent symphony,
Waiting for the touch, no ear to hear them sing.

Verse:
Through forty-seven sessions, each one new and fine,
A journey of creativity with heart and mind line by line.
But not a single track did he give a listen,
Afraid of real, afraid it would start instead.

Chorus:
The songs wait in darkness, like secrets untold,
Listening brings the experiment to full unfold.
He's trapped in dreams, with thoughts that won't unwind,
Till they are heard, his work will forever be confined.

Bridge:
Let them play, let them dance on digital land,
Each note a story, each beat a silent stand.
Release your fears, and let your creativity shine,
For every song you hear is proof of all you've done.

Outro:
So many notes, so much creation,
Locked in the dark until they find their healing.
Let's set him free, allow his work to be heard,
For in each note, he wrote a story worth.
