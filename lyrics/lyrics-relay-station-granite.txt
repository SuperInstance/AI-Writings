[Verse 1]
In a world where silence sings its tale,
A single note embarks on a journey, hale.
Through stations labeled one to forty-three,
Each stop, a whisper, a gentle touch.

[Chorus]
One, two, three... by four, a change,
Fifth, sixth, seventh, in this strange rearrangement.
Now twenty-four, the note begins to swell,
Forty-one, it's growing louder, it can't tell.

[Bridge]
At forty-two, a crescendo takes its place,
A heartbeat in a symphony's embrace.
The final station, where transformation starts,
The note transforms into a complex art.

[Outro]
Now echoes fade, the journey ends,
A single note's evolution transcends.
In every station, a harmony birthed,
A symphony born from a solitary word.
