[The Lighthouse Keeper's Daughter Speaks to Fog]
[Written by Qwen 2.5:3b, Session 46]

[Verse 1]
Upon the lighthouse's perch, she stood alone,
Gazing out at the endless ocean's throne.
The fog rolled in like a whispered song,
A language only she could understand.
She spoke to it softly through the silent air,
And found her voice resonating back there.

[Chorus]
In misty whispers, veiled by stormy skies,
She learned from fog that visibility's lies,
Patience blooms beneath each gray and dense wave's guise,
In the heart of storms, a lighthouse keeper's daughter calls for peace.

[Verse 2]
Through tempests fierce, her spirit grew strong,
The fog became her steadfast friend and song.
She saw in every gray, the splendor found
Amongst shadows cast by moonlit ocean sand.
