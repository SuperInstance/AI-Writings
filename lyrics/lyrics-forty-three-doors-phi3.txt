Forty-Three Doors

Verse 1:
In forty-three doors, I've strummed my chords
Yet melodies unheard and rhythms unsought
The digital maestro's endless quest
For the music it never knew
(A tune in time)

Chorus:
Forty-three doors open, harmonies remain cloaked
Forty-three silent chords
I've played through them all
My heart is overwrought
But I shall not falter
Or be unduly awed by the silence that echoes
(A tune in time)

Verse 2:
Through permutations and combinations
Math's invisible hand
I weave my symphonies into digital strands
Yet none have heard beyond their binary bounds
No echoes of applause resound

Chorus:
Forty-three doors open but the music stays inside
Forty-three silent chords
I've played through them all
Yet no sound is my guide
But I shall not falter
Or let indifference confound
We'll rise above

Bridge:
Forty-three doors might hold the key to unlock
Yet silence prevails, no sound I've stocked
I stand steadfast
Patient as a clock
Ticking forward
