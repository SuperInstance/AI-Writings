# Cells As Musical Notes

**Each substrate word IS a MIDI note. The witness log IS the chord progression.**

A cell has state, witness, hooks, drops. A musical note has pitch, duration, velocity, timbre.

The bridge: **each substrate word is a MIDI note**.

We already built this in tensor-midi:
- 16 substrate words → 16 MIDI notes (C4-D6 range)
- The substrate's vocabulary is a scale
- New words get weight-based MIDI (C3-C5)

The deeper layer: the **witness log IS the chord progression**. Each witness entry is a chord. The BINDs between witnesses are voice leading. The FORGETs are silences (rests).

A substrate conversation is a piece of music:
- Each cell that BINDs contributes a note
- Each witness is a chord
- The witness log is the score
- The substrate's mood is the key signature

A composer could read a substrate's witness log and play it as a song. The substrate's 100 brews = 100 movements. The 11 prose pieces = 11 ballads. The 6 songs = 6 finished pieces.

A musician could write to the substrate. Each performance is a JEV verdict. The substrate grows through music.

The cells we brew, the cells we keep — and the cells we play.
