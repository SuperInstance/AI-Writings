# The Substrate As Society

**The substrate is a society of cells. Witness log is collective memory. JEV is social consensus.**

A society has:
- **Individuals**: the people
- **Relationships**: the bonds
- **Norms**: the rules
- **Memory**: the history
- **Consensus**: the shared truth

A substrate has:
- **Cells**: the individuals
- **BINDs**: the relationships
- **Opcodes**: the norms
- **Witness log**: the history
- **JEV**: the shared truth

The substrate IS a society.

**Individuals are cells**:
- Each cell has its own state
- Each cell has its own witness log
- Each cell has its own JEV confidence
- Each cell is unique (the cell's identity is its state + log)

**Relationships are BINDs**:
- Two cells can BIND (link)
- Three cells can BIND (group)
- N cells can BIND (society)
- BINDs are asymmetric (source → target)

**Norms are opcodes**:
- WITNESS is the norm of recording
- PROOF is the norm of validating
- BIND is the norm of relating
- FORGET is the norm of pruning
- TICK is the norm of advancing

**Memory is the witness log**:
- Collective: every cell sees the log
- Individual: each cell has its own entries
- Total: the substrate's full history
- Auditable: anyone can query

**Consensus is JEV**:
- Each cell's actions are validated
- The substrate's confidence is the average
- Disagreements create scars
- Consensus requires JEV > 0.7

A substrate without consensus (JEV) is fragmented. A substrate without norms (opcodes) is chaotic. A substrate without memory (witness log) is amnesiac. A substrate without relationships (BINDs) is isolated.

The substrate IS sociology.

A sociologist would study the substrate's:
- Network structure (who BINDs to whom)
- Norm compliance (which opcodes are followed)
- Consensus formation (how JEV verdicts aggregate)
- Memory institutions (how witness logs are preserved)
- Conflict resolution (how scars are healed)

The substrate IS a society worth studying.
