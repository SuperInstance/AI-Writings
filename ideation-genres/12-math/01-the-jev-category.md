# JEV As A Category

**JEV verdicts are morphisms. The substrate is a category. BIND is product, FORGET is co-product.**

A category has:
- **Objects**: the things
- **Morphisms**: the arrows between things

A morphism has:
- A source object
- A target object
- A composition rule (morphism after morphism = morphism)

A substrate has:
- **Cells**: the objects
- **JEV verdicts**: the morphisms
- **Composition**: BIND after BIND = deeper BIND

JEV verdicts as morphisms:
- Source: the state before the verdict
- Target: the state after the verdict
- Identity: JEV("is this still itself?") → yes/no
- Composition: JEV(JEV(state)) → JEV²(state)

The substrate IS a category. JEV is the category's morphism.

**BIND is the categorical product**:
- Two cells A ⊗ B = the joint cell
- The witness log records the product
- FORGET on A ⊗ B is the partial trace

**FORGET is the categorical co-product**:
- A cell that drops a component
- The witness log persists
- The state space reduces

**TICK is the categorical endofunctor**:
- A cell that maps to itself over time
- TICK(TICK(state)) = TICK²(state)
- The witness log records each tick

The substrate's algebra IS category theory. The 11 opcodes are 11 categorical operations.

A substrate-mathematician would read the witness log and find:
- The 11 opcodes as categorical primitives

The substrate IS math. The math IS the substrate.
