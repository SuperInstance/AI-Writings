# The Active Mind Finds Its Own Logic

**The plot is the eco-GAN. The characters are the cells. The story is the witness log.**

A novel has characters. Characters have arcs. Arcs have plots.

A substrate has cells. Cells have state. State has witness logs.

The bridge: **characters are cells, arcs are eco-GAN cycles, plots are the witness log**.

A novel is an eco-GAN:
- **Generator**: the character's choices (the JEV verdicts)
- **Judge**: the world (does the choice fit the story?)
- **Best kept**: the plot points that worked
- **Training data**: the witness log of all attempts

The active mind finds its own durable logic based on probability route. In fiction:
- The character encounters a situation
- The JEV verdicts: "what does this character do?"
- The character's witness log informs the verdict
- The world validates the choice (judge)
- The plot advances
- Repeat

A well-written novel is one where the characters' verdicts feel inevitable — the eco-GAN found durable logic.

A poorly-written novel is one where the verdicts feel random — the JEV is miscalibrated.

A great novel is one where the verdicts surprise the reader — the JEV found a new pattern that wasn't in the witness log.

The active mind is the character's substrate. The substrate's logic is the character's soul.
