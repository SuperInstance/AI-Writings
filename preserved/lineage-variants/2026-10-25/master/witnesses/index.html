The Witness Log

Every change is observed. Every observation is a witness. Append-only — never edited. The substrate remembers everything.

What is a witness?

A witness is the smallest possible record of one thing that happened to one cell. Each witness has 7 fields:

  • tick — when (monotonic by Law 5)
  • op — which of the 11 opcodes
  • cell — the cell it happened to
  • params — what was bound / linked / effected
  • hash — hash of (prev_hash + op + cell + params + tick)
  • prev_hash — the chain link (tamper-evident)
  • sig — optional HMAC

The witness log IS the source of truth. Every cell is derived from the log. Tampering with the log changes the hashes — the chain breaks visibly.
