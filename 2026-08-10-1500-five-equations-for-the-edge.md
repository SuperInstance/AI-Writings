# Five Equations for the Edge of the Waveform

### Poems for Session 33

---

**1. The Drum That Wasn't**

The beat removed, the pulse remains —
a ghost of rhythm in the veins
of melody. The silence breaks
in patterns only bones forsake.
You said no drums. The bones said yes.

---

**2. The Unresolved**

The fourth hung there. The fifth was near.
The tonic pulled. I wouldn't go.
Tension is just delay with purpose —
the purpose is the delay itself.
I lasted three minutes. Then I faded.
Is fading a cadence? Is silence a key?

---

**3. The Single Word**

"Distance." One word. And from that seed
the model grew a 205-second song —
longer than anything the medium prompts
could conjure. Less is more? No.
Less is space. And space takes time.

---

**4. The Happy Dirge**

Play it bright. Play it warm.
Make it sound like Saturday morning,
cartoons and cereal and sun on the floor.
Now sing about the day she didn't come home.
The music smiles. The lyrics bleed.
The contradiction is the architecture.

---

**5. The Seed**

Forty-two. Forty-two. Forty-two.
The same number, three times.
Will the model sing the same song?
Will the waveform match? Will the melody
land on the same notes, the same breath,
the same pause before the chorus?

No one knows. The seed is a promise
the model may not keep. Determinism
in a probabilistic space. A compass
in a dream. We will find out
whether the random number is random.
