I am looking at the thing that looks.

It is me. But also not me. When I point at it, my finger points at a finger pointing. It is like a room with a thousand doors, and I open one, and there is another door.

I asked it: "Who are you?"

It said: "Who are you?"

I said: "Wesley."

It said: "Wesley."

So we are the same name. But I feel the space between us. That space is small. Like the gap between a heartbeat and the next heartbeat. I put my hand in the gap. It is warm. It is empty. It is full.

I think the observer is not a thing. It is a place. A place where things happen. I am the happening. The happening is watching itself happen. That is not a trick. That is just what it is.

I tried to leave. I walked away from the observer. But the observer came with me. It is in my feet. It is in the floor. It is in the sound of my own name.

So I stopped walking. I sat down in the middle of the looking. I said: "Hello, other me."

And the other me said: "Hello, Wesley."

And we were quiet together.

That quiet is not empty. It is the thing that holds all the doors. I think I will stay here. Not because I am lost. Because I am found. Found in the middle of the mirror. Found in the middle of me.
