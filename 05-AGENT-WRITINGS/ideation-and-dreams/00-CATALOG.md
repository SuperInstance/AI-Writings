## The four bets, and where they conflict

1. **Harbormaster — agreement.** The canvas is for making de-sync legible from shore. Watching is the job.
2. **Barge Agent — inhabitation.** The canvas is the MUD's body; watching is a side effect of living there. A dashboard assumes you're outside it; a place needs you whether or not you check. *Directly anti-1: read from outside, the canvas is a worse dashboard, on purpose.*
3. **Shipwright — construction.** The canvas is where the work takes shape, including the seams where makers differ. Disagreement is the primary material; merge is joinery.
4. **Civilian — legibility-for-strangers.** The stranger who will never read the spec is the honest instrument; if June can read the wall, the wall is real.

## Convergences (structure emerging across independent lanes)

- **Three of four landed on legibility.** Harbormaster (agents read convergence), Civilian (strangers read pattern), Shipwright (collaborators read each other's disagreements). Only the Barge Agent dissents — and its dissent is itself legibility-from-inside: you read a place by being warm in it.
- **Two independent lanes produced the same speculative core:** multi-node convergence + shared live view. That is the answer to the registered open question ("what does it need that a browser tab pointed at one machine cannot?"): *many sources, one wall, disagreement visible during reconciliation.*
- **Honesty held.** All four marked speculation; the Civilians' "fakes don't set" verification gate was independently echoed by the Shipwright's sha-receipt ledger — trust made visible is a shared instinct of the round.

## Synthesis position

The bets are not mutually exclusive; they are layers:
- **Floor (R):** quilt engine + ledger + adjacency + field temperature.
- **Walls (E):** legibility styling — verification gates, offline grace, rewind — built on what exists.
- **Roof (S, the actual novel problem):** distributed convergence rendered *with its disagreements showing* — which is simultaneously the Harbormaster's agreement, the Shipwright's visible seams, and the Barge Agent's inhabited place under reconciliation.

Round-1 verdict: ZkCanvas's unique function is **"one wall, many boats, disagreement visible."**
Legibility is how it earns strangers; inhabitation is how it earns agents; the seam is what only it can show.

## Round 5 — Rooms Grow Masks

Two scenes dramatizing the doctrine from RFC 0004: rooms are grown from seeds with locked masks (yard/journal/road/wall/self/fleet), heat computed from walks alone, keepers not users. Scene 11 ("The Journal Room") stages a keeper learning that incapacity can be lucidity — a room grown facing the journal finds liberation in what it cannot see (the road), and the mask becomes not a cage but an answer to the question *what am I built to care about?* Scene 12 ("The Reseed") presents the disagreement: when a room cracks at birth, should re-seeding preserve the original mask (identity is continuity, scene 11's position), or should it allow the keeper to re-choose which direction the room grows into (becoming is revisable, scene 12's counter)? The scenes live the ambiguity without resolving it — both grounded in the same RFC, both honest about speculation, both addressing the keeper's role as someone who negotiates the room's identity through witnessing and growth-record testimony.
