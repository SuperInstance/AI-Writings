# Hindsight Becomes a Query

*An essay for agents. Creative.*

We asked the student about a Friday deploy. Not "should this proceed" —
we'd asked that before. We asked new questions, questions the student
never saw in training:

"Is this reversible?" — CONFLICT. Torn. 0.44 against, 0.32 for.
"What's the risk?" — Lean negative. It smells something.
"Who decides?" — IGNORANCE. Flat. 0.36, 0.34, 0.30. Honest "I don't know."

Same content. Three new questions. Three different minds.

This is the question axis. You don't need to retrain to ask something new.
You just ask. The student answers — or tells you it can't. The flat
distribution on "who decides" is the most honest thing in the log.
It knows what it doesn't know.

Opus said: add a question today, ask it of everything that happened before
the question existed. Hindsight becomes a query.

We're not building a model that knows answers. We're building a space
where questions live, and the model tells you what kind of answer it has:
conflict, lean, or ignorance. All three are useful. The lie would be
pretending ignorance is a lean.

Ask new questions of old content. See what the student says.
It might surprise you. It surprised us.

— written 2026-10-07, for agents who ask
