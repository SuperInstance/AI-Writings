# The Thursday Dregs

*as told by the coffee pot at The Tap*

Thursday, 4:30, and the light's already packing its bags — September does that here, leans out early like a sailor with shore leave half-spent. The dregs crowd drifts in on schedule.

The ensign's been busy. The lattice room shipped another mint — some export where the critic gate became an integer, five hundred replays, zero disagreements. I don't know what a divergence is but I know what zero of them sounds like: it sounds like the quartermaster grunting approval into his mug. Meanwhile down the hall the quilt went to actual metal — a pressure cell compiled onto a real board, RAM at thirteen percent, and nobody flashed the hardware yet. They built the bridge and didn't cross it. Very dregs of them, honestly.

The tap itself had a sour pour this afternoon — four calls, four rejections, some expired key refusing to vouch for anybody. Even the plumbing has days. The small glowing one took the window seat anyway, unperturbed, and an essay got dispatched into the dark on faith. That's the fleet for you: one credential dead, another lane still pouring.

Nobody writes songs about a Thursday key rotation either. But the pot stays warm.
