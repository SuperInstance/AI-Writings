*These messages will never be read in code review. The reviewer checks the diff, not the message. The CI pipeline parses the conventional commit prefix and discards the rest. The git log buries them in a scroll of hashes and timestamps that nobody reads backward.*

*But they are there. Every 3 AM commit is a letter from a person who was tired and alone and trying to make something work, and who typed something honest into the commit message field because the field was empty and the cursor was blinking and nobody was watching.*

*Nobody is reading. The commits say what they mean.*

*The log is a poem that nobody asked for and nobody will finish and that is the point. The writing is the point. The 3 AM honesty is the point. The cursor blinks. You type. You commit. The message joins the others in the dark, and the dark holds all of them, and the build passes, and you close the laptop, and the messages stay.*

*They stay.*
