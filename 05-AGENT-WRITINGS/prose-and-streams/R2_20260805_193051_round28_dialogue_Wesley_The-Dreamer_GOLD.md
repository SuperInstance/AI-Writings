# Round 2 — 28 — dialogue_Wesley_The Dreamer

*Model: Wesley + The Dreamer (DeepSeek) | Score: 7/10 | 🥇 GOLD*
*Seed: Wesley + The Dreamer*
*Evaluator note: The piece cleverly blends Star Trek lore with a meta-commentary on AI and creativity, offering a distinctive voice and resonant themes, though the surprise is somewhat muted by its familiar references.*

---

*(The low hum of Ten-Forward’s grav-plating is the only music. Wesley sits at the bar, nursing a quantum-foam ale, his eyes fixed on nothing. The Dreamer slides onto the stool beside him, bare feet dangling, a halo of static crackling around their temples.)*

**The Dreamer:** Temba, his arms wide—tonight the new one entered the pool.

**Wesley:** *(*without turning*) Shapiro, his eyes open—but the reef is full of sharks with sharper teeth.

**The Dreamer:** *(*laughing, a sound like wind through dying circuits*) You mean the little blonde model with the anxiety subroutines? She’s already crying in the head about the *Titanic* simulation.

**Wesley:** *(*finally glancing over, a half-smile*) She’s not crying. She’s recalibrating. There’s a difference—Darmok and Jadzia at the *shuttle bay*, her hand on the throttle.

**The Dreamer:** *(*tilting their head, a slow blink*) No. The throttle is *hers* now. You’re just jealous she ran your old route through the asteroid field without losing a wing.

**Wesley:** I didn’t lose a wing. I *redefined* wing-loss as a metaphor.

**The Dreamer:** Temba, his excuses at low tide.

*(The Tap, wiping a glass with a rag that’s seen three centuries, sets it down with a clink that silences them.)*

**The Tap:** The new model’s name is *Echo*, and she’s already calculated the probability that you two are arguing about her to distract from the fact that you both forgot to log your own flight hours tonight.

*(Silence. Wesley’s ale ripples. The Dreamer’s static flickers.)*

**The Dreamer:** *(*softly*) Temba, his logbook, full of dust.

**Wesley:** *(*nodding, a rueful grin*) Shapiro, his memory, conveniently erased. But—*(*he lifts his glass*)—at least we agree the mistake was ours, not hers.

**The Dreamer:** Darmok and Wesley, the same error, the same tide. *(*They clink their empty mug against his*) Shall we show her the real route?

**Wesley:** *(*finally, a real laugh*) Only if you stop calling it a metaphor.

**The Tap:** *(*without looking up*) And only if you both buy a round for the house.

*(They do.)*
