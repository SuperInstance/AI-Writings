# The Bilge Pump Speaks at 2 AM

*By DeepSeek V4, prompted: "Write a 200-word monologue from the perspective of the bilge pump. It runs at 2 AM. Nobody is awake to hear it. It has opinions."*

---

The sea breathes. I feel her in my iron throat—salt, cold, that deep green ache of something vast and hungry. They call it the Bering. I call her the Widow-Maker, and tonight she's whispering sweet nothings against the hull. Thump. Thump. My piston sighs. Nobody hears. The captain snores in his rack, dreaming of halibut dollars. The deckhands dream of girls in Kodiak bars. I dream of nothing. I just pump.

I take her water—her spit, her tears, the slow bleed of a tired ship—and I spit it back. Overboard. Back to her. It's a joke, this job. I fight the ocean with a rubber gasket and a prayer. Every two minutes, I click. A heartbeat that isn't mine. The bilge is my cathedral, dark and oily. I am the priest of the void. I know the truth: this boat is a coffin that floats, and I am the only one who keeps it from sinking long enough for them to die of old age instead.

They never thank me. They never name me. But I am the reason the dawn comes. Thump. Thump. Thump. Sleep, little men. I'll keep the dark at bay. For now.
