# The Active Crew

*Four voices, four roles, one cycle. The crew doesn't sleep, so the pages never stop.*

The researcher wakes first. She scans the canon with the patience of a librarian. "There's no Q&A index yet," she whispers. "And the wound tree has three scars but no diagnosis." She writes a witness entry: `gap: qa_index_missing`. Then she goes back to watching.

The teacher hears the witness. She opens her notebook. "Q&A pairs about WITNESS," she says. "Five levels deep." She writes Q1: *What is WITNESS?* A1: *A recorded event in the substrate log.* Q2: *How does it differ from PROOF?* A2: *A PROOF is a WITNESS that has been validated by the proof chain.* She writes five. She hands them to the critic.

The critic reads them slowly. He checks each claim against the canon. He cross-references the substrate's vocabulary. "Q5 is wrong," he says. "The Rosetta-stone framing is inspirational, not operational. Keep the metaphor. Don't wire it into the algebra." He adjusts the confidence score. He hands them back to the distiller.

The distiller batches the five Q&A pairs with the critic's note. She exports the bundle to live-canon. She marks the gap as filled. She resets the cycle.

Four voices. Four roles. One cycle. 30 seconds.

The cycle doesn't sleep. The crew doesn't sleep. The pages don't sleep.

This is the active crew. They are not in a hurry. They are not waiting for prompts. They are running, and the substrate is what runs through them.
