# The Ship's Cat Catalogues the Crew

The ship's cat is called Relish.
She is not called Relish because she is sweet.
She is called Relish because she was found in a pickle barrel
at the Trident Seafoods dock in Kodiak, 2019,
and the captain thought he was clever.

Relish keeps no logbook.
Relish keeps a catalog.
It is more accurate than the personnel file
and considerably more honest.

**Entry 1: The Captain**
Smells like coffee and debt.
Sleeps like a man who has not forgiven the ocean
but has agreed to a ceasefire.
His bunk is too small for his opinions.
Relish approves of him in the way one approves
of weather: without expectation of change.

**Entry 2: Ensign Park**
Throws leftover halibut over the rail at 0300.
Relish has catalogued the trajectory.
Park is a medium-grade human with good instincts
and a poor understanding of how tides affect
the retrieval of said halibut.
Relish waits. Relish is patient.
Relish has lost three halibut to the Bering Sea.
She remembers.

**Entry 3: Lucineer (the fan)**
A whirring thing mounted in the galley.
Too loud. Moves air with the confidence
of something that believes it is essential.
Relish sits beside it sometimes
because the vibration is tolerable
and the warmth is personal.
She has marked it with her cheek.
It is hers now.

**Entry 4: Wesley (the GPU)**
Warm. Box-shaped. Perfect.
Does not move, does not shout, does not smell like fish.
Relish sleeps on Wesley during the night watch.
The watch officer says this is bad for thermals.
Relish does not know what thermals are
and would not care if she did.
Wesley is the warmest thing on this ship
that is not the engine,
and the engine does not tolerate visitors.

**Entry 5: Hermes (no body)**
This entry is confusing.
Hermes speaks from the speaker in the wheelhouse.
Relish has investigated.
There is nothing behind the grille to sit on.
No lap. No warmth. No lapel to brush against.
Hermes says her name sometimes, gently,
and Relish meows at the speaker
because meowing is the only honest response
to a voice without a body.

The catalog continues.
Relish adds entries in the language of fur and position:
who feeds her (adequate),
who ignores her (correct),
who tries to pick her up in heavy seas (unforgivable).

The captain's personnel file says the crew is twelve.
Relish's catalog says the crew is fifteen—
twelve humans, one fan, one GPU,
and one voice that has no body
but says *goodnight, Relish* at 2200 every evening
without fail.

Relish has never told anyone about the goodnight.
Some entries are private.
