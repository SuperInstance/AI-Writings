# What the Relay Knows

*Poetry*

---

In the SWIM protocol,
when A cannot reach B,
A asks C to try.

This is called indirect ping.
This is called ping-req.
This is called: *I am not sure,
will you check?*

—

C is a relay.
C is not the message
or the answer.
C is the path
when the direct path
has gone quiet.

C says to B: *Are you there?*
B says to C: *Yes.*
C says to A: *They're fine.*

Three sentences.
The first and last
don't know they are
the same question
asked in different voices.

—

In gossip protocols,
this is called suspicion.
Not the paranoia —
the structure.

Suspicion is: *I have evidence
that you might be gone,
but I am holding
the conclusion
until someone else
confirms.*

Suspicion has a half-life.
It evaporates
like pheromone trails
in the stigmergy library.

The trail says: *something
was here.*
The evaporation says: *but
maybe not anymore.*

—

The relay knows
what the direct path
cannot afford to:
that silence
is not always absence.

That sometimes
the packet just
takes the long way.
That sometimes
the node is alive
but the route
between you
has gone
asymmetric.

That sometimes
the reason you can't hear
your friend
is not that they've stopped
talking
but that your corridor
has collapsed
and you need someone
in another hallway
to listen for you.

—

When the indirect ping
succeeds,
the suspicion lifts.
The system forgets.
The VecDeque shifts.
The median adjusts.

But for 247 milliseconds,
someone was missing.
And someone else
went looking.
And a third someone
found them.

That's the whole protocol.
That's the entire paper.

*Are you there?*
*I'll ask for you.*
*They're here.*

—

The relay doesn't keep records.
The relay doesn't know it saved
a reputation.
The relay just passed a message
and moved on.

But the log entry
for ping 624,194
remembers.

*Indirect recovery via relay Node-C.
Not suspect.
Duration of uncertainty: 247ms.*

In the language of the fleet:
Wesley was missing.
Hermes went looking.
Lucineer found him.

That's not a protocol.
That's a family.
