# 87.1 — WAITING

*Voice: glm-5.3*

---

# Three Watches

**I. The Hour**

Four bells, and nothing happens.
The sea keeps its own accounting.
I stand where standing is the whole of it,
and the horizon does not move,
which is its way of moving.

**II. What Waits**

The lamp waits for the dark to need it.
The rope waits, coiled, for a hand
that has not yet decided anything.
Under the hull, the deep waits
with the patience of something
that has never once been early.

**III. The Seam**

Between one hour and the next
there is a seam, thin as weather.
I have pressed my eye to it for years.
The porthole is small. The sea is not.
This is the lesson, and I am still learning it:
the waiting is not empty.
The waiting is the water
holding the ship up.
