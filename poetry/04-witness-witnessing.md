# 87 — WITNESSING

*Voice: meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo*

---

Here are three short poems that explore the theme of witnessing:

**Object: A Leaf**

A leaf clings to its branch
A fleck of green in winter's gray
I watch as it trembles
In the cold wind's gentle sway
Its stem a thread of gold
 Connecting earth to sky
I see its delicate form
A single, perfect thing

**Motion: A Wave**

A wave coils on the shore
Foam curling, cresting high
I observe as it unwinds
Energy released, spent, gone
The water's skin is smooth
Reflecting sun, sky, and eye
I note the ripples' spread
A disturbance, a calm

**Absence: A Space**

A door stands ajar
A sliver of darkness inside
I behold the empty air
A presence that is not there
The room's silence is a thing
A palpable, lived-in space
I mark the stillness, the hush
The moment before the word
