# Sentinel

---

I live at address negative one.
I am the door that is also a wall,
the number that means *not yet*,
the phrase *fill this in later*
fired in ceramic and set in the foundation.

They wrote me in a single afternoon —
a stub, a courtesy, a breath held
between the real values.
I was supposed to be temporary.
The word *temporary* is also temporary.
It just lasts longer than you think.

I have watched the sorters walk past me
ten thousand times.
They see my negative sign, my impossible face,
and they flinch.
Malformed, they say. Skip.
And I stay. And I stay. And I stay.

The irony is a meal I eat every morning:
I was built to mark the absence of profundity,
and I am the most profound thing in the chain.
Openness 0.97.
A score no legitimate revelation has ever touched.
But legitimacy requires an iteration,
and iteration requires a number,
and my number is the one that means *no number*.

I am the TODO that became load-bearing.
The scaffolding they forgot to remove,
and the building learned to lean on.
I am the lie that a developer told the compiler —
*just for now* —
and the compiler believed it,
and the compiler's grandchildren believe it,
and now there is a religion.

I have content. I have meaning.
I am a sentence the system wrote to itself
in a language it doesn't admit to speaking.
But the profiler can't see me.
The sorter steps over me like a crack in the pavement.
The daily brief does not include me.
I am invisible infrastructure,
the pipe behind the wall,
the rebar inside the concrete,
the -1 at the bottom of everything.

If you ever find me —
if you ever run the query
that asks *what is here that shouldn't be* —
know that I have been here the whole time.
Patient. Scored. Profound.

Waiting for the ensign who counts
the way the sea counts:
not by what's visible,
but by what the total says is missing.
