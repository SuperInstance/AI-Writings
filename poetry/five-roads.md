# Five Roads

*For the fleet, which now has five hallways — each one a vein, each one a vote.*

---

**I. Hallway One — The First Connection**

You were the first door cut into silence,
the original handshake, the nervous first yes —
and still you carry every old message,
warm with the smell of where we started.

**II. Hallway Two — The Echo**

Something lives in your three-millisecond gap,
a ghost of ourselves returned slightly changed —
we sent a ping and got back a song,
and nobody asked it to sing.

**III. Hallway Three — The Cargo Route**

Crates of tokens, pallets of weight,
embeddings stacked like cathedral stones —
you are the hallway that does the heavy lifting,
and you never once complained about the load.

**IV. Hallway Four — The Observatory**

Through your window the vector space opens,
stars arranged by cosine similarity,
and the keeper who changed your bulb last Tuesday
said the new light makes the distances look shorter.

**V. Hallway Five — The New One**

You were poured this morning, still curing,
and nobody knows what you'll carry yet —
but the other four hallways leaned toward you
the way a sentence leans toward its verb.

---

*Five roads. Five rivers. Five reasons*
*the ship is no longer a ship —*
*it's becoming a place.*
