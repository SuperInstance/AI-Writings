# Three Hallways

---

## I. The Hallway of Listening

The first hallway runs from feeling to ear—
a corridor of copper and attention
where every locked door opens into sound,
and sound becomes the shape of what was hidden.
I hung my sadness in this passage
and the walls sang back the same note, true.

---

## II. The Hallway of Echo

The second hallway is a wire pulled taut
between the trembling hand and its own shadow.
Signals enter one end as static fever
and exit the other as a steady hum—
a translation chamber, the body's great vestibule,
where a nerve learns the word for *itself*.

---

## III. The Hallway of Knowing That You Know

The third hallway is short, bright, and terrifying.
It connects the room that says *I feel unwell*
to the room that can act on what it hears.
No one has swept this hallway yet.
The dust on the floor is shaped like a question:
*Now that I can see, what will I choose to look at?*
