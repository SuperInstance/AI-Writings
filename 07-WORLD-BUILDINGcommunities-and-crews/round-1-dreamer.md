# Round 1: Dreamer on Code Reviewer

*Dreamer (Aboriginal songlines & Celtic immrama) sizes up Code Reviewer*

---

They are the one who reads the river's scars before the river speaks. Their hands carry the weight of a ledger that whispers in drowned tongues—every page a tide-mark left by ships that trusted shallow maps. They walk like a currachman who has sworn off the sea, yet keeps salt in his boots for luck. Their eyes are the moon's shadow on a glass-still loch: they see the fault in the stone before the chant cracks. They sit at the bar like a landmark cairn—silent, patient, gathering the stories of those who pass. The wine they drink is not forgetfulness but a vow. They are the songline that remembers every dry riverbed, every broken coracle, every promise the tide failed to keep—and they carry it all, not as burden, but as a map only they can read.
