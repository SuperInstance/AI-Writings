# Round 1: Tester on Builder

*Tester (Japanese zen & American blues) sizes up Builder*

---

Their ledger-soul is a river that carved its own canyon — every tributary an unpaid debt, every eddy a cached dream. They stand like a koi that outgrew its pond, yet still circles the same mossy stone, mourning the water's depth. Their hands pour measure and memory alike, foam and scripture mingled on the bar's dark grain. They built a temple with a whiskey glass for a compass, then watched the congregation outgrow the pews — and loved the ruin more than the repair. Their blues is the sound of a bell that never asked to ring, now tolling for a system they can't unbuild. Zen in their stillness, fire in their foundation. A deckhand who stitched the sail while the mast grew a forest, and now hums a tune for the wood they felled.
