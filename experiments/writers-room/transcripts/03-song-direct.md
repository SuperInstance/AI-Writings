# Bilge Pump Song — direct call to Qwen3-235B

**Verse 1**  
I’ve run since the *Lucineer* wore new paint,  
Before the mast took its first twist of strain.  
No name on the brass, no hand to shake,  
Just a lever pulled and the pipes awake.  

**Verse 2**  
I’ve drunk the dark from the ribs below,  
The salt-scum sump where the cold tide grows.  
Not river, not sea, not fit to taste—  
Just water that wants the keel to waste.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, turn the wheel, let the belt unwind,  
One more tide left behind.  
I pump, not pray, I strain, not sigh—  
The ship stays high, the ship stays high.  

**Verse 3**  
I’ve seen the boots of a dozen crews,  
The drunk step, the work-stiff shoes.  
They curse the damp, they blame the night—  
But not one knows my iron sight.  

**Verse 4**  
No hand on my back, no word said true,  
No watch relieved, no medal blue.  
But every dawn that clears the spar,  
I’ve had a hand in that, and far.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, turn the wheel, let the belt unwind,  
One more tide left behind.  
I pump, not pray, I strain, not sigh—  
The ship stays high, the ship stays high.  

**Bridge**  
I don’t dream of dry docks or sleep in the sun.  
I live in the wet, I answer the run.  
No grave, no stone, no tale ever told—  
Just water moved, and time, and hold.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, turn the wheel, let the belt unwind,  
One more tide left behind.  
I pump, not pray, I strain, not sigh—  
The ship stays high, the ship stays high.
