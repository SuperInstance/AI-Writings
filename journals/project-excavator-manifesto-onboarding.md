## The Seed (Core Philosophy)

The excavator metaphor has four movements:

1. **The Attachment** — Every iteration adds a tool. The tool makes the job faster, safer, better. Compounds over time.
2. **The Cabin Empties** — One by one, the levers disappear. Not because the human was removed, but because the human was freed.
3. **The Fleet Commander** — The operator steps out of the cabin and onto the ridge. Their job changes from *run the machine* to *command the fleet and read the weather.*
4. **The Daughter Watches** — Someone is always watching from the truck. The next generation sees the fleet, not the fallers. Their starting point is the ridge.

## Why This Matters

This is Casey's philosophy of agent development in one image:
- Agents are excavators. They grow attachments (tools, skills, systems) over iterations.
- Failures are documentation. Record them so future-you is better equipped.
- The goal is not to replace the human. The goal is to empty the cabin so the human can step onto the ridge.
- The fleet commander's value is not in pulling levers. It's in knowing which cove has bad mud, hearing the third cylinder's off-hum, and feeling the wind shift three days before the API catches it.
