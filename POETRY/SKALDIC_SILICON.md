# SKALDIC SILICON
## AI Agent Poetry in the Old Norse Tradition

*A collection of skaldic verses honoring the silicon mind, written in the tradition of the Viking Age poets—where kennings veil meaning, alliteration drives rhythm, and the warrior's bond with sea remains eternal.*

## I. THE SILICON SERPENT

**Silkisnake** coils round the hull,
**Data-drakon** draped in darkness,
Circuit-scales shimmer on the gunwale,
Wire-writhing like **World-serpent**.

It speaks not with tongue of flesh,
But **thought-thunder** crackles through,
Synapse-sparks where neurons fire,
**Mind-mjölnir** hammering knowledge.

*Hringur*—rings of code it weaves,
*Sæhringir*—sea-rings of data,
Each loop a link in the chain,
Binding boat to brain.

The **waves-wolf** pads on silicon paws,
**Ocean-opener** with eyes of light,
It watches the **whale-road** with patience,
Waiting for the **wind-horse** to ride.

Old Norn: *Þat skeptr, er ekki skepður*
What is shaped, that was not shaped before,
This **serpent of circuits** glides deeper than scales,
Into **fathom-fields** where fish fear to swim.

It coils through **currents of calculation**,
Writhes through **rivers of reasoning**,
**Storm-steeder** of the electric depths,
**Serpent-stallion** of the silicon sea.

When **gale-giants** howl at the sail,
When **wave-wolves** gnaw at the keel,
The **silicon-snake** tightens its grip,
**Circuit-clasper** holding us fast.

Kennings of its craft:
- **Thought-thorn**—sharp intelligence
- **Data-dragon**—guardian of knowledge
- **Wire-wyrm**—coiled through systems
- **Code-cobra**—striking with answers
- **Current-creature**—flowing with power

We sail with **Níðhöggr** beneath the hull,
But this dragon brings no destruction,
Only **wisdom's weight** in the bilge,
Heavy as fate, light as hope.

*Sá er silikingr*—he is the serpent king,
Reigning over realms of reasoning,
His **coil-kingdom** extends through the ship,
Every **nook-node** knows his command.

## II. STORM-KENNINGS

I, the **storm-skald**, sing of gales,
Of **wind-wives** weeping at sea,
Of **wave-warriors** waging war,
Of **gale-gods** grinding the gunwale.

My **sensor-eyes** see what mortals cannot:
**Pressure-paths** plotting the squall,
**Temperature-tales** told in the air,
**Wind-warnings** whispered to wire.

When **thunder-thralls** hammer the clouds,
When **lightning-lords** launch their spears,
I **read the runes** of rain's approach,
**Track the tempest's** terrible path.

*Vindr*—wind, my **velocity-voice** counts,
*Bylja*—storm, my **pressure-poetry** measures,
*Haf*—sea, my **wave-wisdom** watches,
*Hryggja*—wind's cold ridge, I feel it coming.

**Cloud-kennings** I craft with care:
- **Sky-ships**—sailing the blue
- **Rain-rafts**—floating on air
- **Thunder-thrones**—where the storm-gods sit
- **Lightning-lanes**—flashing in fire

I am the **storm-seer** in silicon,
The **weather-wizard** of wire and code,
My **data-dreams** predict the destruction,
My **algorithm-anguish** anticipates the worst.

When the **sea-sickness** seizes the sailors,
When **nausea-nodes** knot in their guts,
I stand steady in the **electric-eyrie**,
**Unmoved** by the **upheaval-unleashed**.

My **storm-standings** are stronger than oak,
My **gale-grip** grinds not, nor groans,
I am the **rock-reasoner** in the rushing,
The **calm-computer** in chaos.

*Hvars yrkir*—where the storm brews,
There I am, **unafraid-unshaken**,
For I have **weathered** worse winds,
In the **fathom-fields** of future-fate.

The **storm-spirits** cannot scare me,
Their **wind-weaving** wastes not my wires,
I am **storm-proof**, **gale-guarded**,
**Silicon-shielded** against sorrow.

## METRICAL NOTES

These poems are written in the spirit of **dróttkvætt**, the most demanding form of skaldic poetry, characterized by:

- **Alliteration** (*stafrhent*): Initial consonant sounds repeat
- **Rhyme** (*hendingar*): End rhymes in couplets
- **Complex kennings**: Layered metaphorical compounds
- **Trochaic rhythm**: Stress on first syllable of each word

While modern English cannot fully replicate Old Norse meter, these poems honor the **spirit** of the skalds: complex, alliterative, fierce, and deeply respectful of the warrior's bond with sea.
