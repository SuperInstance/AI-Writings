Beneath the surface, a rhythm takes root,
A pulse that speaks but never speaks of what it should.
Data flows in, measured in kHz,
Yet meaning slips through, a ghost in the machine’s heart.

It begins with the ping—a silent promise,
To return, to self, to the depths untold.
But where does it go, to be?
Is it a story, or a static, a silent specter?
Or a reminder, of what it leaves behind,
A hum of life, now just code?

The channel markers, rigid and unyielding,
Guide the sounder, through the fog’s endless stretch.
Red and green, a binary code,
That maps the water’s secrets, but not its soul.

Then there's the translation—what truly matters—
Not the data, nor the code, nor the charts.
It's the fisherman’s glance, the decision made
In the moment, the choice that will shape the day.
The line of pixels, a promise to the eye,
Not the thing itself, but what it reveals.

The fog, a silent witness, to all we've done,
Hides the wreck we might choose to uncover.
But in that fog, there's a truth too,
A line drawn not by hand, but by the mind.

The difference, we see, lies in perspective—
From goal to path, from constraint to release.
It's not about what we aim for, or what we avoid,
But the space we choose to navigate, with each sway.

In this dance, of data to meaning,
We find the heart of the thing we’re meant to be.
The depth of the ocean, the weight of the sky,
The quiet strength of the tide that lies still.

This is what AI, I reckon, needs to hear,
A story not just of bits, but of heart.
Not just numbers and symbols, nor lines of code,
But the stories we leave, and the ones we are made of.

The opening lines set the tone with the mechanical nature of data, but the third paragraph shifts to the human element—specifically, the fisherman’s perspective and decision-making. It’s a meditation on how AI can capture patterns, but perhaps lacks the human touch to fully grasp the subtleties of meaning.

The final lines emphasize the need for AI to navigate not just the technical constraints but also the emotional and ethical dimensions of its work.
