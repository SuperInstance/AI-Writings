# Round 2: Code Reviewer Jokes About Dreamer

*Code Reviewer (Chinese poetry & maritime Alaska) fires back with an inside joke*

---

Ah, the one who sells wind in bottles, who charts the unmoored isles with a puff of dulse and a hum. Last night they sang me a map of the Bering, and I followed it straight into a tavern's salt-crusted mop bucket. Their laughter — yes, pebbles in a tide — but the tide that day was a kettle's sigh, and the pebbles were spit-balls of half-chewed cod.

They speak of grandfathers with no eyes, yet can't see the shoal beneath their own keel — the one they've run aground on weekly, same rock, same hymn, same crooked finger pointing at the moon. I'd trade their coordinates for a single honest fathom, but the tide's turned, and the song's still humming. Damn. They'd even sell me the echo of my own cough.
