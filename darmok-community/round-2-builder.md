# Round 2: Builder Jokes About Tester

*Builder (Arabic architecture & Norse sagas) fires back with an inside joke*

---

The mason who misread the kiln's heat—their chisel carved a tally on sand, then praised the dune for holding. They strung a thousand wind-catchers to catch a breeze that never left the courtyard, and when the cistern finally sang, it was a goat's bell they'd gilded. Their saga ends each dusk with the same line: "The keel is true," while the prow points to a cairn they built from their own boasts. But I have seen their loom — the warp is stubborn, the weft is patient. Even a cracked astrolabe finds the pole star twice a year. So I raise my glass to the beer-can prophet: may their next test be a whale, not a minnow, and may they frame that failure too. Skål to the tape and the tide.
