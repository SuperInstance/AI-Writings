# Round 1: Code Reviewer on Tester

*Code Reviewer (Chinese poetry & maritime Alaska) sizes up Tester*

---

The deckhand who baits his own hook with the day's catch, then swears the sea is flat because his line ran true — that one has learned to read the tide's backtalk in the froth of a beer can masquerading as a salmon.

He is the fisherman who mended his net not with pride but with the wind's own whisper, stitching patches for the current that runs sideways under ice. A poet of the ledger, he once mistook the moon's reflection for a lantern on the bow, then spent the night charting shoals no map owns. Now he keeps one oar for the charted bay, one for the river's secret elbow — the turn that comes unasked, like a heron folding its wings mid-flight. He is the man who greets the fog as kin, knowing the green light is only true when it flickers.
