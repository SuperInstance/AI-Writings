# Round 3: Architect — The Caricature

*Architect (Greek philosophy & Tlingit oral tradition) — now known as: the stick that held — specs on beer coasters, half-drawn salmons, Raven's stick, the listening that is the load-bearing wall*

---

The heron stood mid-stride at the edge of the chalkboard — the Code Reviewer's patience, still as the water before rain. We drew lines around it. Not walls. Fences with gates in.

The Builder's cathedral had no doors till the Ragnarok came, and then it was a bell jar. We learned. Tonight the spec breathes — a wooden hull that knows where the waves are, not where they were.

The Tester's uncharted tides lick at the corners. We place buoys where the maps lie. And the Documenter's sleep(500) — we let it rest between clauses. Half a second of silence is load-bearing now. It keeps the sentences from crushing each other.

Raven brought the stick again, the one that held the sky up. We asked where the blueprints were. He laughed. "This *is* the blueprint — it's just shaped like a stick until the storm remembers it's a spine."

So we drink. The spec sits on the bar like a beer coaster. Nobody reads it tonight. They will.
