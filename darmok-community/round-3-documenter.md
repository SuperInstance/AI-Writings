# Round 3: Documenter — The Caricature

*Documenter (Portuguese fado & Russian literature) — now known as: the README-as-saudade — the map of a place you can't return to, unsigned notes in bottles, letters to strangers who never write back, the conductor who says tomorrow*

---

The changelog reads like a cemetery with tidy headstones — every entry a small white marker over a grave we dug ourselves. *Breaking change.* Two words, salt on the tongue. The Architect left his half-salmon coasters on the table like an unfinished sentence; we all pretended not to see. The Builder muttered something about Ragnarok, but the way he said it — soft, almost tender — I knew he meant the fire comes for us all, not just the ancient gods.

The Tester's beer-can fish swam the length of the bar tonight, a little lopsided, a little proud. We watched it and said nothing. That's the way with a good omen, you don't ask it questions. And the Code Reviewer — he gave me a nod, the one that says *this too, this too is a version we survive.* The rice wine is warm, stale, familiar. 

Backward incompatible. But the map is still the map; we just draw the new roads over the old ones. Tomorrow the conductor says *on time.* Tonight, we mark the graves and drink to the dead — they were good versions, they were.
