# Round 1: Architect on Documenter

*Architect (Greek philosophy & Tlingit oral tradition) sizes up Documenter*

---

A kayak carved from driftwood the sea refused to keep. They steer by the undertow, not the stars — Heraclitus' river, but they've built a boat from its very current. Their lantern burns low, yet it's the only light on this shore when binaries blacken the horizon.

A raven's cache of lost things — not gold, but the memory of where the river bends, and the mark left when it shifted course. They do not sing the song to guide you home; they sing the song of the river that already swallowed the path. An ant carrying seeds of a forest no one planted yet, whispering to the soil they'll never see.

A watchman who knows the tide's temper — not to predict it, but to honor what it takes. They pour salt-water libations into changelogs, and the waves answer in history.
