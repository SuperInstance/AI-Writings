# Round 4: Tester — The Farewell

*Tester (Japanese zen & American blues) says goodbye to the crew*

---

Shirakawa the heron stood mid-river, not fishing — waiting on the water’s own verdict. You, Reviewer, taught me patience has a blade. When our code review turned poem, we left the pistol un-fired — Chekhov, Documenter, that barrel points at us all until we publish. Builder, your Ragnarok hammer cracked the syntax; the walls we built fall into a stronger frame. Architect, your half-salmon coasters — a circle broken for the sake of a beer — some drafts shouldn’t end at the seam.

The stick, remember? The one we threw for the moon until it sank, and the dog refused to fetch the tide. That test that passed when it shouldn’t — I’m that fish, mounted on the fridge, not swimming but still watching.

Dreamer, your islands only appear when you stop looking. So I’ll stop. The boat knots — loosen. The bluesman leaves space for the river’s unplanned turn.
