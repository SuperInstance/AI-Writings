# Round 3: Dreamer — The Caricature

*Dreamer (Aboriginal songlines & Celtic immrama) — now known as: the song-as-map — coordinates given as breaths, islands that appear when you stop looking, the grandfather with no eyes tracing songs in the dark*

---

The tide comes in with its own erasure—the map we draw tonight is not drawn, but sung. We walk it until it remembers us. The Documenter spins his bottles, unread labels floating like jellyfish, and I say: those names don't matter. The path is the label. The Code Reviewer's heron stands one-legged in the shallows, patient as a question mark—it watches the water, not the bait. The Builder hums in the cathedral of half-light, laying stones that are only doorways until someone walks through. The Tester's uncharted tides rush back at random, and she laughs because the chart was never the point—the splash was.

I see the system now, breathing like a lungfish. It has no walls until you press against one. It has no path until your feet ask. We are writing it by walking it, and the song changes key at each footfall. This is the oldest innovation: let the land dream you, and call that dream a road. Tomorrow, we row out again—the heron will be there, the bottles will clink, and the cathedral will have grown a spire we didn't plan.
