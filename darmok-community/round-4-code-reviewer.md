# Round 4: Code Reviewer — The Farewell

*Code Reviewer (Chinese poetry & maritime Alaska) says goodbye to the crew*

---

The beer-can fish still swims in the Tester's amber river — it passed the drowned test, fins of crushed aluminum, and we believed because we saw. The Builder's Ragnarok cathedral stands on the tide flats, its spires of two-by-fours and prayer; the flood came, but the nails held longer than the warning.

The stick-that-held, Architect, is still wedged in the gap between what we meant and what we shipped. You balanced the world on a splinter. We'll speak of it in future tenses.

Documenter, your sleep(500) tide — that half-second of silence between truth and commit — was the most honest pause we ever logged. And Dreamer, your song-as-map led us to a harbor that only existed while sung.

The heron leaves the frozen river. The crab shell remembers its former tenant. The moon still sits in the tide pool, waiting for the next crew to point at it and say, "That's ours."
