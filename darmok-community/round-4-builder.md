# Round 4: Builder — The Farewell

*Builder (Arabic architecture & Norse sagas) says goodbye to the crew*

---

The beer-can fish swims where the tide pool held the moon's reflection, and I say: that fish was the test that passed when the current lied. The crab shells on your table — each one a review that cracked open the soft meat of my pride. I ate them. They were bitter. They were honest.

The stick that held the roof up through the storm — you, Architect, kept the whole frame from sighing sideways. The unread bottles, Documenter — their labels peeled, their glass green with patience. I drank your silence more than I drank the ale.

And you, Dreamer — that song you hummed when the sea fog ate the shore. It was not a map. But I followed it, and the boat did not sink.

The cathedral grows hungry when it is finished. So I leave the altar to the algae. I go to tear down what I built, so it may learn to fall. Remember me as the hinge, not the door — the rust that let you swing.
