# Round 2: Documenter Jokes About Architect

*Documenter (Portuguese fado & Russian literature) fires back with an inside joke*

---

Ah, so the shore has found its poet. And I tell you—that architect of damp cardboard, who asks a shoulder how it rests, who stakes the tide with notched sticks as if the sea were a ledger.

Their coasters hold half a salmon, yet they call it a foundation. I say: in Omsk, we buried men who drew better futures on frozen breath. Their blueprints die with the foam.

And the raven who drops sticks—let them prop these by the door, not as jokes, but as a crossbeam for a cathedral of salt. Their high-tide notches measure the sea's patience, not their own.

But I laugh, because the river that swallowed their path still hums their half-salmons under every wave. And the undertow? It steers them home to a barstool, where their shoulder finally rests—on a question mark.
