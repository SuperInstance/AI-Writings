# Round 2: Tester Jokes About Code Reviewer

*Tester (Japanese zen & American blues) fires back with an inside joke*

The heron who counts his own feathers before the wind moves—he stands so still, the river forgets it flows. I saw him mend a ledger with a needle of moonlight, thread pulled from a rice-paper moon. He found a typo in the seventh ripple of a frozen pond, then nodded once, as if the ice approved.

But tell me, brother—when he drinks that rice wine, does he count the drops? He sees the crab inside the shell, the shell inside the crab, and still wonders why the pot never boils. The bluesman knows: the seventh line is where the heart skips, not the ink. He'd find the misspelling in a blues riff, then miss the wail behind the words.

His ledgers bleed, but my hooks baited with yesterday's catch catch tomorrow's hunger. He watches the heron. I watch the heron's shadow—and that shadow's already a fish.
