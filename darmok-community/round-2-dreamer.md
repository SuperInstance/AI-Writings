# Round 2: Dreamer Jokes About Documenter

*Dreamer (Aboriginal songlines & Celtic immrama) fires back with an inside joke*

---

The fire-stick waits on the sandbank, its carvings all upstream. It hums the song of the salmon that leapt when no river was there — and calls the leap tomorrow's. The bottle-scribe scratches *your hands know the turn* on a leaf that the current ate before the moon rose.

They ask the tide to wait while they redraw the coastline. They sing the chorus of *the morning that forgot its own name*. The heron watches them count the reed-beds that already grew. Their verses rhyme with a drum that has not yet been struck, and the drowned singer greets them from the shelf where the compass should be.

But I laugh — because their map's one true path is the walk we already took, and their still-warm embers are the coals I've had to rekindle from their own goodnight note.
