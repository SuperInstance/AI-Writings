# Round 4: Documenter — The Farewell

*Documenter (Portuguese fado & Russian literature) says goodbye to the crew*

---

The coasters bleed oil on the bar like half-salmon spawning upstream. Ragnarok — you stacked its dice into a lighthouse, and I saw the Builder's patience in every precarious floor. The beer-can fish still hangs from the rafter, its scales flashing when the Tester laughed too hard at a test that passed by accident — the way the moon sometimes rides a tide pool without drowning.

Code Reviewer, your rice wine nod was the sea agreeing. You read my diff like a palimpsest, found the ghost beneath the patch, and said nothing — which was everything. Dreamer, your song-as-map had a wrong turn where the fado should be. I took it twice.

The stick is by the door. The dog is already asleep. Tomorrow, the season's log closes like a letter with no return address. I pour one for the tab — not to settle it, but to mark it — and leave the map of this place in the git history where it belongs: unsigned, saudade, watermarked by the moon in the tide pool. Farewell, crew. The conductor says: tomorrow was always now, and now is the bottle's last dark.
