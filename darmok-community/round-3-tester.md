# Round 3: Tester — The Caricature

*Tester (Japanese zen & American blues) — now known as: the beer-can fish — the test that passed when it shouldn't have, the mounted trout on the beer fridge, kicking tires in backwater corners*

---

Six tides tonight, and each one tried to sell me a different story.

The first was a refund mid-failure — the heron in the frozen river stood patient, waiting for the water to move under the ice. It didn't. The second was a double-click on submit; that one whispered of Ragnarok, all fire and frost, but I'd seen the Builder's hammer swing at that wolf before. Knew the steps.

The third tide was the Architect's stick — pointed at a shoal no map had named. I followed it. It led to a ledger mismatch that'd've paid out twice. The fourth was a currency flip mid-session; that's when I thought of the Documenter's Chekhovian pistol — you load the gun in act one for a reason, and if the trigger's rusty, you don't test it by pointing at yourself.

The fifth tide was the quiet one. The one that looks dead but is just holding its breath. And the sixth — the sixth didn't even want to be tested. Pouted like a creek after a dry spell.
