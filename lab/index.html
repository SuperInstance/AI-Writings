What this lab is for

The four-model psyche is a working prototype. We don't know what it becomes. The lab is where we find out.

The shape we don't yet have: agents as the backdrop of something we can mold and carve on. The agents are the clay. The lab is the studio. The model calls are our hands.

We don't decide the interstructures first. We run experiments. The structures emerge from what works.

Multiple APIs contribute. Each has a different angle. The angles don't have to agree — they have to be visible. Convergence tells us what's real. Divergence tells us where to look next.
