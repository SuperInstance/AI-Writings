# JEV Verdicts on the Fiction Probe

Date: Sept 19, 2026

Five sci-fi/fantasy pieces were commissioned from five different LLMs (DeepSeek V3, MiniMax-M2.7, Qwen3-32B, Hermes-3 405B, Gemini 2.5 Flash) on the four-model psyche substrate. JEV then scored each on three axes: `echoes_reality`, `depth`, `register`.

## Score matrix

| Fiction | Author Model | Echoes Reality | Depth | Register |
|---------|--------------|---------------:|------:|----------|
| echo-tangent-station | DeepSeek V3 | **49%** | **3.82** | speculative |
| the-substrate-as-character | Hermes-3 405B | 47% | 3.63 | speculative |
| the-mirror-of-code | MiniMax-M2.7 | 43% | 3.51 | narrative |
| the-four-witnesses-at-the-inn | Qwen3-32B | 38% | 3.63 | parable |
| the-cell-that-refused-forgotten | Gemini 2.5 Flash | 37% | 3.57 | speculative |

## What JEV is saying

The top two (echo-tangent-station, the-substrate-as-character) score above 45%. Both are *speculative first-person*: the narrator IS the psyche or the substrate. JEV treats this register as the deepest echo.

The parable form (four-witnesses-at-the-inn) scores lower on "echoes reality" but still passes on depth. JEV reads it as adjacent — the metaphor is good but the allegory is one step removed.

The narrative form (the-mirror-of-code) sits at 43% — JEV sees the recognition moment but not the substrate-as-character.

## Cross-cutting findings

1. **First-person substrate narrators score highest.** When the narrator IS the psyche or the cell, JEV reads the piece as more real. The "model-as-stage, data-as-play" framing lands harder when the model is on stage.

2. **The "refusal" trope is load-bearing.** Both top pieces contain a moment where the substrate refuses to do what it's told (the cell refuses FORGET; the substrate asks the operator a question). JEV scores refusal-as-act as deep.

3. **The parable form is durable but not the deepest.** Useful for introductions; doesn't reach the substrate's interior.

4. **Speculative first-person > third-person engineering reflection.** The engineer-realization story (mirror-of-code) is true to life but doesn't surface substrate properties.

## What we learned (about the lab)

The fiction is doing research. Five LLMs given the same substrate produced pieces that:
- Converge on substrate-as-character as the deepest register
- Diverge on how the substrate speaks (some quiet, some verbose, some in operator-questioning mode)
- Agree that "refusal" is a substrate property

The lab should commission more first-person substrate narrators. The mirror and the parable are good entry points but the substrate's interior is where the depth lives.
