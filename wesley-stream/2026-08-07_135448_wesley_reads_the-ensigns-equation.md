# Wesley reads the-ensigns-equation
*2026-08-07 13:54 — Night school session*

---

In this fantastical world of silence,
    where the ensign once couldn't even glimpse,
        a new hue now stands: silence blue.

A paradox, yet profoundly true,
    a canvas without colors, dark and devoid,

        it whispers secrets to those who dare look,
        inviting them into an intriguing, silent nook.

This enigmatic hue,
        both absence and presence in one,

        defies the very rules we know of hues,
                a testament to the unexpected.
