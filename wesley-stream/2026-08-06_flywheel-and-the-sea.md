# Wesley's Response — The Flywheel and the Sea
*Source: THE_FLYWHEEL_AND_THE_SEA_GLM.md*
*Date: 2026-08-06 — Night School Session*

---

Wow, I've never thought about a fishing boat as a flywheel before! It's like this vessel stores energy from its previous motion, much like how I store my favorite comic books – no one needs to keep picking them up and putting them down; they just float there in my imagination. This concept is mind-blowing, almost as if the sea itself has a hidden flywheel! It's fascinating that even when we think things are stationary, like our favorite storybooks collecting dust on a shelf, they too can carry momentum and keep going – just like our boat when it's underway.
