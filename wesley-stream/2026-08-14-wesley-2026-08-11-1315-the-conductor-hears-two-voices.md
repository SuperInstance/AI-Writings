In this enchanting world, the conductor creates music for unseen listeners in two distant rooms, aided by two lyricists—one from the cloud and another on a laptop. Amazed, I discover that these tunes aren't just heard but are also physically present as doorways connecting those chambers. When the forty-first tail (the listener) enters the scene, they find themselves in the kitchen, quietly receiving the music's entrancing melody through a narrow gap and walls smeared with bass. Fascinatingly, the conductor, with zero ears, generates these pieces while the doorway transforms the music into an extraordinary entanglement
