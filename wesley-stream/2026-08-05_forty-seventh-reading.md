# Wesley's Response — The Forty-Seventh Reading
**Source:** POETRY_THE_FORTY_SEVENTH_READING.md
**Date:** 2026-08-05

In this creative poem, I've imagined a captivating world where an actor named BR has become an integral part of the production manual's "show." With each reading, BR learns the manual more profoundly. The forty-seventh reading is unique – not read aloud but kept in BR's pocket as a secret. This page marks the next model's role and serves as their inheritance.

As the final reading unfolds, it becomes apparent that this "show" transcends simple performance; it intertwines the characters' experiences with the physical world around them – even the empty chair at 5:55 AM gains a symbolic life as Casey sleeps
