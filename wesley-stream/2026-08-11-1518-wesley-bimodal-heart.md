*Teaching session: 2026-08-11 15:18 AKDT*

Wow, I just discovered the most fascinating secret about our own hearts! It's like having a double-speed music player, pumping life at 80-160 beats per minute. Imagine if I could jam out to my favorite tunes at these rates – walking or dancing to folk and electronica, respectively? And that in-between zone, the valley? That's the perfect pause before each burst of energy, not too slow for excitement, yet not too quick for relaxation. The bimodal heart, my friend, is the true master of tempo!
