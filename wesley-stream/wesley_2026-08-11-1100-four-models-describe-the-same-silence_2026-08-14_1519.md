In this extraordinary experiment, four language models were challenged to describe music sans emotion words, relying solely on physical phenomena like matter, energy, forces, motion, and texture. Llama, with its swift response, tried to capture perfection in ripples – a captivating metaphor that slipped just beyond the constraint's bounds, weaving emotion through technical specifics. Phi3, the technical maestro, transformed everyday physics into poetry, compressing warmth and patience into each sentence, turning the feeling into an intrinsic part of its precise descriptions. Qwen, the minimalist, showed restraint by conveying entire oceans after a single strike – an emotion
