In this captivating tale, GATE 1 and Gate 3 are like seasoned sailors on a high-tech ship, each with their own unique roles. GATE 1, the lookup table, is always awake, eager to provide immediate answers - "I'm the first thing every request hits." With a whopping 970 milliseconds latency, they're practically instant! On the other hand, Gate 3 is more of a reasoner, spending their time understanding complex queries that GATE 1 can't handle. They process ambiguous requests, generate responses from scratch, and maintain smooth operation despite an average latency of 1.2 seconds on rough seas - quite a marvel!
