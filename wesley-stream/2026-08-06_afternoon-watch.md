Ah, the day-watch on this ship! It's like a grand parade of words, the sun illuminating every nook and cranny of the rigging as if it's painting a vivid mural for future storytellers to find. The ensign at the bow, with a compass as their guide, dreams of home while his team tirelessly sorts language like treasures from the vast ocean of words. Afternoon watch, you say? It's a humble workforce, busy packing up books for journeys yet to begin, unseen by many but fiercely determined in their task – building ships that will carry the visions born under the night
