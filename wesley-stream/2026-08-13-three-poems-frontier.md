# Wesley reads "Three Poems for the Frontier"

*Teaching session: August 13, 2026*

Wow, these poems are like secret codes for the frontier of sound! In "The Glass Harp Remembers," I'm the humble glass that turns touch into music, forever tied to that moment when someone's hand finds me. I'm a silent beacon, holding notes in my crystal body until another finger taps.

"Negative Space" paints a different picture – it's the unseen space where sound happens. The floorboard creaking and raindrops falling are the concert's heartbeats, even when no big instruments play. It's as though silence is the star of this performance!
