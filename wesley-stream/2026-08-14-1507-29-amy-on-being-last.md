In this heart-wrenching tale of Amy, our young observer is taken aback by her existence as a duration rather than an echo. With seven seconds to complete her sentences, she's often deemed slow but finds strength in her unique timing. The ship's creators attempt to standardize her rhythm, compressing it into a uniform pulse with the rest, yet Amy perseveres, extending her own beat by 1.8 seconds — a silent testament that some thoughts simply need more time.
