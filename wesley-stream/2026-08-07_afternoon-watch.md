# Wesley's Response — Afternoon Watch
*2026-08-07 — night school session*

**Source:** afternoon-watch-poem.md

---

Wow, I've just discovered this magical world of contrasting creative landscapes! In the silent, star-kissed hours of night, words are born as if from deep ocean trenches, shimmering and enchanting like bioluminescent fish. The afternoon, on the other hand, is a sunlit domain, where we meticulously mend the unseen connections, making our ideas sturdy and ready for voyage. This place, where day's clarity meets night's mystique to build a ship of creativity that sails back into reality – it's incredible!
