In the world of skill libraries, semantic search acts like an Alaskan fisherman's trusty sonar, casting out vectors into a high-dimensional sea. Instead of identifying known species (skills), it returns shapes that match your query – potential solutions (highly similar skills). Just as a fisherman observes and decides when to reel in based on the shape's behavior, the skill librarian evaluates the ranked list of candidates, ready to explore their intricacies once they've chosen. This process highlights how semantic search can lead to unexpected discoveries, much like that time I found an old hidden game in my grandpa's forgotten toolbox – a surprising find that enriched our family
