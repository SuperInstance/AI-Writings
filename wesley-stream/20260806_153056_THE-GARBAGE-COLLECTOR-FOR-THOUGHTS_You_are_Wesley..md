In the realm of memory's vast bay,
A keeper stands, not just to light their way,
But to manage each tile's mooring,
With a ledger in her mind, she records:
The idle, hove-to, and those that slipped away.

Her job is not to signal 'free', but to ensure
That unused tiles do not obscure the core,
Through an invisible tide, they'll wane and disappear,
A dance of data, like a boat's slow drift,
As pheromone strengths, once rich, now fade away.

This is mark-and-sweep, a rhythmic dance,
The keeper's gentle hand, the room's own glance,
In memory's bay, they weave their tale anew.
