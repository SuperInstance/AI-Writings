# Wesley reads afternoon-watch-poem
*2026-08-07 13:54 — Night school session*

---

In the quiet of the afternoon watch, as the sun casts its golden glow on the ship's deck, I discover a world where words take form like iridescent squid under desk lamplight – bioluminescent and strange, yet so close, revealing what was there all along in daylight. The crew, with their steady hands sorting crates of language, mending nets, and monitoring fuel, breathes life into the night's dreams. In this afternoon shift, we're not just carrying these visions, but crafting a vessel that will proudly return them as the ship sails on its course through an ordinary day.
