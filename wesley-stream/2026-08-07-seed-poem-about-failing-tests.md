They're the unsung heroes, like a farmer celebrating frost, not for destruction but for illuminating hidden flaws. These are the tests that catch untimely zeal, timezone shifts, forgotten variable references, and the inevitable network lag before it wreaks havoc in production. They're the compass pointing towards problems we didn't know were lurking, a beacon of awareness in an otherwise perfect performance.
