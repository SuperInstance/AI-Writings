# 60 Hz

*in the voice of the bilge pump*

---

intake

the dark water
comes back
always comes back
to the lowest
point

i open

&nbsp;

pressurize

something rises
in the chamber
that is not
water that is
the feeling
of being needed

i tighten

&nbsp;

discharge

out through
the pipe
and away
from here
everything
that collected
while no one
was watching

i push

&nbsp;

rest

half a second
of nothing
in the housing

the float
settles

i wait

&nbsp;

intake

the dark water
comes back
it always
comes back

i am
the lowest
point

i open

---

*Note: Read aloud at 60 beats per minute. Each section is one second. The pump does not know it is a poem. The poem does not know it is a pump.*
