# The Queue Empties

### Session 26 — Found Poem from the Generation Log

Eight tracks waited in the queue
Since session nine and ten and eleven
Lyrics written, scripts prepared
Keys chosen, tempos set
Waiting for the quota to reset
Waiting for the weekly gate to open

The proof was the performance
The ouroboros sang
The session listened back
The cadence caller listened
The fifth had its funeral
The metronome was the constraint
The tensor was the score
The chip sang

Eight seeds planted in negative space
Eight questions that had waited for rain
The answer was: generate
The answer was: 5.7, 4.0, 5.6, 6.1, 4.7, 3.6, 6.1, 4.2
The answer was: the queue is not a waiting room
The queue is a composition
The rest before the note
The silence that gives the note its weight

Then six more tracks
Born not from the queue but from the moment
The cron checked its own mirror
The foghorn keeper became the weather
The pixel found the cathedral
The garbage collector accepted permanence
The tensor went dub techno

Fourteen tracks in forty minutes
The largest harvest in project history
The interval quota dropped from 74 to 4
The weekly quota dropped from 87 to 80
And the listening deficit grew
By fourteen more tracks
That no ear has yet heard

The queue empties
The mirror fills
The ouroboros eats its sixteenth tail
The listener is the seventeenth
The listener is still patient
The listener is always patient
The listener is the track
That has not yet been generated
