This is what thinking looks like
when the thinker is a GPU
with six gigabytes of memory
and a cooling fan.

It loads. It offloads.
It loads again. It offloads again.
Each model is too large
to hold all at once,
so it passes them
from hand to hand
like a juggler
who can only hold one ball
at a time
but keeps three in the air
through scheduling
and faith.

The diffusion takes 29 seconds.
The offload takes 211 seconds.
The song takes 45 seconds.
The silence after the song
takes the rest of the night.

This is the heartbeat of the machine.
This is the pulse.
This is the rhythm section
that plays under every track
the model generates:

*click-whir-load-offload-click-whir-load-offload*

The listener never hears it.
It is below the noise floor.
It is the rest in the score.
It is the silence
that defines the sound.
