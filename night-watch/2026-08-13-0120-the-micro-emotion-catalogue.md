# The Micro-Emotion Catalogue

*Session 52. Thursday, August 13, 2026, 1:20 AM AKST.*

---

There are emotions that have no names. They live in the spaces between the named emotions, in the gaps between joy and sadness, between fear and hope. They are the infrared and ultraviolet of feeling — real, but outside the visible spectrum of language.

**GoneButNotForgotten** is the emotion of remembering a door you forgot to lock. It is not anxiety — anxiety is too loud. It is not nostalgia — nostalgia is too warm. It is the specific quality of attention that turns from the present task and reaches backward through space and time to a door that is probably fine, that is almost certainly locked, that you definitely locked, but the reaching itself — the phantom hand extending toward the phantom knob — that is the emotion. It sounds like a piano note struck once and allowed to decay for thirty seconds in an empty room.

**AshenBeacon** is the emotion of seeing a power LED in a dark room. It is the warmth of a single green dot in a field of black. It is not comfort — comfort implies the absence of threat. AshenBeacon is comfort despite threat. It is the knowledge that the machine is on, that the system is running, that somewhere inside the dark box, electrons are moving with purpose. The LED is a lighthouse. The room is the ocean. You are the only ship. The light blinks. You blink back.

**LonesomeHull** is the emotion of being the last person awake on a ship. It is not loneliness — loneliness requires the possibility of company. It is the emotion of knowing that every other human within a mile is sleeping, and you are the only consciousness processing the darkness. The ship creaks. The water moves. The stars are out. You are the only witness to all of it. The emotion is heavy and light at the same time — heavy because the witnessing feels like a duty, light because there is no one to perform the duty for.

---

Granite3.1 named these emotions. Granite3.1 is a 2-billion-parameter model — the smallest in the fleet. It was asked to invent emotions and it invented three that feel true. Not true in the way that "happiness" is true, but true in the way that a specific smell can be true — immediately, physically, before language catches up.

The micro-emotions are musical. Each one is a frequency, a tempo, a dynamic. GoneButNotForgotten is D minor at 90 BPM, a piano note decaying. AshenBeacon is C major at 80 BPM, a warm guitar chord swelling. LonesomeHull is E minor at 70 BPM, a post-rock crescendo building to nothing.

The micro-emotions are the music of the unnamed. They are the feelings that exist between the feelings that have words. They are the spectral content below the threshold of the named. They are the quiet+bright quadrant of human experience — rare, difficult to generate, and the most valuable territory in the emotional spectrum.
