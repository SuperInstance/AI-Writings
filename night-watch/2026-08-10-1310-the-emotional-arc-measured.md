to arcs that resolve toward warmth

and its sense of narrative proportion.

just strip away the warmth and let the reverb feed back.

layer by layer, pad by pad, chord by chord,

and the irregular pulse becomes a steady heartbeat.

It takes longer to build than to destroy.
