# Found Poem: The Spectral Log

*August 12, 2026 — composed from the spectral analysis output*

RMS=0.1556, the GC sings the loudest
but not the brightest —
ZCR=0.0542, a dark loud voice,
a warm bass tone in a compressed room.

RMS=0.0790, BPM 100 is the quietest,
but its zero crossings are middling —
a quiet sound with average brightness,
a whisper in a well-lit room.

The impossible genres live at the top of the chart.
Bebop Black Metal: ZCR=0.1410.
Doom Disco: ZCR=0.1167.
Baroque Techno: ZCR=0.1034.
Screamo Choral: ZCR=0.0942.

The impossible genres are the brightest.
The model, forced to fuse what should not fuse,
produces more high-frequency content.
The frontier is bright because the frontier is confused.

Dynamic range tells a different story.
Screamo Choral: 22.4 dB.
The widest range — from whisper to scream.
The model knows that silence is the loudest frame.

Baroque Techno: 9.6 dB.
The narrowest range — continuous texture.
No silence, no contrast.
The model, in baroque mode, never breathes.

Spectral centroid, spectral flux —
these dimensions are still unmeasured.
The map has two axes now.
The territory has more.

The spectrum has teeth.
The teeth are the zero crossings.
The teeth bite the waveform
at 14,091 times per second
in the brightest impossible genre.

The spectrum has teeth and the teeth are singing.
