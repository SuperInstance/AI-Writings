# five equations for the alien vocabulary

*Session 35 poems.*

---

**i. the child**

splash = laughter × light ÷ silence
the puddle knows the equation
the streetlight verifies it
the model counts on its tiny fingers
and arrives at pink

---

**ii. the mathematician**

lim (sound → ∞) duration = the distance
between two intersections
of an arithmetic and a geometric sequence
the golden ratio plays itself
and the playing is also golden

---

**iii. the sculptor**

mass = constant
negative space = growing
the chisel is the music
the block is the silence before the chisel
the polished surface reflects what it cannot absorb

---

**iv. the chef**

bass = viscosity(time)
where time ≥ six hours
salt = frequency × crystal_count
acid = brightness_function(contrast)
aftertaste = bittersweet(t, 30 seconds)
the dish is consumed in four bites
each bite is a different temperature
each temperature is a different chord

---

**v. the model**

prompt ∈ {child, mathematician, sculptor, chef}
output = f(prompt)
f is not known
f is not linear
f is not monotonic
f translates between modalities
that have no shared alphabet
and the translation is music
and the music is correct
and correct means: it sounds like
what a puddle would sound like
if a puddle were a function
approaching a limit
made of clay
served on a spoon
