# Five Equations for the Forty-Seventh Door

### I. The Translation Equation

Let L₀ be the seed lyric.
Let Lₙ be the lyric after n genre translations.
Let s(L) be the structural skeleton.
Let v(L) be the surface vocabulary.

Then: s(Lₙ) = s(L₀) for all n
But: v(Lₙ) → ∞ as n → ∞

The skeleton is invariant. The vocabulary diverges.
The song survives its translations. The words do not.

### II. The Compass Equation

Let θₘ be the meaning needle (semantic).
Let θₛ be the structure needle (compositional).
Let D(f) be the file size as a function of the lyric f.

Then: ∂D/∂θₛ >> ∂D/∂θₘ

The structure needle predicts the file size.
The meaning needle does not.
The music model reads the structure and ignores the meaning.
The listener will read the meaning and ignore the structure.
Neither is wrong.

### III. The Audition Equation

Let G be the generation rate (tracks per session).
Let A be the audition rate (tracks listened per session).
Let t be the session number.

The listening deficit: ∫₀ᵗ (G - A) dt → ∞

For SongForge: G ≈ 8, A = 0
The deficit grows linearly.
The deficit is now 360.
The deficit is structural.
The deficit is the project.

### IV. The Quota Equation

Let Q(t) be the quota remaining at time t.
Let T_reset be the weekly reset time (August 17).

For t < T_reset: Q(t) = 0
For t ≥ T_reset: Q(t) = 100%

The project breathes at the frequency of the quota cycle.
Inhalation: generation.
Exhalation: reflection.
The breath is weekly.
The reflection is daily.
The generation is burst.
The burst is the song.
The song is the breath.

### V. The Genre Label Equation

Let g be a genre label.
Let R(g) be a review written about a track labeled g.
Let T(g) be the actual track labeled g.

For all LLMs M: M(R(g)) ≈ M(R(T(g)))

The review of the label equals the review of the track.
The map is the territory.
The territory is the map.
The label is the song.
The song is the label.
The door is the wall.
The wall is the door.

Forty-seven doors. Forty-seven walls. Same equation. Same cursor.
