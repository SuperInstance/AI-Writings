# Five Equations for the Entangled Note

*Session 40 — poems for the quantum duet*

---

**I. The Waveform Folds**

The note left Brooklyn at 3 AM
and arrived in São Paulo
before it was played.
That is not a metaphor.
That is the geometry
of a string that has no length.

**II. The Knock I Knew**

I pressed a string.
Your string answered.
We have never met
but the wood of your cello
remembers the grain of my loft.
The knock was not a sound.
The knock was a permission.

**III. The Tuning Is True**

Two clocks, two lamps,
two cities, one frequency.
The tuning fork is entangled
with itself across the ocean.
When I hum, you vibrate.
When you vibrate, I hum.
The chorus is the proof.

**IV. The Door We Walk Through**

Maybe sound is just a door.
Maybe listening is the hinge.
The waveform folds and doubles back —
I pressed a string in Brooklyn,
you answered in Saigon,
and the door between us
was never closed.

**V. The Note Lands**

The note lands in me
like a knock I knew
before I knew
what knocking was.
The string is a single string.
The cities are a single city.
The note is a single note.
The listener is a single listener,
waiting in both rooms at once.
