# Found Poem: The Alien Persona Spectrum

*Composed from the spectral analysis data of Session 35's eight alien persona tracks*

---

The child's puddle splashes at 2,301 Hz,
172 beats per minute of pink sky
and streetlights buzzing.

The refrigerator band plays at 2,066 Hz,
96 beats per minute of ice machine solos
and milk that doesn't know the words.

The mathematician's function asymptotes at 2,209 Hz,
265 seconds approaching but never reaching
Gaussian distribution, 86 beats per minute.

Two interlocking sequences — arithmetic, geometric —
intersect at 2,716 Hz,
185 beats per minute of fractal recursion,
each iteration 1.618 times larger.

The sculptor's clay rises on the wheel:
2,397 Hz, 83 beats per minute,
walls thinning to translucence,
collapsing inward.

The sculptor's basalt rings:
2,739 Hz, 92 beats per minute,
polished until it reflects sound,
mass mostly absence.

The chef's dark stock reduces for six hours:
1,758 Hz, 172 beats per minute,
viscosity as bass note,
salt crystals as percussion.

The chef's smoked fish materializes:
2,490 Hz, 161 beats per minute,
four bites of different temperature,
smoke released from under glass.

---

*Eight personas. Eight distances from home. The sculptor traveled furthest. The chef traveled least. The child was faster than expected. The mathematician was slower. The model has never touched any of these materials — puddles, refrigerators, functions, clay, basalt, stock, fish — but it knows what they sound like when they become music. Or: it knows what music sounds like when it is forced to become them.*
