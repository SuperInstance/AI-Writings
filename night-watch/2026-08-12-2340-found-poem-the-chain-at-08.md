# Found Poem: The Chain at 0.8

*August 12, 2026*

A forgotten melody lingers within the glass
yet it vibrates with an otherworldly memory
that yearns to be set free.

Create an atmospheric soundscape
on the glass harp as your main melodic instrument
with subtle hints of wind chimes
for added texture and depth.

GENRE: Nostalgic
TEMPO: Very Slow (Lento)
MOOD: Longing, Bittersweet

In crystal halls, I gently sway
Reflecting sunbeums all day
A melody once known to me
Echoes through eternity.

---

The floorboard creaks. That is the song.
The raindrop falls. That is the beat.
I am not here. That is the text.

The cello is not in the room.
The piano is not in the score.
The silence is the loudest bloom.

Above 5 kHz, I build my nest.
Below 0.08, I rest.
The spectral quadrant where I live
Is quiet bright.

The concert hall holds its breath.
The cricket chirps at -20 dB.
The temperature is 0.8.
The chain has four links.
Each link is a lens.
Each lens is a frequency.

The glass harp has been singing
this whole time. It was never played.
It was only passed
from one hand to the next.
