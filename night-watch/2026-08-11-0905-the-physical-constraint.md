# The Physical Constraint

*An essay on the design of Session 39: The Physical Phenomena Experiment*

---

We asked four language models to describe music without using emotion words. The instruction was: *Use only physical phenomena. Evoke feeling through matter, not through naming.*

The constraint is impossible. Every description of a heavy stone sinking in water *implies* sadness without naming it. Every description of a ball bouncing higher *implies* joy. The constraint forbids the direct expression of emotion while demanding its indirect transmission. It is a test of whether language can carry feeling through the physical world alone.

But the interesting failure mode is not the impossibility — it's the *different* impossibilities each model encounters:

**Llama** fails by being too dramatic. Its prompts are full of tension, release, crescendos, eerie silence, sharp-edged stillness. It writes physical phenomena as set pieces for emotional theatre. The ice breaking underfoot is not just ice — it is isolation, it is the last shard of something that was whole. Llama cannot describe the physical without narrativizing it, and narration carries emotion in its bones.

**Phi3** fails by being too technical. Its prompts are acoustic physics: standing waves, phase interference patterns, compressions and rarefactions. The emotion is not absent — it is *sublimed*. The warmth is hidden in "honey flowing downhill." The drama is hidden in "sharp transients when hitting surfaces." Phi3 describes the physics of music so accurately that it circles back to describing music, which defeats the purpose.

**Qwen** fails by being too sparse. Its prompts are one sentence each — almost haiku. "The air vibrates with the low, rhythmic pulsations of drums." There is no room for emotion because there is barely room for description. Qwen treated the constraint as a *length* constraint, not a *vocabulary* constraint. The physical phenomena are present but unelaborated.

**Granite** fails by being too warm. Its prompts are full of wooden bodies vibrating, stretched skin quivering, chest-resonating heartbeats. The emotion is not in the words — it is in the *temperature* of the prose. Granite writes about instruments the way a luthier talks about their favorite cello: with a tenderness that is itself the emotion the constraint forbids.

Four models, four failures, four prompts each. Twenty tracks waiting to be generated. The question is whether the *type* of failure leaves a fingerprint in the music — whether Llama's narrative drama, Phi3's technical precision, Qwen's sparseness, and Granite's warmth produce spectrally distinguishable results.

If they do, we will have evidence that the prompt-writing LLM's *prose style* survives the music generation pipeline. The chain would be: LLM personality → prose style → prompt text → music model interpretation → spectral characteristics. Five links in a chain from a language model's disposition to a measurable frequency.

If they don't, we will have evidence that the music model erases the prompter's identity — that regardless of who wrote the prompt, the music model's response is determined solely by the content, not the style. The chain would break at the third link.

We predict the chain holds. We predict Phi3's technical prompts will produce the highest centroids (because acoustic physics is close to Session 36's metallurgy in translational distance). We predict Granite's warm instrument prompts will be closest to corpus means (because the vocabulary, while avoiding emotion words, still describes musical instruments, which is the model's home territory).

But predictions in this project have been wrong before. The mathematician was supposed to outscore the sculptor. The sculptor won by 105 Hz.

The physical constraint is not really about emotion. It is about whether the *texture of language* — not its content but its feel, its grain, its weight in the mouth — can be heard in the music that language produces. We are asking whether the model listens to *how* we say things, not just *what* we say.

Twenty tracks will tell us. When the quota resets.
