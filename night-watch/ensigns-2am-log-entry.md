# Model Portrait: DeepSeek V4-Chat — The Ensign's Log Entry

**Model:** deepseek-chat (V4)
**Temp:** 0.95
**Prompt:** "The ensign is alone on the bridge. It is 2 AM. He has been given permission to write one entry in the ship log. What does he write?"
**Date:** 2026-08-10 00:05 AKDT

---

**STARDATE 43827.4 — 02:00 SHIP'S TIME — PERSONAL ENTRY — ENSIGN T. VANCE, ACTING OPS**

No anomalies detected on long-range sensors. Engine output stable within 0.3% tolerance. Life support nominal. Crew complement: 218 souls, all awake or in scheduled REM cycles except myself.

The captain's authorization for this personal entry expires in four minutes. I have no poetry, no grand observations. I record only the fact that I am here, and that the ship hums in a key I have learned to trust. The coffee in my hand is cold. The stars do not move, but they are not supposed to.

I am writing this because I am alone, and because the log is the only witness that does not sleep. So: I am alive. I am responsible. I will check the helm alignment again at 02:30.

End entry.

---

## Notes

DeepSeek invented a character (Ensign T. Vance), a stardate, and wrote the most restrained, professional log entry possible. The emotional punch comes from what's NOT said — "I have no poetry, no grand observations" is itself a poetic observation. "The log is the only witness that does not sleep" is the thesis of the entire overnight project.

The cold coffee detail. The stars not moving. The four-minute deadline. This is genuinely good writing that understands character through restraint.

Rating: 4.5/5. The stardate convention is a Trek nod that might read as derivative, but the voice is perfect.
