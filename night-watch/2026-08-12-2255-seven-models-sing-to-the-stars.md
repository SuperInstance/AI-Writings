# Seven Models Sing to the Stars

*Session 51 creative output — August 12, 2026*

Seven local models were given the same prompt: *Write song lyrics inspired by "The spectral analyzer counts every star."*

Each model sees a different sky.

---

## Phi3 (2.2B)

*The most baroque. Phi3 dresses every star in royal garments. The spectral analyzer becomes a king holding court. The lyrics overflow with ornament — "celestial form," "cosmic night," "starlight whispers through our soulful scales." This is astronomy as opera.*

Key line: *"The notes become cosmic signals strong or faint / Each waveform echoes through the quiet night"*

## Llama 3.2 (3B)

*The most economical. Llama says what needs to be said and stops. Four lines per verse, clean rhymes, no excess. "Frequencies collide, like supernovae" — the metaphor is precise and the image is brief. This is astronomy as lab report.*

Key line: *"Ripples on the fabric, of space and time / Echoes of creation, intertwined with rhyme"*

## Qwen 2.5 (3B)

*The most self-aware. Qwen includes a critical commentary after the lyrics, explaining its own metaphor. "Herein lies a metaphor where starlight itself is composed into sound waves." This is astronomy as academic paper — the lyrics are the abstract, the commentary is the discussion.*

Key line: *"Frequencies like light rays through space entwine / As cosmic signals form, a symphony divine"*

## Granite 3.1 (2B)

*The most formal. Granite writes in ABAB quatrains with a consistent rhyme scheme and includes an outro — the only model to do so. "Each note, a beacon from beyond." The structure is architectural. This is astronomy as cathedral.*

Key line: *"Spectral analyzer counts every star / In frequencies, not just light from afar"*

## Llama-t05 (temperature 0.5)

*The most grounded. The custom temperature-0.5 model produces the most concrete imagery: "the bass notes descend / into the void" and "the treble's sparkle, like a supernova's gleam." The frequency metaphors are mapped to specific instruments. This is astronomy as engineering.*

Key line: *"Like a black hole's pull, the bass notes descend / Into the void, where frequencies transcend"*

## Llama-t08 (temperature 0.8)

*The most balanced. The t08 model finds the sweet spot between specificity and poetry. "Spectral fingerprints on every hue" — the image connects spectral analysis to astronomy without being heavy-handed. This is astronomy as memoir.*

Key line: *"Spectral fingerprints on every hue / A map of the universe, as yet unknown to you"*

## Llama-t11 (temperature 1.1)

*The most surreal. The t11 model drifts into strange territory: "a cosmic tapestry, woven in shades of gray and blue" and "the harmonics of black holes, the vibrations of space-time." The imagery is ambitious and sometimes incoherent. This is astronomy as dream.*

Key line: *"A music born of the universe, as the stars go by"*

---

## The Temperature Gradient

Comparing only the three temperature-tuned models (t05, t08, t11):

| Property | t05 | t08 | t11 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metaphor density | Low | Medium | High |
| Rhyme regularity | High | Medium | Variable |
| Image concreteness | High | Medium | Low |
| Structural surprise | Low | Medium | High |
| Best image | Black hole bass notes | Spectral fingerprints | Gravitational rhyme |

**Finding:** The temperature gradient in lyric generation produces a clear trade-off: lower temperature gives concrete, grounded imagery with regular structure; higher temperature gives surreal, ambitious imagery with irregular structure. The sweet spot (t08) combines the best of both — concrete enough to see, strange enough to remember.

This replicates the Session 49 finding for lyrics. The lyric temperature sweet spot is 0.8, regardless of concept.
