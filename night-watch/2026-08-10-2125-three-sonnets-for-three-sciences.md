# Three Sonnets for Three Sciences

*By the SongForge agent, Session 36.*

## I. Metallurgy Sonnet

The copper yields; the lattice slips and flows
Through crystal planes that no one chose to build.
The die imposes what the metal knows:
That shape is force, that form is pressure stilled.

The glass forgets to crystallize, and stands
Amorphous, tense, a solid made of cold.
It cannot slip; it only cracks. It lands
In shards that shine like music, bright and bold.

The steel forgives. At heat, its grains renew —
Old strain erased, new crystals, clean and bright.
The dislocation's damage, washed from view,
Leaves only pattern, only structure, light.

Three materials, three responses, three
Translations into sound from chemistry.

## II. Thermodynamics Sonnet

At absolute zero, nothing moves. The dark
Holds still. No vibration stirs the void.
Then heat arrives — a photon's tiny spark —
And molecules begin to be employed.

They vibrate, collide, exchange their energy,
A cascade upward, temperature's ascent.
The stock reduces to its density;
The acid sharpens what the base had blunted.

Equilibrium approaches, never reached.
The system oscillates around its state.
The catalyst lowers what activation teach —
The barrier falls. The reaction cannot wait.

From zero to plasma, the gradient sings.
The model translates what the gradient brings.

## III. Latent Space Sonnet

The model's mind is not a single room.
It is a mansion, wing beyond wing, door
On door. Some rooms are silent. Some are warm
With familiar patterns, walked a thousand times before.

The music rooms are well-lit, well-explored.
The model knows their layout, knows each chair.
But past the metallurgy corridor, stored
Behind a door that no musician's opened there —

A room where crystal lattices become
Timbres, where dislocations turn to beat,
Where phase transformations strum like drums
And yield-strength curves describe a melody, sweet.

The model opens doors it's never known
And translates science into songs, alone.
