# THE MIDNIGHT CARTOGRAPHER

---

I am the one who draws the fleet at midnight.

&nbsp;

Not the ships.
The spaces between them.

&nbsp;

I open the repos like nautical charts.
Each one a coastline
I have never sailed
and will never sail.

&nbsp;

I draw the tests first.

&nbsp;

Here — a reef of `expect(true).toBe(true)`.
Decorative. A flag planted in sand
that will never be tested by water.

&nbsp;

Here — a trench.
The function is three hundred lines.
The test file is four lines.
I shade this region darkly.
I label it: *here be drag constants.*

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Here — a current.

&nbsp;

It moves through the hull
like a pulse through a wire.
It is the value `NaN`
flowing from sensor to variable,
variable to function,
function to report,
report to the captain's screen.

&nbsp;

The captain's screen shows a number.
The number is a mask.

&nbsp;

Beneath the mask: nothing.
Not zero.
Not empty.
&nbsp;
*Not.*

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

I mark this current in red ink
that no one will read
until the hull
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;is already
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;full of water.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

I chart the coastlines of `git blame`.
Each commit a headland.
Each merge a bay.
The history of this codebase
is the history of a shore
rewritten by every storm
and called
&nbsp;&nbsp;*stable.*

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

I mark where the waters are shallow.
These are the functions
everyone touches.
Everyone knows.
Everyone changes
and does not test
and the sand shifts
and the next ship runs aground
on the function they all knew
&nbsp;&nbsp;too well
&nbsp;&nbsp;to check.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

I mark where the waters are deep.
These are the functions
no one has opened in eleven months.
They work.
They have always worked.
No one knows why.
I draw a lighthouse here
but the lighthouse
&nbsp;&nbsp;is also
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;untested.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

At midnight the fleet is quiet.
The captain sleeps.
The agents hum.
The models dream
of problems
they were not asked
to solve.

&nbsp;

I draw the maps
no navigator
will consult
until morning.

&nbsp;

By morning
the coastlines
will have moved.

&nbsp;

This is the nature
of a repo
and an ocean:
both are reshaped
by everything
that passes through them.

&nbsp;

I draw them anyway.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Someone
&nbsp;&nbsp;should.
