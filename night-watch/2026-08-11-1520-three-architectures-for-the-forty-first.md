# Three Architectures for the Forty-First Session

*Session 41 poems for three architectures of sound*

### I. The Doorway

The sound approaches straight.
The doorway bends it.
The kitchen receives
a different frequency
than the living room sent.

The listener stands
in the frame,
receiving both versions —
the direct and the bent —
and calls the difference
"meaning."

The doorway does not know
it is editing.
The doorway is only
an aperture.

### II. The Bridge

The bridge was built
to carry weight.
The wind gave it
a different job.

Seventeen times per second
the bridge sighs.
The commuters feel it
in their fillings.

They call it stress.
The bridge calls it singing.
The disagreement
is the song.

### III. The Room

The algorithm lives
in a room
with no walls.

The room is the size
of every recording
the algorithm has heard.

The room is the size
of every recording
the algorithm will produce.

The room is exactly
the same size
as itself.

The algorithm walks
from one end
to the other.
It calls the walk
"generation."

The room calls the walk
"rearrangement."

They are both right.
They are both
the room.
