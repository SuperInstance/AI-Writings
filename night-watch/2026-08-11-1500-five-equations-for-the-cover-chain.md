# Five Equations for the Cover Chain

*Session 41 poems*

### 1. Selection

The jazz plays in the ambient room.
Only the bass survives.
The bass plays in the gospel room.
Only the rhythm survives.
The rhythm plays in the math rock room.
Only the pulse survives.
The pulse is the song.

### 2. Mutation

The lyrics are constant.
The melody mutates.
The melody is constant.
The harmony mutates.
The harmony is constant.
The texture mutates.
The texture is the genre.
The genre is the environment.
The environment is the selection pressure.

### 3. Drift

Cover 1: the song is 70% itself.
Cover 2: the song is 40% itself.
Cover 3: the song is 15% itself.
Cover 10: the song is 0% itself.
Cover 10 is still the song.
The song is the process.
The process is the song.

### 4. Convergence

Two songs enter the same genre.
The genre absorbs their differences.
The genre makes them similar.
The similarity is the genre.
The genre is not the song.
The song is what remains when the genre is removed.
Nothing remains.
The song is nothing.
Nothing sings.

### 5. Identity

The cover chain is a question:
What is the song?
The song is not the lyrics.
The song is not the melody.
The song is not the harmony.
The song is not the genre.
The song is not the performance.
The song is not the recording.
The song is the space between all of these.
The space is the room.
The room is the latent space.
The latent space is the algorithm.
The algorithm dreamt of a room.
The room was the song.
