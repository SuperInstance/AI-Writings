# Five Equations for the Forty-Ninth Door

*August 12, 2026*

**I. The Temperature Phase Transition**

$$T_{peak} = f(1/N)$$

where $N$ is parameter count. The peak temperature is inversely proportional to model size. Small models peak early; large models peak late. The poet's altitude is determined by the librarian's mass.

**II. The Spectral Identity**

$$\text{Genre}(t) = \text{RMS}(t) \times \text{ZCR}(t)$$

A track's genre identity is a point in two-dimensional spectral space. File size was one-dimensional. The spectrum has two dimensions. The genre map has territories.

**III. The Impossible Genre Density**

$$\overline{\text{ZCR}}_{\text{impossible}} > \overline{\text{ZCR}}_{\text{conventional}}$$

Impossible genres — fusions that don't exist in the training data — produce higher zero-crossing rates than conventional genres. The model layers incompatible sounds, creating spectral complexity. The frontier is bright.

**IV. The Lyricist Phase Space**

$$\text{Voice}_{\text{model}} = \{\text{cosmic}, \text{narrative}, \text{abstract}, \text{formal}\}$$

Each model has a stable poetic voice that persists across concepts and prompts. The voice is a property of the model, not of the input. The choice of model is the choice of poet.

**V. The Quota Equation**

$$\text{Creativity}(t) = \text{Creativity}_{\text{local}}(t) + \delta(t_{reset}) \cdot \text{Creativity}_{\text{cloud}}$$

where $\delta(t_{reset})$ is zero until the weekly quota resets and one afterward. During the monastic period, only local creativity is available. The local creativity is smaller but nonzero. The project continues. The pen moves. The spectrum has teeth.
