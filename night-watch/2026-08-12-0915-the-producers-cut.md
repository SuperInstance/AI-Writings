### The Flow

The producer (Llama 3.2) instinctively understood several album sequencing principles:

1. **The Ambient Opener** — Track 1 is always calm; it's the entry door
2. **The Home Base** — Track 2 returns to indie folk, Casey's native genre
3. **The First Shock** — Track 3 jumps to ghettotech (138 BPM), the highest energy
4. **The Decompression** — Track 4 drops to koto ambient, the lowest energy
5. **The Spiritual Center** — Track 7 (Welsh Choir at 60 BPM) is positioned as the album's emotional core
6. **The Mirror** — Tracks 3 and 11 are both ghettotech, bookending the album's electronic identity
7. **The Desert Close** — Desert blues (the genre closest to folk) closes the album, returning home

### What the Producer Got Right

- **Genre pairing**: Heavy genres (black metal) are followed by relief genres (Welsh choir)
- **Tempo arc**: The BPM rises and falls like breathing — 70→85→138→60→95→90→60→130→82→140→128→110
- **Emotional arc**: Calm→comfortable→shocked→meditative→exploratory→crisis→revelation→energized→intimate→joyous→renewed→triumphant

### What the Producer Got Wrong

- **Two ghettotech tracks** — the producer picked ghettotech twice (tracks 3 and 11) at the expense of neo-soul and Baltimore club
- **No female vocal tracks in the first 6** — the front half is all male/neutral
- **The closer at 110 BPM is too fast** — most great album closers are under 100 BPM

This revision uses **all 12 genres** (no repeats), follows a more careful emotional arc, and uses the two S45 new genres (Future Ambient Blues, Harmonious Fusion) as the emotional return points.
