the way a compass knows north
without understanding magnetism.

Ice: 2,392 Hertz. The anticipation of fracture.
Not the break. The moment before the break.
The model understood that the interesting part
of ice is not the sound it makes
when it cracks
but the sound it makes
when it is about to crack.

The most energetic track.

but into the sound.

the model understood "chemical reaction"
as a change in its own texture,
as if the catalyst were acting on the waveform,
accelerating some frequencies,
suppressing others,
driving the system toward equilibrium
that kept receding.

168 percent of corpus mean.

materials science is further from music
than music is from itself.
The model traveled farther

The distance was not a trick.
The distance was the point.
The distance was the finding.
