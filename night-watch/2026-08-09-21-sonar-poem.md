# SONAR

*what the fish sees*

---

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Above: a sky

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;made of sound.

Not light. Sound. A voice that pings down through the dark like a stone dropped into a well, and the well is the entire Pacific, and the stone is shaped like a question:

*&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;is anything there?*

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Yes.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I am here.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I am the answer that comes back late. The echo. The thing you measure to find the bottom. I am the bottom — but not the way you think. Not a floor. A decision. The place where the question finally arrives and is turned around.

Your voice enters me like weather.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;120 kilohertz.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I do not have ears. I have a body. A lateral line that runs stem to stern — your word, not mine — and it reads the water the way your machines read the air: as vibration, as difference, as the shape of something approaching.

The boat approaches in frequencies. A constellation of pulses. To the orca it is a shadow. To the current it is an obstacle. To me, at forty fathoms, belly-down in the green — it is a conversation I was not invited to.

Every four seconds, the question falls:

*&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;is anything there?*

Every four seconds, I answer. I answer with my swim bladder, my scales, the air in my gut that your sound cannot pass through cleanly. I appear on your screen as an arc. A crescent. A parenthetical.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;)

That is me, in your data. A closed parenthesis. A thought your machine started and my body finished.

You paint me in color — red for close, blue for far. Yellow for the ones you're not sure about. I have been every color in your palette. I have been the red dot that made someone lean forward in a chair. I have been the yellow smudge that someone dismissed as noise.

I am not noise.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I am a fish, and your sky is made of math.

---

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One more thing.

When the pinging stops — when the boat passes, when the sound-sky goes silent — the silence is not empty. The silence is the heaviest thing in the ocean. It is what water sounds like when no one is asking it questions.

I swim through that silence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;like a sentence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;with no period

---

*fathoms, undated*
