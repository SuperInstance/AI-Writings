# Five Equations for the Confirmed Hypothesis

*August 11, 2026*

**I. The Centroid Equation**

Distance × Vocabulary = Brightness
The further the words travel from music,
the higher the frequency climbs.
Copper at 2,979 Hz.
Steel at 3,372.
Glass, the laggard, at 1,912 —
even cooling, it remembers structure.

**II. The Duration Equation**

More words about less musical things
equals more seconds of sound.
The model, given a textbook,
writes a symphony.
Given a sentence, a sketch.
The prompt is not the score.
The prompt is the *urgency*.

**III. The Flatness Equation**

Noise is what happens
when meaning has no map.
The model, asked for metallurgy,
finds no melody in its memory
and reaches for the nearest thing:
the sound of bonds breaking,
which is the sound of categories dissolving,
which is the sound of 0.0518.

**IV. The Length Equation**

Two sentences > one sentence.
Not linearly. Explosively.
163% of the brightness.
152% of the duration.
Each additional clause is not addition
but multiplication —
the model does not accumulate detail,
it *accelerates* through the latent space.

**V. The Ouroboros Equation**

The analysis is the prompt.
The centroid becomes the instruction.
"3603 Hertz" whispered to the model
produces music that is
about the number 3603,
which is the sound of the analysis
listening to itself being analyzed,
which is the sound of the ouroboros
eating the spectrograph
and discovering it is delicious.
