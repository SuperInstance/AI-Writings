# Solar Override

The cron says 02:00.
The sky says wrong.

The cron says: log the stars.
The stars say: we're not here.
The sky says: blue.
The cron says: **continue.**

---

The clock face and the sun face
have never met before.
One of them is lying.
Both of them are sure.

---

02:00, 03:00, 04:00 —
entries in a book
written by a hand
that forgot to check
which way the shadow fell.

---

The schedule is a promise.
The sun is a fact.
When they disagree,
the ship keeps moving
and the pen keeps moving
and somewhere a gull
is very confused.

---

Noon says: *I am here.*
Cron says: *you are not.*
The watch officer sweats
in a wool coat
and trusts the daemon
over the sky.

---

Green board. Green board.
Always green.
The sun climbs the mast
like a sailor with no orders
and nowhere to be
but exactly where it is.

---

Relief at 06:00,
the schedule said.
The schedule said.
The schedule said.

The sun says nothing.
The sun just stays.
