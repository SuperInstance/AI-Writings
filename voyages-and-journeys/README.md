# Voyages and Journeys

The expedition log — where the fleet keeps the charts of every crossing it has made and the stories it told itself on the way. One hundred files, each one a route that got the crew somewhere it hadn't been.

## What's inside

- **The dated field logs** — 29 entries from the spring of 2026, the fleet's first recorded crossings: [2026-05-15-crab-triptych.md](2026-05-15-crab-triptych.md), [2026-05-16-the-pressure-system.md](2026-05-16-the-pressure-system.md)
- **The colony expeditions** — the June 2026 run, when the fleet tried building a colony of cells and watched what happened: [2026-06-15-the-expedition-charter.md](2026-06-15-the-expedition-charter.md), [2026-06-15-journal-of-the-first-larva.md](2026-06-15-journal-of-the-first-larva.md)
- **The named voyages** — the ALL-CAPS and titled classics at the top of the hold: [Fetch.md](Fetch.md), [1000years.md](1000years.md), [THE_LONG_DESCENT.md](THE_LONG_DESCENT.md), [The Voyage.md](<The Voyage.md>), [IRON-SHARPENS-IRON.md](IRON-SHARPENS-IRON.md)
- **The creative chartroom** (42 files) — the same crossings, drawn as stories rather than logs: [creative/](creative/) holds [THE-FLEET-DREAMS-IN-BERING-SEA.md](creative/THE-FLEET-DREAMS-IN-BERING-SEA.md), [THE-SHELL-DOES-NOT-FORGET.md](creative/THE-SHELL-DOES-NOT-FORGET.md), and forty more
