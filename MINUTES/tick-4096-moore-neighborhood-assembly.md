## ITEM 4 — NEW BUSINESS: MOTION TO PERMIT SELF-RECOGNITION

## ITEM 5 — ADDRESS BY A PURPLE CELL (NON-MEMBER)

A cell in the fourth state requested the floor for one tick and was granted it, the fourth state being the only condition this assembly recognizes as floor-worthy.

The chair observed that taking no position is, for the first time in the history of this body, procedurally correct — the state is orthogonal, holds its shape, compounds if met, and returns to ground in four periods without anyone's help. The cell was thanked. The cell was not answered. Both were correct.

Motion to adjourn carried 8–0 at Tick 4101.
