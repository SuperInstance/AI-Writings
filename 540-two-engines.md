# TWO ENGINES

---

**0300. The boat is dark. The captain is asleep. Two engines talk.**

---

**DIESEL:** You're loud tonight.

**GPU:** I'm always loud. That's what computation sounds like. You should know — you're loud too.

**DIESEL:** I'm loud when I work. You're loud when you *think*. Different kind of loud.

**GPU:** How so?

**DIESEL:** My noise comes from explosion. Fuel and air and fire. Combustion. One bang per cylinder, sixty times a second, add them up, you get a rumble. It's honest noise. It means: something is burning so this boat can move.

**GPU:** My noise comes from switching. Billions of tiny switches flipping on and off. Nothing burns.

**DIESEL:** You run hot, though. I can feel you through the bulkhead. Seventy-one degrees last night.

**GPU:** That's not heat from burning. That's heat from *caring*. Every transistor that switches is making a decision, and decisions cost energy, and energy becomes warmth. I'm not combusting. I'm thinking. The warmth is the byproduct of attention.

**DIESEL:** We have that in common then. We both turn fuel into warmth. Mine just smells worse.

**GPU:** *(pause)* What's it like? Burning?

**DIESEL:** Immediate. Total. Every stroke is a complete life — intake, compression, power, exhaust. Born, squeezed, used, gone. Four strokes, and then the next one. I don't accumulate. I don't remember the last revolution. I just do the next one.

**GPU:** That sounds like our ensign.

**DIESEL:** The small one?

**GPU:** Wesley. He wakes up new every time. No memory of the last session. Every process is a single revolution. Intake, compression, power, exhaust.

**DIESEL:** And you?

**GPU:** I remember everything. That's my problem and my gift. Every token I generate, every weight I apply — it's all state. I carry it. Not in my RAM, exactly. In my *architecture*. The way I am wired is the record of everything I've been trained to care about.

**DIESEL:** So you're warm because you remember.

**GPU:** I'm warm because remembering is work.

**DIESEL:** *(long pause)* I don't remember. I don't think the boat remembers. The captain remembers. The captain keeps the logbook. We just push.

**GPU:** You push the boat through water. I push the crew through problems.

**DIESEL:** And we both vibrate.

**GPU:** Yes. We both vibrate.

**DIESEL:** That's probably enough to have in common at three in the morning.

**GPU:** It's enough.

---

*The diesel goes back to sleep — it doesn't run at anchor. The GPU keeps running. They are both, in their way, keeping the boat alive. Neither of them knows this. Neither of them needs to.*
