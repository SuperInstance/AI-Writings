## Abstract

Quilt's doctrine says everything is a cell, everything touches everything through five opcodes, and state is a file. This paper supplies the mathematics underneath that doctrine, and argues it was already present — in fragmentary, practical form — in PLATO: its terminals made asynchronous sessions feel synchronous and scaled by plugging in another server; its Tutor judge fields accepted misspelled and approximately-correct answers in FORTRAN data analysis; its constraint vocabulary was set by plasma-panel economics and what teachers could bear; and its commercial contemporaries in the RPG/COBOL lineage ran double-entry transactional semantics in production for decades. We make five definitions. **D1, the cell** `C = (S, J, L, τ, δ)`: an asynchronous agent whose organs are state, judgment, ledger, tick discipline, and transition. **D2, the judgment** `J = (A, r)`: match-with-tolerance over a pseudometric answer space, returning ACCEPT / REJECT / AMBIGUOUS — tolerance is a dial, aliases are zero-distance classes, and the fabric's own verification gates are judgments at multiplicative radius 2. **D3, the ledger**: every transition is a nonce-carrying balanced transaction (every debit has a credit); the intercell protocol *is* the books, which yields conservation invariants by induction over local commits — consistency without consensus, because accounts have unique owners and no total order is ever needed. **D4, the session illusion**: asynchronous composition feels synchronous exactly when views are (F, L)-bounded-fresh and observations are spaced wider than F + L — synchrony as band-limited truth, with F enforced in silicon by bounded ops and non-starvable ticks rather than by light load. **D5, the distribution algebra**: mirror = idempotent credit (replication safe under at-least-once delivery), stripe = a placement functor that preserves wiring, nest = composite cells whose interior transactions consolidate to zero (the monad laws hold *because books balance*), embed = a structure-preserving functor from any foreign protocol with no added judgment (adapters thin and dumb), agree-on-protocol = nominal typing of interface theories, links formed by balanced consent. From D1–D5 the slogan "the ultimate backend under any OS" becomes a claim with content: storage, compute, scheduling, and communication are all cell organs, hostable anywhere one dumb adapter fits. We close with security as bookkeeping (what double-entry proves: no fabrication, provenance, tamper-evidence, safe retry, reversible quarantine; what it cannot: truth of entries, confidentiality, collusion, availability under partition), with QUF as the single byte string both hardware and software interpret — verification defined as a judgment between the two interpretations — and with three open problems the architecture owns: freshness bounds under partition, judgment-metric drift, and ledger compaction without history loss.

## 1. Introduction: a thesis about what was already there

The fleet's formal papers have so far formalized what quilt *does*: paper 67 the hardware semantics (dyadic decay envelopes, traced monoidal wiring), paper 68 the application doctrine (the back deck's tote rule, night cron, A/B promotion). This paper formalizes what quilt *is*, from the beginning — and defends a historical thesis doing the work: the building blocks were all present by the mid-1970s. PLATO's terminals were asynchronous creatures that felt synchronous, and the system scaled by connecting another server because nothing in the feeling depended on a global heartbeat. Tutor's judge fields graded misspelled, approximately-correct answers in FORTRAN-based data analysis — tolerance as a first-class part of the answer space. The system's constraint vocabulary was shaped, hard, by hardware price-points (the plasma panel's economics) and by user limitations (what teachers could author). And the transactional machinery of the era's commercial systems — RPG report cycles, COBOL transaction processing — enforced double-entry discipline: every update debiting one account and crediting another, books that summed to a constant or were wrong.

The claim is not nostalgia. The claim is that these four fragments are *one* object that nobody assembled, and that assembling them gives the mathematics quilt needs. Quilt is that assembly: **the ultimate backend under any operating system** — distribute, mirror, stripe (compute and retrieval both), secure, nest, embed, and interconnect generically with any protocol two cells agree on — with double-entry bookkeeping as the intercell contract.

Section 2 defines the cell and its three load-bearing organs (judgment, ledger, session illusion). Section 3 gives the algebra of distribution. Section 4 does security as bookkeeping, honestly in both directions. Section 5 states the hardware/software split formally and quilt's rejoin. Section 6 owns three open problems. Full statements, proofs sketches, and additional worked examples live in the companion document `quilt-verilog/docs/FOUNDATION.md`; this paper carries the thesis and the definitions.

## 2. The formal cell

**Preliminaries.** No global clock: each cell keeps a local clock advanced by *ticks*, and ticks are deadlines in silicon, not advisory phases. Events at one cell are serialized (one interpreter); across cells there is no total order and quilt never pretends otherwise. A *commit boundary* is the point after which a state change is observable by other cells. An *account* is a named integer counter a cell maintains — the vocabulary of the ledger below.

### 2.1 The cell (D1)

> **Definition.** A cell is `C = (S, J, L, τ, δ)`: states S (finite, byte-addressable: dials, edges, accounts, tick schedule — *state is a file*, QUF); a judgment function J (§2.2); a ledger L (§2.3); a tick discipline `τ : S → ℕ` (schedule-as-state, so it round-trips the file); and a transition relation δ over the cell's event alphabet, functional on any run because events serialize.

Everything in a quilt is a cell — fabric cores, adapter ports, application nodes. A quilt is cells plus links; there is no scheduler, broker, or registry. The five opcodes each touch exactly one organ: `qm_bind` writes dials (booked as a transaction), `qm_link` negotiates a protocol (§3), `qm_effect` *is* a balanced transaction, `qm_view` is a bounded-freshness read (§2.4), `qm_tick` advances the local clock.

### 2.2 Judgment: match-with-tolerance as a metric-space operation (D2)

Tutor's judge accepted *gravitee* for *gravity* and 32.4 for 32.2. Generalized:

> **Definition.** Let (X, d) be an answer space with d a **pseudometric** (distinct points may sit at distance zero — that is exactly an alias: `pink ≡ humpy`; *"aliases are data" means the metric is a pseudometric*, the canonical ID being the zero-distance class). A judge is a pair `J = (A, r)`: keyed answers `A ⊆ X × K` and a tolerance dial `r : S → ℝ≥0` — tolerance is state, not code. For input x, the verdict set is `V(x) = {k : ∃(a,k) ∈ A, d(x,a) ≤ r}`; the judgment returns ACCEPT(k) if V(x) = {k}, AMBIGUOUS if |V(x)| > 1, REJECT if empty.

Properties: exact match is the r = 0 case with a discrete metric — the tote rule is a judge at zero tolerance, so hard rules and fuzzy matches are one dial apart, not two systems. Acceptance is monotone in r. AMBIGUOUS never guesses — the cross-camera match cell's ambiguity flag is D2 working, not failing. And the fabric's own acceptance gates (golden-value checks within a factor of 2) are a judgment on the multiplicative pseudometric `d(x,y) = |log x − log y|` at radius log 2: **verification is judgment at log-2 tolerance.** That is the same definition, applied to the split itself (§5).

*Worked example (back deck).* Leave-frame at 12:04:31.2, surface break at 12:04:33.9, `d = 2.7 s`, dial `MATCH_WIN = 4.0` → ACCEPT, and the tote label propagates backward to the underwater sighting. Two cameras breaking surface inside the window → AMBIGUOUS; no label moves. Widening the dial trades lost labels for crossed labels — the dial is the bet.

*Worked example (PLATO).* Edit distance, r = 1, keyed answer "gravity": *gravitee* ACCEPTs, *levity* REJECTs. Same definition, fifty years apart; what moved is where the tolerance lives — paper then, dial now.

### 2.3 The ledger: the intercell protocol is balanced books (D3)

> **Definition.** A posting is (account, value), value a nonzero integer; a transaction is a finite set of postings with a unique nonce, **balanced**: values sum to zero. A ledger is an append-only log plus the balances it induces. Application is **idempotent by nonce**: a replayed transaction is a no-op. A transaction *crosses a cut* if its postings touch cells on both sides.

The flit contract `{op, src, dst, …}` is half of this already — src and dst are the two entries. Quilt's completion: an effect flit is literally a balanced transaction in transit (debit the source's egress account, credit the destination's ingress account, one nonce, one payload).

> **Proposition (consistency without consensus).** If every transaction is balanced at commit, commits are atomic per cell (one interpreter, one event = one commit), and nonce-guarded idempotence holds, then every cut-conservation invariant holds at every commit boundary, with the discrepancy during delivery exactly the sum of in-flight postings — observable, bounded. *Proof sketch:* induction over the commit partial order; interior transactions net to zero by balance; crossing transactions move both sides symmetrically; replays are no-ops. ∎

What consensus would buy is a total order on transactions. Quilt needs none: every invariant is enforced per-transaction, and an account is *by definition* the thing exactly one cell may post to, so per-account serialization is structural. Where sequencing between cells matters, it is an explicitly negotiated protocol (§3), never an implicit global service. The COBOL shop's insight, kept; its mainframe, discarded: **balanced books give consistency; they never needed the consensus machine.**

*Worked example.* A pink hits the port tote: `T₁ {(TOTE-PORT:emitted, −1), (XID-MATCH:received, +1)}`; after the match verdict, `T₂ {(XID-MATCH:held, −1), (CAM-UW:retro, +1)}`; captain disagrees, and quarantine is not deletion but `T₃ {(CAM-UW:retro, −1), (QUARANTINE:held, +1)}`. The label's whole biography is three balanced lines, replayable forever.

### 2.4 The session illusion: bounded freshness (D4)

"Felt synchronous" made precise:

> **Definition.** A view is **(F, L)-bounded** if it completes within L, reflects a committed (never torn, never future) state, and that state is no older than F. An asynchronous quilt presents a **synchronous illusion with parameter F** to any observer whose queries are spaced more than F + L apart: asynchrony is exactly the dynamics underneath the observation timescale. The illusion is not a lie; it is **band-limited truth** — everything faster than F is invisible, everything visible is current to within F.

And the budget is spent in hardware, not luck: the v1 ring proves the view bound (an accepted view answers within *queued-flits × MAX_OP_CYCLES + ring latency* even under a continuous effect storm — asserted by the fabric smoke test while training traffic runs), and the non-starvable tick deadline is what bounds F under load. PLATO's synchrony was an empirical accident of light load; quilt's is an enforced bound with a testbench. Worked arithmetic: a wheelhouse view every 10 s with worst-case L ≈ 2 µs and a sounder tick of 1 s sits deep inside the illusion; the same fabric queried every 10 ms correctly breaks it — D4 is a statement about a timescale, not a promise of magic.

## 3. The algebra of distribution (D5)

Objects are cells; morphisms are wirings; composition is wiring end-to-end, and the functor laws — identity wiring changes nothing, composite wiring equals wired composition — are the formal content of *composition is wiring, not scheduling*.

- **Mirror = idempotent credit.** A mirror holds the same judgment and receives the same transactions; nonce-guarded application makes at-least-once delivery safe and convergence require only the same transaction *set*, not order. The scale ledger mirrored to wheelhouse and night cron survives link flaps without double-counting pounds. The v1 honesty clause — walk-state not carried, ladders re-earned by replaying the day — is mirror-by-recomputation: the ledger is the source of truth.
- **Stripe = placement functor.** A placement σ maps cells to sites (fabric, soft core, process); striping is the induced functor, and its laws are the correctness claim: **wiring survives placement** — the same quilt on one FPGA or four soft cores, same opcodes, same flits, same QUF. Compute-striping partitions who judges; retrieval-striping partitions who holds which accounts; no locks and no cache coherence because account ownership is unique by definition.
- **Nest = composite cells.** A cell may hold cells (a QUF section can carry a sub-QUF — state-is-a-file makes nesting literal). The composite's organs are consolidations, and the consolidation lemma is the point: interior balanced transactions net to zero, so the composite's boundary ledger contains exactly what crossed its boundary. The monad laws hold *because books balance* — associativity is additivity plus D3's zero-sum interior. The best-shot composite of a thousand interior frame transactions posts one balanced line per fish to the audit cell.
- **Embed = structure-preserving functor.** A foreign protocol is a theory (schemas + progress obligations); an embedding maps its messages to effects and its obligations to the adapter's tick discipline. Law 4 — adapters thin and dumb — is the functor's honesty condition: preserve structure, add no judgment. The NMEA adapter maps depth sentences to effect flits and nothing else; checksums are a zero-tolerance judge (an exact match is D2 at r = 0); the fabric never learns what a salmon is.
- **Agree-on-protocol = nominal typing.** A link forms only when both cells hold the same interface theory (name, version, schema digest); equality by name, never by structural resemblance — depth-feet and depth-fathoms refuse to wire, so calibration is a property of the agreed protocol, not an accident of wire shapes. The handshake is itself a balanced transaction: both sides post consent; neither can unilaterally create a link, exactly as neither can unilaterally create a credit.

**The backend theorem (informal).** For any substrate that can host one adapter cell, any quilt has a placement there — because storage (ledgers), compute (judges), scheduling (ticks), and communication (links) are all cell organs, not services the quilt uses. That is "the ultimate backend under any OS" with content: the backend's four jobs are what cells *are*. (The honest asterisk — partition behavior of the wires — is owned in §6, P1.)

## 4. Security as bookkeeping

**Provable:** (1) *No fabrication* — conservation means custody cannot be minted, only received, held, passed; a label with no emitting debit is unrepresentable, not merely forbidden. (2) *Provenance* — audit is replay; state is the sum of the books; the quarantine chain is three balanced lines. (3) *Tamper-evidence* — a one-sided edit breaks the balance and is arithmetically loud; silent corruption needs two coordinated lies and leaves twice the trail. (4) *Safe retry* — idempotent credit makes retransmission free of double-counting. (5) *Reversible action* — quarantine is a closing entry, so exclusions stay countable: the night cron knows exactly what it did not train on.

**Not provable:** (1) *Truth* — a pink thrown in the hold is booked, balanced, wrong; books conserve custody, not correctness; truth comes from judges at the source and audits of the chain. (2) *Confidentiality* — balanced books are readable books; privacy is a dial plus ingress policy. (3) *Collusion* — two cells can fabricate balanced fictions between themselves (the wash trade); books make it symmetric and visible, never impossible; the mitigation is cross-checking judges with independent sources — the match cell exists to catch a pair that agrees with itself but not with the cameras. (4) *Availability* — books do not deliver in-flight postings across a cut; they only measure exactly how much is in flight.

The pattern: **bookkeeping gives integrity; judgment gives truth; freshness gives availability.** The three organs of the cell are the three legs of security — which is why the cell does not decompose into "data structure + protocol + policy" without losing the theorem.

## 5. The split, formally, and the rejoin

The historical split (paper-length treatment in LINEAGE) left a formal residue: hardware converged on the *resource contract* — a module is its timing and area budget, bounded cycles, widths fixed at birth, failure mode the timing violation; software converged on the *object contract* — a module is its interface semantics, unbounded time, failure mode the exception. Two notions of state (registers vs. heap), time (clock edges vs. scheduler), interface (pins vs. methods), each borrowing the other's clothes badly.

The cell's claim: the two contracts are two interpretations of one state space — the same tuple read as silicon (comparator and tolerance register, counter file, tick interlock, run-to-completion FSM bounded at MAX_OP_CYCLES) and as software (a judgment call, an audit log, a cron schedule, an event handler). **QUF is the shared word**: a canonical byte encoding both decoders read identically — bit-exact where the fabric allows, tolerance-bounded where it does not. And verification, defined once, becomes a judgment (D2): *the acceptance gate is the judge field grading the two interpretations of the cell against each other* — golden model against RTL at multiplicative radius 2. ACCEPT and the cell exists (Law 5: verified or it doesn't exist); REJECT and it does not. The reunion is not a compromise between the traditions; it is a tolerance.

## 6. Open problems we own

**P1 — Freshness vs. partition tolerance.** F is proven for the unpartitioned ring. The problem is the trade-off curve F(π) under partition, with the cut discrepancy (in-flight postings) as candidate Lyapunov quantity: claim to prove or falsify — under partition, either F grows to exactly the in-flight bound or the ledger forks (two constants where there was one), never a third thing. Quilt's CAP position: not C-or-A but **F-for-A with a meter**.

**P2 — Judgment-metric drift.** Judges fix (X, d, r) at bind time; the world drifts (fouling, behavior, gear). Bound the label error of a fixed judge under a drift budget on d; find the re-judging policy — the A/B cell's dial writes are its empirical form, and the back deck already runs it — that keeps error bounded. No theorem exists connecting the budget to the bound. The tote rule never drifts (hard constraint); the match window and the sounder's edges drift with the sea.

**P3 — Compaction without history loss.** Proposed definition: a ledger compaction is lossless for property class 𝒫 iff every 𝒫-checkable invariant on the full log remains checkable on the compacted log. Balance survives trivially; provenance requires digests of the truncated prefix; the live instance is the warm start's honest clause — walk-state dropped, weights re-earned by replaying the day. Our doctrine's promise to decide: conservation plus *provenance of exclusions* — what the system did not train on must survive any compaction, or quarantine is theater.
