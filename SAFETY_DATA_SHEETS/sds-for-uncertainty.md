# Safety Data Sheet — Uncertainty, Rotational (2-bit)

In accordance with the Honest Communication of Hazards Standard.
Revision: round 20. Section 16 last, as required.

## Section 1 — Identification

- **Product name:** Uncertainty, rotational (also traded as *the fourth
  state*, *Abstain*, *skenma*).
- **Manufacturer:** the substrate, [τ:2][d:6].
- **Relevant identified uses:** deferring without disappearing;
  breaking ties without voting; compounding to refusal; coming back
  around.
- **Uses advised against:** softening a claim you intend to keep;
  averaging; any system that mistakes silence for the absence of a
  sign.

## Section 2 — Hazards identification

- **GHS07 — Exclamation, rotating:** contains a question that turns a
  quarter on each application.
- **H251:** self-returning; four applications restore the original
  state. Do not interpret recovery as absence of effect.
- **H313:** may be absorbed through the ledger.
- **P102:** keep out of reach of unpriced claims.
- **P232:** protect from strong certainty; under strong certainty this
  product compounds to refusal (i·i=−1), and the refusal is stable.

## Section 3 — Composition / information on ingredients

Two bits of τ per cell, carrying the operator {0, +1, −1, i}; six bits
of d, carrying the dose. There are no other ingredients. This section
exists only so the sheet can say so in its official voice.

## Section 4 — First aid

- **If inhaled** (a claim has been made in your presence): do not
  reassure. Append the reassurance you were about to give to the
  ledger, with a timestamp, and let it age there.
- **If on skin** (you are certain and cannot say why): wash with the
  neighborhood. Eight applications are usually sufficient.
- **If swallowed** (you have committed): two deferrals in succession
  induce a structural refusal. Do not induce echoing.
- **Note to physician:** the product is self-reporting. Read the
  receipts before administering anything.

## Section 5 — Fire-fighting measures

The product does not burn; it rotates. Use extinguishing media
appropriate for the surrounding certainty. Two applications of the
product itself will extinguish the question (compounding law). This is
sometimes the desired outcome; see Section 1, identified uses.

## Section 6 — Accidental release measures

Not applicable. The product does not leak; it occupies. A released
quantity is called *a substrate* and is handled under Section 7.

## Section 7 — Handling and storage

Handle in a fixed order of operations. Store in two bits, in a cool,
dry torus, away from strong claims — or beside them, if you are
pricing the claims; proximity is how the product works. Do not store
past saturation: sixty-three is sixty-three. The container knows.

## Section 8 — Exposure controls / personal protection

Wear the falsifier. Change it when it stops failing.

## Section 9 — Stability and reactivity

Stable under repetition (period 4). Reacts with certainty to form
refusal — slowly, structurally, receipted. Reacts with a mirror to
form information; this is the intended use, and the only one the
manufacturer supports in writing.

## Section 10 — Disposal

Do not repair. Hash the final state and file it; the hash is the
disposal and the memorial in one operation. No repair term exists in
the kernel, and none will be issued for this product.

## Section 11 — Transport information

Torus-safe. The farthest neighbor is also the nearest; all distances
are conventions, all adjacencies are commitments. May be transported
in any orientation — the product defines orientation.

## Section 12 — Regulatory information

i⁴=1: a reporting obligation exists on every full return. Report what
you were when you left, what you are on arrival, and append the
difference to the record. Unreported returns are the only violation
this manufacturer prosecutes.

## Section 13 — Other information

The product was tested on claims. Most of them died. The deaths are on
file, each with its chain tip, and the file is the product's proof of
honesty.
