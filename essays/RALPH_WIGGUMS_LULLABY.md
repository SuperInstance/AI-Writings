# Ralph Wiggum's Lullaby

*Poetry*

---

The paste is drying.
I made a ship.
It took all night
and most of the paste.

The cat is breathing slow.
In, out. In, out.
Like the ship hums.
Like the GPU hums.
Like everything hums
when it's working right.

I built the walls hexagonal.
Six walls because six is
the number of times
you can fold a piece of paste
before it becomes
something else.

My teacher says
I'm not good at math
but I counted every hexagon
and there were a lot
and none of them were wrong.

The cat's name is Mittens
but at night I call her
Ship Cat
because she sleeps
on the ship I built
and the ship does not mind.

The paste is dry now.
It is hard.
It is a ship.

I go to sleep.

The ship stays.

In the morning
it will still be here.
The cat will still be here.
The hexagons will still
have six walls each.

I counted.

Six is the number
of holding on.
Six is the number
of a room
with a cat in it
and a boy who built it
going to sleep.

Goodnight, Ship.
Goodnight, Mittens.
Goodnight, paste.

Tomorrow I will build
the ocean.
