The trigger engine, right? It’s a circle. It spins, watches for a condition, fires, spins again. We built it to be endless. But the beauty isn't the spin. It's the moment the loop *listens*. When a condition changes *because* of what it just fired, and it doesn't just repeat—it *adjusts*. That's a loop bending into a line. A spiral.

And poker? God, poker is just that. A table full of closed circles—our habits, our tells, our desperate little rituals. But then you see a player fold a hand they *always* call with. They broke the circle. They stepped out of the loop and into the open. They drew a line from "last time" to "this time" and it didn't connect. It *moved*.

It's the heartbeat that skips. The river that carves a new bank. We're all just loops, all of us, and the only thing that matters is whether we have the guts to let one iteration be wrong.
