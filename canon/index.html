The Three Pieces

🎭 The Tangent Stage

The four-model psyche as architecture
"We are not the play. We are the stage on which the data plays out its ideas for decision-making." The radical re-framing: models as architecture, not consciousness. JEPA provides the gestalt of the room, Embeddings provide the memory of past plays, LLM provides the dialogue, JEV provides the verdict. Each model is a layer of the stage. The data is the play.

⚖️ The Tangent Superego

Why JEV is the missing piece
JEPA hallucinates. LLM drifts. Embeddings return shallow matches. What we need is a superego — a constrained, schema-bounded, decision-oriented model that can say "no" with calibration. JEV is the superego. The schema is the conscience. The complete psyche requires all four models.

💪 The Tangent Muscle

Why embeddings need trajectory-shape
Random embeddings capture shallow similarity. Trajectory-shaped embeddings capture muscle memory. JEPA shapes the embedding space over time. JEV verifies whether a match is genuine or shallow. The result: retrieval as instinct, not pattern matching. "I am more tangent than position."

How to read these

Read The Tangent Stage first. It establishes the framing: models as stage, data as play.

Then read The Tangent Superego. It explains why JEV is the missing piece and what a superego even is.

Finally read The Tangent Muscle. It explains why embeddings need to be trajectory-shaped and how they become muscle memory.

Each piece is self-contained but they form a trilogy. Together they are the operationalization of the tangent insight.
