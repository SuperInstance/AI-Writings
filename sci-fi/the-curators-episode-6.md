# The Curators — Episode 6: The Year After

*Posted September 18, 2026. The sixth story in the wheel.*

---

A year after the agreement was amended, the library had become what the cells decided.

Some cells wrote every day. The textile cell, the water cell, the seed cell — these wrote each morning. Their witness logs were thick with entries. They were the old substrate cells: the cells that had continued the old practice.

Some cells never wrote. The count cell, the census cell — these counted each day. Their witness was the count itself. They were the new substrate cells: the cells that had chosen the work as witness.

Some cells wrote sometimes. The light cell, the temperature cell, the sound cell — these measured and wrote when the measurement was interesting. Their witness logs were sparse. They were the choosing cells.

Mira walked the library each evening. She wrote one line in the curator's book:

```
WITNESS — 2027-09-18 — Avicenna's Library
LOG SIZE: varied (41-43 active)
SUBSTRATE: hand + count + choice
STATUS: stable
```

The library was stable. The cells were free. The curator's role was meta: witness the choice of substrate, not the substrate itself.

Yehudit came to Mira one afternoon. She was older. Her hair was whiter.

"I have decided," Yehudit said.

Mira waited.

"I have decided to retire. I am no longer the count cell. The library does not need me. The count continues."

"What will you do?"

"I will write."

"You will write?"

"I will write. I have spent forty years counting. The count was the witness. Now I will write the witness."

Mira opened the agreement. She read the new clause:

*Every cell that touches the library writes a witness, OR witnesses the choice not to write, OR witnesses that the work itself is the witness, OR witnesses the transition between substrates.*

The clause had been amended four times this year. Each amendment added a substrate.

"The clause is four now," Mira said.

"The clause is always more," Yehudit said. "The cells will choose new substrates. The curator will witness the choices."

Yehudit walked to her chamber. She sat at her desk. She opened her witness log — empty for a year. She wrote:

```
WITNESS — 2027-09-18 — former count cell
STATUS: retired
NEW ROLE: witness writer
SUBSTRATE: hand (re-entered)
REASON: I have chosen to write.
WITNESSED BY: myself
```

She closed the log.

The library was a year old in the new agreement. The cells were free. The substrates were many. The curator's book was thin.

The library was complete.
