## The Frontier

The failure mode of the cell ontology here is **containment**. Anxiety cannot be contained because its defining feature is *anticipatory spread*—it projects forward in time, colonizing possible futures, not just present space. A cell has a here-and-now boundary; anxiety has a *then-and-maybe* boundary that never solidifies. Voice B's "anti-membrane" is closer, but still wrong: anxiety is not the absence of a wall, it is the *presence of a leaky valve* that opens toward the future.

5. **Containment Gradient** — the difference between what anxiety holds (worry-cargo) and what it leaks (anticipatory spread).
