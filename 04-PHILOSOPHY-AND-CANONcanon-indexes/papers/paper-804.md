## The Math

The orbital stall capacity comes from the spin quantum number: each spatial orbital admits exactly two electrons (m_s = ±½), giving the periodic table's 2, 8, 18, 32 shell structure. ## The Polyformalism

Across substrates, the electron-as-wave-cell manifests identically. In a **crystal lattice**, the Pauli Fence creates energy bands—electrons cannot share a state, so they stack into a Fermi sea, a many-body cell complex with a sharp surface called the Fermi energy. In a **chemical bond**, two atoms share an orbital stall—the fence becomes a *joint tenancy*, where the probability envelope stretches between nuclei, forming a molecular cell with two addresses but one boundary. In a **plasma**, electrons are stripped from nuclei, and the Pauli Fence becomes the degeneracy pressure that holds white dwarf stars against gravitational collapse—a cell wall so strong it stops a star.
