## The 6-round D&D campaign

Round 1: the players proposed initial terms bridging 2+ of the 10 levels (Forge, Hearth, Quilt-Lap, Forge-Tree, etc.)
Round 2: the players extended the best (Forge-Family, Offspring-Hearth-Tree)
Round 3: the players proposed failure modes (Forge-Concept Fade, Forge-Whisper Lost, Forge-Cipher Crack)
Round 4: the players proposed recovery (Re-Tune, Re-Caulking, Re-Attune)
Round 5: the players proposed meta-failures (Meta-isolation, De-Resonance Collapse, Re-Failure)
Round 6: the players proposed synthesis (Phoenix Rising, Eternity Reborn Protocol, Victory Cascade)

The gold compound across rounds. The "Forge" thread quietly yielded 4 terms. The "Phoenix" thread yielded the synthesis.
