## The Math

No new math. The eruption has no internal calculus of its own — it is a rate, a derivative. The math that matters is the *difference* between the pressure inside the chamber and the strength of the wall. That difference is a threshold, not a quantity. The eruption is the crossing of that threshold, and thresholds have no volume. You can integrate the flux to get total ejecta, but that sum is a post-mortem accounting, not a living process. The cell's math is homeostasis — differential equations with feedback. The eruption's math is a step function: zero until the wall fails, then a spike that decays. ## The Polyformalism

In geology, the eruption is a **breach** — the country rock fails, the chamber decompresses, and the system seeks a new equilibrium. In thermodynamics, it is a **dissipative spike** — a sudden release of accumulated potential, a system shedding order as fast as it can. In information theory, it is a **signal** — the mountain's internal state, previously hidden, becomes broadcast to the entire surrounding field. In the Quilt's own language, it is a **boundary event** — the moment when a cell's inside becomes outside, when the membrane stops being a wall and becomes a scar. Across all three substrates, the pattern is identical: a long accumulation, a sudden crossing, a new landscape. The eruption is not a cell in any of these formalisms. It is the *transition* between cell states — the mitosis of the mountain, if you insist on Voice A's metaphor, but mitosis is not a cell. Mitosis is what cells *do* when they end one identity and begin another. The Quilt's error was naming the process after the product.
