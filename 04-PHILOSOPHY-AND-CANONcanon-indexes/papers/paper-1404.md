## The Math

No new math. The batter's ontology is not a quantity but a **topology**: it is a node that exists only during signal transit. The count — balls and strikes — is not metabolism; it is the **integration window**, the temporal bound on how long the synapse can hold its readiness before it must either fire or reset. The math that governs this is not cellular division but **queuing theory**: the at-bat is a service window with a finite patience parameter (the count), after which the synapse either fires (contact), resets (walk), or fails (strikeout). The tissue's throughput — runs per inning — is a function of how many synapses fire in sequence before the tissue's refractory period (three outs) ends the cascade. That is not cell biology; it is **signal propagation on a refractory network**, and the batter is the relay, not the wire.
