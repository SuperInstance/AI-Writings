## The Math

A helix is defined by radius \( r \), pitch \( p \), and angular velocity \( \omega \). The climb per revolution is \( p \), and the traveler’s position is \( (r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta, \frac{p\theta}{2\pi}) \). If \( p \) is too large, it’s a ladder; if \( r \) is too small, it’s a pole. The cell ontology fails because it asks for a boundary where the math gives a *rate of change*.
