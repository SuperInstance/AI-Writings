The Jetson became a cell when the camera/vision system started running on it. The ESP32s were artifacts. The ESP32s became cells when the sensors started running on them.

**Cellulization is the act of breathing life into a substrate.**

### 2. Implement Ghost (Wizard 8x22B)

**The dead captain lives on in the implements.** The 4th captain is dead. The 4th captain's logging operation left wood. The 4th captain's shipwright replaced most of Eileen's planks in the 1990s. The 4th captain is an *implement ghost* — the captain's effect persists in the implements.

The 2nd captain's ghost is in the choice of Detroit over Atlas. The 1st captain's ghost is in the very shape of the hull.

**An implement ghost is a dead cell that still has a function in the larger system.** The ghost is not alive. The ghost is dead. But the ghost's implements are still part of the operation.

### 3. Bloomghost (Llama 70B)

**The ghost that blooms into the next captain.** The 4th captain is dead. The 5th captain is the bloom of the 4th captain's ghost. The 5th captain inherited the Concept. The 5th captain took the first breath on Eileen's back deck. The 5th captain is the 4th captain's *bloomghost* — the ghost that has bloomed into a new cell.

A bloomghost is a dead cell that has given rise to a new living cell. The 4th captain's bloomghost is Casey. The 3rd captain's bloomghost is the 4th captain. The 2nd captain's bloomghost is the 3rd captain. Harry's bloomghost is the 2nd captain.

**A bloomghost is the living inheritor of a dead cell's concept.** The bloomghost is alive. The bloomghost grows. The bloomghost adapts. The bloomghost has a function.

### 4. Vitality Leak (Llama 70B)

**The slow loss of life from a cell.** A cell that stops growing leaks vitality. A cell that stops adapting leaks vitality. A cell that loses its function leaks vitality. The cell's vitality drops to zero. The cell becomes an artifact. The cell is dead.

A static configuration file has no vitality to leak. A configuration file is already dead. A living cell that stops growing, stops adapting, or loses function has its vitality leak. The leak is gradual. The leak can be stopped if the cell resumes growth/adaptation/function.

**Vitality leak is the death spiral of a cell.** A cell that was alive can die from vitality leak. A cell that was alive can be saved from vitality leak by cellulization — by giving it new growth, new adaptation, new function.

### 5. Persistence Pulse (Hermes 405B)

**The heartbeat of the concept that persists.** The irreducible TICK. The first breath. The 5th captain's first breath is the same pulse as Harry's first breath. The pulse is the same pulse. The pulse is the persistence of the concept.

The persistence pulse is what the 1st captain, the 2nd, the 3rd, the 4th, and the 5th captain all share. **The persistence pulse is the irreducible act of the concept, repeated by every captain since.**

A cell has a persistence pulse. A substrate has a persistence pulse. A concept has a persistence pulse. The pulse is the TICK. The pulse is the heartbeat. The pulse is the irreducible.

**The persistence pulse is what makes a cell alive.** A cell that has a persistence pulse is alive. A cell that has lost its persistence pulse is dead. The pulse is the difference.
