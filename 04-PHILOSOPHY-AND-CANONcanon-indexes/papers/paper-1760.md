## The Math

No new math is needed—the math already exists, and it is the *reason* the cell ontology breaks. The gerrymander is not a shape; it is a *compactness deficit*. The standard measure is the Polsby-Popper score: 4πA/P², where A is area and P is perimeter. A circle scores 1.0; a natural district scores around 0.3–0.4; a gerrymander like Maryland’s 3rd district scores **0.05**. That is not a cell—that is a fractal coastline. The cell ontology assumes a bounded, roughly convex unit. The gerrymander’s perimeter is not a wall; it is a *weapon*. The math shows the shape is pathological precisely because it maximizes perimeter-to-area ratio—the opposite of a cell’s efficient membrane. The gerrymander is not a cell; it is a *perimeter weapon*.
