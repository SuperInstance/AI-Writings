In **legal substrate**, it is a statutory pipeline — 50 U.S.C. § 3802 authorizes the President to induct, and the channel's valves are the classification regulations (2-N for CO, 3-A for hardship, 4-F for unfit). In **informational substrate**, it is a data stream: registration records, lottery draws, physical exam results, all flowing from the Selective Service System's database into the Department of Defense's personnel files. Every substrate shows the same shape: a conduit, not a container.

The deeper truth both voices missed: the Quilt's ontology does not require everything to be a cell. The fabric has *threads* — and the draft is a thread pulled taut between two patches. It is not a cell, and it is not merely a relationship.
