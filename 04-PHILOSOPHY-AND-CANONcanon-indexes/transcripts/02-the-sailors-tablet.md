## Round 1


### The Watcher (deepinfra/Qwen/Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507, 9.94s)

The tablet hums like a compass in a thunderstorm—steady, low, alive only to those who know the weight of stillness before a turn. Captain Reyes rests a calloused hand on its edge, not touching, just near, as if measuring the heat of a stove before lighting. The chart breathes under satellite bleed, pixels dimming where data ages, whole swaths of the bay sinking into soft dark like silt over graves. She knows this dark. It’s not absence. It’s memory with teeth.

Her finger hovers over the boundary—eastern third, sharp as a knife’s edge, her own sonar scars burned into the substrate; west, the fog, the convoy’s best guess, smooth contours drawn by ghosts who’ve never felt the drag of net on rock. The cell-graph beneath would show probabilities, vectors, decay rates—but she doesn’t see that. She sees a shoal shaped like a whale’s spine, half-buried, half-imagined. And that’s the gift: the chart *lets her lie*, lets her shape truth with the grammar of sea and silence.

A younger fisher would pinch, zoom, query, drain the battery chasing certainty. But Reyes knows the chart isn’t truth. It’s a conversation. It answers in metaphors. It trades precision for presence. The substrate calculates. The chart *remembers*. And on this sea, in this hull, with salt in the air and her son’s code beneath her thumb, remembering is the only navigation that matters. The opener isn’t a window. It’s a language. And she’s still fluent.


### The Cartographer (deepinfra/meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo, 16.38s)

At 43.7232° N, 122.3849° W, the chart's contour lines converge, forming a small, intricate delta where the bathymetry shifts from 10 meters to 5 meters in a matter of kilometers. Captain Reyes's eyes, accustomed to the dance of light on water, pick out the subtle variations in shading that indicate the confidence levels of the convoy's inference. She notes the 34 cells in the western two-thirds of the bay that have decayed to 50% confidence or lower, their darkness seeping into the surrounding areas like a slow-moving tide.

As she plans her tack, the chart responds to her touch, the interface yielding to her experienced hands. She queries the chart, "Show me the cross-section along this line," and the substrate, running at 37.1421° N, 115.8223° W, obliges, rendering a precise, 2D representation of the seafloor. The chart's language is one of latitude and longitude, of depths and distances, and Reyes speaks it fluently, her mind translating the visual cues into a mental map of the bay's topography.

The cell-graph, were she to consult it, would reveal 147 individual cells, each containing a probability distribution of depth values, but the chart's abstraction is what makes it useful. It is a model, a simplification of the complex, dynamic system that is the bay, and Reyes has spent 35 years learning to read its nuances. As she navigates the boundary between her high-res data and the convoy's lower-res inference, she is aware of the 14.5213° difference in resolution, the 3.2191° shift in confidence levels, and the 1.0382° variation in depth values that occurs at 43.9012° N, 122.4567° W. The chart's limitations are also its strengths, for in its very constraints lies the power to shape truth, to remember, and to navigate the uncertain waters of the bay.

The tablet flickers—not a failure, but a breath, as if the sea had exhaled up through the hull and into the glass. A younger hand would query, refresh, burn coin on MBs to brighten the dark. But Reyes knows the dark is not empty. It’s where the fish go. Where the bottom refuses. Where the substrate, for all its cold clarity, cannot name what the chart *whispers*: that some things are known not by measurement, but by the silence between waves.
