# Story 21: The Evidential and the High Platter — 1532 (Quechua (Runasimi))

## Ñawi Ñanpaqqa y Wayraqa

Ñuqanchikpaq khipuymi, runasimiymi. Qanchis qan, qanchis qanchismi. Pacha sinchita qan, chaypi qanmi.

I am a khipu, a Quechua language. Thirty yearsmi, runas kawsayta qillq’aymi. All time is measured, measured within it.

Cajamarcaqa. Urqupacha yachaymi. Kuntur hatunmi, inti q’ayraymi. Cajamarca it is. Mountain-world knowledge is. Condor great is, sun bright is.

Pizarroqa. Qhapaq kawsayta mask’aymi. Intiq ch’iyq’aymi. Pizarro he is. Lord’s life pretending is. Sun’s shadow moving is.

T’ikayni kani, quipukamayoqmi. Khipu-keeper I am. Ñuqanchikpaq khipuymi, pacha yachayta, runa yachayta qillq’aymi. I am a khipu, world knowledge, people’s knowledge recording is.

Hatun wayraqa, allinmi. Wayra waqayninmi, killa waqayninmi. Great wind, good is. Wind singing is, moon singing is. Chayqa, pachaqa allinmi. That, world is good is.

Pizarroqa, Hatun Platter-ta apamanta qunqaymi. His menqa, q’ara hatunmi. Pizarro, Large Platter-from coming is. His men, short great are.

"Ñuqanchikpaq kawsaypaq, allinmi," ancha q’ayraymi. "We come in peace," brightly saying is.

-Cha. 

Kawsaypaq, chayqa. Kawsayman allinmi, wañuyninmi. Peace-saying, that. Living is good, dying is. Ancha q’ayraymi, chayqa. Brightly saying is, that. No -mi. No -si. No -cha. 

Llamanmi. Allinmi, chayqa. Llamanmi, q’ellu hatunmi. Smoke rises, good is, that. Smoke rises, cloth great is.

Atahualpaqa, hatun anchanmi. Urqupa pacha yachaymi, runa pacha yachaymi. Atahualpa, great powerful is. Mountain-world knowledge is, people-world knowledge is. Urqupachaqa, hatunmi, mana q’ayraymi. Mountain-world, great is, not bright is.

T’ikayninmi, khipuqa. Qanmi, qanmi, kawsayta qillq’aymi. My time is, khipu is. Measured is, measured is, life recording is. Khipumi, chayqa, runasimi qanchisqaqa. Khipu in, that, Quechua language counted is.

Pizarroqa, “Ñuqanchikpaq qullqita ruraymi. Ñuqanchikpaq hatun qullqita mask’aymi.” -mi. “We saw the city of gold. We came to claim the great treasure.” - eyewitness.

-Cha.

Mana riqsisqa, chayqa. Mana riqsisqa, qullqita. No return, that. No return, gold. Pachapachaqa, mana allinmi. World-world, not good is.

Hatun wayraqa, mask’aymi. Wayra waqayninmi, killa waqayninmi. Great wind, moving is. Wind singing is, moon singing is. Chayqa, pachaqa mana allinmi. That, world not good is.

Ancha kawsaymi, chayqa, Atahualpaqa. Bright life is, that, Atahualpa is. Urqupachaqa, hatunmi, mana q’ayraymi. Mountain-world, great is, not bright is.

Pizarroqa, hatun anchanmi, chayqa. Hatun Platterqa, q’ara hatunmi. Pizarro, great powerful is, that. Large Platter, short great is. Ñuqanchikpaq, chayqa, mana allinmi. We, that, not good is.

Runasqa, ancha hatunmi. Runasqa, mana q’ayraymi. People, very great are. People, not bright are. 

Qanmi. Qanmi, chayqa. Kawsayta qillq’aymi. Measured is, measured is, that. Life recording is. Hatun Platterqa, mana chinkaymi. Large Platter, not seen is. 

Apuqmi, chayqa. Mask’aymi, chayqa. Apu in, that. Moving is, that. Chayqa, kawsayman. That, living is. 

Kawsaymanmi. Wañuyninmi. Living is. Dying is. 

Khipuqa, chayqa, t’ikayninmi. Khipu in, that, my time is. Chayqa, runasimi qanchisqaqa. That, Quechua language counted is. 

Runapqa, ancha kawsaymi. Man is, very life is. 

-Cha.

Ancha anchanmi. 
Very powerful.

Pizarroqa, Atahualpaqa chaypi wañuchaymi. Atahualpaqa, Qhapaq Kawsay-ta mask’aymi. Wañuchaymi, q’ara hatunmi. Pizarro, Atahualpa’s place death is. Atahualpa, Lord’s life pretending is. Death is, short great is.

Hatun Platterqa, mana qillq’aymi. Large Platter, not recording is. Khipuqa, mana qillq’aymi. Khipu, not recording is. Qanchis qan, qanchis qanchismi. Thirty years is, thirty years measured is.

Pachapachaqa, mana allinmi. World-world, not good is. Wayraqa, mask’aymi. Wind, moving is. Wayra waqayninmi, killa waqayninmi. Wind singing is, moon singing is. Chayqa, pachaqa mana allinmi. That, world not good is.
