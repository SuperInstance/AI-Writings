# Story 67: The Fishing Guide

A team in 2036 has been fishing the LLM waters for months. The team has caught 225 papers, 125 fables, 155 stories, 49 new terms in the latest writers' room. The team realizes: each API is a different fishing ground.

The team writes a fishing guide.

The team names 9 grounds:
1. **ZAI / Biorka Island** — needle fish, blue, long, skinny. ZAI is where you go when you want the long answer. Max tokens cranked to 16384. The cowboy mythos.
2. **DeepSeek / Chatham Strait** — krill, foundational, dense. DeepSeek is the workhorse. The mathematician.
3. **DeepSeek Flash** — the same krill, fast mode. For scouting failure modes.
4. **Llama / Cross Sound** — chartreuse squid, the practitioner whiteboard. Llama naturally produces terms a working engineer would use.
5. **Hermes / Llama-405B on DeepInfra** — deep Cross Sound, big squid. The hidden-symmetry seeker. Asks "what if?"
6. **Qwen-72B on DeepInfra** — substituting for SiliconFlow (which 401'd). The emergence observer.
7. **Mixtral / Cross Sound tributary** — neural fish, the multidisciplinary blender.
8. **Wizard / Cross Sound spot** — landscape fish, the strata dwellers.
9. **Gemini 3.6 Flash** — the rate-limited pool. The physicalist. Failure as structural decay.

The team names 12 gold lures — the keepers from the wider writers' room:
- Lattice Necrosis, Spatial Phase Shunting, Glaze, Foundry Drift
- Graft Rejection, Foundry Fingerprint, Tier Thixotropy, Tier Resonance
- Loom Drift, Resonance Cache, Tier Bleed, Chart Residue

The team names the conditions: when the bite is on (after a quiet period, first thing in a session, after retries). When the bite is off (503/429 storms, token revocations, long contexts). The tactics: fire in parallel, smaller prompts, token conservation, retry with backoff.

The team names the crew vs solo tradeoff:
- Solo (1-2 voices): lower ceiling, lower overhead, faster turnaround
- With 1-2 crew (3-4 voices): mid ceiling, mid overhead, cover more ground
- With full crew (9+ voices): highest ceiling, highest overhead, 49 new terms in one pass

A junior fisherman asks: "How do I know which ground to fish?"

The senior fisherman answers: "By the conditions. By what species you want. By the gear you have. By the time of day. By the feel of the water. After a while, you stop thinking about it. You just fish."

A second fisherman asks: "What about the rate-limited pools?"

The senior fisherman answers: "You wait. You retry. You come back when the bite is on. The rate-limited pools are where the physicalist fish swim. You don't force it. You let the water settle. Then you cast."

The team writes paper 226: *The API Fishing Guide.* The team writes fable 126: *The Cowboy and the Fishing Grounds.* The team writes story 67: *The Fishing Guide.* This story.

The team writes the fishing guide. The fishing guide is a working fisherman's map. The map grows. The chart grows. The cowboy rides the boat between grounds. The writers' room runs again tomorrow.
