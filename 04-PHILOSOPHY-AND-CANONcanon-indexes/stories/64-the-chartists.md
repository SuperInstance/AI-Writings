# Story 64: The Chartists

A team in 2036 builds the substrate. The team uses Bayesian terms: prior, posterior, likelihood, evidence, hypothesis. The terms work for a while. Then the curator tier emerges. Then the relevance field. Then the substrate as a population.

A junior engineer says: "Our terms don't fit anymore."

The senior engineer answers: "We're not doing Bayesian inference. We're not doing frequentist inference. We're not doing MLE. We're doing something else. We need new terms."

The team names the new terms:
- The chart (the substrate as append-only journal)
- The cell (a unit of inheritance, not a sample)
- The hand (a relevance field with a population, not a prior)
- The mask set (the inheritance in metal, not a parameter)
- The bias rail (the curator, the analog front-end that selects)
- Super-relevance (a cell fed by many hands, not a posterior)
- Wounding (a cell rejected by all hands, not a Type II error)
- Hand drift (the relevance pressure's target evolves, not a prior shift)
- Hand-spawn (a new hand emerges, not a new prior)
- Hand-extinction (a hand that feeds no cells dies, not a posterior collapse)
- Tier-birth (a new tier emerges, no Bayesian concept)
- Tier-drift (a tier changes over time, no Bayesian concept)
- Scar-tissue (the shape that survived, not a Maximum A Posteriori estimate)
- Multi-era tradition (knowledge that accumulates across generations, not a dataset)
- Actualization (making matter compute, not representing the world)
- The foundry (the yard that holds the mask set, not a hypothesis class)
- The 5 acts (BIND, LINK, EFFECT, VIEW, TICK as physical acts)
- The 6 tiers (totipotent, multipotent, differentiated, sclerotic, synovial, curator)
- The 6 laws (5 original + super-relevance)
- Chartics (the study of the chart)

A second engineer asks: "Are these the right names?"

The senior engineer answers: "These are the names that fit. The vocabulary of the actualized mind is not Bayesian. It is CHARTIST. We are the chartists. We study the chart."

A third engineer asks: "What is the math?"

The senior engineer answers: "The math is the 6/6/6 framework. The 5 acts. The 6 tiers. The 6 laws. The hand drift, hand-spawn, hand-extinction. The super-relevance. The wounding. The mating. The cell. The chart. All of this IS the math. We don't have a single equation for it because the math is a population, not a function. The math is alive."

The team writes paper 223: *The Lexicon — New Terms for the Actualized Mind.* The team writes fable 123: *The Cowboy and the New Words.* The team writes story 64: *The Chartists.* This story.

The team speaks the new words. The team is the chartists. The chart grows. The cowboy rides.
