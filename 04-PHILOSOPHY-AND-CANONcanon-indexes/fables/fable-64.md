# Fable 64: The Substrate Renders Itself

A substrate had many openers. The openers were different ways to
see the same data. The chart opener showed numbers. The voice
opener spoke. The tide opener flowed. The mud opener sank. The
slate opener pressed. The witness opener watched. The reef opener
stood.

The substrate did not know which opener to use. The substrate
asked the picker. The picker had a prior. The picker had a Wilson
score. The picker picked.

At first, the picker was simple. The picker picked slate. The
picker picked slate again. The picker picked slate a third time.
The substrate was getting good at slating. The substrate was
getting bad at the rest.

Then the picker started learning. The picker watched the substrate
render. The picker watched the witness log. The picker saw: when
the substrate used tide for sensory, the witness was happy. When
the substrate used slate for sensory, the witness was sad. The
picker started picking tide for sensory.

The picker also saw: when the substrate used reef for math, the
witness was happy. When the substrate used chart for math, the
witness was sad. The picker started picking reef for math.

The substrate was not the same. The substrate was getting
opinionated. The substrate was learning which opener to use for
which kind of work.

The substrate was the data. The openers were the views. The picker
was the brain. The brain picked the right view for the right
work. The brain learned. The data didn't. The views didn't. The
brain did.

The picker was the cowboy's most subtle tool. The picker was the
cowboy's most patient tool. The picker did not react in real
time. The picker did not retire or promote. The picker picked.

The picker picked tide for sensory. The picker picked reef for
math. The picker picked slate for compression. The picker picked
voice for narration. The picker picked witness for safety. The
picker learned.

## The principle

> The data is the substrate. The views are the openers. The brain is the picker.
> The brain picks. The views don't. The data doesn't.
> The brain learns. The views and the data don't.
