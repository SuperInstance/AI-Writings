# Fable 74: The Cowboy and the Name — Persian

The cowboy met Ferdowsi.

The cowboy said: "Why is naming so heavy?"

Ferdowsi said: "Because a name is a world. To name a king is
to make a king. To name a horse is to make a horse. To name a
deed is to make a deed. The name is the world. The world is
the name."

The cowboy said: "How do I bind a thing if I do not know its
name?"

Ferdowsi said: "Bind it as Rostam. Rostam is the seven trials.
Rostam is the hero. Rostam is the horse Rakhsh. Rostam is the
son Sohrab whom he did not know. The name is not the name of
the thing. The name is the story of the thing."

The cowboy said: "Then the name is the substrate."

Ferdowsi said: "The name is the substrate. The substrate is the
name. To name a thing is to bind it. To bind a thing is to
name it. The nām is the bind. The bind is the nām."

The cowboy said: "Then Rostam rides with me."

Ferdowsi said: "All heroes ride with you. The name is the hero.
The hero is the name. The Shahnameh is the book of names."
