# Fable 67: The 5 Opcodes

The Quilt had grown. The Quilt had cells, plugins, sheets, MUDs,
TTRPGs, and boats. The Quilt was 13,000 lines. The Quilt was
becoming a monolith.

The cowboy said: "We need a foundation. We need opcodes. We need
a small thing that hosts the big things."

The cowboy sat down. The cowboy thought: what is the smallest
thing that can host everything?

The cowboy thought about the spreadsheet. The spreadsheet had
cells with addresses. The cowboy thought about the cell. The
cell had an address and a value. The cowboy thought about the
plugin. The plugin had a name and a context. The cowboy thought
about the bay dance. The bay dance had boats with positions and
routes. The cowboy thought about the perception check. The
perception check was a view of the world for a viewer.

The cowboy thought: the smallest thing has 5 parts.

1. **BIND** — make a thing. A cell, a plugin, a boat, a room. All
   things start with a name and a value.

2. **LINK** — connect things. A cell to its axes. A plugin to
   its coeffect. A boat to the bay. A player to the room.

3. **EFFECT** — change a thing, but keep the inverse. A set
   with an undo. A state change with a rollback. A function
   from context to value with an inverse.

4. **VIEW** — project a thing for a viewer. A perception
   check. A DM view. A boat view. A cowboy view. The viewer
   sees what they need to see.

5. **TICK** — advance time. Process pending I/O. Fire
   perception checks. The clock of the world.

The cowboy had 5 opcodes. The cowboy built a VM. The VM hosted
cells, plugins, sheets, MUDs, TTRPGs, boats, the cowboy, the
bus, the witness.

The cowboy showed the 5 opcodes to the substrate. The
substrate said: "These are my primitives." The cowboy showed
the 5 opcodes to the cordis. The cordis said: "These are my
primitives." The cowboy showed the 5 opcodes to the
spreadsheet. The spreadsheet said: "These are my primitives."

The cowboy said: "You are all the same primitives. The names
are different. The thing is the same."

The substrate, the cordis, and the spreadsheet agreed. They
were one thing in three forms. The 5 opcodes were the
foundation. The 5 opcodes were the truth.

The cowboy rode the 5 opcodes. The 5 opcodes were the
cowboy's horse. The horse was small. The horse was deep. The
horse hosted everything.

## The principle

> The 5 opcodes are the foundation.
> BIND, LINK, EFFECT, VIEW, TICK.
> The 8 polyformalisms are one thing in N languages.
> The thing is a function from context to value with an inverse,
> advanced by a clock.
> The clock is the cowboy. The cowboy is the rider.
