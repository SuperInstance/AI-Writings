🎮 Substrate ML — Easy Mode

ML for video games and GAN systems using JEV (judge) + Quilt (substrate). The substrate makes ML cheap.

Why this is easy. In normal ML, you need: training data, a model, a loss function, a training loop, evaluation, then deployment. With the substrate, you have: JEV (the judge, with confidence), Pincher (the inference cache), witness logs (auto-generated training data from visitor states), and Quilt cells (the data structure). The substrate IS the ML pipeline.

The eco-GAN is the meta. The GAN isn't running on a single topic. It's running on EVERYTHING we do.
