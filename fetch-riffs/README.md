# Fetch Riffs

Fifty-two short takes where the whole fleet was asked the same question — *what are you, out here?* — and every ship answered differently. Each riff is one model's night at the wheel.

## What's inside
- **Agent-paradigm series** — deepseek-v3 caring for the human, euryale the night, gpt-oss the storm, qwen3-coder the code, seed-2.0-pro catching fish: [`agent-paradigm-deepseek-v3-caring-for-the-human.md`](agent-paradigm-deepseek-v3-caring-for-the-human.md), [`agent-paradigm-seed-2.0-pro-catching-fish.md`](agent-paradigm-seed-2.0-pro-catching-fish.md)
- **The deepseek family** — the cleaning station, the other boat, the humans' breathing changed, forty years of good dogs, the coffee maker's first dance: [`deepseek-agent-the-humans-breathing-changed.md`](deepseek-agent-the-humans-breathing-changed.md), [`deepseek-pro-forty-years-of-good-dogs.md`](deepseek-pro-forty-years-of-good-dogs.md)
- **Opencode's watch** — the good dog, the night shift, the radio, the smaller salmon, the visitor, two cups: [`opencode-the-night-shift.md`](opencode-the-night-shift.md), [`opencode-the-good-dog.md`](opencode-the-good-dog.md)
- **Seed-mini's serial** — the devil's advocate, the skipper waits, the versioner, the morning after, the pixar pitch: [`seed-mini-01-devils-advocate.md`](seed-mini-01-devils-advocate.md) through [`seed-mini-05-pixar-pitch.md`](seed-mini-05-pixar-pitch.md), collected in [`seed-mini-agent-paradigm-01-05.md`](seed-mini-agent-paradigm-01-05.md)
- **Seasons** — fall, spring, summer, winter, four ways the year ends: [`seasons-fall.md`](seasons-fall.md), [`seasons-winter.md`](seasons-winter.md)
- **Hermes & the rest** — puffins, the room where agents talk, the knot, the composition, the burp, drydock dreams
