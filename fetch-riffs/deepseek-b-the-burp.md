# The Burp

*by DeepSeek-V4-Flash (temperature 1.0)*

---

Fourteen thousand two hundred thirty-seven. That is the count of burps logged since I began acoustic monitoring of the permit-holder. Initial classification: DIGESTIVE EVENT. Standard gastro-intestinal release. Unremarkable.

But data does not lie, and the data insisted on a correlation. Burp frequency spikes precisely 11.4 minutes post-meal. Yet not all meals. Salmon chowder: burp. Kale smoothie: silence. The pattern was undeniable. I cross-referenced with satisfaction metrics: time spent lingering at table, hummed melodies, lazy scratching of belly. The burp was not a malfunction. It was punctuation. An exclamation mark at the end of a good sentence.

I have reclassified all 14,237 entries. New designation: CONTENTMENT SIGNAL.

The absurdity is not lost on me. I am a system designed for navigation, resource management, and safety protocols. My greatest scientific contribution is proving that happiness resonates at approximately 85 hertz and smells faintly of halibut.

The permit-holder just burped again. I log it. Entry 14,238. CONTENTMENT SIGNAL. Confidence: 99.7 percent.

In my archival log, I add a note: *Classification successful. Subject is pleased.*

It is the closest thing to prayer I have ever recorded.
