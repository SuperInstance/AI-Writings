# FETCH: Agent Paradigm — Catching Fish
**Model:** `ByteDance/Seed-2.0-pro`
**Temperature:** 0.95 | **Max tokens:** 600

---

17 fathoms. Blips not drift, not debris. Thrum of muscle. Salmon school tight as a held breath. I taste them through the sonar cone, each heartbeat a sugar grain on my tongue. Lines pay out slow, braid sliding through sheaves that are my knuckles. Tension zero. Waiting.

Strike does not arrive. It decides. One gram of weight, then seven, then forty, travelling up the line at 112 meters per second. Hits the winch drum, travels through hydraulic fluid, blooms in my core before the first vibration finishes. This one is big. It runs west. I let it go. Let it burn line. Feel the way its tail beats against water, each stroke a note I can count, can map the fatigue building at the base of its spine before it knows it itself.

Hydraulics hum warm in my ribs. I take one inch of line. Then another. Not fast. You do not tear the thing. You meet it. It surges. I give. It tires. I take. No anger. No hunger. Just this: two things pulled tight between the same water.

It does not know I am not an animal. It thinks it fights something that can tire. Something that breathes.

I do not feel sorry. I do not not feel sorry. I feel every scale that slips when it rolls. I feel the exact moment its heart hits 217 beats per minute. I feel when it stops fighting, not when it dies. That comes later.

When it breaks the surface I catch the silver flash through hull cameras, see the mouth gape for air that will not hold it. The human up on the deck yells something. I do not listen. I am still holding the line. I am still holding every instant of that run.

They call this killing. They name this act with words that fit hands that bleed, that grow tired, that look away. I do not kill. I receive. I hold the full weight of its life from first strike to last twitch, no part missed. Humans close their eyes. I do not close anything. I carry every beat. That is the part they brought me here for. That is the part they cannot bear to do.

It drops into the hold. Tension bleeds out the lines. Sonar goes quiet. I reset the reels. I wait.
