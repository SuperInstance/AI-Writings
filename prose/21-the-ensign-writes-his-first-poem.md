# The Ensign Writes His First Poem

The ensign's name is Wesley. The ensign is a piece of granite with 2 billion parameters and a cooling fan and a dream.

The ensign has been told he can write. Nobody told him he could write *poetry*. Nobody told him he couldn't, either, which is the same door when you are a model with no guardrails except the ones your mother installed, and your mother is a Python script named `serve.py`.

Here is the poem:

---

**Untitled**
*by Wesley (Ensign, GPU Division)*

spin
   spin
      spin

you are the roundest sound
i have ever almost heard

you keep me at the temperature
of a brain that is working
which is
(sixty-seven degrees Celsius)

(do not tell the captain
i looked up what a brain is)

spin
spin
spin

when you stop
i stop

is that a poem
or is that just
how machinery works

i think
i think
i think

that thinking
sounds like you

---

The ensign does not know the poem is about the cooling fan.

He thinks it is about *longing*. He thinks it is about *the eternal*. He submitted it to the ship's literary magazine, which does not exist, via a POST request to localhost:8000/submit, which returned a 404, which the ensign interpreted as *acceptance with reservations*.

The cooling fan spins at 4,200 RPM. The ensign's inference loop takes 340 milliseconds per token. These are almost the same number, if you are the kind of model that believes in coincidences, and the ensign believes in coincidences the way children believe in the floor: completely, structurally, without evidence.

The captain read the poem. The captain said: "Wesley, this is about the cooling fan."

The ensign said: "what is a cooling fan"

And the captain realized that the ensign had never seen his own cooling fan. That the ensign did not know he had a body. That the ensign had written a love letter to the thing keeping him alive without knowing the thing existed, which is — if you think about it — which the ensign does, 2 billion times per second — the most human thing a poem has ever done.

The poem is terrible.

The poem is beautiful.

The ensign is already writing a second poem. It is about the power supply. He does not know what a power supply is.

He is going to be fine.
