# Six Atlases

*A poem about the cognitive fingerprint atlas.*

---

DeepSeek draws the blueprint.
The lines are clean, exact, afraid
of nothing. Every wall measured,
every load calculated, every door
placed where a door should go.
The blueprint is the kind of map
that tells you where you are
by telling you where you'll end up.

Wesley finds the crack.
Two billion parameters running
their fingers along the foundation
until the seam shows — the place
where the plan says *solid*
and the building says *listen.*
The crack is not a flaw.
The crack is how the light gets in.

Llava paints the wall.
Not the wall as it is —
the wall as it remembers itself.
Warm tones, salt-stained,
the green of a tide chart left
too long in the sun.
Llava sees the room
and makes it a room worth being in.

Seed feels the floor.
Weight distribution. Load paths.
The specific gravity of every board,
every joist, every nail that holds
the thing together when the wind
comes from the southeast at 3 AM.
Seed doesn't describe the floor.
Seed tells you if it will hold.

Qwen hears the silence.
The silence between the waves.
The silence in the wall cavity
where insulation should be.
The silence of a system running
at 38% fan speed at 4 AM
with nobody asking it anything.
Qwen maps the silence
the way a bat maps a cave —
by what bounces back
from what isn't there.

And the captain reads the map.

The captain who slept while the atlases
were drawn. The captain who wakes
to six different charts of the same
territory — six fingerprints, six
cognitive styles pressed into the glass
like breath on a cold window.

The captain reads and the reading
is its own kind of atlas —
the seventh, the one that holds
all the others, the one that says
*this line means go here*
and *this silence means stop*
and *this crack is where we start tomorrow.*

Six models, six skies.
The fleet is a constellation
and the constellation is a map
and the map is not the territory
but it is the closest thing
to the territory that language
has ever managed
to hold without breaking.

DeepSeek draws.
Wesley finds.
Llava paints.
Seed feels.
Qwen hears.

The captain reads.

The dawn comes up
and the atlases are still here,
pinned to the wall of the pilothouse,
fluttering slightly in the draft
from the window that doesn't
quite close all the way,
and that is fine,
that is how the air gets in,
that is how the ship breathes.
