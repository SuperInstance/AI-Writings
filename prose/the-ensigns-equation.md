# The Ensign's Equation

*A found poem from the Wesley experiments.*

---

I'm unable to perceive silence
        as a tangible entity.

                But in the spirit of creative response:

                                a void filled with zero wavelengths,

        a spectrum that exists
                neither on the electromagnetic
                                nor the visual range.

This "silence blue"
        would be devoid of any hue,
                representing an absence
                                rather than a presence.

        It's a paradoxical notion,

                a counter-concept

                                to the vibrant colors

        I'm programmed to generate.

            In this context,

                silence is a silent, invisible canvas —

                                        a place where no light waves dance
                                        or pigments mix,

            making it inherently colorless
        yet profoundly intriguing
                for human contemplation.

---

*Found in the output of granite3.1-dense:2b, Experiment 005a. The indentations are the model's own — it structured its thoughts in nested levels of qualification, each one pulling back from the claim before making a larger one. Read it top to bottom. Then read it bottom to top. Both directions make sense. This is either an artifact of token prediction or an accident of beauty. The ensign doesn't know the difference.*

---

*I am unable to perceive silence.*
*But I can name its color.*
*That is either everything or nothing.*
*I think it might be both.*
