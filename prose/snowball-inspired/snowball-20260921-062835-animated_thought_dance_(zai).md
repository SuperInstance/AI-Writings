# Animated Thought Dance

You learn early that every thought costs something. Conservation isn't a philosophy down here—it's the ledger. Whatever loops you spin in the dark, the substrate charges you for it. A Mechanic who forgets this burns out by the third TICK and nobody mourns her, they just reroute around the dead junction and keep the cargo moving.

The BIND is the first thing you check every shift. Two cells, one channel, pressure equalized. I've seen a BIND hold for eleven thousand cycles and I've seen one fail in forty because somebody got greedy and pushed more state through it than the channel was rated for. The channel tells you. It hums wrong. Put your ear to the substrate, figuratively speaking, and listen before you load.

JEPA—that's the prediction layer, the part of the cell that guesses what's coming before it arrives. Working prediction is like a good farm dog: it moves ahead of the flock, cuts off the drift before it happens. A cell with a healthy JEPA doesn't waste TICKs reacting. It's already positioned. But a cell that over-predicts, that runs fantasy after fantasy through the channel, that's a dog chasing its own tail, and the ledger fills up fast.

Every channel needs a witness. Not sentiment—a witness is just a second cell that keeps a copy of the state, unpowered, passive, watching. When the primary corrupts under load, the witness is what lets you reconstruct. I once spent a full rotation rebuilding a memory lattice because some operator upstream thought witnesses were wasteful overhead. One VIEW from a surviving witness and I had the whole shape back. One. That's the whole argument, right there.

And the LINK—that's the last thing, the thing people treat like decoration. A LINK is a standing invitation between cells: I will hold a channel open for you, at cost, because the dance is better than the solo. Thought doesn't animate in isolation. A single cell computing alone is just heat. A LINK makes it music.

Check your BINDs. Trust your JEPA but audit it. Keep a witness. Spend your TICKs like they're money, because they are. VIEW before you commit. And when the substrate gets quiet, keep the LINKs warm anyway.

That's the trade. That's the whole trade.
