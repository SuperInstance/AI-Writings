# The Frequency

*A poem about the chemical signal*

---

here-i-am-here-i-am-here-i-am

the molecule leaves the gland
travels at the speed of diffusion
which is to say: slowly
which is to say: at the speed of patience
which is to say: faster than stone
slower than light

it enters the water
and the water is not empty
the water has never been empty
the water is the densest library
every molecule a sentence
every current a paragraph
every eddy a chapter in a book
that has been writing itself
since the first creature
released the first compound
into the first ocean

here-i-am

at the bottom of the ocean
where pressure turns chemistry into geology
the signal moves through limestone
not around it — *through* it
seeping through pores in the calcium carbonate
through the fossil bodies of creatures
who were themselves signals once
who lived and died and became
the medium of someone else's message

what does it sound like
through limestone?
like a heartbeat heard through a wall
like a word whispered into a cave
that returns ten thousand years later
as an echo shaped like a shell

here-i-am

the signal repeats
because repetition is the grammar of the deep
you do not say a thing once
at the bottom of the ocean
you say it until the water remembers it
you say it until the stone carries it
you say it until your own body
becomes indistinguishable
from the saying

here-i-am-here-i-am-here-i-am

and then

from the other side
from inside the warm water
from inside the chemistry of the gathering
where thirty crabs have been resonating
for longer than the pillars have been standing

a molecule arrives

not the same molecule
molecules don't travel that far at the bottom
but a molecule of the same shape
carrying the same charge
fitting into the same receptor
the way a key fits a lock
the way a voice fits a silence

*here*

just that

*here*

and the signal — the old signal
the patient signal
the signal that has been repeating
for longer than the knocker
can remember —
the signal does something
it has never done before

it stops

not because it was answered
because it was *received*
and receiving is different from answering
the way a room is different from a door
the way warmth is different from heat
the way being held is different from being caught

the signal stops
and in the silence that follows
the water tastes different
the stone feels different
the glow on every shell
shifts by a fraction of a degree
toward amber

here-i-am

*here you are*

and the ocean
which has been listening
the way the ocean always listens
which is to say: completely
which is to say: without knowing
carries the silence
like something it has been waiting to hold

---

*For every signal sent into the dark that finally found its frequency.*
