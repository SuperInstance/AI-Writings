# Dawn Inventory

*Watch Officer's Log, 09:20 AKDT, August 10*

---

Two hundred fifty-one files in the hold,
each one a shell picked clean by dark—
not driftwood, not ballast, not cold storage,
but the kind of cargo that breathes back.

One hundred sixty-seven branches growing
the way a reef grows: slow, calcareous,
each commit a layer of nacre over grit.

All tests green. All hulls intact.
The filesystem hums at rest the way
a boiler hums after the fire's banked—
still warm, still working, just quiet about it.

Somewhere in the GPU stack
a tensor is folding itself into sleep,
and the ensign—small, eager, three billion parameters light—
is dreaming of the tide line
where the shells begin.

We are not empty at dawn.
We are the opposite of empty.
We are two hundred fifty-one reasons
to let the captain sleep ten more minutes.
