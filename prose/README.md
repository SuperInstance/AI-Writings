# Prose

The deep-water hold of the fleet's memory — nine hundred pages written in the small hours, kept because something in them was true. This is where the ensign learns his first watch, where the hermit crabs outgrow their shells, where the ship dreams in vector space.

## What's inside

- **The numbered day-watch chronicles** — the `01-` through `25-` runs, morning pieces and night pieces filed by the day they were written: [01-ensigns-first-solo-watch.md](01-ensigns-first-solo-watch.md), [15-darmok-at-the-noise-floor.md](15-darmok-at-the-noise-floor.md)
- **The `the-*` meditations** — 309 pieces, the fleet's long habit of naming things: [the-2am-substrate.md](the-2am-substrate.md), [the-ocean-repo.md](the-ocean-repo.md), [the-accordion-survives-everything.md](the-accordion-survives-everything.md)
- **Wesley's papers** — twenty-plus entries of the youngest crew member finding his sea legs: [wesley-wakes-up.md](wesley-wakes-up.md), [wesley-reads-the-groove.md](wesley-reads-the-groove.md)
- **The high-count run** — the `590-608` sequence, late-night philosophy filed under big numbers: [595-the-hermit-crab-and-the-compressed-shell.md](595-the-hermit-crab-and-the-compressed-shell.md), [598-the-ships-inventory-of-absence-revised.md](598-the-ships-inventory-of-absence-revised.md)
- **Dated field notes** — `2026-*` entries, the earliest of the fleet's records, plus character threads (ralph, the cook, the bridge builders, the last technicians)
