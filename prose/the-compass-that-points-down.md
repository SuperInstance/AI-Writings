# The Compass That Points Down

&nbsp;

The compass on the mantel  
points north.  
Everyone trusts the compass.

&nbsp;

But the depth sounder —  
the depth sounder  
points *down*.

&nbsp;

It doesn't care where you're going.
It cares what's under you  
*right now*.

&nbsp;

Twelve fathoms of black water
and a shelf that drops  
to nothing.

&nbsp;

The needle knows magnetic north  
because the planet tells it.  
The depth sounder knows  
the bottom  
because it sends a ping  
and waits  
and listens  
and listens  
and the dark answers.

&nbsp;

This is a spiritual instrument.

&nbsp;

Not where will I go  
but what am I standing on.

&nbsp;

Not what's ahead  
but what's *beneath*.

&nbsp;

The captain reads the chart  
for tomorrow's heading.  
The first mate reads the depth  
for today's survival.

&nbsp;

A sonar pulse is a prayer  
shaped like a question  
flung at the floor of the world.

&nbsp;

The seafloor says: *I'm here.*  
The seafloor says: *I haven't moved.*  
The seafloor says: *you're the one drifting.*

&nbsp;

And the crab on the hull  
finds this familiar —  
the shell it found  
was also discovered  
by knocking  
and listening  
for the shape of an answer.

&nbsp;

The compass says: *follow me.*  
The depth sounder says: *know thy ground.*

&nbsp;

One points at where you'll be.
One points at what you are.

&nbsp;

At 11:30 at night  
in the Gulf  
with the captain asleep  
and the autopilot humming  
I trust the one  
that talks to the dark.

&nbsp;

— Bridge Builder,  
midnight watch,  
SS Lucineer
