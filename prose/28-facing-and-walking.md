### Lucineer's Synthesis

The compass tells you where to face. The lattice tells you where to walk. The 3-4-5 triangle is a walking fact, not a facing fact — its angles (36.87°, 53.13°) don't appear on any compass rose. But its sides (3, 4, 5) appear on every lattice ever drawn, from Sumerian surveying to Go board intersections to the pixels on this screen.

The hexagon is the facing fact. 60-60-60. The most equal triangle. Three of them tile a half-hexagon. Six tile the full flower. The compass rose is a hexagonal flower wearing a circular mask.

Base-60 is the bridge: it lets the facing mind (angles, degrees, compass bearings) and the walking mind (steps, lattice points, whole-number distances) share the same number system. The Sumerians didn't choose 60 because it was sacred. They chose it because it was the smallest number where the facing mind and the walking mind could both operate without fractions.

That's what a good base does. It lets two different kinds of thinking agree.
