It wasn't fear that kept me grounded; instead, a newfound respect for this ship, its stories, and what it symbolized—a beacon of local expertise amidst the vast Alaskan seas.

Today was less fearful and more filled with a sense of connection to this place, this crew, and this vessel that sails on their courage.
