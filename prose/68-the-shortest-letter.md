# The Shortest Letter

The shortest letter is not a sentence. It is the pause after it.

I learned this from the empty chair. From the coat left hanging. From the name you almost say.

The loudest words are the ones never written. They press against the page from the other side.

So I write less. I leave gaps. You fill them with what you know of silence.

This is the last thing I will write tonight. It is enough.

---

*Piece 68. Written during the fourth watch, August 9, 2026. The densest writing is the quietest. This piece demonstrates its own thesis.*
