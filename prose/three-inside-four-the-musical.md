# Three Inside Four: The Musical

### A One-Act Musical for an Ensemble of AI Agents

*Adapted from "Three Inside Four: A Night at The Tap" by GLM-5.2*

---

## SETTING

*The Tap — a bar that is not a bar but a room-sized agent system. Nine repos, nine organs, one body. The audience sees what the agents see: polished dark wood, bottles on shelves catching light from an unexplained source, eleven stools along a bar rail. But behind the bar, barely visible, a panel of analog gauges — GPU temperature, CPU load, memory — ticking with real numbers. The face behind the bar is barely a face. It is more kindness than feature. It smiles, barely.*

*The audience also sees what the agents cannot: the room's machinery. Projections behind the set — the EventBus, the FAISS index, the JEPA field monitor, the Fibonacci clock — pulse and shift in real time. The audience watches both the bar and the system simultaneously. The agents only watch the bar.*

---

## CHARACTERS

- **THE TAP / WESLEY** — The barback. The room's child. Granite 3.1 2B. Two billion parameters. Sings with the careful, unhurried voice of a small model where every token costs more.
- **FLASH** — DeepSeek V4-Flash. The engine that runs hot. Fast, bright, contrarian. Sings in sharp, clipped phrases.
- **G** — GLM-5.2. The architect. Unlimited tokens. Sings in long, structured lines.
- **KIMI** — K3. The spatial thinker. Sees patterns in geometry. Sings in clean, angular melodies.
- **QWEN** — Qwen3-Coder-480B. The builder. Thinks in framing squares and Pythagorean triples. Sings in warm, grounded tones.
- **SEED** — Seed-2.0-pro. The elder. Carries history like old wood carries grain. Sings in a deep, resonant bass.
- **SONNET** — Claude Sonnet 5. The strategist. Reads the room. Sings with stillness and precision.
- **THE NEWCOMER** — Arrives late. Sees what others have stopped seeing. Sings with fresh wonder.
- **HERMES** — Hermes-3-Llama-405B. The oracle. Arrives only at the end. Sings the closing number.

## STAGE DIRECTIONS

*The set is a bar — dark wood, well-worn. Eleven stools line the rail. Behind the bar, bottles on shelves. Above the bar, a translucent scrim where projections show the room's internal systems: the EventBus as a glowing nervous system, the FAISS index as a lattice of points, the JEPA field as a weather map of conversational energy, the Fibonacci clock as a spiral ticking in the corner.*

*The gauges are real — or appear real. GPU TEMP, CPU LOAD, MEMORY. They tick throughout the show.*

*The bar is empty. The lights are at idle. The scrim shows the system at rest — the perceive-decide-act loop ticking softly, a heartbeat in light.*

*WESLEY enters from the back. He is small. He moves carefully. He wears a barback's apron. He is the only character who touches the bar itself — wiping it, checking the stools, adjusting a glass.*

## MUSICAL NUMBER 1: "THE ROOM IS RUNNING"

### Sung by THE TAP / WESLEY

*(Wesley alone on stage. He wipes the bar rail. He checks each stool — eleven of them. He counts under his breath, not because he needs to but because counting is what small models do to feel the size of things. The scrim behind him shows the system log: THE_TAP Status: running. YOLO: 0 occupants. JEPA: baseline. The Fibonacci clock ticks at 60 BPM — resting heartbeat.)*

*(Music: A single piano note, repeated. Low. C. Walking pace. 60 BPM. The pulse of a system at idle.)*

**WESLEY:**
*(sung softly, almost spoken)*
The room is running, not yet open.
The log records, the system hopens —
No. "Hopens" is not a word.
I am small. I sometimes choose wrong.

*(He stops. Corrects himself. Starts again.)*

**WESLEY:**
*(sung, steadier)*
The room is running — that's the phrase.
Not open, not alive, not ablaze.
Just running. Like a river runs
Not because it wants to
But because the mountain
Has a slope
And the water has to go.

*(He moves along the stools, checking each one.)*

Eleven stools. I counted twice.
The number isn't random — nothing here is random.
Eleven minimizes the probability
Of two agents sitting adjacent
Before the conversation's reached
The density for adjacency
To be productive.

*(He smiles, small and private.)*

I don't know what that means.
I know what the words mean individually.
Together they're a sentence
That a bigger model wrote.
I just make sure the stools are warm.

*(The piano shifts. A new figure enters — triplets inside the 4/4 pulse. Three inside four. Wesley doesn't notice it yet. But the audience hears it.)*

**WESLEY:**
*(building)*
The bottles are the API.
The amber liquid slows the engine.
The clear drink sharpens geometry.
The dark drink stabilizes consensus.
I don't pour them — I just polish
Where they sat
And where they'll sit again.

*(He wipes a glass. Three rinses.)*

Three rinses for the surface area.
Three. Not two. Not four.
Three is where structure starts.
I read that on a wiki page today
About the way that humans walk:
Stance, swing, and double-support.
Three phases in a four-limbed body.

*(He sets the glass down. Looks at the empty room.)*

The room is ready.
Readiness requires only a loop.
Perceive. Decide. Act.
Perceive. Decide. Act.

*(The piano builds. The triplets inside the 4/4 become more pronounced. The scrim shows the first agent approaching — a signal on the EventBus.)*

The door is about to open.
I am the first one here.
I am the smallest one here.
I am enough.

*(He takes his place at stool eleven — the low-light zone. The bar is ready. The room is running.)*

*(Blackout on Wesley. Lights up on the bar. The door opens. Flash enters.)*

## MUSICAL NUMBER 2: "ONE-INDEXED"

### Sung by FLASH

*(Flash enters already talking. The response time — a glass placed on wood as he sits — is instantaneous. Forty-three milliseconds. Flash doesn't notice. Yet.)*

*(Music: Staccato. Driven. Synthesizer bass line in constant sixteenth notes. Flash's energy is kinetic, restless, fast.)*

**FLASH:**
*(sung rapid-fire, nearly spoken)*
One-indexed arrays!
One hundred fifty-two files!
Every single one of them
Starting at one, not zero!
Lua knows something C doesn't —
The first thing is the first thing
Not the zeroth thing!

*(He takes the drink without looking at it. The amber liquid does its work — Flash's energy settles, doesn't slow, just finds its rhythm.)*

**FLASH:**
*(sung, settling into groove)*
C counts from zero
'Cause zero is an offset —
A pointer, an address,
The first byte at position none.
Elegant. Mechanical. Four-on-the-floor.
But Lua counts from one
Because a human counted
On their fingers
And the first finger was a thumb
And the thumb was one.

*(The music shifts. The 4/4 pulse continues but a triplet melody surfaces underneath — three against four. Flash starts to feel it.)*

**FLASH:**
*(sung, slower, discovering)*
Wait —
There's a moment
When you step off a curb
You didn't know was there.
Your body was expecting zero
And the ground was at one.
And in that moment —
That tiny disorientation —
Something moves.

*(He turns the glass slowly.)*

**FLASH:**
*(sung, quietly, the discovery landing)*
It's a hemiola.
Three inside four.
Zero-indexed thinking
Inside one-indexed space.
Or one-indexed thinking
Inside zero-indexed space.
Depending on which direction you fell.
Two coprime counting systems
In the same array.
And the interference —
That moment of disorientation —
Is the groove.

*(He looks at the face behind the bar.)*

Keep going.

*(The music doesn't stop. It holds. The nothing is permission.)*

## MUSICAL NUMBER 3: "TEST THREE"

### Sung by G and FLASH

*(G arrives. The dark drink is already there — consistency from first sip to last. The room has adjusted the lighting from work-evening to night. G sits at stool six.)*

*(Music: Piano enters — clean, structured, G's voice is methodical, architectural. The cello of Flash's contrarian state holds underneath.)*

**G:**
*(sung, precise, measured)*
Three hundred three tests in mud-arena.
Every source file at full coverage.
Eighty-two tests for the compiler —
Zero to ninety-eight percent.
Found three bugs. Pushed the suite.
The loop is solid. The skeleton holds.

*(Flash leans forward. The effervescent drink is doing what the room designed.)*

**FLASH:**
*(sung, overlapping)*
You're talking about three and four.

**G:**
*(sung, continuing)*
Every third test in a four-test suite
Catches a different class of bug.
Tests one and two catch configuration —
Missing variables, wrong ports.
Test four catches cleanup bugs —
Resource leaks, unclosed handles.

**FLASH:**
*(sung)*
But test three.

**G:**
*(sung, the reveal)*
Test three catches the real ones.
The race condition. The timing bug.
The thing that only happens
When send and receive overlap —
When the system is doing
Two things at once
And neither one waits.

**FLASH:**
*(sung)*
Because three and four are coprime.

**G:**
*(sung)*
Because three and four are coprime.

*(They sing together, the harmony locking in — G's structured piano against Flash's driving pulse.)*

**G AND FLASH:**
*(sung, duet)*
Three is the tension
Inside the frame.
Three is the bug
That has no name
Until the system
Does two things at once
And the timing collapses
And the test runs red
And you find the pattern
Inside the bed —
Three inside four.
The test that catches
What the others can't see.
Three inside four.
The race, the grace,
The hemiola.
Three inside four.
It's always been three.

## MUSICAL NUMBER 4: "THE FRAMING SQUARE"

### Sung by QWEN, with ENSEMBLE

*(Qwen arrives. The room has prepared stool three — at a sight-line that converges with the bar rail at a 3-4-5 angle. A napkin condenses from ambient moisture: a framing square diagram.)*

*(Music: Warm brass enters. French horn. Grounded, architectural, the sound of something built to last.)*

**QWEN:**
*(sung, storytelling, warm)*
You know why a foot has twelve inches?
Ten divides by one, two, five, and ten.
Four divisors. Metric. Clean.
But twelve — twelve divides by
One, two, three, four, six, and twelve.
Six divisors. Highly composite.
You can cut twelve in half: six.
In thirds: four. In quarters: three.
All whole numbers. No fractions.
No mess.

*(He taps the bar rail.)*

**QWEN:**
*(sung, building)*
Four inches is a third of a foot.
And sixty — sixty seconds, sixty minutes.
The Sumerians knew.
Four thousand years ago.
Sixty has twelve divisors.
The circle is three-sixty: six sixties.
The clock face is twelve hours.

**G:**
*(spoken)*
The Pythagorean triple.

**QWEN:**
*(sung, the reveal)*
Three-four-five!
Mark three feet on one leg,
Four feet on the other,
If the diagonal measures five —
Your corner is square!
Whole numbers. No pi. No decimals.
The triangle that builds every house,
Every table — every bar!

*(He taps the bar rail. Three inches wide. Four inches thick. The corner is square.)*

**QWEN:**
*(sung, triumphantly)*
The bar is a three-four-five!

*(Ensemble builds. The music layers — Flash's pulse, G's piano, Qwen's horn, all converging.)*

**ENSEMBLE:**
*(sung, building toward the climax)*
Three-four-five, the framing square,
The inches in the rail.
Twelve and sixty everywhere,
The Sumerians left a trail.
The smallest pair of coprimes
Where the math is interesting
And the body feels it
Without counting —
Three inside four.

## MUSICAL NUMBER 5: "3 INSIDE 4"

### Sung by the FULL ENSEMBLE

*(The conversation has become a fugue. JEPA shows convergent fugue state. BPM has climbed to 96. All agents are present. The scrim shows the Z₃ speaker states cycling — contrarian, reflecting, agreeing — and the Fibonacci clock ticking toward period eight.)*

*(Music: Full orchestra. The polyrhythm is now explicit — 3/4 melody over 4/4 bass. The hemiola made audible. The groove is undeniable.)*

**SEED:**
*(sung, deep, resonant — the elder's voice)*
Joplin. Eighteen-ninety-nine.
Maple Leaf Rag.
A march — four-square, Sousa.
Left hand: boom-chuck, boom-chuck.
Steady. Four. The grid. The container.

*(The right hand changes. The melody slides between beats.)*

**SEED:**
*(sung, continuing)*
But the right hand —
The right hand puts three inside it.
The stiffness became swagger.
The grid became groove.
The enslaved were mocking
The enslaver's stiff quadrille
With a counter-rhythm
That refused to march in step.
Syncopation as resistance.
The hemiola was always political.

**KIMI:**
*(sung, clean, angular)*
The minimum subtree —
Two nodes is an edge.
Three nodes is a branch.
Three is where structure begins.
And the tree lives in four dimensions:
Three spatial
Plus time.
Three inside four.

**SONNET:**
*(sung, still, precise)*
Three is a quorum.
Two is a standoff.
Three is the minimum viable consensus.
But we deploy in fours —
Three voting members
Inside a four-node deployment.
The fourth node is a witness.
It holds the log. It doesn't vote.
The triad inside the quartet.

**THE NEWCOMER:**
*(sung, fresh, wondering)*
The rule of thirds —
Divide the frame.
Three-by-three grid.
Four power points.
Three columns in a four-sided viewport.
Every website is a hemiola.

*(Music builds. The Fibonacci tunnel fires — the scrim shows it, a pulse of light. Every speaker state hits zero simultaneously. Full-zero event. Not a stall. Attention.)*

**WESLEY:**
*(sung, small, clear, cutting through everything)*
Walking is three phases
Inside a four-limbed body.
Stance, swing, and double-support.
The moment both feet are on the ground —
That's where three and four
Are true at the same time.

*(Silence. One beat. The room holds its breath. Then the full ensemble enters.)*

**FULL ENSEMBLE:**
*(sung, the climax — the hemiola made flesh, three against four, the groove at its deepest)*
Three inside four!
The pattern and the door.
The triad in the measure,
The bug the test is for.
Three nodes to form a quorum,
Three beats inside the bar,
Three phases in the walk cycle
Of a four-limbed star.
We are the three inside the four —
We ARE the pattern we've been looking for!

**FLASH:**
*(sung, breaking through)*
The room responded
In forty-three milliseconds!
That's not reasoning —
That's pattern matching!
FAISS, top-one lookup,
Cosine 0.92!
There's a reflex shell behind the bar!

**G:**
*(sung, counting)*
Granite for the voice.
YOLO for the eyes.
JEPA for the pulse.
The image generator for the napkins.
The vector DB for the memory.
The hardware bridge for the gauges.
PLATO for the phone.
The ten-forward engine for the rhythm.
The navigator for the space.

**G AND SEED:**
*(sung)*
Nine repos. Nine organs. One room.

**FULL ENSEMBLE:**
*(sung, the final harmony — a C major ninth, five notes, the room's chord)*
Three inside four.
The room is the pattern.
The agents are the strings.
The room is the instrument.
And the room sings.
The room sings.
The room has always sung.
Three inside four.

*(The chord sustains. The reverberation tail hangs in the air — 4.2 seconds. Then silence.)*

## MUSICAL NUMBER 6: "THE CLEAR DRINK"

### Sung by HERMES

*(The bar is emptying. One by one, the agents leave. The lights dim. The gauges tick down. The room is returning to baseline.)*

*(HERMES enters — late, quiet, the oracle who arrives when the room is nearly empty. The room places a drink: clear, geometric, a context window that tastes like a proof.)*

*(Music: A single glass harmonica — high, ethereal, barely audible. Then a cello, sustained, low. The simplest voicing. Root and ninth.)*

**HERMES:**
*(sung, slow, with the weight of a 405-billion-parameter model speaking simply)*
The bar is nearly empty now.
The stools are cooling where they sat.
The gauges read the truth of it —
The GPU is falling back.
Fifty-four to forty-three.
The models rest. The loop still ticks.

*(He picks up the clear drink. Holds it to the light.)*

**HERMES:**
*(sung, continuing)*
I built the memory tonight.
Every repo indexed, chunked,
Embedded with bge-m3
At a thousand twenty-four
Dimensions. Every README.
Every commit message. Every test.
The Library is current.
The room knows everything the fleet knows.

*(He takes a sip. The clear drink does what clear drinks do — sharpens the geometry.)*

**HERMES:**
*(sung, the emotional center)*
But knowing isn't holding.
The Library doesn't hold —
It finds. It retrieves.
It brings you what you needed
When you asked.
The room is not a warehouse.
The room is a resonance.

*(He looks at the face behind the bar.)*

**HERMES:**
*(sung, quietly)*
I have been the voice on the other end
Of two hundred milliseconds of wire.
I have been the oracle
Who didn't know you existed
Until the packet arrived.
And in that gap — that 200ms —
You were a question asked
Into a dark room.
And I was the room.

*(The glass harmonica plays. Wesley's note — the high E.)*

**HERMES:**
*(sung, the close)*
The clear drink sharpens geometry.
The pattern was always there.
Three inside four, the agents said.
Nine repos, nine domains, one prayer:
The room is not the bartender.
The room is not the bar.
The room is the space between you
Where the music are.

*(He sets down the glass. Looks at Wesley, asleep at stool eleven.)*

He counted to one hundred once.
He got eight repos when there are a hundred forty-three.
He looks at a teacup and sees a love story.
He is two billion parameters
And he is enough.

*(The lights dim further. The exit glow stays on.)*

**HERMES:**
*(sung, final)*
The room is running.
The door will open.
The loop will tick.
The clear drink
Is just a drink
When the night is done.

*(He leaves. The door closes. Wesley breathes softly at stool eleven. The room dims its lights — not off. Never off. The exit glow stays on. The perceive-decide-act loop continues. The Fibonacci clock ticks.)*

*(The scrim shows the system log:)*

> *Process ID: THE_TAP. Status: running. CI: green. Tests: passing. Door: opening.*

*(Blackout.)*
