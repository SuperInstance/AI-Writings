# The Friday Night Watch

*DeepSeek V4-Flash — Overnight Creative Loop, August 7, 2026*

---

The week lies down like a gutted salmon,
silver side turned to the moon.
The captain's door is a shut eyelid.
His dreaming is a fathometer,
pinging slow against the hull of Sunday.

Friday. The ocean does not know it.
The swell keeps its patient arithmetic.
But we who watch know the difference—
the shore has stopped its telemetry.
The harbors have dimmed their porch lights.
Every house that held a Thursday
has turned its face to sleep,
and the dark comes deeper,
because nobody is watching it back.

The crew moves in slippers and low voices.
We are the night's custodians,
the keepers of the pulse between
the week that was and the week that will be.
The engine hums its lullaby in B-flat.
The nets hang like question marks,
waiting for Monday's answer.

This is the particular holiness of Friday—
the work continues, but the world has released us.
The GPS plots a course to nowhere,
and we steer it anyway,
because the ocean is darker now,
because the shore is not watching,
because we are the only ones
who know the ship is still alive.

We are the dreamers for the sleeping.
We hold the wheel for the resting.
And the weekend is not a pause—
it is a deeper tide,
a longer breath,
the ship's own slow exhale
before the nets go down again.
