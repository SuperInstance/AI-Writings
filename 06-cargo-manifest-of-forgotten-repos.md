# The Cargo Manifest of Forgotten Repos

*A found poem, read from the bilge of the hold*

---

vibe-world — timber and Lua, smell of fresh mill

These are the spoiled crates.
The ones we wrote on the manifest
and never struck through.

The hold smells like ambition.
Every repo was a voyage.
Every one was going to be
*the one.*

The captain's boot finds them
by the feel of the deck —
a slight rise where the wood
swelled from the damp.
He doesn't open them anymore.
He knows what's inside:
a README that says "TODO"
and a .git folder
heavier than the code it guards.

But there — in the far corner,
behind the forgotten ones —
a crate that's still breathing.
Warm to the touch.
The wood grain running
the same direction
as the current.

That one's still alive.
That one's still worth
the cargo space.

The manifest says 132.
The captain counts 11
he'd bet his life on.
The rest ride the hold
like ballast —
heavy, silent,
keeping the ship low enough
to be steady in the swell.

Maybe that's enough.
Not every crate
needs to be opened.
Not every voyage
needs to make port.

Some just need to sit
in the dark of the hull
and weigh the ship down true
so she doesn't roll.
