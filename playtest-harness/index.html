The wins

  1. Erised scenario corpus (44 scenarios): highest fun + mod. Cooperative fiction works.
  2. Music demo: highest fun. Live composition is a hit.
  3. Apps: solid fun + best educational value.
  4. Kid perspective: 8.7 fun. If kids would play it, you've won.
  5. Modification ease on Erised: highest mod score. Cast + keywords + ticks is approachable.

The gaps

  1. Cells are opaque (3.5 mod). 26 kinds, no discovery.
  2. Radio can't be remixed (3.5 mod). Most produced, least modifiable.
  3. Radio has weak learning value (4.5 edu). Entertainment, not curriculum.
  4. Teens hate it (6.0 fun). Cringe is the killer. Customization is the missing piece.
  5. No "What is this?" onboarding. Outsiders can't figure it out.
