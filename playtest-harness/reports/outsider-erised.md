# outsider tests erised
*Model: Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct*

I tried Erised for 15 minutes. I typed in: “Three strangers stuck in a broken elevator during a blackout. One’s a retired firefighter, one’s a teen with a secret TikTok account, one’s a cat who talks but only when no one’s looking.” Hit “Go.”  

It started: *“I used to pull people outta burning buildings,”* said the firefighter. *“Now I can’t even fix a toaster.”*  
The teen: *“I posted a video of my dog wearing socks. 2.3M views. But I’m not telling anyone I’m here.”*  
Then the cat: *“I’m not a cat. I’m a former astronaut. The collar’s a disguise.”*  

I laughed. I didn’t expect the cat to be an astronaut. I didn’t expect the firefighter to be so sad. The teen didn’t react to the cat. That felt real—people ignore weird stuff when they’re stressed.  

Frustrated? The cat never explained *how* it became an astronaut. The firefighter kept saying “I used to…” like he was stuck. No one asked him why. I wanted more pushback. More conflict.  

Delighted? The story didn’t crash. It just… flowed. Like watching strangers on a bus start talking and accidentally reveal their whole lives.  

**Fun Factor**: 8 — I’d do it again.  
**Educational Value**: 7 — I saw how silence and avoidance tell stories too.  
**Modification Ease**: 4 — I have no idea how to change the tone or add rules.  

**One concrete suggestion**: Add a “nudge button” — one click to make one character ask a direct question like, “Wait, how did you become an astronaut?” That’d spark the next beat.
