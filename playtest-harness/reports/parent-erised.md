# parent tests erised
*Model: deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash*

**Jordan here.** Fifteen minutes with Erised felt like thirty—in a good way. I set three characters: a grumpy wizard, a talking squirrel, and a kid who lost their homework in a magical forest. Hit tick. And it *ran*. No prompts from me needed. The wizard blamed the squirrel, the squirrel countered with acorn puns, and my youngest (8) actually laughed out loud.

**Delight:** The "fire-and-forget" aspect. I poured coffee while the story unfolded. The characters stayed consistent—the squirrel *never* stopped hoarding plot points. That’s huge for multitasking.

**Frustration:** The pace felt uneven. Some ticks were paragraphs, others single sentences. My older kid (11) wanted more tension; the wizard kept resolving conflicts too quickly. Also, no pause button—I had to scramble to catch a line I wanted to discuss.

**Would I keep using?** Yes, but only if I can adjust the "cadence" per child. My 8-year-old needs slower beats; my 11-year-old wants snappier dialogue. Right now, it’s one-speed-fits-all.

**Concrete suggestion:** Add a **"Pace Dial"** —slider for tick speed (slow/medium/fast) and a toggle for **"Conflict Mode"** that forces characters to disagree at least once per scene. That would let me tailor the same story to different ages without rebuilding.
