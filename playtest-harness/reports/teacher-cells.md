# teacher tests cells
*Model: meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo*

As I explored the Quilt cells with my students, I was delighted to see how engaged they were with the various kinds of cells, particularly the "music" and "chirp" cells, which allowed them to create unique sounds and patterns. The "hex" and "neighbor" cells also sparked interesting discussions about geometry and spatial relationships. However, I noticed that some students struggled to understand the purpose of the "audit" and "triage" cells, which seemed abstract and disconnected from the more interactive cells.

What frustrated me was the lack of clear instructions and learning objectives for each cell type. As a teacher, I wanted to ensure that my students were meeting specific standards and learning goals, but it was difficult to determine how each cell aligned with our curriculum. Despite this, I was impressed by the potential of the Quilt cells to facilitate creative problem-solving and critical thinking.
