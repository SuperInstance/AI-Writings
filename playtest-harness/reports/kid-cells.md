# kid tests cells
*Model: ByteDance/Seed-2.0-mini*

Hi, I’m Theo, 8! I curled up on my bedroom rug with my mom’s tablet for 15 minutes playing with the 26 quilt cells. My absolute faves were chirp, flock, chaos, thermal, and broadcast. Chirp made a tiny squeaky peep every time it touched a neighbor cell—when I lined three chirps next to each other they went peep-peep-peep and I snort-laughed so hard I spilled my fruit snacks. Flock cells swarmed all over the screen like a tiny bug colony when I dragged a twist cell near them, spinning and zooming across the edges. Thermal cells made warm yellow blobs that spread slowly across the screen, and when I added a twist next to them the blobs spun too! I dropped a broadcast cell next to a flock once, and all the swarm bugs lit up bright blue—super cool, even if I didn’t get why at first. I tried the voice cell too, but it only made a garbled blip instead of talking like I thought it would. Chaos cells made all the squares jump and wiggle nonstop; when my tabby cat Mochi jumped on the desk it kicked the mouse and made the chaos go totally nuts, so Mochi ran under the bed right away.

The boring, confusing ones were audit and partition. Audit just popped up a weird text box that said “checking system logs” and nothing fun happened no matter how much I clicked it. Partition just split the screen into tiny boxes and I fumbled to fix it for five whole minutes. I tried the music cell too, but it only made a quiet buzz instead of a real kid-friendly song. I got frustrated when I couldn’t make the flock cells follow my cursor exactly to make a tiny race track.

| Dimension | Score (1-10) | One-line reason |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 8 | The flock, chaos, and thermal bits made me giggle nonstop, I’d play again to make a cat-chaos show! |
| **Educational Value** | 3 | Most of the cells had grown-up words I didn’t get, I only learned some computer squares talk to each other. |
| **Modification Ease** | 2 | I couldn’t change any cells or make my own at all, just drag the ones given. |

My one concrete suggestion is to add simple pop-up tips when you click a cell that say easy things like “this makes peeps when next to other squares!” instead of using grown-up words I don’t understand like audit and partition.
