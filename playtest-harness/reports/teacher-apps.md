# teacher tests apps
*Model: meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo*

As I explored the Quilt production apps, I was delighted by the offline event signage app, which I could envision using for our school's events, such as parent-teacher conferences or science fairs. The app's ability to generate signage without internet connectivity was impressive. I also appreciated the exam integrity notepad, which could help maintain the integrity of our classroom assessments. The patch wall app, with its colorful and interactive display, could be a great tool for teaching students about different topics, such as fractions or geometry.

However, I was frustrated by the lack of clear instructions for some of the apps, such as the homelab alert wall. It took me a few minutes to figure out how to use it, and I worry that my students might struggle with it as well. Additionally, I noticed that some of the apps, like the blog tamper and newsroom witness, seemed more geared towards professional or business settings, rather than a K-8 classroom.

Here are my scores:

| Dimension | Score (1-10) | One-line reason |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 7 | Engaging, but some apps felt more like work than play. |
| **Educational Value** | 8 | Relevant to classroom use, but some apps needed more context. |

To improve these apps, I would suggest adding a "teacher mode" or a tutorial section that provides clear instructions and examples of how to use each app in a classroom setting, including ideas for incorporating them into lesson plans and assessments. This would help educators like myself to quickly understand the potential of each app and effectively integrate them into our teaching practices.
