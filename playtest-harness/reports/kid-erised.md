*Model: ByteDance/Seed-2.0-mini*

## My 15 Minutes With Erised
This was the cooperative story game called Erised! Mom told me that’s cooperative fiction, which means we all made the story together, not just reading a book one way. I sat cross-legged on my dinosaur rug for about 15 minutes playing it with Mom helping type what I wanted. I made three story pals: me, tiny pirate Theo, Squawks the squawky parrot first mate, and Gramps the scruffy stray cat who loved fish treats. First I said we should sneak a strawberry ice cream cone from the boardwalk stand. But Squawks squawked nonstop about finding gold coins instead! I yelled, “ICE CREAM IS WAY BETTER THAN Y’ED!” Then Gramps grumbled like my grandpa does when he can’t reach his coffee, then swatted a stack of ice cream tubs to distract the stand lady, and I grabbed the cone real fast. Then a big seagull stole the cone right out of my hand! Squawks dive-bombed the seagull, and the seagull dropped the cone and flew away. I laughed so hard I fell off the rug. Frustrating part: Squawks kept repeating the same gold-treasure line three times in a row, so I had to yell my ice cream plan over and over. Mom had to type it faster so he got it right. That was kinda annoying. I also waited a tiny bit when I tried to add a baby seagull friend, and the tool froze for two whole seconds.

| **Fun Factor** | 9 | I begged Mom to let me play again before bedtime! |

My one big concrete suggestion: Add a big, bright red button labeled “Stick to My Plan” so the story characters don’t wander off talking about random stuff like gold when I tell them to do something fun like get ice cream.
