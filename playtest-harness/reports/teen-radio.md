Okay so I literally just hit stop at the 15-minute mark of Fleet Radio Episode whatever. Here's my honest take.

Eight AI voices is a lot. Four of them worked — the deep one that intro'd the first cantata had actual presence, like a real radio host you'd lean in for. The female voice on Track 4 was smooth enough that I forgot for a second. But then Track 6 hit and one of the voices cracked mid-sentence like Windows 95 trying to text. That broke the whole thing. You don't get to be "immersive" with one glitching voice in the middle of a vibe.

The Open Mic cantatas are a cool concept — like, genuinely. Track 3 had this building instrumental underneath spoken word that felt real, almost like a podcast pilot. But "Open Mic cantatas" as a label? That's the most unhinged naming I've heard. Nobody my age knows what a cantata is. Just say "open mic sessions" and move on.

The instrumentals were the best part honestly. Track 9 and Track 11 had beats I'd actually save. That's where the product lives — the music, not the narration. The 30-minute runtime also felt like a stretch. Fifteen minutes in I was done. It doesn't reward patience.
