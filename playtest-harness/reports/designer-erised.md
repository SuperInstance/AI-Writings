**Fifteen Minutes in Erised's Story Engine**

Spent fifteen minutes dropping characters into Erised's narrative matrix yesterday—hooked me immediately. The dopamine hit from that first story beat was real: dropped a noir detective and a quantum physicist into what I'd pitched as a "routine orbital survey," hit go, watched them auto-generate their opening exchange about gravitational anomalies that sounded like actual character work instead of prompt templating. That's your core loop right there: input → emergent surprise → validation → repeat.

What delighted me was how the AI respected my framing without being slave to it. Those characters took my "tense professional relationship" seed and ran with it—added subtext, history, actual stakes. Felt like collaborative authoring without the usual human friction.

Frustration hit when the pacing collapsed into repetitive beats after minute eight. Same sentence structures, predictable emotional escalation patterns. Needed manual intervention every third exchange to shake things loose—that's your retention killer. Mechanical storytelling exposed.

The modification system's solid though; swapping character archetypes and scene parameters feels intuitive once you grok the YAML structure.
