# sped tests apps
*Model: mistralai/Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct-2506*

**Lived Experience: Mr. Kim Tests Quilt Production Apps**

As a special education teacher, I’m always looking for tools that adapt to diverse needs. When I tried these eight Quilt apps for 15 minutes, I noticed how they leveraged portable "cells" to create unique solutions—but some were more intuitive than others.

The **patch wall** and **homelab alert wall** delighted me. The guided tamper-evident features in the **invoices** and **exam notepad** felt reassuring, like a digital layer of security. But the **offline event signage** confused me—it lacked clear instructions for students with visual processing challenges.

Frustrations:
- **Complexity**: Some apps assumed prior tech knowledge, making them hard for students with executive function difficulties.
- **Accessibility**: No built-in screen reader compatibility or adjustable font sizes in the **newsroom witness** app.
- **Modification Limits**: The **blog tamper** app’s edits required coding, which isn’t feasible for most of my learners.

**Scores:**
| Dimension | Score (1-10) | One-line reason |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 7 | Engaging but needs clearer entry points. |
| **Educational Value** | 6 | Potential, but too niche for some. |
| **Modification Ease** | 4 | Only for advanced users. |

**One Suggestion:** Add a **"quick-edit mode"** to the patch wall and invoices with drag-and-drop options, so students with motor or cognitive limitations can customize workflows without coding.
