# kid tests apps
*Model: ByteDance/Seed-2.0-mini*

Hi, I’m Theo, 8! Today I tested the patch wall Quilt app at my library’s after-school maker club for exactly 15 minutes, like the grown-up helpers asked. The grown-up said this uses Quilt cells to keep all my patches saved, so I didn’t lose the triceratops sticker I drew last week! Drag-and-drop was so easy, it stuck right to the virtual quilt frame just like my mom’s fabric quilting pins. I added a rocket ship patch, a sparkly unicorn square, a glow-in-the-dark moon patch, and a pepperoni pizza patch from the app’s pre-made fun bits.

The best part was tapping the secret confetti button—gold sparkles exploded all over my quilt, making my friend Lila gasp! But ugh, I accidentally deleted the pizza patch when I clicked the tiny ‘delete’ circle instead of the ‘make bigger’ one. I pouted for 10 whole seconds. Worst of all, I couldn’t save my quilt to show my dad later: I clicked all the blue buttons and none said “save for later”. I had to take a blurry tablet photo instead, which made the sparkles look blobby.

| Dimension | Score (1-10) | One-line reason |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 9 | I wanted to stay and make a pizza-themed quilt instead of going to snack time! |
| **Educational Value** | 6 | I learned digital patches can move like real fabric quilt pieces! |
| **Modification Ease** | 7 | Moving patches was super simple, but deleting and saving was tricky |

My one concrete improvement idea: Make the save button big, pink, and shaped like a cupcake so little kids like me can spot it instantly, and add a thick sparkly yellow border around the delete button so I don’t accidentally tap it when I’m trying to make my patches bigger.
