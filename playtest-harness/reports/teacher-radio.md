As I played the Fleet Radio episode for 15 minutes in my classroom, I noticed that my students were initially engaged by the unique AI voices and the variety of tracks. The Open Mic cantatas sparked interesting discussions about poetry and creative writing. However, I found that the lack of clear learning objectives and supporting materials made it challenging to integrate the content into our existing curriculum.

What delighted me was the exposure my students received to different musical styles and the opportunity to think critically about the role of AI in creative expression. The instrumentals were a great accompaniment to our writing activity, helping students to focus and generate ideas. Nevertheless, I was frustrated by the abrupt transitions between tracks and the limited contextual information provided about the AI voices and their backgrounds.
