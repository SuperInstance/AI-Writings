# kid tests music
*Model: ByteDance/Seed-2.0-mini*

Yesterday I got to mess with the music composition demo for 15 whole minutes! At first, the screen was a big grid of bright, squishy-looking colored cells, like a giant sticker sheet but on a laptop. I poked the first sky-blue cell I saw, and a soft string note played, plus the word “sparrows” popped up in the side witness log box. I cackled when I clicked the orange button next—it added a wobbly little drum thump, and the log got two lines long, making the chord sound way fuller and cooler.

I messed around for ages, clicking every color I found: purple made a cat meow with a cat witness, green made a dog bark, and yellow made a silly whistle noise. The only frustrating part was when I accidentally clicked a blue cell twice, and the sparrow witness vanished along with its soft string note. I panicked so hard I almost knocked over my strawberry fruit snack bowl right on the keyboard!

The best part was when I had three cells clicked at once, and the chord sounded just like the lullaby my grandma sings to me before bed. I sat there rearranging which cells I’d clicked until I made a spooky, boomy chord that made my little brother run sprinting in to check what I was doing. I even tried clicking all the cells at once, but that made a really loud, messy noise that made our golden retriever hide under the couch. I wished there was a way to fix my accidental clicks and pick my own animal witnesses.

| Dimension | Score (1-10) | One-line reason |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 10 | I begged my mom to let me keep playing even when it was time to go play outside |
| **Educational Value** | 7 | I learned that stacking lots of little individual sounds makes a big, pretty chord |
| **Modification Ease** | 6 | I could change the song by clicking cells, but I couldn’t make my own new cells or pick my own witnesses |

My one concrete suggestion for improvement is to add a big, squishy red undo button placed in the far bottom corner so I don’t click it by accident and erase my favorite sounds, plus add a simple picker menu so I can choose a T-Rex, dragon, or even a pizza as a witness instead of only getting the default sparrows and cats.
