**15 Minutes with Quilt: A Designer's Firsthand**

Diving into Quilt felt like unwrapping a mechanic's toolkit disguised as magic. The portable inventory guardian hooked me immediately—watching my digital assets get real-time protection triggered genuine satisfaction. That's your core loop: act, see consequence, feel reward. Dopamine hit, check.

The patch wall surprised me next. Each cell update felt like placing a tile in a mosaic, except the tiles were working software pieces. My inner completionist wanted to connect everything. Tamper-evident invoices especially delighted—seeing blockchain-like transparency from simple spreadsheets felt powerful.

But friction emerged. The exam integrity notepad's interface fought me. Too many clicks between thought and action breaks flow state—fatal for retention. The newsroom witness app showed promise but hid its best features in menus I didn't think to explore first.

What impressed most was how quickly these became tools, not tutorials. Within minutes I was solving problems, not just learning concepts. That's educational design done right.

The offline event signage frustrated me least but excited me most—imagine physical spaces that update themselves. That's where modification ease becomes crucial.
