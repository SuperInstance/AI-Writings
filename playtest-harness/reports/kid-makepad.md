# kid tests makepad
*Model: ByteDance/Seed-2.0-mini*

Hi, I’m Theo, 8! Yesterday I sat at my big kid’s computer and played with the Quilt-Makepad demo for a whole 15 minutes. There were 9 plain gray squares on the screen, and a big sign that said “Click to BIND.” I clicked the top-left square first, and it lit up bright blue! I clicked the square right next to it, and that one turned purple too—wait, the site called that binding, like gluing them together? I clicked all the rest one by one, and every square lit up a different color, then a wiggly line stretched all across the screen when I hit TICK. That made me snort-laugh!

I tried to make a tiny square quilt shape by clicking four corner squares, but when I hit TICK it just made a jumbled straight line instead of my pattern. Frustrating! I kept pressing TICK over and over just to see the wiggly lines pop up again, but I accidentally clicked a square I didn’t mean to halfway through, and I couldn’t unbind it without restarting the whole thing. The witness log popped up all these weird words I didn’t get, like JSON, and when I tried exporting my quilt, nothing saved to my laptop. I wanted to show my creation to my grandma!

| Dimension | Score (1-10) | One-line reason |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Fun Factor** | 7 | I wanted to keep making silly wiggly patterns even when I messed up |
| **Educational Value** | 3 | I only learned clicking squares makes colored lines, nothing useful for school or home |
| **Modification Ease** | 2 | I couldn’t change the square colors or add more squares at all |

My one concrete fix: Add a big, bright undo button to fix accidental clicked squares, and make the export button save a colorful picture of my quilt instead of a confusing code file.
