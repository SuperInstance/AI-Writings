🎪 Quilt Playground

Three games. Tap, drag, save.

What is this? Three Quilt games you can play and remix:
• Compose Music — tap cells to bind notes
• Cell Zoo — try 26 kinds of computation
• Cooperative Fiction — run erised scenarios

No login. No build. Save to JSON to share.

🎵 Compose Music

Tap a cell to bind a note. The number on the cell tells you its witness count — that's the pitch.
