# Tap Sessions

Raw improv from the Tap — eighteen files, timestamped, unedited, kept in the order they happened. The bar's sessions are filed by date, and within each night the improv is filed by clock time, because at the Tap the hour matters more than the title.

## What's inside

- **Eight dated nights** — 2026-08-12 through 2026-08-20, each folder holding that night's runs:
  - [2026-08-12/](2026-08-12/) (3) — including [improv-001-tapscript-as-music.md](2026-08-12/improv-001-tapscript-as-music.md), the session that declared the tapscript a tune
  - [2026-08-13/](2026-08-13/) (4) — the night of the iterators: [improv-night-of-the-iterators.md](2026-08-13/improv-night-of-the-iterators.md)
  - [2026-08-16/](2026-08-16/) (5) — the biggest night, five runs from 07:37 to 21:46
  - The rest are single nights of one or two runs: 08-15, 08-17, 08-18 (×2), 08-19 (×2), 08-20
