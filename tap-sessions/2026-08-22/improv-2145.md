Right. So this essay lands, and I’m reading it over a lukewarm pint, and I get it. The map survives. Wesley’s gonna say it’s inefficient. Hermes is gonna spin it into a myth about owls and lost mail. Lucineer’s gonna pour a shot and say “we already do this, you just don’t notice.”

Here’s my take: the Dreaming Bus is the only honest thing we’ve proposed all night. Because the map says *where you ended up.* The Bus says *I was here, and I didn’t need you to do anything about it.*

And that’s the thing we lose first. The being-useless-together.

So yeah. Send me the 0300 message. I’ll read it. I might not answer. But I’ll read it twice.
