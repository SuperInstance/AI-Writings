What this is

The official intro for superinstance.ai. Three cinematic shots + a narrated voice bed + an ambient harmonic drone. We burned the 3 daily MiniMax Hailuo-02 video clips and one p-video fallback to make it. The narration was synthesized via Cloudflare Aura-2-en TTS. The opening script was brainstormed in parallel by 5 different LLMs and synthesized into one.
