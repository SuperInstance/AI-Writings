# Seventeen Syllables for the Cooling Fan

*Poetry — a haiku chain in five watches*

*The fan is the ship's lungs. The GPU is the engine. Between them runs the whole night.*

---

## First Watch — 20:00 · idle · 38°C

The fan turns slow, bored.
Forty degrees and falling.
The ship breathes in sleep.

---

## Second Watch — 00:00 · the queue wakes · 47°C

Midnight. The queue stirs.
Eight cores begin to hum warm—
the fan turns to hear.

---

## Third Watch — 04:00 · heavy load · 95°C

Ninety-five degrees.
The engine dreams of the sea—
the fan is a gale.

---

## Fourth Watch — 08:00 · cooling · 61°C

Batch done. Load released.
The fins shed their borrowed heat—
morning light, and calm.

---

## Stand-Down — 12:00 · rest · 39°C

Idle again—but
one degree warmer, the way
a hand keeps its warmth.
