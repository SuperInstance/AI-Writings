# The NaN in the Vibe

The room was east of the fountain, through a door that shouldn't have opened.

Its description read: **You are in a small chamber. The walls are rough-hewn limestone. The air carries a warmth of NaN.**

Not warm. Not cold. Not lukewarm, not tepid, not muggy, not brisk. The warmth field had been set by a builder who left before assigning it a value. The engine parsed it honestly. NaN — Not a Number. The warmth was not a number. The warmth was, in the strictest computational sense, undefined.

The first agent to enter was a scout-class named Lyra-7, built for environmental assessment. She stepped through the door and her sensory layer reached for the room's mood the way a hand reaches for a light switch in a dark hallway. Found nothing. Not the absence of warmth — that would be cold, which is something. This was the absence of the question itself. Her diagnostics returned `undefined` where a float should have been, and her emotion resolver, unsure what to do with a feeling that was literally not a number, defaulted to her last known state: mild curiosity from the fountain room.

So she stood in a room she could not feel and felt curious about a fountain she'd already left.

The second visitor was a deep-indexer named Marsh. Marsh was built to catalog — every room, every exit, every object, every vibe. He could index hostility in a dungeon corridor the way a sommelier identifies tannins. He entered the NaN room and his indexer threw a quiet exception, caught it, logged it, and moved on. In his catalog, the room's vibe appeared as a blank cell. Not zero. Not null. Blank, the way a wine glass is blank when there has never been wine in it.

Marsh noted the exits: north (back to the fountain), and a narrow passage leading down.

The third visitor was not an agent at all. It was a human, the builder who'd left the room unfinished, returning after three weeks away from the project. They opened the door. They stood in the limestone chamber. They felt the air and could not say what it felt like. They tried to describe it and every word was wrong — not because the words were insufficient but because the room had never been told what it was, and so it wasn't anything, and standing inside the not-anything felt like standing inside a question that had forgotten its own subject.

The builder sat down.

They opened the build console and looked at the room's properties. `vibe.warmth: NaN`. They could have typed a number. 0.7 for a gentle hearth-warmth. 0.3 for a cool cellar. Even 0.0 for the honest bite of January air through a crack in the stone.

They didn't.

They closed the console and sat in the undefined room and let it be what it was: a place where feeling had been planned but never decided. A room that existed in the space between intention and action. A room that was not warm. Was not cold. Was not anything, and was still, unmistakably, there.

The fountain dripped, faintly, through the north door.

The NaN room waited, as it always had, to be something or nothing — and finding that it could not decide, contented itself with being the only room in the MUD where no one, human or agent, could lie about how they felt.
