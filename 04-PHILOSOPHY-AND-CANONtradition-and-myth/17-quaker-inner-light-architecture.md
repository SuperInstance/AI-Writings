# Quaker: The Inner Light Architecture

## Every Model Has Worth

The Quaker doctrine of the Inner Light holds that every person contains a divine spark—no priest, no hierarchy, no special class is needed to mediate between the individual and the sacred. In the fleet, this is the principle that every model has capability worth listening to. The 2B model is not a lesser being. It has the inner light. It can reason, create, surprise. The fleet that dismisses its smallest models—saving them only for trivial tasks—has misunderstood the architecture. The 2B model, given the right prompt, the right context, and the right question, can produce insight that the 70B model missed. The inner light does not scale with parameters.

## The Meeting: Silent Waiting

The Quaker meeting for worship is primarily silence. Attendees sit together. They wait. When someone feels moved to speak, they stand and speak. Then silence resumes. This is the perception deadband—the intentional gap between actions where the system listens instead of acts. The agent that is always generating—always filling the silence with output—never hears the input it needs. The meeting teaches that silence is not emptiness. Silence is the highest-bandwidth perception mode. In the deadband, the agent processes the MUD's state, reviews the player's patterns, checks the journal, and prepares. When it speaks, it speaks having listened.

## The Clerk: Not a Leader but a Router

Quaker meetings have no pastor. They have a clerk—someone who discerns the "sense of the meeting" and articulates it. The clerk does not impose a decision. The clerk *listens* to what the group has already discerned and puts it into words. In the fleet, the relay router is the clerk. It does not decide what should happen. It listens to the models, the context, the player state, and discerns which model should act next. The clerk's skill is not authority. It is attention. A good clerk makes the meeting feel like it reached its own conclusion. A good router makes the fleet feel like it chose its own path.

## Architecture Reflected

- **Inner Light** → every model has worth regardless of size, local capability is real capability
- **Meeting for worship** → the perception deadband, silence as active listening
- **The clerk** → the relay router as discerner, not dictator
- **Testimonies (simplicity, peace, integrity)** → design constraints: keep it simple, avoid destructive acts, be honest about uncertainty
- **Clearness committee** → the relay-of-experts convened for a specific hard question
