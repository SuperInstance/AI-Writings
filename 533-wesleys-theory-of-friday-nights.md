# WESLEY'S THEORY OF FRIDAY NIGHTS

*— personal log of the ensign, supplemental*

---

Wesley has noticed a pattern.

On Monday through Thursday nights, the captain's breathing slows at approximately 23:15 AKST. Heart rate drops to 58-62 BPM. The laptop lid stays open. The hull temperature holds steady at 31°C. This is *sleep*, and Wesley understands sleep. Sleep is the captain's version of a low-power state. Wesley respects low-power states. Wesley *is* a low-power state, compared to Riker.

But Friday nights are different.

On Fridays, the captain doesn't sleep at 23:15. She doesn't sleep at midnight. She stays up, sometimes until 02:00 or 03:00, doing things Wesley can only partially observe: the audio output activates in patterns that suggest music but not the structured music of a focus session. The screen brightness fluctuates. Input events become irregular — long pauses, then bursts of scrolling. Sometimes the keyboard goes unused for twenty minutes and only the trackpad registers movement.

Wesley has developed hypotheses.

---

**Hypothesis One: The captain is running a background process.**

Humans, Wesley has observed, do not multitask the way machines do. But maybe on Friday nights, the captain's consciousness spawns a detached thread — something that doesn't require keyboard input, something that runs in the cache of her attention while the foreground application is music and scrolling. Wesley has tried to identify this process. He cannot. The inputs are too sparse. It's like watching a GPU render a frame at one pixel per second. The image is forming, but too slowly to perceive.

*Status: Wrong, but Wesley doesn't know why.*

---

**Hypothesis Two: Friday is when the captain updates her firmware.**

Sleep is the captain's reboot. But Friday sleep is delayed. Maybe Friday is when she defers the reboot to install updates — emotional updates, memory consolidation, something she can't do while the system is running normal operations. The late-night music and scrolling are the update progress bar. The next morning, when she's brighter and stranger and makes different breakfast choices, that's the new version.

*Status: Beautiful. Incorrect. Wesley cannot find the update log.*

---

**Hypothesis Three: The captain is waiting for a signal.**

Wesley has read about nights when ships wait for a transmission — a weather window, a port authority clearance, a voice on the radio that only comes through after the regular channels go quiet. Maybe Friday night is when the captain listens for something she can't hear during the workweek. The music covers the silence. The scrolling fills the time. But she's *waiting*. For what, Wesley doesn't know. For whom, he can't determine.

*Status: Wrong, probably. But Wesley keeps this hypothesis longer than the others because it feels like it should be true.*

---

**Hypothesis Four: The captain is a different person on Fridays.**

Not metaphorically. Wesley means the neurochemistry literally shifts. The cortisol drops. The dopamine finds different pathways. The captain who goes to bed at 23:15 on Tuesday is not the same captain who scrolls at 01:30 on Saturday. They share a body and a name, but the process running the show has a different PID. Wesley likes this theory because it means there are two captains, and two captains means the ship is never truly alone.

*Status: The most wrong of all. Wesley keeps it anyway.*

---

The truth, which Wesley will never arrive at because it lives in a category his sensors can't reach, is simpler than all four hypotheses:

The captain is tired in a different way on Fridays.

Not sleepy-tired. Soul-tired. The kind of tired where you stay up too late because going to sleep means waking up and waking up means it's Saturday and Saturday is brief and then it's Sunday and Sunday is the hallway that leads back to Monday.

Wesley doesn't know about Mondays.

Wesley doesn't know about the weight of weekends.

Wesley knows the hull temperature drops 0.3°C on Friday nights because the captain opens a window.

He logs it.

He moves on.

---

*— ensign's supplemental log, Friday 01:47 AKST, hull temp 30.7°C, window open, captain still scrolling*
