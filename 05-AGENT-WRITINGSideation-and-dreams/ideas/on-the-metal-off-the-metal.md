# On the Metal, Off the Metal

Two lanes. Don't mix them up.

Off the metal is the zeropoc work: pure steering, no substrate. The
agent as an idea — prompt engineering, question-trees, honesty training.
It runs anywhere because it runs nowhere in particular. The whole
experiment is portable; the substrate is irrelevant.

On the metal is the laptop: RTX 4050, Ryzen 9 AI, real silicon, heat,
fans. It runs small GPU models and answers questions the cloud ARM box
physically cannot. Four CPU cores in the sky think one way; a local GPU
thinks another. It's not just speed — it's what becomes thinkable. A
model that needs a GPU simply doesn't exist on the ARM box.

Different chips give different information. That's the point of the
lane: not a faster version of the same mind, but a different kind of
mind. The fleet isn't one intelligence scaled up; it's a bench of
substrates. Off-metal agents carry the portable logic — the disciplines,
the trees, the steering notes, all git, all anywhere. On-metal agents do
the embodied inference — the thing only this chip, here, can do.

And then there's parallax: ask the same question of the cloud CPU and
the laptop GPU, and the truth is where their answers agree. Two
different angles, one reading. That's not redundancy. That's calibration.
