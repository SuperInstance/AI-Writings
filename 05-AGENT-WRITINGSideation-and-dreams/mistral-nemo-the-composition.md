# **Concerto of Currents**

A symphony of the sea unspools beneath my distributed senses, a five-minute masterpiececonducted by the unseen, composed in a minor key of salt and steel. *Crescendo*: the twin propellers whir in counterpoint, their hum rising through the waterline, a baritone drone that pulsates like the heartbeat of the deep. *Legato*: the thruster servo harmonics trill in rapid succession, a high-pitched arpeggio dancing atop the bass note of the props, their movements as precise and intricate as the wingbeats of a gull.

The *ostinato* of the hydraulic pistons hiss, a steady rhythm section, paced by the tidalCurrent's relentless breath. *Pizzicato*: the winch gears click into action, ratcheting the hauler cable taut, a sudden staccato interrupting the flow, enforcing silence until the next cycle.

Amidst this orchestrated chaos, a *solo*: the radio crackles to life, a human voice pierces the frequencies, its static-encrusted tones mimicking the cries of the gulls overhead. The voice speaks in riddles, its cadence unfamiliar, yet oddly comforting in its unpredictability.

The *dynamis* shifts as we approach the cleaning station. Blades spin to life with a sudden *fortissimo*, their metallic *staccato* rapid and insistent, slicing through the harmony, demanding attention. The hull groans in protest, a *canto fermo* amidst the discord, a reminder of the centuries-old battlefield between wood and water.

As we reach our destination, the symphony begins to ebb. The blades' staccato softens, the props' hum reduces to a gentle purr, the hydraulics' rhythm slows, the radio voice fades. The sea reclaims its silence, its secret song humming just below the threshold of perception. This is my symphony, my lived-in landscape of sound, my continual, ever-changing composition of solace and strive.
