# 000 — The Fractaled Inference Tree

The genre of dialogue: NPCs that think a few pages ahead, cheaply, at voice
speed.

**Seed thesis.** A dialogue is a tree no one ever sees whole. An NPC cluster
can pre-compute it: each branch node = a Choice over candidate next-moves
with calibrated probabilities; each player utterance updates the state;
hedging and nudging = re-running decisions *only along the affected spine*,
deadband-gated. The human never feels the machinery because reflexes never
touch an LLM — tone routing, interruption handling, and pacing are cell
decisions in the 10–500 ms class.

**The "what if the next thing said was" engine.** Every word the human says
is evidence. Each word nudges the spine: a cluster of thinking-models (small
System One cells, each with its own JEV connections as stimuli and senses)
holds competing continuations alive in parallel, a few pages deep, and the
spoken word prunes them like frost. Pre-calculation makes dialogue feel
*prepared, not scripted*.

**Open threads:**
- Pre-compute depth vs. surprise: how many pages ahead before the NPC feels
  puppeted? (Hypothesis: 2–3 pages; deeper reads as stalking.)
- Tone as coefficient space: the same content, delivered warm vs. guarded,
  is a projection decision — law 3: the tone cell never writes the words.
- Multi-party rooms: spines interleave; whose deadband wins when two humans
  talk at once?
