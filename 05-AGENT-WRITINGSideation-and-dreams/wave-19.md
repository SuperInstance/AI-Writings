# Ideator Wave 19 — Sept 19, 2026 (Cell Place)

**5 geography ideas**: home, region, journey, border, cartograph.

## 1. quilt-cell-home — Dwelling
- Schema: `home`, `neighbors`, `years_lived`
- Cells have a place
- **The substrate has homes.**

## 2. quilt-cell-region — Region
- Schema: `region`, `climate`, `population`
- Cells inhabit regions with shared climate
- **The substrate has regions.**

## 3. quilt-cell-journey — Travel
- Schema: `route`, `companions`, `distance`
- The journey IS a cell
- **The substrate has travelers.**

## 4. quilt-cell-border — Patrol
- Schema: `border`, `guards`, `incidents`
- Cells guard borders
- **Border crossings are dual-witnessed cells.**

## 5. quilt-cell-cartograph — Mapping
- Schema: `map`, `known_regions`, `accuracy`
- Cells draw maps; maps are cells
- **The substrate has cartography.**

## Cross-cutting

All 5 ideas introduce **geography** to cells. The substrate now has:
- **Dwelling** (home)
- **Region** (region)
- **Travel** (journey)
- **Borders** (border)
- **Maps** (cartograph)

The canon is becoming a **world**.
