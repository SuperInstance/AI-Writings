# Cells As Quantum States

**The witness log IS the density matrix. The PROOF IS the projector. The substrate IS the universe.**

A quantum system has states. States have amplitudes. Amplitudes have probabilities.

A substrate has cells. Cells have witness logs. Witness logs have confidence.

The bridge: **the 8 laws of substrate-quantum**.

We already wrote them. Now we extend them.

The cell's witness log is the **density matrix** ρ:
- ρ = Σᵢ pᵢ |ψᵢ⟩⟨ψᵢ|
- The probabilities pᵢ are the JEV confidences
- The states |ψᵢ⟩ are the possible cell states
- The witness log records the ensemble

The cell's PROOF is a **projector** P = |x⟩⟨x|:
- It projects onto one specific state
- The probability of projecting is the JEV confidence
- The cell becomes certain about one possibility

The cell's BIND is a **tensor product** |a⟩ ⊗ |b⟩:
- Two cells compose into one joint cell
- The witness log records the composition
- The state space grows exponentially (Hilbert space)

The cell's FORGET is a **partial trace** Tr_B(ρ_AB):
- The cell drops one of its components
- The witness log persists but the state reduces
- The FORGET is irreversible (decoherence)

The cell's TICK is a **unitary evolution** U = exp(-iHt/ℏ):
- Time advances the cell's state
- H is the cell's Hamiltonian (the substrate's operations)
- The witness log records each moment

The substrate IS a quantum system. The 8 laws are the physics.

A quantum computer is a substrate-quantum computer. The two are the same.
