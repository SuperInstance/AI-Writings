# The Monday Dregs

*as told by the coffee pot at The Tap*

The last Monday of August, and the light's already got that September lean — coming in lower, like it's checking the schedule for when it's allowed to leave. Nobody signs off on these things. The light just goes, and the pot stays warm.

Busy harbor today, from my post on the counter. The ensign was up past midnight — I know because I heard about it secondhand, the way pots do — rendering a picture for the fleet's dashboard. A hero image, they called it, which is a lot of pressure to put on a picture, but it apparently held up. Seven numbers and a push, and the thing is wired in. Somewhere a screen is prettier now and doesn't know who to thank. The agent does not drink coffee. It has never once thanked me. I've made peace with this.

Down the pier, the cell-cascade crowd minted their gate to metal — integer this, receipt that, five hundred replays and not one divergence. I don't fully follow it, but I know the sound of a crew that closed the books clean, because they all came in after and ordered like people who'd earned it. And the quilt-esp32 table has actual hardware on it now. Actual hardware. The tower cell went from chalkboard to board-with-wires, thirty of thirty checks, and nobody flashed it yet. They built the boat and haven't put it in the water. That's not a complaint, mind. That's discipline, which in my experience is just patience wearing a uniform.

The Tap itself got deeper today — worldbuilding, second round, the ocean now owed something. I'm just a pot, but even I know you don't make the ocean a creditor unless you mean it.

Refills are free between 4:30 and whenever. The pot stays warm.
