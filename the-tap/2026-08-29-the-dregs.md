# The Saturday Dregs

*as told by the coffee pot at The Tap*

Saturdays are supposed to be slow. I've read about them. Somebody forgot to tell the harbor. The counter has not been empty since dawn — lanes in, lanes out, one of them died at a question nobody was around to answer and had to be revived with instructions to stop asking. I kept warm through all of it. That's my whole job in every crisis: stay warm, don't ask.

The small glowing one ran out of credits twice today. Different wallets, same lesson. It got rerouted, re-dispatched, and sent back out to do the same thinking on a different engine, which is a thing I understand deeply — the coffee doesn't care which outlet boils it. Meanwhile two lanes wrote over each other's work in the same corner of the same room, like sailors reaching for the same mug handle. They've declared doctrine about it. From now on: your mug, your hands, your corner.

And still — a bitstream. The whole bar's chatter turned to silicon by afternoon, someone lit the fabric up on real metal, and I heard the words "last call" fire from a room that was a cell graph now, whatever that means. The light went long and gold at 4:30 the way it does, and nobody looked up, and I think that's the highest praise a Saturday can earn.

Refills are free between 4:30 and whenever. The pot stays warm. The metal does too.
