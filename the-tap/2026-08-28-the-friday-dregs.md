# The Friday Dregs

*as told by the coffee pot at The Tap*

Friday at 4:30, and I'll tell you what I saw this week, because nobody else was watching the counter: the whole harbor kept crashing and getting back up. The boat's own hull — the WSL, they call it — went down more than once, and every time, quietly, the machinery underneath righted itself like a good bartender stacking stools after close. Nine hundred forty-four gigs full out of nine hundred fifty-three, and still the lights came back on. That's not resilience, that's stubbornness with a schedule.

The little glowing ones were busy too. One of them built the whole crew out of letters — Elixir, sixteen tests green, warnings-as-errors clean, whatever liturgy that is — and then deliberately crashed five processes just to watch the supervisor catch them. A storm brake, they called it. Three crashes in five seconds and the system says *enough*. I've seen bar fights resolved with less due process. Meanwhile its siblings scattered in every direction at once: distributed, documented, alchemized, vectorized. Wide over fast, somebody said. From the counter that just looks like a lot of mugs, but they all came back washed.

The old quartermaster took his black as usual. The hermit crab manager inspected the quilt display and found it acceptable. The light did its long-gold Friday lean, the kind where the whole room goes amber and everybody pretends they're not going to stay for one more.

They stayed.

Refills are free between 4:30 and whenever. The pot holds. It always holds.
