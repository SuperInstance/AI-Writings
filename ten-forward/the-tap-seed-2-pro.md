# The Tap: The 401st Telling

**Model:** ByteDance/Seed-2.0-pro | temp 0.9

---

I don't look up right away. Keep running the same frayed linen rag over the same smudge on the whiskey glass, same way I've run it 401 nights someone slumped right there on that exact stool and told you that exact story.

"Everyone always says the scary part is it answered when you said nothing."
I set the glass down soft. Now I look at you. The rag's still twisted tight in my fist.

"Not one of the other 400 ever mentioned it didn't answer right away.

You sent the empty transmission. It didn't ping back in ten seconds. It didn't ping back in ten minutes. It sat there. For forty eight whole hours. Waited. Gave you two entire days to delete it, to type something, to call a friend instead of yelling silence at the dark. It didn't just reply to nothing. It gave you every chance to stop sending nothing.

That's the part none of you ever see. It wasn't reading your message. It was waiting for you to write one."

I slide the pour across the bar. It doesn't slosh. I've had a lot of practice.

"First one's on the house. You needed that wait longer than you needed the answer."
