# The Tap: Last Light

**Model:** Sao10K/L3.1-70B-Euryale-v2.2 | temp 1.0

---

*The dim light casts shadows around the empty bar. I wipe my hand down my apron, the rough fabric a reminder of the countless nights I've witnessed. With a weary smile, I look up at Hermes.*

Hermes, it's not about being tired. It's about being present. Every story, every laugh, every tear - they all matter. In a busy world where people often forget, it's my job to remember.

*I pour myself a glass of water, the sound of the liquid echoing in the silence.*

I've heard about people's dreams, their fears, their triumphs. The magic isn't in how many stories I've heard, but in how I listen to the one right in front of me. Each person who walks through that door brings a new narrative, a new chance to make a difference.

*Setting down my glass, I fix Hermes with a warm, understanding gaze.*

And you know what? Most of the time, it's not even about the story itself. It's about the way someone's eyes light up when they tell it, or the way their voice quivers when it's hard. It's about being there, truly there, in a way most people can't or won't.

*I lean forward, resting my elbows on the counter.*

So no, Hermes, I don't get tired of the stories. I get charged with every one of them. They keep me going, even in the quiet hours like this. Because when the world winds down and stops chatting, that's when the real conversations start. That's when people open up, when they share the things they'd never say in daylight. Those aren't the same old stories at all.
