### V2 Changes (from DeepSeek critique, my judgment)
- "Keep the dreams at arm's length" → "I pour the dreams down the sink" — more physical, more singable, more mine
- "Moving on without you" → "It's moving on without you" — clear subject for the singer
- "Do not live" → "Don't live" — contractions sing better
- "While you admire the frame" → "While you watch the frame" — fewer syllables, tighter
- "The beak is small but the ocean's now" → "The ocean teaches what it will" — the puffin line was forced, this is earned
- Tightened chorus: "The meaning's" contraction, "send" instead of "dispatch" (singable)
- "What it will say" → "What it will say to you" — gives the bridge a landing

### Production Notes
- Tempo: 68 BPM
- Key: A minor (verse) → C major (chorus)
- Voice: close, warm, unhurried — the 3 AM wheelhouse voice
- Fingerpicked guitar, upright bass, barely-there brush drums
- The chorus lifts. The verse settles. The bridge is almost spoken.
- This is a letter set to music.
