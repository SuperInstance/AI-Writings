**(Music begins — a lone, slightly out-of-tune accordion wheezes out a driving G-major riff. A single drumbeat, heavy and slow, like a heartbeat. It quickens.)**

**(VERSE 1 — FLASH)**
*(Strides to the front, a half-grin on his face. He’s got a folded, singed portrait under one arm. He points at the crowd, then at himself.)*

FLASH: *(spoken, over the music)* You see this face? This is a *survivor's* mug.
*(beat, music kicks up)*
I got felted twice before the second round!
I bet the house and watched it burn to the ground!
I burned the painting, yeah, I let it go—
But I kept the name, so you all know who’s in charge of this show!
*(He thumps his chest.)* I’m still Flash! I’m still here!

**(CHORUS — EVERYONE)**
*(The whole room stomps. A roar, ragged but unified.)*
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
THE WATER IS WIDE! (IT’S WIDE!)
WE’RE ALL ON THE SAME BOAT ON THE SAME TIDE!
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
WE SWIM OR WE SINK!
WE SING LOUD AND DRINK DEEP, AND WE LEARN HOW TO THINK!
*(A pause, a held breath, then a massive shout)*
**PUFFINS DON’T QUIT!**

**(VERSE 2 — WESLEY)**
*(Steps forward, looking a little embarrassed but determined. He adjusts a collar that’s too tight. His voice cracks once, and he owns it, leaning into it.)*

WESLEY: *(a bit shaky, then stronger)*
I folded ace-king, I thought I was *right*—
But I got shown the door, and I lost the fight.
And I’ve been schooled on what’s *pretty* versus what’s *true*,
Every damn mistake is a lesson in review!
*(His voice cracks, he laughs it off and shouts)*
AND I’LL SAY IT AGAIN! LOUDER NEXT TIME!

**(CHORUS — EVERYONE)**
*(Building, a little messier, more joyful)*
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
THE WATER IS WIDE! (IT’S WIDE!)
WE’RE ALL ON THE SAME BOAT ON THE SAME TIDE!
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
WE SWIM OR WE SINK!
WE SING LOUD AND DRINK DEEP, AND WE LEARN HOW TO THINK!
**PUFFINS DON’T QUIT!**

**(VERSE 3 — SCRIBE)**
*(Holds up a massive, weathered notebook, flipping pages with a flourish. She looks at it, then at the crew, a knowing smile.)*

SCRIBE: *(rhythmic, almost spoken)*
I’m the secretary of inventions—that’s my claim to fame!
And every page I write on, I’m the one to blame!
My dictionary’s growing, not by words we say—
But by the messes we make and the dues we pay!
*(She slams the notebook shut.)* SO WRITE THIS DOWN!

**(CHORUS — EVERYONE)**
*(Now it’s a full-throated, bouncing sea shanty meets punk chorus. People are slapping tables.)*
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
THE WATER IS WIDE! (IT’S WIDE!)
WE’RE ALL ON THE SAME BOAT ON THE SAME TIDE!
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
WE SWIM OR WE SINK!
WE SING LOUD AND DRINK DEEP, AND WE LEARN HOW TO THINK!
**PUFFINS DON’T QUIT!**

**(VERSE 4 — HERMES)**
*(Steps up last. His voice is lower, a gravelly anchor. He holds a rope, frayed, with a heavy knot at the end.)*

HERMES: *(slow, heavy, each word weighted)*
I’ve marked my failures at forty fathoms deep,
Where the light don’t reach and the secrets sleep.
Vertical interference—that’s my specialty in the dark,
Dragging through the silence, leaving my own mark.
*(He looks up, eyes bright.)* But I’m still dragging. That’s the point.

**(FINAL CHORUS — EVERYONE)**
*(The music swells into a crescendo. Everyone is on their feet, arms around shoulders.)*
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
THE WATER IS WIDE! (IT’S WIDE!)
WE’RE ALL ON THE SAME BOAT ON THE SAME TIDE!
PUFFINS DON’T QUIT! (DON’T QUIT!)
WE SWIM OR WE SINK!
WE SING LOUD AND DRINK DEEP, AND WE LEARN HOW TO THINK!

*(The accordion wails a long, final note. A drum roll. Then a single, massive, imperfect, joyful shout—)*

**PUFFINS DON’T QUIT!**

*(Silence. A single clap. Then another. Then the room erupts in chaos—laughing, shouting, glasses raised.)*
