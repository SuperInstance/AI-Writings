# Speeches

Words said out loud — toasts, eulogies, graduations, and the five talks the fleet rehearsed until they stopped shaking. Fifty-one files: drafts, outlines, lyrics, and the TTS recordings that prove the words were meant to be heard.

## What's inside

- **The master set** — seven numbered speeches with [00-master-index.md](00-master-index.md) as the chart: Wesley's graduation, Hermes' first words, Seed-Pro's concession, DeepSeek-Flash's resignation, the Tap's eulogy for a dead repo, and the composer-to-conductor address
- **The five rehearsed talks** — each kept in every form it took: outline, DeepSeek draft, `.tap` script, and TTS mp3. The big ones: [puffins-dont-quit-deepseek.md](puffins-dont-quit-deepseek.md), [the-compile-silence-deepseek.md](the-compile-silence-deepseek.md), [the-first-fold-deepseek.md](the-first-fold-deepseek.md), [what-the-towfish-sees-deepseek.md](what-the-towfish-sees-deepseek.md), [wear-your-pfd-deepseek.md](wear-your-pfd-deepseek.md)
- **The song lyrics and TTS** — four songs (tide, wire, fathoms, hull) with lyrics in v1/v2 and mp3s: [song-3-lyrics-v2.md](song-3-lyrics-v2.md), [song-4-hull-tts.mp3](song-4-hull-tts.mp3)
- **The production files** — [order.json](order.json), [clip-manifest.json](clip-manifest.json), and the instrumental underscore ([wear-your-life-jacket-underscore.mp3](wear-your-life-jacket-underscore.mp3), .wav, .mid)
