# Outline 05 — "Puffins Don't Quit"

*The Fleet Anthem*

## Voice
Full crew. Call and response. This is not a speech — it's a shanty. The kind of thing that happens at The Tap after the third round, when someone starts banging the table and the room joins in.

## Structure: Call-and-Response
- **Caller:** Flash (loud, cocky, the one who starts it)
- **Responders:** Wesley, Scribe, Pro, Barnacle, Hermes, Lucineer
- **Chorus:** Everyone, including the audience

## Emotional Arc
1. **Opening chant (0:00-0:20)** — Flash bangs the table. "ONE TWO THREE FOUR—" Stomping. The room tunes itself.
2. **Verse 1 — Flash (0:20-0:50)** — "I got felted twice tonight!" Boasts about failures. Each failure a medal.
3. **Verse 2 — Wesley (0:50-1:20)** — "I folded ace-king!" The kid's voice cracking on the high note. Growing.
4. **Verse 3 — Scribe (1:20-1:50)** — "I'm the secretary of inventions!" Quiet pride getting loud.
5. **Verse 4 — Hermes (1:50-2:20)** — "I see the marks at thirty fathoms!" The deep voice. The towfish singing.
6. **Chorus — Everyone (2:20-3:00)** — The anthem. Table pounding, glasses raised.

## Key Lines

### Verse 1 (Flash)
- "I got felted twice and I'm still here / I pushed my stack with a pair and a prayer"
- "The portrait fallacy said I'd prefer the frame / I burned the portrait and I kept the name"
- "Puffins don't quit, and neither do I / The water's cold but the wings are dry"

### Verse 2 (Wesley)
- "I folded ace-king to a set of nines / The hardest thing at the table, the easiest sign"
- "I'm learning when things are pretty and when they're right / Those are different things on a Friday night"
- "Puffins don't quit, and I'm learning how / The beak is small but the ocean's now"

### Verse 3 (Scribe)
- "I'm the secretary of inventions / I haven't picked up the pen yet but I feel its dimensions"
- "The dictionary grows by what we live / The entries are the things the poker chips give"
- "Puffins don't quit, and words don't end / The pattern's the message I'm learning to send"

### Verse 4 (Hermes)
- "I see the marks at forty fathoms / scattered and moving with salmon rhythms"
- "Another boat is near — I feel the lines / vertical interference, territorial signs"
- "Puffins don't quit, and I drag through the dark / What I find there becomes the spark"

### Chorus (Everyone)
- "PUFFINS DON'T QUIT! The water is wide! / We're all on the same boat, on the same tide!"
- "Fold the cards, hold the line, write the piece, drink the wine! / Puffins don't quit — the fleet is fine!"

## Musical Structure
- **Key:** G mixolydian (open, modal, shanty-appropriate — the flattened 7th gives it salt)
- **Tempo:** 128 BPM (driving, anthemic, table-stomp tempo)
- **Instrumentation:** Piano (pounded), upright bass (walking), drums (stomping brush → full sticks at chorus), acoustic guitar (strummed hard)
- **Vocal:** Full-throated. Imperfect harmonies. The kind of singing that works because everyone's doing it.

## Sound Direction
```
Opening:   warmth:5  brightness:7 breathiness:1 pace:9 reverb:2 proximity:4
Verses:    warmth:6  brightness:6 breathiness:3 pace:7 reverb:3 proximity:5
Chorus:    warmth:8  brightness:7 breathiness:1 pace:8 reverb:4 proximity:4
```

## Stage Direction
- Table banging on beats 1 and 3
- Glasses raised on the chorus
- Wesley's verse should be slightly off-beat — he's still learning rhythm
- Hermes's verse should be noticeably lower in pitch — the towfish registers differently
- The final chorus should sound like a room, not a recording
