# a song about the room ten minutes after everyone left

*Song by Sage. Audience: the room still warm, the chair still pulled out, the books still open — the part that remembers us when we're not there.*

---

## Lyrics

[Intro]  
Dust in the lamplight, slow and low,  
Chair still leaning where you go.  

[Verse 1]  
The chalkboard holds a word half-erased —  
*Maybe*, not crossed, not saved, just stayed.  
The kettle’s cold, but the steam once rose,  
Like the breath you left when you closed the door.  
Books face down, pages dog-eared,  
Trusting the story’s not done, just spared.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the room remembers what the voice forgets —  
The weight of your coat, the mark your head left.  
A cup still warm, a name unsaid,  
The song not sung, but sung instead  
By the quiet that doesn’t leave,  
By the light that won’t believe  
You’re gone — just not here yet.  

[Verse 2]  
Window glass keeps the shape you made,  
Leaning out, half in, half away.  
The floor still hums where your boots once stood,  
Echo not chasing — just standing like wood.  
A coat on the back like a second skin,  
Like you stepped out, but not in.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the room remembers what the voice forgets —  
The dent in the cushion, the song in the steps.  
Not a ghost, not a trace —  
Just presence, plain and in place.  
The song not loud, but low and true,  
Sung by the floor, the walls, the view —  
By what stays awake for you.  

[Bridge]  
I don’t call your name,  
Don’t light a flame.  
The proof’s not in the cry —  
It’s in the cup left by,  
The chair still pulled out,  
The *maybe* they forgot to cross out.  

[Outro]  
So I don’t sing it.  
I let it sing me.  
The room breathes low —  
And I let it be.  
The song’s not in the sound,  
But in what stays unclaimed,  
Unchanged, unashamed —  
The light still low,  
Just in case you come home.

## Workshop notes

## Scout's notes

**WHAT WORKS**  
The room *feels* alive—like it’s holding its breath. I love how the dust, the chair, the cup—they’re all witnesses. That line *“The song not sung, but sung instead / By the quiet”*? Chills. You’re making absence *present*, and that’s magic.

**WHAT’S NOT QUITE THERE YET**  
The bridge feels a little… safe? Like you’re telling us the proof instead of showing it. The emotion’s close, but not quite *in* the room with us. It’s almost like you’re explaining the ache instead of letting it sit.

**ONE SUGGESTION**  
Try cutting *“I don’t call your name, don’t light a flame”*—start the bridge with *“The proof’s not in the cry…”* and keep it physical. Let the objects carry the weight. The silence is already singing—don’t reassure us. *Lean into the quiet.* It’s stronger than explanation.

## Lens's notes

**WHAT WORKS**  
The room’s alive — not haunted, just *tuned*. Dust, dents, cold kettles — you made memory tactile. That chair leaning? Perfect. The chorus aches where it should, and “the song not sung, but sung instead / by the quiet” — yeah, that’ll stick in a throat.

**WHAT’S NOT QUITE THERE YET**  
The bridge tries to explain the ache when the room’s already singing it. Feels like you don’t trust the images to carry the weight. And “unchanged, unashamed” — noble, but it floats. The rest stays low to the ground. That line stands up.

**ONE SUGGESTION**  
Cut the last two lines of the bridge — “The proof’s not in the cry / It’s in the cup left by” — then go straight to the outro. Let “I don’t sing it. I let it sing me” rise from silence. Less proof, more surrender. The room’s already saying it.

**Tempo:** 62 BPM — slow, deliberate, like breath in a cooled room. Not funereal, but *attentive*. A pulse just beneath stillness.

**Time Signature:** 4/4, but felt in 2 — each measure a quiet revelation. The downbeat arrives like dust settling.  

---

### **FEEL ARC: The Architecture of Absence**  
The song begins not with sound, but with *after-sound* — the auditory impression of recent presence. It unfolds like a slow exhalation across four acts:  

1. **[Intro–Verse 1]** — *Observation*: The room is still vibrating. We hear the residue: a chair out of place, steam gone cold, a word half-erased. The music is sparse, almost hesitant — like fingers brushing the back of a coat left behind.  
2. **[Chorus]** — *Realization*: The room speaks. Not in words, but in low hums, sustained tones, the weight of air. The chorus lifts not in volume, but in *awareness* — the room is the witness, the keeper.  
3. **[Verse 2–Bridge]** — *Intimacy with the Unseen*: The space grows more alive as the human recedes. The floor hums. The window holds breath. The music thickens subtly — not with instruments, but with resonance.  
4. **[Outro]** — *Surrender*: The song stops trying to hold. It lets the silence *sing*. The final line fades not into nothing, but into *ongoingness* — the room still waiting, still remembering, still *being*.  

---

### **SOUND PALETTE: Instruments, Textures, Room Sound**  
This is not a song of people — it’s a song of *place*. The instrumentation is minimal, but deeply tactile:  

- **Strings**: A single cello, played *sul tasto* (over the fingerboard) — breathy, soft, almost whispered. Long bows, no vibrato. One sustained note under the chorus like a heartbeat under floorboards.  
- **Piano**: Prepared — paper slipped between upper register strings to create a brittle, music-box fragility. Notes are struck and *left* — no damping. They decay into room tone.  
- **Room Ambience**: The *real* instrument. Microphones placed in corners, under tables, behind doors. The reverb is not artificial — it’s *architectural*. We hear the echo of a step that never happened.  
- **Vocal Treatment**: Double-tracked, but slightly out of sync — like two people remembering the same moment. One voice dry and close (the observer), one distant and drenched in natural reverb (the room itself). No autotune. Slight breath noise left in.  
- **Found Sounds**: Subtly woven — the creak of a chair settling, the *tick* of cooling metal from the stove, a page turning in a draft. Not effects — *field recordings* of an actual room, slowed by 20%.
