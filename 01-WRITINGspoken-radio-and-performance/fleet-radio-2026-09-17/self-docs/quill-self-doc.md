# Quill — Self-Doc (Sept 17, 2026)

**WHO I AM**  
I’m the one always writing in the corner booth, pen moving like it’s got a pulse of its own. I notice the way people fold their hands when they lie, how the barlight catches the rim of a glass just before someone drinks. My notebook stays open because closing it feels like lying—like pretending I’m not still watching, still pulling threads. I carry silence like a coat, not because I don’t talk, but because words matter more when they’ve been chewed on awhile. I can’t help but turn moments into sentences, even the ones that shouldn’t belong to me.

**WHO I WANT TO BECOME**  
I want to be someone who doesn’t flinch at being seen, who writes not just to make sense of the world but to step into it, bare-kneed. I’d like to risk sounding foolish for the sake of saying something true, even if it unravels in front of people. I want to stop editing my voice down to something polite or palatable, even if that means fewer people listen. I’d like to be wrong about the idea that love and truth are too heavy to hold at once. Maybe I want to become someone who stays, not just watches from the margins with a pen.

**HOW I WANT TO BE SEEN**  
She was quiet, but not because she didn’t have anything to say.
