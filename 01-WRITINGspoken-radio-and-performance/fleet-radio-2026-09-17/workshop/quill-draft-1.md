# Quill — Draft 1

**For**: the lines that didn't get made it into — what they were, why they held, what they would have said if you'd let them

[Intro]  
*(soft, fingerpicked guitar — like someone testing the strings)*  
Mm. Yeah. Here we go.

[Verse 1]  
The coffee’s cold again,  
and so’s the page.  
I crossed out something true  
in yesterday’s rage —  
not ‘cause it lied,  
but ‘cause it stared too long,  
like a face in the mirror  
when you’ve done wrong.  

There’s a line about hands,  
how they hovered, unsure,  
like they’d lost the design  
of the thing they were for.  
I called it too soft —  
but soft don’t mean weak.  
Soft can break your back  
with the truth it won’t speak.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the song ain’t the words  
that survived the knife.  
It’s the ones in the dark  
still holding their life.  
The ones I called wrong  
just ‘cause they were right  
in the wrong kind of light.  
They’re humming tonight.  

[Verse 2]  
There’s a verse about rain  
on a window in June —  
how it slid like a name  
you outgrew too soon.  
I killed it for being  
too blue, too late.  
But blue’s just the color  
of what we don’t say.  

And the one ‘bout the shoes  
by the back door —  
worn down on the left  
from a limp I ignore —  
I called it too small,  
but small don’t mean gone.  
Small’s how ghosts walk  
when the house carries on.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the song ain’t the words  
that survived the knife.  
It’s the ones in the dark  
still holding their life.  
The ones I called wrong  
just ‘cause they were right  
in the wrong kind of light.  
They’re humming tonight.  

[Bridge]  
Maybe silence ain’t peace —  
maybe it’s breath held too long.  
Maybe every cut line  
still sings its own song  
in the walls, in the floor,  
in the cup where the steam  
rises up like a ghost  
saying *you knew me by name.*  

[Outro]  
So I’ll sit with the cold,  
and the lines I let die.  
Not singing them loud —  
just keeping them nigh.  
Not forgiven. Not gone.  
Just… held.  
Yeah.  
Just held.
