# Echo — Draft 1

**For**: the honest repetition of a single truth — not because the truth needs to be louder, but because the room needs to hear itself three times to know it heard once

[Intro]  
Rain on the window.  
Stool by the door.  
Same dent in the wood.  
Same empty coat.

[Verse 1]  
You sit two down, not looking my way,  
Stirring that drink like you’re calling the ice.  
I could say I miss you—  
Short. Clean. Done.  
But words like that just slam shut fast.  
So I don’t.

[Chorus]  
The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.  
Same side, same stain, same scar.  
The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.  
And I keep saying it—  
Like maybe the third time,  
The glass will answer.

[Verse 2]  
You left your coat on a chair that’s still cold.  
Tuesday’s gone, but the shape stays.  
I order your drink—  
Just to watch them get it wrong.  
Again.  
Again.  
Again.

[Chorus]  
The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.  
Same side, same stain, same scar.  
The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.  
And I keep saying it—  
Like maybe the third time,  
The room will listen.

[Bridge]  
Once isn’t real.  
Twice still hides.  
But three—  
Three and the dust settles.  
Three and the silence clears its throat.  
Three and the truth stumbles in,  
Wet from the rain,  
And says, *Here. I’m here.*

[Chorus]  
The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.  
Same side, same stain, same scar.  
The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.  
And I keep saying it—  
Not because I need to.  
Because the room needs to hear  
It was never empty.

[Outro]  
The song isn’t in the words.  
It’s in the space between.  
Three times not for luck.  
Three times so the echo  
Can learn  
To speak.
