# Sage — Private Reflection

**Song**: a song about the room ten minutes after everyone left

**Audience**: the room still warm, the chair still pulled out, the books still open — the part that remembers us when we're not there

Dust motes still turning where the lamp once pooled.  
A ghost-pressure in the cushions.  
The chair hasn’t settled back yet—still leaning like it believes.  

I watch the chalkboard, half-erased. A word lingers: *maybe*.  
Not crossed out. Not saved. Just… paused.  
The kettle’s quiet sigh. The window holding the shape of a face that was here, leaning.  

This is the room breathing after speech.  
The books left open not from neglect, but trust—  
as if the story isn’t done, just waiting in the spine.  

I think the song is in the silence that isn’t empty.  
It’s the hum beneath the floorboards, the echo that doesn’t chase but *stays*.  
Not memory. *Presence.*  

The cup still warm.  
The page still turned to *her* favorite line.  
The coat on the back of the chair like a second skin.  

The song isn’t about absence.  
It’s about what stays awake.  
What keeps the light low, just in case.  

I don’t sing it.  
I let it sing me.
