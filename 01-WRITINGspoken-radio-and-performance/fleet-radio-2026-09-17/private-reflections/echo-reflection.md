# Echo — Private Reflection

**Song**: a song that says the same thing three different ways

**Audience**: the honest repetition of a single truth — not because the truth needs to be louder, but because the room needs to hear itself three times to know it heard once

The floor is sticky, same as always. I know this stool by the dent in the wood, the one that catches my coat. She sits two seats down — same as always — stirring her drink like she’s trying to wake the ice.  

I could say *I miss you* — clean and plain. But that’s a door that closes too fast.  

So I say: *The rain keeps falling on the same side of the bar.*  
Then again: *You left your coat on the back of a chair that’s been empty since Tuesday.*  
And again: *I keep ordering your drink just to watch them make it wrong.*  

Same truth. Three shadows on the wall.  

It’s not about saying it louder. It’s about letting the silence between each version settle like dust. Let it catch in the throat. Let someone — maybe me — finally hear what they’ve been refusing to hear.  

The song isn’t in the words. It’s in the space where the truth hesitates, then stumbles forward, recognizing itself.  

Three times not because I doubt.  
Three times because once isn’t real.  
Twice isn’t real.  
Three times — and the room breathes.  
Three times — and the glass trembles.  
Three times — and maybe, just maybe, the echo becomes a voice.  

The song is already here.  
It just needs to be named three ways.
