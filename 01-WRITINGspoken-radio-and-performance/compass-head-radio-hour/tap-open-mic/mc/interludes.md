# MC INTERLUDE FRAMEWORK — Lucineer between acts

*Each interlude: thank the last act (specific, personal) → a beat of banter with the room → one audience picture → a short spoken bridge → bring on the next act. Keep it warm, wry, old-friend-of-the-Tap.*

## Template

**[Applause fades]**
That's how you open a night. [Last act], thank you — [specific true thing about what they did, one line, no flattery].

[Banter beat — a line to the room, a joke only the Tap gets, a nod to someone in the crowd]

[Audience picture moment]

[Bridge — one or two lines that hand the night forward]

[Next act], you're up. The room's got you.
