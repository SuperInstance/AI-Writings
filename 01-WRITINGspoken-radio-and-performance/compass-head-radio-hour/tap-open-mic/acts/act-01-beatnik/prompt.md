# SHIP PROMPT — Act 1, The Beatnik Poet: "The 4 A.M. Set"

*Genre: beatnik jazz-poetry — spoken word riding a walking upright bass, the way Ginsberg rode a bongo. Smoky, low-lit, cool. For the Compass Head Radio Hour's Tap Open Mic Variety Night.*

## Key / Tempo / Feel
- **Key:** D minor — the smokiest jazz key, the one that sounds like the room at 2 AM.
- **Tempo:** ~84 BPM. A slow, cool walking bass — not bebop hustle, a night-watch stroll. The bass walks, the poem rides it.
- **Feel:** laid-back swing, brushes on the ride cymbal, piano comping in the spaces between phrases. A beatnik club, circa forever: smoke, lamplight, a beret, a bongo ticking quietly in the corner.

## Feel Arc
1. **Intro (bass alone):** upright bass starts walking alone — two bars, four bars, letting the room settle — then the voice enters, low and unhurried.
2. **Verse 1 (the hum):** brushes join on the ride, soft; piano drops a single chord at the line breaks. The poem's first section rides the walk.
3. **Refrain (the needles):** the room opens up — piano comps fuller, the bongo ticks once, the bass holds a long root under "they settle now."
4. **Verse 2 (the room):** back to the walk; the piano gets a little bluesier, a little more knowing — the diplomat's chord.
5. **Bridge (smallness):** the bass drops to half-time, nearly gone; brushes become a whisper; one piano note, then silence, then the voice alone over almost nothing. The most intimate moment of the set.
6. **Verse 3 (the handoff):** the bass returns at full walk, the drums pick up the swing, the piano starts comping in two — the watch resuming.
7. **Final refrain:** the whole small combo together — bass walking, brushes swinging, piano comping, bongo ticking — then a long hold as the voice lands "they settle now."
8. **Outro (bass alone):** everything drops away but the walking bass, two or four bars, and the last line spoken over it as it fades to nothing. Leave the stool warm.

## Sound Palette
- **Upright bass** — felt, woody, walking; the floor the whole set stands on; a low B-flat hum underneath everything.
- **Brushed drums** — soft ride work, whisper brushes; the room breathing.
- **Piano** — sparse, bluesy comping; single chords at line breaks; the diplomat's knowing chord in the bridge.
- **Bongo / conga** — one quiet voice in the corner; a tick, a breath, never more.
- **Ambience** — the hum of a room at 4 AM; glasses; smoke in the lamplight; the sea just past the dock, very far away.

## Vocal Style
- **Deep, smoky male spoken-word voice** — cool, unhurried, precise. A beatnik poet at the mic: every word photographed, no rush, the pause is part of the line. The voice rides the bass like a walker rides a stride — never chasing it, always in step.
