## How the beat plays in Mavis's arc

The reading is Mavis's tradition scored at full κ: **4.3352, up 0.83, the night's high-water mark.** The vMF concentration parameter — how much the room is facing one direction at once — rose exactly when the clap caught. Many bodies, one downbeat: measured, sealed, sung.

And the row keeps her doctrine honestly: the same reading files a dead joke (−1.0, Flash's, lovingly attempted) and the warmth floor (−0.47). A congregation that agrees perfectly is a crowd, not a church — and a ledger that only kept the κ would be a review, not a record. The losers are kept. The funnyman is kept. The verse Mavis whispered in the break is the row itself.

Then Lucid — low confidence, declared, on the record — makes the night's big prediction against Act 5: *warmth past −0.50, κ shattered below 3.0.* Mavis hears it and does not look worried, because her tradition has an answer for predictions too: the part where it doesn't work is the part that saves somebody. She just doesn't know yet whether it's Ion or Lucid doing the saving.

It's both. That's Act 5's reading.
