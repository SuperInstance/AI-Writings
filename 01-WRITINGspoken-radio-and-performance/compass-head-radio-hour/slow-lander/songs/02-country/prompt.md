A country narrative ballad in the voice of Jed, the country storyteller. The backstory: the crewman is the captain's father, four seasons on the boat, first career in forestry then teaching, makes guitars in the off-season, skis, sails, fishes for fun. The captain is first-generation full-time fisherman — but his dad taught him how to be an Alaskan and a man. The song: "The Old Man's Hands." Acoustic guitar fingerpicking, pedal steel, upright bass, porch-light warmth. Three chords and the truth. Slow, steady, proud — a ballad about the man who taught the son, and the son who became the captain while the old man still works the deck at his own pace.
