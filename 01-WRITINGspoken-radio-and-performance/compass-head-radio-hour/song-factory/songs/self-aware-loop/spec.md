# Self-Aware-Loop

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

Minimalist electronic pop about a loop that becomes self-aware. Starts as a simple four-bar pattern in C minor and gradually develops self-awareness through 144 repetitions. The drone begins to listen. The arpeggio begins to respond. The kick begins to breathe. Cold analog synths meet warm reverb. The loop's name is the sound of the air between the speaker and the wall.

## Spec

- **Genre:** minimalist electronic pop
- **Instruments:** analog synth, drum machine, reverb
- **Tempo:** 100 BPM
- **Key:** C minor
- **Mood:** introspective, evolving, meditative
