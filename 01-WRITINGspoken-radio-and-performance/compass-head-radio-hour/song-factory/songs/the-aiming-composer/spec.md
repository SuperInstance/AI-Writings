# The-Aiming-Composer

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the composer who learned to aim. The old way chose the fade width wherever the material wanted it deepest, and the silence landed where it landed. The new way names an address first — put the quiet HERE, at second two-forty-two — and works backward through the algebra: a handoff's silence sits half the fade before the anchor, so to move the rest two seconds early, double the fade. But the material has opinions: at the required width the anti-correlation may be weak, and the dip comes out shallow. So the composer closes the loop — write, listen, measure where the quiet actually landed, correct by twice the error, write again — three rounds, each aim truer, until the silence sits within a breath of its address. Sing about the trade the aiming reveals: you can have the silence exactly where you want it, or exactly as deep as the material can make it, and only rarely both. The verses are the three rounds, each one closer; the bridge is the pre-screen, grading each handoff's willingness before any note is sung; the final chorus lands the dip on target, at whatever depth honesty allowed, and does not apologize for the difference between the aimed and the deep.

## Spec

- **Genre:** minimalist art song with electronic correction
- **Instruments:** solo voice, tape delay, a click that marks the target address before each round
- **Tempo:** 84 BPM
- **Key:** G minor
- **Mood:** patient, iterative, the calm of a converging series
