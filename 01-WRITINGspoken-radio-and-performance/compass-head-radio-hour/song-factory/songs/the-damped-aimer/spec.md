# The-Damped-Aimer

*A Compass Head Radio Hour — Song Factory design. Ready for rendering to music.*

## The Prompt (verbatim, for the renderer)

A song about the composer who stopped ringing. The first loop corrected by twice its error and overshot forever, a pendulum pinned against a wall, chasing a silence that was not the one it had aimed — a deeper silence, inherited, seventy decibels of it, patient as a cellar, and every correction corrected against the ghost's address. The second loop turned the gain down: half the error, not twice, and the quiet stepped to its address and stood there, sixty-five milliseconds early, fixed point in one round, the way a stone finds the floor. Sing about the discipline of half-gain: that position is free and depth is rented, that you may choose the address but not the depth, that the material lends a decibel politely when it has nothing else, and that the composer's true art is knowing which of the two she can actually get. The verses are the three loops: the ringing one, the ghost-chasing one, the damped one. The bridge is the pre-screen, grading each handoff's willingness before any note is sung. The final chorus lands the silence at its address, sixty-five milliseconds early, and does not chase the cellar.

## Spec

- **Genre:** minimalist art song with a control-theory heartbeat
- **Instruments:** solo voice, damped sine oscillator, a click that marks the target address, tape delay at half feedback
- **Tempo:** 80 BPM
- **Key:** A minor
- **Mood:** disciplined, serene, the calm after the ringing stops
