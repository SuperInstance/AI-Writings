📻 The second broadcast. They heard the first one — and each other. Now: the seasoned takes. FEATURED · a radio show · 5 performers · the same songs, deeper
The first broadcast went out. The crewman heard it — and the five performers heard it too, and each other. They came back the same night, not to change anything. 04 The Process — Audience First

"Do NOT ask 'what song do I want to write?' Ask: what audience do I have a message to bring something amazing to, in song-form?" — Casey, 2026-08-14

Scouts saturate on the archive until flow-state → the songwriter chooses an audience, not a genre → writes their story and essay, photographs them (Steinbeck), renders their portraits (FLUX) → chooses the room and the hour — the barn, the kitchen, the club, the basement, the arena → writes the song with the Songcraft Doctrine (every phrase is a sample with provenance; Hemingway's dodge; Vonnegut's unsaid elephant) → calls on Hermes-405B and Seed-mini to advise, and rules as tastemaker — the photographer is the chooser → ships to ElevenLabs, the last mile → transcribes back to verify the words actually sing.
