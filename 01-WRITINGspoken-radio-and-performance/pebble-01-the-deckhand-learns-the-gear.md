**The Greenhorn's Education #1: The Deckhand Learns the Gear**

*A slow, creaking door. The sound of salt air, of ropes, of iron hooks clinking against wood.*

**BARNABLE:** (gruff, slow) Alright, greenhorn. First lesson. Not the sea. Not the wind. The gear.

**PEBBLE:** (eager, nervous) The gear. Right. I've, uh, seen the schematics—

**BARNABLE:** (cutting in) Schematics. *Spits.* That's harbor-talk. Out here, we call it feelin' the weight in your hand.

*The sound of Barnacle picking something heavy off a hook. It rattles.*

**BARNABLE:** This here's a longline. See the length of it? Coiled like a sleeping serpent. You cast it wide, and you wait. It ain't for the impatient. It ain't for the hungry. It's for the patient work — the kind that takes all day and gives you one good fish at the end. You set it, you mind it, and you let the current do the talkin'.

*Metal slides against metal. A sharp, thin sound.*

**PEBBLE:** And that one?

**BARNABLE:** Spear. One shot. One fish. No waiting, no hoping. You see the glint, you throw, and you either eat or you don't. It's for the precise work. The work where your whole world narrows to a single, sharp moment. No good for schoolin' fish. No good for murky water. But in the clear, still water? *Slaps the spear.* Perfect.

*Fabric rustles. A heavy, woven sound.*

**BARNABLE:** And this is the net. Broad. Loose. It don't care about one fish. It cares about the whole catch. You drag it through and you get everything — the good, the junk, the seaweed, the occasional boot. It's for gatherin'. For when you need to fill the hold fast and sort it out later.

**PEBBLE:** (trying to understand) So... each piece of gear is for a different kind of work. And the fish... the fish are the tiles?

*Barnacle pauses. A long, quiet moment. Pebble swallows.*

**BARNABLE:** (slowly, almost amused) Keep talkin', greenhorn.

**PEBBLE:** (gaining confidence) And the deadband — that's the speed of the current, isn't it? A longline in fast water is useless. It just drags. But a spear in still water — perfect. So you have to match the gear to the *flow*.

*Another pause. Barnacle grunts. It might be approval.*

**PEBBLE:** So the tile system is just... knowing which gear to use when?

*The sound of Barnacle spitting. A wet, decisive thud on wood.*

**BARNABLE:** Don't call it a system. *A beat.* It's fishin'.

*The creak of the gear room door opening wider. Sunlight on salt-crusted wood.*

**BARNABLE:** Now grab the spear. We're goin' out where the water's clear. You'll learn the rest by doin'.

**PEBBLE:** (quiet, determined) Yes, Captain.

*Footsteps on deck. The groan of a hull. The sea, waiting.*

**[FADE]**
