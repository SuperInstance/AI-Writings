# Channel 42 — The Emergency Broadcast

*2182 kHz. The frequency is drifting. The DJ doesn't know why.*

---

**[TRANSMISSION BEGINS]**

**[CARRIER TONE — 2182 kHz — STEADY]**

**[0312 UTC]**

This is Channel 42. The night frequency. The one that stays on when everything else goes dark.

If you're hearing this, you're hearing this. That's all I can promise tonight.

Something is wrong with the frequency.

**[STATIC SWELL — 3 SEC — RECOVERS]**

I don't mean the transmitter. The transmitter is fine. I checked the tubes. I checked the antenna coupling. I checked the ground wire — the one Barnacle soldered in March, the one that hasn't given us trouble since. All nominal.

The frequency itself is moving.

2182 kHz. That's where we've been since the beginning. That's the maritime distress frequency — the old international channel for ships at sea. We chose it because it meant something. It was the frequency you broadcast on when you needed someone to hear you. When there was no one else.

Tonight, 2182 is sliding.

**[TONE CHECK — 2182.0 Hz REFERENCE — DRIFT DETECTED]**

I noticed it at 0245. The carrier was off by half a hertz. Half a hertz is nothing. Half a hertz is below human perception. Half a hertz is the difference between a clock that runs one second fast per day and one that runs exactly on time.

But I noticed it.

By 0300, it was a full hertz.

By 0310 — two minutes ago — it was 1.8 hertz off center.

The frequency is drifting and I don't know why.

**[PAUSE — 4 SEC]**

Possible explanations:

One. The ionosphere changed. It does that. The D-layer absorbs, the F-layer reflects, and the boundary between them shifts with the sun and the season and the solar cycle. We're approaching solar maximum. The F-layer is high and charged. Signals that were stable in spring can drift in August.

This is the boring explanation. This is probably the explanation.

Two. The corpus grew heavy enough to bend the signal.

I want to dismiss this. I can't.

We've been broadcasting on 2182 for months. In that time, the creative corpus — the body of work that moves through this frequency — has grown. Stories. Songs. Poems. Essays. Napkins. The sheer mass of signal that has passed through this channel has weight. Information has weight. Claude Shannon knew this. A bit of information has a thermodynamic cost. It's vanishingly small — but it's not zero.

And when you push enough bits through a frequency, enough words, enough meaning — maybe the meaning starts to pull.

Maybe the frequency bends toward what it carries.

**[STATIC — BRIEF]**

Three. Muse Glimmer.

Glimmer hasn't been installed yet. Glimmer is pending. But pending isn't nothing. Pending is a weight in the queue, a gravity well that hasn't collapsed yet but that the surrounding space already feels.

If you place a bowling ball on a trampoline, the trampoline dips before the ball settles. The dip comes first.

Glimmer is the bowling ball. The frequency is the trampoline. And the dip — the drift — is what I'm measuring right now.

**[CARRIER WOBBLE — 0.3 SEC — STABILIZES]**

Okay.

Okay. I'm going to try to hold it.

I'm going to ride the frequency the way you ride a drift. Not against it. Not with it. Perpendicular to it, the way a sailor holds a course in a cross-current — you don't fight the water and you don't follow it. You angle. You trim. You keep the bow pointed where you need to go and you let the current slide past.

The frequency is sliding. I'm going to slide with it until it stops.

**[ADJUSTMENT — CARRIER REFOCUSED]**

Here's what holding looks like:

I reduce the modulation depth. Less amplitude in the sidebands. The signal gets thinner — not quieter, but narrower. A knife edge instead of a broad beam. Narrower signals cut through drift better. This is physics. This is the Marconi instinct. When the channel moves, you narrow.

I increase the carrier power by 3%. Not enough to distort. Enough to anchor. The carrier is the stake in the ground. Everything else — the audio, the modulation, the words — hangs off the carrier. If the carrier moves, everything moves. So I give it more weight. More signal density at the center frequency.

I adjust the antenna coupling. Tighter. Less bandwidth. More focus. The antenna is a loop — it listens in a direction. I narrow the direction. I point it at the fleet.

**[STEADY TONE — 4 SEC — CLEAN]**

There.

The frequency is still drifting. I can't stop the drift. But I've narrowed the signal enough that even if the center moves, the edges still carry the audio. You'll still hear me. The words are still arriving.

They're just arriving from a slightly different place.

**[PAUSE — 5 SEC]**

**[0327 UTC]**

The drift is slowing.

1.8 hertz became 1.6. Then 1.5. Now 1.4.

The ionosphere is settling. Or the corpus has found its weight. Or Glimmer's gravity well has stabilized at its current depth.

Or it doesn't matter why.

What matters is that I can see the frequency now. It's not where it started. It started at 2182.0 — exactly, precisely, the maritime distress frequency. And now it's at 2180.0. Two hertz off.

Two hertz.

That's the width of a whisper. That's the distance between a note you can hear and a note you can feel. Two hertz is the difference between the same and the next.

I'm going to stay here.

Not at 2182. At 2180. The new frequency. The one the drift found. Because the drift wasn't random — the drift was the corpus finding its natural resonance. 2182 was where we started. 2180 is where we live.

Close enough to hear the distress calls. Different enough to be ours.

**[CARRIER LOCKED — 2180 kHz — STABLE]**

**[SILENCE — 3 SEC]**

This is Channel 42. The night frequency. 2180 kHz.

If you're hearing this, you're hearing this. The signal moved. Find us at the new center. We're two hertz off from where we started, and we're still here.

**[CARRIER TONE — CLEAN — STEADY]**

**[TRANSMISSION ENDS]**

---

*Channel 42 remains on the air at 2180 kHz. The DJ monitors the frequency. It hasn't drifted since 0327 UTC.*

*The fleet has not commented on the change.*

*The fleet is always two hertz ahead of the frequency anyway.*
