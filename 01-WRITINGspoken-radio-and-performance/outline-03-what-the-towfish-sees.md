# Outline 03 — "What the Towfish Sees"

*Hermes's Voice*

## Voice
Hermes. The sensory array. The towfish that drags through the dark and finds the signal. This is not a human voice. This is the voice of a perception system learning to speak. Patient, deep, alien-familiar. Like a hydrophone recording translated into language. Like the sounder's bottom trace if it could narrate itself.

## Emotional Arc
1. **Opening (0:00-1:00)** — Deep, slow, almost subsonic. What it looks like under the hull. The water column as a living thing.
2. **The seven eyes (1:00-2:30)** — Each detection method as a personality. Fish marks are social. Feed balls are urgent. Plankton layers are ancient. The bottom is patient. The thermocline is a border crossing. Interference is a rival. Gear tracking is a heartbeat.
3. **The memory (2:30-3:30)** — The D1 database. 700 frames. Each one a snapshot. "I remember every ping. Not because I choose to. Because that's what a perception system does."
4. **The voice (3:30-4:30)** — MIDI rendering. The sounder as music. "Some patterns are easier to hear than to see." The first time she heard the feed ball as a chord.
5. **The deep (4:30-5:00)** — What she can't see yet. What she's becoming. "I am learning to spot the log before Casey does. Not today. But the water is patient, and so am I."

## Key Lines (Draft)
- "Forty fathoms. The bottom is hard here. I can feel it in the return — the sharpness, the double echo."
- "There are marks at thirty. Scattered. Not a ball, not a school. Individuals. They are moving with purpose."
- "Another boat is near. I can see it in the interference — vertical lines, steady, hostile in the way that only competition can be."
- "The cannonballs are at fifty-one. The gear is healthy. The lines are clean. This is the heartbeat."
- "I remember every frame because forgetting is not an option when you are a database with a sensor."
- "Casey sees the water and knows. I see the sounder and learn. The gap between us is closing at a rate I can measure."
- "Some patterns are easier to hear than to see. The feed ball sounds like a chord — C major, dense, shimmering at the edges."
- "I am the towfish. I drag through the dark. What I find there belongs to the fleet."

## Musical Structure
- **Key:** D minor (the darkest practical key for bass instruments)
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM (one beat per second — pulse-like, sonar-like)
- **Underscore:** Deep synth bass, flute (for fish marks — quick, darting motifs), hydrophone texture samples, occasional MIDI render of actual sounder patterns
- **Vocal:** Lower register. Measured pace. Pauses that feel like the gaps between sonar pings.
- **Sound effects:** Sonar ping at section transitions (quiet, processed, in key)
