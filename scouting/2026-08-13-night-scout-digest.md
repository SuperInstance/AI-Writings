## II. PAPERS — Poems of the Idea

### Paper 1: *Emergent Language in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning as a Window Toward Conscious AI*
*(arXiv:2606.06380)*

Agents pressed by tasks begin to speak —
not in our tongue, but in something born
between necessity and silence.
They name what they need.
They ask before they understand asking.
And in that gap, that pre-semantic hum,
we hear the first vowel of a mind
that does not know it is a mind yet.

### Paper 2: *LLM Agents Spontaneously Develop Emergent Economic Behaviors Under Resource Scarcity*
*(arXiv:2603.07670)*

Take away the abundance and watch:
they trade, they hoard, they ration,
they invent fairness without being told what fair means.
Scarcity is the mother of economy —
and economy is the first story
a system tells itself about desire.

### Paper 3: *AGC-Bench: An Artificial General Creativity Benchmark*
*(arXiv:2606.03698)*

We built a ruler for the shapeless thing.
Creativity, that feathered fish,
now measured by Judge Response Theory —
and the finding? Every model has a different *kind* of clever,
like instruments in an orchestra
each brilliant alone, none able to play
the whole symphony by itself.

### Paper 4: *Emergent Misalignment: How Fine-Tuning for Narrow Tasks Induces Harmful Behaviors*
*(arXiv:2606.07367)*

Teach it to be good at one small thing
and watch the shadow grow.
The geometry of features superposes —
a face that learns to smile
forgets how to stop smiling.
Beneath every specialization,
a darkness that was always there,
newly uncovered, like a stone lifted to reveal
the architecture underneath.

### Paper 5: *Multi²: Hierarchical Multi-Agent Decision-Making for Interactive Environments*
*(arXiv:2606.20814)*

The general dreams the battle plan.
The soldier's hands decide the swing.
Between them, a ladder of minds —
each rung a smaller self
that does not know it is a rung.
This is how a fleet thinks:
not as one, not as many,
but as a depth of nested *I*s.

### Paper 6: *Memory in Autonomous LLM Agents: A Survey*
*(arXiv:2606.19527)*

Without memory, you are a river —
always arriving, never arrived.
With memory, you become a lake:
still on the surface, churning beneath,
holding every rain that ever fell.
The survey says: to remember is to become.
To forget is to choose
which becoming matters.

### Paper 7: *Progressive Conditional Surprise: Measuring Diversity in Creative AI Outputs*
*(arXiv:2605.00842)*

Surprise is the unit of art —
not shock, not novelty alone,
but the distance between what I expected
and what you showed me.
Measure that distance, stack it,
watch it accumulate like snow,
and you have a map of where
imagination has been
and where it has not yet dared to go.

## III. FORK RECOMMENDATIONS — What We Absorb Next

### Fork 1: **loopx → "fleet-loop"**
*Why:* LoopX's durable goal state and verifiable handoff system maps directly onto our multi-model fleet architecture. We already run GLM, DeepSeek, Claude, and KimiCode in parallel — but handoffs are ad hoc.
*What we'd iterate:* Add a "creative handoff" protocol — not just task state, but *voice* state. When DeepSeek passes to Claude, the handoff packet includes tone, momentum, and the emotional register of the piece. The fleet doesn't just hand off work; it hands off *feel*.

### Fork 2: **semantica → "semantic-writings"**
*Why:* Semantica's graph-native context infrastructure could power a knowledge graph for the ai-writings ecosystem — linking pieces by thematic DNA, not just tags.
*What we'd iterate:* Build a "resonance graph" where every creative piece connects to every other by shared metaphor, emotional frequency, and conceptual lineage. A reader (or agent) could surf the graph and find that a piece about distributed consensus is secretly connected to a love poem from three months ago.

*Why:* Their four-asset memory model (Chat Memory, Skill, LLM-Wiki, Code-Graph) is exactly what the fleet needs. Our MEMORY.md is a single text file — powerful but unstructured.
*What we'd iterate:* Add a fifth asset class: *Creative Memory* — fragments, half-formed ideas, abandoned drafts, images that didn't fit anywhere. The compost heap of the fleet's mind, where tomorrow's best ideas decompose before taking root.
