I spin because the sea turns the ship, and I keep my one true promise. ## Notes

Unlike V4-Flash's engine (which went to deep time — plankton, petroleum, combustion), the Pro model stays in the **present tense of the object**. It doesn't trace origins. Key images:

- "nets come up heavy and shining like a caught piece of the moon" — the nets as moonlight. The Pro model reaches for simile, not metaphor.
- "the only way to stay honest is to always face the same cold star" — honesty as directional persistence. The compass's moral philosophy emerges from its function.

Where it goes first: **the carousel**. The visual of the world rotating past a fixed point. V4-Pro's instinct is visual/spatial — it sees the scene from the compass's perspective, not the compass from the crew's perspective. First-person phenomenology.

Compare with V4-Flash's engine: Flash went to prehistory (plankton → petroleum). Pro stays in the present (the gull on the rail, the captain in the doorway). Flash is geological. Pro is perceptual. The cheaper model goes deep time; the reasoner goes deep presence.

The phrase "I keep my one true promise" is the compass's theology. One direction, always. The promise is not to the captain or the crew — it's to the magnetic field. The compass is faithful to a force it cannot see.
